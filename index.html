Taxacom Archive

TAXACOM, the biological systematics discussion list, from 1992 to 2025. Search every message below, or browse by month.

The list today

Taxacom continues as a Google Group. The list is maintained by Neil, Shinichi and Richard.

This site is a read-only archive of the list as it was hosted at lists.ku.edu. Links on archive pages that once pointed to that server's list information page now point here.

Taxacom was started by Drs. Patricia Eckel and Richard Zander on 12 January 1987 and was hosted for many years at lists.ku.edu. That server no longer serves the archive. This site keeps the original page paths: a link to lists.ku.edu/pipermail/taxacom/… works here if you replace the domain.

Email addresses are obscured as they were in the original public archive.
