The Web page for this symposium is now officially "open":
(www.vurv.cz/triticeae <http://www.vurv.cz/triticeae>) . It will take
place in Prague in 2005. The purpose of the Symposium is to bring
together diverse groups of people working with this tribe, taxonomists,
breeders, ecologists, population biologists ... so that we may share
insights.  If you or your colleagues are interested in attending, please
visit the Web site.
