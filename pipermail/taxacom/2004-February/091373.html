This is to advise that all issues of TTR volumes 1 & 2 (1998 - 2000) and
now available in their entirety in PDF at this link
http://tils-ttr.org/report01.html  These are located at our TILS web site
( http://www.tils-ttr.org ).

Volumes 3 & 4 (2001-2003) still have only the first page of each issue
available.

TTR deals with Lepidoptera.  Most papers have been on US taxa, but there
are also papers on taxa in Argentina, Canada, China, and Pakistan.  We
would like to be publishing a much higher % of non US papers and more on
moths. Vol. 4 will be finished soon.

Ron Gatrelle
