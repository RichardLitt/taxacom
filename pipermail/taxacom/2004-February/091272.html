2004 
VISITING ASSISTANT PROFESSORS
BOTANY DEPARTMENT, MIAMI UNIVERSITY (OHIO)

We seek individuals for academic year 2004-05 to teach primarily at the
undergraduate level.  Courses may include one or more of the following:
Introductory Biology, General Botany, Field Botany, Evolution of Plant
Diversity, or Ecology of North America.  Participation at the graduate
level is possible, with the course load constituting a full-time VAP
appointment beginning 16 August 2004.  A Ph.D. in Botany, Plant Biology, or
related field is required. Review of applications will begin April 1, and
will continue until positions are filled.  Send a cover letter addressing
teaching experience, a CV, and names and contact information for at least
three references to:  Linda E. Watson, Chair, Department of Botany, 316
Pearson Hall, Miami University, Oxford, OH  45056.  Specific questions
regarding these positions can be obtained by contacting Linda Watson at
513-529-4200 or watsonle at muohio.edu.   http://www.cas.muohio.edu/botany/
