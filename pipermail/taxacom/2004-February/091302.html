I mentioned earlier on Taxacom that the closed-mouth smile appears to be
unique to humans and orangutans. After further communications with several
primatologists this characteristic appears to still hold true. I now have a
color picture of an orangutan with the smile at:
http://www.sciencebuff.org/primates/Human%20origins/Orangutan-human/Evidencefororangutan.htm

It is located at the bottom of the page under potential synapomorphies.
