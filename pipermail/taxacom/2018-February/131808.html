     One recent paper shows a tree in Figure 1 (taken from Legg et al. 2013) with Remipedia as sister group to Hexapoda, forming a clade called Miracrustacea.  And then Malacostraca as sister group to the Miracrustacea.   However, I've also seen trees showing Cephalocarida as part of Miracrustacea (but that seems less certain).
