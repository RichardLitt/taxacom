“Moreover, those who dichotomize are forced to use loss [as a character],
and this is exactly what they do. But loss, as absence, is not a
differentiation; because it is not possible for species to be by virtue of
non-existing characters, such as the footless or the wingless, along with
species having wings and feet.”


(on parts 642 b 22-26).
