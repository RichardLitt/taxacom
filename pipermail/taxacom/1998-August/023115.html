1998 
Recent dialog on TAXACOM regarding various aspects of plant
nomenclature suggest that further, focused discussion might be of
value.  With this in mind, the Internet Communications Committee of
the American Society of Plant Taxonomists has established a web site
at:

http://arnica.csustan.edu/aspt/

that allows all with an interest in this topic to post positions,
arguments, or questions that can form the foundation for subsequent
dialog. While various subtopics can be developed as 'threads' within
this system, it is centered on discussion of issues pertaining
to plant nomenclature. This 'discussion web' is now empty, and anyone
with an interest in the topic, especially recent issues regarding
proposed change, is hereby invited to initiate the system by posting
articles.

This site, which is also available via the ASPT homepage at:

http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/aspt/aspthome.htm

is under development by Dr. Steven J. Wolf and supported by
CSUBIOWEB at California State University Stanislaus.
