1998 
NSF Doctoral Dissertation Improvement Grant program

NEW Announcement and Deadline

Dear Organismal Biology Colleagues:
     NSF has just issued a revision to its Doctoral Dissertation
Improvement Grants program, serving the programs in Ecology,
Ecosystems, Systematics, and Population Biology (in the Division of
Environmental Biology, DEB), and in Animal Behavior and in
Ecological and Evolutionary Physiology (in the Division of
Integrative Biology and Neuroscience, IBN). The new annoucement is
available electronically through the NSF Web site (www.nsf.gov), as
NSF 98-151. It replaces the old NSF 96-132, and importantly, it
announces a NEW DEADLINE starting THIS YEAR: the Third Friday in
November.
     Please get the word out to your colleagues, if interested.
