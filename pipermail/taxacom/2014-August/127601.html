For those interested, I have added pdfs for a variety of Leon Croizat
articles between 1971 and 1994, including some on aspects of
paleoanthropology published in a local Venezuelan magazine. Also included
is Croizat's 'panbiogeography in brief' written in French. It is ironic
that in my early days I experienced protestations from English speakers as
to why Croizat never published an abbreviated version - as if there were no
other language in existence other than that of English.

The list is below (includes a couple already posted as well) from
http://johngrehan.net/index.php/panbiogeography/croizat-publications

John Grehan
