Taxacomers with revolutionary spirit may be interested in a three-part paper:

Zander, R. H. 2014. Classical determination of monophyly, exemplified with Didymodon s. lat. (Bryophyta). Parts 1, 2 and 3.
Phytoneuron 2014-78: 1-7<http://phytoneuron.net/2014Phytoneuron/78PhytoN-MonophylyPart1.pdf>,   79: 1-23<http://phytoneuron.net/2014Phytoneuron/79PhytoN-MonophylyPart2.pdf>:   and 80: 1-19<http://phytoneuron.net/2014Phytoneuron/80PhytoN-MonophylyPart3.pdf>.

In which I attempt to formalize (find the statistical and logical basis) of the heuristics classical taxonomists use to intuit monophyly. We are apparently good at it but can't say why. Until now. If I'm right.
