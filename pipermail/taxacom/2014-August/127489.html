The management of the Taxacom mailing list is delighted (finally) to announce: Herp-Names-Crimes-and-Recriminations, a new mailing list dedicated to alleged professional misbehavior pertaining to the application, re-application, valid and invalid publication of biological names in herpetology.

Postings relevant to this topic will be moved from the Taxacom list as appropriate, as will subscribers.



To subscribe to Herp-Names-Crimes-and-Recriminations, simply send a blank e-mail to:

  herp-names-crimes-and-recriminations-subscribe at mailman.nhm.ku.edu<mailto:herp-names-crimes-and-recriminations-subscribe at mailman.nhm.ku.edu>

          (Subject and Body of message are ignored.)



To post to Herp-Names-Crimes-and-Recriminations, send your message to:

  herp-names-crimes-and-recriminations at mailman.nhm.ku.edu<mailto:herp-names-crimes-and-recriminations at mailman.nhm.ku.edu>

          (Only members may post.)
