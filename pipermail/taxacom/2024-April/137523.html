In IRMNG I currently have Scolytidae Latreille, 1804 as a synonym
of Curculionidae Latreille, 1802 with the remark "Treated as subfamily
(Scolytinae) of Curculionidae in Bouchard et al., 2011.", that reference
being

 Bouchard, P.; Bousquet, Y.; Davies, A.; Alonso-Zarazaga, M.; Lawrence, J.;
Lyal, C.; Newton, A.; Reid, C.; Schmitt, M.; Slipinski, A.; Smith, A.
(2011). Family-group names in Coleoptera (Insecta). *ZooKeys.* 88:
1-972., *available
online at* https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoi.org%2F10.3897%2Fzookeys.88.807&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cb1e53bb51e1c4b3ae67b08dc539ef573%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638477190026558554%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C40000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=HH30JQ4u22KPiOt%2FaMnd2Ys%2B3RNA7EHyVQBHpwUx0QE%3D&reserved=0

Of course more recent publications may come to a different conclusion, not
yet considered for IRMNG, but that is what I have.
