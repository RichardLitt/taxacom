The Systematics Association has planned an exciting programme of talks and activities for this year's meeting, our first in-person conference since 2019. We look forward to welcoming you to Reading - only 30 minutes by train from London.  You can find more information on the conference website: https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsystass.org%2Fevents%2Fbiennial%2F&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Ce583e29f805741b9cfd108dc57bf129b%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638481725366063065%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=2tCHbCFx6G8C8yX5clgKf4x%2FBewUdHW4M%2BuGkutGGbw%3D&reserved=0.



The programme is designed to be especially attractive to students, and we hope many students will join us:

  *   Just £120 for three days for student Systematics Association members – including the scientific programme, a choice of workshops, refreshments and lunches, and a free welcome drink at our student networking event
  *
Opportunity to present their work to a friendly and supportive, specialist audience
  *   Bursaries towards travel and accommodation costs
  *
Prize for best presentation
  *

IMPORTANT DATES:
- Conference: 19th to 21st June 2024.
- Early bird registration and applications for bursaries close 14th April.
- Standard registration and abstract submission closes 31st May 2024.

2024 Biennial Conference Organising Committee and The Systematics Association Council
