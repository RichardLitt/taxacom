Dear friends and colleagues,

In February the eminent lepidopterist Paul Opler (CSU Fort Collins, Colorado) lost his battle with cancer. With his wife Evi Buckner-Opler, I published an obituary and his list of publications in the program and abstract volume of the 71st Annual Meeting of the Lepidopterists's Society that starts today in Billings, Montana. The paper can be accessed here: https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.researchgate.net%2Fpublication%2F372450784_Obituary_Paul_Alexander_Opler_1938-_2023_with_list_of_publications_-_Denver_Museum_of_Nature_Science_Reports_21_13-22%2Freferences&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C756afaecd4544d8bd01c08db88601046%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638253718410462443%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=MXZ45byf1g91YqOoe26WXvOHWn8%2BtadQUDp5SbNAZpM%3D&reserved=0
