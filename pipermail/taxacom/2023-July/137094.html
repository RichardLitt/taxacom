Yes, that’s correct, Ray,

The Reptile Database doesn’t have most Hoser names because the herpetological community decided to ignore them.

That’s because you refuse you provide meaningful data in your species descriptions, with most of them having no illustrations, no maps, no tables, no sequences, no statistics, no phylogenies, and above all, poor descriptions (such as diagnoses based on “larger size”, "lower incidence of scale anomalies”, "more rhomboidal-shaped dorsal scales” (example for Morelia mippughae Hoser 2003, Newsletter of the Macarthur Herp. Soc. Issue 40, August 2003:4-9, which by the way, is in the Reptile Database as a synonym of Morelia spilota).

BTW, we did not DELETE Russian names, we temporarily removed a bunch of Russian papers in protest over the Russian invasion of Ukraine (we do not regret that).
