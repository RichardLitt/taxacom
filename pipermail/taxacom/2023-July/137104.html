> Evidence for the age and origin of original peoples in the Americas is an
> object lesson on fossils and other remains providing a minimum age estimate
> as new finds continually push back the minimum age. Researchers have
> now analyzed
> pendants made of bony material from sloths and concluded that the carved
> and polished shapes and drilled holes were the work of deliberate
> craftsmanship. Dating of the ornaments and sediment at the Brazil site
> where they were found point to an age of 25,000 to 27,000 years ago -
> several thousand years before some earlier theories had suggested the first
> people arrived in the Americas, after migrating out from Africa and then
> Eurasia. Here the note is on 'some earlier theories' as the older age
> corresponds more with Croizat's predictions made in the 1950's and 60's
> (including trans-oceanic voyages) than all those later  imposing
> anthropological 'authorities'. Of course no good prediction ever goes
> unpunished.
