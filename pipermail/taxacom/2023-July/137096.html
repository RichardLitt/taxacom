Peter - thanks for the succinct summary of the Hoser taxonomic situation.
His past rants gave very little coherent reasoning. He seems to be under
the delusion that everyone is obligated to accept whatever taxonomic
designation anyone may propose. Based on the reasons you listed, the
decision no to accept names lacking such necessary criteria would seem very
reasonable. Just as reasonable as not accepting a species name lacking a
designated type no matter how good the description. It would seem at the
very least that if Hoser is aware of the additional data needed for his
names to be acceptable by colleagues that it would be a straightforward
matter of providing that information.

Cheers, John
