Not only does Doug handle complaints against him with aplomb, he also does the apparently incredibly difficult task of NOT including every thing ever written on any particular thread in his responses.

I receive taxacom as a digest.  At the top of the digest is written:

"When responding to a message, please do not copy the entire digest into your reply”.

Apparently most posters do not know this, and they do include just about every response in their response.  This makes for a digest that is about 15 kilometers long to read (hyperbole here).

Would taxacom users please try using a bit of netiquette and pare down their responses to just the previous one to which one is responding?
