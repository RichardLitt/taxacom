'Surely the ICZN should be mindful of satisfying the actual users of
scientific names, rather than pandering to outdated tradition?'
    While many of the traditional names (and morphological terms) are old
they are not outdated - we use them every day. The easiest way of dealing
with the huge number, which can be overwhelming for a beginner,  is
learning a bit of basic Greek and Latin vocabulary.

> Hi Thomas,

> I agree with your students! I simply memorise the currently accepted name -
> and I am sure that's what most people do. Most users of names (scientists
> who are not taxonomists and the public) know little if anything about the
> Code and gender agreement. My belief (which could easily be tested with a
> survey of such users) is that many grudgingly accept that species are
> sometimes moved to new genera, but most are bewildered when the spellings
> of species-group names are also altered. If the spellings were fixed this
> would benefit the majority of users of scientific names. Surely the ICZN
> should be mindful of satisfying the actual users of scientific names,
> rather than pandering to outdated tradition?
