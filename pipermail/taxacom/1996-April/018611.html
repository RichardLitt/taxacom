Terry,

For expert assistance with an image processing problem, your friend should
consider signing on to the SciArt-L listserv.  It is sponsored by the Guild
of Natural Science Illustrators and is a forum for professionals in the
field.  Much of the listserve discussion centers on *exactly* the type of
work your friend is doing.

To subscribe, send an electronic mail message to: listserv at unl.edu

...with the following command in the body of the message:

subscribe SciArt-L Your Name

As an alternative, you could contact Pauline Denham Tandon, the list
moderator.  She would be glad to post a query to the group for you.  Her
address is pdenham at unlinfo.unl.edu.
