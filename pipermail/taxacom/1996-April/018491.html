Dear All,

Over the last few years a project between Herbarium Bogoriense in Indonesia
and the Arnold Arboretum of Harvard University in the USA, has been working
on the flora of Bukit Baka Bukit Raya National Park in Indonesia.  We have
developed an interactive key, partially illustrated, to the >220 tree and
shrub genera we found there.  This key will be extended in scope to
encompass the entire island of Borneo over the next couple of years.

The key can be downloaded in compressed form  from
[ftp://huh.harvard.edu/pub/bbbr/bbbr@.exe].  Enjoy, and if you find any
errors or room for improvement please let me know.

All the best,

Jim
