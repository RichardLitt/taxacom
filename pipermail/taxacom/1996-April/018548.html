1996 
Dear friends,
   Like most of you, I have accumulated a significant amount of technical
literature over the years. Much of it has sat unread on my shelf since the
day it arrived. Others I do occasionally use, but could just as easily
look up in the library on those rare occasions when I need to. My hayfever
being as bad as it is, I find it desirable to minimize the amount of stuff
gathering dust around here. Hence I offer the following items to anyone
who can provide them a good home. I anticipate multiple requests, so
please try to convince me that your institution would make a better home
than some other place which might request the same item. All I ask for
these is reinbursement for postage and handling, i.e. US$1/issue of
journal and US$5/book.

Thanx.

Joseph Laferriere
JosephL at ccit.arizona.edu
