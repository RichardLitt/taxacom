Dear Jorge,

Van Doesburg used to work at the National Museum of Natural History at
Leiden but he retired many years ago. He is still an honorary worker there
and comes in every now and then. The best way to get into contact with him
is by snail mail:

Dr. P.H. van Doesburg
National Museum of Natural History
P.O. Box 9517
NL-2300 RA Leiden
Netherlands

If it concerns a matter of some urgency, for example concerning the
collection, then the best way is to address the curator of the group you
work on by e-mail on:    ent at nnm.nl
