The herbarium at California State University, Chico (CHSC) has an almost
complete set of Madrono volumes 1-12 that we would like to trade for
herbarium specimens.  These volumes are not bound.  I don't have the notes
handy, but I think volume 1 is from the teens or twenties.  Volume 12 is
1953-1954.  Volumes 1 & 2 are in 17 parts, Volumes 3-12 are in 8 parts.
This collection is minus Volume 6 nos. 1, 3, & 6, and Volume 12 no. 3. 
Madrono is subtitled "A West American Journal of Botany" and is produced by
the California Botanical Society.  We will pay shipping for the journals,
you pay shipping for the herbarium specimens.  If you or your institution
is interested, please make an offer for all or part of these journals to: 
lpjaneway at compuserve.com
Thanks, Lawrence
