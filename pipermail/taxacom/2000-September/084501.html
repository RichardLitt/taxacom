Dear TAXACOMERS
Here's a small bit of news.
My book 'Fig insects of Kerala' has came out.

Citation:
Priyadarsanan, D.R. 2000, FigInsects of Kerala, Rec. zool. Surv. India,
Occ.Paper No. 182: i-iv, 1-175pp (Published: Director, ZSI, Calcutta)
Price:  $20 (foreign)/Rs.300/= (India)

Apart from the introduction, the book contains a  review of all papers
on the fig insects published untill 1995
Keys and descriptions of all subfamilies, tribes, genera and species of
fig insects including the non-pollinators (of Kerala) and a complete
bibliography.
This publication deals with 6 subfamilies, 23 genera and 62 species.
It also includes descriptions of 17 new species

Copies are available with
