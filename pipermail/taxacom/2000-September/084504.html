Hello,
The last three years I've made a research on the species Draba in naturpark
Karwendel, Tirol, Austria. Thus I found few specimens of Draba aizoides that
differ from others, because of the high number of hairs on the leafes: the
leafes are hairy not only at the edge of the leafes (as it is usual for this
species), but also on upper- and underside. In literature (Hegi's Flora) I
found a variation named -var. trachyphylla O.E. SCHULZ- with leafes also on
upperside little hairy. Chromosomal analysis (2n=16) on those special
specimens concludet to be 16 as all other Drabas I found. So I just wanted
to ask, if someone found similar variation of this species....
Thanks, so long Christian
