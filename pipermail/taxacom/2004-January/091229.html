2004 
For those of you that have an interest in the taxonomy and nomenclature
of plants in cultivation, I am glad to be able to announce that the new
(7th) edition of the International Code of Nomenclature for Cultivated
Plants (Cultivated Plant Code or ICNCP) will be published in February.

If you wish to receive further details, please reply to
dvader at indhort.demon.co.uk with the message ICNCP in the subject line
and I shall be glad to forward you full details.

Piers Trehane
Rapporteur, ICNCP 2004
