2022 
Catalogue of Life (COL) and the Global Biodiversity Information Facility
(GBIF) work together to assemble and improve a comprehensive expert-driven
list and classification for all the world's species. Construction is
by semi-automated methods but depends on engagement with the taxonomic
community to source and maintain component datasets for each taxonomic
group.

COL and GBIF have published a vacancy for a Global Species Catalogue Editor
to support this work. More details can be found here:

https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.catalogueoflife.org%2F2022%2F08%2F15%2Feditor-vacancy&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C5c0527dbc5044bd7ec0c08da7ffd7ba1%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C637963023937175631%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=63KlA9JV1Pv9tUO6jVr4bAMhtrnJWFE27RQLZ7K9iTY%3D&reserved=0

Please circulate this vacancy to anyone who may be interested and suitable.
