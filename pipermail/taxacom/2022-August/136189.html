Dear all,

With our best regards from Ecuador and China, we would like to send you a
friendly reminder about our Chinese-Neotropical Botanical Diversity Virtual
Symposium to be held tomorrow Wednesday and Thursday.


Dates/Hours
Wednesday 17th August 2022, 07:00-10:15 (Ecuador Time); 20:00-23:15(China
Time)
Speaker: Qing-Feng Wang, China. Talk: Diversity and Evolution of Aquatic
Plants
Speaker: Xavier Cornejo, Ecuador. Talk: Botanical Studies in Coastal Ecuador
Speaker: Yaowu Xing, China. Talk: The Origin and Assembly of the World
Richest Temperate Alpine Flora
Speaker: Katya Romoleroux, Ecuador. Talk:   Endemic flora of the Ecuadorian
Páramos


Thursday 18 August 2022, 07:00-10:15 (Ecuador Time); 20:00-23:15(China Time)
Speaker: David A. Neill, Ecuador. Talk: Botanical Studies in Amazonian
Ecuador and the sub-Andean cordilleras
Speaker: Limin Lu, China. Talk: Temporal and spatial comparisons of
angiosperm diversity between eastern Asia and North America
Speaker: Hugo Valdebenito, Ecuador. Talk: Flora of the  Galápagos Islands
Speaker: Hui Liu, China: Talk: Distribution, evolution and adaptation of
plant functional traits of Magnoliaceae in China


Previously registered participants, and one hour in advance (5:30 am
-Ecuador time/19:00 Hours -China time) you will receive an email with a
personalized link for the event.

For new attendees, please go to this site, and register:

After your registration, you will receive an email with a personalized link.
Once you open this link, you will need to type the mandatory fields again.
Please make sure you write down your complete name for the certificate.

There is a capacity for 500 participants, so if you are unable to join the
meeting, you will still be able to attend it through our Youtube Channel.
