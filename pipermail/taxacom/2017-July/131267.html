Am 04.07.2017 um 20:54 schrieb Thomas Pape:
> Michael:
>
> You are entirely correct that Scopoli (1772) gave the spelling "brunnichii", and he may have named the species after the Danish zoologist Morten Thrane Brünnich.
> As for deciding the correct original spelling in the sense of the Code, you mention that the name is "commonly given as Argiope bruennichi (Scopoli, 1772)".
> If that spelling is in prevailing usage, the following Article kicks in:
>
> 33.3.1. when an incorrect subsequent spelling is in prevailing usage and is attributed to the publication of the original spelling, the subsequent spelling and attribution are to be preserved and the spelling is deemed to be a correct original spelling.
>
> /Thomas Pape
