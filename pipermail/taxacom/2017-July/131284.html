Dear all,

The Biodiversity Heritage Library<http://www.biodiversitylibrary.org> will be evaluating website features and functionality to identify ways we might improve the user experience. As members of the biodiversity community, your feedback will be incredibly valuable in helping identify how we can best serve your needs. Your input will help us identify and prioritize which improvements and features to focus on in the design process.

Thank you to anyone who has already submitted response through the banner on the BHL website! If you haven't taken the survey and would like to provide your feedback, the survey will be online through Friday, July 28.

Click here to take our survey!<http://s.si.edu/BHLUserSurvey>

We appreciate any thoughts or suggestions you may be able to provide! If you have any questions or comments, please contact Pamela McClanahan at mcclanahanp at si.edu, or you can always submit comments through the BHL feedback form<http://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/contact#/comments>.

Thank you!
