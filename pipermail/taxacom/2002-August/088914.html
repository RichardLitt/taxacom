2002 
Dear scarab enthusiasts,

I am announcing the release of version 2 of the electronic
publication entitled "Checklist of the Scarabaeoidea of the Nearctic
Realm."  This taxonomic checklist includes all scarab beetle species
from North America and northern Mexico.  Included are all valid names
with a complete list of synonyms in their original combinations with
authors and dates.

The new version contains many corrections and updates from the first
version (released last year).  THANKS TO ALL THOSE WHO SENT ME
CORRECTIONS AND COMMENTS ON THE FIRST VERSION!!

I would like to hear from anyone who has further corrections or
additions to the list.  Copies of the list can be downloaded in PDF
or MS Word format from the following website:
http://www-museum.unl.edu/research/entomology/nearctic.htm

This research was supported by NSF-PEET DEB-9712447 to B. C.
Ratcliffe and M. L. Jameson and NSF-PEET DEB-0118669 to M. L. Jameson
and B. C. Ratcliffe.

Enjoy!

Sincerely,
Andrew Smith
