Diana Lipscomb has instructions for Nona and Winclada on her web page,
http://www.gwu.edu/~clade/faculty/lipscomb/. Winclada is a windows

data sets and manipulate and print trees. Both programs can be downloaded
from http://www.cladistics.com.
