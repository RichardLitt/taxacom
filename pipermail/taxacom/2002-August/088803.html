At 09:06 PM 8/16/02 +0530, Gurcharan Singh wrote:

>I would like to know the first author (and the publication) to use  the
>term Systematics in literature.

See T. F. Stuessy, Plant Taxonomy: The Systematic Evaluation of
Compoarative data (ISBN 0-231-06784-4, Columbia University Press, New York,
1990), pp. 5-6 for a discussion.  He quotes Linnaeus, Gen Pl. ed. 5 (1754):
"Let two enquirers, one a Systematic, and the other an Empiric ..."  He
also mentions books with the phrase "Systematic[al] Botany" in the title
from 1809 and 1827.
