The following message was previously sent to NHCOLL-L:

I am forwarding this request for Dr. Robert Dickerman of the Division of
Ornithology at the Museum of Southwestern Biology, University of New
Mexico.  Please respond directly to him at: bobdickm at unm.edu.

Dr. Dickerman is looking for a source of blue archival-quality paper for
use as skin labels for topotypes of birds.  The labels will be attached
to dry specimens only.  While archival-quality paper is available, it is
sold only (?) in light colors.  Does anyone know of a source for colored
(preferably blue) paper?  On a related matter, is it possible to dye
"archival quality" paper so that it can retain this quality?  Thank you
for your assistance.
