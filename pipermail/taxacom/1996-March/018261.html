 In reply to Dr. Carvajal's query, I am working on a revision of the
 Mexican species of Trifolium, as well as a monograph of Trifolium
 section Involucrarium, and a treatment for the Flora of Chile.

 The Zohary & Heller monograph was still available from the publisher
 as of last fall.
