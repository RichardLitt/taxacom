Announcement

Research Entomologist Positions

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture, announces the availability of 2 permanent full-time Research
Entomologist positions in Insect Systematics.  Both positions will be at the
Laboratory's location at the U.S. National Museum of Natural History,
Washington, D.C.

Recruitment will be for positions in:

1) Systematics research on Chrysomelidae and related families(Coleoptera).

2) Systematics research on Noctuoidea (Lepidoptera).

We anticipate that these positions will be open for applications from April
1 through 8, 1996.  These positions will be available immediately, with
actual start date negotiable.  Salary range will be from  $37,094 - $52,876
(GS 11-13) depending on qualifications.
