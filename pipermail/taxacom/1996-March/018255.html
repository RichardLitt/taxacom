In my message concerning the Macintosh font "Planets" (which has male and
female symbols), I failed to be specific about some of the ftp sites from
which the shareware font can be downloaded and have therefore received a
few email messages from those wishing to know what the ftp sites are. Some
specific sites are listed below. A more full listing of ftp sites for this
and other fonts and other software that are downloadable can be seen on
(and downloaded from) the web at:  http://www.shareware.com/

For those of you without web access, you can ftp from:

Australia:
archie.au/micros/mac/umich/system.extensions/font/type3/planetsvuw.sit.hqx

Switzerland:
nic.switch.ch/mirror/umich-mac/system.extensions/font/type3/
     planetsvuw.sit.hqx

US:
mirrors/apple.com/mirrors/mac.archive.umich.edu/system.extensions/font/
     type3/planetsvuw.sit.hqx

UK:
src.doc.ic.ac.uk/computing/systems/mac/umich/system.extensions/font/
     type3/planetsvuw.sit.hqx


Hope this helps,

Neal
