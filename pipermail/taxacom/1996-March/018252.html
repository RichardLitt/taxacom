WordPerfect 6.1 for Windows contains the font 'WP IconicSymbolsA' which
includes 'male' and 'female' symbols, and can be used in other editors that
work under Windows. I use it in Pagemaker 5.0 to insert 'male-female'
symbols in. The 'male' symbol corresponds to % (Shift+5) sign on the US
keybord, and the 'female' symbol corresponds to & (Shift+7) sign.
