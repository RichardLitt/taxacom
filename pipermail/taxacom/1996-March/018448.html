1996 
______________________ Teaching Fellowship Available __________________

The College of Charleston Department of Biology announces the availability
of a postdoctoral teaching fellowship for the academic year 1996-97.  The
position provides mentored experience in teaching at the undergraduate-
level in introductory general biology and in upper-division courses in the
fellow's area of specialization.  The fellowship is intended to provide
teaching experience for those seeking permanent academic positions at the
college level.  A Ph.D. in a Biological Science is required.  Applicants
should submit by April 15 a letter stating teaching interests and career
goals, a c.v. and two letters of reference to:

Professor Louis E. Burnett
Chair
Department of Biology
College of Charleston
Charleston, SC 29424
