1996 
Internet Directory for Botany:

We have created a common home page on the WWW, the new
'Internet Directory for Botany' for two popular indexes
to botanical information available on the Internet, i.e.,
the alphabetically arranged 'List of WWW Sites of Interest to
Botanists' and the categorically arranged 'A Collection of Botany
Related URLs'.

The home page of the Internet Directory for Botany is
in Canada, at

http://meena.cc.uregina.ca/~liushus/bio/idb.html

The 'List of WWW Sites of Interest to Botanists' has been renamed
'Internet Directory for Botany - Alphabetical List', and is
accessible from Canada (home), Finland, or France via

http://meena.cc.uregina.ca/~liushus/bio/botany.html
http://www.helsinki.fi/kmus/bio/botany.html
http://biomserv.univ-lyon1.fr/botany/botany.html

'A Collection of Botany Related URLs' is now the 'Internet Directory
for Botany - Subject Category List', and can be reached from Finland
(home) or Canada from these addresses:

http://www.helsinki.fi/kmus/botmenu.html
http://meena.cc.uregina.ca/~liushus/bio/subject/botmenu.html

The pages belonging to the 'Alphabetical List' and the 'Subject
Category List' are thus at their old addresses in Canada and Finland -
and these sites now have mirrors of each other. Both lists are
searchable, frequently updated, and there are also pages indicating the
latest additions and changes. There are now over 1500 links in the
Internet Directory for Botany. Suggestions of new links are welcome,
as well as corrections of broken links.

Additional information re. additions and corrections to the
'Internet Directory for Botany', mirrors and
citation, etc. can be found at

http://meena.cc.uregina.ca/~liushus/bio/info.html
