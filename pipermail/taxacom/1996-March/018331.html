     Erinyes (= Eumenides) - goddesses (pl.)  of revenge (= Roman Furiae) -
    three daughters of  the  Earth fertilized with Uran's  blood  drops and
    Night (Alecto,  Maegera, Tisiphone).  Alope -  two alternate  meanings:
    (1) a town in Locris Opuntica (Central Greece); (2)  hairless, baldness
    (cf. alopecia recently occurred among Ukrainian children).
