The Web site for the Manual to the Plants of Costa Rica, previously
accessible only through INBio in Costa Rica:

http://www.inbio.ac.cr/manual.plantas/plantae.html

can now be seen at the Missouri Botanical Garden:

http://www.mobot.org/manual.plantas/plantae.html

This hierarchical list of names and specimens for over 60'000
collections and approximately 8,500 species of vascular plants has Costa
Rican distribution maps for all species and representative color photos
for most families.  The Garden site should assure State-side users more
efficient access.  Due to the efforts of Gregorio Dauphin at INBio, the
list now also includes many bryophytes.
