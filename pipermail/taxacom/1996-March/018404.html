Dear Southeastern Biologists:

We would appreciate receiving pickled flowers of Schisandra glabra to use
in comparative studies to ascertain if a recently discovered Schisandra
species in central Mexico is conspecific with S. glabra.  The flowers can
be preserved in ethanol or Copenhagen mixture
(10:1:8-ethanol-glycerol-water).  We are mostly interested in receiving
female flowers.
