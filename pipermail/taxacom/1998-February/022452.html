Dr Dessart is presumably thinking of "TL-2" (Taxonomic Literature, ed.
2, prepared in 7 volumes, by the late Frans Stafleu and Dick Cowan (Dick
Cowan died on 16 November and Frans Stafleu on 16 December 1997 - see
the February 1998 issue of Taxon).  The work was continued by Stafleu
and Erik Mennega (latterly mainly by Mennega) with a "Supplement", of
which 4 volumes have been published and a fifth (to the end of the
letter D) is expected.  Erik Mennega has sadly also just died [on 28
January of a heart attack], so it would seem unlikely that the
Supplement will be continued.

The 11 published volumes run to some 6240 pages of factually rich text -
a bit much even for the Web.  Most volumes are available from Otto
Koeltz, who handles the publications of IAPT (International Association
for Plant Taxonomy).  A full bibliography, derived from Koeltz's list
and with Koeltz's book "ID's" and prices, is appended after Dr Dessart's
message.
