 "                     H.B.K.

                             Laharpe 52

Ex Hb. Mus. Paris

Mixed (illegible) this a second specimen, 6 androc, in fruit, is
probably........

Orinoco"

       Could be possibly this specimen from the Venezuela Guayana flora

national d'Histoire Naturelle. Types are in BR, P.
