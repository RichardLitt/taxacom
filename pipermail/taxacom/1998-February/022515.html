Hi taxacomers:

I am pleased to announce that in CONABIO's web site we recently launch an
electronic version of a Guide for identificacion Birds and Mammals that
are most traded in Mexico and are under the protection of CITES
convention.

This guide wich is in a dynamic database format, contains information on
identification, distribution, maps and high quality images.
