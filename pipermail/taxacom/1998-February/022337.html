1998 
I have just returned from Panama and I have brought back some freshwater
polychaetes inhabiting tubes and living among a phylactolaemate bryozoan
colony. I found them in an inlet off of the canal near to the San Miguel
locks.

I have them alive in my lab now, but will preserve them soon if it looks
they are dying off.

If anyone knows anything about such creatures, I would love to hear about
them. I will be willing to send off a specimen to interested people.

Thanks.... Scott Roush
