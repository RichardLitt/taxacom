In reply to Diana Horton's query about textbooks:

I use "Plant Taxonomy and Biosystematics" by C. A. Stace (Cambridge U.
Press; ISBN 0-521-42785-1).  It's becoming a little dated, but it does
have reasonably broad coverage of theory and practice.  This text
provides material for about 2/3 of my lectures; the remainder of the
course is a survey of major families of flowering plants.
