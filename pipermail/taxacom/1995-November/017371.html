There is a WWW site with information on the Wollemi pine. It was apparently
last updated this past February, so it may not be the most up-to-date
information, but it includes some beautiful line drawings. The URL is

http://kaos.erin.gov.au/life/end_vuln/plants/liv_fossil.html

and if you're not in Australia, may take a while to access. According to the
WWW site, the description is supposed to appear this year in the journal
_Telopea_; does anyone know whether it's been published yet?
