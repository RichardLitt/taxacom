Are there any bird, mammal or herp interest groups out there?  I have
enjoyed all the plant and invertebrate commentary on taxacom and have every
intention of continuing to subscribe.  However, as Asst. Curator
(collection manager) of mammal, bird, and herp collections here, I had
hoped there might be some lists out there more closely related to my areas
of interest.  I currently also subscribe to the "natural history
collections list" so I don't need info about that.

If anyone can help me out, I'd appreciate it.  Thanks!      ... Liz

The main list for workers interested in reptile and amphibian
research is herp-l.  To subscribe, send the message:

subscribe herp-l yourname.

Send it to:

listproc%xtal200 at harvard.harvard.edu.

Note that the character "l" before the characters "200" is an alpha
"l", not the number 1.

Also of interest may be the amphibian decline list.  To subscribe,
send the message:

subscribe amphibiandecline your name

To:

listproc at ucdavis.edu
