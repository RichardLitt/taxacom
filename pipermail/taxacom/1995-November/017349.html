 Thew folks in the state of Maryland, may need a food package or two at
this time of the year. They are at it again with still more Entomophagy.
The November issue 16:2 of Phaeton, the official newsletter of the
Maryland Entomological Society has till more recipes for bug to eat. This
issue includes the directions for John The Baptist Bread; Polpette Alla
Salsa Di Pomodori; Tenebrio Balls; Dry-Roasted Insects; Insect Marinade;
and Basic Cooked Insects. They state that there will be still more. For
those of you who can not wait they note that: {This information has been
drawn, liberally paraphrased from: The Joint Services Newsletter, 11th
edition, l992; published by the British Royal Army Medical College,
Millvank, London SW1P 4RJ, editor:David Malone}

Wonder what the Winter Solstice season will bring???
