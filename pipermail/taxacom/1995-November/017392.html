1995 
NATURAL HISTORY/BOTANY: The Evergreen State College seeks recently
trained biologists with 1 - 10 years of experience for full-time
continuing and full-time visiting  faculty positions beginning Sept. 15,
1996.  Seek applicants who can incorporate plant-based field studies into
team-taught interdisciplinary programs.  Disciplinary/methodological
strengths should include historical/comparative approaches (systematics,
biogeography) and/or experimental approaches (ecology, forestry).
Knowledge of flora and ecology of Pacific Northwest, initiation of
student-oriented research program, and experience with herbarium
techniques desired. Application procedures: Faculty Hiring Coordinator,
TESC, Olympia, WA 98505 or e-mail pinsonr at elwha.evergreen.edu.
