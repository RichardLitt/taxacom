               Dr. James L. Luteyn, New York Botanical Garden, has
          recently placed an abbreviated taxonomic treatment of the
          Ericaceae from his "Flora of Ecuador" manuscript on the
          World Wide Web.  The treatment includes descriptions of the
          family and 21 genera, keys to the genera and 218 species, ca
          45 color images, and 10 line drawings.  It can be accessed
          at http://huh.harvard.edu:1995.  Comments are welcomed
          and may be sent to jluteyn at nybg.org.
