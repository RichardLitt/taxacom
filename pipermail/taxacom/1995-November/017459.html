     Please excuse cross-posting.
     The University of Nebraska State Museum is wanting to inquire
about exhibit quality small mammal taxidermy.  If you know of
taxidermists specializing in small mammals or know of other lists
(this posting to NHCOLL-L & TAXACOM) to which postings can be sent
please reply directly to Dr. Patricia W. Freeman, Curator of Zoology
at pfreeman at unlinfo.unl.edu .
     The species currently being considered as part of a western
Nebraska diorama are Bushy-tailed Woodrat (Neotoma cinera), Myotis
bats, and Northern Pocket Gopher (Thomomys talpoides).
     Thank you in advance for your assistance.
