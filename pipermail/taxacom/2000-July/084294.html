Scott,

You might want to at least consider reprogramming the forms to provide users
with at least some feedback, if this data field is left blank, or left blank
for all but certain "exempt" taxa (mouse, Drosophila, Arabidopsis, etc.)
that their submission is incomplete in a very important respect.

 The feedback might include additional detail about the taxa, such as links
to various taxonomic websites specializing in information about specific
taxa (ITIS, IPNI, preferred respositories for specific taxa, or sites
dealing with curatorial practices, ASC or various "taxonomically" oriented
socieites, etc.).  This could definitely help in the educational processes,
without adding further burden to your staff.

Stuart
