Nah, it's the Long Branch Saloon of Dodge City, Kansas, in the days of
Marshall Matt Dillon (or was that Mike Dillon?). Cowpokes from miles around
would congregate there, which is the source of the term "Long Branch
attraction". It was later declared an attractive nuisance and rerooted.
