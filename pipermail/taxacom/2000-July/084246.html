DGPE - London 2000

DEVELOPMENTAL GENETICS AND PLANT EVOLUTION

International meeting sponsored by the Systematics Association.

Venue: Natural History Museum, South Kensington, London

The conference aims to bring together developmental geneticists and plant
molecular biologists with plant morphologists and anatomists, as well as
molecular systematists and plant evolutionists. It is intended to provoke
discussion and to be a springboard for stimulating new botany

Date: 20-22 September 2000

For PROGRAMME, SPEAKERS, ABSTRACTS AND REGISTRATION FORM see:
http://www.icmb.ed.ac.uk/research/cronk/conference/conference.html
