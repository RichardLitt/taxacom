The excellent work by the people at NCBI (including
systematists/taxonomists) should be experienced by all taxacomers.
PubMed references not only molecular and medical journals but other
scientific journals such as J Econ Entomol and J Exper Zool.

http://www3.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Entrez/index.html
After doing a search on your favorite organism, check the 'Related
Articles' when you find an article of interest.

Peter: Revision history is available from
http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:80/htbin-post/Entrez/girevhist
There is a clear link to it from the nucleotide search site
http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:80/entrez/query.fcgi?db=Nucleotide

Incidentally- I found out about papers I did not know about
previously referring to my group of interest by doing a search in the
nucleotide search site.
