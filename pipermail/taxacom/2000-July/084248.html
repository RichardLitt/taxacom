Opening for Biologist/Botanist, Zilker Botanical Garden, Parks and
Recreation Department, Natural Resources Division, City of Austin, TX
[Permanent position; Closing Date: 08/11/2000 1:00 PM, Requisition ID: 86 -
3586]

Position Announcement: Seeking highly motivated, out going, and independent
worker to join the staff of the Zilker Botanical Garden (see
http://malford.ci.austin.tx.us/cityjobs/jobs_detail.cfm?Requisition_ID586)
.

Primary Tasks: Responsible for development and maintenance of plant
conservation collection at Zilker Botanical Garden, labeling and database
entry of living plant collections, identification of native and ornamental
plants, acquisition of collecting permits from public and private
landowners, developing cooperative conservation programs with outside
agencies and researching threatened and endangered plants.  Related tasks
include public education, public speaking, and the training and supervision
of volunteers in species and habitat conservation.

Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in the natural sciences such as
ecology, conservation biology, botany, horticulture, or other related area
or four years experience directly related to job duties.  Masters and Ph.D.
welcomed.   Working knowledge of database programs, preferably MS Access,
valid driver's license, willingness to travel and work weekends and
holidays, ability to perform work outdoors in all weather conditions,
independently and with others. Preferred: knowledge of Texas flora and field
experience, experience with horticulture, landscaping and publishing
helpful.

Licenses:  Obtain Texas Structural Pest Control License (for pesticide
applications) within 6 months of employment.

Position pays $2679-3348/month, depending on qualifications, benefits
included. Application deadline is 11 August 2000, 1:00 P.M.  More
information on the job and benefits is available at the City of Austin Job
Search webpage at <http://www.ci.austin.tx.us/cityjobs/default.htm> (select
View new jobs, then scroll down to Biologist position).  Applicants must
submit a completed City of Austin Application Form (download off the Net at:
http://www.ci.austin.tx.us/cityjobs/applic.htm).  Please read thoroughly the
"Application Instructions" that are listed on the latter site. City of
Austin Application Form MUST INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING REQUISITION ID NUMBER: 86
- 3586.  In addition, applicants should submit a r?sum? (CV), a letter of
interest, college transcript, and three references.  Mail city application
and additional forms to: Employment Center, P.O. Box 1088, Austin, TX 78767,
or FAX to (512) 499-3321.  Applications MUST be received by 08/11/2000 1:00
PM.  The City of Austin is an Equal Opportunity/Affirmative Action Employer.
[Posted 24 July 2000].  For additional information contact Justin Williams
at (512) 478-6875.
