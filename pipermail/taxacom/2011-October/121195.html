You don't need continuous land (e.g. a lost continent) for vicariance. For example, two metapopulations on an active island arc?(surviving in situ on individually ephemeral islands) can be separated by rifting. There has been extensive volcanism over large areas of the Pacific plate since the time of its origin, and on the Hawaiian chain at least back to 85 Ma (at which point the chain disappears into the Aleutian trench).?The dispersal model for Hawaii relies entirely on the suggestion that?there was a period (33-29 Ma) in which no islands (Clague et al, 2009:1) or no high islands (Clague et al., 2009: 10)?were emergent, but the calculation this was based on is dubious. For example,?it predicts current elevations of the Hawaiian volcanoes to be much lower than they actually are, so it probably also underestimates the heights of earlier islands.
