validity depends on one diagnosis (or description) in Latin (or from 1-1-2012) or English ... and any number of others in any number of languages. The 'one' makes the name valid (available, in ICZN speak) unless it is challenged as not diagnostic (very rare). The application of the name is determined by the type; the diagnosis in this repect is not that important since the diagnostic characters included are essentially taxonomic opinion and outside the scope of nomenclature :-)

with less haste,
