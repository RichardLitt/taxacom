Isn't it interesting that two papers have appeared recently that both estimate the number of species, both use essentially the same data (e.g., Catalogue of Life and WoRMS), and yet they arrive at very different answers?

The two papers are listed here: http://iphylo.blogspot.com/2011/10/how-many-species-are-there-and-why-do.html

Rather than dismiss one or other (or both) of the studies, surely the question is how come the results are so different?
