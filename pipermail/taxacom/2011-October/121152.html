On Saturday October 29th, 10:30am-1:30pm, we will hold an open discussion on
the future for e-only publication in zoological nomenclature at the Natural
History Museum, London. As you are no doubt aware, e-only has been passed by
the ?botanical? nomenclature committee, and the issue is hot for the ICZN.

The meeting will be in the Dorothea Bate Room, Palaeo Dept, NHM. There is no
charge and all are welcome to attend. We expect nothing less than heated
debate and fiercely defended opinions. Be part of it!

To help with logistics and provide you with details on entry, please let me
know if you will attend:
iczn-em at nhm.ac.uk
Coffee and tea will be provided, unless fisticuffs result.


Ellinor Michel
