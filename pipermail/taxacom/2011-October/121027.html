The Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, is running a Tropical Plant Identification Course in 2012.

http://www.kew.org/learn/specialist-training/courses-a-z/tropical-plant-family-identification/index.htm


Taught by botanists from Kew's Herbarium, all with an in-depth knowledge of the plants and habitats of the tropics, the course will give participants an overview of 70 of the most commonly encountered tropical plant families, as well as an introduction to plant morphology and identification tools.



Dates of next course: 23 April - 4 May 2012



Course poster and application form here:



http://www.kew.org/ucm/groups/public/documents/document/kppcont_026816.pdf
