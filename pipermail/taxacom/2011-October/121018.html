Hi Eric,              
       From what is presently known of the phylogeny, Bromeliales is
indeed close to Commelinales (Liliales is more distant, so any
similarity to Bromeliales would be a mixture of convergences and
plesiomorphies).     
       Bromeliales (only a family in APG) is at the very base of APG's
bloated Order Poales, so Order Bromeliales actually splits off right in
between the Commelinales-Zingiberales group (on the one hand) and the
traditional Poales on the other hand.  
       What would make perfect sense to me would be to keep Bromeliales
and Typhales as separate Orders and then the rest of the families could
remain in their Poales.  Some people might think such a Poales is still
too large, but it would at least be a workable compromise that would
offend a lot fewer endusers.
