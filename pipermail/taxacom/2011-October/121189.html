Note that a recent discussion with publishers reminded us that the name
?e-only? is a slightly misleading simplification in that what is really
being discussed is whether an electronic document should be considered the
version of record, even if it is followed by a print copy at a later time.
It has at its heart questions of archiving.

We will have representatives of both the yay and nay camps, from end users
to taxonomists to publishers, and we expect lively debate.
