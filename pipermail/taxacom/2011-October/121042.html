it's Latin or English ... not Latin and/or English ... there was never an 'and/or' ... the full reports and the new wording are in the official reports in Taxon and about 15 other journals including MycoKeys. The 'Code' is now the NC (International Code for the Nomenclature of algae, fungi and plants) - not the ICBN.
