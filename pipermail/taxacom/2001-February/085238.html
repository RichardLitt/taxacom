2001 
The 2001 meeting of TDWG will be held in Sydney from the 9th to
11th November, 2001.

It will form part of a series of meetings (Species 2000, HISCOM,
IOPI, SPP) mainly happening in and around Royal Botanic
Gardens, Sydney then moving on to Canberra.

Further details will be posted on the TDWG web site as they
become available.

http://www.tdwg.org
