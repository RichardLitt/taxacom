In a similar vein, Corpscon.exe is a free Windows-based program from the Army
Corps that is available at
<http://mapping.usgs.gov/pub/software/current_software/corpscon_4.12/>.  It
converts coordinates in virtually any system to coordinates in virtually any
other system.  It can process single coordinate pairs entered manually, or
process ASCII files having long lists of coordinates.  The output can be to the
screen, printer, and/or file.  I use Corpscon frequently to convert coordinates
from UTM to Lat/Lon and vice versa.  It is the most convenient of several that I
have tried.
