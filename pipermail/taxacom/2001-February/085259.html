Chinese professors Li Jingke and Chen Peng published soft booklet in Chinese
language only "Studies on fauna and ecogeography of soil Animal" in 1993.

Xantholinus parajaponocus, Dicentrius chineus, Ontholestes hairaerensis,

etc.
