From: Ali kelly

There are free converters for different datums at Geocommunity,
http://software.geocomm.com/coorconv/
You may have to experiment a bit to find out which works best for you.
Corpscon allows for batch conversions.  A simple converter for Lat/Long to
UTM and vice versa is at Zetaware http://www.zetaware.com/.  They have a
free version that allows for manual (single point) entry and conversion.
There are quite a few more converters out on the net, I use NorthernLight
to search on "convert utm" or something similar, which gives me many pages
of links.
Ali
