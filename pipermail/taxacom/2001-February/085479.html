A new version of the Bibliography of the Neuropterida has been posted to
the web. Version 6.2 contains a total of 9542 citations and includes 567
new citations and 691 updated citations. This searchable bibliography is a
comprehensive, world wide, source of literature information on the insect
orders Neuroptera (lacewings, antlions, owlflies), Megaloptera (alderflies,
fishflies) and Raphidioptera (snakeflies). The bibliography also contains
exhaustive coverage of the work of the Spanish Jesuit Padre Longinos Nav?s,
who published on numerous orders of insects (among other things!). The
bibliography, thus, may also be of some interest to entomological
researchers whose primary work is not on the Neuropterida.
