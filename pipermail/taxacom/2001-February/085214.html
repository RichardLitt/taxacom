NEBC GRADUATE STUDENT RESEARCH AWARD(S): Each year NEBC offers
     up to $2000 in support of botanical research conducted by graduate
     students. 1 March 2001 is the deadline for submission of proposals.
     Additional details may be found on the NEBC web site.
     <http://www.herbaria.harvard.edu/nebc/Graduate.html>
Please pass the word to graduate students who might be (or are) working
with plants that occur in the New England flora.
