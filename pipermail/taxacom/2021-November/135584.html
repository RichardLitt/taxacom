Hi All,
          I was reading about Dryococelus australis, the Lord Howe Island stick insect ("tree lobster") wiped out by rats by 1920.  But then a small population of them found in 2001 on Ball's Pyramid (20 km. offshore) and then a breeding program to save them.
          But plans for reintroduction on Lord Howe Island will presumably be delayed when some rats were recently found on the island despite a massive eradication project.   Below are two weblinks to articles this year about the rats, and a third weblink to a 2017 article about the stick insect once thought extinct.
                                         ---------------Ken Kinman
