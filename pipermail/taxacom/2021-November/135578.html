I believe the general recommendation is not so much to italicise, but to 
make sure the name 'stands out' from the surrounding text.

The Zoological Code [1] says
in Appendix B - General Recommendations:

"6. The scientific names of genus- or species-group taxa should be 
printed in a type-face (font) different from that used in the text"


That makes clear that for instance in a italicised paragraph, another 
style should be chosen (for instance, bold, or even regular).

So, if one wanted to avoid italicised text altogether for readability 
that seems still within that ZR recommendation -- just choose 'a 
type-face (font) different from that used in the text'.

[1] https://www.iczn.org/the-code/the-code-online/
