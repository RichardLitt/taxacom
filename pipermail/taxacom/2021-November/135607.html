 Francisco,I have to disagree in that "doubtfully included" has nothing to do with ambiguous. A species id doubtfully included in a new genus if the author indicates that there is doubt about its inclusion in the new genus. Obviously, it cannot therefore be considered the type species. In the present case, the author was certain (not doubtful) that the species was included in the new genus, there is just some ambiguity about which species exactly the author was certain about!Cheers, Stephen
