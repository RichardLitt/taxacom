Pictures show *Tambana catocalina* Moore, 1882.

The text references *Trisuloides catocalina* Moore, 1883,
but gives *Trisuloides polyphaenaria* Warren, 1912 as its synonym,
which is a synonym of *Tambana catocalina* Moore, 1882 instead.

Therefore, "1883, Proc. zool. Soc. Lond. 1883: 17" is probably a *lapsus 
calami*.

If this is so, then the type species of *Antitrisuloides* is
*Tambana catocalina* Moore, 1882 and
NOT *Trisuloides catocalina* Moore, 1883

An alternative interpretation could be "misidentified type species",
check out Art. 70.3.
https://code.iczn.org/types-in-the-genus-group/article-70-identification-of-the-type-species/
