Dear Rod,

Congratulations with your new service which seems rather promising,
especially if it manages to cover a large number of publishers of taxonomic
journals!

Regarding Pensoft and some points raised by others in this tread, I think
that you can use also OAI-PMH, *besides RSS*, to harvest all content from
one-stop web service end point (see ours at:
https://arphahub.com/about/OAI-PMH). Through OAI-PMH you can also select
Pensoft's ca. 20+ journals that publish taxonomic records (not just ZooKeys
or PhytoKes). The high-level taxa associated to each article can be taken
from the *<subj-group> *in each article XML. RSS are fine and great,
however they cover only the last 100 newly published items and not always
give straightforward  access to the full-text article XML. In other words,
you have to rely mostly on metadata in this case.

In addition, Pensoft provides human-readable email alerts (daily, weekly or
monthly*, *on choice of the user) from user-selected journals, taxa or
subject categories. When selecting a taxon or taxa from *"Taxon categories**"
*tab in user's personal profile alert setup, the system will inform you
about all new articles in all selected journals, published in relation to
that taxon or taxa.

I hope this helps.

Regards,
