Scott - I share that sentiment. Although I probably feel less
excitement about nomenclatural issues, that does not detract from its
attraction for others. I have found taxonomy is a window through which one
enters many other worlds - of evolution, biogeography, systematics, ecology
etc. Ironically I was deterred from taxonomy after taking a post grad
course that was DULL, DULL, DULL. If anyone could have made the subject
less attractive I would be amazed. And to add to the irony, taxonomy  was
always in my bones from the point I started collecting natural history
items, bugs mostly.  As one prominent taxonomist informed me, with a PhD in
ecology, no one (including him) is going to support your entry into
taxonomy. Fortunately, it was in the US that I did find that support, and
from taxonomists who owed me nothing.

John Grehan
