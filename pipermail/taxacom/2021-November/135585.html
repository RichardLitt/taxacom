Good to hear about this successful effort, especially for the future
enhancement of Dryococelus numbers. This stick insect has a molecular age
(22-50 Ma) which is older than the current islands (7 Ma). Of course there
are many now-submerged seamounts in the vicinity and LH is part of a
sea-mount chain where the oldest rocks are about 30Ma.
