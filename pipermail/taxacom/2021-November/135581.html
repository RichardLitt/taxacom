Back in the days when manuscripts were drafted on a typewriter (no italics option), we used to underline scientific names.  My understanding was that the tradition of highlighting scientific names in some way (underlining, italics) has its origins in the practice of representing words from languages different from the primary language of the text.  Scientific names, being Latin, are almost always (especially in modern times) words in a language different from the surrounding text.  So, perhaps if these standardized styles already accommodate offsetting alternate language words from the main part of the text, the same style should be applied to scientific names.

In any case, the failure to italicize is the lesser offense.  The much greater offense is the capitalization of the first letter of the species epithet, which I continue see with disarming regularity in even respectable news venues.
