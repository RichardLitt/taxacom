> Hi John,
>
> Yes, this is being updated regularly as I mess about with the code, and
> explore adding other sources (such as Chinese language journals that you
> expressed a desire to see).
>
> There isn’t a set time of day I add new content, but I hope to do this on
> a daily basis (yet another thing to automate). I will make the journal list
> available shortly (need to write some code to display it).
>
> The original plan for this app was to use RSS feeds provided by journals
> to get the latest list of articles each journal publishes. While some
> publishers do support RSS many journals don’t, so I have to come up with
> other ways to get this information. RSS was very popular in the heyday of
> blogging and news feed readers but with the demise of Google Reader
> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Reader and related apps RSS has been
> fading away a bit, which is a pity.
>
> Regarding bioinformatics and “big data”, I have my own issues with various
> projects (which I blogged about), but I think in this case the issue is
> that (a) it’s actually quite challenging to build a tool to poll the
> literature for new species - as I’m rapidly discovering - and (b) what are
> the incentives for providing such a service? I tend to think that people
> act rationally with respect to incentives, and so if things that you want
> to see happen aren’t happening then that’s a close that the  incentives
> aren’t there.
>
