An excellent opportunity for the more technically inclined...

ADVERSTISEMENT

Bioinformatics Distributed Information Systems Programmer

The University of Kansas Natural History Museum and Biodiversity
Research Center seeks a programmer with advanced skills to assist with
the development of infrastructure and interfaces for a global,
distributed information system for biodiversity related resources, and
to assist with the development of analytical and visualization tools to
work with biodiversity information. Required qualifications: degree in
computer science or 3 years relevant programming experience using C++,
VB, Java, C#, PHP, Perl; excellent communications skills; ability to
program database connectivity and cross-browser web applications;
experience programming for NT, Win2000 servers; working knowledge of
COM, .NET. This is a full-time, non-tenure track, grant-funded position
with the university and comes with excellent benefits. Some travel
required. For complete description, or to apply submit a cover letter,
r?sum?, and the names and contact information for three references to
Dr. Dave Vieglais, NHMBRC, 1345 Jayhawk Blvd., Lawrence, KS 66045,
vieglais at ku.edu. Review of applications begins 15 April 2002. KU is an
EO/AA employer.

(Note: I will be out of office for most of March, please email me if you
have enquiries about his position)
