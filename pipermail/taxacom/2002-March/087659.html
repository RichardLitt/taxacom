Berlin, Humbolt University, has one of the finest collection of flies (Diptera, Insecta) in the World. They are again looking for a curator.

See below
==================== 
The Diptera curator position in Berlin is (once again) opened for
applications. Dead line is April 4th. As you know this is one of the
most important Diptera collections of the world, full of types and
historic material. It is worth to be cared for by one of the finest
Dipterists of the world!! Therefore I think that this information should
reach as many Dipterists as possible. I wondered if you have a world
Dipterist mailing list available, which could be used for that purpose.
