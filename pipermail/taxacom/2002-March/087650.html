2002 
Postdoctoral Fellow in Molecular Systematics of Fishes

   Applications are solicited for a two year, NSF-funded postdoctoral
position. The project concerns the phylogeny and evolution of basal gobioid
fishes; job responsibilities include extraction, amplification, sequencing,
and analysis of sequence data.  The successful candidate will have a PhD.
in organismal biology and evolution; emphasis in Ichthyology and experience
in generating DNA sequence data are strongly preferred.  Applications are
accepted until April 15, 2002 for a start date of July 1, 2002. The salary
will be $33,000 annually, with medical and dental benefits included.  To
apply, send curriculum vitae, reprints, three letters of recommendation
(e-mail is acceptable: thacker at nhm.org), and a statement of research
experience and interests to Dr. Christine Thacker, Natural History Museum
of Los Angeles County, 900 Exposition Blvd., Los Angeles CA 90007.
