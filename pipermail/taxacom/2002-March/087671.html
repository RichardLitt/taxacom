Dear TAXACOMers,

I have a copy of:

C..A. Gardner. 1923. Botanical notes - Kimberley Division of Western Australia - Kimberley Exploration Expedition, 1921. Forest Department Bulletin No. 32. Perth, Government Printer. 103 pp, ill. & map.

The Kimberley Exploration Expedition, under the command of W. R. Easton, took place April to October 1921. The Botanical notes cover mainly the woody plants of the region. 
Anyone working on the flora of that region would be welcome to my copy and can contact me directly.
