This is probably Phelsuma dubia (Dull-green Day Gecko) which is apparently
common in Dar es Salaam. See the latest guide by Spawls, Howell, Drewes and
Ashe (2002) "A field guide to the reptiles of East Africa' published by
Academic Press.
