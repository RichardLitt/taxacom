     I don't know the exact date, but it was late 1964 (maybe October or
November?).  You might contact the U.S. Forest Service for an exact date,
since it was some "numskull" working for them who gave Currey permission to
cut down Prometheus.
     Or you might try Currey's 1965 paper published in the journal Ecology
(about the bristlecones in that stand in Nevada).  I can't imagine that he
would have mentioned cutting down the tree (especially in the journal called
"Ecology"), but you never know.  I don't how long it took for him to realize
he had killed the world's oldest tree (or when the story finally hit the
newspapers).
