'Invertebrata' is an informal newsletter produced by the Queen Victoria
Museum and Art Gallery (QVMAG) in Launceston, Tasmania. It carries articles
and items of interest about the biology and conservation of Tasmanian
invertebrates.

 From 1994 to 2001, 'Invertebrata' was photocopied and mailed to about 200
subscribers in Australia and overseas. Beginning in 2002, 'Invertebrata' is
electronic-only at

www.qvmag.tas.gov.au/zoology/Invertebrata/Invertebrata.html

The 'Invertebrata' website also has tables of contents for all
print-version issues, plus the last 12 print-version issues (1998 through
2001) as pdf downloads. Over time, all individual items from the print
version will be added to the website for online reading.

QVMAG apologises to subscribers who were given the above URL recently and
were unable to access the website. The problem was traced to a fault in the
State-wide networking infrastructure.
