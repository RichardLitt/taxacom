Symposium on Plant Life of South Asia


International symposium on biodiversity of Flowering Plants of South Asia,
is being organized at the department of Botany, University of Karachi,
November 1-5, 2002. The program shall consist of plenary lectures, paper
presentation, poster session on the following topics:

1.      Classical and experimental taxonomy in its broadest sense
2.      Phytogeography
3.      Reproductive biology
4.      Ethnobotany
5.      Wild relatives of cultivated plants
6.      Conservation biology
7.      Molecular systematics
