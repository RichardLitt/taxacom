There have been lot of sad days (or weeks) in the history of botany.

The large number of endemic vascular plant species that formerly existed on Centinela ridge in Ecuador comes to
mind.  The figure of about 90 endemic species along that ridge has been reported (see "The Diversity of Life"
by E.O. Wilson, pp. 243-244).  Evidently, the majority of these became extinct when the bulldozers came in to
clear for agriculture.
