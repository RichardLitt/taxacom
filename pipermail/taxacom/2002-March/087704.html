2002 
"The Neotropical Botany Pages: A Cooperative Information Center for  Digital
Documentation of Neotropical Plant Diversity"

Dear Taxacommers:
  We announce the grand opening of a new website-clearinghouse of botanical
information at <www.botanypages.org>.
   This site has numerous links from the front page to sections on
Myristicaceae, Lecythidaceae, Bat-Plant Interactions, and the botany of a
new conservation concession in Madre de Dios, Peru, known as Los Amigos.
The latter has an image gallery of ~1200 images, mostly of plants,
searchable by family.  We hope plant specialists in various groups will send
us their determinations of unidentified species!
  Some of these sites are undergoing various additions and stages of
improvement.  We welcome your comments.
Amanda Neill <aneill at nybg.org>
