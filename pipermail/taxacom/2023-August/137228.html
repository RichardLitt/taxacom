The ICNafp has Art. 38:
https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.iapt-taxon.org%2Fnomen%2Fpages%2Fmain%2Fart_38.html&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C8bcda80d97b04b58516008dba86cfea2%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638288958455381541%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=%2Bn5PpCRfXi5TDK8fsg9%2FJgd1vmttnuGlgMVi0P3uDZ4%3D&reserved=0

Note that Art. 38.4 provides the opportunity to ask for
a ruling on whether or not a particular description or
diagnosis meets the requirements for valid publication.
And for pre-1908 names, Art. 38.7-8 deal with illustrations
instead of a description or diagnosis.
