BIOfid is a long-term project sponsored by the German Research  
Foundation (DFG), and is a collaboration between the Senckenberg  
Society for Nature Research (SGN), the University Library Johann  
Christian Senckenberg, and the working group Computational  
Humanities/Text Technology at Goethe University, Frankfurt. BIOfid  
enables semantically enhanced access to current and historical  
biodiversity literature.

3 positions are available (work locations Frankfurt and/or M?ncheberg  
near Berlin):
Ref.#11-23012 Research Associate Taxonomist and Morphologist (m/f/d)
Ref.#11-23013 Research Associate Ecologist (m/f/d)
Ref.#11-23014 Scientific Programmer (m/f/d)
