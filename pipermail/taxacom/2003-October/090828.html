2003 
Taxonomic Databases Working Group Annual Meeting 21-26 October 2003

To help us organise a successful meeting it is vital that delegates
register as early as possible. Please register if possible by the end of
Sunday 19 October (Portugal time) - before would be helpful. Registrations
after this date will attract a Late Registration Surcharge of 20 Euro
(twenty Euro). Registration will continue to be available until 24 October,
on-line or in person at the meeting venue.

Register on-line at https://www.regonline.com/?10342

or visit the TDWG meeting site at http://www.tdwg.org/2003meet/TDWG2003.htm

Thank you for your cooperation.

Regards,
