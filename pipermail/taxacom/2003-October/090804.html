Carlos Sarmiento wrote:
>I would like to have some advice on the availability
>of an electronic system to transfer locality data from
>a database onto distribution maps.

Assuming you have coordinates, use Online Map Creation:
http://www.aquarius.geomar.de/omc/

Very easy.  Upload set of locality coordinates, choose
projection and coordinates of map boundaries, add rivers
etc. or not, and save as EPS.  Results are excellent.
