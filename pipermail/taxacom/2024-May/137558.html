Briefly, Zander's pluralistic systematics is based on the incompleteness of each of the theories: A method that cannot falsify a hypothesis is as unscientific as a hypothesis that cannot be falsified. Cladistics generates only trees of shared ancestry, not serial ancestry. Taxa evolving seriatim cannot be dealt with by analyzing shared ancestry with cladistic methods. Hypotheses such as adaptive radiation from a single ancestral taxon cannot be falsified with cladistics. Cladistics offers a way to cluster by trait transformations but no evolutionary tree can be entirely dichotomous. Phylogenetics posits shared ancestral taxa as causal agents for dichotomies yet there is no evidence for the existence of such taxa. Molecular systematics uses DNA sequence data for tracking evolutionary changes, thus paraphyly and sometimes phylogenetic polyphyly<https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FPolyphyly&data%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C59291590d4ac4d157ee408dc6bc00538%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638503721731474453%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C40000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=wB5SpkEWgs1G3aNxsFr8IFi3d93bLmmxqiqMVfIcgGM%3D&reserved=0

I recommend to non-cladist taxonomists my recently published "Minimally monophyletic genera are the cast-iron building blocks of evolution" in the Ukrainian Botanical Journal 81: 87-99. 2024. It is available at  https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fukrbotj.co.ua%2Farchive%2F81%2F2%2F87&data%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C59291590d4ac4d157ee408dc6bc00538%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638503721731474453%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C40000%7C%7C%7C&sdataÆnY1xGPvR%2BCOViRXJxYpuYWmxjb3aou%2BAqkDu3AFes%3D&reserved=0  also ResearchGate and Academia.edu. The paper promotes the genus as basic unit of evolution, standard morphology as used in taxonomy as key to understanding trait evolution, distinguishes process-based traits into novon and ancestron, and demonstrates that Bayesian support is high and firm because it involves conjugate priors.
