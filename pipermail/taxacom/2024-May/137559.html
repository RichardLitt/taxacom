Dear all,

My colleague Jean-S?bastien Girard and myself are busy entering around 7000
"new" zoological genus names published over the period 2015-2022 approx (in
addition to the 4000 or so already held) to the master IRMNG (Interim
Register of Marine and Nonmarine Genera) database for the benefit of
downstream users; generally we can access the original publications
concerned by some means or other to check authorships, spelling, family
placement, etc. but for 2 newly entered names we can access the names via
the abstract of the paper via online copy / open access but are unable to
read the full text (without paying for the privilege), So if anyone has
access to the following work and can tell us the proposed family placement
of these particular genera, I/we would be very grateful!

The work is:
Garvie, C.L. (2021). The macrofauna of the Tehuacana Limestone Member
(Danian, Kincaid Formation) of Central Texas, with the description of a few
new taxa from the Pisgah Member. *Bulletins of American Paleontology.* 399-400:
1-292. DOI: 10.32857/bap.2021.399.01

and the names are
Parmesalia Garvie, 2021 (p. 62), and
Paleofusus Garvie, 2021 (p. 123)

There are a number of other new names proposed in this work but I think we
have all these covered (fingers crossed).

Any assistance gratefully appreciated, and will be used to upgrade the
existing IRMNG records for these 2 genera.

Tony Rees, New South Wales, Australia
