FYI:

 

The Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT) is preparing to move
from the current interim space into our new, permanent, LEED-certified
green building, adjacent to the Fort Worth Botanic Garden.  This will
disrupt normal herbarium business for 12 months.

Please hold all shipments and loan requests for the BRIT-SMU-VDB
Collections, and contact us before scheduling any visits between May
2010 and May 2011 (scheduled grand re-opening).

We look forward to being able to better serve the botanical community
with much-improved space and equipment.  For more information about our
new facility, please visit http://www.brit.org/brit-future/
<http://www.brit.org/brit-future/> .


Thank you for your patience during this time!
