Barry Roth wrote:

>Page priority: not so much an urban legend, I think, as a relict 
>from an earlier edition of the Code.

I have the 1961 Code, and it is demonstrably NOT in that version; it 
is only a minor clause within a recommendation (Rec. 24A in the 1961 
Edition), and I'm not aware of it ever having been otherwise. The 
principle of the First Reviser, has, at least since 1961, been that 
the reviser is free to choose, and page priority is therefore 
immaterial, though a reviser is free to use that criterion for 
situations where all other things are equal. Given that the 1961 
version is the first published Code version, I'd be curious to know 
which older edition you had in mind. ;-)
