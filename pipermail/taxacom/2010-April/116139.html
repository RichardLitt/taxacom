The Missouri Botanical Garden (MBG) announces the  NEW *Shirley Graham Fellowship*. The Fellowships are open to professionals in the field of botany/plant sciences from universities,governmental or non-governmental institutions, and organizations in Mexico, Central America, South America, Madagascar, Vietnam, and Tanzania.Please find the information in the links below. For questions please contact the fellowship coordinator.
http://www.mobot.org/plantscience/ccsd/opportunities.shtml

http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/Research/jobs.shtml

The Missouri Botanical Garden also offers two other fellowship opportunities, all deadline 31 July 2010.

** The Bascom fellowship for Latin American Women; and

** The Missouri Botanical Garden fellowships for botanists from Ecuador, Peru and Bolivia.

More information and applications guidelines.

http://www.mobot.org/plantscience/ccsd/opportunities.shtml
