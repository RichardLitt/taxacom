Training course for Scratchpads (basic level).
A one-day training course will be given in the Royal Belgian Institute  
of Natural Sciences, Brussels on Monday 14th June.  The course is  
intended for those new to Scratchpads or those thinking about giving  
them a try.  No previous knowledge is assumed, but a familiarity with  
databases and data structures is an advantage.  The course is free,  
starting at 11:00, finishing at about 17:00 and lunch will be  
provided. You are welcome to bring your own data to upload into your  
own, new Scratchpad or come for so help if you're just starting out  
with a new site.  You should bring your own wireless-enabled laptop  
with the Firefox browser.

To register please go to:
http://scratchpads.eu/scratchpad-training-courses
