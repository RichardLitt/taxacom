2010 
The Peabody Museum of Natural History at Yale University is pleased to announce the publication of the April 2010 issue of its Bulletin of the Peabody Museum of Natural History, Volume 51, Issue 1, with the following articles:

The Fossil Flora of the Winthrop Formation (Albian?Early Cretaceous) of Washington State, USA. Part II: Pinophytina
by Ian M. Miller and Leo J. Hickey
 
Avifauna of the Sipaliwini Savanna (Suriname) with Insights into Its Biogeographic Affinities
by John C. Mittermeier, Kristof Zyskowski, Edward S. Stowe and Jonas E. Lai
 
A New Stomatolepas Barnacle Species (Cirripedia: Balanomorpha: Coronuloidea) from Leatherback Sea Turtles
by Michael G. Frick, John D. Zardus and Eric A. Lazo-Wasem
 
A New Species of the Roughy Genus Hoplostethus (Teleostei: Trachichthyidae) from the Philippines
by Jon A. Moore and Kathryn A. Dodd

The Bulletin of the Peabody Museum of Natural History is indexed in both SCIE (Science Citation Index Expanded) and JCR (Journal Citation Reports), beginning with Volume 50.

Abstracts and full text of papers published in the Yale Peabody Museum's Bulletin beginning with Volume 47 are available online to institutional subscribers of BioOne (http://www.bioone.org/), an electronic database of high-impact bioscience research journals.

Back issues of Bulletin monographs (Volumes 1 through 46) and Postilla short papers (Volumes 1 through 232) are available as free downloadable and searchable PDFs on the Yale Peabody Museum website athttp://www.peabody.yale.edu/scipubs/scipubs_bulletin.html 

Manuscripts based on the Peabody collections are welcome. Print copies of most Bulletin and Postilla issues are also available for purchase. Visit http://www.peabody.yale.edu/scipubs/index.html or contact peabody.publications at yale.edu for more information.
