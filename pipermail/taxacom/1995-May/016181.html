1995 
  Longwood Gardens, Inc., the world's premier horticultural display garden, has
an outstanding employment opportunity for a horticultural taxonomist.
Responsibilities include identifying plants, especially cultivars; continued
development of computerized plant records programs; and teaching botany and
plant taxonomy. Duties include processing data for plant accessions, plant
record changes, maps and labels.
  Applicants should have a Ph.D. in Botany or Plant Sciences with experience
and interest in systematics of cultivated plants, be proficient with database
software (CAD helpful), and should be able to interact with a diverse staff on
plant identification and plant records related-issues.
  We offer an excellent starting salary and an outstanding benefits package.
Please mail your resume with salary requirements to: Mr. Kiran Taunk, Business
Division Manager, Longwood Gardens, Inc., P.O. Box 501, Kennett Square, PA
19348  An equal opportunity employer.
