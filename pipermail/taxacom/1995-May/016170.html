Please be aware that Parker left out at least three arthropodian groups :
the Protura, Diplura and Collembola.

You might also want to have a look at the project "The Tree of Life :
A Phylogenetic Navigator for the Internet" of Maddison & Maddison at
the WWW URL "http://phylogeny.arizona.edu/tree/phylogeny.html".
