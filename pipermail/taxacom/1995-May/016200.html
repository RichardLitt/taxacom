1995 
          INTERNSHIP

          in

          BIOLOGICAL ILLUSTRATION

          at the

          CALIFORNIA ACADEMY OF SCIENCES
          Golden Gate Park, San Francisco


          The California Academy of Sciences announces an internship
          in biological illustration.  The intership is open to an
          undergraduate student interested in developing illustration
          techniques related to biological specimens.  The intern will
          work with one or more Academy scientists to develop
          illustrations that may be used in scientific publications.
          The Academy is a comprehensive natural history museum
          supporting research and collection activities in the
          disciplines of Anthropology, Botany, Geology, Invertebrate
          Zoology, Entomology, Diatom Research, Ichthyology,
          Herpetology, Ornithology and Mammalogy.

          Internship Period:  Full time 10 weeks during the Summer or
          part time during the Fall semester.

          Stipend:  $1,200 for the Internship Period.

          To Apply:  Send a letter of interest, resume, sample of
          previous work and a letter of recommednation from a faculty
          member to:

               Biological Illustration Internship
               c/o Director of Research
               California Academy of Sciences
               Golden Gate Park
               San Francisco, CA 94118

          Materials must be received at the Academy by June 30, 1995.

          Questions can be addressed to the Director of Research at
          (415) 750-7351 or via Email  pdalporto at cas.calacademy.org

          This internship is made possible in part by a gift from
          Maryjo Koch, Author and Illustrator.
