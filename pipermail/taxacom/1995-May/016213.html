1995 
Taxacom subscribers interested in more information on a proposal
for an African Herpetofaunal Biodiversity Programme should email
Raw at zoology.unp.ac.za. The proposed programme envisages projects
in taxonomy, biogeography, conservation and other related fields
and is intended as a multi-party collaborative venture. The
region of interest is Africa as a whole, as well as its islands,
including Madagascar.
