          PUBLIC LECTURE .....  RESERVATIONS ARE NECESSARY
          ______________________________________________________


          MARY P. WINSOR,
          University of Toronto

          will present

          "LOUIS AGASSIZ'S IDEAL MUSEUM: in Pursuit of Virtual Nature"

          at the

          California Academy of Sciences
          Golden Gate Park, San Francisco

          on

          Friday, May 19, 1995 at 6:00 P.M.
          _______________________________________________________


          Professor Mary P. Winsor is from the Institute for the
          History and Philosophy of Science and Technology at the
          University of Toronto.  She is a leading authority on the
          history of biology and its institutions.  Her latest book,
          "Reading the Shape of Nature: Comparative Zoology at the
          Agassiz Museum" (University of Chicago Press, 1991), will be
          the basis for this lecture.  It will address the nature and
          importance of museums in general, and should interest anyone
          who wants to understand them better.

          THIS LECTURE IS FREE OF CHARGE.  However, due to limited
          seating, RESERVATIONS ARE NECESSARY.

          Reservations can be made by contacting Patricia Dal Porto:
          by Email  pdalporto at cas.calacademy.org
          by phone  (415) 750-7351
          or by fax (415) 750-7346
