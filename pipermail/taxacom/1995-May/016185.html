1995 
The Museum of Vertebrate Zoology at UC Berkeley is pleased to announce its
WWW server is now online at the following URL:

http://www.mip.berkeley.edu/mvz/

Summary descriptions of all collections and a policy statement for those
wishing to request loans of frozen or ethanol-preserved tissues are
available. Information about the MVZ Information System Re-engineering
Project is also provided.  We welcome any comments or requests for information.
