1995 
A new and exciting discussion group now exists (micro-sat) to exchange
information on microsatellite marker investigations for arthropod species.
The discussions will focus on (1) laboratory protocols (2) data analysis
(kinship, genetic variation, population structure, etc) and (3) exchange of
primer sequences. All individuals that would find this group of interest
are welcome to join. Please send all inquiries to Thomas_Chapman at SFU.ca

Thank you,

Tom Chapman
Behavioural Ecology Research Group
