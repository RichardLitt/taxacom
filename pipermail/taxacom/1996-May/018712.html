Looking for Experts on Nudibranchia, Marine Turbellaria & Actiniaria
of Sri Lanka/Indo-West Pacific

A colleage in Sri Lanka is interested in contacting experts in these
marine invertebrate groups for collaboration over newly collected
specimens and historical material (including previously unpublished
drawings) relating to taxa described from Sri Lanka (Ceylon) by
E. F. Kelaart in the 1850s. Please get in touch with me and I will
pass on the information to him.

Priyantha Wijesinghe
