Coleus repens a.k.a. borraja de indias, oregano, and Coleus amboinicus
with same names could be your plant. Oregano cubano, oregano hondureno,
and many other localized oreganos are often one of these two plants. They
are used as a fresh herbal condiment and also as medicinal in
tisanes.Both are succulent, have a strong turpentinish smell.
