The best way to publish the book is together with CD-ROM or on CD-ROM only. If
you produce the CD-ROM only, the new description and synonyms has to be printed
in small booklet which can be included as a cover for CD.  To produce one disk
is around 3 US$. Use GIF or JPG format. It does not help if you zip them or not,
they will have the same size.
