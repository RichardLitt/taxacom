                        ANNOUNCEMENT OF COLLECTION TRANSFER

The Texas Cooperative Wildlife Collection at Texas A&M University has
acquired the Collection of Reptiles and Amphibians from  Midwestern
State University in Wichita Falls, Texas.  The TCWC is currently
inventorying the 5000+ specimens, and they will be available for loans
and information requests in the near future.

Please address all loan and information requests for these MWSU
specimens to the TCWC:

R. Kathryn Vaughan, Ph.D.
Asst. Curator, Herpetology
Texas Cooperative Wildlife Collection
Department of Wildlife and Fisheries Sciences
Texas A&M University
College Station, TX  77843-2258   USA
