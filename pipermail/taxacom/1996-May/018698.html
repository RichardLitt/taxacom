Colleagues:

The University of California Museum of Paleontology (UCMP) announces the
electronic publication of the UCMP Data Model for paleontological collection
management.  It's being published in advance of the upcoming "Workshop on
Management Guidelines and Computerization Standards for Invertebrate
Paleontology" which will be held in Washington, DC on June 6-15, 1996, and
it is hoped that it will serve as a starting point for discussion. The data
model is located at:

http://www.ucmp.Berkeley.Edu/museum/datamodel/datamodel.html


All comments and suggestions are welcome.

David R. Lindberg
Asst. Director, UCMP
