CDEFD Information Model for Biological Collections

Partial results of the Concerted Action project
"A Common Datastructure for European Floristic Databases" (CDEFD)
funded by the European Commission, DG XII, Biotechnology Program.

The model is now available on the Web at:

http://userpage.fu-berlin.de/~wgb/CDEFD/cdefd.html

This is an unreviewed pre-print of a contribution to be published in the
Proceedings of the European Science Foundation Workshop
"Disseminating Biodiversity Information",  Amsterdam, 25. - 27. 3. 1996.
