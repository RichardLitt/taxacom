>Given all the botanists who read TAXACOM, etc., do any of you know of good

I don't think this one has been mentioned:
http://cawley.archives.nd.edu/cgi-bin/lookdown.pl

As someone mentioned, all these things have to be used intelligently, with
some knowledge of the language.  A few days spent perusing the discussions
of grammar in Wm. Stearn's Botanical Latin would be time well spent.
