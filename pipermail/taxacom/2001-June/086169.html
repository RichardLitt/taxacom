Dear colleagues

A preliminary program for the joint Evolution and Systematics Conference,
Melbourne University, 16-18 July 2001, is now on line at:
http://www.zoology.unimelb.edu.au/aes/sasb_aes_program.html

I think you will agree that the program looks very interesting, and a
rewarding 3 days is assured.  It is not too late for us to accept late
registrations.  However, accommodation at Ormond College must be finalised
in the next few days, before Friday 22 June (but preferably earlier -
immediately).  After that time, no further rooms can be promised at Ormond
College, and late registrants will have to make their own accommodation
arrangements.

We hope to see as many of you as possible in Melbourne

(Apologies for those of you who have already registered, and those on the
SASB list, who will have
received some of this information separately)

bye, Robin (on behalf of the Organising Committee)
