Hi all, I have placed some of my stereo images transferred to red-green system of Upper Cambrian (490 Million years old) Orsten fossilson my website. You can look at them using normal stereo glasses with red left and green right. Such things may even be available in toy shops. Would be nice to read some comments before setting up more of them.
