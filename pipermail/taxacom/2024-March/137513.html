Vazrick,

Although unorthodox, Kemal & Ko?ak (2005) described a species that did 
not have an available name in a Code compliant manner. It would be 
easier to introduce a new replacement name, but in that case they could 
not designate a holotype and paratypes, as the syntypes of maxima 
Staudinger, 1901 would automatically become syntypes of the replacement 
name.

I have not seen the Errata of Weidenhoffer & Bozano (Nov. 2007), but 
they were incorrect in stating that uighurica Kemal & Ko?ak, 2005 was a 
replacement name for maxima Staudinger, 1901. The name was described as 
a new species and ottostaudingeri Weidenhoffer & Bozano, 2007 is a 
junior synonym.

Adam.

> Good day everyone,Here is a nomenclatural question for the Code geeks.
> 1. in 1901 Staudinger described ?Cigaritis Cilissa var. Maxima? from Turkey: ?Kurd. oc. (Malatya); Mesop. s. (Mardin)?. Syntypes, including 5 males and 3 females, are kept at the Zoologisches Museum der Humboldt Universitaet.
> 2. The name maxima is preoccupied by Aphnaeus vulcanus var. maximus Elwes, 1893.
> 3. Recognizing this homonymy,?Kemal & Kocak (2005) described a new species (not a replacement name), ?Cigaritis uighurica (sp. n.)? (Type Locality: Malatya, Turkey), declared ?Synonyms: maxima Staudinger,1901 nec Elw.,1892?, provided a brief?description, designated a holotype now deposited in CESA, and listed many paratypes, none of which were selected from among the extant syntypes of maxima Staudinger. > 4. Weidenhoffer & Bozano (Oct. 2007) proposed the name ?ottostaudingeri (nom. nov.)? as the replacement name for maxima Staudinger, treating uighurica Kemal & Kocak as nomen nudum and a junior objective synonym of ottostaudingeri Weidenhoffer & Bozano 2007.
> 5. In an Errata corrige released soon after the latter publication, Weidenhoffer & Bozano (Nov. 2007) clarified that in fact uighurica cannot be considered a nomen nudum as the taxonomic act by Kemal & Kocak, however unsatisfactory, seemed to comply with relevant provisions of the ICZN and thus it should be recognized as the valid replacement name for maxima.
