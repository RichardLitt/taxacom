Good news: it was not because you were tired, Adam.
The name would also have been available, had it been a junior primary 
homonym.

The term "available" refers to a status a name gets when it is 
established in a Code compliant way. And then it keeps this status for a 
pretty long time, no matter what happens out there in the world. Only 
the Commission can interfere with a ruling to withdraw the availability 
of a name.

"Art. 10.6. Effect of invalidity upon availability. A name once 
available remains so irrespective of its invalidity as a junior synonym, 
a junior homonym, an unjustified emendation, an unnecessary substitute 
name, or a suppressed name, unless the Commission has ruled otherwise 
[Arts. 78.1, 78.2]. (Even if the taxon concerned is no longer classified 
as animal its name remains available [Art. 2.2])."
