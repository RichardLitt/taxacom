Several systematics journals use "Editorial Manager" to interface with
authors. MS Internet Explorer users from the United States commonly have
problems working the menus, which jam or disappear. Sometimes changing
the View tab to a different encoding forces a menu up, but this is
haphazard.

 

I downloaded a free copy of Mozilla Firefox browser, and it worked
perfectly with Editorial Manager. I encourage all authors who have
problems with Editorial Manager to use Firefox instead of Internet
Explorer.
