Note also that we have two other 'community' databases from the TDWG home
page

1. A database of Biodiversity Informatics relate NETWORKS
(http://www.tdwg.org/biodiv-networks/). We have 53 so far noted.

2. A database of Biodiversity Informatics related EVENTS
(http://www.tdwg.org/events/)

Anyone who is registered on the TDWG site can add or edit any of the records
in any of the databases.
