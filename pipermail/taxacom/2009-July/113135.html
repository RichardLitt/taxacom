The TRGP (Taxonomic Repertory of the Genus Peperomia)
(http://www.peperomia.net/repertory.asp) 

provides online access to a scanned image of the types of most of the 1617
accepted names (+ 1400 synonyms) of this genus. I'm trying to include the
acronyms of the herbaria where isotypes are located. It is done already for
the herbaria mentioned below. If your herbarium is not yet listed and you
have Peperomia types in your holdings, I would be grateful if you would
inform me about them. They will be included in the TRGP as soon as I get
your update.
