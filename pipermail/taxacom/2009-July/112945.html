Tony and others:

The new site looks very interesting and is a useful summary of key resources. 

You may also be interested in the open access BiologyBrowser site we have been running for a few years now (www.biologybrowser.org) which evaluates, categorises and indexes web based resources in biodiversity (mainly animal biodiversity allied to Zoological Record subject coverage).

We currently have around 150,000 resource pages listed and categorise by organism group, subject, and geographical location.  We continue to add resources at around 60,000 per year. Any feedback to improve the site would be welcome as we are about to undertake a redevelopment.
