Dear colleagues,
 
I'm trying to find the distribution of the beetle Proterhinus (Proterhinidae). Nothing on Wikispecies, EOL, COL, GBIF, or the other websites referred to in recent taxacom correspondence, or Google scholar.  Zimmerman (1948) lists it from Hawaii (164 endemic species!), plus New Zealand, New Caledonia, Fiji, Samoa, Phoenix Is.,  Austral Is., Society Is., and Marquesas Is., also Canary Is. 
 
I'd be grateful for any further information.
 
Thanks,
Michael Heads

Wellington, New Zealand.
