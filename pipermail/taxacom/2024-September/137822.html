Thanks to everyone for the feedback! It's probably going overboard not to
just use eyebrow-shaped. I only questioned whether there was some technical
term as an editor in the past had suggested not using 'colloquial' terms
for shapes (e.g. birds beak-shaped). Ophrysiform and ophrydiform are
interesting ideas, although the latter appears to also be a fish group so
probably not so good to use. I received a suggestion for a supine comma,
but the supine does not work for the orientation in this case (a genitalic
structure). So I will mention all this to my senior co-author and let him
decide (and I anticipate that given the unfamiliarity of the alternative
terms he will stick with eyebrow. Regardless, much appreciate the input on
such obscure matters.

John
