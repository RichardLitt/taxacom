> I haven't looked into the Achladaeus case yet, but, in general, it is
> quite hard for an old generic name to remain a nomen nudum. All it needs to
> be made available is some sort of description, illustration, or inclusion
> of at least one available species name. A name may be a nomen nudum when
> originally published, but it can be validated at any subsequent time.
