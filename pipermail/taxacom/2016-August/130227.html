Hi John,

I just checked, and currently Catalogue of Life (2016) is getting data
for Hepialidae
from LepIndex (2012 version) which would be why newer names would not be
there. I am wondering if the names are included in "Butterflies and Moths
of the World" (http://www.nhm.ac.uk/our-science/data/butmoth/) at the same
institution (NHM in London) and whether there are plans for CoL to use that
as an improved source, as I have seen criticisms of LepIndex as the best
source these days; if they are there, then you do not need to enter them,
and if not, it might be worth contacting that database's compilers.

As an alternative, I like to host as many new names as I can find (with
realistic levels of effort) into my own system "IRMNG" (www.irmng.org), for
which e.g. you can see current holdings for Hepialidae here:
http://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=taxdetails&id=100937 . (Note, there may be
additional genera already held but not yet allocated to that family,
presently under "Lepidoptera - awaiting allocation"). My species names for
Lepidoptera will be somewhat out-of-date, being based on the version of
LepIndex as held in Cat. of Life 2006 (maybe much the same as today) but I
am happy to have additions from newer sources as well. IRMNG content is
also regularly exported to other systems for their use as desired including
those of GBIF, EOL (Encyclopedia of Life), Glonal Names/Global Names
Resolver, OTOL (Open Tree of Life), WoRMS (for marine taxa) and more. Take
a look and see what you think...
