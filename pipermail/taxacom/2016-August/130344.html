Genius of “and” - we need both originals AND the transcriptions if we are to do useful work. Images are hard to compute with, text is much easier. Scans of pages and labels gives you a nice, human-readable image gallery. Transcriptions (OCR) gives you GBIF and searchable BHL, amongst other nice things. #feedthemachines
