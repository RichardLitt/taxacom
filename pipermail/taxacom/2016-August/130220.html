Thanks for all the replies, on and off the list. Two recommendations that seemed particularly useful to me are:

Dictionary of Biology: Deutsch/Englisch - English/German Springer 2014

Encyclopaedic English-German and German-English dictionary by Muret, Eduard Published 1891

The first is very new and the second rather old but both had a good proportion of the words that I was looking for.
