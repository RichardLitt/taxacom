The World Spider Catalog is excellent for all of the things mentioned. One aspect that is less than ideal even in many curated lists of species is the inability to search with geographic names e.g. countries. Of course curated lists are put together by taxonomists for taxonomists and the focus is on taxa rather than areas but the most important end user IMHO is the local naturalist who wants to generate an authoritative list of species for the area he/she is interested in. There are some curated lists that go beyond type localities and incorporate distribution information for each species based on the literature covered but even in these cases searching by geographic terms is usually not possible (e.g. WSC). (Ironically, when the WSC existed as many html files it was possible to do a simple text search on each file!)

I realize this is only tangentially relevant to the current thread and I do appreciate the huge effort required to include searchable geographic distribution information for each species. But I do think that should be the ultimate goal for all curated lists of species.

> all, Cat. of Life contributors for which we should be very grateful in the
> main; speaking from minor past interactions the CoL is always interested in
