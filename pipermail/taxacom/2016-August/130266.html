In my experience, the "scientific names on this page" feature has high
levels of both false positives and false negatives.  However, my experience
is biased towards older works that are likely to be more challenging for
OCR, and may also have more names missed in the list used to identify
possible taxonomic usage.  The most reliable way to improve the results
would be to have a way for users to flag the problems.

Unfortunately, people do often rely on such indexing as data for
analysis.  Some molecular workers think that simply taking the oldest date
from the Paleobiology Database is good calibration for molecular clocks,
for example.  Putting everything online and linking it makes it quite easy
to do a big meta-analysis without paying any attention to data quality.  It
also facilitates better studies, of course, but checking the primary data
is a part of a reliable study.
