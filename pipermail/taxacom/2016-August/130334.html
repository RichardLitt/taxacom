That is true, but how common are self-published papers these days? Not very! Hoser and who else?? Most of what is published goes through peer review, which is typically lacking on a "closed" website. Now, peer review is, of course, far from perfect, but imperfection is arguably preferable to absence! The worry with "closed" websites is that the biased data will be harvested by aggregators like GBIF etc. and actually used for "decision-making" (whatever that really amounts to?)
