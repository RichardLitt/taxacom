Tony,

There is a revision of Aenetus (at least for Australia) that will be
published in the not too distant future, so this particular bone can be
laid to rest then. This situation is one where a nomenclatural change has
been made by someone for a database rather than in a publication and it
seems that for whatever reason this is hard to undo even though the
original change was invalid - as the Tree of Life link given by Stephen
shows, the Achladaeus name is a nomen nudum. Even the nhm site includes a
quote to that effect! see http://www.nhm.ac.uk/our-
science/data/butmoth/search/GenusDetails.dsml?NUMBER=174.0&& Anyway I am
sure this situation is no more challenging than for many other groups. At
least I am working on a group that not many are interested in, and I don't
have to contend with the kind of problems described in taxacom for
herpetologists. I should be grateful.

If species are transferred to another genus then yes, in my experience it
is pretty much standard practice for Lepidopterists to place author names
and dates in parentheses.

John Grehan
