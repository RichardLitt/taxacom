 Hi Mary,
 
 According to McKenna &
 Bell, "Classification of Mammals above the Species
 Level", the author of the genus Panthera is Oken, 1816.
 You can get the same information via IRMNG e.g. this
 search:
 
 http://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&searchpar=0&tComp=equals&tName=Panthera&action=search
 
 I agree, Catalogue of Life is
 deficient in nomenclatural information at levels above
 species (which qualifies my remark earlier about the
 usefulness of downloading e.g. World Spider Catalog data via
 that route if you are interested in information above
 species rank).

 A problem that I have with many of the
 databases that I have used is that they ignore (or do not
 reveal) the authors of names above the rank of species. I
 find that Wikipedia is often the most useful resource in
 this regard. For Panthera, even Wikipedia let me down.

 author of Panthera should be given as (Linnaeus 1758) but I
 wish CoL routinely provided such information.  I like CoL,
 but the absence of author citations for higher ranks, IMO,
 lowers its value.
