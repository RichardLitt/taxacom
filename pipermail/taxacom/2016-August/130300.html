Hi Mary,

According to McKenna & Bell, "Classification of Mammals above the Species
Level", the author of the genus Panthera is Oken, 1816. You can get the
same information via IRMNG e.g. this search:

http://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&searchpar=0&tComp=equals&tName=Panthera&action=search

I agree, Catalogue of Life is deficient in nomenclatural information at
levels above species (which qualifies my remark earlier about the
usefulness of downloading e.g. World Spider Catalog data via that route if
you are interested in information above species rank).
