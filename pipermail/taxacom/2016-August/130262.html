Hi Stephen,

again I actually agree with you. Maybe not as emphatically but you are
correct. I have not read the recent paper you cite, but stating that his
names are not up to scratch and hence will be ignored was not the best way
to do this. People have tried all sorts of ways, including stating that the
journal is not "simultaneous identical copies" because of various printer
errors, which is beyond the intentions of the Code.

I do not know what the actual opinions of the authors were, obviously they
did not want to use the Hoser names. They could of just left it at not
using them, as per Kaiser et al. 2013, possibly also cited the Rhodin et
al. 2014. Comment on Spracklandus. The Hoser 2013 paper is one of those
covered by the comment. In that we requested volumes 1-24 of AJH be
declared unavailable for nomenclature. Hence they are all in limbo
effectively. Until the ICZN decides.

Reality is the names will never be used. There is nothing I could do to
change that.

Cheers, Scott
