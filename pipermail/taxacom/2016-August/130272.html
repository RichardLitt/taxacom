Dear Mike

I know this is not what you asked for, but if you are collecting
information about Gerdt Hatschbach you can find more than 200,000 specimen
records in which Hatschbach is named as collector or identifier through a
free text search of GBIF occurrence records at
http://www.gbif.org/occurrence/search?q=Hatschbach. Further filters down
the right hand side enable you to sort according to collection, type
status, year etc. Hope this helps.
