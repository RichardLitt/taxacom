Answers do exist to the issue of privately or self-supported websites.

On longevity, albeit dependent on the survival of the hosting service, I,
with a modest grant from the Royal Entpmological Society, have just paid for
a ten-year contract, including the annual domain name charge. The total was

and archive one's web site. That should ensure future proofing and they have
a regular update procedure (one can also request an update be made).
