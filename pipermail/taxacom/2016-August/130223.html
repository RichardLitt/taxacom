ZooBank

(http://zoobank.org)

But I'm biased....

We will soon be submitting a grant proposal to link together GNI (which emerged from uBio), GNUB (which includes ZooBank), and Catalog of Life (among many others, ultimately including GBIF, WoRMS, etc.) such that updating any one of these has the effect of updating all of them.

Entering content into ZooBank (anyone can create a user account and start cranking away) is limited to original descriptions of names only (along with the associated literature citations), but the underlying database (GNUB) is open to ALL taxon name usages (e.g., various spellings, combinations, synonymy treatements, etc.), and I would be happy to work with you to get those entered as well.
