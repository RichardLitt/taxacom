Tony and Doug,
Looking in more depth at the Aellopos/Spathobatis problem, it could be a whole lot more complicated! It is messy, as paleontological taxonomy often is. There is actually little that I can find to support the synonymy of the two generic names, except for the widespread usage of the combination Aellopos bugesiacus (Thiollière) as valid! This presumably refers to Spathobatis bugesiacus Thiollière, 1849 (presumably the valid name), though to make matters worse it is usually attributed to Thiollière, 1854! Aellopos Münster, 1836 appears to have been based by monotypy on Aellopos elongata/elongatus Münster, 1836, whose current status is entirely unclear to me at this stage!
Cheers,
Stephen

 it should...) As a workaround for now, for such names I
 presently add a comment "Validity: Name as supplied in
 source(s) stated in "basis of record" without

 synonym or other)".
