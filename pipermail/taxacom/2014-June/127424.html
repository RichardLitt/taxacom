Well Raymond, it is hard to make any judgement without seeing Schleip's reasons for claiming L. hoserae to be non Code compliant. I guess we must wait until his paper is published. Looking at the original description of L. hoserae, I cannot see anything wrong. You designated a holotype, stated the holotype repository, and gave an albeit brief diagnosis. If the paper was published in hard copy in the usual way, then I cannot see any problem. I really do hope that Schleip doesn't try to pull that "not scientific (enough)" trick again, thereby concocting a bogus non-compliance with Art. 8.1.1 (see http://www.nhm.ac.uk/hosted-sites/iczn/code/index.jsp?article=8&nfv=true#1)! That would piss me off!
