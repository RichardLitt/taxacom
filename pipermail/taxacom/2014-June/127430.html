The Taxacom web interface is temporarily down as we move to a new host computer.  All mail commands should continue to work fine, including subscribe and unsubscribe transactions (see below).  We will have the web interface for managing accounts operating again soon.   

All mail to the list and mail distribution to existing subscriptions will work normally during this interim period.

Many thanks for your patience.

Jim Beach & Peter Rauch
Taxacom List Owners 



Email commands for Taxacom membership account management:

Taxacom-join at mailman.nhm.ku.edu
by sending a message to this address, a new member can request subscription to the list. Both the Subject: header and body of such a message are ignored. 

Taxacom-leave at mailman.nhm.ku.edu 
by sending a message to this address, a member can request unsubscription from the list. The Subject: header and body of the message is ignored. 

Taxacom-owner at mailman.nhm.ku.edu
Sends an e-mail directly to the list owners.  The Subject: header and body of the message may be ignored by the listowners if they are not paying attention, or have had insufficient caffeine.
