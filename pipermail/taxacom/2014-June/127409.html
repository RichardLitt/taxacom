Ken,

I thought Mike was just citing the ducks as another example of a
Madagascar-NZ connection so I am not sure how you see it that he "fails to
mention that the "Madagascar-New Zealand-subantarctic island" duck clade is
only about 2.9 million years old (between a most likely minimum 1.78 mya
and most likely maximum 3.95 mya)." But one might ask you why you quote the
assertion that the duck clades is "only about 2.9 million years old
(between a most likely minimum 1.78 mya and most likely maximum 3.95 mya)"
when the divergence estimates are calibrated using fossils and so even the
'maximum 3.95 mya' is a minimum.

It may indeed be hard for you and other to "imagine stretching those dates
back far enough for a vicariance event" but that disbelief has no bearing
on how old the taxa are.

You say that the seeds of Euphorbia "probably" stuck to the bodies of a
flying bird. Can you back up that assertion of "probably"?

John Grehan
