> > certainly any species may spread to occupy a much larger areas, but this

> > through means of survival becoming means of dispersal.

> > It is not so much about disagreeing with Ken over panbiogeographic

> Ken?s
> > view is based on assumptions about what is correct or not rather than on
> > empirical evidence. Ken believes that fossil calibrated (or any
> historical
> > calibration) of molecular divergence ages not minimal even though that
> goes
> > against the facts. Panbiogeographic reconstructions are based on spatial
> > analysis that recognizes the existence of allopatry being widespread
