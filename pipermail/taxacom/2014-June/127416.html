Rich,

Ken's position (and I think Jason has a similar outlook, but I may be
confused about that), as outlined in various postings, is that molecular
divergence dates are actual or maximal and so for those cases they falsify
earlier origins and any associated vicariance possibilities. Ken does not
appear willing to accept the reality that fossil calibrated (this is the
most common approach, but island ages are also sometimes used and with
similar problems) ages are minimal estimates only. Ken has asserted several
times that he rejects origins much older than fossil calibrated dates
because he cannot accept them (and that is OK as his personal belief, but
it carries no scientific weight).

John Grehan
