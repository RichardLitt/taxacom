Hi Ken,

On p. 74 of my 2014 book I cited the beetle family Chaetosomatidae, known
only from Madagascar (Malgassochaetus) and New Zealand (Chaetosoma,
Chaetosomodes). A similar pattern in plants occurs in the southern
hemisphere clade of Crossosomatales; it's in Madagascar (also the adjacent
coast of Africa and Cape Town) (Aphloia, Geissoloma) , New Zealand (Ixerba)
and New Caledonia (Strasburgeria). (Both examples are cited, with
references, in my 2014 book, p. 74).
