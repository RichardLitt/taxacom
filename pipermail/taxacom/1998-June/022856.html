1998 
We are pleased to announce the continuing publication of the Taxonomic Atlas
series, devoted to describing and illustrating the marine benthic
invertebrates of southern California. When completed the 14 volume series
will include all major invertebrate groups collected between 50 and 1000
meters depth. Over 140 new species have been described to date.

For further information, please visit our website at:
http://www.sbnature.org/atlas/
or email me at the address below.
