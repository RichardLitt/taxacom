        I am presentely working on phylogeny and taxonomy of Camptosema
(Leguminosae: Phaseoleae: Diocleinae) and allies. I am attempting to
combine morphological and molecular data to test hypotheses of phylogenetic
relationships. I had made trips aiming to collect and to photograph some of

found in northern Argentina, Ururuguai and southern Rio Grande do Sul
