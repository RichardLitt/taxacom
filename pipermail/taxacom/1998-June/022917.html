Ricardo,
        The American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists maintains
a website for museum and instution codens or abbreviations.

http://seahorse.ims.usm.edu/~musweb/asih_codes.cgi

The information for the fish collection is:
Coden:  MCSN
  Museo civico di Storia naturale
  Piazza Hortis 4
  Trieste, ITALY
  Curators: Sergio Dolce
  Remarks: The collection is neither cataloged or computerized. 1,000-10,000
  specimens, including skeletons. No Types. Italian freshwaters, Adriatic
  Sea.
