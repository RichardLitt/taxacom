Vertebrate paleontologists please note:

Due to extensive renovation being done this summer, the collections of the
Texas Memorial Museum's Vertebrate Paleontology Lab (Univ. Texas, Austin)
will be inaccessible (even to us!) from now until the end of August.  We
apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.  We hope to be back to
business as usual by the first of September.
