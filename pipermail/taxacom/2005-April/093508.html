L.E. Carroll, I.M. White, A. Freidberg, A.L. Norrbom, M.J. Dallwitz,
and F.C. Thompson. 2002 onwards. Pest fruit flies of the world.
http://delta-intkey.com/ffa

L.E. Carroll, A.L. Norrbom, M.J. Dallwitz, and F.C. Thompson. 2004 onwards.
Pest fruit flies of the world - larvae. http://delta-intkey.com/ffl

New versions of these packages, which include descriptions, illustrations,
and interactive keys, are now available. There are separate keys for adults
and larvae because the larvae of many species have yet to be thoroughly
described. The adult system includes about 190 species, including a few
Anastrepha species that are non-pests but commonly encountered in U.S.
ports, and the larval system includes about 80 species. The interactive keys
are available in Intkey, Lucid2, and Lucid3 versions.
