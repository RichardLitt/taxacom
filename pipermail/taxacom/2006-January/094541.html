We are pleased to announce the offering of a 2 week class on Tunicata at
the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute Bocas del Toro research station
in Panama, on Isla Colon in the Caribbean. The class will be held August
13-27. Full details are at  http://striweb.si.edu/taxonomy2/
Deadline for applications is March 1, 2006.
Please post this flier and mention it to anyone you think might be
interested. Thank you.
