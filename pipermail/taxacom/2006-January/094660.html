THE NEW YORK BOTANICAL GARDEN
POSITION ANNOUNCEMENT 
Laboratory Technician/ Molecular Systematics
Responsible for DNA extraction, gene amplification (PCR), purification,
and automated sequencing of standard genes being tested for application
as an identifying plant DNA barcode. Will coordinate and maintain a
database of plant tissues, DNA samples, and data, while testing and
implementing new methods of data collection automation using current
machinery. Full-time, ten month position requires at least a B.S. degree
in biology or chemistry and experience with standard molecular biology
techniques including nucleic acid extraction, PCR, cloning, automated
DNA sequencing, etc., or a willingness to be trained in these
techniques.
