Judy:

You are right and it all goes back to the breakup after the MASTER 
(Linnaeus) passed on.

The botanists stayed together, but immediate, for example, Fabricius became 
the Master of Entomology, with his 1775 (Systema Entomologiae), etc., and 
the first to set principles in the Animal Kingdom was again Fabricius, but 
only for Entomology in his Philosophia entomologica ... (1778).

Yes, Strickland did develop a later code for Zoology, but entomologists 
subsequently develop their own nomenclatural codes, etc.

Cheers,

Chris

community -- at least not a functional united community in the way that 
botanists do. We have many taxon based communities and it is too easy to 
say -- let the entomologists worry about it, etc.  Pitching to this 
fractured audience is a much more difficult task.
