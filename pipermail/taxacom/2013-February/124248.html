All:

I'm just back from a series of meetings in Europe, and have been doing my best to monitor this thread (catching up on emails now).  I was so inspired by Donat's contribution that I donated £100 of my own money via the ICZN website. I want to echo Michael's challenge to every taxonomist on Taxacom to contribute some amount.  The money is of course important, but perhaps even more important is the demonstration of support for the organization.

Aloha,
Rich
