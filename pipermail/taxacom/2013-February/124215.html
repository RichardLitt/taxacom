Without getting into specifics of the current budget (someone else who has that authority can answer that question), I can however give a bit of history into the budget of the ICZN when I was its president 10 years ago. Remember these figures are 10 years old -- they do not get smaller with every year. Inflation and the cost of living causes them to get significantly larger.

At that time the total expenditures were £69814.

The majority of that was for salaries (none were full-time salaries then and none are today). Other expenses were for printing of the Bulletin (£10700), office supplies, depreciation of office equipment, and funds for attendance at one meeting in Bangkok (£1255).

Ten years ago we knew that at the rate of continued increased costs to run the Secretariat and the continued decreased revenue stream (mainly sales of the Code, subscriptions to the Bulletin, and donations), the ICZN would go broke in 4 years. Thus, there would need to be an injection of quite a bit of funding from which the office could then work off of the interest earned. We organized an "appeal" -- and made a presentation at the Bangkok Pacific Science Congress regarding that.

Larger donations from various sources did in fact come in and postponed the inevitable for 10 years rather than the initial projected 4 years. However, the appeal did not get anywhere near the millions that were necessary to keep the ICZN functioning off of interest of an endowment of sorts.

Attempts to gain more revenue elsewhere were thwarted with folks not wanting to buy the Code nor subscribing to the BZN and a clamoring instead for everything to be available for free and digital. So, ICZN was left with operating almost solely from donations.

The fact that the current expenses seem to some to not be a trivial amount is quite possibly due to a lack of understanding of how the Secretariat has operated now and in the past and the cutbacks that have continually been made over the decades to cut expenses yet still able to keep cases flowing in and out as fast as possible to allow nomenclatural questions to be answered in a timely manner.

The donate button is nice, but "be assured" it is not the ultimate answer -- it is only a band-aid solution that will continually have to be sought in order to keep the ICZN afloat. What is still needed is a large endowment to secure the work of the Secretariat now and in future years without having to seek continual donations.

Either that or develop a completely different way of processing the dozens and dozens of cases that come in each year. If someone has a solution to that, please speak up!

-Neal

Seconding Stuart's question, I have a few others, formed from my
experience as a former board member of a much larger organization, and a
former Member (i.e. owner) of a Foundation:

Who actually owns the Trust?  How are those owners chosen, and by whom?
Does anyone paid by the Trust directly participate in choosing them? Do
those legal owners practice their expected oversight with appropriate
participation?

Who actually makes the hiring and firing decisions? Who sets salaries? Who
reviews those decisions?

Why, if the cost of living in London is a major problem, does the Trust
not do what the Royal Entomological Society did, and move?

There are more, but those will do for a start.

Mike
