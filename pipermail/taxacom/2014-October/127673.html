The 6th edition of the International Congress of Zoology of Grigore 
Antipa Museum (CZGA) will be held from 19-22 November 2014 in Bucharest 
(Romania).
The following Keynote Speakers have confirmed their participation in the 
CZGA 2014:
1. Alberto Ballerio, International Commission on Zoological 
Nomenclature, “Current topics in Zoological Nomenclature: ZooBank, 
Electronic Publication and Taxonomic Vandalism”.
2. Rafael Araujo, Museo Nacional de Ciencias Naturales, Madrid, Spain, 
"Knowing and conserving the West Palearctic naiads”.
3. Joseph Bryja, Institute of Vertebrate Biology, Academy of Sciences of 
Czech Republic, Brno, Czech Republic, "African rodents - important 
biogeographical model group despite poor knowledge of their alpha taxonomy".
4. Frank Wesselingh, Naturalis Biodiversity Center, Leiden, Netherlands, 
“Drivers of Pontocaspian Biodiversity: Rise and Demise”.
5. Lutz Bachmann, National Centre for Biosystematics Natural History 
Museum University of Oslo, Norway, "Exploring the treasures of natural 
history collections; lessons from flatworms and whales".
All the information about the conference, the registration process, the 
fees and the deadlines can be found on our website (www.czga.ro).
We extended the early bird fee deadline until October 10, 2014. Please 
check our registration section for details.
