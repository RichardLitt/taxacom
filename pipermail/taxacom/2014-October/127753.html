I find the title of the conference to be slightly misleading. Firstly, systematics does not underpin all of biology. Secondly, taxonomy underpins systematics, and so is even more fundamental. Basically, systematics is the study of the evolutionary relationships between taxa, and taxa are the constructs of taxonomy.
