Yes, that looks like a distinct improvement! As to the best terminology, that is not an easy question, not only depending on the question of who the users of the database are, but complicated by the fact that the database operates across nomenclature Codes.

"Invalid name" is formally defined in the zoological Code, but it is sometimes also used for botanical names, although only informally: it then means something different. 
I suppose there are two options: either follow the terminology of the relevant Code exactly (while indicating what is the relevant Code), which will be hard on most users, or to use informal, unambiguous terms (such as "accepted" or "current", versus "not accepted" or "not current", and "not a formal name" or "not a scientific name"). Feedback by users should help find the optimum.
