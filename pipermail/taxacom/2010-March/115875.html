Second circular

The fifth international Rubiaceae and Gentianales Conference  
Stockholm, Sweden, September 6?10, 2010.

The Bergius Foundation has the privilege to host the fifth  
International Rubiaceae and Gentianales Conference on September 06 to  
10, 2010 in Stockholm, Sweden. This conference endeavours to bring  
together international researchers that share a common interest in  
the study of the Gentianales. The meeting will be an exciting  
opportunity to exchange new ideas, to share results, as well as to  
strengthen international collaboration. We would like to extend our  
warmest welcome to all of you for joining us for this conference. We  
are looking forward to meeting you in Stockholm.

Conference Theme and Statement

The theme of the conference is ?Biodiversity in the light of  
historical information?. Phylogenetic analyses and the use of  
historical data have greatly enhanced our ability to understand  
biodiversity in space and time. This international conference will  
focus on recent progress in the use of biodiversity data for improved  
estimation of phylogeny and its various applications. This will hold  
promise for the future use of Rubiaceae and the Gentianales as model  
systems for understanding flowering plant evolution.

Presentations and topics

The following topics are planned for oral and poster presentations.
* Biodiversity, taxonomy and species richness
* Progress in phylogeny
* Turning phylogenies into classifications
* Dating lineages
* Biogeography and paleoclimates
* Character evolution
Of course, presentations of work such as revisions in the Gentianales  
are also welcome.

Venue

The conference will take place at the Royal Swedish Academy of  
Sciences is situated in Frescati, a few kilometers N of the centre of  
Stockholm, close to the Stockholm University, the Swedish Museum of  
Natural History, and the Bergius Botanic Garden. The metro station  
and the local train station "Universitetet" are within five minutes  
walking distance.

General information

Detailed information on the conference sessions, invited speakers,  
and the programme will appear at the conference web site as soon as  
they are available. Please visit http://www.bergianska.se/b3/

The official language of the conference will be English. Translation  
facilities will not be available.

Registration

Registration is not yet available, but it will be possible to  
register by using a web based form at the conference website. The  
registration will be open from early April until the end of July 2010.

At this time, however, if you would like to receive further  
information about the conference (circulars via email), please  
contact the organising committee (kent.kainulainen at botan.su.se).

The conference fee will include lunches (or lunch boxes) as well as  
the conference dinner. An excursion to Linnaeus' home will also be

Abstract submission

A web-based abstract submission will be available on March 1st and  
end before July 1, 2010. The abstract should not be longer than 200  
words excluding authors and title. Abstracts of conference  
presentations (including posters) will be available from the web site  
and registered participants will get a printed booklet at the  
beginning of the meeting.

Travel grants

A few travel grants intended to support students and scientists from  
developing countries will be available. Participants supported by  
travel grants will be required to give oral presentations. Travel  
grants are intended to help in funding travel to the meeting [meaning  
that a grant may or may not cover the entire cost] by reimbursement.

Those legible may apply from March 1, 2010 via email. The application  
should consist of personal details and information about current  
appointment. Student applications should be accompanied by a letter  
of recommendation from their major professor (main supervisor/ 
advisor). There is no requirement of registration prior to applying,  
but a proposed presentation abstract should be included. This  
abstract should be submitted through the conference abstract web  
submission page, which will also open on March 1.
Please send applications to to Prof. Birgitta Bremer  
(birgitta.bremer at bergianska.se).
