We regret to inform you that Dr. Grady Webster, Professor Emeritus at the
University of California-Davis, passed away peacefully last night (Thursday
October 27) as the result of a stroke he suffered on October 21. A memorial
service will be held on Saturday, November 5 at 2 PM at the University Club
on the UC Davis campus.
