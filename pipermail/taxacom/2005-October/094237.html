The University and Jepson Herbaria are seeking an Administrative Curator.
Application information at http://jobs.berkeley.edu

Here is a summary:

Posting Title: Administrative Curator
Requisition: 003459
Department: University/Jepson Herbaria
Location: Main Campus-Berkeley
Salary: Annual: $45,100 to $54,100
First Review Date: 10/12/2005
This requisition will remain open until filled.
Job Description:
Under the supervision and general direction of the Herbaria Director, the Administrative Curator is overall manager of the collections comprising over 2 million preserved plant specimens and is also responsible for the associated library, other archival materials, and facilities; serves as primary point person for research and public service queries directed at the herbaria; and works closely with the Director and development staff to pursue external funding for the collections.
