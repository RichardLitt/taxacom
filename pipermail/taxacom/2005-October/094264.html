2005 
To accelerate the discovery and study of the world's biodiversity, the U.S.
NSF invites proposals from teams of investigators from U.S. eligible
institutions (and their international colleagues) to conduct a worldwide,
species-level systematic inventory of a major group of organisms in need of
modern taxonomic investigation. Please see the newly issued Program
Solicitation NSF 06-500 posted on the NSF web site:

http://www.nsf.gov/pubs/2006/nsf06500/nsf06500.htm

Thank you,
