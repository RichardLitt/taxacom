You might also try the SpeciesLink project at
http://splink.cria.org.br/.  This project concentrates on collections in
São Paulo state and is still underway, with databanking of many of the
collections incomplete, but it turns up about 60 individual collections,
including some from other states.  Be sure to include the "SinBiota"
database in your search, since this cites much material from recent
projects which has not been incorporated into the main hebarium collections.
