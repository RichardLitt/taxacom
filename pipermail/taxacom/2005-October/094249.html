2.      COMPETENCY REQUIREMENTS
2.1.    Minimum qualification requirements
An MSc (lecturer) / PhD (senior lecturer) in Plant Anatomy or Plant Systematics
2.2.    Minimum experience required: number of years and type of experience
Approximately 4 years relevant experience in Light and Electron Microscopy and related techniques as applicable to Botany.
Expertise in the field of cytology, plant structure or systematics and an interdisciplinary interest within environmental sciences.
Proven (accredited) record of publications on aspects of plant structure or plant systematics.
