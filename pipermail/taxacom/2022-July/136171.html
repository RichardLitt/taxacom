2022 
Dear all,

With great sadness, the family of Montserrat Rios Almeida announces that
after several years of fighting cancer, Montsita passed away in Quito
yesterday, Wednesday, July 27, 2022. In accordance with her wishes, her
remains will be cremated and scattered in a forest in the Mindo sector
(Pichincha, Ecuador).

Peace be upon her grave, and may her scientific legacy endure forever.

Alina
