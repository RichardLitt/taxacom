2014 
The International Congress of Zoology of Grigore Antipa Museum (CZGA) is 
an annual zoological meeting held inRomania, organised by the "Grigore 
Antipa" National Museum of Natural History. The congress includes 
invited lectures and oral/poster presentations by participants.

CZGA 2014 (6th edition) will be held from 19-22 November 2014 in 
Bucharest (Romania).

CZGA 2014 will be an excellent opportunity for zoology scholars to meet 
colleagues and to present and discuss the results of their research 
projects.

As invited key note speakers, highly recognized national and 
international zoologists will present the newest progress in the fields 
of Taxonomy, Faunistics, Zoogeography, Ecology, Systematics, 
Conservation Biology and Evolutionary Biology.

All the information about the conference, the registration process, the 
fees and the deadlines can be found on our website (www.czga.ro).

If you register the Congress before September 30th 2014, you will 
benefit the extended early bird fee (60 EUR). Please check our 
registration section for details (http://czga.ro/pagina.php?id=10&lng=en).
