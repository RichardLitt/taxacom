Greetings (and pardon cross-postings),

Georgia Southern University’s Department of Biology invites applications
for an *Assistant Professor in Plant Biology*, with an emphasis on
systematics and/or evolution of plants. This position requires research,
teaching, and service responsibilities along with a terminal degree. The
full text advertisement, including information about the department,
faculty, and the complete position announcement with all qualifications and
application instructions, is available at
http://cosm.georgiasouthern.edu/biology/. Deadline for applications is *October
27, 2014*. Georgia is an open records state. Georgia Southern is an AA/EO
institution. Individuals who need reasonable accommodations under the ADA
to participate in the search process should contact the Associate Provost.
