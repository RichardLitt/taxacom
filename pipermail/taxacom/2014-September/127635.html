Hello, Scientists!

We are collecting data on actual digitization processes for Entomology collections worldwide. This survey targets dried, pinned collections only. We are interested in the basic workflow steps and how much time it takes to accomplish them. The survey is 15 questions and will only take about 10 minutes of your time. Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
