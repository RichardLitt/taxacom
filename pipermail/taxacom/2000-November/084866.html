2000 
I am pleased to announce that Alison Kelly has joined the Intermountain
Herbarium as Collections Manager.  Alison completed her M.S. at Utah State
University, the title of her thesis being Predictive Mapping of the
Vegetation of the North Fork John Day Wilderness Area.  Since graduating,
she has worked for both the Bureau of Land Management and U.S. Forest
Service (at different times), becoming involved, among other things with
bryophytes, lichens, and databasing of collections.

She can be reached by email at ali at biology.usu.edu or
herbarium at biology.usu.edu and by phone at 435-797-1584.
