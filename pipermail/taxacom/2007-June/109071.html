Dear all,

EDIT (European Distributed Institute of Taxonomy) is a Network 
of  Excellence under the FP6 programme. More info on http://www.e-taxonomy.eu.

WorkPackage 8 is developing a training centre, the EDIT's School of 
Taxonomy. The school's goal is to federate training providers around 
a common vision for the future of taxonomic training in Europe. The 
school will bring together existing training resources and new 
training opportunities into a coherent and widely publicised training 
programme for taxonomy.

The school is looking for training providers to build up a team of 
highly skilled staff coming from a wide range of institutions with a 
taxonomical and educational background.

We invite you to take a closer look at the EDIT website:
http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/news.php?optimurl=School-of-taxonomy-deadline-extended
and if you are interested in joining the "committee of training 
providers" please fill in the pro forma.

Feel free to distribute this request further.
We are looking forward to receiving your response.

Thank you for your cooperation.
on behalf of the WP8 Team
