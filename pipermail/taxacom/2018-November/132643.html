Hi all,

the COST Action MOBILISE (one of the projects linked to DiSSCo) has opened
a call for short scientific or technical visits related to Digital
Collections. This allows scientists, collection managers and data stewards
to visit natural history museums and other facilities across Europe to
enhance scientific and technical capacity around digital natural history
collections.

This is an open call, with as deadline for submission 15 December 2018

Period in which the visit must take place:
1 Feb 2019 to 15 April 2019

More info can be found here:
https://dissco.eu/blog/call-short-scientifictechnical-visits-related-digital-collections
