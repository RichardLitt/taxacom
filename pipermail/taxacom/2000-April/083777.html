I asked my students to give me a hard copy of their labels for insects going onto pins, and their labels going into vials with alcohol specimens.  I ask for the hard copy in order to check for spellings and other errors. 

To make sure that I understood which labels were for what, one girl had written, with arrows pointing to the appropriate labels, "Vile labels".  

Needless to say, this caused a real snorting roar of laughter from me.  I am still giggling every once in a while when I think about it.
