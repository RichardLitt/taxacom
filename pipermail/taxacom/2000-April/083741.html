2000 
INDEX HERBARIORUM and PLANT SPECIALISTS INDEX

Updated information for 2111 herbaria (71%) in 140 countries (86%) listed in
INDEX HERBARIORUM, edition 8, and its supplements (published in TAXON) is
available for searching by institution, city, state, acronym, staff member,
correspondent, and research specialty (http://www.nybg.org/bsci/ih/ih.html).
Telephone and fax numbers and e-mail and URL addresses are included.

Note that the Index is fully searchable on research specialty, so it also
serves as a PLANT SPECIALISTS INDEX.

PLEASE REVIEW THE ENTRY FOR YOUR HERBARIUM.  SEND UPDATES AND CORRECTIONS
TO: pholmgren at nybg.org <mailto:pholmgren at nybg.org> 

Updated information is available for all herbaria registered in INDEX
HERBARIORUM in the following countries: Alderney, Angola, Armenia,
Australia, Austria, Azerbaijan, Barbados, Belarus, Belize, Benin, Bolivia,
Bosnia and Herzegovina, Botswana, Brunei Darussalam, Bulgaria, Burkina Faso,
Burundi, Cameroon, Cayman Islands, Central African Republic, Costa Rica,
Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Fiji, French Guiana, French Polynesia, Gabon,
Greenland, Guadeloupe, Guam, Guatemala, Guernsey, Guyana, Honduras, Iceland,
Jamaica, Jersey Islands, Jordan, Lebanon, Lesotho, Lithuania, Luxembourg,
Malawi, Mauritius, Moldova, Mozambique, Namibia, Nepal, New Caledonia,
Norway, Oman, Panama, Puerto Rico, Saudi Arabia, Seychelles, Singapore,
Solomon Islands, Sri Lanka, Suriname, Swaziland, Sweden, Togo, Trinidad,
U.S.A., Vietnam, Virgin Islands, and Yugoslavia.

Updated information is available for 75-99% of the herbaria in Argentina,
Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Cuba, Czech Republic, Estonia, Finland, France,
Germany, Greece, Indonesia, Ireland, Italy, Korea, Mexico, Netherlands,
Papua New Guinea, Romania, South Africa, Spain, Switzerland, Tanzania,
Uganda, and Uruguay.

Updated information is available for 50-74% of the herbaria in Chile,
Colombia, Ecuador, Egypt, Ethiopia, Hungary, India, Iran, Iraq, Isle of Man,
Israel, Japan, Kenya, Latvia, Libya, Malaysia, Morocco, New Zealand,
Nicaragua, Pakistan, Philippines, Poland, Portugal, Republic of China,
Senegal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Thailand, Turkey, Ukraine, Venezuela, and
Zimbabwe.

Updated information is available for 25-49% of the herbaria in Dominican
Republic, El Salvador, Georgia, Ghana, Kazakhstan, Nigeria, Paraguay, Peru,
Russia, Sudan, Turkmenistan, and U.K.

Updated information is available for 1-24% of the herbaria in Myanmar,
People's Republic of China, and Zambia.

No updated information is available for the herbaria in Afghanistan,
Algeria, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Haiti, Ivory Coast, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan,
Liberia, Madagascar, Malta, Mongolia, Niger, People's Republic of the Congo,
Réunion, Sierra Leone, Somalia, Tajikistan, Tunisia, United Arab Emirates,
Uzbekistan, Vanuatu, and Zaire.

This file will be updated every few months, so please send updates and
corrections to Patricia K. Holmgren (pholmgren at nybg.org) or Noel H. Holmgren
(nholmgren at nybg.org).
