Dear Colleagues,

This is to inform you that the volume 9 of Bionomina was just published,
both online and on paper.

The content of this issue is as follows:

Evangelos Vlachos. Forming taxon names from Greek words. (1-26).

Alain Dubois. What is an anonymous publication? Is the International Code
of Zoological Nomenclature anonymous? (27-34).

Ivan Löbl. Stability under the International Code of Zoological
Nomenclature: a bag of problems affecting nomenclarture and taxonomy.
(35-40).

Alain Dubois. An often overlooked Rule of the Code, and its bearing on the
authorship and date of the nomen Proteidae (Amphibia, Urodela). (41-49).

Neal Woodman. On the original description of the Sacred Shrew, Sorex
religiosa I. Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire, 1826 [nec 1827] (Mammalia: Soricidae).
(50-53).

Merry Christmas and Happy New Year to all!

Alain Dubois
