For those few on this list who might be interested, there is now a
regularly updated taxonomic list of species and genera of Hepialidae south
of the USA posted at
http://www.johngrehan.net/files/9214/4925/3630/Catalogue_of_the_Neotropical_Hepialidae_vs_2_Dec_1st.pdf

This is an updated electronic version of a publication originally by Mielke
& Grehan (2012). Each new version will be numbered and dated. This resource
may not be as sophisticated as those major databases that many refer to on
TAXACOM, but perhaps helpful for those who may be interested in this
relatively obscure group. If anyone on this list subscribes to any
Lepidoptera list I would be most grateful if you could forward this
announcement of the resource.

Thanks,

John Grehan
