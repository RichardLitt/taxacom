Hello Everyone!,

Got data in your database? Have you shared it? Is your data available 
via one or more other websites? Can you get enhanced, modified, 
standardized, georeferenced, annotated data from these sites back into 
your database? Re-integrating data can be challenging for many reasons.

A new Data Management Interest Group at iDigBio plans to discuss issues 
surrounding shared data. We'd like to document the help, information, 
and infrastructure the community needs in order to re-integrate data. If 
you need help, please come to this meeting and share what issues you 
face. If you have ideas for how to help, please join us.

*What:* Webinar to kickoff a new iDigBio Data Management Interest Group 
<https://www.idigbio.org/content/data-management-interest-group-kickoff-webinar>
*What format*: A short presentation will be followed by open discussion 
for all participants.
*When:* Thursday 7 August 2014, 1:00 - 2:00 PM EDT
*Where: *http://idigbio.adobeconnect.com/datamgmt
(nothing to install, use any browser) Headsets are recommended (they 
help remove echo).
*Who: *All are welcome, please invite your colleagues, no registration 
needed.
*Questions?* Please send to any member of the group: Data Management 
Interest Group 
<https://www.idigbio.org/wiki/index.php/IDigBio_Working_Groups#Data_Management_Interest_Group>

See you soon! Deb Paul, et al
