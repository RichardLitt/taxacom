The Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew is offering a course in Applied Plant Taxonomy, Identification and Field Survey skills, with financial support from the NERC.

Course dates:  two weeks, full-time, Sept. 29th to Oct. 10th 2014.

The course is designed for early career environmental scientists and PhD students of any discipline, with competitive bursaries supported through NERC funding.

Please follow the link for further information and application form:   http://tinyurl.com/KewNERC
