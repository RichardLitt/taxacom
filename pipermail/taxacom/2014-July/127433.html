I am pleased to announce the availability of remote participation at the upcoming small herbarium digitization workshop, to be held 31 July 2014 in conjunction with Botany 2014. All workshop proceedings will be broadcast and recorded via Adobe Connect. The link for remote participation is shown below. Encourage your colleagues to log in and participate. No special software is required. Virtually any internet browser will work.
Date/time: 31 July 2014, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. MDT.
Agenda: https://www.idigbio.org/wiki/images/c/c1/Botany2014Agenda.pdf.
Wiki: https://www.idigbio.org/wiki/index.php/Small_Herbarium_Workshop_Botany_2014.
Remote participation: https://idigbio.adobeconnect.com/smallherb
