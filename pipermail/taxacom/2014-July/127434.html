E-mail commands for Taxacom account management:



Taxacom-join at mailman.nhm.ku.edu<mailto:Taxacom-join at mailman.nhm.ku.edu>

By sending a message to this address, a non-member can request a subscription to the list. Both the "Subject:" header and body of such a message are ignored.



Taxacom-leave at mailman.nhm.ku.edu<mailto:Taxacom-leave at mailman.nhm.ku.edu>

By sending a message to this address, a member can be unsubscribed from the list. The "Subject:" header and body of the message are ignored.



Taxacom-owner at mailman.nhm.ku.edu<mailto:Taxacom-owner at mailman.nhm.ku.edu>

Sends an e-mail directly to the list owners.  The Subject: header and body of the message may be ignored by the listowners if they are not paying attention, or have had insufficient caffeine.
