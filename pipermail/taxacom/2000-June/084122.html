African Entomology 2001

13TH ENTOMOLOGICAL CONGRESS
organized by the
ENTOMOLOGICAL SOCIETY OF SOUTHERN AFRICA

Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal

2nd to 5th July 2001

Announcement

The KwaZulu-Natal entomologists invite you to participate in the above conference which will be held on the campus of the University of Natal in Pietermaritzburg. We intend to provide a forum for the dynamic exchange of information and ideas relevant to entomologists of all persuasions and with particular emphasis on the needs of Africa. Your input is required to make this a success, and suggestions for symposia, special sessions and/or keynote speakers will be particularly welcomed.

Please supply the details below, by mail, fax or e-mail, if you are interested in receiving the First Circular which will give further information.
