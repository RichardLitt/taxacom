A new version of the TIARA organismal bioinformatics project website is
available at http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/tiara/. The TIARA project currently
contains data for arthropod taxa, primarily insects and primarily from the
south-central United States. Major additions to the new version include:
(1) links to >1000 additional insect images [now with ca. 1300 total
images] and (2) additional mapping functionality for the five-state region
comprising New Mexico, Texas, Oklahoma, Arkansas and Louisiana in the
south-central United States. For additional information on new data
additions to the site, see the "What's New" page linked off the TIARA main
page. As always, comments and corrections are welcome.
