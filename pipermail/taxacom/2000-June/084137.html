Might I ask that people refrain from using subject titles such as "info
request" - for two reasons:

1. It does not help when searching list archives.

2. It is irritating to have to open messages just to see if they are of
personal interest.

In this case, a title such as "Info request - shipment of Allium" would be
appropriate.

Thanks.

Rob
