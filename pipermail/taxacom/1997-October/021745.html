1997 
     NEW YORK BOTANICAL GARDEN NORTH AMERICAN BRYOPHYTE CATALOG

     Additional records of moss specimens from Alaska, Canada and Greenland
     have recently been added to the New York Botanical Garden North
     American Bryophyte Catalogue web site:

     (http://www.nybg.org/bsci/hcol/bryo/bry3.html).

     MOSS FAMILIES AVAILABLE FOR SEARCHING:

     Andreaeaceae, Andreaeobryaceae, Aulacomniaceae, Bartramiaceae,
     Bruchiaceae, Bryaceae, Bryoxiphiaceae, Catoscopiaceae, Dicranaceae,
     Disceliaceae, Ditrichiaceae, Encalyptaceae, Fabroniaceae,
     Fissidentaceae, Funariaceae, Grimmiaceae, Hedwigiaceae,
     Hookeriaceae, Lembophyllaceae, Leskeaceae, Leucobryaceae, Meesiaceae,
     Mniaceae, Myriniaceae, Neckeraceae, Orthotrichaceae, Pottiaceae,
     Pterigynandraceae, Ptychomitriaceae, Schistostegaceae, Sphagnaceae,
     Splachnaceae, Takakiaceae, Tetraphidaceae, Thuidiaceae,


     ANTHOCEROTE AND HEPATIC FAMILIES AVAILABLE FOR SEARCHING:

        All.


     The total number of records now available for searching at the
     abovementioned site now stands at  43,022.

     The NYBG North American Bryophyte Catalogue project is supported in
     part by a grant from the National Science Foundation.
