You may not need to find an emendation for the gastropod name spelling to be considered available -- see prevailing usage . Article 33.3.1 says if the misspelling points back to the original publication of the name it misspelled, and is in is prevailing usage, it is deemed to be the correct original spelling.NealOn Stardate 12/16/25, 9:36 AM, "Taxacom on behalf of David Campbell via Taxacom" <taxacom-bounces at lists.ku.edu<mailto:taxacom-bounces at lists.ku.edu> on behalf of taxacom at lists.ku.edu<mailto:taxacom at lists.ku.edu>> scribbled the following tidbit:If anyone is interested in a proposed ICZN appeal regarding a juniorhomonym in Hawai'ian sphinx moths, let me know.  The gastropod seniorhomonym of Tinostoma is nomenclaturally valid for senior homonympurposes (intentional invalid emendation, has been used post-1900), but isnot useful for nomenclature nor biology and can reasonably be suppressed,allowing current usage for the moth to continue.
