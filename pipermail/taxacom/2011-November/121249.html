The Science & Horticulture Applications Development Team, part of the IT Department at RBG Kew, is seeking talented IT / biodiversity informatics / GIS staff to carry out exciting and innovative multidisciplinary projects as well as managing business as usual.

To support the delivery of Kew's Breathing Planet Programme (BPP), we are seeking to recruit to three posts - Senior Developer x 2 and a service desk team leader. You will work with the science applications team, key scientists and other contracted resources to ensure continuation of business as usual service provision to internal and external stakeholders. In addition, you will take on Science and Horticulture Systems project-related tasks as and when required.

Pro-active and quick thinking, you will possess a recognised qualification in Computer Science or Biodiversity Informatics, GIS or equivalent work-based knowledge.

For further information and an application pack please go to our website www.kew.org/aboutus/jobs<http://www.kew.org/aboutus/jobs>.
Alternatively, please contact the HR Department on 020 8332 5184/5150 (24 hour answerphone). Please return your application ideally by email to jobs at kew.org<mailto:jobs at kew.org> or by post to: HR Department, 47 Kew Green, Kew, Surrey TW9 3AB. Please quote reference: 5020/25.

Closing date: 21 November 2011.
Interview date: w/c 5 December 2011.
