 Oh, really? Except for the most common
 situation in shallow-water marine invertebrates. Once one  actually bothers to look more or less carefully at this  "common, widespread uniform species" and discovers a huge  species complex beyond the façade of this "common" or even  "cosmopolitan species", the importance of the type  localities somehow becomes crystal clear.
