     In 1998, Thomas Cavalier-Smith calls this taxon Subphylum Deinobacteria (in "A revised six-kingdom system of life"; Biol. Rev. (1998): page 218).   He refers to it as Deinobacteria Cavalier-Smith, 1986, which I assume is his 1986 paper "The kingdoms of organisms" (Nature 324:416-417).   That would antedate the Weisberg et al. paper by two or three years.
