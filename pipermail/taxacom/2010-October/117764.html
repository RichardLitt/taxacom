Dear all,

the new EU project ViBRANT will start in December and have its launch  
symposium  in January, back to back with the EDIT Year 5 meeting.  The  
meetings will be in Paris, EDIT from 17th to the 19th, and ViBRANT on  
20th and 21st.

Please see:

http://vbrant.eu/content/scripting-life

and

http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/year5event

Please note that the ViBRANT programme is still in the draft stage and  
we are waiting for speakers to confirm attendance before publishing  
their names.  Soon now, very soon ....

Most likely to be of general interest to this list will be the  
symposia on the Tuesday and Thursday.  On the Wednesday EDIT will be  
demonstrating some its software products and there will be a  
Scratchpad training course:

http://scratchpads.eu/scratchpad-training-courses
