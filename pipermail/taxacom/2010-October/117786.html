Dear all,

EDIT-DEST is offering, within the framework of the Modern Taxonomy 
course programme, a course on "Botanical Nomenclature", 24-28 January 
2011, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.

The course is ideal for early career or future taxonomists who want to 
learn more about the ICBN and how to apply good nomenclatural practice 
when undertaking taxonomic revisions, compiling checklists etc.

Deadline for registration is approaching: 1 November 2010.
 
Detailed information and registration at:
http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/modern-taxonomy-course-programme-2010-2011
