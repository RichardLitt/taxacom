Abstract Submission Deadline for Oral presentations is fast approaching

All abstracts for Oral Presentations must be submitted by 31 October 2010.

The Scientific Program Committee reminds you to submit an abstract of original research online via the Congress website for presentation at the XVIII International Botanical Congress 2011 to be held from 23-30 July 2011 in Melbourne, Australia.

Oral presentations must be relevant to one of the accepted <http://www.ibc2011.com/Accepted.htm> General Symposia while poster presentations can cover any topic within the seven broad themes.

Abstracts for posters may be submitted until 1 February 2011, and anyone whose submission for a talk is unsuccessful will be asked whether they wish to have their presentation considered for a poster.


Click here<http://www.ibc2011.com/Abstracts.htm> to submit an abstract for presentation at the Congress. If you are unable to access the website to submit your abstract, please contact the Congress Secretariat to make alternative arrangements.

Send to a Friend - If you know of fellow colleagues who may be interested in attending IBC2011, please send this message onto them.
