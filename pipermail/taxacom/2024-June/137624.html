Hi John,
Any package from abroad containing "wildlife" needs an approved form 3-177, best before it is even sent to the US. This is the Declaration for Importation or Exportation of Fish or Wildlife.
You can file these forms online (my collections manager does this fortunately, so I don't have the URL), but we never have problems to get these approved. Specimens coming in without such form on file either slip through (90%), or get confiscated or returned.
We never try the "slip through" approach. That's probably why we are in good standing with USFWS.
Cheers
