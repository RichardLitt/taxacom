2024 
For all wildlife, you need a filled out US Fish and Wildlife form 3-177,
and copies of collecting and export permits from the country of origin.
Depending on the contents, you may need additional permits (e.g., CDC
permits for potential biohazards, permits for IUCN Appendix I materials
from country of origin and destination, USDA permits for various wildlife
covered by them).

It's all great fun.
