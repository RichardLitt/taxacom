Dear colleagues,
Since 2023 we have been working on developing simplified protocols for the
fast and cheap digitization of natural history collection in Ukraine
threatened by war. The initial draft of the book has been already deposited
at Zenodo (https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoi.org%2F10.5281%2Fzenodo.10251788&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C10ece984f0744ff1585508dc94596fc5%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638548359065749815%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=k%2BvSf80ObpPz10RruyK2ui9KVoOXL9zYtKG0BKnOy4o%3D&reserved=0) and will be updated
soon. However, we also would like to ask you to share your experience with
digitization of your collections and your opinion regarding some
controversial aspects.

Hence, being a PI of the team, I will be thankful for filling out the
survey which should take ca. 15-20 minutes of your time -
