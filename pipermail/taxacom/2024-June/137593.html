recently published new genera into the IRMNG database, ideally all placed
tio family level per either the original work or a more recent treatment,
and have come up against the following 3 new fossil coral genera (Rugosa)
from a 2013 work for which we do not have relevant families (the abstract
is available online). If anyone with access to the work in question could
supply this missing information, it would be appreciated!

McLean, R. A., & Copper, P. (2013). Rugose corals from the Early Silurian

and the three new genera therein for which we are seeking family placement,
if possible, are: Cladopaliphyllum McLean & Copper, 2013,
Cormorantia McLean & Copper, 2013, and Chaloupella McLean & Copper, 2013.
