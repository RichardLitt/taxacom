Hi Mary,
Yes. *Tetraponera continua* was described as *Sima penzigi **continua *by
Forel, 1907, combined as *Sima (Tetraponera*) *continua* by Emery, 1921,
then as *Tetraponera continua* Wheeler, 1922. It was subsumed back as a
subspecies of *T. penzi*gi and treated that way until it was re-raised to
species by Chomici et al., 2015.
More details and the relevant literature are available at
https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fantcat.org%2Fcatalog%2F450890&data%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C1fe7a442d1694c36a79408dc8773a693%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638534177991475540%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=miB4MNBM2%2B6loR%2FJpdT0kom9WYZ1sM91eHBXwHUON0g%3D&reserved=0.
