Hi Mary,
Not an expert, but contacted Dmitry Dubovikoff, who is not in the list. So
he replied:
Now these taxa are valid. Two different species: Tetraponera penzigi (Mayr,
1907) and T.continua (Forel, 1907). The last one was described as a
subspecies of the first, but now they are separate species.
Recent updates and references can be found at antcat.org.
