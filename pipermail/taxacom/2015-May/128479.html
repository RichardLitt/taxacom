Caryophyllales 2015. An international conference at the Botanic Garden and Botanical Museum Berlin-Dahlem, Freie Universität Berlin, September 13-18, 2015.

Dear Colleagues and Collaborators in the Caryophyllales Network:

This is to invite you to attend Caryophyllales 2015 in Berlin, which will be held at the Botanic Garden and Botanical Museum Berlin-Dahlem, Freie Universität Berlin. Besides bringing together the latest research developments, the conference will foment the Caryophyllales Network, which aims at joining forces for the elaboration of a global monographic synthesis of the order Caryophyllales.

The conference website is available at http://caryophyllales.org/caryophyllales2015 and provides information on the preliminary programme, registration, abstract submission, workshops and tours as well as accommodation.

To register please fill in the registration form available at http://caryophyllales.org/caryophyllales2015/registration and return it to Caryophyllales at bgbm.org<mailto:Caryophyllales at bgbm.org>. Early registration with reduced conference fee is available until June 30, 2015.

We were successful in obtaining partial funding from the German Research Foundation (DFG) and will be able to support participation of scientists from certain countries (see http://caryophyllales.org/caryophyllales2015/funding for details). Deadline for the application of travel support is June 15, 2015.

If you have any questions regarding the conference or need an invitation letter to obtain travel documents please contact the conference office at Caryophyllales at bgbm.org<mailto:Caryophyllales at bgbm.org>.

We are looking forward to an exciting conference and hope to meet you in Berlin in September.

With best wishes

Sabine von Mering, Walter Berendsohn, Thomas Borsch
