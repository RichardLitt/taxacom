Colleagues,

Registration for the course, Philosophy of Biological Systematics, 
taking place 7-11 September 2015 at the Royal Belgian Institute of 
Natural Sciences, Brussels, will close on 1 June 2015. A description of 
the course and the registration form can be found here 
<http://taxonomytraining.eu/content/philosophy-biological-systematics>. 
This course offers a unique opportunity to explore relations of the 
principles of systematics to those of scientific inquiry in general, 
from which many of the current perspectives and approaches in 
systematics can be critically assessed.

If you know students or colleagues who might find this topic of 
interest, please pass along this information.

Thanks,
Kirk
