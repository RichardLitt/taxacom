The citation is (6)13: 440.
i.e. Series 6 vol 13.

Since the number of specimens is not stated, although vaguely implied, 
it is better to treat the extant specimen as a syntype since no holotype 
was designated. There is no proof that Rothschild did not see more than 
one specimen.
