Migration to Zimbabwe, 1900?1965 - Tracing Machona" by Zo? R. Groves,
Springer International Publishing, ISBN: 9783030541033. It must be an old
name no longer on current maps or charts, which I have. I still don't know
just where the locality is, but evidently somewhere on the Malawi shore
after all.
