A 'contains' search in literature (not logged in) for "Norwegian Scientific Expedition to Tristan da Cunha" gets 13 results.
A 'contains' search for "tristan da cunha' gets 35 results, including the variants/abbreviations in presentation of the expedition name and some false positives for this context.

The pelecypods work by Soot-Ryen shows up in the 2nd search. There are 2 entries. I'll delete the surplus one next time I log in (they get merged by the system).
