AI is unlikely to be able to do this well any time soon. Any LLM cannot be
trusted to generate complex, new content that would require discretion
about what to include and what not to include. LLMs depend upon what they
have as input, and cannot effectively create novel text.

However, it could already be useful for double-checking and editing such
keys, and for perhaps writing them using more accessible language.
