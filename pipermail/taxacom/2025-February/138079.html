Hi Tony,

By some strange coincidence I am currently checking names of fungi (sensu lato) in Index Fungorum where an infraspecific name (e.g. variety, or subspecies) is currently listed as published before the parent species. Many have been resolved, typically because the parent species, a combination, was also published when the infraspecific name was published. This is just a consequence of inadequate 'synchronization' with early listings like Saccardo Sylloge Fungorum (late 19th to early 20th century) and Zahlbruckner's lists of lichen forming fungi (early 20th century). Confounded by some missing digitization of pertinent books / journals or lack of access to digitized content due to 'copyright'...

Onward and upward as the adage goes,

Paul
