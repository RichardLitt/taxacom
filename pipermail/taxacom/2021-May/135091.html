Just to be sure things are clear: at the end of each issue, you're given 
an actual date of *printing* for this particular issue, and an actual 
date of distribution (= publication) for the *preceding* issue.

Thus, https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58270921 indicates that 
https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58270811 was printed on 15 Feb 
1982, but the date of its distribution is to be found at the end of the 
next issue, i.e.: https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58276127 = 2 
Mar 1982.

Cheers, Laurent -
