Dear Laurent,
Thank you for pointing out that the distribution dates of one issue are to
be found in the next issue. I wrote this note, including links to pages:
---
*Bulletin du Muséum National d'Histoire Naturelle, Paris, 4e série, section
A - Zoologie, biologie et écologie animales*: Each number has a final page
stating the date when the printing of the number was concluded and the date
when the previous number was distributed (published). See example of
printing date for volume 3, number 3, on page 935 of the same, at BHL
<https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58270921>. See example of
distribution date for volume 3, number 3, on page 1228 of volume 3, number
4, at BHL <https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58276127>.
---
I keep my myriapodological dating notes online (
https://github.com/Archilegt/Myriatrix/wiki/List-of-resources-for-dating-articles-and-other-publications).
Feedback is welcome.
Kind regards,
Carlos

Just to be sure things are clear: at the end of each issue, you're given
an actual date of *printing* for this particular issue, and an actual
date of distribution (= publication) for the *preceding* issue.

Thus, https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58270921 indicates that
https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58270811 was printed on 15 Feb
1982, but the date of its distribution is to be found at the end of the
next issue, i.e.: https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/58276127 = 2
Mar 1982.

Cheers, Laurent -
