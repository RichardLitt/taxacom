Sad when a reputable peer-reviewed journal can't even follow the ICZN 
Code. Indeed, this name fails on multiple Code requirements. I presume 
there was no disclaimer in the paper that the proposed name does not 
form part of the scientific record, which would enable them to use the 
name without it even being treated as a nomen nudum. Perhaps that was 
the idea behind the use of the word "provisional".

Adam.
