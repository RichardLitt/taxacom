Yes, the question is how this will work out, in the
long term. For example it is noticeable that Tony Rees
found "Version of Record online: 14 April 2021" on
the paper itself, and a general statement by Wiley
that their Early View publications are the "Online
Version of Record", while the Early View date of the
paper is given as 02 March 2021.

It may well be that requirements need to be tightened,
but that will have to await future evaluation. It may
well be no simple matter, and perhaps an argument
to require Registration in 'botany' as well.

> Personally, I would not trust the publishers' date of publication of 
> the "version of record" to be a reliable date of publication. I regard 
> the valid date of publication to be only when the compiled version is 
> put together and published not the date on which the so-called 
> "version of record" first appears. As Stephen says, publishers often 
> change aspects of the version of record to hide errors. Sometimes 
> these are important such as omission of date of publication on article 
> (making it unavailable under the ICZN), omission of ZooBank 
> registration, changing data, even on occasion changing the actual name 
> of a new species! Given that I deal with a group of only 25,000 
> species the practice must be rife across e-publications.
>
> Another thing to be careful of is that an e-only publication may 
> appear to be validly published because it belongs to a series that 
> previously had a printed version (but no longer) or that there is no 
> valid registration of an e-archive on ZooBank. Maybe the latter has 
> been fixed but it was certainly the case until recently with at least 
> one journal.
