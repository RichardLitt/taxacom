1998 
Northern Arizona University has graciously transferred their fungus
collection to the University of Michigan Fungus Collection (MICH). The
NAU collections add to the numerous collections of mushrooms, truffles,
and false-truffles from the Intermountain Region held by Michigan. Most of
the collections were made by Jack States (Emeritus Professor).

In addition to being from a relatively under collected region, the NAU
collection contains material that was used in writing:
        States, J. S. 1990. Mushrooms and Truffles of the Southwest. Univ.
                Arizona Press, Tucson. 233 pp.
