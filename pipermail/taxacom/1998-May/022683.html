Example :
http://www.ummz.lsa.umich.edu/software/   ....Botanical Treasures....

Photographic pictures of american flowers.
The 90 images are all in tiff format, meaning that they are editable 300 dpi
bitmaps and can be opened by any word processor or image-processing program.
The package is freeware, and is freely distributable by any service, so long
as the original archive is not broken into smaller units.
