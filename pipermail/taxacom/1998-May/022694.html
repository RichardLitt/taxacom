DIN stands for "Deutsches Industrie Numer", and ISO stands for
"International Standards Organization".  All has to do with what Kodak,
or another US company, set up as ASA - American Standards Association,
for the sensitivity value of film.  For example, Kodachrome can be
purchased in ISO 25, 64 and 200.  ISO and ASA values are the same.
