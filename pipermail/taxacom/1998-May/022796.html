1998 
The Plant Resources Center of The University of Texas at Austin is pleased to announce the first number of Lundellia, a journal of botanical systematics, named in honor of the late Dr. Cyrus L. Lundell.


The contents of the first number are:

The Texas species of Glandularia (Verbenaceae) - Billie L. Turner

Systematic implications of DNA restriction site variaiton in Hymenoxys and Tetraneuris (Asteraceae, Helenieae, Gaillardiinae) - Mark Bierner & Robert K. Jansen

The Texas plant collections of Mary S. Young - Carol A. Todzia

Ocotea heribertoi (Lauraceae), a new species of rain forest canopy tree from the Isthmus of Tehuantepec, Mexico - Tom Wendt

A new species of Ageratina (Asteraceae: Eupatorieae) from southern Oaxaca, Mexico - Jose L. Panero & Jose L. Villasenor

Pavonia paneroi (Malvaceae), a new species from Puebla, Mexico - Paul A. Fryxell

A revision of Thenardia (Apocynaceae, Apocynoideae) - Justin K. Williams


This first issue is available at a cost of US$20 for individuals and US$30 for institutions.
