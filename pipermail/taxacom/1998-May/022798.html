1998 
ROYAL BOTANIC GARDENS, MELBOURNE
PACIFIC DUNLOP RESEARCH FELLOWSHIP
BRYOPHYTE SYSTEMATICS
UP TO 3-YEAR TENURE

The Royal Botanic Gardens, Melbourne (Australia)
invites applications for this research fellowship
sponsored by Pacific Dunlop Limited. Funds are
available to cover relocation expenses, a small
operating budget and a salary of AUD37,312-42,524

The Fellowship is available in the field of Bryophyte
Systematics, preferably liverworts. You must have a PhD
and experience in modern bryophyte systematics. The
Fellowship is available to start between June and
December 1999.

Other research programs at the Royal Botanic Gardens
include: systematics of vascular plants (Legumes,
Rhamnaceae and Rutaceae); systematics and ecology of
macrofungi and freshwater algae; selection and
propagation of ornamental plants; floristics of
cultivated plants; and conservation genetics.

For a position description contact Virginia Henderson
(ph: +61-3-9252 2300, fax: +61-3-9252 2350, email:
recptn at rbgmelb.org.au). For further information contact
Dr Tim Entwisle (ph: +61-3-9252 2313, fax: +61-3-9252
2350, email: tentwi at rbgmelb.org.au).

Applicants should send a proposed research program,
curriculum vitae and the names of three referees to the
Human Resources Manager, Royal Botanic Gardens,
Melbourne, Birdwood Ave, South Yarra, Victoria 3141,
Australia by 8 July 1998.
