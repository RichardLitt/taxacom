This message is to notify TAXACOM subscribers that the URL for PollyClave, a
server-based program for interactive identification using DELTA databases has
changed.  The old URL,
        dev.library.utoronto.ca/www/polyclave/,
is no longer available and cannot automatically redirect users to the new URL.

Please update your bookmark for PollyClave to the following:
        prod.library.utoronto.ca/polyclave/

Thank you, from the PollyClave crew.
