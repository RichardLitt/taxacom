Roni Robbins, colleague of Roger Bamber, tells me

... This phrase seems to come from a paper written by Joel Hedgpeth (an eminent
Pycnogonid scientist) and repeated by King & Crapp (1971) in their paper
"Littoral Pycnogonids of the British Isles".

Hedgpeth, J. W. (1963) Pycnogonida of the North American Arctic. J. Fish.
Res. Bd. Can. 20: 1315-1348.

King, P.E. & Crapp, G. B. (1971) Littoral Pycnogonids of the British Isles.
3: 455-480


For recent keys to NE Atlantic Pycnogonids see:

Bamber, R.N. (2010) Sea Spiders (Pycnogonida) of the north-east Atlantic.  Synopses of the British Fauna (New Series) No. 5 (2nd Edn).  Linnean Society of London, 249 pp.
