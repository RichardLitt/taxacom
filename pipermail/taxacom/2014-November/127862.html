The titan arum of the Liberty Hyde Bailey Conservatory at Cornell University will likely start blooming this weekend. In anticipation of this even a live web-camera has been installed at http://titanarum.cals.cornell.edu/video/. This is the same plant that bloomed in December of 2012 and short videos of that event are available at the same address.
