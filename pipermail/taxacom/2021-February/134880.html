Dear list,
 
Please find below the information on a special issue. If possible,
please also help to advertise this opportunity to fellow academics who
could be interested.
 
The "Plants" MDPI journal Special Issue "Diversity and Phylogeny of
Plantago" is now set up. Please read the announcement here:
 
https://www.mdpi.com/journal/plants/special_issues/diversity_phylogeny_plantago
 
Deadline for manuscript submissions: 31 May 2021. Manuscripts on
morphology, palynology, cytogenetics, embryology, chemistry, molecular
biology, reproductive biology, systematics, phylogeny, paleontology,
ecology, evolution and biogeography, and other related topics will all
be considered for this issue. We will also accept contributions
concerning economic aspects and uses/applications of Plantagineae
species. One of the most significant advantages of publishing in this
issue is much greater visibility as articles published in thematic
issues usually have higher citation rates.
 
With best wishes,
 
Alexey Shipunov
 
Guest Editor
