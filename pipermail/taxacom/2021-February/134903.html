Sorry, but this inflammatory rhetoric is inappropriate and reminds me of 
Pro-Trump "stop the steal" people.  Please refrain from over-the-top 
incendiary speech aimed at inflaming passion instead of engaging discussion.

The discussion should be "can we move to a more efficient model of 
getting understudied taxa described in a revolutionary way" vs "should 
we modify time-tested methods of the last 250 years to achieve the same 
goal instead?"

Keep the discussion productive and positive, PLEASE!  I do not have a 
dog in this fight, and think the discussion is needed, but not in a 
"call to battle."

Mike Ivie
