Dear all,

EDIT (European Distributed Institute of Taxonomy) is a Network of 
Excellence under the FP6 programme. More info on http://www.e-taxonomy.eu.

Work package 8 "Training and public awareness" has designed a 
questionnaire to inventory existing training courses in taxonomy in 
Europe and worldwide.

Please take 10 minutes of your time to help us gather this very 
valuable information and visit:
http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/news.php?optimurl=assessment-of-training-resources-for-taxonomy 


This survey is a unique opportunity for you to let us know about
- training courses you provide in the field of taxonomy, or,
- your opinion on training courses you have followed.

Feel free to distribute this request further. The questionnaire will 
be left open till 19 January 2007.

Do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions.

Thank you in advance for your cooperation.
With best regards,

for the WP8 team
