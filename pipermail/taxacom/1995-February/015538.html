AL GENTRY SABIACEAE MATERIAL

     Alwyn H. Gentry, of the Missouri Botanical Garden, was working on
a long-term study of the Sabiaceae when he died over a year ago.
Material for this study, including herbarium loans and his own
collections will soon be returned to the home institutions and
intercalated into the Missouri general collection.

     If anyone works on the Sabiaceae and is interested in Gentry's
material of this family before it is dispersed, they should contact
me.
