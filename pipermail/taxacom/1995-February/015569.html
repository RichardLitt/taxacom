Regarding the Leiden University collection of 4500 sketches, I would suggest
that the sketches should be scanned the earliest and store in the digital form
If you do not have the expertise or the required hardware/software, you could
get it scanned at some place where a computer and a scanner available. It won't
cost you much. Evnen if you are not able to put it in a graphic database immediately,
you can store the scanned images ion some computer media (floppy/hard disk,
cartridge etc. ). Later you can think of putting in into a graphic database.
I would strongly recommend immediate scanning of the sketches as they are
very valuable information.
