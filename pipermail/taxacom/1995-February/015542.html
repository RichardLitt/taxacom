There is a jar opener that attaches under a cabinet and opens multiple
sizes of jars.  I have not personally used it, but I know of others who
have, and are delighted with the ease of use.  I have seen it advertised
in catalogues like "Improvements" and those other junk mailings we all
receive by the bushel each month.  It is not very expensive either.  You
simply slide the cover into the opener and twist the bottom of the jar
while the top remains in position.  Perhaps this would work for you.
