Greetings...

We are beginning the planning for undertaking a conservation survey of our
various collections here at Rancho Santa Ana Botanic Garden.  This will
include our living collection on the grounds, as well as our germplasm
collection, botanical library, and extensive herbarium.  As Administrative
Curator of the herbarium, I am interested in the experiences of other
herbaria who have undergone this sort of a survey.  I would especially like
to hear from those who have performed these surveys for herbaria.
Responses from people in other disciplines would be welcome too, of course!

This message has been cross-posted on TAXACOM and NHCOLL-L.  General
comments on conservation surveys of collections may be of interest to
others and could be posted to the list(s), or responses may be sent to me
directly.

Any insights you folks could provide would be most helpful.

Thanks

Steve Boyd
