HI MikeAn alternative is to upload your scanned pdfs to the Biodiversity Literature Repository and make them accessible this way (https://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/) . This allows you to upload all the pdf and generally make them accessible up to 2000. The policy is explained on the home page.This has the additional advantage that you get a DOI that you can attach to the bibliographic references in your future articles and thus save your colleagues a lot of work to find the pdf of the cited work.BLR is a community of CERN's Zenodo archive,  and jointly organized by Plazi and Pensoft. Depending on the keywords you attach, your make the uploads part of a growing number of articles and illustrations covering biodiversity. https://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/search?page=1&size=20 .
