Hi Donat,You've made arguments about copyright and images that you judge to be compelling, I'm not particularly convinced by them. This doesn't mean that I'm on the side of the "big players". Nor does this mean I'm against open access, quite the opposite. I too want open access to information and knowledge, and, like you, feel that I am making a contribution towards that goal.Everybody is free to evaluate your arguments, decide for themselves whether the arguments are compelling, what the potential risks are, and act accordingly.
