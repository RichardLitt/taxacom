This may interest those involved with collections.

The White House Office of Science and Technology Policy just released a blog about the work of the Interagency Working Group on Scientific Collections, the information Clearinghouse with agency policies for their collections, and the registry of US Federal Scientific Collections.
https://www.whitehouse.gov/blog/2016/12/09/finding-federal-scientific-collections-just-got-easier-1

The general registry for global scientific collections is available for use at:
http://grscicoll.org/
