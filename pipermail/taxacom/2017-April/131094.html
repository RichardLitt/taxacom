Dear Taxacomers,

 
Interested in science education, innovative e-learning, biodiversity and climate change?

 

 
 

Then join us at the BIOTALENT EU Conference: Tackling biodiversity challenges through innovative e-learning, a one-day event presenting the Erasmus+ BIOTALENT educational project. Various experts in science education will present innovative ways in which science and environmental education can contribute to conserving biodiversity.

 

 
 

Moderated by biologist, writer and journalist Dirk Draulans, a panel discussion will touch upon a variety of topics, ranging from the use of natural history collections as an educational resource to the competencies needed for the labour market.

 

 
 

Join us also for an exciting lunch featuring some creepy-crawly delights and an on-site guided visit to the Poison exhibition with a sting in its tail!

 

 
 

Please spread the word on this event by forwarding this invitation to fellow educators and any others who might be interested. 

 

 
 

When: May 18th, 2017

 

Where: Museum of Natural Sciences <http://www.naturalsciences.be> , Brussels

 

More info and Programme here <http://biotalent.myspecies.info/content/eu-conference> 

 

Free Registration: please register here <http://biotalent.myspecies.info/content/register-tackling-biodiversity-challenges-through-innovative-e-learning#overlay-context=content/eu-conference> 

 

Registration deadline: 5 May 

 

 
 

See you there!
