Those who have been following the discussion of regulation of Digital Sequence Information may be interested in this article:

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2211912420300651 - Published in Global Food Security journal.

"Greater equity in use of GSD will not come from ABS schemes, but through greater cooperation and capacity building. In this context, it is key to assess the needs of developing countries to ensure an effective access to and use of GSD, which should be based on a strategic plan defining priorities and involving all relevant (public and private) stakeholders."
