REQUEST FOR CELTIS MATERIAL

Mr. Ali Sattarian, PhD student in Plant Taxonomy, is requesting samples of 2-3 young-mature leaves on silica gel particularly of African Celtis species (Ulmaceae/Celtidaceae) for molecular research. Other species are welcome too.Voucher specimens preferably with flowers and fruits are also wanted. 

Please send the samples to:

Biosystematics Group, Nationaal Herbarium Nederland, Wageningen University Branch, Wageningen University, Gen. Foulkesweg 37, 6703 BL, Wageningen, The Netherlands.

Your co-operation is solicited.

Prof. dr. L.J.G. van der Maesen

email: jos.vanderMaesen at wur.nl
