FAUNA CARPATHICA MEETING
will be held in High Tatras Mts (Slovakia) at June 4-5, 2003

Objectives of the meeting:
- Presentation of local resources at governmental and institution level
(research institutions in charge of biodiversity), projects on
biodiversity of Carpathian fauna (realised, running as well as planned
for future)
- Introduction to general survey of Carpathian fauna diversity
- Identification of taxonomic resources (experts, database, literatures)
on Carpathian fauna and the gaps in knowledge, particularly in
check-lists.
- Presentation of the Fauna Europaea project goals, its tools and
results in Carpathian countries
- Assessment of gaps in knowledge of Carpathian fauna
- Establishment of links between leading institutions dealing with
Carpathian fauna
...

All relevant information and registration form is available at
http://www.fns.uniba.sk/~kzo/faeu/carpathian.htm

Please, forward this message to all relevant persons.
