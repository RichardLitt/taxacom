Dear biologists,
Many thanks to everybody that helped me with the identification of my
plants from the Costa Rican paramo at Cerra de la Muerte!

They were:
1. Castilleja talamancensis (Scrophulariaceae or now rather
Orobanchaceae). Found in Costa Rica and Panama.
http://arbetsplats.slu.se:4032/Shared%20Documents/Castilleja.jpg

3. Chaetolepis cufodontisii (Melastomataceae). Endemic to Costa Rica.
http://arbetsplats.slu.se:4032/Shared%20Documents/Melastomataceae.jpg

Many thanks for your help!
