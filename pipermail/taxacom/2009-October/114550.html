It is with great sadness that I write to you to tell you that our old
friend and fellow-malacologist, Dr. Jorgen Knudsen, passed away last
night. He had a bladder infection, which he had had several times
during the last couple of years, so nobody thought it was serious. But
this time his body did not respond to the medication and the bacterial
count just continued to rise, and then his vital organs shut down.

Please pass the message to those who knew Jorgen Knudsen.

Best regards,
Kathe
