I also agree with Vladimir and Mary, especially Mary's suggestion re the 
online availability of such a resource.  Online there's a greater 
opportunity to update with new information, not to mention provide 
access to current questions and their answers that might be associated 
with individual tools. There's actually quite a tradition of 
well-formatted and useful online user manuals in some communities (e.g. 
R), so that publishing such a review (user meta-manual?) online has many 
precedents. Such manuals can be downloaded and printed by those who like 
hardcopy, or used online, where the links (and animations, videos, 
etc.?) they contain will function.

---TAD.
