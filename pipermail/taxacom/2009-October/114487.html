The International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN), acting
in 
the capacity of adviser and arbiter, assists the zoological community 
through generation and dissemination of information on the correct use
of 
the scientific names of animals.

This includes the publication of the Bulletin of Zoological Nomenclature

containing applications to ( http://www.iczn.org/Cases.htm ), and
rulings by 
( http://www.iczn.org/Opinions.htm ), the Commission. We welcome input
from 
the scientific community on published applications in the form of
Comments ( 
http://www.iczn.org/Comments_on_Cases.htm ); Comments sent to the ICZN 
Secretariat are considered by the Commissioners in their deliberations.

To help readers keep track of Cases currently open for input, we have 
developed a list of open Cases, now available here: 
http://www.iczn.org/List_of_open_cases.html .  We will update this
monthly.

We look forward to your contributions to this core process in achieving 
sense and stability for animal names.
