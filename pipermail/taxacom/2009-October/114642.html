> "ALPHABETICAL LIST OF PLANT FAMILIES WITH ASSOCIATED ACRONYMS (2009)"
>
> They are not ACRONYMS, but they are abbreviations. An acronym is a  
> word formed by the first letter or several letters of a string of  
> words.   RADAR and SCUBA are acronyms. Your abbreviations are not all  
> acronyms. For example, Balanitaceae BLT, Balanopaceae BLN,  
> Balanophoraceae BNP, Balsaminaceae BLS, are not acronyms.  They are  
> not words. They do not have both vowels and consonants.
