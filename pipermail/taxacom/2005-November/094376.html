At 05:10 PM 11/23/2005 +0200, you wrote:
>1. Is'nt a cladogram a hypothesis of relationships AND of character state
>distributions?

A cladogram is an explanatory hypothesis - it is the product of an
inferential act where one causally accounts for shared similarities as
effects of common ancestry, or by way of other events, i.e., homoplasy.
