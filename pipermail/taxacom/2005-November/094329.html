I've tweaked iSpecies (http://ispecies.org) to now display NCBI
LinkOut's (if they exist).

In English this means that if NCBI knows about other web sites that are
relevant to a taxon, iSpecies will show you these. In a few cases the
LinkOuts are erroneous, but in general they often lead to much useful
information (e.g., the excellent amphibian resources you discover if
you try
http://darwin.zoology.gla.ac.uk/~rpage/ispecies/?q=Rana+palmipes)
