No sloppy excuses for sloppy semantics please. Purpose is a standard
language permeating modern biology and it is the same language of
intelligent design. Purpose should have no place in evolutionary
biology, yet it does. If it's just 'sloppy' semantics it would seem
there are an awful lot of sloppy scientists being published by a whole
lot of sloppy prestigious journals. Who is to say that they are not all
really closet intelligent designers?
