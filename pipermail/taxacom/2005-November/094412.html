2005 
Hope this is an appropriate group to announce that the "World Ichneumonoidea
2004. Taxonomy, biology, morphology and distribution" by Dicky S. Yu, Kees
van Achterberg (Braconidae) and Klaus Horstmann (Ichneumonidae), an
interactive database on CD/DVD, is now available. Ichneumonoidea is the
largest superfamily in the order Hymenoptera, and has 40,936 valid species.
Please visit www.taxapad.com <http://www.taxapad.com/>  for details and
browse the list of 114,506 scientific names with their respective valid
names.
