Dear all,
We have recently initiated a project studying genetic variation of North American populations of exotic sickleweed (Falcaria vulgaris, Apiaceae) compared to that in its native range in Eurasia. We are seeking kind volunteers/collaborators who could send us DNA samples (leaf tissue stored in silica gel) from their areas. If you could help us, please let us know and we will  contact you on how we could provide  you with silica gel baggies, paid postage envelops and instruction on sample collection.  We will acknowledge you in any publication resulting from this project.

Sincerely,

Madhav Nepal and Gary Larson
South Dakota State University Herbarium
