We are currently recruiting for the following post in the Herbarium at
Kew. Please could you circulate these details to anyone who may be
interested.

Best wishes
Dave Simpson

Royal Botanic Gardens Kew

POST
Poales Systematist, Ref: 757
HOURS OF WORK
Full-time
JOB STATUS:
Permanent
SALARY PER ANNUM:
Salary is dependent on experience and starts from £20,085 to £40,000
DEPARTMENT:
Herbarium, Monocots Systematic Teams and Associated Units
JOB DETAILS:
You will research the systematics of grasses and related groups,
developing a world-class research programme with Kew and national and
international collaborators. You will also assist in the day-to-day
curation of the Commelinid collections and, wherever possible, seek out
and secure funding opportunities.
ESSENTIALS:
You will have a PhD of equivalent in a relevant subject along with
expertise in plant and morphological and molecular systematic
techniques. Excellent organisational and interpersonal skills are a must
along with the ability to make sound judgements and confidence when
working with colleagues.
CLOSING DATE:
27 May 2004

Further details and and application form can be found at:
http://www.kew.org/aboutus/jobs/REF757.html
