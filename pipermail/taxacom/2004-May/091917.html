2004 
The Leiden branch of the Nationaal Herbarium Nederland has a 4-years PhD
position available to study the taxonomy, phylogeography and conservation
status of European populations of Viola persicifolia and allies. In
addition to research, the PhD student is also expected to teach botany to
students of biology at Leiden University, The Netherlands.

Applicants should have a MSc in Biology, a good knowledge of European
vascular plants, experience in fieldwork, greenhouse experiments,
taxonomic/phylogenetic/phenetic methods and molecular techniques (DNA
sequencing, AFLPs).

Salary ranges from 1.702 EURO gross in the first year up to 2.283 EURO
gross in the fourth year when working full-time.

A detailed project description can be obtained from Prof.Dr. Pieter Baas
(baas at nhn.leidenuniv.nl) or Dr. Barbara Gravendeel
(gravendeel at nhn.leidenuniv.nl).

Individuals interested in this research opportunity should email
m.vanschoonhoven at fwnbur.leidenuniv.nl or send a letter of application to
Universiteit Leiden, Faculteit Wiskunde en Natuurwetenschappen, attn. M.
van Schoonhoven, Dienst Personeel en Organisatie, Einsteinweg 55, 2333 CC,
Leiden, The Netherlands. Emails and letters should be sent before the 30th
of May 2004, mention the vacancy code NHN-L/PCNE, and contain a curriculum
vitae and the names and addresses of two references.
