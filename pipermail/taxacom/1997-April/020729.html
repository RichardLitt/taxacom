For everyone on the Internet with an interest or potential interest in
agricultural or useful plants, the URL should be booked in your Internet
browser:
   http://www.ars-grin.gov/npgs/tax/taxfam.html
The above URL takes you directly to the taxonomic data in the Germplasm
Informations Network (GRIN) of USDA Agricultural Research Service.  The
following information was copied and pasted directly from there.  Please
note that the native range of Pachyrhizus erosus is in Mexico and Central
America as far south as Costa Rica, and the native range of P. tuberosus
is in SOuth America.  Both are cutlivated, and both, unfortunately, are
referred to by the common name jicama, amongst many.

Taxon: Pachyrhizus erosus (L.) Urban


Genus: Pachyrhizus
Family: Fabaceae alt. Leguminosae. Also placed in: Papilionaceae
Nomen number: 26286.
Name verified on 28-Oct-1988 by Systematic Botany Laboratory. Last
updated: 23-Aug-1994.
Species priority site is: Southern Regional PI Station (S9). 10
accessions (by country).
Protologue: Symb. antill. 4:311. 1905.

Common names:

     jicama
     potato-bean
     yam-bean


Uses for taxon:

     Environmental uses: soil improver (green manure)
     Human food: vegetable (fide Pap Agric Univ Wageningen 90-3:6. 1990)
     Animal food: forage (fide Pap Agric Univ Wageningen 90-3:6. 1990)
     Vertebrate poisons: mammals (fide Lampe and McCann 1985)

Species range:

Chiapas, Mexico; Guanajuato, Mexico; Guerrero, Mexico; Jalisco, Mexico;
Michoacan, Mexico;
Morelos, Mexico; Oaxaca, Mexico; Puebla, Mexico; San Luis Potosi, Mexico;
Veracruz, Mexico;
Costa Rica [northwest]; El Salvador; Guatemala [central & west]; Honduras
[west]; Nicaragua
[west]; cult. or natzd. in trop. areas worldwide
