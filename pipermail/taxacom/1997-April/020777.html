Following up on recent electronic publication threads here:
For an interesting perspective on accessibility of electronic media , from
the POV of an eastern European research chemist, see the most recent
(May-June 1997) issue of "American Scientist"  for the article "Mozart, Ford
and the Information-Superhighway Paradox", pp. 207-209.  The "paradox"
involves the need to increase accessibility of data while at the same time
ensuring an adequate return to those who create it.
