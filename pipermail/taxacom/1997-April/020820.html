1997 
The Systematics Association
First Biennial International Conference
St Anne's College, Oxford
19-21 August 1997


Second Announcement
The Systematics association announces the first of a new biennial series of
international conferences to provide a forum where students, amateurs and
professional systematists can present their research and discuss topics of
interest.

The conferences will be designed to cover broad subject areas of
systematists working on a wide range of organisms and using different
techniques. They are particularly intended to enable systematists at an
early stage in their career to present their work to a diverse,
professional audience.
