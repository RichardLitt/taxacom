Dear Taxacomers,

Those of you interested in tiger beetles and/or insect conservation
might be interested in visiting a recently "finished" website I
created. The site is part of a Nature Conservancy project that was focused
on assessing  the conservation status of Connecticut's (USA) tiger beetle species.

the URL:
http://viceroy.eeb.uconn.edu/CTB/home.html

The site has keys to species, dozens of high-quality color images and
data for the 14 species known from CT.  I welcome feedback.

Sincerely,
Derek Sikes
