We have in the herbarium a collection of about 30 old wall charts showing
flower structure, anatomy, pollination, etc. from various families.  The

3' in size.  They bear the name Fung, Koch, Quentell'sche Neue Wandtafeln

artists who produced them.  Does anyone know for instance if the Darmstadt
company still exists?
