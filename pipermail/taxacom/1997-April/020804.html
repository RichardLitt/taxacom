1997 
Due to renovations of the Carnegie Museum of Natural History
herbarium (CM) which resulted in the cases being inaccessible,
outgoing loans and return of borrowed material from CM have been
delayed.  We have now moved back into our renovated quarters and
are starting to process loan requests received during this period.
New requests for loans can also sent at this time and specimens
will be promptly sent.

We apologize for any inconveniences caused by this delay in
responding to loan requests and in returning borrowed specimens.
