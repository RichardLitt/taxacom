>Some weeks ago James Solomon asked about UTM / greenwich degrees conversions.
>
>Pierre Rasmont made them in a simple program 'UTM' and 'MTU' some years ago,
>published in:
>
>Rasmont, P. & J. Andre, 1989. Applications d'un logiciel de projection U.T.M.
>a la surveillance des invertebres. - Inventaires de Fauna et de Flore 53:
>227-225 (Museum Paris).
>
>In the program Carto Fauna Flora, particularly the menu shell recently issued
>(CFF-edit) there is also a conversion utility for various grid types.
