RESEARCH ASSISTANT, non-tenure track 12-month position with half-time
appointment of 20 hours per week, available September 1, 2001. Salary
$13,500-14,500 plus benefits.

B.S. or B.A. in entomology or similar field of organismal biology.
Broad training in insect taxonomy, familiarity with taxonomic
literature and library use, knowledge of general insect collecting
and curation techniques. Excellent verbal English, writing, and
typing skills. Familiarity with Macintosh and Windows computer
systems, and Microsoft Word.

Duties include collection, curation, and identification of insects,
particularly Hymenoptera. Collection may include local or long
distance trips, curation may include specialized techniques such as
making slides or preparing specimens for SEM examination. Other
responsibilities include databasing of specimens, word processing,
library research, and purchase and upkeep of laboratory supplies.
