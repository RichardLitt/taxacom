Colleagues,

Articles and volumes within the journals "Pacific Insects" and 
"Pacific Insects Monographs" are now available online as pdf files on 
the Bishop Museum's web site. These two journals were started by the 
late J. Linsley Gressitt as a medium for the umbrella project 
"Zoogeography and Evolution of Pacific Insects". Pacific Insects (its 
name changed to International Journal of Entomology in 1983) was 
published from 1959-1985. Pacific Insects Monographs was published 
from 1961-1986.

A few of the articles and volumes are not yet available as pdf files. 
As we continue our digitization project for Bishop Museum Press, we 
will fill in these gaps and hope to have the remaining articles and 
volumes available soon.

Pacific Insects Online:
http://hbs.bishopmuseum.org/pi/

Pacific Insects Monographs Online:
http://hbs.bishopmuseum.org/pim/

Aloha,
