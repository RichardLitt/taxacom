Dear TDWG Supporters

This is a call for general presentations at TDWG 2007 for the open sessions
on Thursday September 20. We would welcome any presentations relating to the
work of Biodiversity Information Standards (TDWG) on this day. 

The morning sessions (Integrating biodiversity data) are chaired by Jessie
Kennedy (jessie(at)nesc.ac.uk) and Renato De Giovanni
(renato(at)cria.org.br).

The afternoon sessions (The Application of TDWG standards) are chaired by
Stinger Guala (Stinger(at)stingersplace.com) and Stan Blum
(sblum(at)calacademy.org).

If you would like to present your work, please contact the Session Chairs or
submit an abstract directly via instructions at

http://www.tdwg.org/conference2007/abstracts/.

NOTE: Early registrations and abstract submissions close 2400 UTC August 15.

The full schedule is being populated at

http://www.tdwg.org/conference2007/programme/schedule/

Best Wishes
