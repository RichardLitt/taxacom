Professorship at MNHN

The Mus?um d?Histoire Naturelle de Paris (France) will be recruiting, 
during the next few years, several Professors of different levels in the 
fields of Systematics and Evolution of Plants (including Bryophytes, 
Pteridophytes and Phanerogams). These positions will be located at the 
Paris Museum with a strong link to the herbarium. No teaching will be 
required (although it is possible) but part of the Professors? 
activities will be devoted to the management of the herbarium. As is 
usual in France, these positions offer direct tenure.
Any person interested please contact us rapidly as the process of 
recruitment in France is quite complex and includes an a priori national 
procedure.
