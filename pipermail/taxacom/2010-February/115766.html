Dear all,

Further to my last post, the following list of genus level misspellings for fossil bivalves in the online version of J.J. Sepkoski's compendium of fossil marine genera is given by the authors of a paper by Krug et al., 2007, and is available online at http://geosci.uchicago.edu/pdfs/jablonski/ContrarianData_BivalveGenera.xls (I have extracted the relevant misspellings for your interest, so you can see the types of errors in an example real world list, for Bivalvia alone):
