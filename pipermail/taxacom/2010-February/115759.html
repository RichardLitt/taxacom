a further example of the decline of taxonomy in N.Z. today concerns an undescribed Phytophthora pathogen which is causing a sudden?dieback?of our iconic Kauri trees, see http://www.google.co.nz/search?hl=en&source=hp&q=Kauri+Phytophthora&meta=&aq=f&oqdespite all the press coverage, and the fact that everybody now has to disinfect their boots after visiting kauri forests, nobody will fund the taxonomy of the Phytophthora to give it an official name! And this despite the NZ$19m/4 years publicly funded IO2 OBI to document the N.Z. land biota, from which we have seen rather little output in the last couple of years!
