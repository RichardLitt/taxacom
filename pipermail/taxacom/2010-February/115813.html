Dear butterfly biologists everywhere!

The deadline for lower-cost registration is coming soon for the 6th  
International Conference on the Biology of Butterflies. Held this  
year during June 29 - July 2 at the University of Alberta, this  
meeting has taken place at irregular intervals since 1981 and  
recognizes the role that the study of butterflies has played in our  
understanding of both evolutionary biology and ecology.

Please view the Conference webpage at:  http:// 
www.biology.ualberta.ca/biobutterfly2010

Deadline for lower-cost Registration:            March 31, 2010
Deadline for Abstract submission:            May 17, 2010
