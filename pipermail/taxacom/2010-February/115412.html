Have you ever read a daily taxonomic newspaper? Here it is: Zootaxa

 

http://www.mapress.com/zootaxa/collections/nova/index.html

 

If you use social networking things such as Twitter, you will find Zootaxa
updates of new contents everyday at:

 

http://twitter.com/zootaxa

 

Those have a twitter accounts can use mobile phone to receive these 140
letters headines every day, automatically reaching them (rss).

 

It would be even a better newspaper if all the authors would pay the little
page charge so we are not only aware that they publish, but what they do.

 

It would be even better, if Zoobank would be ready to accommodate all the
new names and continue on building the GBIF sponsored work to register all
the names.
