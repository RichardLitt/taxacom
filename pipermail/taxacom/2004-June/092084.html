Pierre's inference above is correct in that my concern for polymorphic
characters is for those characters where outgroup character states
occuring in just some of the outgroup species and are also found within
the in group. The question then becomes one of whether the pattern is
due to plesiomorphy or parallelism as has been pointed out and
algorithms are one way one might present an argument either way.
