Some of us have gotten email from BMC Evolutionary Biology, an
all-electronic journal, touting free access and quick turn-around and etc.
See http://www.biomedcentral.com/bmcevolbiol
<http://www.biomedcentral.com/bmcevolbiol>  for info.

If you look through the web site closely enough, you will find that they
charge authors US$525 per article unless their institution has paid a
membership in BioMedCentral.

I recommend the totally free Phyloinformatics http://phyloinformatics.org/
<http://phyloinformatics.org/>  instead.
