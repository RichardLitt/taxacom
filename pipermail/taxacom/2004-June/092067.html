2004 
The University of Amsterdam is seeking candidates for a full
professorship in Systematic and Geographic Zoology. The search
committee is especially interested in candidates with the ambition to
link expertise in systematics to new developments (for instance
advances in molecular phylogenetic reconstruction, evo-devo, the
comparative method, biodiversity informatics, biodiversity
assessment).
If you would like to know more about the position yourself, or would
like to suggest suitable candidates, please send an e-mail to Peter
van Tienderen at tienderen at science.uva.nl, preferably before 18 June
2004.
