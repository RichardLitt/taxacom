2004 
The Conservatoire et Jardin botaniques of Geneva is pleased to announce
that a database (Sistema de Informaci?n Bot?nico Flora del Paraguay,
SIB-FdP)
containing all taxa published so far in the series Flora of Paraguay is now
available online (http://www.ville-ge.ch/cjb/bd/fdp/index.html).  The
database aims to provide a solid basis for the further development of
botanical knowledge on Paraguay and provides interactive links to
taxonomical, morphological and biogeographical data about all species
treated.  Keys for genera and species, and critical literature related to
the different taxonomic groups are also included. The data has been
standardized according to the strict editorial guidelines used for the
published volumes of the flora. All comments or recommendations about the
use and content of the current version of this new database are very
welcome and should be addressed to: florapara at cjb.ville-ge.ch
