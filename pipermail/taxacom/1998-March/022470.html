Scrophulariaceae, has just been published and is available from the

Kraft of the Smithsonian Institution Libraries at: nkraft at sil.si.edu.  If email
is not available, which he prefers, then call him at 202-357-1994 or write
to him at the Smithsonian Institution Libraries, MRC 154,  Smithsonian
Institution, Washington, DC 20560.
