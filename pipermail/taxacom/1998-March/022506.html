Just a note to inform that the following paper IS NOT recognized by Croizat
as representing his own ideas, so that's not a good starting point to
understand Croizat's theory  (v. Croizat, L., 1982. Vicariance/Vicariism,
Panbiogeography, "Vicariance Biogeography," etc.: A Clarification.
Systematic Zoology 31:291-304; for the reason why):


Croizat, L., G. Nelson, and D. E. Rosen, 1974. Centers of Origins and
Related Concepts. Systematic Zoology 23: 265-287;
