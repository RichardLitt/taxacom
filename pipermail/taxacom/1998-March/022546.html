1998 
Monocts II - 27th September to 2nd October 1998. Sydney, Australia.

The Second International Conference on the Comparative Biology of the
Monocotyledons, and the Third International Symposium on Grass Systematics
and Evolution

STOP PRESS - ACCOMMODATION

Intending participants should note the following on availability of
accommodation in Sydney.



Unfortunately both the Barker Lodge Motor Inn and the Gemini Hotel are now
full for the conference period.  Rooms are still available at the other
places.  Additional rooms have now been booked at the following
establishments.  Booking forms for THESE NEW establishments should be sent
(or faxed) directly to Professor Chris Quinn along with registration
forms.  Delegates will still be responsible for settling their
accommodation expenses directly with the proprietor.

Kensington NSW 2033
Fax to UNSW on 61-2-9385 1558

Single room only $89
Double/twin $95
Triple $110

or divan.  Restuarant; refrigerator and cooking facilities; undercover
parking.
10 minutes walk to UNSW; on bus route to city. Locality map - at bend in
Anzac Parade between the words "Kensington" and "Anzac".
