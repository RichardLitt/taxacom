At 4:14 PM -0600 3/25/98, Neil Harriman wrote:
>Ladies and Gentlemen:  Word is, The Compleat Cladist is the best beginner's
>book of its kind.  I have a graduate student who really needs a copy, and
>it is now out of print. Can anyone help?  Many thanks.
>
Diana Lipscomb has produced a short text, "Basics of Cladistic Analysis,"
for her own course in systematic biology.  It covers most of the same
ground as the "Compleat Cladist" and is succinct, readable and
understandable.  She has indicated that it may be available over the WWW.
She can be contacted at biodl at gwu.edu.
