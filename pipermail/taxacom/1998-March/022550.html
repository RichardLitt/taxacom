1998 
     The New York Botanical Garden has added a new set of pages to its website,
     entitled: "Employment Opportunities at the New York Botanical Garden
     Herbarium" (http://www.nybg.org/bsci/herb/herb2.html).  At this site, those
     interested can find out about positions that are currently available,
     general information about the New York Botanical Garden, an overview of the
     current herbarium staff and the kind of work they do, employee benefits and
     links to websites that deal with the New York Metropolitan area.

     Currently we have positions available for curatorial assistants in the
     Cryptogamic Herbarium and summer interns both the Phanerogamic and
     Cryptogamic Herbaria.
