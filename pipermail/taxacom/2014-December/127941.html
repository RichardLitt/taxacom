You're invited!

to an iDigBio-hosted *Webinar*: Overview of the GBIF IPT Installation 
and Set up
*When: *January 7th, 2015 from NOON to 2 PM EST
*Where:* http://idigbio.adobeconnect.com/ipt/
*Presenters: * Laura Russell (VertNet), Kyle Braak (GBIF) and Alberto

Have you ever wondered what the "IPT" is? (that's Integrated Publishing 
Toolkit, brought to you by GBIF). Would you like to learn more about the

software package? What are the requirements to set one up? What are the 
advantages? What are the questions you need to be prepared to ask your 
IT staff? If you are the IT staff, what are your questions for the 
webinar hosts? Find out the answers to all your questions - mark your 
calendars! See you there.

for the: Data Standards, Data Sharing, and Demystifying the Integrated 
Publishing Toolkit (IPT) Workshop 
<https://www.idigbio.org/content/workshop-data-standards-data-sharing-and-demystifying-integrated-publishing-toolkit-ipt> 
being held jointly in Gainesville, Florida and Ottawa, Canada January
