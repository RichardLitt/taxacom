Taxacomers,

The Algonquin to Adirondacks Collaborative <http://www.a2alink.org>
facilitates a network of partners to connect lands and people across  
the Algonquin to Adirondacks region of Ontario & New York  
<http://www.a2alink.org/uploads/7/6/8/5/7685208/1904164_orig.jpg?330>,  
a critical link for biodiversity and ecological resilience in eastern  
North America. We are building a database of conservation and  
connectivity research and resources to help inform the conservation  
activities of our partner organizations  
<http://www.a2alink.org/partners.html>. Papers can be peer-reviewed or  
not, and topics can range from landscape conservation to ecology,  
sociology, stewardship, and beyond. Please submit any relevant  
research, in PDF form or as citations, to Lilith Wyatt  
<coordinator.a2a at gmail.com> ideally by Friday, January 9, 2015. For  
more detail see full call-out here
<http://www.a2alink.org/uploads/7/6/8/5/7685208/a2a_request_for_relevant_research_nov_2014.pdf>.
