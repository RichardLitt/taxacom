Assistant Professor of Ecology/Evolution/Systematic Biology 

Employer: Department of Biological Sciences, National Sun Yat-Sen University
Location: Kaohsiung, Taiwan 
(Google map: http://goo.gl/FcbFV9)
Discipline: Ecology, Evolution, Systematic Biology 

Position Type: Full Time 

Job Type: Faculty 


Further information 


Applications are invited for a tenure-track Assistant Professor
 position in Biology starting August 2015. Candidates must have a PhD in 
Biology with a specialization in Ecology, Evolution or Systematic 
Biology, preferable with postdoctoral research experience, and a teaching and publication track record 
commensurate with experience. We seek a highly trained individual who can demonstrate excellence in
 teaching and research, working with a culturally diverse student 
population. Responsibilities include teaching undergraduate and graduate
 courses, including, non-science majors courses, introductory courses, and advanced courses in Ecology, Evolution and Systematic 
Biology, and supervising 
undergraduate research.
 

To Apply: The application deadline is 10, January, 2015. The required application documents include a
 letter of application, curriculum vitae, teaching philosophy,
 description of research interest, and 3 letters of reference. The electronic version (pdf) should be submitted to Prof. Dr. Minna Hsu (hsumin at mail.nsysu.edu.tw) by our deadline. The hard copy is also needed, but it can be sent separately via airmail to our department address:

Prof. Dr. Minna Hsu, Department of Biological Sciences, National Sun Yat-Sen University, 70, Lien-Hai Road, Kaohsiung 804, TAIWAN

Please indicate your name on all application materials. If 
sending multiple files as attachments, please label all files with your 
first and last name. 

All qualified applicants will receive consideration for 
employment without regard to race, age, color, religion, sex, national 
origin, or disability.
