If we try to establish "prevailing recent usage", since 2000 Google Scholar
has 81 hits for "anura dumeril" versus 338 for "anura fischer". This may
change, perhaps, with a more recent start date, per the more recent
publications by Dubois... On the other hand, it would be interesting to
establish how many folk here or elsewhere agree with Dubois' analysis of
the situation, also bearing in mind that Dum?ril apparently did not use the
name in the same form (i.e., he used Anoures, not Anura).
