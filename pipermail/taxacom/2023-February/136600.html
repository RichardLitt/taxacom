It might for now, but I also have some (other) genus names plus names at
other ranks without authorship, so it is not a general solution...

Separately, I have just remembered that the principle alluded to above of
"prevailing recent usages" applies to the name itself (where multiple
variants are in use), not to the ascribed authorship; if the latter is
wrong, it can simply be corrected; so my example counts previously given
via e.g. Google Scholar are a misdirection...

That said, my present inclination is to follow Dubois (2019, 2020) and
ascribe authorship of Anura to Dum?ril (1805 not 1806, thanks Neal), but
would be open to being persuaded otherwise if there are alternative views
around; also to update the Wikipedia and Wikispecies entries to read the
same (also maybe Wikidata, though not too familiar with that as yet).

Regards - Tony
