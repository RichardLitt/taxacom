Also there exists the 2019 paper by Dubois and Ohler, in which the authors
contend that:

"the nomina used by Fischer (1813a) for the frogs and the salamanders were
not new nomina, but explicit subsequent usages, without any change in rank,
of the nomina of Dum?ril (1805)".

If this is correct, then the Dubois (2020) usage would be correct, and the
Blackburn & Wake, 2011 usage wrong.

Reference is Dubois, A., & Ohler, A. (2019). The nomina Anura, Urodela,
Ecaudata and Caudata, credited to ?Fischer von Waldheim, 1813?, do not
exist, with comments on the nomenclature of higher zoological taxa and on
the authorships and dates of other amphibian nomina. Bionomina, 14(1), 1-68
(full text available via ResearchGate).
