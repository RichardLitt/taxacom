 Tony said "I realise that the ICZN Code does not regulate names and/or cited?authorships above the rank of family (or more correctly, family-group),?however I was wondering whether anyone can assist me with the "best" i.e.?most correct authorship for the amphibian order Anura."
I say: There is no "correct" authorship, precisely because it is not regulated by the ICZN Code. There are bibliographic facts about where and when the name appeared, but these facts have no relevance to taxonomy or nomenclature. Priority does not apply. I do hate it when people fixate on irrelevant details!
Cheers, Stephen
