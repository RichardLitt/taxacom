What a pity! I was already looking forward to see an incorrectly formed 
-opsidae name, and now this...

But Ceratopsidae saves it:

Ceratopsidae Marsh, 1888, based on Ceratops Marsh, 1888, original 
spelling formed not in accord with Art. 29.1.
