Returning to the subject of my original post - The AMNH Amphibian Species
of the World database, version 6.1 as at today, has this to say about
"Anura" and apparently related names (subset of a very large number of
names above family rank applicable to frogs). For some reason that I cannot
imagine, the authors of this system decline to give an authorship for
"Anura" as an accepted order. So, once again I throw this back to the
community of Taxacom for any advice and assistance that may be available in
selecting a preferred authorship for "Anura"...

> Yes Stephen, one could do that, with a certain amount of re-engineering
> (not under my control any more in this case)... however 99%+ of the time
> the "name+author+year" system does work (which is why it has been adopted
> as the de facto standard in virtually all taxonomic data systems as well as
> the taxonomic literature) - e.g. in the example given above, the crustacean
> order is Decapoda Latreille, 1802 while the cephalopod order is Decapoda
> Leach, 1818 (now Teuthoidea - or possibly Teuthida - Naef, 1916 (Teuthida
> is accepted in ITIS at this time but not in WoRMS, more on that would be a
> separate thread...)
>
> Go e.g. to ION/Zoological Record and you will find "name+author+year" as
> de facto taxon identifiers, same in Neave, same in Global Names Index, same
> in ITIS, same in GBIF, etc. etc. At least some attempt at standardization
> in these matters is clearly helpful :) There are a few instances where such
> a construct is not unique, where indeed some workarounds are required -
> e.g. in my case I tend to use name|| ||authority, name|| ||authority|| [2]
> and so on, others may have their own preference...
