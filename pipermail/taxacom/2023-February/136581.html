> Interesting question. With respect to "how many known only from the type",
> one would require knowledge of revisions where a species may have
> originally been described from a single type, but subsequently known for a
> multitude of specimens. Of course, naming a new species from a single
> specimen can often be tricky if the differences are limited. I am guilty of
> that myself, but in most cases it is likely I would be dead before
> sufficient duplicate material was available, if ever (or they become
> extinct). I figure better to name such specimens now and let the future
> work out synonymies if they exist.
