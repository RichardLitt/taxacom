An emendation is any demonstrably intentional change in the original spelling of a name other than a mandatory change.

This means that the *intent* to change the original spelling must be somehow demonstrable, but it does not have to be explicit.
It also means that it does not matter whether or not the change is linguistically correct.
	
/Thomas Pape
