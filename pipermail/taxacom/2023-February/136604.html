Hi Stephen, once again I agree with you to a point, but then differ: for
example, it is a fact that Blackburn & Wake, 2011 cite the order as Anura
Fischer von Waldheim, 1813. So one could give (on WikiXXX if desired) a
particular authorship "according to [wherever]"; and note any alternative
views as published. Just my take of course. Sometimes it becomes important,
for example according to my recollection there are 2 different orders
Decapoda, one a Crustacean group in current use, the other something else
(cephalopods? cannot remember), so once again authorship of the name in
question is a desirable feature.
