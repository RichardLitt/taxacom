As background... myself and one colleague are trying to back fill missing
genus (and animal subgenus) names to IRMNG (our master taxon database) for
the period 2015-2022 - as at April this year, a bit over 7,000 on our
current "to do" list. My colleague is working forwards on author names
starting from "A", and I am working backwards starting from "Z" and we hope
to meet somewhere in the middle. As you will see by the current request, I
am presently ploughing through "S", while he is doing a good job and is
about to start on "F". We estimate there is around another 8 months to go
on this particular task, if enthusiasm does not fail first :)

Regards - Tony
