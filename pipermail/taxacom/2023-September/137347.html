Greetings Taxacom Folks,

We're excited to open registration for TaxonWorks Together 2023. Please 
bring your perspectives on how to "Describe Life" to the 4th annual 
event on TaxonWorks, its community, and the broader world of 
biodiversity informatics. Our sessions and speakers plan to touch on 
topics that often appear here on Taxacom (e.g. The Future of Species 
Description, Perspectives on Taxon Pages, and in support of taxonomy -- 
bringing actionable practices and tools to practitioners).

We will be highlighting what's new, what's changed, and what is on the 
horizon. Some activities will be highly-guided, some information style, 
and some unconference style. Zoom space may be limited, please don't 
wait! Questions welcome.

Your colleagues, students, developers, staff, and leadership, all very 
welcome.
Please share this notice with your various communities worldwide, thanks!
