Dear Taxacomers,

If you know a PhD student from Ukraine who wants to spend 12 months in
Helsinki working on plant systematics please forward the following
scholarship opportunity to them and be in touch with me at
peter.poczai at helsinki.fi

EDUFI Fellowship for doctoral students from Ukraine provides doctoral level
students from Ukraine the opportunity to continue their academic work in
Finland and supports the academic community and the reconstruction of the
higher education system in Ukraine.

Find more from here:
