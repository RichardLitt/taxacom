Dear all,

I also pointed this out last year, but apparently there are 
ample supplies of copies of all three versions of the 
International Rules of Botanical Nomenclature (new, uncut) 
at Versandantiquariat Robert A. Mueller Nachf.
 bestellung at mueller365.de :

1906, Vienna Rules (Briquet - 3CG46)
1912, Brussels Rules (Briquet - 2P2508)
1935, Cambridge Rules (Briquet - 3CG45)
and also the Verhandlungen of the 1905 Vienna Congress 
(Wettstein & al. - 2P2556)

I hope this is of some use to somebody,
Paul
