Murrell, Z. E.2010.Vascular Plant Taxonomy, 6th Edition.Kendall-Hunt 
Publishing Company: Dubuque, Iowa.632 pp.

This text is a continuation of the Walters and Keil textbook series, 
reworked to follow the Angiosperm Phylogeny Group 
classification.Paperback, designed for sophomore/junior level students. 
The text is not intended to be encyclopedic and does not require an 
extensive botany background.
