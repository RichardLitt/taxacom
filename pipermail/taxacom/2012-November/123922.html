Torrey Botanical Society Offers Grants -Deadlines 31 December 2012

The Torrey Botanical Society annually offers four grant competitions.  The grants provide $1000--$2500 each to support graduate student research, undergraduate and graduate student training, and symposia.  A wide range of botanical subjects are eligible for support.  Application instructions are posted at http://www.torreybotanical.org/grants-awards/ .

The four grant competitions are as follows.
1)     Torrey Botanical Society Graduate Student Research Fellowship.  Beginning this year, three grants will be awarded in this competition (only one in the past).  In addition to the customary $2,500 grant, the Society also will award a second grant of $1,500 and a third of $1,000.
2)     Torrey Botanical Society Andrew M. Greller Graduate Student Research Award for Conservation of Local Flora and Ecosystems.
3)     Torrey Botanical Society Undergraduate and Graduate Student Training Fellowship.
4)     Torrey Botanical Society Symposium Award.
