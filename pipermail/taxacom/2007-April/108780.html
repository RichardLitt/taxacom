Trying to get a trace of fame on my shoulders:

I was inspired by Richard Zander's TAXACOM and especially by his FLORA ON
LINE and I started to send botanical notes to the mailing list I stole from
my friend. BEN (Botanical Electronic News) is now distributed to almost 1200
direct subscribers and it is nicely archived by Scott Russell at the
Oklahoma University web site:
http://www.ou.edu/cas/botany-micro/ben/
Check it for the recent treatments of the genus Hieracium in the Pacific
Northwest or for the treatment of the genus Eleocharis in British Columbia.

Congratulations TAXACOM and thanks for your inspiration!

Adolf Ceska, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada
