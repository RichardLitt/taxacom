Dear all,

Vayreda y Vila, Estanislao, [Vayr.], sometimes spelled Vayred_R_a, 
appears to be the author of Pinus sylvestris L. var. olivicola, a taxon 
well mentioned by J. Franco do Amaral in Flora Iberica [Pinaceae], 
although without a reference.

I have, so far, been unable to locate the protologue (let alone to get 
copies of it and of the title.page of the work concerned).

This Taxon is NOT listed in either of the enumerations of plants 
published by Vayreda in "Anales de la Sociedad Española de Historia 
Natural". It might be included in his "Catálech de la flora de la Vall 
de Nuria...", 1882, or in any other publication co-authored by Vayreda, 
of which I might be unaware of and to which I have no access.

Any help with this item in one way or another would be very much 
appreciated.
