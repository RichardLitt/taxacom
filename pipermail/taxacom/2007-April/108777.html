2007 
Four Australian Postdoctoral Fellowships in Taxonomy

These postdoctoral fellowships form part of the Australian Government's
Commonwealth Environmental Research Facilities (CERF) National Taxonomy
Research Hub. The positions will contribute to the goals of the hub,
particularly addressing recognised taxonomic gaps in knowledge and
enhancing the nation's capacity to manage the environment effectively.
These research projects will be expected to create, collect, manage and
deliver data in systems and formats compatible with emerging new
biodiversity data management systems delivering web-based information on
Australian plants and animals, moving towards the creation of a 'one
stop shop' for accessing key information on Australia's biodiversity.  

The 3-year Postdoctoral Fellowships are focused on the taxonomy of
aquatic macroinvertebrates, environmental weeds, ants and small
terrestrial vertebrates.
