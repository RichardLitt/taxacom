uBio has created an RSS feed for Zootaxa, "A rapid international
journal for animal taxonomists".  New publications are indexed and
available for taxonomic browsing through uBioRSS or can be accessed
via RSS at:

http://names.ubio.org/rss/rss_feed.php?username=zootaxa

We have also created a feed for The Biological Bulletin although this
feed currently only contains articles containing taxon names.

http://names.ubio.org/rss/rss_feed.php?username= biobull

uBioRSS is a taxonomic RSS aggregator that provides taxonomic
browsing of thousands of articles from hundreds of journals.
Viewers can select taxonomic groups or input their own species lists
for monitoring.

http://names.ubio.org/rss
