We have added 84 journals from BioOne to the list of RSS feeds
currently consumed by uBioRSS bringing the current total to 488.
uBioRSS monitors titles and abstracts from biologically-relevant
journals and information sources via Really Simple Syndication (RSS)
and processes them with Taxonomic Name Recognition algorithms.  The
titles and abstracts are then made browseable via classifications
such as Species 2000, ITIS, or NCBI.   Users can specify taxonomic
groups to monitor via profiles that create customized RSS feeds that
effectively monitor the journal list for any mention of the species
contained within the taxonomic group.

http://names.ubio.org/rss/
