The Taxonomic Databases Working Group (TDWG: www.tdwg.org) is seeking an
experienced (English language) Technical Author to develop a range of
documentation to support its standards.  The person will liaise with members
of TDWG subgroups to design and develop informal documentation such as
user-guides, primers, a web site, and assist with formal documentation
suites. 

Applicants should have a university degree or equivalent.  Experience with
interoperability standards would be required and familiarity with TDWG would
be an advantage.

The work would be part time, and on a contract basis with the Global
Biodiversity Information Facility (www.gbif.org).  Approximately 6 months
full-time work equivalent would be anticipated over the next 18 months.

While the work would be done at the contractor's location, some travel may
be involved.

Expressions of interest with a brief r?sum? should be forwarded to:

    Lee Belbin
    TDWG Infrastructure Project Manager
    lee at tdwg.org
