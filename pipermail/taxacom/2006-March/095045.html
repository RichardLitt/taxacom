SPNHC-NSCA 2006 Abstract submission deadline extended to
10 April 2006

Just when you thought you missed the deadline to submit an
abstract for the joint annual meeting of the Society for
the Preservation of Natural History Collections and the
Natural Science Collections Alliance, you find out the
deadline has been extended to 10 April 2006. So don?t
delay. For additional information and to access the
downloadable abstract submission form please go to
http://www.msb.unm.edu/meetings/SPNHC-NSCA2006/talks.html
and follow the instructions.  We are inviting submissions
for oral and poster presentations pertaining to all
aspects of natural history collection care and management
as well as presentations focusing on the meeting?s theme
of productive partnerships.  We strongly encourage poster
submissions.

Other dates to remember:
Early registration ends March 24, 2006
Conference rate cut-off date for Hotel Albuquerque is
April 23, 2006
SPNHC-NSCA 2006 Meeting: May 23-27, 2006

See you in scenic Albuquerque in May.
