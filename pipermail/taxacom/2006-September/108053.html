About this time last year, there was a post requesting if any calendars were
available that feature quality images of arthropods, so forgive me if the
below is not appropriate for this mailing list. I'll try my hand at it
anyway.

 

In the interest of elevating awareness about how beautiful arachnids can be,
the Canadian Arachnologist is hosting an image contest with public voting
for a 2007 arachnid calendar. More details can be found at
http://canadianarachnology.dyndns.org/calendar/. The deadline for image
upload is Friday, October 13th and voting begins the next day.
