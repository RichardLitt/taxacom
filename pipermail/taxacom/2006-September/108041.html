Dear All,
      For those who haven't yet heard, multiple sightings of Ivory-billed 
woodpeckers in the Florida Panhandle over the past year now offers even 
stronger evidence that this species is not extinct.  Some of this evidence 
is available at Mennill's website (first link below), and a full online 
article (paper in Avian Conservation and Ecology) is available through the 
second link.
    ---Cheers,
              Ken Kinman

Florida" at:
