Dear taxacomers,

A note to let you know that we have posted an
interactive catalog of the colombian Cicadellidae
(Hemiptera: Insecta). This is a funded project of the
Universidad Nacional de Colombia with support of the
University of Kentucky (NSF DEB 0205982).

The page includes a large number of pictures, keys to
the genera and a list of the reported species for the
country.  

You can access the page at the following addrees:

http://www.unradio.unal.edu.co/ciencias/proyectos/ins_cien/CATALOGO/pagina_ppal.htm

we appreciate your comments.
