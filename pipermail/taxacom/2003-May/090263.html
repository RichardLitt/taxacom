Hi all

My apologies for any cross-posting.  We are pleased to announce the release of volume 2 of Vulpia.  Peer-reviewed papers in volume 2 include the following:

KEYS
Krings, A.  Contributions to the flora of Nags Head Woods, I: Key to the trees and shrubs.  Vulpia 2:1-14.

Krings, A.  Contributions to the flora of Nags Head Woods, II: Key to the vines.  Vulpia 2:15-22.

Stucky, J.M.  Winter twig keys to common, native, fully deciduous trees and phanerophyte shrubs of the North Carolina eastern Piedmont.  Vulpia 2:23-42.

Fantz, P.R.  A teaching tool: A key to selected Gymnosperms.  Vulpia 2:43-64.

Fantz, P.R.  A teaching tool: A key to selected Fagaceae.  Vulpia 2: 65-76.

EXSICCATAE AND LISTS
Krings, A.  Exsiccatae of Carolina Richardia (Rubiaceae) studied in an analysis of floral variation.  Vulpia 2:77-80.

Fantz, P.R., T. Lasseigne, and D. Mays.  Cultivated dicotyledonous taxa at the NC State University Herbarium, II: Additions to the collection in 2002.  Vulpia 2:81-93.


A searchable archive has also been added since volume 1.  For more information, including author guidelines, subscription information, and access to the papers, please visit: http://www.vulpia.ncsu.edu

Best wishes,

Alexander
