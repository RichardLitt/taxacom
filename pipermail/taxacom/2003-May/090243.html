2003 
IV Ecuadorian Botanical Congress, II Biodiversity Conservation for the
Andes and Amazonia Congress.

The Ecuadorian Foundation of Botanists, FUNBOTANICA, the LOJA Herbarium
and Universidad Técnica Particular de Loja invite you all to attend the
IV Ecuadorian Botanical Congress, and II Biodiversity Conservation for
the Andes and Amazonia Congress. The event will be held in Loja, Ecuador
from August 25th to the 30th. Due dates for reception of abstracts is
May 30, 2003.

Additional information is available at http://www.biodiversidad2003.org
and at http://www.joethejuggler.com/Funbotanica.


Alina Freire-Fierro
Missouri Botanical Garden
FUNBOTANICA, Website Administrator
