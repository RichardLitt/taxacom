If possible, I'd like to get some leaf material (or even whole
voucher specimens) of some of the different geographical
entities of Trillium ovatum (as described by Marilyn Renner).
I'm hoping to finish the DNA studies this year rather than
tacking on an extra year to the program; so I'd like to get
leaf material before I make my trip west next spring to
actually see the plants in their respective habitats.
I'd particularly like to get some material from the eastern-most
populations (e.g., Colorado, Wyoming, Montana) and from
the northern California populations of T. ovatum ssp. oettingeri.
