> REQUEST
> The Biosystematics Group of Wageningen University solicits material for molecular analysis of the genera in the family Celtidaceae (previously in Ulmaceae). It concerns the genera Celtis, Trema, Lozanella, Ampelocera, Pteroceltis, Parasponia, Aphananthe, Gironniera and Chaetachme. We would be very obliged if you could send us this material. Particularly but not exclusively African species are of interest.
