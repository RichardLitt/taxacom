TAXONOMIC DATABASES WORKING GROUP -- 2004 ANNUAL MEETING
Hosted by Landcare, New Zealand, Christchurch
October 11-18, 2004

The online registration site for TDWG 2004 is now available at:
https://www.regonline.com/Checkin.asp?EventId=16339

Multiple attendees can be registered and paid in a single transaction.
Registrations can be partially filled out, saved, and completed later.  If
you registered last year, address and contact information can be re-used or
updated.  If you plan to attend, please register as soon as you can.

Meeting schedule and details can be found at the meeting's main site:
http://www.tdwg.org/2004meet/TDWG_2004.htm

Please contact me if you have any questions or problems with the
registration site.
