Postdoc in Zurich

I am looking for a botany PhD graduate with strengths in
phylogenetics and statistics, and interested in analysing a very rich
dataset on the evolution of the species diversity in the Cape flora,
South Africa. This post-doc position is available as of now, and
funding is guaranteed until end of May, 2005, but we may find another
5 months worth of funding. You will be part of a dynamic research
group integrating ecological, palaeoclimatological, and phylogenetic
information to develop models to describe the generation and
maintenance of the plant diversity in one of the species-rich areas
of the world. A somewhat dated description of the project can be seen
at
http://www.systbot.unizh.ch/institut/personen/professoren/linder.htm
If interested, please send cv to Peter Linder at
plinder at systbot.unizh.ch
