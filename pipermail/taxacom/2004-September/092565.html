Dear Dr. Jim Endersby:
        I would recommend the following book to you.  One of the
interesting points that comes out of this book is that Rafinesque's body
still lies underneath a soccer field in a Philadelphia park, and is not
buried in honor at the the campus of Transylvania University at Lexington,
Kentucky.

Boewe, Charles. (editor). 2003. Profiles of Rafinesque. The University of
Tennessee  Knoxville, Tennessee, USA. 456 pp., Illustrations.
