The issue is complicated. Neocicindela is endemic to N.Z., but it has been given generic rank (again!) by Larochelle & Larivière (2013), who split it into two genera, the other genus being their new genus Zecicindela. The problem is that the sister taxon of these are an Australian taxon which is still treated as a subgenus of Cicindela. There appears to be lots of disagreement about taxon ranks in tiger beetles, some taxonomists even retaining family status for the group!
