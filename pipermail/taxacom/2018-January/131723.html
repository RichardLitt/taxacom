Dear Taxacommers,

The European ITN project IGNITE <http://www.itn-ignite.eu/> starting 1st of
January 2018 within the Marie-Sklodowska-Curie EU grant program, announces
15 PhD/Early Stage Researcher (ESR) positions in invertebrate genomics and
biodiversity informatics for a maximum duration of 36 months.

The deadline for applications is 28th of February 2018 (23:59 CET). All
positions will be opened on 1st of July 2018 or as soon as possible
thereafter.

To apply and for general information about the positions, please visit the
central application tool available here: https://portal.graduatecenter-
lmu.de/ocgc/ignite.

For a detailed description of the individual projects please see:
http://www.itn-ignite.eu/projects/

Best regards,
Lyubomir
