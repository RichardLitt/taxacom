Hi Jose,

A quick GBIF search, using the Advanced Search feature with
Basis of Record  = Preserved Specimen (so somehow captured)
Elevation between 4545 and 9999 meters

Results in 101,777 records (some are Phylum = Arthropoda, Class = Insecta)

Limit to Arthropoda = 23,451 records
Limit to Insecta = 23,006

Hope that helps :-) Under Insecta, I see Hymenoptera, Coleoptera, Leps, 
Orthoptera, Odonata, Hemiptera, Diptera, Tricoptera, Plecoptera, 
Siphonaptera, Ephemeroptera, Neuroptera, Megaloptera, Blattodea, 
Psocodea, Mantodea, Archaeognatha, Rahidioptera, Mecoptera, and Dermaptera

Of course, you asked how they were acquired. I did not delve farther. I 
leave that to you. Happy sleuthing,
