Dear Andy,
Thank you for your reply. I know the wiki page and the BHL landing page for
the AfN. I included a link to the AfN in BHL in my previous email. “Dated”
volumes in BHL is not what I am looking for. BHL has a long way to go to
get their dates straight. I am asking for articles by researchers who have
actually looked beyond the volumes' “year” (what one often sees in BHL) or
the printed year (often not the one highlighted by BHL) and have done
research on dating the volumes, the issues, and whatever articles or
fascicles may have been issued as separata. That’s what I am looking for:
publication dates as exact as possible, for “publication units” as granular
as possible.
Cheers,
