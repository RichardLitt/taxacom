As far as records of high-elevation collections: One has to be careful to disambiguate aeolian drift specimens from those that actually live at those elevations. There are also records of hill-topping at mountain tops. I’m not sure about records >14,500 feet but we in Hawaii have collected calliphorid flies hill-topping at 13,800 feet (summit of Mauna Kea).

-Neal
