2022 
 Hello,
Kew science is currently recruiting for 5 x 4 year research fellowships as
part of our Future Leader Fellowship in Plant and Fungal Science programme.
These will develop an area of research within one of Kew's research
priorities, i.e.
- Ecosystem Stewardship;
- the Seed and Stress Biology research group within the Trait Diversity and
Function Priority;
- Digital Revolution;
- Accelerated Taxonomy;
- and the Millennium Seed Bank within the enhanced partnerships priority.

Probably of most interest to taxacom readers are the fellowship posts in
(i) digital revolution: which covers digital collections, names & taxonomy
management, scientific software development, & AI/ML techniques for data
exploration & integration across our information resources and (ii)
accelerated taxonomy: which aims to to speed up the description, naming and
classification of the plant and fungal kingdoms using new data, tools and
technologies.

If you've informal questions or need to make a contact with a Kew member of
staff re the development of a research plan, then do get in touch -  I'll
do my best to help.

all the best
Nicky
