Mesophyllaceae unite the majority of multiporate corallines. Their pore
filaments display wide structural diversity to such an extent that discrete
types can be recognized as unequivocal synapomorphies for genera occurring
across South Africa-Australia-New Zealand-Chatham, predating the known
fossil record.
