Dear all, hymenopterists in particular,

The XXVI International Congress of Entomology (ICE2022) will be held in Helsinki, Finland, in 17-22 July 2022. Originally, the congress had to be held already in 2020 but due to the pandemic, it was postponed twice.

In this connection, we are organizing a symposium titled "Advances in hymenopteran biodiversity research and conservation".

The deadline for abstract submission will be 12 May 2022 (see https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fice2020helsinki.fi%2F&data=04%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C7e83c28501c04bceb29108da1e0c49d3%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C637855335390511867%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000&sdata=4Wm9VrrnDyd1vKmMT6gvu5kx1kib8UC9JoyBkWL3WsA%3D&reserved=0). For those who registered to the conference and the Hymenoptera symposium already in 2020 and still plan to attend, new registration and abstract submission is not needed.

While there are only a few weeks to the abstract submission deadline, we still welcome new submissions and hope to meet many Hymenoptera researchers in Helsinki in July.

Sincerely,

Juho Paukkunen and Marko Mutanen
Hymenoptera symposium organizers
