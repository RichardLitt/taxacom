In response to the recent inquiry about other plant-oriented lists,
I've summarized some of them below.  A couple have been mentioned before,
but I thought that it might be convenient to have all of the addresses in
one place.

Send the message below to mailserv at scuacc.scu.edu

        subscribe orchids
