Notice to Diptera Systematists on TAXACOM (and apologies to those on
entomo-l for this cross-listing):

As some of you may be aware, Chris Thompson (USDA-SEL) and I are compiling
a Directory of Diptera Systematists. We would like to add as many email
telephone,and fax numbers as possible to the entries we have (about 1800
dipterists are entered so far).

Those of you on TAXACOM who would like their email addresses, telephone
numbers, and fax numbers added to your entries (and to make sure you are
already entered in the directory), please send an email message to Chris
<mnhen121 at sivm.si.edu> with the following in the body of the message:

email address
fax number (if you have one)
telephone number
current postal address

... and to make things hairy for Chris, forward this message on to every
Diptera systematist you know (chain letter-style . . . kinda). The more
messages with email addresses we get (that is, Chris gets), the more
complete the directory will be.

And PLEASE don't hit the "respond" key to this message or everyone on
TAXACOM will be getting unwanted email from you. Send your message
directly to Chris.

Thanks! And I'll be looking forward to seeing a bunch of you at ICD-3 in
Guelph.
