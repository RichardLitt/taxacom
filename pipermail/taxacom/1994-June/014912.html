     I am interested in building a network of entomologists for the
purpose of exchanging insect specimens with people in other parts of the
world.  I am working on bee flies (Diptera:Bombyliidae) and would like to
build a worldwide collection for phylogenetic work. If anyone is
interested in obtaining specimens from the Southwest (U.S) in this way,
please drop me a line.
     Also, does anyone know of an entomological listserv (esp. systematics)?
