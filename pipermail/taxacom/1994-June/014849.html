A while back, there were a few postings about RangeMapper 2.0.  I
am considering purchasing this program for mapping distributions
of plant taxa.  Since there are several mapping programs
currently available, I would like to hear comments from people
who are at present using this program to produce range maps of

I would be happy to receive all comments directly and then post a
summary of the comments later.  Thank you for your time.

becoming proficient with Macs.
