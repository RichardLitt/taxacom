It's a Historical Narrative Explanation of a one-time event, not a Nomological Deductive Explanation. See
Bock, W. J. 2004. Explanations in systematics. In: D. M. Williams DM & P. L. Forey  (eds.). Milestones in Systematics. Systematics Association Special Volume 67: 49?56.
www.mobot.org/plantscience/resbot/Repr/Add/Bock2004Expl.pdf 

Apparently there is a confusion between figuring out universals, using physics' "least action" versus figuring out historical events, using phylogenetics similar but different "parsimony." Bock admits his fault but has the grace to explain a better way.
