Users of NDE who haven't been able to get it to install on Vista can  
download a new installer from http://taxonomy.zoology.gla.ac.uk/rod/NDE/nde.html#Download 
  (just click on the "Download" link). I've tested this on Windows XP  
and Vista, and it seems to work. NDE itself is about a decade old, and  
I've turned my attention to other things, but I hope this will help  
those NDE users who still find the program useful, but have been left  
out in the cold by upgrading to Vista.
