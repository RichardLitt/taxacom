2009 
7-9 October 2009 at Louisiana State University, led by Dr. Lowell Urbatsch 

   Field and hands-on workshop for native plant identification emphasizing
identification of Asteraceae, grasses, sedges, and other major groups. During this
workshop participants will learn the distinguishing features of native
plants and their preferred habitats. Useful tools for identifying plants
including various on-line resources such as interactive keys will be
covered. 

Further information and application available here -
http://www.herbarium.lsu.edu/keys/workshop/Plant%20ID%20workshop%20LSU%20Oct%207-9%202009.pdf
