Hello Taxacomers,

A new, free iPhone/iPad app is now available from the Apple App Store 
that brings the taxonomic information available from the HOL web 
application to your iOS mobile device. The app, appropriately enough, is 
named HOL mobile.

Some additional information and screenshots of the HOL app can be found 
on the HOL Google+ page 
(https://plus.google.com/u/0/101268750407660322692/posts/bhvHmEzVy4p). 
Or, you can download it from the App Store and try it yourself 
(https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/hol/id646020193?mt=8).

Feedback is very much appreciated.
