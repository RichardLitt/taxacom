> ?the scientific content of a paper is much more important than publication metrics 
>or the identity of the journal in which it was published.?

Of course the scientific content is more important than 
publication metrics, especially as in taxonomy you often 
have small groups of scientists
who are able to cite such a paper.
But, especially in nomenclature and taxonomy and internet 
possibilities in recent days there should be a minimum of 
journal requirements.
Everybody can run his own writing everything he wants 
without any peer-review. So the "identity of the journal" 
should not be ignored.
