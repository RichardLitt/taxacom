2006 
The 8th Systematics Association "Young Systematists' Forum" is being held at the Natural History Museum, London on THURSDAY 7th DECEMBER. This is an opportunity for students and postdocs to present a talk or a poster about their research in an informal setting.

The deadline for submitting abstracts is approaching, and there are still some places available for oral presentations. We would also welcome poster presentations. Registration is free, but if you wish to attend please send an email to Juliet Brodie (j.brodie at nhm.ac.uk). Abstracts for talks or posters should be submitted by 17th NOVEMBER.
 
Further information can be found on the Systematics Association website: www.systass.org/ysf/
