Hi Folks -

 

After being out of print for over a year, we are pleased to announce the
publication of the Second Edition of the Checklist of the Land Snail and
Slugs of California by Barry Roth and Patricia Sadeghian. This
publication documents the distribution of the 279 species known in the
state, as wells as, providing nearly 200 full color photographs of type
specimens.

 

For more information go to:
http://sbnature.org/Merchant2/merchant.mvc?Screen=PROD&Product_Code=1021
11&Category_Code=pu&Product_Count=2 

 

Or contact Patricia Sadeghian at: psadeghian at sbnature2.org.

 

Enjoy!
