~ Just released ~

Cheilostomatous Bryozoa
from the
Solomon Islands

by
Kevin J. Tilbrook

 

The Cheilostomatous Bryozoa from the Solomon Islands is the first
detailed study of this fauna from this island nation. A total of 184
species, in 44 families, are described and extensively illustrated by
SEM images. Included are 72 new species, five new genera and two new
families.

 

For more information go to: http://www.vetigastropoda.com/Bryozoa.html 

 

Or contact Patricia Sadeghian at: psadeghian at sbnature2.org.
