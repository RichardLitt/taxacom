Biodiversity Informatics 

Closing Date: November 20, 2006

The Botanic Garden and Botanical Museum Berlin-Dahlem seeks a Biodiversity Informatics who will be responsible for developing taxonomic databases and a web editor based on the Berlin Model (http://www.bgbm.org/biodivinf/docs/bgbm-model/). The successful applicant will be responsible for providing support for scientific users at the BGBM and other international institutions as well as importing data into a complex taxonomic database structure.

Requirements: a degree in Informatics/Biology or related fields. 

Desirable: The successful applicant will have a good working knowledge of database development and experience in higher level programming. The applicant should have experience in developing data base-driven web applications, modelling of complex information domains as well as having participated in interdisciplinary IT projects. An interest in taxonomy, systematics and biodiversity computer science as well as good English communication skills is also desired.

Salary: German Federal pay scale Vgr. IIa/Ib BAT (i.d.F.d. Anw.-TV FUB)

Applications accompanied by covering letter and proof of degree must be submitted by 20 November 2006 to:

Botanic Garden and Botanical Museum Berlin-Dahlem
Freie Universität Berlin
- AV -
Königin-Luise-Str. 6-8
D-14195 Berlin
Germany

Or by email to: BiodiversityInformatics at bgbm.org

The position is open starting December 1, 2006 and ends by May 31, 2007 with the possibility of an annual renewal.

The FUB adheres to the German disability guidelines and is an Affirmative Action/Equal Opportunity Employer. Applications from woman are especially encouraged.
