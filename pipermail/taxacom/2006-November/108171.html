Folks,

 

Approximately a month ago, I posted a link to a public voting booth for an
arachnid image competition. Top 12 images were to be featured in a 2007
calendar. The calendars are now ready for order at
http://canadianarachnology.dyndns.org/calendar/ where there is also a
description and a link to all the voting results.

 

Proceeds will contribute to the continued development of The Nearctic Spider
Database as well as a museum with significant arachnid holdings (yet to be
determined).
