Roderic Page wrote:

> This also illustrates the problems with linking to digital resources 
> using URLs, as opposed to identifiers such as DOIs.

There can be problems with DOIs, too. My only attempt to use one wasn't 
encouraging. I was sent a reference, including a DOI, to add to the list on 
the DELTA Web site. The DOI was apparently wrong, and I couldn't get it 
fixed; in the end, I thought it best to omit it. Here's a precis of my 
correspondence on the matter.

________________________________________
Date: Tue, 13 Dec 2005
From: Mike Dallwitz
To:  help at ingentaconnect.com

In the article
     Revets, S. A. 2005. A key to the unilocular hyaline Foraminifera.
     Journal of Micropalaeontology, 24(2): 145-158. DOI:
     10.1144/0262-821X04-001
at
     http://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/geol/jm/2005/00000024/00000002/
         art00004
the DOI seems to be incorrect. When I submit it to http://www.doi.org/, I
get the message
     The DOI 10.1144/0262-821X04-001 cannot be found in the Handle System.

________________________________________
