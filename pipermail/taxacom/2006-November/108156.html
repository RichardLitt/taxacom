JeF:

Your inquiry may be of interest to others as well, so I'll reply to all.


I visited the Hermosillo Herbarium about 1993 to try and find the type
of Leptochloa digitatiformis Beetle, but the curator was away at the
time and the person who assisted was unsure where the type speicmens
were.  Surely somebody else will be able to reply to the status of this
herbarium better in more detail.

However, the Department of Renewable Resources, University of Wyoming,
which includes the former Range Science Department, has a herbarium
(WYAC, ca. 60,000 specimens) where many of Beetle's collections are
housed.  There may be a duplicate of the type there.  I suspect if you
contact Dr. Dan Rodgers(rodgers at uwyo.edu) he can provide more
information about whether its accessibility.  As far as I know, the
herbarium in that Department is used little.

Neil
