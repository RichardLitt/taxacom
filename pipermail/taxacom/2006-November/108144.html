2006 



Graduate student herbarium curator assistantship anticipated to replace
recent graduate. Available January 2007 or until a suitable candidate is
found.  This a half-time position equivalent to a teaching assistantship
available to a fully enrolled graduate student in Biological Sciences whose
primary focus is systematic or evolutionary biology.  Duties of the
position include assisting the director and collections’ manager with
herbarium operations.  Meeting the academic qualifications for admission to
a graduate degree program in Biological Sciences is essential.  Please
submit your application electronically.  Details on how to apply for
admission to the graduate program in Biological Sciences are found at:
http://www.biology.lsu.edu/grads/prospect/how_to_apply.html.  Please
contact L. Urbatsch, leu at lsu.edu, or D. Ferguson, dfergu1 at lsu.edu if you
wish to receive consideration for the assistantship or for further
information.
