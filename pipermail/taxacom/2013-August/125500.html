I have been asked to repeat the information. The form itself is at
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1xcY-mT7r8VQ14io6wgu9FXEo1ZQc4GBGPsBl9FdnpjI/viewform

If you wish to download the existing data or view the summary on line go to
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheet/ccc?key
hnFOYXufcFrdGZ5RzliNzJNcFdBMzJXM21XRVlzVUE#gid=0
To download data, click on "File" above the spreadsheet and the option should appear. To see the summary, click on "Form" and select the appropriate option.

If contributing to a regional network not on the list, please enter its name or url in the "other" box.

The 82 listed herbaria that have responded so far (yea!) are: ANHC, APCR, ASU, AUB, BARC, BDI, BHSC, BISH, BKL, BRIT-SMU-VDB, BRY, BUT, CAS/DS, CBS, CDA, CIC, CM, CMC, COLO, CONN, CONV, CSB, CHRB, DAV&AHUC, DBG, DSC, EKY, EMC, EMNH, ETL, FLAS, FLD, GA, HAH, HNT, IND, JEF, JOTR, KANU, KEN, KHD, KSC, LSU, LYN, MESA, MICH, MIN, MISS, MWI, NA, NCSC, NCU, NHA, NMC, NMSU, NYS, OSC, OWU, PH, PTBG, PUL&PUR, RM,, RMS, SDC, SEMO, SRP, TAES, TAMU, TEX&LL, UC&JEPS, UCR, UNM, WVA, USF, USFS, USUUB, UTC, UWSP, VFWO, VT, WARM, WIS, WTU, YU&CBS.
