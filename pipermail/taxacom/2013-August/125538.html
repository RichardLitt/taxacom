Wherever you store it, I would advise first converting to PDF/A which is 
the ISO Standards for PDF Archiving.  This can easily be carried out in 
most PDF software and in Microsoft Word.

PDF/A strips some proprietary stuff off and converts to standard fonts 
etc. and stores information on the font etc. within the PDF to allow 
current and future reading of the PDF regardless of the software.
