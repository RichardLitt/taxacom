Dear Taxacom Entomological Community:
We are in the process of planning a spatial and functional reorganization
of the National University Insect Collection. It would be of much help to
us to receive copies of other insect museum's internal regulations and
manuals of museum procedures and maintenance of the collections. This
information would help us select concepts and practices from other
institutions and adapt them to our working conditions, ultimately with
the goal of facilitating the study of our fauna as well as the
interaction with the global systematics community.  Thanks very much in
advance for your help.
Sincerely,
