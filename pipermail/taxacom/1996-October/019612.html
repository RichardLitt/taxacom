I have been asked to distribute this announcement via the Taxacom list.


University of Dublin, Trinity College, Ireland

is seeking

A Lecturer in Botany.

Applications are invited for the above post, which will be tenable for a
fixed-term of five years, commencing as soon as possible.
The appointee must be able to contribute to the teaching and research in the
Botany Department and preference will be given to candidates with a proven
track record in the area of molecular systematics or molecular ecology.

Salary scale: Lecturer II: Irish Pound 14,456 - 22,929 per annum.
              Lecturer I:  Irish Pound 29,175 - 35,708 per annum.
Appointment will be made on the Lecturer II scale within the salary range
Irish Pound 14,456 - 20,397 per annum, at a point to accord with qualifications
and experience to date.
