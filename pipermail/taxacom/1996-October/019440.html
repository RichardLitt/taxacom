1996 
A notice entitled "Plant pest regulations; review of current provisions;
advance notice of proposed rulemaking and notice of public meeting" has
been published by the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service of the
U.S. Dept. of Agriculture in the Federal Register 61(189): 50767-50770,
Sept. 27, 1996.  This regards importation and interstate movement of
plant pests.  For more information, contact smccammon at aphis.usda.gov or
bflanders at aphis.usda.gov.

Please do not contact me for copies -- I am about to go in the field for a
month!
