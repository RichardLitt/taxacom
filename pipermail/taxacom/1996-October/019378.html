1996 
Announcement No. RA-42-6

Position: Botanist, GS-430-11/12.

Location:  USDA/ARS/Plant Science Institute
           National Germplasm Resources Lab.
           Beltsville, MD.

Description of Duties:  Incumbent will undertake an ecogeographic
survey of the wild and cultivated taxa of a major U.S. crop.
Distributions of taxa within geographic regions and ecosystems,
taxonomic relationships of the species to the crop, analysis of
taxa in germplasm collections, vulnerability of wild populations,
and other factors will be used to determine conservation
priorities for the crop.  Literature review, herbarium studies,
field collections, and interactions with crop experts will be
involved.  Conservation recommendations will involve in-situ and
ex-situ practices.

Special Requirements:

Knowledge of plant taxonomy, conservation biology, agriculture or
horticulture, ecology, economic botany and a foreign language.
Skill in the use of computers and databases and botanical
collecting in remote areas.
