Please note the following URL:

http://www.nybg.org/bsci/res/hall/costaric.html

The site is entitled:  Agaricales of Costa Rican Quercus Forests

It describes a project conducted by Gregory Mueller and Roy Halling and
includes explanatory text as well as images and commentary on agarics/boletes
(mushrooms) and a few other miscellaneous macrofungi found in that habitat. The
authors' email addresses can be found at the bottom of the opening page.
