ASSISTANT PROFESSORSHIP/CURATORSHIP AT THE DEPARTMENT OF ENTOMOLOGY
ZOOLOGICAL MUSEUM, UNIVERSITY OF COPENHAGEN, DENMARK

A position for a systematic entomologist to carry out research on the systematics, phylogeny, morphology, and zoogeography of terrestrial Arthropoda is open to appointment as soon as possible. 

Appointment to the position as assistant professor requires a Ph.D. (or equivalent) degree. The announced position is of indefinite duration and can be upgraded to associate professorship (tenured) after the assistant professor has been evaluated as qualified to an appointment at that level. This evaluation must take place no later than 3? years after employment as assistant professor, but can take place earlier, if the appointee so wishes. 

For details about the job description and qualification requirements, please see http://www.zmuc.dk/commonweb/Job/jobs.htm.
