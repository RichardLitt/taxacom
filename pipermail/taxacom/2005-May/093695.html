The Ohio State University Herbarium, Department of Evolution, Ecology and
Organismal Biology, seeks a motivated individual for a teaching/curatorial
staff position.  The teaching responsibilities include a local flora
course and the plant portion of a course in organismal diversity.
Curation includes maintaining the cryptogamic collections.  There are also
opportunities to pursue individual or collaborative research.  An
individual with a PhD and commitment to excellence in teaching is
preferred.  Contact Dr. John Freudenstein (freudenstein.1 at osu.edu) for
further information.  Deadline for application is May 15.
