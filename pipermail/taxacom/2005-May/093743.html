Due to overwhelming demand for Heads' molecular paper I have posted
links at http://www.sciencebuff.org/heads_publications.php

 

To those who have already requested copies of the molecular paper I also
send Heads' recent paper "Towards a panbiogeography of the seas" which
should be essential reading for any biogeographer - particularly those
who did not believe panbiogeography did not apply to marine life
(believe it or not there were some). The article also refers to the
molecular clock question. A pdf is also available at the above link.
