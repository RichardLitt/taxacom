How about this example in Mollusca of names that are synonyms and homonyms, by the same author in the same year in different publications with different type specimens.

Puncturella hendersoni Dall, 1927: Proceedings of the United States National Museum 70(2668): 9, type locality off Sambo Reef, Florida.
