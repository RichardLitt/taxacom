within GBIF I'm currently data basing crustacean type specimens held in the

of Phyllopoda, which are labelled as type specimens. However, there is no
additional information available from a handwritten catalogue or so (also the
internet did not help)and I was not able to track down the respective original
references, in order to check if those species have ever been formally
published. Can anybody help? The information in the vials is a follows:

Triops elegans Wolf; (Sebka Sevjonni, Tunis; Dr. Kosseler)

Estheria roseni Wolf; (Feudschen, Transkaspien; Baron v. Rosen)
