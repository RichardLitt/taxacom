Now there is a way students with dispersalist supervisors can access
Croizat without being caught! I have started a web page with Croizat's
publications beginning with Space, Time, Form. The introduction and
chapter 1 are up in pdf form. I'm still experimenting. The introduction
was done as a photo image so it may be too large a file (I will reduce
it later). Chapter 1 is at the best compromise I could work out between
size and resolution. Any feedback/comments/problems on that attempt
would be welcome. The files are at:

http://www.sciencebuff.org/croizat_publication_copies.php
