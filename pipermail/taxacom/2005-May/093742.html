Reminder to U.S. investigators (and their colleagues): The Biodiversity
Surveys and Inventories Program at the National Science Foundation of the
USA supports expeditionary work to discover, describe, document and voucher
plant, animal, and microbial diversity throughout the world. The next target
date for proposal submission from eligible US institutions is July 9 (there
is no longer a separate deadline or announcement for this program).
Information can be found on the Division of Environmental Biology web page
through www.nsf.gov.
