I am glad to inform you that all published content (over 16,000 articles)

discovered and accessed in the Biodiversity Heritage Library
<https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.biodiversitylibrary.org%2F&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C8617918a3f2b44a3995008dd99ef47b9%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638835975184303699%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=fzWu0Jl08ULnR6SWdDlBu4MmzFn18F3AamR%2FgDBkewk%3D&reserved=0> (BHL).

automatically upon publication.
