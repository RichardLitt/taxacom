My experience with Delta:
1. If Delta (including Delta.exe, intkey.exe) is installed on the 
PC/Notebook to which pen drive will be attached, carrying folder of each 
database (with accompanying files such as intket.ink, chars, items, images 
etc.)   on pen drive should be sufficient.
2. To cover the risk of Delta not installed it is advisable to copy and 
carry the whole Delta folder (including folders for each database) in the 
pen drive.It should work
