Dear Taxacomer's
Thermopsis turcica is an endemic species of Turkey with 3-4 ovaries in  
a single flower. As far as I know, Thermopsis turcica is the only  
species with several ovaries in the Fabaceae family. I could not  
obtain more information about other Thermopsis species. I will be  
pleased to receive information about other Thermopsis species if they  
have similar characteristics (i.e. several ovaries in a single flower).
