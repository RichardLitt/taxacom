Reading University offers the MSc in Plant Diversity plus four undergraduate
modules in this area (each module is equivalent to 40 contact hours)
including a final year honours module covering the writing of floras and
monographs featuring a series of plant nomenclature lectures by Dick
Brummitt.  Plant taxonomy, at least, is far from dead in the University
system!  The MSc started in 1969 and has run continuously (albeit under the
occasional change of title) since then.
