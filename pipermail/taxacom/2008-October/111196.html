Yes! Hook-up a DiGIR or Tapir access point to your db and register yourself
with GBIF. Your proposed ideas (blitzing listervs and choking Inboxes) may
result in a quick burst of interest, but this won't be sustained unless you
have something that can be searched. My advice would be to roll into your
proposal detailed plans for how you intend to expose your data when you do
not yet have a web presence.
