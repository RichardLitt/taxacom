Our unique, 4-year accelerated Ph.D. program focuses on the history and
interactions among species, within and between biotas, and across time
and space. We offer a multi-faceted curriculum featuring field work,
teaching assistantships and collections-based research; 2:1 faculty to
student ratio with structured mentoring; and 200 active scientists, 40
curators/professors, 32 million specimens and a 400,000 volume natural
history library. Admission is highly competitive and there is full
funding including a generous stipend, personal computer and research
budget for those selected.

Application deadline is December 15, 2008 for Fall 2009 enrollment.
Visit http://rggs.amnh.org <http://rggs.amnh.org/>  for more information
or email admissions-rggs at amnh.org.
