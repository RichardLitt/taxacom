Hi Tony and Robin,                         I have to disagree.  I don't think this was inevitable (or wise or brave).  We have long known (since Archaeopteryx) that birds evolved from reptiles, but that is not a good reason to subsume one into the other.  By such reasoning, we could argue that Amphibia and the other tetrapod classes should be subsumed into Sarcopterygii.  Or subsuming all the higher plant phyla into Phylum Charophyta.  Or how about dumping all the insects into the Crustacea.  Such reasoning is just as unwise with respect to Aves as it is with these and many other examples.  So I don't think it is inevitable that birds have to be dumped in with their reptile (dinosaur) ancestors, any more than it is would be inevitable that we dump tetrapods in with the sarcopterygian fish (or Insecta into Crustacea).                            ----------------Ken
