Ah, I see what you mean. Very messy indeed. AA has credited BB with the
name and references BB’s manuscript. However, did AA (in his "few words”)
use text from BB’s manuscript to define/describe the species (If zoology,
see Article 50.1). Also, did AA cite any types?

Quite simply, did AA make BB’s species name available by citation in AA's
publication, even though AA misidentified the species?

I’m sure this situation must have arisen before and someone very familiar
with the Code of Nomenclature and could advise ...
