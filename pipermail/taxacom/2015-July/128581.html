I agree with Ken. However, I still have Marc van Roosmalen
on my mind, who spent considerable time in prison. Part of 
the charges were that he had offered to name species after 
whoever would donate money to a particular charity.

The reasoning ran something like this: he was in government
employ when he discovered those species, and therefor the
right to name those species was government property. Offering
to sell governement property was stealing from the government, 
so ...
