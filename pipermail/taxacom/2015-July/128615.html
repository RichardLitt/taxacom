There are certainly big problems with the idea of "consensus". Given that all sorts of people are using all sorts of classifications for all sorts of reasons, isn't a "consensus" just going to deliver some meaningless "one size fits all"? Also, the result will depend on who is included/excluded from the "consensus" group. Theoretically, "consensus" doesn't give due weight to the opinions of the most intelligent people, because (by very definition) they are a minority! Hence "consensus" tends to drag things down to the lowest common denominator. This isn't always a problem, but it certainly can be.

Stephen
