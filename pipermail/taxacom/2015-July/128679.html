Well, these were included in the Code, from 1906 up to and including
the 1952 editions, as an example of exsiccata that were effectively
published. Exsiccata have been disallowed only from 31 Dec 1952.

See also TL-II : http://biodiversitylibrary.org/page/33120202

According to The Plant List, the Global Compositae Checklist
accepts such names as validly published.
