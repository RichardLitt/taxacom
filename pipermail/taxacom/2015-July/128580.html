Personally I don't oppose raising funds by selling honorific names----if the funds go to a good cause (especially if it helps fund future scientific work in this time of shrinking budgets).  It would certainly be preferable to species names that raise no money at all and are honorific for less noble reasons----such as naming a species for a rock band because they happened to be listening to that rock band's music when they discovered the new species.  A scientific donor certainly deserves being honored more than someone's favorite rock band.                          --------------Ken
