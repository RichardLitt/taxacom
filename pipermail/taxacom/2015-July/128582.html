It would seem that naming for money is constrained by the level of
independence one has from an employer. Presumably university researchers
who are able to earn additional money through books, for example, would
have no such problem. Otherwise it would appear that the option is limited
to those who are technically amateurs - assuming the named specimen is not
from an institution such as a museum that 'owned' the specimen (presuming
that this entitled them to any proceeds - wonder if that is a caveat on any
loan forms?) - and assuming that the receiving institution is willing to
accept the specimen without some of the proceeds.

John Grehan
