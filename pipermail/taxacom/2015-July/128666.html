Sorry, but the fish classification is a mess. It's an attempt to include
all sorts of recent molecular results, most of which are not particularly
stable (there is a lack of consistency in results among different
studies) or well supported by other lines of evidence. This is going to
cause considerable confusion for anyone that tries to use it.

Tony
