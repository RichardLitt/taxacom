Mammals may be on a different lineage to that of 'reptiles', but then birds
and dinosaurs may be said to be on a different lineage to that of
'reptiles' as well. I guess the point I was trying to make (and that Ken
made it more effectively) is that it seems that 'reptiles' has been rescued
from paraphyly by expanding its coverage to include birds (along with the
extinct dinosaurs). If synapsids were the next sistergroup to the new
'reptiles', one could conceivably expand the group reptiles to include
mammals as well. Personally I have no problem expanding the term reptiles
to include all the classic reptiles (turtles, lizards, snakes) along with
crocodilians and dinosaurs (including birds), but the expansion would seem
to be purely arbitrary (although it does rescue the term from paraphyly by
keeping birds out. Does that make rational sense or am I still off the
rails?.

John
