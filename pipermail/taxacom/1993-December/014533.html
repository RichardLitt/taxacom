Note: Issue" refers to consecutively numbered issues of Flora Online.
"Version" number is applied as is done in computer program
nomenclature, that is, version 1.0 indicates the original publication,
1.1 (1.2, 1.3) a minor revision, 2.0 (3.0, 4.0) a major revision.

Because magnetic media fade over time, librarians should copy
electronic journals to new media regularly, at least every five to
seven years.
