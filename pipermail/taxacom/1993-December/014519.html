1993 
Forams '94, an international symposium on benthic and planktic foraminifera
and a continuation of the highly successful Benthos series of meetings, will
meet in Berkeley, CA, July 5-8, 1993, for technical sessions and July 9
for field trips to modern and fossil localities in California.  The final
date for registration, housing, food, wine and field trips is April 1, 1993.
Theme sessions will include extinctions and radiations, geochronology,
sequence biostratigraphy, paleoceanography/paleoclimatology, and
 paleoenvironments.  For the Final Circular, including all registration,
 housing, food,and
field trip options and costs, and the instructions for submission of abstracts,
 Please write FORAMS '94, Museum of Paleontology, University of California,
Berkeley, CA 94720, or email jlipps at ucmp1.berkeley.edu
