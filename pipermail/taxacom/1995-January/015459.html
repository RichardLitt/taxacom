          Susan Farmer asked after the US endangered species list. If you
          have Web access, take a look at URL
          http://www.fws.gov/endlist.html where you will find text files of
          the listed animals and plants current through the end of Nov
          1994. They also have a list of candidate species, and the full
          text of the Endangered Species Act of 1973, as amended, should
          anyone be interested. Cheers,
