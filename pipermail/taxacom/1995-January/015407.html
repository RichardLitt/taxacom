1995 
The US Fish and Wildlife Service has issued a new listing of animal
species on their lists of candidates for future endangered/threatened
species status.  The list covers over 2000 species including all category
1, all category 2, and some category 3 candidates.  Basically it is a
corrected and updated compilation of previous lists, and does not propose
anything for endangered or threatened status.  The introduction includes
some useful comments regarding what categories 1-3 actually mean.  The
citation is FEDERAL REGISTER 59(219): 58982-59028, November 15, 1994.  It
is available from Division of Endangered Species, FWS, Mail Stop 452
ARLSQ, Washington, DC 20240 or regional offices.
