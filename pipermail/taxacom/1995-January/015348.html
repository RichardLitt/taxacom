I have not heard of a Tasmanian lion, but I think the animal you are
after is one of the thalasine (I know the spelling is wrong, but that is
the way the word is pronounced!) critters.  They were dog-sized, had a
stripe or two.  Last ones killed out in Tas and/or Australia late last
century or early this one.
