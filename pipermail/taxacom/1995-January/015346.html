I'm interested in this subject. I cuurently manage two huge databases, one on
genera and family level taxa in Curculionoidea (Insecta, Coleoptera), presently
with some 11,500 entries. The other is on species level taxa within the same
group, currently more or less complete until 1850, having now some 17,000
records, and progressing to present (now some 5,000 more records added).
This last is expected to reach some 80,000 entries.
I will be in the BMNH, Entomology Section, from 23 January to 3 February.
For more information, please, contact Dr. Christopher H. C. Lyal, in this
