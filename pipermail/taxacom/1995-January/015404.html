Check out the News section article "New Rule Could Squelch Shipments"
(SCIENCE [AAAS], 6 Jan 1995, pg 22), R. Stone) regarding the USFWS
draft/proposed regulation changes on "trafficking" in specimens, a
topic heavily discussed in entomo-l.

While the article isn't as current as entomo-l, it _does_ spread the
word about this issue to a far broader group of disciplines. (Even
uses the T-word --a section header no less.)
