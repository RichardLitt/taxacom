Bay Area Malacologists
1995 Gathering
1st Announcement

In keeping with the success of the 1994 gathering, we will meet again in 1995
at the California Academy of Sciences in San Francisco. The meeting date is
Saturday, March 11, 1995, from approx. 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. Mark your
calendars and plan to give a presentation if you attend. Terry Gosliner and I
hope that the phylogenetic theme pursued last year will be continued this
year. Additional information on attending and giving a talk will be posted to
MOLLUSCA and TAXACOM shortly. For further information, contact:
