I have been using Tyvek labels on a herpetological wet collection
for at least 15 years. All labels were typed using an ordinary
portable typewriter with an ink ribbon. In a few the ink has
disappeared but the impression of the text is still clearly
visible. I have not detected any deterioration of the material in
ethanol during this time. Hope this is of use.
