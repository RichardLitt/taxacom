Since there have been several inquiries here recently concerning locating
the Gray Card Index and other data bases at Harvard, thought it might be
worthwhile to list sites accessible via gopher and Netscape/Mosaic here:

Gray Cards
gopher://huh.harvard.edu/11/project_information/authority/botany/gray_cards

Arnold Arboretum
gopher://huh.harvard.edu/11/collections_info/aa

Herbarium
gopher://huh.harvard.edu/11/collections_info/huh

Museum of Comparative Zoology
gopher://huh.harvard.edu/11/collections_info/mcz

Harvard University overall WWW site with links to above under construction,
see
http://golgi.harvard.edu/harvard.html
