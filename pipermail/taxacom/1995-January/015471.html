On Wed, 25 Jan 1995, Susan Farmer wrote:

> I'm trying to find a copy of the Federal Endangered Species List.  I know
> it has to be Out There Somewhere.  Can somebody tell me where to find it?

The list:  Endangered & Threatened Wildlife and Plants
           (50 CFR 17.11 & 17.12)

The law:   Endangered Species Act of 1973.
           (PL 93-205. 16 USC 1531 et seq.)

Also try URL:

http://town.hall.org/environment/wild_soc/endanger.html
