The Division of Mammals at the KU Natural History Museum would like to
announce their presence on the net.  We now have a WWW site containing
general info about the collection, collection management policies (including
loan and destructive sampling protocols), a list of type specimens, and other
miscellany, with more to be added as time permits.  Our URL is:

http://ukanaix.cc.ukans.edu/~mammals/start.html

Enjoy--
