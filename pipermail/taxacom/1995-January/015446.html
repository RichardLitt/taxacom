I've used "Rite in the Rain" paper produced by the J.L. Darling
Corporation, Tacoma, WA 98421-3696, in alcohol for 3-5 yrs with good
results.  It is apparently available in sheets and rolls of various sizes.
I think it was originally developed for use in field notebooks for
surveyors.  Such field notebooks are pretty much standard equipment
among many ecologists I know.
