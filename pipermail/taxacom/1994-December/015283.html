1994 
                           Apomixis & Taxonomy

                                Workshop

                            8-11 August, 1995
                Institute of Botany, Academy of Sciences
                          Pruhonice near Prague
                             Czech Republic


                             FIRST CIRCULAR

The Workshop should give the participants an opportunity to present and discuss latest
results achieved in the fields of breeding system variation, mechanisms of apomixis,
genetic diversity of populations with presence of agamospermy, and taxonomic treatment
of apomictic groups. The term of the Workshop follows the 1995 IOPB Symposium
(including the excursions).

Preliminary programme

Each block of presentations will be opened by an invited speaker. Every evening will be
devoted to informal discussions.

Aug 8, Tue
Morning registration at the Club Hall of VSUOZ, Pruhonice.
Reproduction mode variation, Agamospermy vs. sexuality, Origin and mechanisms of
agamospermy.

Aug 9, Wed
Mechanisms of agamospermy, Diversity within and among populations of apomicts,
Agamospecies.

Aug 10, Thu
Principles of taxonomic treatment of groups with presence of agamospermy.

Aug 11, Fri
An excursion to the Prague vicinity (no extra costs).




Accommodation and Logistics

Pr honice, a nice village at the south-east margin of Prague, is an attractive place with
an old chateau and a large park, and represents a tourist centre, with great tradition in
botany and ornamental plant gardening, with a number of hotels, restaurants, shopping
centres etc.
     The Workshop takes place in the Club Hall (= Klubovna) of Institute of
Ornamental Plant Breeding, Pruhonice. Accommodation is available within five minutes'
walk from the Club Hall.
     The Pruhonice Chateau harbours Institute of Botany, Academy of Sciences, and
Botanical Section of National Museum (herbarium PR); it is possible to arrange
individual studies in the herbarium, as well as in the herbarium of Charles University,
Prague (PRC), each of the herbaria amounting to about two milion specimens.

Pruhonice is easily reached from Prague centre (bus, cab - about 20 min.). Prices of a
single/double room (incl. breakfast) do not exceed USD 45/65, but may be considerably
lower. Orders for accommodation on the application form should be sent to the
organizing committee as soon as possible because final prices may decrease with number
of participants announced in advance.
