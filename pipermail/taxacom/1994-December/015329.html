1994 
CALIFORNIA ACADEMY OF SCIENCES
SUMMER SYSTEMATICS INSTITUTE
1995


The California Academy of Sciences announces a new internship program
in Systematic Biology for summer, 1995.  The Summer Systematics
Institute matches 10 Under graduate students with Academy scientists
to conduct research, as well as participate in tours, seminars and
lectures related to biodiversity, evolutionary biology and global
change.  A $1200 stipend will be awarded to each intern.


General Information

The 1995 Summer Systematics Institute will last 10 weeks

12 June 1995 - 25 August 1995


All applications must be received by 15 February, 1995.  No late
applications can be considered.


Notification of status will be mailed to all applicants by 15 March, 1995


Application materials can be obtained from:
