1994 
CALIFORNIA ACADEMY OF SCIENCES
14TH ANNUAL
TILTON POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWSHIP IN SYSTEMATICS

The California Academy of Sciences announced the 14th Annual Tilton
Postdoctoral Fellowship in Systematics.  Applications are invited for
collections oriented research and curation in any of the fields of
systematics represented at the Academy.  The applicant's doctoral
degree must have been awarded within the past 5 years.  This 1-year
fellowship includes a stipend of $22,000 plus $1,000 for expenses.

To Apply:  Applicants should provide:
* A Curriculum Vitae
* Detailed description of the research to be undertaken during the
Fellowship period, including the desirability of working at the
Academy.
*Names, addresses and phone numbers of 3 references

Application materials should be addressed to:
Tilton Fellowship Committee
c/o Director of Research
California Academy of Sciences
Golden Gate Park
San Francisco, CA  94118

Deadlines:
Applications should be received no later than 1 February, 1995.
Award of the Fellowship will be made by 1 April, 1995
Starting date of Fellowship: 1 September, 1995

Inquiries concerning the Tilton Fellowship can be made by Telephone
(415-750-7277), Fax (415-750-7346) or email
(pkociolek at cas.calacademy.org)
