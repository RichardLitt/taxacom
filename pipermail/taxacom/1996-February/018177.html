School and undergraduate students are exempt from the DELTA registration
fees. Exemption may also be granted in cases of financial hardship. (This is
intended for people who are substantially worse off than we are; don't
forget that we work in a taxonomy department too!) Details are in the
Conditions of Use, which are included with the package as the file
DELTA.USE.

If your students are using the programs in course work, you must register,
but this is free unless you are also using the programs for other purposes
(e.g. research).
