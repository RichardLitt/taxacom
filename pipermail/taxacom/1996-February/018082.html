A new World Wide Web site has joined the Internet, at the address of

What is VanityMail ?

VanityMail is an original idea based on a Global E-Mail service that allows
many people to share the power of E-Mail and the fun of word combinations,
with a dash of Mnemonics thrown in..

Using the growing importance of a persons E-Mail address and delivering a
world class E-Mail service, VanityMail.com give any Internet user options
never dreamed of before in choosing their E-Mail or WWW address.

In short, what your E-Mail address is and how memorable, dynamic and
industry related it is has a growing importance that will only continue to
develop in the years to come.

If you have ever seen a persons E-Mail address that looks like
"Dmilligan at pst.dnsq.bc.ca", or 6298916765 at compuserve.com and compare it with
a address like "Mostly at Harmless.Com, Happy at Birthday.Net, Sweet at Aroma.com,
David at Letterman.Com, Slick at Legal-eagle.com and you start to see which style
makes an impression.

Through a process of assembling the DNS control of select domains we, have
created a quality group of really cool Domain Names. On any given E-Mail
system and in the Internet as a whole, addressing or finding a computer or
person is a function of the Domain Name System  called DNS. there can only
be one aroma.com and with a few exceptions only one flower at aroma.com in the
world.

The service being offered here allows any person, anywhere, to setup  any or
all of two E-mail services.
The first option is called an alias account and will allow the user to
select an user name@ Domain Name  from an extensive list, creating their own
unique E-mail address. This type of account will have all E-Mail sent to
their VanityMail address automatically forwarded to a existing E-Mail account.

The second option is a full POP Mail account residing on the Vanitymail
server, this type of account will allow you to send, store and retrieve your
E-Mail from the VanityMail server. The benefits of this option are that our
VanityMail server is very fast for retrieving and sending mail and your mail
account will be independent from your ISP or access provider.

A real bonus of both types of accounts, is that VanityMail Services will
allow you control over your E-Mail address so if you move or you change
access providers your E-Mail address will always remain the same, as the use
of E-Mail for primary communications increases, this will be a very
important feature.

As an extension of the services offered, VanityMail will provide both
personal hosting of Web sites and pointing of HTTP service contracted to use
the Domain Names of VanityMail Services.
