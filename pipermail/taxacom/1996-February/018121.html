There have been at least two papers written addressing this question:
        Dance, S. P., 1960. "Grimwood's Island", its geographical position
and its connection with Hugh Cuming.  Journal of Conchology, vol.24
pp.443-445 (including map).
        Rehder, H. A., 1959.  L'identite de l'Ile Grimwood.  Bulletin de
la Societe d'Etudes Oceaniennes, no.127/128 pp.46-48.

The conclusion was that Grimwood's Island is probably the atoll called
Fangatau (a.k.a. Fangataufa or Cockburn Island), southeast of Mururoa, in
the Tuamotu Archipelago.
