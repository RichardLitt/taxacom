Clark,
I just visited your website and ftp'd your woolbats font. Aside from all
the other interesting pictures, you have a serviceable Mars and Venus
sign in that. I found that they work better if I set the font size
slightly smaller on the male sign than the female sign to get the circles
the same size on printout. Also, bolding seems to darken them up a bit
when printed small.

Thanks for all your work on creating these font sets.

NOTE: To the list... Be sure to read Clark's philosophy relative to
sharing his copyrighted materials. I was inspired...hopefully this will
spread.

Best wishes,
