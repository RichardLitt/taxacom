>     I was directed to you to answer a request for a good botanical
>dictionary.
>     My mother is an avid, self-taught herbalist who occasionally
>presents to groups on topics botanical.  She is looking for a
>botanical dictionary that gives pronunciations of the Latin names as
>well as descriptions and (perhaps) uses.
>     I would greatly appreciate it if you would forward me the
>bibliographic entries (author/title will suffice) of some of your
>favorites.

Dear Mark,

There are many, but I will give you a few:

- A Glossary of Botanic Terms (with their derivation and accent),
  by B.Daydon Jackson (Hafner Publ. CO. INC. 1971)

- Stearn's Dictionary of Plant Names for Gardeners,
  by William T. Stearn (a Handbook on the Origin and Meaning of the
  Botanical Names of some Cultivated Plants; Sterling Publ. Co.Inc.; 1992)

- Plant Identification Terminology, An Illlustrated Glossary,
  by James G.- and M.W. Harris (Spring Lake Publ. Utah, 1994)

The latest is a very nice illustrated book.

Regards, Eric
