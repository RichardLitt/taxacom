1996 
                         INTERNSHIP

                             IN

                   BIOLOGICAL ILLUSTRATION

                           AT THE

               CALIFORNIA ACADEMY OF SCIENCES



The California Academy of Sciences announces an internship in biological
illustration.  The internship is open to an undergraduate student
interested in developing illustration techniques related to biological
specimens.  The intern will work with one or more Academy scientists to
develop illustrations that may be used in scientific publications. The
Academy is a comprehensive natural history museum supporting research and
collection activities in the disciplines of Anthropology, Botany, Geology,
Invertebrate Zoology, Entomology, Diatom Research, Ichthyology,
Herpetology, Ornithology and Mammalogy.


Internship Period:  Full time 10 weeks during the summer.

Stipend:  $1500 for the Internship Period.

To Apply:  Send a letter of interest, resume, sample of previous work and
letter of recommendation from a faculty member to:

Biological Illustration Internship
c/o Director of Research
California Academy of Sciences
Golden Gate Park
San Francisco, CA  94118

All materials must be received at the Academy by 30 April, 1996.

Questions can be addressed to the Director of Research at 415-750-7277 or
via email ayingling at cas.calacademy.org.


This internship is made possible in part by a gift from the Fellows of the
California Academy of Sciences.
