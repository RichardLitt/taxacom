VERTEBRATE PALEONTOLOGIST

The Department of Geological Sciences of the University of Texas at Austin
is now seeking a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Quaternary vertebrate
paleontology, for an appointment to begin September 1, 1997.  The successful
candidate will conduct research and direct education in Quaternary
vertebrate paleontology, focusing on collections-based research in one or
more of the following areas:  paleoclimate, Quaternary trends in
biodiversity, morphological and molecular systematics, and human impact on
Nature.  A strong field program is an essential component of this research.
The successful candidate will employ the most modern analytic and
computational methods in both teaching and research, and will add new
courses to our paleontology curriculum at undergraduate and graduate levels.
A Ph.D. is required.  Please send statement of research and teaching
interests, resume, names and addresses of three to five references, plus any
supplemental information to:  Chair, Search Committee, Department of
Geological Sciences C1100, The University of Texas, Austin, Texas 78712.
Closing date for applications is February 28, 1997.  The University of Texas
is an equal opportunity/affirmative action employer.

For additional information on the WWW:

        http://www.utexas.edu/

        http://www.utexas.edu/cons/geo

General information on the Vertebrate Paleontology and Radiocarbon Laboratory:
        http://www.utexas.edu/research/vprl/

Please forward this announcement to interested individuals and appropriate
