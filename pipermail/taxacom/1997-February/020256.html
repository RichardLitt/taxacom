Boy, are they ever.  For a wonderful example of acknowledgments (not to
mention titles) gone berserk, check out the following:

Moran, R. 1962.  Cneoridium dumosum (Nuttall) Hooker f. collected March 26,
1960, at an elevation of about 1450 meters on Cerro Quemazon, 15 miles south

cooperation this work would never have been possible."

Where's a good copy editor when you REALLY need one?
