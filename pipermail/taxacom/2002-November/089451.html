VASCULAR PLANT SYSTEMATICS TENURE-TRACK POSITION
Department of Biological Sciences  <www.humboldt.edu/~biosci/>
Humboldt State University

Professional Qualifications: A Ph.D. in the biological sciences from an
accredited university or college is required at the time of appointment.
Expertise in the systematics of vascular plants is essential. Preference
will be given to broadly-trained organismal botanists who can demonstrate
experience with curating herbarium collections. Teaching experience is
essential, at least at the graduate assistant level.

Candidates should be well-versed in both traditional and modern approaches
to studying the diversity of vascular plants and must be committed to
teaching in the field, laboratory, and herbarium.

Dedication to superior instruction of undergraduates is a long-standing
tradition at Humboldt State University and is the primary criterion in
promotion and tenure considerations. The Department of Biological Sciences
is known for providing students with balanced and well-rounded degrees in
biology, botany, and zoology. The candidate must have a demonstrated
interest and ability to teach a broad range of courses to diverse students
(majors, general education students, etc.). Candidates whose
characteristics and qualifications also suit them to prepare individuals
to function in a culturally and ethnically diverse society are especially
encouraged to apply.

Professional Duties: The successful candidate will teach courses in
introductory and advanced plant taxonomy. Other course assignments may
include Ethnobotany and Agrostology, depending on experience and
departmental needs. The candidate will be encouraged to develop specialty
courses at the upper division and graduate level. The successful candidate
will assume the duties of Curator of the Vascular Plant Herbarium. The
candidate will also advise undergraduate students in Biology or Botany and
supervise Master?s level research. Development of a research program is
expected. The candidate will also serve on faculty committees and
otherwise assist the functioning of the university.

General Information: Humboldt State University is committed to achieving
the goals of equal opportunity and endeavors to employ faculty and staff
of the highest quality reflecting the ethnic and cultural diversity of the
State. For more information see www.humboldt.edu or
www.humboldt.edu/~biosci.

Rank & Salary: Rank and salary are dependent upon the appointee's
qualifications and experience. The current CSU Salary Structure is located
at <www.humboldt.edu/~facpers/Salary%20Schedule%2002-03.xls>

Application: Qualified candidates should submit a detailed curriculum
vitae or resum?, undergraduate and graduate transcripts of academic work,
a statement on teaching philosophy, statement of research interests, and
three recent letters of recommendation from persons familiar with the
candidate's professional preparation and experience.
