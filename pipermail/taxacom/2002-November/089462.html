BIOD-IBERIA consists of the research facilities (including natural
history collections, laboratories, etc) of the National Museum of
Natural Sciences of Madrid (MNCN) and the Royal Botanic Gardens (RJB),
both institutions part of the Spanish Council for Scientific Research
(CSIC).

As you may know, the European Union is supporting a programme under the
5th FP (Improving Human Potential) that enables researchers from all
over Europe to undertake research visits (up to 3 months) to
BIOD-IBERIA.

The EU covers travel and accommodation expenses, per diem, as well as
research related costs (bench fees, consumables, etc).

The next deadline is 30th November 2002. So, if you are interested in
the programme, please visit either of the following web pages and fill
the electronic form, which includes a brief description of the project
you propose to undertake during your visit:

http://www.museociencias.com/     ("Biod-Iberia Research Grants")
 or
http://www.rjb.csic.es/

Regards,

G. Nieto Feliner
