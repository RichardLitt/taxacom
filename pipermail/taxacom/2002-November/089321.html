Here are a couple of literature citations (plus a search engine suggestion):
     (1) James J. Childress, 1995.  "Are there physiological and biochemical
adaptations of metabolism in deep-sea animals?".  Trends in Ecology and
Evolution, Vol. 10:30-36.
     (2) Allen G. Gibbs, 1997.  "Biochemistry at depth".  Fish Physiology,
Vol. XVI, pp. 239-278.
     (3) And although many adaptations are due to life at high pressures,
many others are due to low oxygen levels.  For specific information on that,
I would suggest going to your favorite search engine and do a search for:
minimum oxygen zone adaptations.  I tried this at google.com, and I got over
2,700 hits (many of which looked very promising).  Good luck.
