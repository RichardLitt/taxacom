Science is a method and works best when applied without preconceptions, or
at least when one's preconceptions are understood. Not withstanding, there
have been many people of diverse religious beliefs that have applied the
method successfully. Science would not have made much progress without the
foundations such people provided, particularly in its "early" years. I find
attempts to attack the method by some people of faith to be repugnant. I
also find the attitude of some "true believers" in the scientific community
regarding religious belief equally repugnant. We chose the conceptual
frameworks that best fit our humble accumulations of data and live with the
consequences.

Phil Bunch
