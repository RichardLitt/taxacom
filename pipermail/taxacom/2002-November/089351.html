Actually quite a lot of the "historical" Web is indeed archived and
retrievable, via the "Wayback Machine" at http://web.archive.org . But, as
usual, there are caveats. Not all web pages lend themselves to archiving via
the mechanism used by that site (for example, pages generated dynamically
from a database, or those otherwise restricting Web "crawlers"). And there's
no real guarantee that the "Wayback Machine" will always be there.
