The same data can give different phylogenies. With the same data set, you
can get (AB)C supported by 3 advanced characters, (AC)B supported by 2
advanced characters, and (BC)A supported by 2 advanced characters. We then
have 3 phylogenies with nearly the same support. Do "all scientists choose
the simplest explanation," in this case? Or do we have what is essentially a
tie? At what point would we have sufficient reliability that a clade might
actually be named (without people laughing)?
