2002 
The Fourth Biennial Conference of the Systematics Association

Trinity College, Dublin, Ireland

18-22 August 2003

Thematic sessions include "Biodiversity databases; change and
challenge", "The systematics of species-rich taxa" and "Human evolution
and environments"
However, the meeting is for everyone, and contributions - posters or
presentations - can be on any topic. Students are particularly
encouraged to participate. There is a bursary scheme which will
contribute to the costs of registration, accommodation and subsistence
for students and recently graduated students.

More details from
http://www.systass.org/biennial2003/index.html
