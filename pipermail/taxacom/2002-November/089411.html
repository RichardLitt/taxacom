Geoff,
     Just because Bathymodiolus species are hydrothermal vent specialists
(at least as adults) does not mean that their larvae cannot be transported
in ship ballast water.  Especially 20th Century ships which might have
enough sulphur contaminants to keep even adults alive along enough to
transport.
     And if they have non-feeding larvae, such transport would be even
easier.  For all we know oil tankers might attract the larvae of such
mussels (like insects are to pheromones), and be transporting them all over
the place.  So much we don't know.
          ----- Cheers,   Ken
