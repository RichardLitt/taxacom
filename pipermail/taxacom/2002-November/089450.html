> (Calpe eustrigata) of Malaysia is exclusively a blood feeder.  Or are they

I have not heard of the recent discovery, but there is a really great
article on Calpe eustrigata:

Banziger, Hans 1971. Bloodsucking moths of Malaya. Fauna 1: 4-16.

(A real shame that Fauna: The Zoological Magazine didn't survive.)

I do not think that the feeding is restricted to females for reproduction,
but it is simply how the adults feed (perhaps not exclusively either?).
Others in the genus Calpe feed by piercing fruits. Additionally, a variety
of lepidopterans from a few families have been known to feed on spilled
mammalian blood, or even get it from excess blood expelled by blood-filled
mosquitoes.

Fun stuff!
