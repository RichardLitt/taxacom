The multiplication sign is used to denote taxa believed to be of hybrid
origin.  Under ICBN, you have a choice of denoting a hybrid via formula
(Quercus marilandica X Q. velutina) or via a binomial (Quercus
Xbushii).  Appendix I of the ICBN covers naming hybrids.  I don't much like
binomials for hybrids, unless it is something that actually has an
ecological role.  For example, Quercus Xbushii is found on sandy soils in
SE Iowa, ~50 km north of the range limit of one parent, Q.
marilandica.  Clearly, it has taken on "a life of its own" and has some
claim to a name.
