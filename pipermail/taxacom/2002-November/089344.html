At 10:17 PM 11/10/02 -0800, Curtis Clark wrote:
>I'm not sure what you mean by "formal synonymies". It seems that either
>source could only generate nomina nuda, but if those are in use, they
>should be cited.

I prefer NOT to include names that are Not Validly Published (as defined by
ICBN) in the formal listing of nomenclature that usually starts off the
treatment of a species in a monographic account.  They have no standing
under the Code and emphatically are NOT names as defined by the
Code.  Instead, I would mention them in the text, saying, "Johnson (1878)
called this Planta alba but that name was never validated ..."
