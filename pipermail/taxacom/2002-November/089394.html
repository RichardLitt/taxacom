Hello folks,

Starting December 1, the ALL Species Foundation will close its San Francisco
office because of a lack of general operational funding. However, both the
Foundation and the idea behind it will continue. Although several ALL
initiatives are moving forward, ALL will be laying off staff and is now
searching for partners who are interested in acquiring, customizing, and/or
integrating the variety of biodiverity informatics applications we have created
over the past year.  If you're interested in any of the applications, please
contact me by November 22nd.

The applications include: the species toolkit search engine at
http://www.speciestoolkit.org/, a system for creating web pages for
taxonomists, and an application for displaying and browsing specimen images.

If this sounds interesting to you, please visit:
http://www.all-species.org/ipopportunities.html
for more information.

Thanks,
Dave Thau
