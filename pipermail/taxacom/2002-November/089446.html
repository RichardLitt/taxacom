To unsubscribe from Taxacom, send email to listserv at usobi.org
with this one-line command (only) in the body of your email
message:

UNSUB TAXACOM

You must send the command FROM your subscribed email address. If
you can no longer access that subscribed userid, send me a
private note asking me to delete your subscription (tell me what
you believe the old address is so I know what to delete)
