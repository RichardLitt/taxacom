2002 
A year before the U.S. NSF Assembling the Tree of Life competition, under
the Biocomplexity in the Environment competition, four awards were made for
large-scale Tree of Life studies, including DEB 0120635 (to Cunningham,
Martin, Regier, Thorne, and Shultz) for "Deep Arthropod Phylogeny from 100
Targeted Nuclear-Coding Sequences" and including crustaceans in the taxon
sampling. The three other Tree of Life awards made through that 2001
competition are: DEB 0120709 (plants), EAR 0120646 (annelids), and DEB
0120718 (hexapods). Award abstracts are posted on the NSF website
(www.nsf.gov).
        Sincerely,
        James Rodman
