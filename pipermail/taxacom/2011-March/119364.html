Thank you all who replied through or off the list!

I looked at the number of genera of Rosoideae/Rosaceae instead (perhaps 
sufficient, because monophagous insects often do not discriminate 
between closely related host species). Assuming that there are 17/36 
genera in Europe and 22/55 in China, there should be roughly 30-40 
species of Empria in China (about 12 have been found so far). Seems likely.

Best wishes,
Marko
