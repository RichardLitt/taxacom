The Charles Horton Peck correspondence for 1865-1879 with George W.
Clinton is presented online in its entirety. This is a collection of
letters physically housed at the Buffalo Museum of Science in Buffalo,
New York. The letters include discussions of bryology and mycology, and
provide a window on mid 1800s American botany. This is part of a larger
work in progress, the Correspondence of G. W. Clinton, transcribed and
annotated by Clinton Papers editor P. M. Eckel.
