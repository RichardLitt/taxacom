Ed Baker has constructed an online presentation of the Draft BioCode that is
available for comments on each section. This way you can follow and
contribute to discussion threads on each Article. This will keep discussion
more focused and easier to retrieve for input in later drafts:

http://iczn.org/category/biocode/biocode

Furthermore, we are constructing links with the corresponding sections in
each of the 'special Codes', though this is still a work in progress. As an
example, see here:
http://iczn.org/content/principle-1
This is not yet ready for inputting comments, but as sections become they
ready will become visible in the commenting area.

We look forward to your input!
