1994 
Announcement of a new newsletter for anyone interested in plants belonging
to the family Cucurbitaceae. The Cucurbit Network publishes twice a year
(beginning Spring 1994) The Cucurbit Network News. To join send $5 ($10
outside of North America) to: The Cucurbit Network, P.O. Box 560494, Miami,
FL 33256. For more information e-mail me at: tcandre at med.cornell.edu. Let
me know if you would like to receive a flyer before deciding whether to
become a member.

Thomas Andres
