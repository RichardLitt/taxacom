As an addendum to Scott Miller's list of biological collections
accessible over the Internet, I would like to add that the electronic
version of the fish collection catalog at the Texas Natural History
Collection is indeed complete.  To the best of my knowledge, this
database remains one of the few instances in which the data for an entire
collection's holdings is available over the Internet.

Carol K. Malcolm
Texas Natural History Collection
Texas Memorial Museum-UT Austin
