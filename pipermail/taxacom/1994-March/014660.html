12 March 1994

    To Scott Miller's list of Biological Collections via gophers should be
added the plant specimen database for the flora of Mount Kinabalu in Borneo.
This database has been available since last fall on the Biodiversity and
Biological Collections Gopher at Harvard.  The menu hierarchy is: Biodiversity
Information Resources; Floras; Flora of Mount Kinabalu, Sabah, Malaysia.
    Following Scott's tabular organization, this database should be listed as
follows:

    SUBJECT                                         SIZE      COMPLETE
    =====================================================================
    PLANTS

    Flora of Mount Kinabalu                        16,300        no

    ---------------------------------------------------------------------

    Relevant references in which segments of the database have been published
are:

Beaman, J. H. and Beaman, R. S.  1993.  The gymnosperms of Mount Kinabalu.
     Contr. Univ. Michigan Herb. 19: 307-340.

Parris, B. S., Beaman, R. S., and Beaman, J. H.  1992.  The Plants of Mount
     Kinabalu: 1. Ferns and Fern Allies.  Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.  165 pp.
     + 5 pl.

Wood, J. J., Beaman, R. S., and Beaman, J. H.  1993.  The Plants of Mount
     Kinabalu: 2. Orchids.  Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.  xii + 411 pp. + 84
     pl.
