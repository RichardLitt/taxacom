> Dear Colleagues,
>
> Please accept my apologies for the cross-posting.
>
> Many of you contributed to discussions this past week in response to the
> article published in Nature on "Taxonomy Anarchy" (
> https://www.nature.com/news/taxonomy-anarchy-hampers-conservation-1.22064).
> Many are also among the 80 people from at least 26 countries who have
> consented to serve as authors on an article that has been drafted in
> response.  Although some have suggested that no response is warranted,
> there appears to be near-universal agreement in the assessment of both the
> wisdom and the practicality of implementing the proposal included in the
> Nature article.
>
> The reason for this email is to let you know that the draft response is
> now close to final form, and we expect to submit it for publication next
> week.  If anyone who has not already seen the draft would like to review
> it, and especially if anyone not already listed in the document as an
> author would like to serve as an author, please let me know.
