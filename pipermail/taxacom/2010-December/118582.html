The first list of published illustrations and images extracted from the
GrassWorld database has been downloaded to the GrassWorld Scratchpad
http://grassworld.myspecies.info/ .  It comprises most of the figures
from the grass literature that I have been able to access, but does not
include earlier illustrations from Index Londinensis. In cases where I
have not been able to find illustrations of figures or plates I have
noted these.  Hopefully these gaps will be filled, by collaborators to
GrassWorld, where illustrations or plates are known to them.  Names
follow the classification recently placed on the  GrassWorld Scratchpad.
