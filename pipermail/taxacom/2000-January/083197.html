I am interested in establishing contacts with other botanists who are
interested in the systematics of Chamaesyce (Euphorbiaceae) in
particular with those familiar with C. prostrata and E. chamaesyce in N.
America and Europe. I have just commenced a revision of the Australasian
taxa and I am also interested in the weedy species that have been
introduced into Australia. Various names have been applied to these and
their correct identity needs to be established.
