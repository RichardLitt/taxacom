Dear all:
It seems that my idle curiosity as to whether there was a 'market' in bacteriological names was misconstrued by some.
I do not know anything of Boris Becker except that he is a tennis star and no thought of impugning him in any way.
I did know that Dr Brian Tindall, a microbial taxonomist, works at DMZ where the lists of bacterial names are maintained, and so I thought that his information had come from that source. It now appears that it was from the news media, not from his professional links, and moreover, that the recognition was honorific not purchased.
My real crime was inadvertantly including the Taxacom list in my mail to Brian, thereby adding to your bandwidth, quite unnecessarily.  
John McNeill
