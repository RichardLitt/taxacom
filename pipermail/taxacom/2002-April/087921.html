Ken and others,

There is growing acceptance, at least among those cladistically minded, that the
termites, cockroaches and mantids represent a monophylum, hence considered the
order Dictyoptera.

The internal structure of the Dictyoptera is a moot point (especially the placement of
Cryptocercidae = woodroaches), but the following is likely: Blattaria + Cryptocercidae
is sister to Mantodea, with the Isoptera as sister group to that complex.
