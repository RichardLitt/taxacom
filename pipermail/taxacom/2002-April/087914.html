John O. Corliss, at the spry young age of 80, is the proud father of
a new baby girl.  As many zoologists know, John served many years
(1972 - 96) as a commissioner for the International Code of
Zoological Nomenclature, and he was a co-editor of the 1985 edition
of the code.  The baby, Catharine Megan, was born on April 9, was 21
3/4 inches (55.25 cm) in length, and weighed 9 lb 11 oz. (4.4 kg).
John claims to be busier than ever these days, but he is a great
correspondent and can be reached at the following addresses:

e-mail: jocchezmoi at aol.com

postal:  P.O. box 2729 (or 730 Yale Road), Bala Cynwyd, PA 19004 USA

I am sure he would appreciate cards or letters from old friends.

Sincerely,
Robert A. Andersen
