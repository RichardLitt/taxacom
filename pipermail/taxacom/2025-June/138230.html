> Dear Tony,
>
> The statement "Chilodontidae Bronn, 1859 (Ciliophora) is now
> unavailable" should be incorrect - a name once available remains so
> (Art. 10.6) unless the Commission has ruled otherwise.
> Art. 11.7.1 states that a family-group name must meet the criteria "when
> first published". When the name was established in 1859 the genus-group
> name was not suppressed. Art. 11.7.1.5 does not come into effect here.
>
> Chilodontidae Bronn, 1859 was suppressed, but not ruled to be
> unavailable. The Commission does usually not rule names to be unavailable.
>
> Best wishes
> Francisco

> > submitted in 2011), namely (as I understand it):
> >
> > - Chilodontidae Eigenmann, 1910 (not 1903 as previously given) in Pisces
> is
> > ruled to be an unjustified emendation (by Fowler, unsure what date) of
> > Chilodidae Eigenmann, 1910, not a justified one as previously given, so
> the
> > correct name for this fish family is now Chilodidae (the ICZN ruling
> > actually deals with these names at the level of subfamily)
> >
> > - Chilodontidae Wenz, 1938 in Mollusca is therefore ruled to be not
> > preoccupied, so the proposed replacement name Chilodontaidae (e.g. as
> used
> > in Bouchet et al., 2017) is no longer required (again dealt with at the
> > level of subfamily)
> >
> > - Chilodontidae Bronn, 1859 (Ciliophora) is now unavailable since its
> type
> > genus, Chilodon Ehrenberg, 1834 is now suppressed for priority (I have
> that
> > as replaced by Chilodonellidae Deroux, 1970. in IRMNG, hopefully that is
> > correct).
