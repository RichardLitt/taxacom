If I were to make a stab at the classification aspect (probably totally
uninformed with respect to taxonomic niceties) I would put humans and
orangutans together as Hominidae, and Pan-Gorilla as Pongidae, and then
these collectively under Hominoidea. Orangutans are like humans in having
some prominent autapomorphies so they are just as "accelerated" in their
own respect.
