2004 
A new version of Flora Zambesiaca online is available on the
Royal Botanic Gardens Kew website http://www.kew.org/efloras/
This latest release now includes the dichotomous keys to all of the
taxa and allows easy browsing of the taxonomic hierarchy. The
"Full Search" query now features additional pick lists for the
endemic status of the taxa and their habitats.
