1993 
                           ASSISTANT CURATOR

   The Department of Botany and Plant Pathology, Michigan State University,
invites applications for a permanent position as Assistant Curator of the
Michigan State University Herbarium.  The successful candidate will be
expected to assist the Director in maintaining an active exchange program,
updating records, incorporating into a database existing and new collections,
overseeing routine operations of the Herbarium, and coordinating a volunteer
network. Minimum requirements include a degree in botany or plant systematics
involving monographic or floristic experience with tropical plants.  A
familiarity with the flora, botanical literature, and geography of the
Neotropical region is desirable. Applicants should call (517) 336-1662 for an
application form. Please reference position number C30106.  For additional
information applicants are encouraged to contact: Dr. Jose L. Panero,
Director, Michigan State University Herbarium, Department of Botany and Plant
Pathology, Michigan State University, East Lansing, MI 48824-1312.  Phone
(517) 355-4696, fax (517) 353-1926. The closing date is 28 January 1994.  If
you are unable to reach Dr. Panero in the immediate future, you may call or
fax John H. Beaman at the same numbers.
