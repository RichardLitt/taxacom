NEWS

     INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON ECOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT.

              DRAKE BAY, PENINSULA DE OSA,
              Costa Rica - Central America.
                  June 20-24th, 1994
                    (Indian summer)

                                               BULLETIN NO. 5.

==============================================================

    RECENTLY WE WROTE AN AGREEMENT WITH THE EDITOR OF THE JOURNAL OF
TROPICAL BIOLOGY AIMED TO PUBLISH AS A SPECIAL ISSUE THE PROCEEDINGS
OF THE INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON ECOLOGY AND ENVIRONMENT TO BE HELD
IN COSTA RICA DURING JUNE 1994.   THIS JOURNAL IS DISTRIBUTED AROUND
THE WORLD BY THE BRITISH NATURAL HISTORY BOOK SERVICE.  WE HOPE OUR
ACHIEVEMENT WILL BE OF SPECIAL INTEREST FOR YOU TO LECTURE IN THE
CONFERENCE.

FOR INFORMATION ABOUT THE CONFERENCE OR ABOUT THIS JOURNAL, PLEASE
