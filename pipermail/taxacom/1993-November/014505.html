             is glad to greet you as a participant of the

                              FOUNDERS

               Our invitation is specially extended to:
     - leaders  and  specialists  of  national and international natu-
                        re-protective bodies;
     - Russian and national companies, firms, organizations and funds;
     - public nature-protective movements and unions.

                         Purpose of the Forum

     - business and commercial success of the Forum participants;
     - development of national ecological business and ecological reo-
rientation of Russian economy using market methods in close  co-opera-
tion with the state;
     - to get foreign capital and international ecological organizati-
ons to  take part in the settlement of Russian ecological and economic
problems;
     - increase of Russian business role in the settlement of interna-
tional and global ecological problems;
     - co-operation of state authorities, industrial and business cir-
cles and public organizations in the elaboration of principles of  na-
tional ecological policy, in the choice and realization of strategy of
russian-ecological reconstruction&

                     Summary program of the Forum

     - United meeting of the Forum participants "About national ecolo-
gical policy of Russia" (3 June 1994);
     - Governmental Programmes;
     - Industrial and business Programme:
       The 2nd International Scientific-practical Congress "Ecology of
Russia" (6-10 June 1994);
       The 2nd International Exhibition-Fair "Ecology of Russia" (4-12
June 1994);
       "Green book of Russia" (3 June 1994);
     - Programme of public organizations;
     - Open report meeting "Ecology of  Russia:   problems,  projects,
proposals (in accordance with the Forum results) (5-15 July 1994).

               United Meeting of the Forum participants

     - Report of the National Organization Committee:  "Declaration of
the Forum: "About national ecological policy of Russia".
     - Supplementary  reports of the main parts of the Declaration and
the most important complex and having  a  special  purpose  ecological
projects and programmes (supplementary reports will be chosen from the
best problem reports, submitted at the Forum).
     - Taking the Declaration "as a basis".

         The 2nd International Scientific-Practical Congress
             and the Exhibition-Fair "Ecology of Russia"

     Reports R & D and production of  the  whole  spectrum  of  ecolo-
gy-oriented economy,  science  and technology,  service sphere will be
presented at the Congress and the Exhibition-Fair. United Theme of the
Congress Sections and Exhibition-Fair exposition.

     - Ecology and economy (state,  business,  society,  international
relations).
     - Air protection (cleaning technology,  monitoring, having a spe-
cial purpose programmes).
     - Water protection (cleaning,  water management, resources, prog-
rammes).
     - Land-protection  (production  and consumption waste management,
processing technologies, secondary resources, cyclic technologies).
     - Resource-saving and ecologically-clean technologies and produc-
tion.
     - Territorial ecosystems,  economy,  investments (territorial in-
dustrial complexes, regions, Russia as a whole, global ecosystem).
     - Measuring, monitoring, analysis.
     - Medical and ecological R & D and services.

     Synchronous holding of the Congress and the Exhibition-Fair, as a
single whole, with a united theme, gives an opportunity to present for
the Russian ecological market the products at all stages the Technolo-
gical cycle  -  from complex ecological economic projects,  scientific
ideas and R & D to the production and sale of finished products.

                        "Green Book of Russia"

     "Green book of Russia" will be published as a set of booklets  in
10 parts:
     Part 1.  United meeting of the Forum participants. Declaration of
the Forum "About national ecological policy of Russia".  Theses of the
repots. Information. Advertising.
     Parts 2-9.  The  2nd  International scientific-practical Congress
"Ecology of Russia".  Sections 1-8. Programs and theses of the section
repots. Programs  of  the  previous (the 1st Congress "Ecology of Rus-
sia-93" sections. Informations. Advertising.
     Part 10.  The  2nd International Exhibition-Fair "Ecology of Rus-
sia". Catalogue of participants of the  previous  (the  1st  Exhibiti-
on-Fair "Ecology of Russia-93". Information. Advertising.
     Report theses and firm annotations will be published  in  Russian
and English.  The book will be published by the time of the Forum open-
ning.

               Open Report meeting "Ecology of Russia"
                    problems, projects, proposals
                (on accordance with the Forum results)

     Meeting of the National Organization Committee "Ecology  of  Rus-
sia" with  founders  and invitation of the Forum participants. Discus-
sing and confirming of the Forum results.  Adoption  of  the  measures
programme "Ecology-95".

  The World  Forum  "Ecology  of  Planet Earth.  The third thousand
   years". "Green book of Planet Earth. The third thousand years".

     The National Organization Committee plans to hold the measures  of
series "Ecology  of  Russia" yearly,  timing to the 5 June - The World
Environment Day,  enhancing the experience, scale and level of organi-
sation of the programs included in these measures,  in order to:  hold
The World Forum "Ecology of Planet Earth. The third thousand years" on
5-15 June, 2001 in Moscow and publish "Green book of Planet Earth. The
third thousand years".

     The National Organization Committee "Ecology of  Russia"  expects
that the  World Forum-2001 will be one of the Russian contributions to
the "Rio process" started by the high-level UN Conference of  Environ-
ment and Development (Rio de Janeiro, 3-14, 1994).

     The National  Organization  Committee "Ecology of Russia" is sure
that a high status of the National Forum "Ecology  of  Russia",  prog-
rams, a strong interest which the Russian and world business communiti-
es, international environmental and financial organizations display  to
these programs,  and  active  support  and direct participation of the
highest state authorities of Russia in the Forum  programs  will  give
every participant  an  unique opportunity to strengthen prestige of his
company, sign the most profitable sale and purchase contracts, as well
sa contracts for diverse projects and services, joint co-operation ag-
reements, contracts for participation in the investment projects,  for
organizing joint measures and other forms of the co-operation with the
international business.

     Participation in the Forum programs will give you an unique chan-
ce:
     - to find the best way for the settlement of your own problems;
     - to  strengthen  your reputation in the international ecological
business circles and  environmental communities;
     - to  make  a  valuable contribution to the settlement of Russian
ecological problems.

     With your participation the National Forum  "Ecology  of  Russia"
will become  one of the biggest events in the business and public life
of Russia and get a wide international resonance.

     If you want to participate please not be late to send your appli-
cations for  participation,  proposals on co-operation and requests to
the address:
