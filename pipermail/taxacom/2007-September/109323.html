Dear all:

I would appreciate your help spreading the word about the Jessup and McHenry
Awards (http://www.ansp.org/research/opportunities/jessup_mchenry.php)
offered by the Academy of Natural Sciences.

The McHenry Fund is restricted to botanists, who are invited to visit us to
study our botanical collection. (See collectors represented at
http://www.ansp.org/research/biodiv/botany/pdf.Mears1981.pdf.)

The deadline is October 1st.

Many thanks!

Alina.
