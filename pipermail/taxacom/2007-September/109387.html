The picture is correct (I think it kind of adds insult to injury that it's
the same as Fig. 1A in the paper), but the mushroom - which, if the paper
is correct, is indeed the oldest known - is Palaeoagaracites antiquus,
which is covered in the parasitic fungus Mycetophagites atrebora, which in
turn is hyperparasitized by Entropezites patricii.  The last, which is
what it's being advertised as, is probably the most scientifically
significant but just looks like some very, very tiny structures inside the
Mycetophagites hyphae that I as a non-mycologist would probably take no
notice of whatsoever.
