I'm building a database of journal names, abbreviations, and ISSN  
(International Standard Serial Number), mainly to help my reference  
parsing tools that David Shorthouse alluded to in an earlier post  
(see also http://ispiders.blogspot.com/2007/08/gimme-that-scientific- 
paper-part-iii.html).

As many of you know, there are all manner of abbreviations for  
journal names in existence, so trying to automatically paper  
bibliographic citations can be a nightmare. I've created a simple  
search form for my database (http://bioguid.info/journal.php ). If  
you enter a journal name or abbreviation it will try and find a  
match, and displays a list of what it found, ranked by a score.

I'd welcome any feedback (e.g., journals not found, incorrect  
matches), as well as any electronic lists people may have of  
abbreviations. The bulk of the database was seeded by data from  
PubMed, but this obviously doesn't cover a lot of taxonomic journals.  
The better this journal database, the better the reference parsing  
tools will become.
