Post doc. position in plant mitochondrial evolution 

at Laboratory of Molecular Systematics, Natural History Museum of
Denmark, University of Copenhagen

The post doc. position is associated with the project "Molecular
Systematics - Limits and Challenges" financed by the Danish Natural
Science Research Council. The project is centered on aspects of
mitochondrial gene evolution including occurrence and formation of
processed paralogs, transfer of mitochondrial genes to the nucleus, and
substitution rate changes. Research will have a phylogenetic focus and
it will be centered on the monocots. For a more detailed project
description, application requirements, and deadline; see
http://snm.ku.dk/opslag/evolution/
