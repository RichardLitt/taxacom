2008 
The Virginia Museum of Natural History, which recently opened a new
90,000 square foot, purpose-built facility, invites applications for two
assistant curator positions.

One position requires expertise in recent invertebrates; the other
requires expertise in paleontology. These are full-time, twelve-month,
career-track positions, starting June, 2008. 

The full job descriptions and application instructions are available at:
www.vmnh.net.  Only online applications are accepted and must be
submitted no later than 5 p.m. March 31, 2008. 


Nancy D. Moncrief

Curator of Mammalogy
Virginia Museum of Natural History
21 Starling Avenue
Martinsville, VA 24112, USA
