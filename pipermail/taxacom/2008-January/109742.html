Subject: Conducting Research in Malaysia - Important Notes for Researchers

 

Dear Colleague,

 

Let me remind you the rules and regulation conducting research in Malaysia.


 

Kindly review the following website for research application in Malaysia.  

 

This will enhance your understanding in carrying out research in Malaysia.
This may also expedite your application before your arrival in Malaysia.  

 

Please also be reminded that you are strongly adviced not to make any plan
of conducting your surveys or research (data / sample collection) before
recieving any approval from the authority.

 

The website you should closely review is

 

http://www.epu.gov.my/New%20Folder/application%20and%20approval/undertaking%
20research(new).htm

 

Should you have any questions regarding conducting research in Malaysia,
kindly contact the governing body - Economic Planning Unit of Malaysia
(EPU).  

 

 

 

best wishes,
