Here I agree with both Paddy and Anna.

General discussions of functionality - what systems do, what they  
should do and what they should not do etc - should probably occur on  
Taxacom.

Discussions of how they do it (techie stuff) or detailed discussions  
of controlled vocabularies etc - should probably be somewhere else and  
TDWG has built a collaboration environment of wikis and mailing lists  
to facilitate precisely this kind of activity.


Roger
