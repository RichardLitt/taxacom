New internet site: Euphorbiaceae of Thailand

Check out: nhncml.leidenuniv.nl/thaieuph

The site allows you to identify up to genus, and for some genera up to
species level. Descriptions, nomenclature, literature references,
distributions (in Thailand and worldwide), uses,  and Thai names are
provided. Where present photos, drawings and distribution maps have been
added.

The site also allows searches on scientific names (accepted and synonyms),
vernacular names (in Thai and transliterated), and uses (per commodity
group). A list of contributors with addresses is provided.

The site will be extended in the future with more descriptions, keys and
especially plates.
