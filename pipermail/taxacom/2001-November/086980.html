2001 
Field Museum's Invertebrate Division has completed a massive retrospective
computerization and archival-quality rehousing project of its entire
pulmonate land snail collection.  In addition to other land snail groups
(Field Museum's Hubricht collection) that were previously computerized,
information for 140,000 lots (= series) of pulmonates is now accessible on
the web at

http://fm1.fieldmuseum.org/collections/search.cgi?dest=inverts

Support from the National Science Foundation (DEB-9616372) is gratefully
acknowledged.
