Dear list members,

A new issue of Entomotropica (Dec. 2001) is on-line at 
www.entomotropica.org, the contents are:

Biology and laboratory culturing of the root-feeding flea beetle, 
Longitarsus columbicus columbicus Harold, 1876 (Chrysomelidae: Alticinae): 
a potential natural enemy of Lantana camara L. (Verbenaceae) in South Africa

Evaluation of Metaphycus sp. (Hymenoptera: Encyrtidae) as agent of natural 
control of Capulinia sp. near of jaboticabae von Ihering (Hemiptera: 
Eriococcidae)

Population fluctuation of Spodoptera frugiperda (Smith) (Lepidoptera: 
Noctuidae) on corn under three tillage systems

Detection of Entomophthorales fungi pathogenous to phytophagous insects in 
southern Bahia, Brazil

List of insects related to curcubits located in the Museum of Insects of 
Agricultural Interest at CENIAP-INIA in Venezuela

Tynacantha marginata Dallas (Heteroptera: Pentatomidae), predator of green 
stink bug (Nezara viridula L.) in Venezuela

Bemisia tabaci (Gennadius, 1899) biotype B, first record for Venezuela 
using RAPDs- PCR

Toxicity of five insecticides in a laboratory strain of Rhodnius prolixus 
Stål, 1859 (Hemiptera: Reduviidae) from Venezuela

New records of Psorophora Robineau-Desvoidy, 1827 (Diptera: Culicidae: 
Aedini) from Venezuela

First record of Eratyrus mucronatus Stål, 1959 (Hemiptera: Reduviidae) in 
the domiciliary atmosphere in Venezuela

Diagnosis and pictorial key for the species of the genus Anastrepha 
Schiner, 1868 (Dipteral: Tephritidae) of economic importance in Venezuela

Coptera haywardi Loiácono (Hymenoptera: Diapriidae) a parasitoid of pupae 
of Anastrepha spp. (Diptera: Tephritidae) in Venezuela

First record of genera Glipodes and Boatia (Coleoptera: Mordellidae) for 
Colombia

Book review: Guía para la determinación de los artrópodos bentónicos 
Sudamericanos. Fernández HR, Domínguez E. (Editores)
