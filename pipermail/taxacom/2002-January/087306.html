Dear all,

I would like to add three more journals from Korea.  I believe these
journals happily accept manuscripts from other than Asia.

Insecta Koreana (since 1990)
- English only
- max. 4 times a year
- accepts lengthy papers and monographs on insect systematics
- occasionally publish illustrated catalogues (e.g., Korean butterflies,
noctuids, tortricids, curculionids, ichneumonids, ...)
- The board of advisory council include Drs. Y. Arita, D. Davis, J. B.
Happner, I. M. Kerzhenr, K. C. Kim, V. S. Kononenko, L. Ronkay, P. J.
Spangler, A. Shinohara, O. Tadauchi, and Sk. Yamane.
- Editor, Center for Insect Systematics, Kangwon National University,
Chuncheon, 200-701, KOREA
- e-mail: Editor in Chief: Dr. Park, K. T., cispa at kangwon.ac.kr
- Annual subscription fee is US $30.

Journal of Asia-Pacific Entomology (since 1962)
- English only
- recently changed the format and cover design, trying to upgrade its
level
- biannual
- accepts any entomology-related articles
- published by the Korean Society of Applied Entomology who also publish
another journal, Korean Journal of Applied Entomology that are published
4 times a year and in Korean or English.
- Managing Editor, Korean Society of Applied Entomology, School of
Agricultural Biotechnology, College of Agriculture and Life Sciences,
Seoul Natioanl University, Suwon 441-744, KOREA
- e-mail: kaentom at netian.com
- Annual subscription fee is US $30.

Korean Journal of Entomology (since ..., uhr...well, the vol. # is 32 in
2002)
- 4 times a year (twice in Korean and twice in English)
- mainly for pure science but now accepts all
- published by the Entomological Society of Korea
- Editor-In-Chief, Korean Journal of Entomology, Korean Entomological
Institute, Korea University, 1, 5-ga, Anam-dong, Sungbuk-gu, Seoul
136-701, KOREA
- e-mail: entoko at korea.ac.kr
