People can judge for themselves; the discussion is (until it is archived) at:

   https://species.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikispecies:Village_Pump#Citation_templates


> However I acknowledge it could be modified to be

Note that in the opening post in that discussion, I asked:

    Can we standardise on {{cite journal}}? Do we need to modify it?

> here is a lot of resistance to changing it,
> largely because the issue has been raised a number of times and the current
> standard got community support.

The "community support" arrived at in past discussions is for the
visual rendering, not the underlying markup , not the method of
implementation. {{cite journal}} can easily be made to replicate the
current visual rendering.

> Another
> difficulty is the formatting, the botanists wants refs the way they are used
> to, the zoologists want it their way.

A "switch" (parameter) in the template could easily make the rendering
configurable allowing both types of output.
