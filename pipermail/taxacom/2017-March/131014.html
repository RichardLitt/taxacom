Dear Monocot researcher, welcome!

 Under the motto “Monocots for all: bringing the whole from its parts” we welcome researchers from different fields of investigation to participate in the Monocots VI Conference. This meeting will be held in Praiamar Natal Hotel & Convention, in the tropical touristic city of Natal, Northeastern Brazil, from October 8th-12th, 2018. The Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte will promote the event, which will be sponsored by the Brazilian National Counsel of Technological and Scientific Development (Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico – CNPq).
The local organizing committee supported by a board of advisors invites the submission of symposia proposals (see attached pdf file). Soon, our website will be online for registrations and more details on abstract submission and important dates.
We request you to forward this email to other researchers in your university. Sorry for cross posting.

See you in Natal!
