As BHL continues to scan the biodiversity literature, more articles are being extracted by BioStor http://biostor.org . Here are some recent highlights:

1. Three volumes of the Journal of Arachnology are now available http://biostor.org/issn/0161-8202/year/1984

2. Several volumes of Records of The Western Australian Museum from 1970’s onwards  http://biostor.org/issn/0312-3162/year/1974

3. Coverage of the Bulletin du Museum national d'histoire naturelle now extends into the 1940’s

4. Extensive coverage of the Gardens' Bulletin, Singapore http://biostor.org/issn/0374-7859/year/2014
