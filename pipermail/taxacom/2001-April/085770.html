Taxacomers,

I recently found this on "Global Gazatteer" on the web at:

http://www.calle.com/world/

It is great for finding the specific localities for label data (i.e. lat,
long, and altitude).  It isn't searchable, but is easy to use with some
pretty snazzy maps when you click on the locality from a list.  It has the
added bonus of no advertisements and banners.

If you know of something similar let me know directly and I will compile
them and send all the suggestions to the list.
