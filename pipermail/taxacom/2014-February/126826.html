Hi Susan,

I have some lists of homonyms - some valid names, some not - available via http://www.cmar.csiro.au/datacentre/irmng/homonyms.htm . The genus level lists are quite complete (probably tediously so for your needs), the species level list (much shorter) is just a smallish number as presently held in my database of species names (the latter is perhaps 60-70% incomplete at this time). Both lists include homonyms within as well as between Codes.

Hope this may be of use,

Regards - Tony
