Dear taxacomers,
I already wrote about posting courses without detailing the costs. The same
rationale applies to posting open positions. Please, keep in mind that
salary range is a *must*. Put yourself in the shoes of applicants that
might be spending considerable time peering through dozens of similar
calls. In the case of the Database Analyst position at the Department of
Plant Biology, Ecology, and Evolution at Oklahoma State University in
Stillwater, the hiring range (contingent upon available funding) is $16,73
- $25,90 hourly. Please, also note that a search for position number AS8796
in the OSU job database will retrieve nothing. For your search, use Req ID
"req7612". I hope that this additional information saves some time to
potential applicants.
Cheers and good luck,
