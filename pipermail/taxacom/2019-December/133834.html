Dear all,

If anyone is interested, I have now added the list of pseudofossil genera
as given by  Häntzschel, 1975
(Häntzschel, W. (1975). Miscellanea Supplement 1. Trace fossils and
Problematica, second edition. In Treatise on Invertebrate Paleontology,
Part W )

to the relevant place (currently a "phylum" in Animalia, however I may need
to reconsider this as a subset are plants) in the IRMNG hierarchy, see here
for the list:

http://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=taxdetails&id=11914597

There are a couple more (Genus Radiarites Ghare, 1982 and  Repentella
Müller, 1968) that I already had, not in the Häntzschel list, plus one of
Häntzschel's cited genera, Aspidella Billings, 1872, is presently
considered an Ediacaran body fossil (Cnidaria incertae sedis) so has been
placed there (reference is Gehling, J. G.; Narbonne, G. M.; Anderson, M. M.
(2000). The first named Ediacaran body fossil, Aspidella terranovica.
*Palaeontology.* 43(3): 427-456).

If anyone has any further suggested additions or alterations I will be glad
to hear them (I have not yet done an exhaustive check for any other names
missing at the generic level).

Regards - Tony
