I was slightly confused, but have sorted myself out.  Part W Miscellanea
was published in 1962 and has a chapt on Trace Fossils. What Tony gave a
link to was Part W Miscellanea *SUPPLEMENT 1*, published in 1975, which is
entirely a revised and expanded work on Trace Fossils by Hantzschel, and
without the chapters on Worms and Conodonts present in the original W
volume.

So there are two different W volumes, when perhaps the extra volume should
have been assigned a succeeding letter of the alphabet, or had a clearer
indication of content by its title. IMHO.

Geoff
