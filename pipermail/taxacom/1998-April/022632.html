Dear Taxacomers,

I am glad to announce that my STEPHANIDAE HOMEPAGE (Hymenoptera) is now
available to public access at
http://iris.biosci.ohio-state.edu/projects/stephanids

The page was optimized for NETSCAPE v. 2.02, and you will find some things
out of format if using earlier versions. Please be also aware that many
parts are still unfinished.

While there, please make sure you will not miss the following:

1. A new way of presenting morphometric data.
Path: [Ongoing Projects]->[Revision of the Genus Hemistephanus]->[Material
and Methods]

2. A method specific for collecting stephanids, under [Collecting Stephanids]

I would greatly appreciate receiving your suggestions and commentaries about
the site--from eventual misspellings to other subjects you would like (or
expect) to see in a site like that. Please send messages directly to
aguiar.2 at osu.edu

I hope you like it!
