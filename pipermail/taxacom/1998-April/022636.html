The University of Reading, UK.
School of Plant Sciences - Department of Botany
Lectureship in Plant Diversity & Biodiversity Informaties

Applications are invited for a new lectureship in botanical aspects of
biodiversity.  Candidates with research interests in biodiversity
information systems and informatics will be welcomed.  Informal
enquiries may be made to f.a.bisby at reading.ac.uk. The department is one
of the centres for the NERC Taxonomy Initiative and part of the RAE 5*
rated School of Plant Sciences.  Candidates should have a PhD.

Salary in the range of 16,045 to 21,016 (Pounds Sterling) pa (Grade "A")
with USS benefits.  The appointment is intended to be "permanent"
subject to satisfactory completion of a three year probationary period.

Apply for further particulars and Application Forms (2 copies) to the
Personnel Office, The University of Reading.  Whiteknights, PO Box 217
READING RG6 6AH.  Telephone (01 18) 931 6771 (answerphone).  E-mail:
Persorineloreading.ac.uk giving name and full address.  See the School
Web Site: http://www.reading.ac.uk/Acadepts/sb/home.html for general
department information.  Closing date 27 May 1998.  Please quote
reference number AC9811.

These details can be found at;
http://www.rdg.ac.uk/Jobs/html/job_vacancies_-_teaching_3.html
