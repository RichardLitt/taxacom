I tend to agree with Vlad that the ICZN Code (not just e-only publication) is far too complicated. I tried to get a note published on this topic (ironically in a Pensoft journal), but after two rejections I really can't be bothered trying for a third time. Magnolia Press solve the e-only problem by simply preregistering every article that they publish, whether it contains nomenclatural acts or not. Then they don't have to detect nomenclatural acts in the manuscripts.
 
Stephen
