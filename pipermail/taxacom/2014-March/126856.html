Passing this along...

The Missouri Botanical Symposium will be held on March 21st and there are
some fascinating speakers and topics this year!   The symposium will take
place on the campus of the Missouri University of Science and Technology
(MST) in Rolla, Missouri.  The deadline for registration is March
14th(soon!).  Cost is $40 regular and $25 for students and lunch is
included.
For a full list of presentations and to register, you can visit
www.missouribotanicalsymposium.org.  Any updates will be posted on the
facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/mobosympo.  Topics are pertinent
to those outside of Missouri as well.
