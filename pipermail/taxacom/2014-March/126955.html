Annotated Correspondence of G. W. Clinton (1862 - 1878):
Compiled by P. M. Eckel, Editor, Clinton Correspondence
March 25, 2014

This is an update and summary of work accomplished in producing an online representation of the collected letters of Judge George W. Clinton. Judge Clinton was an amateur botanist, co-founder of the Buffalo Society of Natural Sciences, Buffalo, New York, and son of Governor DeWitt Clinton, of Erie Canal fame. The set of letters and accompanying journal and photograph album are at the Buffalo Museum of Science, and provide primary historical data on relationships between botanists of the period 1862 to 1879.
