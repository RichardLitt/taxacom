The Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF) has advertised two
vacant positions to be filled at the GBIF Secretariat in Copenhagen: 

 

·         Programme Officer for Content Mobilisation - To support publishers
of biodiversity data and to assist GBIF Participants to develop and
implement content mobilisation strategies

·         Programme Officer for Content Analysis and Use - To support
research communities and organisations making use of GBIF-mediated data and
to work with stakeholders to improve the fitness-for-use of these data for
research and analysis

 

Information on these positions and the application process can be found at
http://www.gbif.org/newsroom/opportunities.  The closing date for
applications is 22 April 2014.  

 

Please circulate this notice to any who may be interested.

 

Donald Hobern, GBIF Executive Secretary
