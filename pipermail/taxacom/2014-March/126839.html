A copy of Llorente, J., Morrone, J.J., Bueno, A., Pérez-Hernández, R.,
Viloria, Á. and Espinosa, D. 2000. Historia del desarrollo y la recepción
de las ideas panbiogeográficas de Léon Croizat. Revista Academia Colombiana
Ciencias 24, 549-577 is now posted at
http://www.johngrehan.net/index.php/panbiogeography/panpublications/llorente/along
with a link to an English translation. This article is among the most
extensive overviews of the history, criticisms and applications of
Croizat's panbiogeography.

There is no intention for this translation to be authoritative. I did it
for my personal interest (note caveat at the beginning of the translation),
but I anticipate that there may be some other potential readers who cannot
read Spanish and who might find this translation sufficiently useful for
general interest.
