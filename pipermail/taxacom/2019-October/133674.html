Why would the location of the most primitive species indicate the centre of
origin? Darwin thought that the most advanced species occupied the centre
of origin (and had pushed out the more primitive, inferior species to
remote areas).

>  Hold on there one minute John!On the scale of "science nonsense", this
> might not even qualify! The key word is "suggests". The fossil does
> demonstrate that the oldest and most primitive *known* member of the group
> is from the Southern Hemisphere, so that it *evidence* that the group
> evolved there. It is the simplest explanation of the known facts, that's
> all. Otherwise, you have to posit the existence of unknown like fossils in
> the Northern Hemisphere, for which there is currently no evidence, and
> consider it chance that the first such fossil just happened to be found in
> the Southern Hemisphere. Sure, that it *possible*, but nobody seems to be
> saying that a Southern Hemisphere origin it is 100% certain, just that it
> is suggested by the new evidence.Stephen
