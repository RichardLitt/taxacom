Another form of so-called 'evidence' that is comparable to that of fossils
as the center of origin is the misrepresentation of phylogenetic sequences
as dispersal sequence between taxa. This is often referred to as Hennig's
rule which is a sort of linguistic slight of hand that transforms an
assumption into evidence. There is nothing empirical to support the
contention that phylogenetic sequence necessarily represents a dispersal
sequence. If might, or might not. But it is an interpretation of phylogeny,
not a form of evidence. But it is incorrectly presented as evidence in a
massive number of biogeographic papers. Darwin's center of origin and
dispersal biogeography certainly involves a great deal of fabrication of
evidence.

John Grehan
