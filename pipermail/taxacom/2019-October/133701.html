Dear listers,

We are a group of researchers investigating the publication culture in
taxonomy across all taxa.

If you work with Taxonomy / Systematics, we kindly invite you to help us by
filling a short survey.
It should take no more than 5 minutes of your time.

You can find the survey here:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdC9BQk9vjsTccztL8sXmeGJUWi20F5XUKnHDWjcbc4BXLGCA/viewform

It is entirely anonymous and the data gathered will be used for a
publication on an academic journal.

Thank you very much for your cooperation and feel free to share the survey
with your colleagues.
