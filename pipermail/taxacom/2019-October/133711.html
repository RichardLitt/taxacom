Yes, exactly that. With molecular estimates misrepresented as actual or
maximal dates everything is reduced to hash, and not very good hash at
that. When fossil calibrated dates are misrepresented in this way the
results are meaningless when it comes to supposedly assessing 'dispersal'
and 'vicariance' since the results are all pure fiction. All supposed
evidence for chance dispersal is an artifact of this misrepresentation of
what the data actually is. What really surprises me is that creationists
haven't even grasped this or they would be having a field day with it.

John Grehan
