Stephen - the assertion that a southern hemisphere origin "is suggested by
the new evidence" is indeed nonsense. The fossil can only suggest that a
clade was present at a particular place and time. Empirically it says
nothing about the origin of the taxon with respect to younger fossils
elsewhere. Its quite possible that the group had its origin from a
widespread ancestor that was in both the northern and southern hemisphere
and the (current) oldest fossil happens to be located in the south. It does
not matter that the fossil may represent the basal lineage as this may just
represent an initial vicariance. So dreaming up a local (southern) origin
is not science at all, just nonsense.

John Grehan
