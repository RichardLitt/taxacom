I side with John and Michael in arguing that the fossil is silent on the
origin of the group - I'm not even sure whether "origin" (whether in
systematics or biogeography) is a question that can be addressed
"evidence". All we know is that a member of the clade was present at that
time and place. These arguments were played out in the early 80s, though I
suspect our views are in the minority.

Tony
