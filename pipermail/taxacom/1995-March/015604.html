        MAMMALIAN ECOLOGIST


The Department of Wildlife, Fish, and Conservation Biology at the
University of California, Davis, is pleased to announce that it is
accepting applications for a tenure-track, Assistant Professor position in
Mammalian Ecology.  Information specific to this employment opportunity and
information regarding the Department of Wildlife, Fish, and Conservation
Biology can be obtained at http://www-wfb.ucdavis.edu/.  General
information regarding the UC Davis campus can be obtained at
http://www.ucdavis.edu/.  This position will soon be announced in Science
and Nature.
