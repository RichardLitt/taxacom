The botanists here at Texas A&M would like to introduce our new www page.
(While the rest of the world has been arguing nomenclature,
cladistics, and what-have-you, we have been busy marrying field
botany and the latest advances in html technology...)

We invite you then, to check out
http://www.isc.tamu.edu/FLORA/LCP/LCPINTRO.HTML.

This is a prototype text-and-graphics tour of the flora of East
Central Texas.  (We also invite you to explore our other links--see
the bottom of the homepage...)

manhart at bio.tamu.edu
