At 05:36 PM 8/22/01 -0400, Byron J. Adams wrote:

>Are there any guidelines or "rules of thumb" for establishing common names
>for organisms that heretofore are known only by their scientific names?

Well, if you must, I would suggest coming up with a noun to represent a
genus -- e.g., "mite-gobbler" or whatever, and adjectives to distinguish
the various species of that genus -- e.g.,"green mite-gobbler" "slimy
mite-gobbler" "Johnson's mite-gobbler" etc.  At least in this way there
will be a strong parallel between scientific and vernacular nomenclature.

Though I still think it'd be better to educate users.
