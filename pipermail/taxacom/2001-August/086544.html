Dear taxacomers:

We are wood people and not too familiar with the prevailing usage (or
strict rules) of how to properly cite botanical names. The question which

Pinus, whether with a capital (first) letter *and* italicized; or
capitalized and *not* italicized; or *not* capitalized *and* italicized
(this is our currently used version); or *neither* capitalized or italicized.
