What amazes me is that someone viewing or hiking the Grand Canyon, coupled
with the "facts" of paleobiology - indeed there were different organisms
in deeper-time rocks, could consider that the earth is 6 mya.

Moreover, what is unfortunate, and taboo, is that the paleobiologist's
view is a religious - "religiaire" = to bind - one.  Many can feel the
traverse of time going down to various facies, viewing differently aged
(potassium-argon, etc) rocks and odd life-forms and marvel how tiny modern
time is. Who cares if evolution is falsifiable; science is knowledge and
falsifiability is simply one way of knowing, an especially important
consideration if one needs to be published for "real veracity". Something
is off here.
