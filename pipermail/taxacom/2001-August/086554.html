This is a great post. Thank you for it. I was looking not long ago for the
information contained in 23.9.6 as a reference for someone else. I had read
it but did not remember where in the code it was. I will now highlight it
so as to find it easier the next time. Whether as and editor, reviewer or
author (all with Lepidoptera) I do not allow "check lists" to "count" as
any usage.

I think the Code says little on the topic of nomenclators and indices or
lists of names because taxonomists should not even have to ask if such
subjective "popular" publications qualify as systematic determiners.  I had
to deal with this as author in 1998 when determining what did and did not
count by the Code relative to the name Chlosyne ismeria, and in 2000 as
editor relative to old names in the butterfly genus Celastrina.
Personally, I don't even think Field Guides for the general populace should
be given any heed relative to systematics and taxonomy. Unfortunately, to
lay people these surface - and lots of pretty pictures - "works of
literature" are the final word on what is and is not, and what  X  is to be
known as. The worst thing though is that the two or three people who end up
being the frequent authors of these also become considered the taxonomic
authorities and experts - when the only thing they are really experts at is
taking a lot of great pictures.

Ron Gatrelle
