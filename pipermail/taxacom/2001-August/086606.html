Right?the glossary of the ICZN attempts to provide general definitionsfor terms used elsewhere in that work. In that sense, the'definition' I quoted applies to any mention of the term"prevailing usage" in any context within ICZN 4 (1999).The point here is that it is not clear, or - looked at another way -it is open to interpretation by anybody exactly what publicationsshould be counted in.
