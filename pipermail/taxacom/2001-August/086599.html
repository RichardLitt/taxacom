Because of space constraints and the availability of the Gray Card Index
on the internet, the Herbarium of the University of Alaska Museum (ALA) is
giving away a copy of both the Gray Herbarium Index to names of western
hemisphere vascular plant taxa and the Torrey Index to North American
Botanical Literature to any interested party willing to pay for shipping.
The Gray Herbarium Index was published by Harvard University and includes
names published up to the early- to mid- 80's.  The Torrey Index was
published by the Torrey Botanical Club and includes citations to
literature published from around 1968 through the early- to mid- 80's.
Each index consists of 2-inch by 5-inch cards.  Each index occupies 48
card-file drawers 18.5 inches long (some drawers are less than full).
