sorry to cross post but some of you are everywhere and others are not.

the holidays brought a nice surprise. lady friend of mine and her husband
had been to englad and sent me a T-shirt, booklet, map, and paper article
on The National Dragonfly Museum at Ashton Mill, Oundle, Northamptonshire
Britain. Yet another thing new to me. This is on the estate of Ashton
Wold, the home of Dr. Miriam Rothschild - "the Flea Lady". Quite an
operation and undertaking. Those of you who have access to back issues of
The Times (London) may want to check out the full page article on page 14
- Mind and Matter - of the Monday l5 July l996 issue which includes a
wonderful picture of Dr. Miriam Rothschild.

Those of you interested in further information may write: National
Dragonfly Museum at Ashton Mill, Ashton Wold, Ashton, Nr Oundle,
Northants,PE8 5LZ  tel:01832 272427  It is a registered charity (#1046086)
in England so I assume there are supporting memberships for those of you
interested.
