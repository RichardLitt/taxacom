THE UNIVERSITY OF GUAM SOLICITS APPLICATIONS TO ESTABLISH A LIST OF
ELIGIBLES FOR THE FOLLOWING POSITIONS (TENURE/NON-TENURE TRACK
APPOINTMENT, PART/FULL-TIME - SUBJECT TO AVAILABILITY OF FUNDS):

ASSISTANT TO ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR
(ENTOMOLOGIST)

LOCATION:  COLLEGE OF AGRICULTURE AND LIFE SCIENCES, UNIVERSITY OF GUAM

COLLEGE DESCRIPTION:  The College of Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS)
prides itself in providing high quality and useful agricultural teaching,
research and extension work relevant to industry needs; protecting the
environment; enhancing economic opportunities; and providing for the
social well-being of the people in Guam and the Western Pacific Region.
CALS has recently moved to a new multimillion dollar facility overlooking
the Pacific Ocean.  Spacious research labs are modern installations that
were designed with input from scientists.  CALS/AES is currently composed
of ten research scientists and their staff, who operate several
experiment stations.

CHARACTER OF DUTIES:  This is a tenure-track position.  Primary duty is
to pursue research grants in order to develop innovative pest management
systems, to maintain existing programs and to curate the college's insect
collection.  Other duties include teaching undergraduate students from
various degree programs and graduate students in the environmental
science program and some extension work.

EDUCATIONAL BACKGROUND: Required:  Applicants must have a Ph.D. in
entomology.  Incumbent must have an established publication record, with
a strong background in biological control.  Desired:  Experience in
tropical agriculture is preferred.

APPLICATION PROCESS:  Persons interested in applying should summit a
current curriculum vita or resume including three (3) letters of
recommendation, all official graduate transcripts (sent directly from the
awarding institution/s), copies of all unofficial undergraduate
transcripts, and request for application to:  George C. Wall, Ph.D.,
Chair, Entomology Search Committee, c/o Human Resources Office, 303
University Drive, UOG Station, Mangilao, Guam 96923.  For further
information, please e-mail gwall at uog.edu call (671) 735-2100/2148 or fax
(671) 734-6842.

APPLICATION DEADLINE:  Application for Employment Forms will be accepted
Monday through Friday except holidays, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., until 5:00
p.m., DECEMBER 15, 1996.

WORK ELIGIBILITY:  Federal law requires presentation of eligibility to
work in the United States within seventy-two (72) hours of reporting for
employment.  Specifically, 8 USC 1324A requires the employer to verify
the identity and eligibility to work in the United States of all newly
hired employees.  The University of Guam is required to comply with this
law on a non-discriminatory basis.  If you are hired to fill a position
with the University of Guam, you will be required to present valid
documents to comply with this law.

WOMEN, MINORITIES, AND PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES ARE PARTICULARLY
ENCOURAGED TO APPLY.
