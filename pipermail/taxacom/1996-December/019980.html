Several recent messages have asked about a collection management program.
Robet Colwell has produced a program called "Biota" currently available
through Sinhauer for 125.00 dollars US.  This program was demonstrated at
the recent Entomological Society of America meeting.  I was quite impressed
with it.  A Mac version is currently available, with windows 95 and 3.1 to
follow.
