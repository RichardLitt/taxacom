1996 
The first circular of the "Euro-American Mammal Congress: Challenges
in Holarctic Mammalogy" is ready and will be mailed within the next few days
to more than 9,000 individuals.

Distribution will be made through mammal societies that have been very
kind for accepting to send the circular to their members and by direct mail
to individuals included in the Congress Organization mailing list.

However, there are a few countries that are not well represented or are not
represented at all in our distribution list. These countries are: Albania,
Armenia, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Cyprus, Iceland, Macedonia, Montenegro, Romania,
Serbia, Slovakia, and Slovenia.

For that reason, we would appreciate very much that you send us the names
and addresses of mammalogists of these countries that you know.

Thanking you in advance for your help.

Fernando Palacios
Museo Nacional de Ciencias Naturales
Madrid
