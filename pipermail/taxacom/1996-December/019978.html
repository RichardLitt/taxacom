A more interesting list of all types names(some real, some not) can be
found in Frizzell, D. L. 1933. Terminology of types. American Midland
Naturalist XIV: 637-668.

It includes such types as: Agriotype,fototype, holoplastotype, and
teratotype, and citations of their first use or defintion( 233 total).
