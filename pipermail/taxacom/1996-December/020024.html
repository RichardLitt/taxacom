Dear Taxacomer,

I don't know of an electronic listing of Cronquist's system, but such a
table (complete system, hierarchically organized) exists at the end of the
excellent D.J.Mabberley's "The Plant-Book", the newest version of which has
been published recently (this year) by Cambridge University Press (UK).

Maybe you could scan this part of the book and then process it
electronically for the special use you need.

Hope this helps.
