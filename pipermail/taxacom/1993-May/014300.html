  This title on Biodiversity has recently been published:

  Fenger,M.A., E.H. Miller, J.F. Johnson and E.J.R. Williams, Editors. 1993.
  Our Living Legacy: Proceedings of a symposium on Biological Diversity.
  Royal British Columbia Museum, Victoria, B.C. 392 pages.

  Contains 26 chapters on Biological diversity in B.C. Authors include David
  Suzuki, Wade Davis, Jack Ward Thomas, E.C. Pielou.
