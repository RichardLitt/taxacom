VI ARGENTINE CONGRESS IN PALEONTOLOGY AND BIOSTRATIGRAPHY
Trelew-Chubut March 28 thru April 3, 1994


The congress will be held at the city of Trelew, Patagonia.  The
program intents to cover the modern topics that have been
discussed in paleontology today, like the use of cladistic methods
to access phylogeny in fossils, paleopathology, paleomechanics.

The summary of the topics coverd during the congress:

TAXONOMY
PHYLOGENY
CLADISM
BIOSTRATIGRAPHY
PALEOBIOGEOGRAPHY
PALEOCLIMATOLOGY
PALEOBIOMECHANICS

Symposiums

Origin and Evolution of Amphibia, Reptila and Dinosauria
New Insights on Mammal Phylogeny and Biogeography
Origin and Evolution of Major Plant Groups
Paleoinvertebrates

Submitted papers will be accepted until December 31st 1993.  More
