                      BIN21 DISTRIBUTION

It may be useful to provide the list of distributers of the BIN
Workshop Proceedings once more, since there have been some
changes and also some people missed reading the addresses.
Please remember that the Proceedings are heavy and distributers
may request postage reimbursement.

UNESCO (via Dr E Da Silva) have kindly taken over the
distribution of some 500 copies of the Proceedings to the UNESCO
MIRCEN network and people associated with the Diversitas
programme. For this we are very grateful.


OTHER NEWS:

Steps are being taken to extend the Technical Working Group to
include active biodiversity internet groups. Details of the
expanded group will be posted to the List in due course.

The recommendations of the workshop are to be reported to the
following biodiversity conferences (programmes available from
barbara at biostrat.demon.co.uk on request):

Intellectual property, indigenous cultures and biodiversity
conservation. St Anne's College, Oxford, UK, May 14th 1993

Norway/UNEP Expert Conference on Biodiversity. Trondheim, Norway,
May 24-28th, 1993

IUBS, Institute of Biology meeting, May 20th, London, UK, May
20th 1993.

  UP-DATE ON BIN21 DEVELOPMENTS SINCE THE WORKSHOP IN BRASIL


Following the Workshop and the establishment of an interim
steering committee and specialist committees, the following
activities have taken place:

[1] Editing of the Workshop Proceedings

[2] Publishing of the Workshop Proceedings by UNEP (2000 copies)

[3] Distribution of the Proceedings via volunteer distributers
    (workshop participants) in different geographical regions;
    additional distribution of 500 copies by UNESCO

[4] Report of the Workshop to UNEP/Nairobi meeting (January
    1993), Green Centre, Oxford conference (May 1993), Trondheim
    Norway/UNEP meeting (May 1993) and at other appropriate
    events

[4] Setting up of a biodiversity List Server on the Internet
    network (biodiv-l, accessed by nearly 300 different people).
    Biodiv-l is managed by the Tropical Data Base, Campinas,
    Brasil

[5] Establishment of an interim BIN21 Secretariat at the Tropical
    Data Base, pending a call for proposals for a permanent
    Secretariat

[6] Expansion of the Technical Committee to form a Technical
    Working Group (TechWG) composed of people active in the world
    of linking information resources via the Internet gopher
    systems and other mechanisms. The TechWG to advise on the
    best mecahnisms for a) linking resources and b) establishing
    a Directory of information resources as a permanent resource

[7] Assessment of financial needs for a) a meeting of the TechWG
    and b) a permanent Secretariat

[8] Preparation and submission of proposals for financial
    support.
