Dear TAXACOM members:

I am writing to call attention to our MS and Ph.D. programs in Botany at
Miami University.  We have a comprehensive, broad-based botany program
featuring a wide range of expertise in botany in the broad sense.  If you
or your students are interested in pursuing a Master's or Ph.D. degree in
the plant sciences, I encourage you to visit our departmental web page at
http://www.cas.muohio.edu/botany/ to see a list of our faculty and their
research interests.  There are also descriptions of our facilities,
including the Herbarium, the Electron Microscopy Facility, the Center for
Bioinformatics and Functional Genomics, the greenhouse facilities, and so on.

Please feel free to contact me if you or your students have questions about
our program, and I'll make sure your questions get answered!

Sincerely,
M.A. Vincent
