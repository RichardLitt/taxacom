Friends and fellow leppers,

         Just thought I'd let you know that I have just finished adding a
large number of pictures to the Georgia Lepidoptera website.  Many (but not
all) additions are listed in the recent additions section at the top of the
home page.  I will be sending a more detailed list out, but wanted to let
those of you who have been waiting for the long promised updates that the
updates are now here.  I have also lightened a number of the somewhat
darker images so that they should be much more useful for identification
purposes now.

Enjoy!
