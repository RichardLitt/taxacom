Stearn, Botanical Latin has a 30-page chapter (XVII) on “Geographical Names”. The third edition of the work is on the web in various formats at: https://archive.org/stream/BOTANICALLATINWILLIAMSTEARN/BOTANICAL%20LATIN%20WILLIAM%20STEARN_djvu.txt

For example a searchable pdf is at https://ia600805.us.archive.org/3/items/BOTANICALLATINWILLIAMSTEARN/BOTANICAL%20LATIN%20WILLIAM%20STEARN_text.pdf

Stearn does discuss Linnaeus’s use of geographical terms that are at variance with modern assumptions, e.g. “Aethiopia” being an area near the Cape of Good Hope, not in northeastern Africa
