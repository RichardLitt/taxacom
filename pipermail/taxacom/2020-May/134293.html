Hi Jonathan, thanks to Francisco's digitising and databasing efforts, I
believe that this is what you need:

http://www.animalbase.uni-goettingen.de/zooweb/servlet/AnimalBase/list/taxa?from_reference=4


I have a slightly similar list in IRMNG, but just for genera, at
https://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=sourcedetails&id=143024 (hopefully
complete but not checked for that).
The IRMNG list contains families and present taxonomic placement for
genera, but to download the higher taxonomy in machine readable form you
would do best to access the machine readable (DwCA) version of the data,
available via https://www.irmng.org/download.php .
