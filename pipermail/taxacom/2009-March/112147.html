Hi Paul,
      That sounds like a good proposal to me, as long as it only gives
Epacridoideae priority over Styphelioideae.  I wouldn't want to see
Styphelioideae totally suppressed, so both can be used as subfamilies by
those who recognize a full Family Epicridaceae.  I don't normally go
down to subfamily level (especially for plants), but below I have listed
the subfamilies I would currently recognize for Ericaceae and
Epacridaceae (and their cladistic codings):
