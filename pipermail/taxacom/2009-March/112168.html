A challenge
<http://ukimages.gmimage3.com/sendlink.asp?HitID=0&StID=9459&SID=6&NID=8

issued to researchers and users of biodiversity information: to prepare
and present a real-time demonstration of the capabilities in your
community of practice to disseminate new biodiversity-related data. The
challenge will take place during e-Biosphere 09, and possible challenge
subjects include: 

*	Capturing data in private and public databases; 
*	Conducting quality assurance on the data by automated validation
and/or peer review; 
*	Indexing, linking and/or automatically submitting the new data
records to other relevant databases; 
*	Making these data available to relevant audiences; 
*	Make the data and links to the data widely accessible; and 
*	Offering analytical tools for users interested in exploring the
data.
