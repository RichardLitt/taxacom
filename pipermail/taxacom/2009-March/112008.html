Reading the article, the authors of the list seem to have confused several 
things under the general label of "gobbledygook".  One is the use of words 
beyond the grade-school vocabulary.  Another is the use of cliched phrases 
and slang.  And a third is an air of pretentiousness.  If the last two were 
eliminated, the first, by itself, would seldom present a problem, if used 
in moderation.
