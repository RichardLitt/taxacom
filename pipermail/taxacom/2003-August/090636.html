I am surprised Doug, you forgot one.

Years ago, I think in Sphecos, Jim Carpenter proposed the subgenus
Nuclearbombus as a way of getting rid of all of those names!

However, your records does not even come close to fly taxonomists.
Today, Tom Pape recognizes some 326 synonyms of the genus Sarcophaga.
And yes, as one might expect there are other taxonomists who consider
most of them as valid!

So, since genera are truly arbitrary, the real measure of synonyms is
the number for a species.

In this regard it should be noted that a dipterists, Robineau-Desvoidy,
described one common European species 245 times and distributed those
names among 5 genera!
