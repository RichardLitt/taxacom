Simple interactive identification of the vascular flora of Western Australia is available in FloraBase, http://florabase.calm.wa.gov.au .  The identification process is simplistic, without iterative interrogation, 'best' character ranking or the inclusion of 'unknowns' in the taxa matched.  However, it is based on a maintained DELTA dataset and so a fully interactive key to the 12,500 vascular plant taxa in WA is possible in future.  

We have found that a web-enabled key of this nature integrated into the herbarium's other maintained datasets of names and specimens, plus taxon images and maps is a very powerful tool for both scientists and the community.

Additionally, interactive keys (using Intkey) to the families and genera of flowering plants in WA are soon to be added to FloraBase, with specialist keys for certain significant genera also well advanced.

I would strongly concur with the notion that illustrated glossaries and detailed character definitions are an essential tool in the development and presentation of interactive keys.  They should be considered an integral part of the data coding and development of any interactive key product, regardless of the specific software tool chosen.
