Having reached Bacon, going further back seems irresistible. As for the
inspiration of the "scientific revolution", invisibles colleges and
all,  this one on the teachings of Pythagoras:

"What is the wisest thing? Number; but second the one who assigns names to
things."

from Iamblichus Vita Pythagorae, DK 58C4 and KRS 277

A few soothing words for the troubled world of systematists.
