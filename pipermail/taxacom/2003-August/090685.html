You can take it a step further and say its in the Urbanus "proteus group"
which at least eliminates about 50% of the other Urbanus species.

Sticking my neck way out - it has a character that is unique to U. proteus
itself - that's the way the green "fades out" towards the margin of the
hindwing.   All the other described species in the "proteus group" have a
sharply defined edge to the iridescent green.   However, this is a character
that is hard to interpret using a bullet lamp on a pinned bug.  So - the
lack of an edge to the green could simply be a photographic artifact.

Do you have a photo of the ventral wing surfaces of this specific bug (as
opposed to a different bug [but seemingly the same species] photographed
perhaps minutes later - many of the "proteus group" species are very common
and fly together (perhaps using the same early successional hostplants??).
There are better characters below.
