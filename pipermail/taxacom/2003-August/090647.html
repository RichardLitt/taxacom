I don't think Linnaeus was Chinese, but he said something similar:

Nomina si nescis, perit et cognition rerum.
["Who knoweth not the names, knoweth not the subject."]
If you don't know the names, your knowledge of the things perishes.
Linnaeus, Critica Botanica No. 210, 1737
