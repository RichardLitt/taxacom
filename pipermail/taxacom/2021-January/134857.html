Well I dunno... here in Australia we do indeed have lots of common names
based on misleading external resemblances... we have Australian salmon that
are not salmon, as you say silky oaks that are not oaks, Australian magpies
that are not magpies... All fine with me since these have become the
"legitimate" common names. However the common name for the koala is the
koala, pure and simple. "Koala bear" just grates... - and makes me think
that the user should be educated to know better (sorry if this offends,
maybe it is just me assuming a superiority when I should not). All right,
I'll shut up now, and run away fast!!
