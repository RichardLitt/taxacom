One refutation of ID comes from Behe's own department at Lehigh University,
where ALL of his colleagues signed a statement that they reject his “theories”:
https://www.lehigh.edu/~inbios/News/evolution.html <https://www.lehigh.edu/~inbios/News/evolution.html>

Apart from that, we don’t have to explain here why ID is nonsense: there is no evidence for it.
Creationists, of course, claim that there is no evidence for evolution.
I usually point out to them that there are at least 5 million studies supporting evolution:
https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C5&q=evolution&btnG=&oq=evo <https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0,5&q=evolution&btnG=&oq=evo>
and they should read those first before they make that claim again :)
