Out of any reason, just came along this email from June 2013.Quentin’s idea of starting a project à la human genome to tackle the huge knowledge hidden in the ca 500M printed, daily growing corpus is not forgotten, and the longer the more relevant to complement the global initiatives to sequence, to digitize collection of  life one Earth, and image via iNaturalist et al global biodiversity. With GBIF in place we also have an aggregator and access point to this wealth making use of persistent identifiers to link all up.At Plazi we now have tools in place to highly automatically extract data, especially taxonomic treatments, figures and to an increasing degree materials citations, and to make it findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable through the Biodiversity Literature Repository (BLR). GBIF is an interesting point by using all the liberated data by importing each article as dataset, and then display each treatment on a species page, including one to many treatments (similar to World Flora Online)  including the cited figures, a collection of material citations as occurences (which hopefully will be changed to materials citation at some point). In the best case, the entire process from an article to showing up in GBIF and BLR  occurs fully automatically, including al the many links.This is about the last years output: 76,641 #taxontreatment including 6,650 new species described in 2020 liberated; Current total access to 391K treatments, 216K figures in @biolitrepo , from 41K articles.  In the Biodiversity Literature Repository we have an increasing amount of FAIRizised published taxonomic data  soon including 100K taxonomic treatments as DataCite objects with its own DOI https://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/search?page=1&size=20&subtype=taxonomictreatment&subtype=figure ,  and 32K datasets in @GBIF https://www.gbif.org/publisher/7ce8aef0-9e92-11dc-8738-b8a03c50a862   .Happy read of the liberated,  new described species in 2020: http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/srsStats/stats?outputFields=doc.uuid+doc.doi+bib.author+bib.title+bib.year+bib.source+tax.name+tax.kingdomEpithet+tax.orderEpithet+tax.familyEpithet+tax.status&groupingFields=doc.uuid+doc.doi+bib.author+bib.title+bib.year+bib.source+tax.name+tax.kingdomEpithet+tax.orderEpithet+tax.familyEpithet+tax.status&orderingFields=tax.kingdomEpithet+tax.orderEpithet+tax.familyEpithet&FP-bib.year=2020&FP-tax.status=%22sp.%20nov.%22&format=HTMLThis year will see a substantial increase through the processing of backlogs of journals we now process routinely.
