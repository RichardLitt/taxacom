A multiuse common name like "The Weta" is a fun journal title to search
for on the web.

There's the giant insect, the film companies, the media companies, the
place names, and not forgetting the San Francisco Bay Area Water Emergency
Transportation Authority.

A small niche publication is rather inconspicuous, but here it is online:
"The Weta is the news bulletin of the Entomological Society of New
Zealand"

John Grehan's article in pdf is here:
https://weta.ento.org.nz/index.php/weta/article/view/386
