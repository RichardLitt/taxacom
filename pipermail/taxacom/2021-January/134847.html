Yes, Frank, it is definitely wonderful that we have many open access
peer-reviewed journals out there. What is definitely not wonderful is
having many paywalled journals out there, rubbing the logical fallacy of
the journal impact factor on the face of their readers, and publishing a
significant portion of the new taxa discovered every year. And by the way,
the last time I looked, peer-review was not a requirement for zoological
publications to be Code-compliant.
