To be more confusing, Nuculoidea is a genus in the superfamily Nuculoidea,
in Bivalvia.  Also, the Code does not specifically regulate homonyms above
the familial level, so Pulmonata or Decapoda can be used in Mollusca and
Arthropoda.  However, such homonyms are a common source of confusion in
databases, just as disallowed homonyms are.
