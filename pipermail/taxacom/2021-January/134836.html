Dear Igor,

this article is a joke and refers to the Australian hoax of the Drop 
Bear, a fictional carnivorous koala bear, see 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drop_bear

In Germany, we have a similar case with the Wolpertinger - and in this 
case even actual specimens exist in public collections, see 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolpertinger.

Best wishes,
