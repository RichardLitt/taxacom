Hi Ken,

Understand that you feel that way since it seems that you and Jason are
frustrated when your claims or interpretations are contested. But
regardless, the bottom line remains the same:

1) fossil calibrated molecular divergence estimates are minimums only
(claims for anything else is indeed transmogrification)
2) Priors are just guesses
3) molecular clocks are not set in stone as the only arbiter of
phylogenetic age
4) Older tectonic ages are not falsified by fossil calibrated ages (since
the latter are just minimums)
5) tectonic correlations are empirically observable
6) No one (to my knowledge) has provided an empirically grounded
falsification of any proposed tectonic correlation (i.e. demonstrated that
taxa are younger than the correlated tectonics)
7) Panbiogeographic studies have generated novel geological predictions
that have subsequently been corroborated by geologists - its one case where
evolutionary theory is not simply a matter of belief, but an actual means
of predicting new knowledge that was later found to be true (I notice those
who dismiss panb biogeography step around this one :)

Cheers, John Grehan

> dispersal (“Interestingly it is dispersalism that invokes miracles and
