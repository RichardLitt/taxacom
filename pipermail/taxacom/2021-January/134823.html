2021 
Since December 29 2020 at Plazi and BLR we are testing our new automated processing of taxonomic articles, 100 at a time. The results are here http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/static/newToday.html and a summary of what has bee extracted is here http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/dioStats/stats?outputFields=doc.uploadDate+cont.pageCount+cont.treatCount+cont.matCitCount+cont.figCountZen+cont.bibRefCount&FP-doc.uploadDate=%222020-12-29%22-&format=HTML. The processing includes quality control which only allows passing documents to the public that fulfill a minimal set of criteria. Otherwise they are held back until  the errors are corrected, and released thereafter.

The yield  alone includes 1,271 new species published in 2020 and 2021. http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/srsStats/stats?outputFields=doc.uploadDate+bib.year+tax.status&groupingFields=bib.year+tax.status&FP-doc.uploadDate=%222020-12-29%22-&format=HTML

To set this in  context, here the results for the  data liberated during 2020: http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/dioStats/stats?outputFields=doc.uploadYear+cont.pageCount+cont.treatCount+cont.matCitCount+cont.figCountZen+cont.bibRefCount&groupingFields=doc.uploadYear&FP-doc.uploadYear 20&format=HTML including 17,691 new species http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/srsStats/stats?outputFields=doc.uploadYear+tax.status&groupingFields=doc.uploadYear+tax.status&FP-doc.uploadYear 20&format=HTML .

Data liberated from publications from 2020:  http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/dioStats/stats?outputFields=doc.gbifId+bib.year+cont.pageCount+cont.treatCount+cont.matCitCount+cont.figCountZen+cont.bibRefCount&groupingFields=bib.year&FP-bib.year 20&format=HTML  including 7,118 new species http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/srsStats/stats?outputFields=bib.year+tax.status&groupingFields=bib.year+tax.status&FP-bib.year 20&format=HTML

The data is accessible through the Biodiversity Literature Repository<https://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/search?page=1&size >, TreatmentBank<http://tb.plazi.org/GgServer/static/newToday.html> at Plazi and GBIF<https://www.gbif.org/publisher/7ce8aef0-9e92-11dc-8738-b8a03c50a862>. Consider a taxonomic publication digital accessible knowledge when the treatments are in BLR<https://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/search?page=1&size &subtype=taxonomictreatment> and the materials citation in GBIF - that is when your data is fully part of the digital knowledge and contributing to a better understanding of our biodiversity.
