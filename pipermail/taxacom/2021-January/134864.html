Not so fast - we are talking scholarly, not popular usage I would say
(popular = not always correct...) - I found 1 paper from 1967 with "koala
bear" in the title, thus:

Antidiuretic activity in the pituitary gland of the *koala bear*
<https://www.nature.com/articles/216916a0>
NE Yesberg, OE Budtz-Olsen, K Sharples - Nature, 1967

As compared with 10,000+ with "koala", see e.g.:
https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C5&q=%22koala%22+%22journal%22&btnG
There is always an outlier or two, but I would say the trend is clear...
