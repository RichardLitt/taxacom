Dear Taxacomers, 

Please note that the Scientific Programme of the Xth Symposium of the
International Organization of Plant Biosystematists (IAPT interest group),
Evolution of Plants in Mountainous and Alpine Habitats, which will be held
in the Tatry Mts. from 2nd to 4th July 2008 was uploaded to the symposium
website.

For the Scientific Programme, please click here:
 
http://www.guarant.cz/iopb2008/Text/scientific-program?MenuItemId=10 

In order to give the possibility to attend the meeting also for those who
still have not registered, the Early registration deadline was prolonged
until 15th April 2008.

Details on excursions were uploaded to the symposium website as well and
they are available at:

http://www.guarant.cz/iopb2008/Text/excursions?MenuItemId=18


If you have any questions or any difficulties with the registration, please
contact our office at iopb2008 at guarant.cz

We are looking forward to meet you in the Vysoke Tatry Mountains in July!


Happy Easter ,
   

IOPB Team
