FULL-TIME ONE-YEAR TEACHING POSITION

The Department of Biology at Saint Mary’s College, a Catholic college 
for women located in Notre Dame, Indiana, invites applications for a 
one-year full-time position as a Visiting Assistant Professor for the 
2008-2009 academic year starting 15 August 2008. The successful 
candidate will teach introductory biology laboratories in the fall 
semester and two lecture sections for an introductory biology course for 
non-majors in the spring. In addition she/he will develop and teach a 
four-credit upper division botany course, with laboratory, in her/his 
area of expertise in the spring semester. The department may open a 
tenure-track position search next year for a botanist/environmental 
biologist. Education at the Ph.D. level is required with teaching 
experience preferred. Send letter of application, curriculum vitae 
including listing of courses taken and taught or unofficial transcript, 
and contact information for three references to: Doris Watt, Chair of 
the Search Committee, Department of Biology, Saint Mary’s College, Notre 
Dame, IN 46556. Review of applications will begin 25 April 2008 and 
continue until the position is filled. Women and minorities are strongly 
encouraged to apply. Saint Mary’s College is an equal opportunity employer.

/Saint Mary’s College has a strong institutional commitment to diversity 
and seeks applications from historically underrepresented racial and 
ethnic minority groups, those who have had multicultural experience, and 
those who can demonstrate a commitment to diversity./
