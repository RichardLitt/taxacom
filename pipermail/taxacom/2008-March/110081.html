First Workshop Announcement
===========================

Biodiversity Informatics:
challenges in modelling and managing biodiversity knowledge

10th July, 2008
Cardiff University (Wales, UK)

In association with the 25th British National Conference on Databases
(BNCOD) we are planning to hold a Biodiversity Informatics workshop
immediately after the main BNCOD conference, which runs from 7th to
9th July 2008.

Scope
=====
Papers on all aspects of modelling and managing biodiversity
knowledge are invited. Possible topics include:

- Interoperation of biodiversity systems
- Biodiversity data quality
- Supporting the taxonomic process
- Visualisation and manipulation of biodiversity data
- Modelling biodiversity concepts

This list is not intended to be exhaustive or to exclude
papers that do not fall into one or more of the above
categories.

Submission
==========
Original papers of up to 8 pages in Springer LNCS format are invited.
Submissions should be e-mailed as PDF documents to:

Andrew.C.Jones at cs.cardiff.ac.uk

Style files and instructions for producing your paper in Springer
LNCS format may be obtained by visiting the following URL:

http://www.springer.com/computer/lncs

and following the link labelled "Information for LNCS Authors and
Volume Editors".

Publication
===========
There is the opportunity for up to two of the best papers to be
published in the BNCOD conference proceedings (Springer-Verlag
Lecture Notes in Computer Science); it is planned to make the
remainder available to attendees on CD-ROM.

Presentation
============
At least one author of each accepted paper will be expected to
attend the workshop and to give a presentation (provisionally
20 minutes + 10 minutes questions).

Demonstrations
==============
If you would like to give a demonstration at this workshop
(not necessarily linked to a submitted paper), please e-mail
Andrew Jones to discuss this further.
