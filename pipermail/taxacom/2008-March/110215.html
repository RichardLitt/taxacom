A08/42

UNIVERSITY OF OTAGO
Te Whare Wananga o Otago

Dunedin, New Zealand

Lecturer in Botany (Confirmation Path)

DEPARTMENT OF BOTANY

Applications are invited for the position of Confirmation Path  
Lecturer (equivalent to American ‘tenure-track Assistant Professor’)  
in the Department of Botany.

The Department currently has interests in biotechnology, conservation  
biology, ecology, ecophysiology, genetics, marine botany, mycology,  
plant physiology, systematics and virology. See our website: www.botany.otago.ac.nz 
. The Department’s involvement is currently expanding in several  
programmes, especially Genetics.

Applicants should have an enthusiasm for research and both enthusiasm  
and flexibility in teaching.

Specific enquiries may be directed to Professor Bastow Wilson, Head of  
the Department of Botany, Tel + 64 3 479 7572, E-mail bastow at otago.ac.nz

Reference Number:  A08/42. Closing Date: Friday 09 May 2008.

APPLICATION INFORMATION

With each application you must include an application form, an EEO  
Information Statement, a covering letter, contact details for three  
referees and one copy of your full curriculum vitae.  For an  
application form, EEO Information Statement and a full job description  
go to: www.otago.ac.nz/jobs   Alternatively, contact the Human  
Resources Division, Tel 03 479 8269, Fax 03 479 8279, Email job.applications at otago.ac.nz

Equal opportunity in employment is University policy.
