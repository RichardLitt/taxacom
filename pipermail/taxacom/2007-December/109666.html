The Department of Biological Sciences, National Sun Yat-Sen University, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, invites applications for a professorship of systematic botany

Job Title: Assistant/Associate Professor
Employer: National Sun Yat-Sen University
http://www.oia.nsysu.edu.tw/english/index.php 
http://www2.nsysu.edu.tw/biology/En/Introduction.html
Location: Kaohsiung, Taiwan
 
Job Description

The Department of Biological Sciences of National Sun Yat-Sen University is seeking for candidates for a tenure-track faculty position in Botany. Candidate for this position is required to present demonstratable interest in teaching and research in the following fields: (1) Plant Systematics and Evolution or (2) Plant Ecology. 

Qualifications & Experience
 
Essential: A Ph.D. degree in a biological or related subjects and postdoctoral research experience
Desirable: Capable to communicate and teach in Mandarin and English.
 
Application
 
Applicants should submit a statement of career goals, a resume, the graduate transcript, publication reprints, and three letters of recommendation by January 31, 2008 to:
 
Prof. CHANG Hsueh-Wen
Chairman
Department of Biological Sciences
National Sun Yat-Sen University
70, Lien Hai Rd., Kaohsiung, TAIWAN, R.O.C. 80424

For any other questions about the position please contact Dr. LIU Ho-Yih at +886 7 5252000 ext 3618 or hoyih at mail.nsysu.edu.tw
