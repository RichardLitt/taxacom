The Postscript fileof the ASC model was produced from Word Perfect 5.1
(directing output to an Apple Laserwriter II and captured as a file).
The resulting file apparently prints correctly on a Sunsparc printer and
an Apple Laserwriter.  Various people are also testing printability to
HP LaserJet IIIsi and IV printers.

Unless you can suggest a better way to convert the original WordPerfect file
to Postscript, I can only recommend that you try the alternative formats --
the original WP 5.1 or plain ASCII.  (The ASCII version has no pagination,
but series of 10 CR-LFs corresponds to a page break -- if you care to search
and replace.)

In regards to it's "very, very draft" look; well yes, it was written by a
committee over a relatively short period, and is intended to serve as
point of departure for further discussion and elaboration.  We must
begin at...  Fill in the rest yourself, or perhaps more to the point,
"We must begin."

-- Stan
