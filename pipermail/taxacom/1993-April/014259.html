publication.  This would not treat collections explicitly, but might be relevant
to database issues.

Computer Software: Copyrights, Patents, and the Challenge of Technological
Change by U.S. Office of Technology Assessment, 237 pp, Hardbound, 1993,
ISBN 0-912702-72-9, Cat #0272-9, $54.50  available from Global
Professional Publications, 15 Inverness Way E, Englewood, CO 80112.

Phone 1-800-854-7179
FAX   1-303-792-2192

Here's what the ad says..
"A groundbreaking work on a critical and controversial topic by over 150
contributors and advisors from industry, research facilities, electronic
publishing, law, government, and educational institutions.  Examines the
rapid and complex technological changes and trends in computer software
technologies and their possible effects ont eh U.S. and global
intellectual property system.

Identifies and discusses three major issues:
 1) the scope of copyright protection for software,
 2) patent protection for software-related inventions and algortihms, and
 3) complications facing libraries and commercial and private producers
and users of digital information, including computer-based mixed media
products.
