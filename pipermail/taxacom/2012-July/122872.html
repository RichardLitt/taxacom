A derivative of the data paper format designed to publish online
interactive keys is illustrated through an exemplar
paper<http://www.pensoft.net/journals/zookeys/article/3409/moschweb->,
describing MOSCHweb <http://www.tachinidae.eu>, a new web application for
constructing keys. The model is described in a ZooKeys
editorial<http://www.pensoft.net/journals/zookeys/article/3581/publishing-online-identification-keys-in-the-form-of>and
associated press
release<http://www.pensoft.net/news.php?n=144&SESID=6abfcf6e015f2eb9c9d4cd3069e39422>.


The model gives the authors of online keys the opportunity to be credited
for their efforts through a permanent scientific record and a proper
citation mechanism.
