To those that need to identify  members of the Cyperaceae, new identification tools are now available at the Louisiana State University Herbarium web site.  The new keys are for Cyperus and Rhynchospora of North America, north of Mexico.  The keys are written in Lucid 3.4 and are free - http://www.herbarium.lsu.edu/keys/
Feedback, especially positive, welcomed.   :)
