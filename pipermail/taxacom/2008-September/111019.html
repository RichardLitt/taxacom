Jim Croft wrote:
>Given the fact that for the most part taxonomists are once
>opinionated, doctrinaire, possessive, elitist, authoritarian and

Something that always bugged me about zoologists was their checklists,
with some taxonomic names flip-flopping from one checklist to the next,
as each new one came out, reflecting an ongoing "Is too!", "Is not!"
argument in the literature.  Wikipedia is refreshing in that neutral
POV requires that such cases simply be noted as unstable.  Of course,
unstable is unsatisfactory.
