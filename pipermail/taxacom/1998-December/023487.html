As pointed out by John Bruner, within the ASIH list there is no GMNH.
However, there is (was) a GNHM that was used to refer to the
Naturhistoriska Riksmuseet, in Goteborg, SWEDEN by some. Could these
have been confused?

If you do find a GMNH, and it is a fish or herp collection, please
advise us so that we can emend the ASIH list.  Although the ASIH list
exists, there is no certainty that all codes used in the past were
identified.  Likewise changes are made from time to time and we need the
assistance of the community to keep track of these.
