prepared (in German) for a book entitled "Darwin & Co." that will appear
next year with C.H. Beck Verlag, Muenchen (Ilse Jahn & Michael Schmitt,
eds.). This manuscript is 30 pages long, and I can offer to send through
e-mail it to anybody interested. I searched for other biographies and/or
