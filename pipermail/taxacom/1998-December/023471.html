        What Stuart Fullerton is referring to by the
"arnett/samuelson/nishida 'insect and spider collections of the world'"
is the following website of the List of Codens of Entomological and
Arachnological Collections, kept at the Bishop Museum:

http://www.bishop.hawaii.org/bishop/ento/codens-r-us.html

What This Web Site Is
This web site is the clearing house of information on the insect and spider
collections of the world. The information presented here is based primarily
on the 1993 book, "The Insect and Spider Collections of the World", 2nd
edition, by R.H. Arnett, G.A. Samuelson & G.M. Nishida, with substantial
additions and corrections.
