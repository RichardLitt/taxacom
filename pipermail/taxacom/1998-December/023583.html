1998 
A new version of "DeltaAccess", an application for Microsoft Access
97 (under Windows 9x or NT 4.0) can be downloaded from

http://www.bgbm.fu-berlin.de/projects/DeltaAccess

Besides many bugfixes and general improvements, the major new
features are

* a HTML form generation that enables you to publish your Delta
projects on the internet in an editable format. Thus collaborators
can return their corrections to you, which you then can reintegrate
into your database;

* a summarize function to generate species descriptions from specimen
descriptions, and generic descriptions from species descriptions.

You can import and export DELTA files that follows the TDWG DELTA
standard. Basic character and item editors are provided. See the
preview at

http://www.bgbm.fu-
berlin.de/projects/DeltaAccess/PREVIEW_DELTAACCESS.HTML

I wish you a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year, with or without
DeltaAccess :-)
