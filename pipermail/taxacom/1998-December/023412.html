Dear Taxacom list subscriber,

Scientific Reference Resources (SRR) is pleased to announce
the inauguration of a new publication: New Entomological Taxa (NET).
It catalogues the current literature describing new taxa or
nomenclatural revisions of insects, arachnids, acarines, and
myriapods, as well as parasites and pathogens of these arthropod
groups.  NET will be published in both printed and on-line formats. A
prototype issue is available for examination at:

http://www.sciref.org/net/newtaxa.htm

A printed prototype issue will be sent on request.

We are interested in your feedback concerning the content or
organization of NET. Please direct any comments to the Editor,
Dr. Michael L. Grieneisen  at:  net at sciref.org

We strive to cover as much of the literature as possible,
within the boundaries of the scope of coverage.  Information
regarding journals which should be included in our searches, but do
not appear on our list (see http://www.sciref.org/net/alljrnl.htm)
would be most appreciated.

Sincerely,
