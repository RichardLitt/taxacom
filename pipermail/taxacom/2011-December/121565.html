Thank you. It is just that we were caught somewhat by surprise.

I am noting
* that the old website (http://tl2.idcpublishers.info/) is no longer
redirecting to Brill, but is now showing a referral message, which
is indeed much better!
* that TL-2 is indeed available at BHL
     - http://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/103414
     - http://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/103858 (Suppl.)
* that the Smithsonian site promises extra search options
(including an "advanced search" window) over and above 
what is now possible at BHL, and perhaps over what was
possible at the old, IDC, site (I have not tried this). 

Paul

PS: perhaps the IDC referral message could also mention the
Smithsonian site?
