Dear Geoff,

Yes, I have a more modest goal - linking names to citations to the digitised literature. I just want to see the name in context (and eventually mine that context for other data). 

Yes, ideally taxonomic information would be published in a structured form and automatically consumed by databases and other services. We are some way from that goal, and changes in current publication processes will still leave the vast majority of described taxa untouched.
