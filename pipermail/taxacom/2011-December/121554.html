Dear all,
Efloraindia (efi) just completed 1,00,000 messages in about four & a half
year of its working.
This is a great landmark in India's e-group history.
See this at home page:
https://groups.google.com/group/indiantreepix?hl=en&pli=1
All this has been documented on Efloraofindia (efi) website also:
https://sites.google.com/site/efloraofindia/home
Other groups can follow its example to document India's Flora & Fauna & its
diversity.

Efloraindia (eFI in short) is the largest google e-group in the world in
this field & the largest nature related e-group (and the most constructive)
in India devoted to creating awareness, helping in identification etc.
along with discussion on & documentation of Indian Flora.
