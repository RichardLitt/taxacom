Dear members
I have been regularly pointing out the corrections/missing names in The
Plant List, but this last one was really alarming for me and those who
follow botanical naming closely: I want to share this:


"The more I go through the Plant List, the more disappointed I get.  A list
which claims in the opening statement "*The Plant List* is a working list
of all known plant species", has numerous names missing. Though a big eye
sore the existence of unresolved names can be understood, but no
explanation can be found for names which are missing from the list.
Interestingly all these missing names are there in IPNI, and at least could
have been included in the List like so many other unreslved names. I find
no explanation for missing names, unless there was a great hurry to publish
the List in 2010. Here are some more names missing from the List. Flora of
British India was Published by Kew Botanists, and I find strange these
names are missing. I have already listed several mising names. Simple
search of two genera Argyreia and Lettsomia revealed that your list
includes 70 records for former and 26 for latter, whereas my list below has
76 names which either belong to these genera or their synonyms from India
alone, and are missing from your list.
"....................................................."In its form the list
is rather putting practicing taxonomists to embarrassment before the public
in general and scientific community in particular."
