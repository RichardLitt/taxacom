Hi Claus,

I have a database "IRMNG" which allows you to do fuzzy matches on "most" genera of animals, plants, etc. and perhaps 50% of species names. For example try this:

http://www.marine.csiro.au/mirrorsearch/ir_search.go?searchtxt=Halicyoides+mamus&hlevel=species

Of course the species level results will certainly be incomplete, but the genus level information should have a more likely chance of showing lexically similar names (except that for some short names the only a subset of similar matches are shown to prevent the list getting too long).

I had less success with Cenis uhleri or Cateres pennatus via this method since, in contrast to the example above, there are already some quite close genus matches (and a different logic for displaying the results, I should probably standardize...); however for these you can force the system to do a fuzzy match on species alone by entering an unrecognizable genus e.g. "xx+uhleri" and see what you get.

You can also enter just an epithet or just a genus for an exact match into the global names index, http://gni.globalnames.org/ , which has a lot more species than IRMNG, and see what looks similar in the other component, however there may be a lot of results to scroll through.

Let me know if you have any success,

Regards

Tony Rees, Hobart
