I am glad to see agreement that creating a Tree-of-Life,
an all-encompassing classification, is necessarily Original
Research. In this respect Wikispecies is indeed an OR-project.
This is not necessarily bad, but the more "creation" goes into it,
the more room there is for disagreement.

I don't really see that Wikispecies needs to include an in-depth
classification, after all, it is supposed to be a directory of species,
and, on the face of it, does not really need to include more depth
than up to family, or perhaps order.

Speaking in general, I don't particularly understand this compulsion
of building complete classifications and the religious fervour
with which these classifications are then pushed upon the
unsuspecting public. Building complete classifications is best
left to the Ken Kinmans of the world, puttering in their own
corner [no offense meant to Ken Kinman], and then putting
out their own books, websites, etc.

Paul
