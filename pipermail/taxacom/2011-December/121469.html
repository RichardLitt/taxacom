*DEAR COLLEAGUES!*
We invite you to participate in the I Scientific Conference ?Modern
phytomorphology?, which will be held 24-26 April 2012 in Lviv (Lemberg,
Ukraine) on the basis of Botanical department of Biological faculty of Ivan
Franko Lviv National University.
Especially we invite the scientists, who are specialized in the field of
anatomical and morphological studies of plants, fungi and lichens. Also we
invite the scientists, who use anatomical and morphological data in own
investigations of biology and ecology of underlined organisms.

The papers will be published as a separated book.

*WORKING AREAS OF CONFERENCE*
? theoretical bases and history of structural investigations of plants,
fungi and lichens;
? anatomical and morphological investigations of plants, and their
application in the other fields of botany and ecology;
? anatomical and morphological investigations of fungi and lichens, and
their application in the other fields of mycology and lichenology.

*PAPER FORMAT*
The paper size shall not exceed 6 ?-4 pages; font ? Times New Roman, size ?
12, indentation ? 1,25 ?m, 1,5-spaced, width fitted; margins 2 cm on all
four sides; file format ?.doc? or ?.docx?. The file name must consist of
the first author name (e.g. ? novikoff_text.doc).
Text sequence: article title (12 pt.) ? author?s names (12 pt.) ?
institute, postal address, e-mail (12 pt.) ? summary and keywords in the
language of main text (10 pt.) ? main text (12 pt.) ? references (10 pt.) ?
if the main text language is different from English, than you also must
print the article title, institute, postal address, e-mail, summary and
keywords in English (10 pt.) ? titles for figures and tables in the
language of main text and in English (12 pt.).
References (author and year) in the text are cited in the rounded marks.
Images are given as separate ?.jpg? or ?.tiff? files (300 dpi), or they may
be placed at the end of the main file on the separated pages. Tables are
given as separate ?.xls? or ?.xlsx? files, or they may be placed at the end
of the main file on the separate pages.
