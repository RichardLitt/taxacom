MalaCo: Electronical journal of French Continental Malacology 
  
MalaCo (ISSN 1778-3941) is a free electronic annual or biennial journal
dedicated to the promotion and knowledge of the continental Molluscs of
France. 
Subjects focus on biology, ecology, faunistics and conservation of
continental Molluscs. Moreover, MalaCo is a platform for the announcement
of upcoming events (congress, publications, working groups etc.). 
However, no description of taxon new to science will be accepted. 
Papers in English are accepted, but with a detailed abstract in French
(which can be translated by the editoria l board).
