Cassia grandis, widely introduced (Fabaceae broadly Caesalpiniaceae
narrowly, i.e., a bean).   Grows "native" in Central America but since the
black stuff is a medicinal, has been planted and then subsequently moved
all over the place.  Is a Pleistocene megafauna fruit.  Dan Janzen
