I think you are wrong.  The point of doing maximum likelihood is to fully
generalize the cladistic enterprise, but to maximize the use of all
available information.  I don't see how sorting characters before you
analyse them has any effect on the outcome.  In ML, you in effect propose
all possible synapomorphies (for all characters), and evaluate them based on
their joint probability.  More correctly, you propose all possible series of
changes that lead to the observed data (which include the synapomorphies),
and select the tree that is most likely reponsible for the most probable
evolutionary paths (all evolutionary paths are considered).

I think we are not talking the same language here.  That "ain't my momma's
definition" of a phenetic enterprise...
