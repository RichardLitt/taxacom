Interesting ancillary characters to include is that the only other known
species of Pthirus is Pt. gorillae (Phthiraptera : Pthiridae), while the
only other Pediculus species (Phthiraptera : Pediculidae) in Africa is
Pe. schaeffi on both Pan troglodytes and Pan paniscus. Pediculus also
occurs outside Africa, but unfortunately I cannot check on which hosts
(Ferris' 1951 The Sucking Lice should have details).
