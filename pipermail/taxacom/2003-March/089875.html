ANNOUNCEMENT

8th International Symposium on Neuropterology
26-29 July 2003
College Station, Texas, USA

The 8th International Symposium on Neuropterology will be held in the
Department of Entomology at Texas A&M University in College Station, Texas,
USA, from the 26th to the 29th of July 2003. The three-day scientific
program of the Symposium is open to all fields of research on neuropterid
insects (Neuroptera, Raphidioptera and Megaloptera), including, but not
restricted to, systematics, biological control, faunistics, behavior,
paleontology and general biology. Two excursions will follow the formal
scientific meetings.

Copies of the symposium announcement (containing information on fees,
accommodations, excursions, etc.) and registration materials can be
downloaded from the symposium web site
(http://entowww.tamu.edu/research/neuropterida/isn8/index.html), or
requested directly from the symposium organizer (see contact information
below).

In order to facilitate the finalization of certain symposium activities,
pre-registration is requested by 1 April 2003. Registrations received after
that date will be charged a late fee.

8th International Symposium on Neuropterology web site:
http://entowww.tamu.edu/research/neuropterida/isn8/index.html
