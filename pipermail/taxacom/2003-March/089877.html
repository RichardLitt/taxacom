I made no distinction here between the bonobo and the chimp in terms of
this group and the orangutan. Whether one considers the nearest relative to
be chimp, bonobo (either would seem to make Pan paraphyletic unless one
takes it to be chimp + bononbo) or gorilla, the basic competing hypothesis
seems to come down to a choice between our nearest relative being
knuckle-walking/thin enamel African apes or the non-knuckle-walking/thick
enamel Asian orangutans.
