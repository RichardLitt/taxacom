but - to start.  the tern  heebiejeebies  was a word coined (invented) by an
american camic-strip cartoonist  W. DeBeck (1890-1942) and has the slang meaning
"a condition of extreme nervousness caused by vear, worry, strain, etc.  the
jitters, the willies  (random house college dictionary -  1980 ). good luck with
that.

so - to have the heebiejeebies is to have the jitters, the willies, or to be
fastly nervous.  "nervous".
