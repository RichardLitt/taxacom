Recently the Yale University Herbarium (YU) moved to its new permanent
location, and we left behind about 150 standard herbarium cabinets (Lane).
These are 40 yrs old or so, but still in decent condition (don?t expect
anything shiny though!).  The old mercury cloride gaskets have been removed,
so the gaskets need to be replaced.  We have found that 3/8? thick silicone
gasket will properly seal the cabinets.  All cabinets are free and up for
grabs, although you must arrange for their removal.  Anyone interested
should contact me directly.

Best,
Nico
