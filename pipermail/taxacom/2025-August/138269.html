My feeling (which I believe is correct) is that botanical tradition is to
use a bit more Latin than zoological tradition (these days). So a botanical
work is more likely to say (e.g.) Smith et Jones, 1905, while a zoological
work would use Smith & Jones, 1905. For my compendium, which spans both
botanical and zoological names (the latter outnumber the former by around
4:1), I standardise everything to a single format, using "&" and not "et"
to link names together ("et al." being an exception).
