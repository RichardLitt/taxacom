The Symbiota2 project team invites you to attend the fourth open webinar on the Symbiota2 project. Each webinar is independent of the previous ones. They are minimally structured to encourage feedback and interaction, but each will start with an overview of recent progress.

TIME: Wednesday, July 14 at 10.30 am MDT
Location: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/82515787249
