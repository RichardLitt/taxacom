As one who has no learned expertise whatsoever on nomenclature, I do find
it problematic to 'recognise' or use nomen nudum names. I had this occur in
my group (ghost moths) where a Chinese author wrote on the biology of a
species using a name without the required designations (designated type
specimen and description). So there is no way to know what they were
referring to. Obviously it might be a 'real' and new species, but no way to
know, and so I see no value in this case continuing to "use an unavailable
name as if it were valid". I get the impression that people are free to
choose to ignore some or all of the code. Whether such choices are
preferable or not is in the eyes of the beholder.

John Grehan
