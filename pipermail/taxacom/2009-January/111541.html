The thanatos part of the etimology (as many said) can now be
dissmissed. I finally receive a correction from the person who told me
the thanatos story.

I found this:

botany
    1696, from botanic (1656), from Fr. botanique or M.L. botanicus,
from Gk. botanikos "of herbs," from botane "a plant, pasture, fodder."
The -y is from astronomy, etc. Botany Bay so called by Capt. Cook on
account of the great variety of plants found there.

on this web site:

http://www.etymonline.com/index.php?term=botany
