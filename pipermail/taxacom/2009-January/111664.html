Expedition Volume XIII, p. 1-171+plates) has > or < in front of several 
names in the synonymies.  There is no obvious connection to the status 
assigned to the taxon, nor is an explanation evident in the text.  As 
both Dall and the Harriman Expedition cover many taxa besides mollusks, 
I thought someone working with other taxa might have an idea as to what 
they signify.  Suggestions?
