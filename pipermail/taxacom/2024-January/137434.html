Hi all,
BON is looking for a Part-Time Bee Biodiversity Data Assistant (remote
work). Details below and attached. Please share with anyone who may be
interested and contact Neil Cobb (neilscobb at gmail.com) with applications or
questions.
Thanks and Happy New Year!
Erika

*Job Title: *Part-Time Bee Biodiversity Data Assistant



*Location: *Remote



*Duration: *Temporary position. 10-20 hours/week



*Start date:*  As soon as possible



*Pay Rate: *Based on qualifications. $17-22/hour







*Job Description*

We have an opportunity for a detail-oriented individual to join our team as
a Part-Time Bee Biodiversity Data Assistant. This temporary position offers
10-20 hours per week, providing flexibility for students or those with
other commitments.





*Responsibilities*



*1. Data Entry and Management:*



● Utilize Excel and Google Sheets for data entry and organization.

● Manage and update bee biodiversity datasets.



*2. Research and Web Harvesting:*



● Collect information from various online sources related to bee
biodiversity.

● Create bibliographic files summarizing relevant data.





*Qualifications*



● Strong attention to detail and accuracy in data entry.

● Familiarity with Excel and Google Sheets.

● Ability to conduct online research and gather information effectively.

● Basic proficiency in English; Spanish language skills are advantageous.







*Preferred Skills *(advantageous, but not required)*:*



● Familiarity with SQL databases.

● Previous experience with biodiversity data or environmental sciences.





*Rate of Pay*



Based on qualifications $15-$22 per hour





*How to Apply:*



If you'd like to contribute to native bee research and gain valuable
experience in biodiversity data management, send your *resume* and a *brief
cover letter *to *neilscobb at gmail.com* <neilscobb at gmail.com>. Please
highlight your relevant skills and why you are interested in this role.





***Deadline for Applications: Open until filled***
