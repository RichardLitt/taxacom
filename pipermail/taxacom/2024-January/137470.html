Is there an actual reason to make this a vox hybrida (half-Greek, 
half-French)...?

My inclination would be to treat Uleiota as a latinization (with change 
of ending) of ὑλειώτης (uleiôtês), which Greek dictionaries translate as 
'forester' -- itself formed from ὕλη (ulê), forest + -ώτης (-ôtês), a 
Greek masculine noun-forming suffix, used i.a. to form some demonyms. 
(In English, this suffix becomes -ot, as in 'Cypriot', an inhabitant of 
Cyprus; this derivation is consistent enough with Latreille's subsequent 
statement that the name means "habitant des forêts".)

Although ὑλειώτης is clearly masculine in Greek, Uleiota "takes the 
gender normally appropriate to [its] changed ending", which makes it 
feminine under Art. 30.1.3. (This would be true even if Latreille had 
treated it as masculine or neuter, explicitly or implicitly, I believe.)

Latreille himself subsequently treated the name as feminine ("Uleiota 
testacea") in this : https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.biodiversitylibrary.org%2Fpage%2F41892702&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cce200391906141556a1508dc1cfd3c35%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638417121758006895%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C41000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=AbZmk50n43CAPUSy61Tpcm4uXq6L2Wj3kufLhXZNe4E%3D&reserved=0.

However, Uleiota appears indeed to have often been treated as masculine 
-- e.g., see the many hits for that "Uleiota planatus" produce in Google 
-- presumably because it was given the gender of the Greek word which it 
was derived from.


This name was introduced in :

Latreille PA. 1797. Précis des caractères gérériques des insectes, 
disposés dans un ordre naturel. F Bourdeau, Brive; Prévôt, Paris.

p. 46 : https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.biodiversitylibrary.org%2Fpage%2F42201834&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cce200391906141556a1508dc1cfd3c35%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638417121758006895%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C41000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=kvCZNFko3hsTTP3CehfW94PwYmxYIQAc4LyeTz%2FUBJo%3D&reserved=0
