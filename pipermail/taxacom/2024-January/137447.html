> combination_ made with a species name (Am. cirratus Blyth, 1860) 
> turned into a subspecies?
>
> G

It would only be a new combination if Tomiyama (1936) was the first 
author to place the taxon cirratus Blyth, 1860 in genus Taenioides.

A taxon in the species-group has the same status whether it is treated 
as a species or subspecies, so changing the taxon cirratus Blyth, 1860 
from species to subspecies, within the genus it has previously been 
placed, is not new.
