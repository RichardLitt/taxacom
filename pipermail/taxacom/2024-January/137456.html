Interestingly, there is another Barnes, Richard Hawksworth Barnes, who was born in Colombo in 1831.
He made a collection of reptiles in Ceylon (Sri Lanka) and sent it to the British Museum.
He lived in Kensington, London (1871), and Dorset (1901), according to

Beolens, Bo; Michael Watkins, and Michael Grayson 2011
The Eponym Dictionary of Reptiles.
Johns Hopkins University Press, Baltimore, USA
