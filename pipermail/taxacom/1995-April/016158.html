Jean Thioulouse at the University of Lyon has registered with Lycos his URL
http://biomserv.univ-lyon1.fr/Ecology-WWW.html
for the WWW sites of interest to ecologists and botanists that we announced
earlier.

If you are looking for a particular botanically or ecologically related WWW
site, try using Lycos
http://lycos.cs.cmu.edu/

because of the high use of the search engine, recommend using the Lycos
search form, small catalog (if your browser can handle forms)
http://agent3.lycos.cs.cmu.edu/lycos-form.html
