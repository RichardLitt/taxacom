Dear Taxacom Subscribers:

If you no longer wish to receive Taxacom mail please
QUIETLY unsubscribe yourself or see the other options
for reading Taxacom on the Reference Card I e-mailed
yesterday.  No more flames, no more ranting and
raving about how bored you are with the current thread,

weeks and not being able to cope with your e-mail.

    E-mail to:  listserv at cmsa.berkeley.edu

at: beach at violet.berkeley.edu.
