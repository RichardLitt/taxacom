A good reference on the effects of and adaptations to fire is:

Fire In North American Tallgrass Prairie.  Edited by: S.L. Collins and
   L.L. Wallace.  University of Oklahoma Press, Norman, OK, USA.  (1990).

while the book focuses on North America, I think the concepts would be
applicable elsewhere.
