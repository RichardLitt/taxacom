                    Posted for and on behalf of the

 =========================================================================

   The Australian Nature Conservation Agency invites applications from
   persons interested in the following position:

      Australian Biological Resources Study
      Central Office
      Black Mountain
      A.C.T.

      Scientific Editor
      (Senior Professional Officer Grade C)
      $44,018 - $47,814 (Australian currency)
      (PN 445)

   The successful applicant will coordinate production, editing, preparation
   and publication of specific volumes of the Algae of Australia and related
   Australian Biological Resources Study (ABRS) publications, and supervise
   the work of authors, illustrators and support staff.

   The successful applicant will also assist in the development of
   procedures and conventions relating to the production of ABRS
   publications.

   Eligibility and other requirements: Possess an appropriate degree or
   diploma in science or an equivalent qualification.

   Selection will be based on assessment against specified selection
   criteria. Applicants should therefore ensure that in their application
   they address their claims in terms of the selection criteria.

   The position profile may be obtained by telephoning (06) 250 0265.
   [ email to Sean Morton: smorton at anca.erin.gov.au ]

   Applications should be forwarded to:

   The Recruitment Officer
   Australian Nature Conservation Agency
   GPO Box 636
   CANBERRA ACT 2601

   by 21 April 1995

   The Australian Nature Conservation Agency is an equal opportunity
   employer and provides a smokefree working environment.

 =========================================================================

not covered in the position profile, contact Tony Orchard:
