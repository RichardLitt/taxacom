Luis Ruedas has provided a corrected citation for the paper by Jared Diamond on
the ornithological skills of the Fore people of New Guinea.  For those that
might be interested, it is:

Diamond, J.M.  1966.  Zoological Classification System of a Primitive People.
Science 151:1102-1104.

My apologies to any (like Luis) that might have pawed through the entire 1965
volume of Science looking for the article.
