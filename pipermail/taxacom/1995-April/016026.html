The Borror Laboratory of Bioacoustics has placed the summary catalog of its
holdings of animal sounds on-line. The Laboratory is housed in the Museum of
Biological Diversity of the Ohio State University and is maintained by D.A.
Nelson and S.L.L. Gaunt. To view it, point your web browser at the following
URL:

http://iris.biosci.ohio-state.edu/borror_lab/intro.html

You may contact Sandra Gaunt electronically at sgaunt at magnus.acs.ohio-state.edu
