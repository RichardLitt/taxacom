TAXACOM subscribers who seek alternative mailing lists where they might
air their views on environmental issues may wish to consult:

 Ecogopher at University of Virginia, USA

The directory item "Communication:  Archives of Environmental Mailing Lists"
holds brief descriptions, subscription instructions and archives for a wide
array of lists, e.g., Biosphere, Women and Environment, Environmental
Ethics, Fish, Forest, Pollutants.  There is also a link to an Environmental
CHAT site.
