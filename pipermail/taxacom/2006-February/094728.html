Fred:

I am not familiar with the North American flora. But as far as I know,
there is no species of that family in the US.

But, you might want to try the general family description of
Balanophoraceae on:
http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/research/APweb/orders/santalalesweb2.htm#Balanophoraceae
A picture of a representative of that family from Ecuador:
www.uni-bonn.de/~uzsmxn/plant_galleries/images_yasuni/Ombrophytum_peruvianum.jpg

According to your description, it is pretty likely to be a parasitic /
saprophytic vascular plant. Check with the Flora of North America. There
are not many higher plants like that.
( http://www.efloras.org/flora_page.aspx?flora_id=1
http://hua.huh.harvard.edu/FNA/ )

Cheers,

Holger
