We are now seeking applicants for the following position at the Illinois
State Museum:

Position Title: Curator of Zoology
Work Location: Illinois State Museum
Research and Collections Center
1011 East Ash Street
Springfield, IL 62703

Job Description: Oversight of collections and research program
development in vertebrate zoology including molecular approaches to
systematics, population biology, biogeography, natural history, or
analyses of ancient DNA; research and publication on zoology
collections, which would include Midwestern fauna, biogeography, natural
history, and related themes; participation in the Museum?s
interdisciplinary Landscape History Program; curation and upgrading of
zoology collections; collections database management; generation of
external funds for research and collections; participation in exhibition
and educational programming in the sciences.

Qualifications: Ph.D. in zoology or related discipline required.
Candidate should have: (1) educational background and research
experience in discipline relevant to the Museum?s collections and
programs, including Quaternary vertebrate zoology, molecular approaches
to systematics, biogeography, natural history, or analyses of ancient
DNA; (2) strong commitment to and experience with interdisciplinary
approaches and research; (3) experience in museum, university, or
comparable setting; (4) experience with zoological collections and
database management for zoology collections; (5) strong publication
record and the potential to generate outside funding; (6) experience
with educational programming in the sciences preferred (7) supervisory
experience preferred..

Salary: Commensurate with education and experience.

Benefits: Generous vacation with personal and sick leave, including 12
paid holidays. State-sponsored group health, dental, vision and life
insurance.

Application: Send cover letter, curriculum vitae, and the names and
addresses of three references to:
Human Resource Office, Illinois State Museum
Spring & Edwards, Springfield, IL. 62706-5000
217-782-5969 (fax) 217-782-1254
e-mail: cmontgom at museum.state.il.us
Position available June 1, 2006 and open until filled.
To receive full consideration submit application by April 1, 2006.

Technical Questions regarding this position should be addressed to Dr.
Everett D. Cashatt, Search Chair, Illinois State Museum, Research &
Collections Center, 1011 East Ash, Springfield, IL. 62703. Phone
217-782-7475;
e-mail: cashatt at museum.state.il.us.

Applicants should note that this position is exempt from the State
Personnel Code and is under the Board of Directors of the Illinois State
Museum. This is not a Bargaining Unit position nor a Merit Comp
position. The Illinois State Museum does not discriminate on the basis
of race, color, sex, national origin, age or handicap in compliance with
the Illinois Human Rights Act, the Illinois Constitution, Title VII of
the 1964 Civil Rights Act, Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of
1973, as amended, and the U.S. Constitution.
