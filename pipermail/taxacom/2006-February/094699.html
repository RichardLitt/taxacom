Dear group,

I'm posting this question to the community to learn of colleges/universities
that have an official undergraduate major in 'Systematics' or 'Phylogenetics'.

my understanding that in most cases, students at these institutions declare
their actual major as 'biology', 'zoology', 'entomology', 'botany', etc.
