The California Academy of Sciences invites applications for the John T.
Howell Curatorial Chair

of Western North American Botany. Applications are solicited from
outstanding individuals

the systematics and

evolution of vascular plants in western North America, ideally with an
interest in California plant

diversity. We are particularly interested in individuals addressing key

peer-reviewed journals, curation

leadership capacities,

mentorship of students are

desired. For a full job description and to apply for this opening, please
