I have read it, it is nonsense.

If you have a name, it is probably one that has been used before. 
Someone with that name, possibly even the one you were named after, may 
have done something that someone finds offensive. Should you change your 
name?? No.? You are not that offending person, and the name is yours in 
your case, not theirs.? It is a moniker for identification and 
communication. The same goes for species epithets.? They belong to the 
species, not the offending person.

I suspect that none of the authors have had a species named after them, 
and are just jealous.

Mike
