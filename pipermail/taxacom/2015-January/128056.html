It's a vain hope, I know, and there is lots of legacy data out there 
in every possible format. But can't we decide from here on just to 
use decimal degrees for latitudes and longitudes? They're just 
numbers, after all. Why do we have to treat them as being made up of 
weird multiples of 60 instead of using the decimal format that we use 
for every other unit? Pounds, shillings and pence are long gone - so 
should minutes and seconds be.
