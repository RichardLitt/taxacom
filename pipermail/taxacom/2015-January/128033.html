Dear Taxacomers,

You are kindly welcome to submit *by 28 February 2015* the manuscript(s)
for publication in the next 7th and 8th volumes of Modern Phytomorphology
journal. All accepted manuscripts will be published by June 2015 in
electronic versions only and will be deposited on
http://phytomorphology.org/ . Publication of manuscripts of any volume is *free
of charge*.



Please fill online registration form for each of manuscripts before
submission: http://phytomorphology.org/manuscript-submission/

Please see details about general rules and submission procedure here:
http://phytomorphology.org/journal/for-authors/



Sincerely,

Andrew Novikoff
