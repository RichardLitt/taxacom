All users should be aware, however, of the huge errors that can be introduced by conversion.  Tony Rees made a presentation to OBIS once about this (since marine data were nearly always DMS -- and now DD -- because the ocean lacks landmarks; only nearshore records may refer to landmarks), and showed that a conversion moved his home from the nice bayside locality it occupies outside Hobart to the hills well inland.  I'd bet he has it archived somewhere that you could find to caution people.
