FRESHWATER ALGAE IN AUSTRALIA

by Tim Entwisle, Jason Sonneman and Simon Lewis

A colour guide to the conspicuous genera of freshwater
algae in Australia. Over 300 colour photographs supplemented
by elegant hand drawings and computer-generated identification
icons. The audience is students, water-managers, naturalists,
limnologists and even seaweed folk - anyone who wants to discover
the exciting world of freshwater algae. It will be as useful outside
Australia as in.

The text is simple and easy-to-read, and there are no
dichotomous keys. Instead the genera can be 'keyed' out
using colour-coded icons.

Note that this is not a comprehensive Flora and it goes to
genus level only. However, we provide references to literature
which can be used for species-level identification.
