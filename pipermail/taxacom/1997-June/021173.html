1997 
A significant number of Taxacom subscribers have not sent a

CONFIRM TAXACOM

command to listserv at cmsa.berkeley.edu (NOT to taxacom), in order to
renew their subscription.

Those who haven't done so, but who wish to continue their subscription
must act _today_ or their subscription will automatically lapse.

You can check on the status of your subscription renewal by sending
email to listserv at cmsa.berkeley.edu (NOT to taxacom) with the command:
query taxacom

If the response contains an entry saying:
"N/A   Your subscription must be confirmed as soon as possible!"
you'll know that you are still not renewed.


IF YOUR SUBSCRIPTION LAPSES UNINTENTIONALLY...

You will be notified if your subscription lapses. If that
happens and you wish to re-subscribe, you can send email to
listserv at cmsa.berkeley.edu (NOT to taxacom) with the command:

subscribe taxacom Yourfirstname Yourlastname

Replace "Yourfirstname Yourlastname" with your real name.
Peter
