Backer, in his monumental Explanatory Dictionary (Verklarend Woordenboek,
1936) writes that Scaevola was named after Gajus Mucius. After giving the
famous episode he continues: 'The limb of the corolla has the shape of an
extended hand; in the original species (and also in the Javanese S. frutescens)
the flowers are initially white and turn brown later, is if they were
scorched.

Hope this helps.
