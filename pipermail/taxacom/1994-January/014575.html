1994 
     We have the sad duty of reporting the passing of Dr. Rolf Singer, at age
87.  Dr. Singer, a member of the Field Museum staff for many years, died in
Chicago late Tuesday evening, 18 January 1994, of heart failure.  Dr. Singer
had a long and productive career in mycology and published over 300 papers and
several books, among them "Agaricales in Modern Taxonomy" (now in the 4th
edition).  We will miss Rolf as a colleague and friend.
