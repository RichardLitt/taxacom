1994 
                    MEETING ANNOUNCEMENT

                THE HERPETOLOGISTS' LEAGUE
                            AND
   THE SOCIETY FOR THE STUDY OF AMPHIBIANS AND REPTILES
                     1994 JOINT MEETING

The joint 1994 meeting of the Herpetologists' League (HL) and The
Society for the Study of Amphibians and Reptiles (SSAR) is being
hosted by The University of Georgia Museum of Natural History and
the Savannah River Ecology Laboratory at the University of Georgia
campus in Athens, Georgia from July 28 through August 1, 1994.
All interested persons (members of HL or SSAR or non-members) are
welcome.  All attendees must pay the registration fee.  The pre-
registration fee is $80.00 for students and $115.00 for non-
students.   The deadline for pre-registration is May 15 for
authors and June 20 for all others.  The registration fee after
that date is $160.00 for everyone.

The full meeting announcement, description of activities, call for
papers, pre-registration form, and full information on
accommodations will be mailed to HL and SSAR members in the United
States, Canada, Mexico and the Caribbean in early February 1994.
Overseas members will not automatically receive the meeting
announcement, but may request a mailing by contacting Amy Edwards
(see address below).

For information concerning general program arrangements, contact
Amy Edwards, Museum of Natural History, University of Georgia,
Athens GA 30602-1882 (phone 706-542-4137; FAX 706-542-3920; email:
aedwards at zookeeper.zoo.uga.edu).

For information concerning paper or poster sessions, contact
Martina Conrad, Savannah River Ecology Laboratory, P.O. Drawer E,
Aiken, SC 29802 (phone 803-725-2472; FAX 803-725-3309; email:
conrad at srel.edu).

For information concerning registration procedure and lodging
reservations, contact Information Desk, Georgia Center for
Continuing Education, University of Georgia, Athens GA 30602
(phone 706-542-6658; FAX 706-542-5990; email:
reedn at gacsrv.gactr.uga.edu).
