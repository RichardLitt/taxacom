I am looking for verifiable records of plum curculio, CONOTRACHELUS NENUPHAR,
from the western U.S. with the EXCEPTIONS of Montana and Utah.  There are
listings in catalogs, etc., but for the most part I have not been able to
substantiate these records.

I would appreciate it very much if those of you associated with insect
collections would check to see if you have any specimens from the area in
question.  If you do, please send me the label data.

Thanks!
