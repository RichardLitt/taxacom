The ICBN is on-line -- i as follows:

     International Code of Botanical Nomenclature (Tokyo Code). The rules
on plant nomenclature,
     adopted by the Fifteenth International Botanical Congress, Yokohama,
August-September 1993.
     Electronic version of the original English text. The www version is
available from the web server of the
     Botanic Garden and Botanical Museum of Berlin-Dahlem, Germany.

The URL is

        http://www.bgbm.fu-berlin.de/iapt/nomenclature/code/tokyo-e/

Notice the careful, legalistic wording to the effect that the only official
version of the ICBN is the hard copy printed version!
