Dear Listers,

Since the last textbook on zootaxonomy in Hungarian was printed 20 years
before it is high time to prepare an new one. Before the negotiations with
the potential authors will start we would like to have an overview what
kind of textbooks are used widely for this topic.  We are thinking on an CD
ROM accompaniment, as well, containing many of the illustrations, texts
having non general interest, tests and a kind of exercise book. Do any of
you have similar volume out on the market? We thinking on a userfriend,
easy to learn textbook.

We would be most welcome your experiences and ideas.

Please send the answer to my e-mail address.
