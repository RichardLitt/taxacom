1998 
--------------------------------------------------------------
The Botany Department of The Natural History Museum, London (BM) has just
launched a new online newsletter. Plant Cuttings brings together highlights
of current botanical research at the Museum, recent events and news of
botanists working behind the scenes.  Issue 1 of Plant Cuttings can be
viewed at:
http://www.nhm.ac.uk/botany/cuttings/issue1/index.html

Plant Cuttings will initially be issued once every six months.  If you have
any comments, questions or suggestions please get in touch!.

Karen Sidwell, Steve Cafferty, Dylan Fuller, Sally Henderson and Alex Monro
