SUBJECT: Tiger Beetles of CT USA: a key to larvae

Dear Taxacomers (specifically those with specimens of larval tiger
beetles from Connecticut),

I have completed an on-line key to the larval stages of 13 of the 14
species of Cicindela that occur, or once occurred, in the state of
Connecticut.

The key is part of a website devoted to the taxonomy & conservation of
CT's Cicindela species and may be accessed via:

http://viceroy.eeb.uconn.edu/ctb/home.html

Feedback, constructive criticism, etc. is welcome..

Yours,

Derek Sikes
