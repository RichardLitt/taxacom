Derek Sikes recent post about the tiger beetle key prompted me to plug our
web-based key to Odonata larvae here at the UMMZ.  It is still a work in
progress, but people have found it to be very useful.  The images have been
scanned directly from the microscope and are the next best thing to
actually having a specimen in front of you. Set your browser to:
http://insects.ummz.lsa.umich.edu/michodo/test/home.htm  and give it a try!
