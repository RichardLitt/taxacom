Current version of the ICBN (Tokyo Code) is available on the site of IAPT

English version can be accessed through:

http://www.bgbm.fu-berlin.de/iapt/nomenclature/code/tokyo-e


Alternately web search for International Association would lead to their
main page where nomenclature would take to the Tokyo Code in English,
French, German and Slovak in that sequence. English version is avaiolable
in full form with all articles and examples.
