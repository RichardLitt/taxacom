Dr Muniz's problem has a practical solution, not a strictly
nomenclatural one, and hence applies whether his "species Y" falls under
the botanical or zological Code.

If there is no type, no figure, and insufficient data to identify the
taxon to which author X's name Y applies, then it is impossible to use
Y, and, being unusable, it simply lapses.

Of course if some taxonomic terrorist comes along who "knows" what
author X meant and proceeds to neotypify, there may be a problem, but
both Codes now have mechanisms to deal with such cases if they disturb
existing nomenclature (rejection under Art. 56 of the ICBN and
suppression under the plenary powers of the Commission under the ICZN).
