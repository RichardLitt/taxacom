Something a little more hands-on: I've been using 1-dram 15 x 45 mm 
glass vials for alcohol storage of insects, mostly because I like being 
able to see things without taking them out and they seem to leak less 
than plastic microvials (even with O-rings).  Unfortunately I can't find 
anything good and reasonably inexpensive to store them in.  They're just 
slightly too big to fit in the boxes for microcentrifuge tubes, and for 
some reason the plastic boxes that would fit cost ~$7+ apiece.  I've 
been using the cardboard -80 freezer boxes with 8x8 dividers, but 
they're kind of flimsy (especially when they get wet).  Anyone have a 
better solution?
