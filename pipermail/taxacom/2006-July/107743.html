Mailing information required by USPO for shipment of herbarium material 
is as follows:
*
Museum specimens*. Dried plant material. Of no commercial value.
E714.2.2.g. Library Mail. DMM Issue 56, 7 Jan 2001. Page E-144.
Dried botanical specimens, without commercial value, for scientific 
study only.
Incapable of transmitting agricultural diseases. Return postage guaranteed.

The last item about return postage is an option available to the shipper 
but not required.
