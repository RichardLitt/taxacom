Dear All,

I want to make sure that everyone is aware of the time and location  
of the upcoming 2 days NSF workshop on imaging and data sharing.  We  
will meet on Saturday the 29th and Sunday the 30th of July at the  
University Campus in Chico, CA.

Saturday 29th, @ 314 Bell Memorial Union, 9:00 am - 5 pm
Imaging workshop
Panel: Stinger Guala (USDA), Reed Beaman (Yale), Chris Frazier (UNM),  
Austin Mast (FSU), and Melissa Tulig (NYBG)

Sunday 30th, @ 207 Butte Hall computer lab, 9:00 am - 4 pm
Data sharing workshop (featuring hands-on DiGIR training)
Panel: Meredith Lane (GBIF), John Wieczoreck (UC Berkeley), Steve  
Ginzbarg (UA), and John Clark (NBII)

Best,
Nico
