Rosalind,

Ilex anomala in Hawaii is probably not a good choice for a study of leaf
miners. This tree has very few obligate herbivores, and as far as I know, no
recorded leaf miners. You (or your student) might want to check out the
Agromyzidae section of Insects of Hawaii for ideas on other leaf miner
systems to study.

Zimmerman EC. 1980. Insects of Hawaii, Volume 13 - Diptera: Cyclorrhapha
III. pp. 190-229
