Dear All,

Encyclopedia of Life has recently announced a deadline extension for the
2011 EOL Rubenstein Fellows
competition<http://www.eol.org/content/page/fellows>through
*September 30, 2010*.

Please send all application materials to fellows_program at eol.org by midnight
on September 30, 2010. If you have previously submitted an application and
would like to use this time to revise it, please feel free. Simply submit
updated materials by September 30. We encourage all applicants to download
our revised application form (online since August 22) from
http://www.eol.org/content/page/fellows.

Please direct any  additional questions you may have to
fellows_program at eol.org and feel free to pass this update along to any
colleagues or contacts who may be interested.
