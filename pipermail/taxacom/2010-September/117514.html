Dear colleagues,

the Zoologische Staatssammlung Munich will celebrate its bi-centennial 
birthday in early 2011. Accordingly, we are currently looking for taxon 
names associated with our first director ("conservator"), Johann Baptist 
Ritter von Spix.
Find enclosed a preliminary list, what we already have collected.
We would be very grateful, if you could inform us about any other taxon 
name associated with Spix.
Many thanks in advance.

Species names in honour to Johann Baptist von Spix (1781 - 1826)

Birds
Cyanopsitta spixii Wagler, 1832, Spix´s Ara 
Synallaxis spixi Sclater, 1856 - der Spixschlüpfer, engl.: Spix's Spinetail,
Ortalis spixi Hellmayr, 1906 - das Hokkohuhn,
Grypus spixi, Gould, Spix's Saw-bill -(Trochilidae)
Xiphorhynchus spixii (Lesson, 1830) Spix's Woodcreeper (Furnariidae-Dendrocolaptinae)


Mammals
Aotus spixi (Pucheran, 1857), Spix Nachtaffe
Oedipomidas spixi (Reichenbach, 1862) -(Callitrichidae oder Callitrichinae).
Midas spixi Elliot. 1913 - ein Synonym von Saguinus [Oedipomidas] geoffroyi .
Galea spixi (Wagler, 1831), Spix's Yellow-toothed Cavy

Reptiles und Amphibia
Micrurus spixii Wagler, 1824 -
Santanadactylus spixi Wellnhofer 1985 - Santana Formation.
Acanthochelys spixii (Duméril & Bibron, 1835) = Emys depressa Spix 1824.
Platemys radiolata spixi, Peracca, 1895. -
Chironius carinatus spixi (Hallowell, 1845) -
Leptodactylus spixi Heyer (1983) -(Leptodactylidae),

Fishes
Selene spixi (Castelnau, 1855) -(Carangidae).
Cathorops spixii (Agassiz, 1829) -(Ariidae) Madamango Sea Catfish.

Molluscs
Asolene spixi (d'Orbigny, 1837) -(Ampullariidae),“Spixi snails”
Anodontites spixi d'Orbingy,1835-, Mycetopodidae,
Helix spixiana Pfeiffer, 1841 = Streptaxis candida.
Spixia spixii see under “Gattungsnamen”.
Insects and other invertebrates
Casignetus spixi (Perty, 1830) -, Lucanidae. = Chalcimon spixi
Caladomyia spixi Säwedal, 1981 (Chironomidae).
Sabethes spixi Cerqueira, 1961 (Culicidae)
Acanthognathus spixi spider (?family)
Tetracha spixii (Brullé, 1837) (Cicindelidae),

Genera
Spixia (Pilsbry & Vanatta, 1898: Stylommatophora
Spiximorpha Diptera, Syrphidae, probably invalid emendation from Sphiximorpha
Spixoconcha:  Mycetopodidae
Spixomyia: Diptera, Tachinidae, valid subgenus
Spixosoma: Diptera, Conopidae, available but invalid
Spixostoma: Pisces


Species which were described by Spix and are now named in Englisch or German according to
him:
Thyroptera tricolor (english: Spix`s disk winged bat, deutsch: Amerikanische Haftscheibenfledermaus).
Spix-Nachtaffe (Aotus vociferans Spix, 1823).
Spix-Guan (Penelope jacquacu Spix, 1825),
Spix’s Whiptail Cnemidophorus ocellifer Spix, 1825 Plant species
Eriocaulon spixianum (Mart.) Steud.,(Eriocaulaceae, Poales),
Polypodium spixianum Mart. ex Mett.,(Polypodiaceae),
Duguetia spixia,(Annonaceae, Magnoliales)
Ocotea spixiana (Nees) Mez.,(Lauracea) plant genus
Spixia (Asteraceae) genus name.
