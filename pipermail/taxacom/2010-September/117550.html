Dear colleagues,

 

The Van Eeden Foundation encourages and supports research projects in the fields of botany, ecology, and ethnobotany or with a conservation viewpoint, that are conducted in the Guianas, the Netherlands Antilles, and Aruba. The Foundation grants financial aid for internships, fieldwork, publications, lectures, and other research costs. More information including the online application form can be found on the website  --> --> -->  www.vaneeden-fonds.nl 

 

The website of the Van Eeden Foundation is now online. Please spread the word.
