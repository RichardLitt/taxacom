  On behalf of the NSF working group for ADBC, we would like to note 
that soon we expect a series of Frequently Asked Questions to be 
posted.We also expect to facilitate community discussion by establishing 
a WIKI site through NSF to allow potential applicants the opportunity to 
form collaborations which can then be further developed off the wiki 
site. Each of these efforts must be approved by the NSF policy office, 
but please stay tuned. All questions should be sent by e-mail 
correspondence to biodigit at nsf.gov.
