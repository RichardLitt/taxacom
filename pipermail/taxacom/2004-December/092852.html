The second circular and associated documentation about this
international conference on palaeoentomology, palaeoarthropodology and
amber, to be held from 7-11 February 2005 at the South African National
Biodiversity Institute in Pretoria, are available from
brothers at ukzn.ac.za . They will soon also be available at
http://www.palaeoentomolog.ru .

Although the closing date has just passed, additional contributions
will still be welcomed until early January.
