Biological Science Educator
Miami University
Oxford, OH 45056 USA

Description of Position
Botany or Microbiology or Zoology: Assistant or Associate Professor
to develop and teach science education and teach science courses in home
department; pursue pedagogical scholarship; mentor graduate students;
promote continuing development of the Master of Arts in Teaching program
in biology, as well as other collaborations in science education.
Require: Doctorate in an appropriate scientific discipline or in science
education. For appointment at associate level, require established
record of scholarship. Desire: active scholarship in science education;
bachelor's or master's degree in non-terminal degree field, either
science education or appropriate science discipline. Submit curriculum
vitae, statements of teaching and scholarship goals, e-mail address and
arrange for three letters of recommendation to be sent to R. James
Hickey, Department of Botany, 316 Pearson Hall, Miami University,
Oxford, Ohio 45056. Contact phone number is 513-529-6000. Screening of
applications begins January 15, 2005 and will continue until the
position is filled. Miami University is an Equal Opportunity/Affirmative
Action Employer.
