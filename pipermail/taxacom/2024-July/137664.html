AI is not all bad. My wife found the phrase erga pro logou in a work written in the 1800's, reporting it as carved by a student on a tree. We could not find it on the Web, on Wikipedia, or Google Scholar. As a last resort I asked the poe.com AI about it and got the correct answer immediately "Work before talk." No citation of source, but doubtless correct. Apparently, even when correct, AIs do not stand on the shoulders of humans.

Neither of you has commented on the fact that, within its single page length, the PDF includes the same paragraph twice.

Having tried to help clean up a few documents that people "improved" with AI, the mind-numbingly droning character of the text is the most obvious clue this is AI-generated.

For any of you familiar with UK radio comedy shows, the tone is often exactly like that of sentences in the show "Just A Minute" where speakers have to talk on some topic without repeating any words. The focus is on playing the word game and remaining on-topic rather than actually saying anything novel or thought-provoking or even authoritative.
