Dear colleagues,

An outstanding new publication:
TRUJANO-ORTEGA MA, LUIS-MARTÍNEZ AR, VARGAS-FERNÁNDEZ IS, ÁVALOS-HERNÁNDEZ
OM, LLORENTE-BOUSQUETS JO. Introduction to the analysis, synthesis, and
comparisons of endemic butterflies in Mexico. Zootaxa. 2024 Jul
12;5479(1):1-73.

It includes beautiful maps of hundreds of species and subspp.,
highlighting, among many other things, massive endemism on the Guerrero
terrane of SW Mexico.
