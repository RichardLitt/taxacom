There are a few ways you could try to demonstrate that your email is human-written rather than AI-generated:

1.      Include personal details or context that an AI wouldn't know.

2.      Reference recent events or conversations you've had with the recipient.

3.      Use more natural, conversational language with some imperfections.

4.      Offer to have a phone call or video chat to discuss further.

5.      Explain your thought process or reasoning in a distinctly human way.

6.      Include a handwritten signature or personal touch.
