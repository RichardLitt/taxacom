Neither of you has commented on the fact that, within its single page
length, the PDF includes the same paragraph twice.

Having tried to help clean up a few documents that people "improved" with
AI, the mind-numbingly droning character of the text is the most obvious
clue this is AI-generated.

For any of you familiar with UK radio comedy shows, the tone is often
exactly like that of sentences in the show "Just A Minute" where speakers
have to talk on some topic without repeating any words. The focus is on
playing the word game and remaining on-topic rather than actually saying
anything novel or thought-provoking or even authoritative.
