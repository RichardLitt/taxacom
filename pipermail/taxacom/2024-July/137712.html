Dear John and Jeremy,

I agree that the action taken is measured and reasonable. However, it is causing quite a stir in wikipedia because wikipedia generally follows other databases and the workload will be huge. There is resistance to making any changes until they are well established elsewhere (I have argued that that is likely to leave wikipedia incorrect for a long time).

There would be much more that might be considered in this line. Previous decisions have sided with not hiding history, so not changing a name that commemorated the use of a grass species to whip slaves. Changing all such names might obviously be very disruptive.

Best wishes,

Nadia Talent
