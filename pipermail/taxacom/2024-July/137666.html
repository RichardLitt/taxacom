Cyclogramma Doubleday, 1848 was made available on the plate, with Cycl. 
rubens Doubleday, 1848 as type by monotypy.

I do not know where the information on the dates for Doubleday's 
1846-1850 work were taken, and why Hemming (1967) cited 1847. Doubleday 
in the text p. 219 indicated that Pl. 27 was published in 1848.

Cyclogramma Perty, 1849 is a junior homonym of Cyclogramma Doubleday, 1848.

If this helps.
