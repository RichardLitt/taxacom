Richard, the Banerjee / Crimson paper that you say was AI-generated already has a number of red flags right at first sight:

1. A dubious publisher
2. A “review" that has literally only 1 page — seriously?
3. No obvious data, figures, tables — how can this be a credible scientific paper?
4. No email address of the author — always a red flag, and common in many predatory journals.
5. DOI link doesn’t work

Well, at least the references I checked were legit and not hallucinated.

I did find and email the author — no response yet.
