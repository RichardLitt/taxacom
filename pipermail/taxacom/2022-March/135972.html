Each submitted manuscript will now be carried through the peer-review,
editing, publication, dissemination and archiving stages without leaving
the platform?s collaboration-centred online environment.



The journal is also expanding to extend its scope beyond Te Papa?s
collections and areas of expertise to include all aspects of museum
research, with a focus on Aotearoa New Zealand and the Pacific.

The articles will be openly available in PDF and machine-readable XML
formats, and semantically enriched HTML for better reader experience.



We have just started receiving the first manuscripts, so the articles will
soon start popping up on our new website. We are also working on clearing
the rights to bring all past issues of Tuhinga to the new platform and to
Diamond Open Access status.

Tuhinga is the successor of a long line of journals dating back to the
early days of New Zealand?s Colonial Museum. Together, these journals have
acquired over 150 years? worth of academic knowledge provided by the
museum?s curators, collection managers, and research associates, as well as
external contributors, across disciplines, from Archeology to Zoology.



With this renewal and the new platform, we hope Tuhinga will be ready for
the next 150 years.
