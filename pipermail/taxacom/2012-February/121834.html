Would you please post the attached job announcement?  The California Native Plant Society (CNPS) is a science-based advocacy organization with over 9,000 members organized in 33 chapters across the state.  The Society’s members work in cooperation with county, state, and federal agencies, elected officials, NGOs, and other non-profit organizations to conserve California native plants and their natural habitats, and increase understanding, appreciation, and horticultural use of native plants.  CNPS Program staff and committees undertake vegetation assessments, determine rare plant status, sponsor workshops on ecological and taxonomic methods and best practices, and recently held a conservation conference that had 800+ participants.
