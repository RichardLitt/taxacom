2012 
We often loudly bemoan the loss of systematics positions, so we should
show some joy that there are currently 4 jobs listed with the
Entomological Society of America for insect systematists in the USA -- 3
tenure-track university jobs at major institutions (Purdue, UCDavis and
BYU) and a museum technical position at the AMNH. Plus, there are 5 paid
internships offering training!

http://entsoc.org/listjobs

Let's see some joy!
