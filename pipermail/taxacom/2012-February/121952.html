> Taxacom subscribers will miss Don's insightful contributions and often
> acerbic responses to discussion on this List.  Apart from his extensive
> and detailed knowledge of the Australian Diptera fauna, he had a deep
> interest in systematic theory and enjoyed taking contrarian stances,
> especially to what he considered mainstream dogma.

>
>
> Donald Henry Colless
>
> 24 August 1922 to 16 February 2012
>
> Don started with CSIR0 in 1947 for a period of 3 months followed by roles
> in North Borneo and Malaya where he conducted mosquito research.  In April
> 1960 he returned to CSIRO and was promoted to chief research scientist in
> the 70's.  Don was the chief curator of the ANIC from 1971-1977 and worked
> with CSIRO until his retirement in 1987.  He returned to CSIRO soon after
> his retirement as an Honorary Fellow and remained as such for the
> remainder of his life.  Don was a noted systematist and a world authority
> on the taxonomy and biology of true flies (Diptera).
