Just to alert you that the new genus Pantherina He, 2018 (Mammalia: Soricidae; see https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2018.058 ) is a junior homonym of subgenus Pantherina Curletti, 1998 (Coleoptera: Buprestidae; see http://www.organismnames.com/details.htm?lsid=1345516).
