Lee Belbin has just finished editing two new resources for the bioinformatics community that might be of interest, aimed at putting together some resources for newcomers.
 
A Getting Started page:
http://www.tdwg.org/getting-started/ <https://mail.gaiaresources.com.au/exchweb/bin/redir.asp?URL=http://www.tdwg.org/getting-started/> 
 
A Getting Started wiki:
http://wiki.tdwg.org/twiki/bin/view/Starting/WebHome <https://mail.gaiaresources.com.au/exchweb/bin/redir.asp?URL=http://wiki.tdwg.org/twiki/bin/view/Starting/WebHome> 
 
As time goes on we hope that the wiki will start to develop some good additional resources for newcomers.  Please feel free to suggest things, document them or provide answers on the wiki.
