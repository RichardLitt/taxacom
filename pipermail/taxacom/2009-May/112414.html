Dear All,

Continuing on from yesterday's theme of RSS feeds for names, I've
created a RSS feed for Zootaxa. The feed is in the list on http://bioguid.info/rss
  , or can be accessed directly here:

http://bioguid.info/rss/zootaxa.php

The feed contains a list of the latest articles published in the
journal, links to those articles, and a list of names extracted from
the article title using uBio's findIT tool. If you use a feed reader
it should be a useful way to keep track of what Zootaxa is publishing.

For the geeks, the RSS feed is RSS 1.0, so you can get bibliographic
details and uBIO LSIDs from the feed source.
