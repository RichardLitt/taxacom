One of the issues that was raised recently, centred on the SARS virus
http://www.who.int/bulletin/volumes/83/9/707.pdf
an issue that relates to human health.

This would be also true of any biological material needed to identify  
pathogens/invasive species.

A paper has been published dealing with a limited example of trying to  
keep type material of prokaryote species "in circulation" without  
infringng the issue of "ownership". All very tricky:
http://ijs.sgmjournals.org/cgi/content/abstract/58/8/1987
