2009 
Cancelation of the International Congress of Systematic and Evolutionary
Biology (ICSEB7)



5-10 July 2009, Veracruz, Mexico



Due to the low levels of registrations and hotel bookings, plus withdrawal
of several of the major speakers, it is necessary to cancel ICSEB7.



We greatly regret this decision, but there is no realistic alternative.



Any person having paid registration fees will receive a full refund, and
this will be automatically initiated.  For any questions, please contact
icseb.evol at univie.ac.at.



Best wishes,
the organizers
