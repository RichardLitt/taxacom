In a way, it is a simple question, for which there is a simple answer.
Scientists use deduction, induction, abduction and reasoning by analogy,
if they are organized in thinking. The point of doing taxonomy is to
understand variation globally for a group of species such that when a
possibly new species in the group is detected, reasoning by analogy can
provide a guide as to whether it should be described or not. 

Knowledge of the philosophic basis behind scientific reasoning allows
one to make mistakes with confidence and clarity.
