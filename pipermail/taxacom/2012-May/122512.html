Policy, as set at the moment, is that a decision is made when
60% of the Committee votes in favour, or 60% votes against.
If it has not appeared in a Report, there is a good chance no
decision has been made yet, either way. Discussion in the
Committee is (supposed to be) kept within the Committee,
so until a Report is published information is scarce. There is
no time limit, although if it appears the Committee is definitely
unable to reach a decision it can refer the matter back to the
General Committee.
