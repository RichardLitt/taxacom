Yes, the "Proposals and Disposals" site at the Smithsonian
can be very useful, but the Malus domestica proposal is not
listed, nor are the actions taken on the Malus toringoides 
proposal, although this has been reported on by the special 
Committee and the General Committee, and has been accepted 
by the Melbourne Congress. The Malus domestica proposal 
was published in April 2010, so it looks as if the site lags more 
than two years behind? Perhaps an additional person should 
be assigned to keep it up to date?

Publishing the deliberations of a Committee looks to be a very
attractive idea, but perhaps for some groups of taxa the outside
pressure is felt to be too severe for such openness to be risked?

Paul
