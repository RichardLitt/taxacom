A "data paper" describing a dynamic and continuously updated database of
vascular plants (Florabank1) has been published by PhytoKeys:
http://www.pensoft.net/journals/phytokeys/article/2849/florabank1-a-grid-based-database-on-vascular-plant-distribution-in-the-northern-part-of-belgium-flanders-and-the-brussel.
The manuscript has been generated from metadata uploaded through
GBIF's Integrated
Publishers Toolkit (IPT) <http://ipt.inbo.be/resource.do?r=florabank1>,
from where a Darwin Core Archive of the data ( (87 MB; 3,380,175 records)
can be downloaded as well.
