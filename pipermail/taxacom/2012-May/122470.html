Do taxacomers approve of paper titles which preface the nitty-gritty of the descriptive part with an adaption of a cliche phrase such as those below?

At the bottom of the deep blue sea: a new wood-boring bivalve (Mollusca, Pholadidae, Xylophaga) from the Cape Verde Abyssal Plain (subtropical
Atlantic)

Between a whale bone and the deep blue sea: the provenance of dwarf males in whale bone&#8208;eating tubeworms

These two aren't bad actually, having some wit & relevance, but is this 'lightening up' with embellishments a phenomenon to be encouraged? It seems to be getting more common. I suppose it can make a paper more memorable if original.

Here's another ocean-related one.

Descending into the abyss: Bathymetric patterns of diversity in decapod crustaceans shift with taxonomic level and life strategies.
