Dear colleagues,

at my position at the university of Oldenburg, I inherited a large collection of slides from histological and anatomical studies from my predecessor, Wolfgang Eber. Since we have no use for them anymore, I offer them for free (i.e. against shipping costs if more than 5 Euro). A list of slides in an excel sheet is available from http://www.plant-evol.uni-oldenburg.de/en/42638.html

The slides are practically all from undocumented plants and therefore are most likely relevant for teaching purposes or exploratory studies. In many cases paraffin-embedded blocks are also available. If you are interested, please contact silvia.kempen at uni-oldenburg.de
