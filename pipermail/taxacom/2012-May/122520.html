Paul & al.:

The Nomenclature Section of the International Botanical Congress in Melbourne in July 2011 accepted Art. 53 Prop. G to establish an Appendix to list binding decisions regarding confusability of names and also a new resolution to establish an Appendix to list binding decisions made on valid publication of names (see http://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/iapt/tax/2011/00000060/00000001/art00033 and http://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/iapt/tax/2011/00000060/00000005/art00034 ).

These new Appendices will appear in the Appendices section of the forthcoming Melbourne Code which will be online and so will provide the needed databases to which you refer.

John

Yes, the "Proposals and Disposals" site at the Smithsonian can   be very useful, but the Malus domestica proposal is not listed, nor are the   actions taken on the Malus toringoides proposal, although this has been   reported on by the special Committee and the General Committee, and has been   accepted by the Melbourne Congress.
