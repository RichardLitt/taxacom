A position of *Adjoint scientifique* (?Scientific assistant?) is available
in the Dpt of Invertebrates of the Museum of Natural History of Geneva
(Switzerland). The full announcement is available at :

http://www.ville-geneve.ch/administration-municipale/offres-emploi/detail/offre/418/


and a short English translation is provided below. Such positions are at
the intermediate level (a MSc but no PhD is required) and imply both
support and scientific activities.

Mission and Responsibilities: Collaborate to the administrative and
scientific administration of the department. Develop scientific research.
Take part to the development of temporary and permanent exhibitions as well
as to pedagogic activities.

Requirements: MSc or equivalent title. Experience and excellent knowledge
in the systematics of a non-arthropod invertebrate group, with a preference
for Mollusca, Porifera or Platyhelminthes (although any other group will
also be considered). Experience in collection management. Interest for
scientific popularisation. French and English (other languages an asset).
Interest for fieldwork.

Please note that, if selected, you will need to live in or around Geneva.
Application deadline is *June 19*.

If interested, do not hesitate to contact me for further information.
