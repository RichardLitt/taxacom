An excerpt from the position description:

"the postholder will be responsible for leading the development, promotion
and day to day management of the Dictionary of UK Species database and
associated website. Tasks will include ensuring that taxonomic content of
the Dictionary is kept up to date by soliciting new checklists for species
groups that are not yet included, updating existing lists to reflect current
taxonomic understanding, and maintaining a hierarchical master
classification for all UK taxonomic groups."
