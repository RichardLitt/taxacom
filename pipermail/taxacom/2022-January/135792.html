Indefatigable Taxacom Subscribers --

Best wishes for 2022!

On Monday, 10 Jan around 4-5 PM U.S. EST, GMT -6,  the Taxacom list will hop over to a new mailman list server at: Taxacom at lists.ku.edu. In case anyone needs to update the safe senders list for their spam filters or their e-mail software's autofill address list, Taxacom e-mail will be sent from that address.

Most people will probably need to do nothing beyond noting the change in address for any new postings or replies you may care to send. We are also going to forward incoming e-mail to what will then be the old server address to the new one, after the changeover.

And we will migrate subscriber names, e-mail addresses, mail preference settings, and the Taxacom archives back to 1992.

Should be painless.  Let's Go!
