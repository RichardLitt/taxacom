Hi Henrik,

Is the data for this study available? The data published with the paper is not “data" but instead a PDF of three additioonal charts.

Anyone wanting to reproduce this study - or build upon it - can’t at the moment because the data (sequence accession number and publication identifier) isn’t included in the paper.

Do you plan to make that data available? I’m only toom aware of how much effort is involved in building such datasets. If you and your coauthors were to upload the data on, say, Zenodo, you would create a valuable (and citable resource).
