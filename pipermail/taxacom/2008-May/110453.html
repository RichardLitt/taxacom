Hi Taxonomists at large, 
         Are there any aphid taxonomists out there??  Particularly any who would be interested in seeing some aphids from a little studied part of Greece.  I have been sending the Homoptera from my biodiversity study of Wetland Kerkini (an unfunded project at the moment) the Sakis Drosopoulos in Athens, he has had two masters student do batches of the Auchenorrhyncha, but the aphids are languishing and feeling unloved, so any leads to an interested party would be gratefully recieved.
   
  Other groups in need of an expert include Ichnuemonidae, Phoridae, Chironomidae (some work has/is being done but I have 100 or more tubes waiting) and Diplopoda
   
  For information about the survey, and to see who is already involved visit www.lake-kerkini.earthlife.net/project.html
