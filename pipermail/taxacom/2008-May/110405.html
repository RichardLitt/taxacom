Dear Taxacomers and subscribers to Herbaria List Serve (apologies for 
duplications),

The Ebbe Nielsen Prize will be awarded for the seventh time in 2008 to a 
person or small team that is demonstrating excellence in combining 
biodiversity informatics and biosystematic research.

The Science Committee of the GBIF Governing Board seeks nominations for 
the Prize, which are due at the Secretariat by 1 July 2008.

For more information, please see   
http://www.gbif.org/Stories/STORY1210001689/

Know someone who should be nominated? Please refer that information to 
the Head of a GBIF Delegation.

To find GBIF Heads of Delegation, go to 
http://www.gbif.org/GBIF_org/whoiswho/gb/, and click on "Voting 
Participants", "Associate Country Participants", or "Other Associate 
Participants".  Within these categories, the Participants are listed, 
with the membership of their delegations.  Clicking on a person's name 
will take you to their contact details.

Best regards,
Meredith Lane
GBIF Public & Scientific Liaison
