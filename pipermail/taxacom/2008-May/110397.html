Well, genetic clusters indicate isolation, but not necessarily different species. The fact of exemplars of the same taxon being distant on a molecular cladogram (e.g. "massive homoplasy") is evidence of stasis in evolution, maybe through stabilizing selection. A molecular split necessarily representing one or more speciation events is more a slogan than theory based on evidence.
