Resumen:
The Cerambycidae, commonly known as longhorned beetles, logicorns,  
capricorns, round-headed borers, timber beetles, goat beetles, or  
sawyer beetles, are one of the largest and most varied families in  
the Coleoptera, with size alone varying from 2 mm (Cyrtinus sp.) to  
180 mm (Titanus sp.). With world-wide distribution whereve woody  
plants are found, these wood-borers have long been a favorite with  
collectors, and with their great variety of colors and forms, they  
are ofter thought of as one of the most esthetically pleasing  
families of beetles. The present work includes approximately 8,700  
described species and subspecies representing close to 1,500 genera,  
found from Canada and Alaska to Argentina and Chile, and throughout  
the West Indies (including several species from Costa Rica).  
Additional taxa are being described each year by workers in several  
countries
