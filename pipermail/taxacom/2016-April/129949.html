Hi,

We are hiring for a plant systematist and research group leader (all plant taxonomy).
For more details and to apply visit this link:
https://careers.sciencenewzealand.org/jobdetails/ajid/XbnQ7/16728-Research-Leader-Plant-Systematics,16728.html

Official blurb:

Our Systematics team is seeking a senior Plant Systematist to lead plant systematics in Landcare Research and to undertake pure and applied systematics research into New Zealand's vascular flora. The successful applicant will join a team of 15 botanists and botanical technicians working at New Zealand's largest herbarium, Allan Herbarium CHR, and in association with the living ethnobotanical collections at Lincoln. Work is office, laboratory, collections, glasshouse and field-based. Future expanded research leadership opportunities, encompassing a systematics focus broader than plants, may also develop.

This is an open term (full time) position, and is based at Lincoln, Canterbury.
