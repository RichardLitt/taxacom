Linking developmental process and phylogenetic pattern:
evolution of leaf morphology in the Leguminosae
A University of Reading Research Studentship

Start date: 1 October 1999

Supervisors:
Dr Julie Hawkins - systematics
Dr Nick Battey - developmental genetics

Applications are invited from graduates or finalists who have or expect
to obtain a first class or good upper second class honours degree in a
relevant subject. The preferred candidate will have an interest in
systematics or evolutionary biology. Some experience of molecular
techniques would be an advantage.
