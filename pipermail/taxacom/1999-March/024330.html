1999 
        The collections of the Zoological Museum, University of Turku, Finland
cannot be used 1.4.-31.8.1999, because they have been packed and
transferred to another building for a complete renovation of the museum
building. The arrangement of the collections after move back to the museum
will take some time. Therefore visits to museum and loans in
September-November 1999 may have some problems and delay. Normal scientific
cooperation of the staff and return of loans from other museums are not
affected by the renovation, except that all members of the staff must have
the holidays almost simultaneously.

        Pekka T. Lehtinen, curator until 30.4.1999 (retirement)
        Michael Saaristo, temporary curator from 1.5.1999
