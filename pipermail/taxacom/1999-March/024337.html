1999 
New information is available at Field Museum's
Mollusk Collection web site:

<http://www.fmnh.org/collections/invertebrates/>

In the ongoing project of "databasing" our entire land snail collection,
you now have access to

(1) the Hubricht Collection of Eastern North American land snails

(2) the following families from Field Museum's main collection:

    Bradybaenidae
    Helicidae
    Helminthoglyptidae
    Onchidiidae
    Rathouisiidae
    Veronicellidae

Other families (as well as auxiliary information about our collections)
will be added as our data entry project progresses.
