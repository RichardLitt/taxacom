challenge of sorts--couldn't someone come up with some spiffy poetry
in Latin?

_Quomodo Invidiosulus Nomine Grinchus Christi Natalem
Abrogaverit_

Which is now available from the Common Reader.  See:
http://www.commonreader.com/1/1291.html

Apologies to those outside the U.S. who might not appreciate the
sublime whimsy of this book.
