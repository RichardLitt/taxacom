1999 
Post doctoral fellow:  Postdoctoral fellow needed to work on an NSF-funded
project "Phylogeny and development of the panicoid grasses."  The
successful applicant will have skill and interests in development
morphology, and will continue ongoing projects on inflorescence and floral
development in the panicoid grasses.  Experience with SEM, embedding,
sectioning, and light microscopy required.  Familiarity with molecular
biology and/or molecular systematics preferred.  Please send letter, CV and
names and phone numbers of 3 referees to: Elizabeth A. Kellogg, Department
of Biology, University of Missouri-St. Louis, 8001 Natural Bridge Road, St.
Louis, MO 63121, or via email to: tkellogg at umsl.edu.  Position is available
immediately. Deadline for receipt of applications March 25. The University
of Missouri-St. Louis is an affirmative action/equal opportunity employer
that is committed to excellence through diversity.
