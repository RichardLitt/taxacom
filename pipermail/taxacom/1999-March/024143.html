Dear all,

For illustrating information material about the Threatened Plants
Appeal, which has been launched to help conserving some of the
world's most threatened plants, we - The Friends of the Royal
Botanic Gardens, Kew - urgently seek for images of the following
species:

- Lysimachia minoricensis (endemic to the Balearic island of
  Minorca, extinct in the wild)

- Hibiscus liliiflorus (Rodrigues, Mascarenes)

- Bromus interruptus (British native grass, extinct in the wild)

- Orthodontium gracile ('slender thread moss', threatened
  throughout Europe)

Any help would be greatly appreciated.

Thanks to everybody!

Wolfgang
