1999 
The American Society of Plant Taxonomists website has moved to:

http://www.sysbot.org

The site includes current information on the ASPT, its publications
(Systematic Botany and Systematic Botany Monographs), and programs.
A full text index of over 1300 ASPT members can be queried by
location and research interest (see 'Membership') and a similar
index is available for titles and abstracts (partial) of papers
published in Systematic Botany and Brittonia (pre-1976 - see
'Systematic Botany', 'Search....').  The site also provides access
to the current ASPT Newsletter (constantly updated) and an archive
of prior versions.

This move, with assistance and support from ASPT Corporate Member
Allen Press, will enhance our ability to develop new site features
and additional content in 1999.
