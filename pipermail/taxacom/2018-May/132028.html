digitization from back when they digitized all they could get hold of:

>From Hathi "The digital images and OCR of this work were produced by

non-commercial purposes."

I'm not sure exactly how that works with Hathi or if I'm interpreting it
correctly, but it's an extra category in their copyright restriction
