Dear all,

Apologies for cross posting. Would anyone have a pdf of the following publication from the International Organization for Biological Control of Noxious Animals and Plants. West Palaearctic Regional Section?

"Determination List of Entomophagous Insects, Issue 12" (also recorded as Volume 16, Issue 3 of IOBC/WPRS bulletin) by S. Vidal as Editor and published in 1993. I cannot find even a mention in the IOBC website (bulletins there only show until 1998, older ones are not available).

If someone has the book but not the pdf, I would appreciate a scanned copy of just one page (or so). I am trying to find the mention in that book of the parasitoid wasp Choeras dorsalis (Spinola, 1808) (Hymenoptera: Braconidae, Microgastrinae) as parasitoid of the caterpillar Ethmia terminella Fletcher, 1938 (Lepidoptera, Depressariidae). It is likely that both the name of the parasitoid wasp and the caterpillar host would be mentioned with different names at the time (even the Lepidoptera family might be different at that point).

Thanks a lot for any help you can provide!
