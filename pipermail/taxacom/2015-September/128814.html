Dear all,

The first Legume Morphology Symposium will happen in November at Brazil.

A number of international researchers will get together to share results on
morphological studies focusing on Leguminosae, which surely can be of
interest to people working on other plant groups.

See http://goo.gl/JskVfQ for the preliminary program.

Early-bird registration and abstract submission for either poster
presentation or selected talks is open until October 5th. Registration ends
by October 15th.

See the attached flyer and the website for more info!

http://legumemorphology.wix.com/symposium2015
