Hi all, 

the German Carnivorous Plants Association (GFP) announces a fellowship for a young researcher (BSc, MSc or PhD level) conducting a thesis on carnivorous plants. The fellowship covers 3000.-€ and can be used for any aspect related to the thesis of the applicant (expenses for field research, laboratory equipment and/or consumables, travel expenses etc). Salaries are excluded, however. 
Details on the call and application guidelines can be found on the GFP homepage:

https://www.carnivoren.org/naturschutz/grant-proposal/ <https://www.carnivoren.org/naturschutz/grant-proposal/>

Best, 
Thomas.
