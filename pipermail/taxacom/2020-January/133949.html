The 50.000th dataset in GBIF (https://www.gbif.org/dataset/168df1fe-de0d-4314-a991-aef829ca2865)  is a taxonomic article which has been taken apart, data such as taxonomic treatment and figures have been made open accessible and packaged in a format that GBIF can directly import, and make it accessible as part of their infrastructure and with that adding a basionym, that has so far not been available, including its type.

The story behind is here: http://plazi.org/news/beitrag/50000th-gbif-dataset-a-brief-history/2e2e10c9f5f1826c7ebd0bb60e3e8211/
