Dear taxacomers,
I have developed a shapefiles with geomorphological and phytogeographical
divisions of Ukrainian Carpathian Mts. Now I would like to publish a
datapaper together with these shapefiles, so everybody could use them for
their biogeographical studies.
This work was not financially supported, so it would be great to publish my
outcomes for free or for some small charge.
Could you recommend any good journal for my purposes?

I am considering about Oryx (https://www.oryxthejournal.org/), but not sure
whether they accept datapapers and whether they support publishing of
additional materials (i.e. shapefiles).

Thanks in advance for any suggestions!

Best regards, Andriy.
