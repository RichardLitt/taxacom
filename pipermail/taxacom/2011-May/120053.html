Of course it assumes that what we think we know about the geological history of an 'oceanic island' is true. And 'never connected to the mainland' is tricky anyway. Galapagos may never have had a connection to the American mainland at any one time, but it did sequentially through the island arc that was at one time at the Galapagos, and at another time at the American 'mainland'

The biogeography of Juan Fernandez includes biotic features that belie the simplistic notion that its biota is all 'oceanic'.

John Grehan
