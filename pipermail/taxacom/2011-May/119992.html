Something strange in this exchange. Phenomena involving multiple occurrences of multiple entities in a complex environment are all aleatory, all stochastic, all chance-ridden. The central limit theorem makes it look like straight causality, that's all. Figuring out what happened is probabilistic, and as such disquieting to those who need a simple, mechanistic answer. I don't know of a method other than intuition for this.
