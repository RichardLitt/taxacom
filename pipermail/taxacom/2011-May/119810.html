_Latest News on the
Adelaide Conference_
  * The rate of [1]submission of abstracts to the Adelaide Barcode
    Conference keeps rising so the organizers are _extending the submission
    deadline_.
  * The new deadline for submitting abstracts is _midnight_ (Eastern US
    time) on _Wednesday, 15 June 2011._
  * Be sure to suggest categories of sessions in which your abstract fits
    best:
       + Plenary sessions for abstracts of broad interest to the barcoding
         community, or
       + Taxonomic sessions if your abstract is aimed at taxonomic
         specialists of a particular group, or
       + Thematic sessions if your abstract is about the application of
         barcode data to non-taxonomic problems.
  * The deadline for [2]Applications for Travel Bursaries from developing
    countries also extended to _15 June 2011_.
