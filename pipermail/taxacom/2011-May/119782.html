Hi one and all,

Tegula is singular, female, and plural is tegulae.

Tegulum is singular, neuter, and plural is tegula.

I really do hesitate going to "tegulums" for a word 
not in standard use even among arthropodologists. 

Thanks to Robert Mills, Richard Zander and others.

Robin
