Subject: 100 new lichen species published in Phytotaxa
An open access article describing 100 new species of lichens is just published in Phytotaxa. This article shows excellent collaboration between lichen taxonomists and shows how descriptive taxonomy can make an impact. All new species are linked to EoL pages. The article can be downloaded at http://www.mapress.com/phytotaxa/content/2011/pt00018.htm
I hope this article will be well received.
