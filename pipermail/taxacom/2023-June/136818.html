    On Thursday, 22 June 2023 at 10:57:32 pm NZST, George Beccaloni <g.beccaloni at gmail.com> wrote:  
 
 Personally I love Abba, and I quite like Pink Floyd?as well! IMHO there should be little control of who and what you can name a species after. Imposed rules will often be subjective. For example, if you proposed to base the name on a local name for that organism, one problem is that species ranges will often encompass several different human cultures with different languages and names for the organism in question. Choosing one of these local names may upset the peoples using alternative names... I think there should be no restriction about naming species after humans, living or dead, but that there should be a system in place to allow renaming of species who were named after disgraced people. Problem is that this will often also be subjective - e.g. people may take offence at a species being named after an inhuman war criminal like Putin, but others love him..
George
