Abba new genusEtymology The genus-group name honours the Swedish pop group ABBA whose songs and subsequent musicals Mamma Mia! (2008) and Mamma Mia ? Here We Go again! (2018), provided hours of entertainment for the authors.

Not that Abba are offensive (except to my musical ear), but really, for a journal called Evolutionary Systematics (Pensoft!), is this really appropriate? Perhaps the spider genus Abba can compete with the spider genus Pinkfloydia for the most fans! Personally, I'd go for the Floyd!
