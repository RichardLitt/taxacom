I would like to announce my new book "Fractal Evolution, Complexity and Systematics."

The subtitle is "An edge-of-chaos study of evolutionary adaptations leading to survival of periodic environmental catastrophes using bryophytes as model organisms." It uses a novel method of analysis, and includes two new species and some new genera in mosses.

I've kept it inexpensive by self-publishing to avoid the savagely avaricious publishing houses. It has been reviewed prepublication.

It is just now available at Amazon. I hope it catalyzes some interesting new directions in research.
