A new unpaid TREEFINDER version is online  at www.treefinder.de .

New features are:

- parametric bootstrapping from a dialog
- simulation of partitioned sequences
- sequence simulation along reports
- mtArt protein model
- various other improvements

Testing of phylogenetic hypotheses and substitution models is very easy
now. One can examine the stochastic variation of all parameters under a
particular null model and compute all kinds of statistics.
