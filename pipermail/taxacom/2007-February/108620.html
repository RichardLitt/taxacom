Just a note to announce our revamped website at http://utc.usu.edu/keys/ 
<https://bioserver.biology.usu.edu/exchweb/bin/redir.asp?URL=http://utc.usu.edu/keys/>

The site now features
* a web based demonstration of a Lucid based key - just click the play 
button
* an illustrated glossary to select genera of sedges, grasses and orchids
* fact sheets to accompany all genera listed above
* many high quality images
* Google interactive mapping for distribution data

_Keys currently available_

* Carex - 500 species of North America and their true global distributions
* Platanthera
* Poaceae - with complete North American keys to:
* Panicum
* Dichanthelium
* Bromus
* Pooid Genera of North America
* Many others
