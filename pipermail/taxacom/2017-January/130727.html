Am 10.01.2017 um 14:12 schrieb Thomas Pape:
> Note also, that the Code does not regulate cases, where the date of publication is given in error.
I disagree with this statement. Art. 21.4 rules such cases. A date that 
was given incorrectly still meets the requirement of a specified date. 
The date must be corrected by the community (for example in ZooBank 
entries), but the availability should remain unaffected.
