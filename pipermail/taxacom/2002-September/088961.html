I may have a very personal view of systematics, but I usually see
what I call the four the corner-stones of systematics (taxonomy - the
recognition and formal description of taxa, nomenclature - the formal
naming of taxa, phylogeny - the analysis of taxon relationships, and
classification - the formalized hierarchical arrangement of taxa into
groups) as very separate units which together forms the 'whole' - the
science of systematics.

I think that is it very important to make a clear distinction between
taxonomy, nomenclature and classification as these concepts are mixed
up all the time.
