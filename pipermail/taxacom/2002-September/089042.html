Are we moving back in time?  Encouraging private collections undermines everything we have tried to accomplish in terms of specimen preservation for teaching, research, and future endeavors!  Museums are the recognized format for specimen preservation with the experience, access to object conservation research, an environmental controls that cannot be achieved in private collections.  Why would anyone encourage private collections!
