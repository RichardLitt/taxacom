The National Institute of Water and Atmospheric Research is seeking to
appoint a Collections Manager to run the marine invertebrate collections held
at NIWA, Wellington, New Zealand.

Details are available on the NIWA website at:
http://www.niwa.co.nz/about/jobs/collection

Requests for position description, selection criteria and further information or
applications, should be addressed to: Margaret Devlin, National Institute of
Water and Atmospheric Research, P.O. Box 14-901, Kilbirnie, Wellington,
New Zealand. Phone +64 4 386 0300 Fax +64 4 386 0574
<m.devlin at niwa.co.nz>

Applications close Friday, 18 October, 2002

The appointee would be working in the Marine Biodiversity and Systematics
program. General information on this program can be found at:
http://www.niwa.co.nz/rc/prog/marinebiodiversity/
