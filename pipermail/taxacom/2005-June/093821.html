Let me respond to Doug's challenge to be "up-front" about our coverage. At the end of this message are the latest statistics on coverage in our database. The primary objective of this work is to pull together all the names of Hymenoptera that have been used in the literature and, as we go, to connect those names to citations in the literature, and to then link those citations to electronic versions of the literature (either as pdfs or, in the future, to xml texts). Along the way, we've tried to share these data with other community efforts like ITIS and uBio. This is clearly a work in progress, and always will be.

We've benefitted from the contributions by others in this process: extensive additions have been made by Jim Carpenter, Donat Agosti, and Alexandre P. Aguiar, and several people have contacted me with individual additions and corrections. I'd like to take this opportunity to encourage others to participate and help to make this as valuable a tool as possible: just drop me an e-mail! I view this as an effort done for and by the our community. An expert will clearly recognize the holes in the HNS coverage from the table below. I don't see any way of estimating what percentage of the names in the literature are represented here.
