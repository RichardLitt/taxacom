2005 
6th International Conference of Hymenopterists

Sun City, South Africa

22 * 27 January 2006

The International Society of Hymenopterists invites all interested to participate in the above scientific conference which will be held at Sun City, Northwest Province, South Africa. This will be a forum for the dynamic exchange of information and ideas relevant to those interested in Hymenoptera. Your input is required to make this a success, and suggestions for symposia, special sessions and/or keynote speakers will be particularly welcomed. 

The First (Final) Circular, Registration Form, etc. is available from Denis Brothers at brothers at ukzn.ac.za.

Venue
The venue is part of a tourist complex renowned for its sporting and other attractions, including the adjacent Pilanesberg National Park (where big game are on view). It is ideal for a family holiday and is in a malaria-free area. Additional information is available at http://www.suninternational.com/Resorts/SunCity/. Accommodation will be at the Cabanas complex in Sun City.
