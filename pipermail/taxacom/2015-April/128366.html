Only once actually Mike, as I recall! How many times has Scott Thomson been heavily involved in dispute with others? Perhaps you don't know this, Mike, but there are people out there who take pleasure from obstructing and devaluing the well meaning contributions of others, particularly if these contributions are on a larger scale than they are capable of. Perhaps you are one of these people?? At any rate, I was only "reporting" the facts that there has been a big upheaval at Wikispecies, and that, in my opinion as someone who knows a thing or two about nomenclature and taxonomy, the result is a slide downhill in reliability of information. That's all. I'm not asking for your help, Mike. You are pretty much the last person I would come to for that. From a personal point of view, my block from WS is actually a good thing. I was wasting too much time there. My time can be much better spent building up other sites -sites free of Wikimedia politics.
 Neverthless, I thought that recent events at WS were worth at least a few lines on Taxacom. It is at least informative to see who replies and what line they take! I have now crossed you off my "Xmas card list". have a nice day...
