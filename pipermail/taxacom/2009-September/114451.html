>People interpret the same paper differently. They will read the same paper and assign different names to the same species. The repeatability of this system is low.

Who are "people"? People at large, or competent taxonomists? It is not clear to me that the repeatability of taxonomy is low, at least not in the sense that you seem to be suggesting! It is just that people at large with limited taxonomic experience will tend to misinterpret and get things wrong. But how does pulling the information out of the paper and in to a database help????
