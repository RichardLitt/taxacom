2009 
TDWG 2009 (Montpellier, France, November 9-13)

Please note that Early Registration closes September 14 and abstract submission closes October 1. If you haven't already done so, please register for TDWG 2009 at https://www.regonline.com/TDWG2009.

The conference web site (http://www.tdwg.org/conference2009/) contains details about the meeting, travel, accommodation, Montpellier information including restaurants, history, excursions and our sponsors.

NESCent Phyloinformatics VoCamp: Those interested in phyloinformatics data standards will be interested to note that, sponsored by NESCent, there will be a VoCamp held in association with, and partial incorporated into, the TDWG conference. The application deadline is September 18, 2009. Please see here for details: http://docs.google.com/View?id=dhdjhbvd_66gr5cdqfq

Abstracts are required for all oral presentations, posters and computer demonstrations. Although verbal presentations are limited please note that we will also accept 'Contributed Abstracts' as we did for the first time for TDWG 2008, allowing your work to be published in the Proceedings of TDWG.

Regards,
