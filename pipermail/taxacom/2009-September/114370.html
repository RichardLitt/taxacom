Whether anyone wants to front up with the evidence or not is up to them. This is a very simple question and there are people on this list who are convinced that the chimpanzee theory of relationship is correct so it should be no hardship to cite the set of uniquely shared characters for humans, fossil hominids, and chimpanzees. But I'm betting that no one will be able to.
