If the volume of Taxacom mail or the list's signal to noise ratio is
bothering you,  consider setting the DIGEST option for receiving Taxacom
mail in a single daily increment.

 

Digests reduce distraction and group multiple Taxacom postings into one
email (typically 10-20 inclusions per day).  Today we changed the DIGEST
parameters for the Taxacom list, so that DIGEST subscribers should
receive only 1 email per day from the list - unless someone brings up
morphological versus genetic data again!  (Jim Croft bite your lip.)

 

To change your Taxacom subscription to DIGEST mode, go to:

 

http://mailman.nhm.ku.edu/mailman/listinfo/taxacom

 

at the bottom of the page enter your email address and click on the
'Unsubscribe or edit options' button.

 

On the next page about 1 screen down, in the gray box area, set the
option 'Set Digest Mode' to 'On' by clicking on the radio button to the
right.

 

Then, at the bottom of the page, click on "Submit My Changes".
