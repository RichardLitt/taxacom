I can add some references to the g1 paper Una cited:

Hillis, D.M. 1991. Discriminating between phylogenetic signal and random
noise in DNA sequences. in M.M. Miyamoto and J. Cracraft, eds.
Phylogenetic analysis of DNA sequences. Oxford University Press, New York.

Hillis, D.M., and J.P. Huelsenbeck. 1992. Signal, noise, and reliability
in molecular phylogenetic analyses. Journal of Heredity 83:189-195.

Huelsenbeck, J.P. 1991. Tree length distribution skewness: an indicator of
phylogenetic information. Journal of Heredity 88:189-195.

Kallerjo, M., J.S. Farris, A.G. Kluge, and C. Bult. 1992. Skewness and
permutation. Cladistics 8:275-287.

A rather complete MS word document containing references to all manner of
methods of phylogenetic inference can be found in the download folder in
RASA 2.1 (Mac only to date).  See URL below.
