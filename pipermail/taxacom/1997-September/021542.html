Friends:

    Many thanks to my buddies who have properly pointed out to me that
this plant whose label is trying to say "Helocharis nodulosus
Schultes" ought to be considered Eleocharis, sure enough. The silent
"H" strikes yet again.

    This plant is apparently a member of the SEUS Flora, as
Eleocharis montana (Kunth) Roemer & J. A. Schultes.
