Hello all.  I am seeking to communicate with those
who have interests in resting forms of temporary
pool invertebrates, especially those that would be
retained in sieved soil samples between 0.710 mm
and 0.100 mm.  I am learning to identify these
organisms and am looking to converse with others
of similar interests.  My work involves sorting
and culturing.  I am especially interested in
learning more about rotifer, flatworm and
tardigrade resting forms.  My limited "expertise"
is with large branchiopods.
