Sorry I came into this a little late but I see no one has addressed the
question of format of web citations. There is a style for electronic
citations.

For web pages, it usually follows the format of:

 [author] [year] [document title] [date of last revision of document]
[full url] [date accessed]

For more details on web citations and other forms of electronic citation
(ftp, etc.), see:

http://www.cas.usf.edu/english/walker/mla.html
