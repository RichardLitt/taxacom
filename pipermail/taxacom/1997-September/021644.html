Research Bryologist
We are seeking to appoint a Bryologist to the Cryptogamic Plant Reserach
Division of the Department of Botany at the Natural History Museum, London.
Applicants should have a good research degree and publication record, and
have the ability to plan, organise and attract external funding to support a
research programme on Bryophytes.
The appointment will be initially for a period of three years with the
possibility of extension to five years and eventual permanency. Starting
salary will be in the range 16,895 -27,321 pounds sterling, depending on
qualifications and relevant experience.
Application is by CV and covering letter together with the names and
addresses of two referees. For further details, please write enclosing an A5
SAE to
Gilda Paul, Personnel Section, The Natural History Museum, Cromwell Road,
London SW7 5BD, UK. Closing date for receipt of applications is 24 October 1997.
For informal enquiries, please contact Dr Mary Gibby at the address below.
