Dear colleagues:

Version 1.0 of NCU-3e (Names in current use for extant plant
genera, electronic version) is now available on the IAPT site under:

http://www.bgbm.fu-berlin.de/iapt/ncu/genera/Default.htm

We hope that we have succeeded in eliminating most of the errors
introduced in the conversion process from text file to database. In
addition, corrections with respect to standardization of author
names, book and periodical titles have been made.
