>Hello Taxacomers:

>We are curious about the correct name of Rapanea laetevirens Mez. what we
>consider is the rigth one, but in recent papers we have found that Myrsine
>laetevirens (Mez.) Arechav is also in use.

Rapanea is a segregate of Myrsine characterized by the staminal tube adnate
(vs. partly distinct) to corolla  and lack (vs. presence) of a style.  Some
authors consider that sufficient for a genus, others don't.
