This is something of a non sequitur.  The hypotheses you refer to are
estimates of the true tree.  We hope that our best hypotheses reflect
biological reality; that's a given.  Whenever one says something like "we
don't know if we have the true tree", I take it to mean "in the best
hypothesis".
