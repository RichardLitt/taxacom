The Entomological Society of America's newest journal, Insect Systematics and Diversity, is highlighting a group of recently published taxonomic papers in a special collection.

This collection brings together works on topics ranging from the taxonomy of the Allodapine bees to a discussion of whether new species can be described from photographs.

All papers are free to view for a limited time.

Read the ISD taxonomy collection at https://academic.oup.com/isd/pages/taxonomy_collection https://academic.oup.com/isd/pages/taxonomy_collection
