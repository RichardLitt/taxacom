Making your server claim to be in the US might help with publications from
the early 1900's, but for much of Haithi you need to be on the campus of an
institution with a subscription - remote access isn't an option that I can
find.  Entering search terms and seeing if Haithi thinks they are found in
the text isn't very useful, given the unreliability of OCR - a display of
tables of contents would be much more helpful in determining if a reference
might be worth pursuing with interlibrary loan or similar programs.

> Dear XXXX,
> I am forwarding this message to you from the BMS World Mission about the
> year of death of John A. Ewing, author of "Lanka: The Resplendent Isle: The

> Society, London). As you can see, he died in 1951, i.e. 61 years ago. I

> photographs in that book to be still alive. For example, we know that some

> (Old Testament) longevity!
