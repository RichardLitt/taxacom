Dear Priyantha and all,

the librarian you contacted at Hathi Trust was correct regarding the 
copyright regulations. Most countries in the world including the US and 
EU protect works until 70 years after death of the author(s) and some 
even longer (e.g. Mexico with 100 years). However, in some countries the 
time period is a little shorter (e.g. 60 years in India and 50 years in 
Sri Lanka or Canada), see 
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries%27_copyright_lengths.

Best wishes,

Martin
