On 2/28/19 2:14 PM, Robert Zuparko wrote:

2) Which species has the most junior synonyms?



There is a complicating factor here, in that some taxonomists don't consider subspecies to be valid taxa, in which case it could easily be a carabid beetle (e.g., a Carabus or Cicindela s.l.) or butterfly that has more synonyms, though disputedly so. Apis mellifera has 29 valid subspecies and another 72 synonyms, homonyms, and misspellings in addition to those 29 valid names (but no more than 13 for any individual subspecies), but I don't think that's fair for your question. If you avoid things that have this level of contentiousness, and eliminate unavailable names from consideration, then the two I know are good contenders are the cerambycid Prionus californicus, which has 29 available and undisputed junior synonyms, and the scarab Cotinis mutabilis, which has 28. Again, the key here is "available and undisputed".

There may be taxa with 30 or more, but not familiar to me, at least.
