My recommendation: cite the original spelling of the name as hönei, and 
the correct spelling that must be used for a currently valid species 
name as hoenei.
Höne is very probably of German origin in the sense of the Code. 
Theoretically this could also be a Hungarian or Turkish name, but this 
one should be German. Höne in this spelling is not a very frequent 
surname, Höhne is more frequent.
Hermann Höne (1883–1963) was a German lepidopterist.

Best wishes
Francisco

>     before 1985 and based upon a German word,
>     the umlaut sign is deleted from a vowel and
>     the letter "e" is to be inserted after that vowel
>    (if there is any doubt that the name is based
>     upon a German word, it is to be so treated)."
