The Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT) is moving to new
facilities on 1 June 2008. This will cause disruption to visitor
services and access to the herbarium (BRIT-SMU-VDB collections) and
library, so please contact us before arranging any visits between 15
April and 31 July 2008.  We ask that loan requests, or shipment of any
transactions to BRIT be delayed until August 2008.

Our new address starting JUNE 1st will be:

Botanical Research Institute of Texas

500 E 4th St.

Fort Worth, TX 76102-4025   USA

 

BRIT e-mail addresses and phone/fax numbers will not change.

 

Thank you for your patience during this time.
