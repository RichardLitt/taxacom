Dear all,

EDIT-DEST is offering, within the framework of the Modern Taxonomy 
course programme, a course on "Biological Nomenclature", November 2010, 
Muséum national d'Histoire naturelle - Paris, training provider: Prof. 
Dr Alain Dubois.

The course is ideal for early career or future taxonomists who want to 
learn more about international codes of nomenclature and relationships 
between phylogeny, taxonomy and nomenclature.

Deadline for registration is approaching: 8 September 2010.

Detailed information and registration at:
http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/modern-taxonomy-course-programme-2010-2011

With best regards,
