The Encyclopedia of Life (EOL) development team is ready to open up testing of the new EOL API to a community of testers in advance of its general release.

The testing period will run from August 12 to September 2.  If you would like to participate in this beta program, please review the terms of the beta test at http://www.eol.org/content/page/beta_api.  From this page, you will be able to access API documentation, review test plans and provide feedback.

If you have questions about the beta test, or would like to open a direct line of communication to the EOL development team, please contact us at api at eol.org.

Thank you in advance for your contributions.  We are looking forward to working with you!
