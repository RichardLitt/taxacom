Dear all,

EDIT-DEST is offering, within the framework of the Modern Taxonomy 
programme, a course on "Basics of Taxonomy: describing, illustrating and 
writing biodiversity!"
Date: 8 - 19 November 2010.  Location: Sven Lovén Centre for Marine 
Sciences, Kristineberg, Sweden

The course is ideal for early career or future taxonomists who want to 
learn more about DELTA, digital drawing, scientific illustration, 
writing and communication.

Deadline for registration is approaching: 1 September 2010.

Detailed information and registration at:
http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/modern-taxonomy-course-programme-2010-2011
