I think we are too focused on different trees in the forest. If we base classification on evolution, and a decent apprehension of evolution requires many different techniques and angles of investigation, then it isn't morphology versus DNA. Morphology alone isn't enough to fully characterize a taxon in an evolutionary context. All sorts of biosystematic and genetic data help characterize a taxon.

Of course, morphology alone is usually useful for identification, and DNA isn't.
