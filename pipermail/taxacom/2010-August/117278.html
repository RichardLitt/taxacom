Yes, I see, lately I have not been following Eucalypts, but at the time
I looked at all the wood anatomical papers by Dadswell & al. on the topic, 
which were exemplary studies. However, these happened a long time ago, 
and relatively little has been published on their wood anatomy since. 
As Dadswell & al. already described the dichotomy, I was happy enough 
with the splitting-off of Corymbia, which otherwise appeared to be 
making sense as well, and reduced the uncomfortably large genus. 
However, I understand that the decision to split Eucalyptus proved to be
'less than universally popular', which in retrospect makes the 
Corymbia-paper a rather courageous act.
