Robin,

The way your pseudoscorpions were found is unusual (light trap with moths), so you might have some interesting species. The very small species is also and adult, it belongs to Cheiridiidae, probably Apocheiridium (the number of hairs on the movable chela finger should be counted).

I send you a picture of Apocheiridium ferum, you will notice the resemblance, but the chela of your specimen should be checked.
