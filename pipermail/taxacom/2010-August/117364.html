Hi Robin,
       As of 2005, there apparently were only four species of
pseudoscorpions (in two families) recorded from Alberta:             
 Family Cheliferidae: Chelifer candroides
 Family Chernetidae: Americhernes oblongus
                              Hesperochernes canadensis
                              Deinocheirus sp.        

       This information is from a publication by Chris Buddle of McGill
University, so he is probably your best bet for an identification, or
for directing you to someone who can do so:

chris.buddle (at) mcgill.ca
