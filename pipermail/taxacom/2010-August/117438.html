Well of course everyone would *like* that.  But no amount of wishing
is going to make it happen.  I'd like it if morphological
taxonomists would not write descriptions that bear little
resemblance to their type specimens, just like I'd like it if
molecular systematists didn't misidentify their specimens.  But both
things happen, and will continue to happen.
