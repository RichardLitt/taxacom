Dear all

What about species descriptions based on behaviour? I can imagine the
description of a species that is based on its separation from others
by behaviours- e.g. acoustic signals. In such cases the specimen
recorded may be indistinquishable morphologically from others but is
nevertheless be considered a new and distinct species.

Mike Wilson
