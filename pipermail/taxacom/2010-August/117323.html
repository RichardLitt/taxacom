Taxacomers,

A pest control guy caught a spider on a "glue board".

It is Catchmaster, Model # 72TC.  It says, "non-poisonous Mouse 
and Insect Glue Board.

I phoned the pest control guy to ask what solvent to use so that I 
can remove the spider from the glue and then examine it to determine 
the species (it was belly-side down).

He recommended "PAM".  Now Pam softens the glue, but there are 
still great gobs of the glue on the spider.

Anyone out there know a better solvent?  Surely this problem has 
arisen before?
