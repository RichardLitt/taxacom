Terrific! One always learns something from TAXACOM . Thanks, Peter - and even more thanks to Gandhi for setting it up and for updating it. 

Mary: if you query Nees von Esenbeck, you get to http://asaweb.huh.harvard.edu:8080/databases/botanists?id=100225
Which has lots of info about collections he used. As I said to you previously, it would be worth looking at CGE, LD and HBG for your grass queries.


TL-2 is also available electronically. I wonder whether Barnhart is in the queue for BHL to digitise?
