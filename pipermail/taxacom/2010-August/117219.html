"potential new species" is a bit of a vague term, but if I understand it 
correctly, then there is no reason to name such things! The "big gap" is in the 
argument below between "potential new species" and "this undescribed diversity" 
- actually it is only a POTENTIAL undescribed diversity! An interim naming 
system (e.g. candidate species) isn't going to help taxonomists one bit to keep 
up with the relentless stream of "potential new species" "discovered" by the 
molecular systematics - how is that supposed to work? The mole. systs. (hey, 
very apt abbrev.!) should not name anything on the basis of just supposed DNA 
differences, and if they do, then they should be prepared for most balanced 
taxonomists to treat their names as synonyms ... Everything is a "potential new 
species" until a balanced taxonomist has examined it ...
