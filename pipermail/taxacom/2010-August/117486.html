Software Developer - Tulane University Museum of Natural History

 

The Tulane University Museum of History (TUMNH) invites applications for a
software developer primarily focused on integrating geospatial analysis into
an existing natural history collection database network architecture
(Fishnet2, http://www.fishnet2.net).   Minimum required qualifications
include B.S. or M.S. in computer science, geographic information systems
and/or biology.  The successful candidate should have working knowledge of
all of these disciplines. Experience within the field of biodiversity
informatics preferred.  Desired software experience includes C#, ASP.net,
Javascript, MS SQL Server, Geographic Information Systems, Spatial Query and
Analysis, GeoServer, OpenLayers.   The position is located at TUMNH in Belle
Chasse, LA (http://www.museum.tulane.edu). Salary $50,000-$60,000 (depending
on experience) plus excellent benefits.  Send resume/professional vitae
including education, all relevant professional experience, and names and
contact information for three references to Nelson Rios
(nelson at museum.tulane.edu).  Review of applications will begin Oct 1st and
will continue until the position is filled.  Tulane University is an
Affirmative Action/Equal Employment Opportunity/ADA Employer. Women and
minorities are encouraged to apply.
