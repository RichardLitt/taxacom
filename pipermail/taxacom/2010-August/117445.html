> The problem is that you never know if a source is reliable until you
> actually check out the raw data (type specimens, sequence
> chromatograms, etc.) for yourself.  As an example - the last
> monograph on one of the groups I'm (trying to) work on was done by
> someone with a good reputation who has published many good papers
> over many years.  The key uses a lot of vague terms (e.g. "larger"
> vs. "smaller"), as was common at the time, so I looked at the types
> to figure out what he meant.  Turns out the terms aren't just vague,
> but have essentially no resemblance to the actual specimens.  Which
> means pretty much throwing out ~200 descriptions and starting from
> scratch.  Fun.
