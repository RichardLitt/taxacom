Robin,

I use product available on the shelves of the cleaning supplies of most 
hardware stores called "Goo Gone."  It comes in a spray bottle or just a 
plastic squeeze bottle.  I  prefer a squeeze bottle for removing 
specimens from a glue board.  Placing a few drops on your spider and 
around the perimeter will loosen the specimen.  Once removed, you can 
then float the specimen in a small dish to remove the rest of the glue.  
Goo Gone contains petroleum distillates - has a citrus oder.

Tim
