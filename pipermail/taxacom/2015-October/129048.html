Certainly no reptile. But there is at least one species named after the BHL:
http://blog.biodiversitylibrary.org/2015/04/a-new-snail-species-named-in-honor-of.html

And there are a couple of reptiles named after other organizations, e.g. Anolis bicaorum,
named after the Bay Island Conservation Association (BICA).

I guess there is no global database of etymologies (or eponyms), although there are a few taxon-specific ones,
e.g. for birds, reptiles, amphibians, and mammals. Or is there?
