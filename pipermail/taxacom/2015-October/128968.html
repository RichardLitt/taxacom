Dear all,

 

Thank you very much to the more than 400 of you who have completed the
survey on natural history collections: faster access to more data and
sorry for cross posting. Undoubtedly some of you missed the deadline of 16
October 2015 due to other commitments while others may only just be
receiving the survey for the first time. To facilitate all those that would
like to complete the survey, we are extending the deadline to Friday 20th
November 2015. Please share the information about your collections through
this simple survey whether you have started digitizing or not. The survey
should take you an average of 10 to 20 minutes to complete. By participating
in this survey you are helping the collections community to gather basic
information that is essential to the discovery of your collections, planning
and leveraging resources  for future digitisation and imaging of collections
worldwide. This is an excellent opportunity for the collections community to
let the world know what you have in store and forge a united approach on how
to unlock the vast wealth of data and information that remains inaccessible.


 

The survey targets Administrators, Curators, Data, Information and
Collection Managers of natural history collections.
