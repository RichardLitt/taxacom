 Well, the "if it is
 impossible to preserve a specimen" applies
 only to "a name of a new species or
 infraspecific taxon of 
 microscopic algae or
 microfungi (fossils excepted ..."
 
 For other names of new taxa (which is most
 groups), a type 
 may not be an illustration
 from 2007 onwards.
 
 Obviously, existing illustrations as types are
 unaffected. Also,
 it remains possible to
 designate illustrations as types for 
 existing taxa.
