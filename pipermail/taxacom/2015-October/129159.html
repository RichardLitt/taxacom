Hi Tony,                        You could reduce the number of your phyla in Protista fairly quickly.  Oomycota, Hyphochytridriomycota, Labyrinthista, and Sagenista are all heterokonts.  Cercozoa and several others are now usually combined into a Phylum Rhizaria.  I assume Discomitochondria could be eliminated if it is (as I suspect) the Discoba group (Euglenozoa, Percolozoa, and Loukozoa).  And I believe that Apicomplexa and Sporozoa are still synonyms.                       --------Ken-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
