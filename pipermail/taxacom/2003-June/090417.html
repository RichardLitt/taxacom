CALL FOR PAPERS
--------------------------------
Joint international Forum on Biodiversity Information will be held for
following up the first Global Taxonomy Initiative Regional Workshop in Asia
and in conjunction with the 7th Global Biodiversity Information Facility
Governing Board Meeting. Forum Programme is organized with open-ended
symposia, workshops, poster sessions/computer demonstrations, in parallel
with GBIF events to which participation is invitation only.

THE DEADLINE (31 July 2003) FOR SUBMISSION of ABSTRACT is approaching.

SUBMISSION of ABSTRACT can be accepted by e-mail only.
Go to http://www-gti.nies.go.jp/forum2003/submission.html
and please, send your abstract to biodiv at ics-inc.co.jp,  $B#N#O#T (B to this
listserv.

The website (http://www-gti.nies.go.jp/forum2003/) has updated information
on forum programme and side meeting schedule.
