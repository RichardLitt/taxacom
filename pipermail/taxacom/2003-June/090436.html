Rienk de Jong wrote:

>If the majority determines your evidence in favour of a close
>human-orangutan relationship not strong enough to change their view, try
>to find stronger evidence to convince them, instead of feeling wronged.

My concern was not about feeling wronged. I do not recall making statements
to that effect - but if I did I stand corrected!

>Let us stick to the evidence and leave personal feelings out of the
>discussion, even when others become personal.

Yes - the nature of evidence is my focus as the work proceeds. I do leave
my personal feelings out of the discussion - at least to my recollection.
However, I see nothing wrong (and I am open to correction) with posting on
this list the personal feelings that have been expressed by opponents of
the orangutan theory (mainly in their opposition to having the orangutan
issue brought to public attention). By brining attention to such responses
those on this list interested in the development of this issue will have a
broader context by which to evaluate or otherwise assess the situation to
their individual satisfaction.

John
