>>   LACM lays off 23 Hello SDNHMer's,
>>
>>   I have some very sad news. 23 positions were eliminated at the
>>Los Angeles County Museum of Natural History yesterday, including
>>departments, curators, collections managers, exhibits, and others.
>>I don't have all the details but I'm sure we will hear more about
>>them as the information becomes available. The entire Herpetology
>>staff was fired, given a few moments to collect their belongings,
>>and told to leave the building (not much gray area there). This
>>includes Herpetology's collection manager Kent Beaman and curator
>>David Kizarian. Also fired were the curator of mammalogy,
>>curatorial assistants in birds and insects, a research librarian,
>>and the only surviving botany curator. Research and collections had
>>a total of six people terminated. I know of other cuts in exhibits
>>and operations, but it is reported that there were none in
>>administration. They will certainly need our voices to help protest
>>these deep cuts.
