Here is the Lucid price announcement from the CBIT newsletter.  A rather

Lucid(tm) Price Increase
It has been some years since any adjustment has been made to the pricing
of the Lucid(tm) products. In that time a 10% Goods and Services Tax has
been introduced, which we have absorbed, and other costs have generally
risen by at least 3% per annum. In order to recover some of this lost
ground the pricing associated with LucidT Professional will rise by 10%
for sales made after 30 June 2003. More information see
