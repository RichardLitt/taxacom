Dear Taxacomers,
I would like to invite you to publish your outcomes in our journal Plant
Introduction <http://www.plantintroduction.org/index.php/pi>, supported by
the M.M. Gryshko National Botanical Garden of the National Academy of
Sciences of Ukraine (NBG). This is a Ukrainian journal publishing since
1999.
We have no indexation in Scopus or WoS, but we, of course, also do not
pretend for potentially highly indexed publications. We will be glad to
consider any preliminary outcomes, short communications, review reports,
and so on. From our side, we promise conscientious reviewing, editorial
support of your manuscripts, and open publication of accepted manuscripts
online as soon as they are processed. There are no charges or any
additional payments - all publications are free and open and fully covered
by the NBG.

Topics of interest of the journal are: experimental and structural plant
biology; theoretical bases and practical aspects of the introduction,
breeding, selection, and acclimatization of food, forage, industrial crops,
officinal and ornamental plants; conservation of biodiversity and
environment; park science; landscaping and park design; usage of introduced
plants; floristic reports, etc.

Please do not consider this as spam. We are just trying to make our small
journal more widely known, integrate it into the international scientific
community, and survive beside such monsters as Elsevier, Wiley, MDPI, and
others. Therefore we will be thankful to you if you help us by contributing
your research outcomes.

Best regards, Andriy.
