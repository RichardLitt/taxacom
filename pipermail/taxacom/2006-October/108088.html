2007 Graduate Student Symposium

 Co-hosted by


The Marine and Estuarine Graduate Student Association


Gulf Coast Research Laboratory, The University of Southern Mississippi


and


The Graduate Student Group


Dauphin Island Sea Laboratory, The University of South Alabama


and


The Marine Environmental Researchers


Louisiana State University

 

March 2-4  2007 Gulf Coast Research Laboratory, Ocean Springs, MS

EARLY REGISTRATION DEADLINE DECEMBER 31, 2006

ABSTRACT SUBMISSION DEADLINE FEBRUARY 1, 2007

 

We'd like to invite you to attend a fun-filled, research-oriented,
affordable symposium being held at the University of Southern Mississippi's
Gulf Coast Research Lab in Ocean Springs, MS. This symposium provides a
forum for students to present their research in a formal but non-threatening
environment and to network with students and faculty from across the region.
All university students and faculty in aquatic, marine and biological
sciences are invited to attend and all graduate students are encouraged to
present. Awards will be given for best oral and poster presentation and oral
presenters will receive feedback. We have an exciting program lined up and
new this year is a career fair. Dr. George Benz, director emeritus of the
Tennessee Aquarium Research Institute and associate professor at Middle
Tennessee State University, will give our keynote address.

 

 

REGISTRATION: $20  (includes t-shirt, Fri. night social, Sat. lunch)
Late registration (after December 31, 2006) $25

DORMITORY: $10/night (includes breakfast the following morning)

BREAKFASTS: $3/each (available for individual purchase for those not staying
in the dorms)

BANQUET: $10/person (includes wine tour of Walter Anderson Museum of Art and
Low-Country Banquet)

 

Please send any questions to Allison Kennedy (
<mailto:allison.kennedy at usm.edu> allison.kennedy at usm.edu) or Josh Cook (
<mailto:josh.cook at usm.edu> josh.cook at usm.edu). Online registration and
abstract submission will soon be available on our website - more details to
follow. We look forward to seeing you in 2007 at what promises to be a great
event!
