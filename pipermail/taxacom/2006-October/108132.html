Hi Tim,

KML, a file format very similar to XML and easy to build on the fly.

Seems that we all tree work on the same idea, but 
at different taxonomic levels and on different geographic range.

Examples of Google Earth links can be seen also in the myNature Project portal:

http://mybiosis.org/nature/portal.php?pagename=novels&novelid=8

I know it is in Romanian, but you will know to 
click on Google Earth links near Cypripedium 
calceolus and Adonis vernalis links. Choose open 
with GE and the earth will swirl and zoom to 
Romanian land :-). I think you will like it.

To visit the species pages (similar to what you 
have) you will need to register (but perhaps it is worth)
http://mybiosis.org/nature/portal.php?pagename=taxon_details&genus=Cypripedium&subgenus=&species=calceolus&subspecies=&highertaxon=ORCHIDACEAE&dataset=PLANTS&languagename=English

http://mybiosis.org/nature/portal.php?pagename=taxon_details&genus=Adonis&subgenus=&species=vernalis&subspecies=&highertaxon=RANUNCULACEAE&dataset=PLANTS&languagename=English

Look forward to hear your reactions.

Sincerely,
Adorian
