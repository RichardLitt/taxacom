Dear All,
      Wollemi Pines are finally available for purchase by the public in the 
U.S.  They are apparently only available through National Geographic's 
online website
(http://shop.nationalgeographic.com) for shipments beginning in 
mid-November.  I'm not sure when they will be available for Canadians.  
Maybe in time for Christmas?  Plans are to sell them through various Garden 
Centers beginning next spring.  What a comeback for a species one thought to 
be extinct.
