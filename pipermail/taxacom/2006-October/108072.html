2006 
Closing date: 16 October 2006

Job Ref - NHM/CML/EB
Job Title - Collections Manager (Lepidoptera)
Location - South Kensington, London
Salary - £26,652 - £30,000 pa
Employer - Natural History Museum

Description 

Overall Purpose

To manage the Natural History Museums collection of Lepidoptera (butterflies 
and moths);  lead the team of Lepidoptera curators, co-ordinate the collections 
activities within agreed guidelines approved by the Head of Collections;  
organise databasing, loans and the supervision of visitors and access to the 
collections;  contribute to policy development;  take on full curatorial 
responsibility for an area of the collection.

RESPONSIBLE TO:   Head of Collections, Entomology (Band 3)
RESPONSIBLE FOR: Lepidoptera Curators, Band 5 - currently there are 5 
Lepidoptera Curators   (full-time equivalents). Various short-term contract 
staff and volunteers.

Main Tasks & Responsibilities

 Curation management.  Manage curation of the Lepidoptera Collection on a day-
to-day basis; supervise, and train curatorial staff as necessary.  

 Appraisal and preparation of annual forward job plans for Lepidoptera 
curators. 

 Take full curatorial responsibility for a defined area of the Lepidoptera 
Collection; databasing, loans, visitors and access generally, and collections 
development.

 Contribute to the formulation and implementation of the Collections 
Management work programme.

 Contribute to the Entomology Departments research programme, as agreed with 
Head of Collections.

 Promotional activities (talks and behind the scenes tours) and fund -raising 
/ consultancy work.

 Corporate activities, as required.

 Deputise for the Head of Collections as required.



PERSON SPECIFICATION

Essential Experience & Competencies

 Degree in a biological science or equivalent.
 Minimum of 5 years natural sciences collections management experience.
 Curation of insect collections.
 Successful management of junior staff.
 Collections documentation and databasing experience.
 Demonstrable knowledge of and interest in entomology.
 Computer literacy.
 Track record of high achievement in previous employment.

Desirable Experience & Competencies

 Degree in zoology or entomology.
 Post-graduate degree in entomology or taxonomy.
 Post-graduate degree / diploma in Museum Studies / AMA.
 Recognised management qualification. 
 Experience in insect collections management in a large museum or similar 
institution.
 Involvement in collections-based research. 
 Project management experience. 
 Fieldwork experience. 
 Financial management and administration. 
 Report writing experience.
 Demonstrable knowledge of and interest in Lepidoptera
 Demonstrable knowledge of and interest in entomology. 
 Interest in Lepidoptera systematics.
 Knowledge of Lepidoptera at family level.

Personal Qualities

 Self motivated  and able to motivate others
 Tenacity / drive / determination 
 Effective communicator (verbal and written)
 Good interpersonal skills 
 Pragmatic approach to problem solving 
 Common sense and adaptability

Other Requirements

 A willingness to travel in the UK and abroad for short or long periods.

 The post-holder will be expected to work in cooperation with four other 
Collections Managers within the Department of Entomology and to deputise for 
the Head of Collections, as required.

 The post-holder will be expected to work in cooperation with Researchers 
within the Lepidoptera Division without losing sight of the Collections 
Management responsibilities and priority.

 The collections and personnel of the Lepidoptera Division will likely be 
divided between the Museum in South Kensington and the Annex at Wandsworth.  
The post-holder will be expected to operate across both sites, and perhaps 
others in the future.
