There may be some on this list, particularly those interested in
Lepidoptera and cicadas, who know of John Dugdale, a long time prominent
entomologist in New Zealand who passed away last week. He kindly helped
many entomologists over the years, including myself and I was pleased to
name a new genus of ghost moths - Dugdaleiella - in his honor. For me this
will be an enduring loss.

John Grehan
