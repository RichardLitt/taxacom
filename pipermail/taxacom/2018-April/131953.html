Dear colleagues, first and foremost the entomologists among you,

waivers are available until the end of the year for publishing in the DEZ, allowing the publication of articles without page charges. The Deutsche Entomologische Zeitschrift (German Entomological Journal) is a high-quality forum for comprehensive taxonomic works, morphological and biogeographical contributions, looking back on 160 years of publishing entomological articles. For some years now it is published by Pensoft, offering a number of semantic enhancements to the content as well as the distribution of the published taxonomic information to global data repositories and indexes. The Impact Factor is 0.697.

For further information have a look at the website https://dez.pensoft.net/

I would be very pleased to receive your manuscript. If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate contacting me.

Kind regards,
Dominique Zimmermann
