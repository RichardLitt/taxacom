Leach 1815 proposed Limnebius
Agassiz 1846 emended it to Limnobius (unjustified emendation: Limnebius is not preoccupied)
Wollaston 1864 proposed punctatus in Limnebius
Wollaston 1865 transferred puncatatus to Limnobius (and listed Limnebius in synonymy)
 — Since Wollaston’s 1865 paper mentions both Limnebius and Limnobius, the action could be interpreted as an emendation (unjustified) or it could be a subsequent usage of Agassiz’s 1846 Limnobius

In any case, the correct combination is still Limnebius punctatus Wollaston, 1864 since Limnobius was unjustified.

spelled as "Limnebius". In a later paper, he corrected the generic spelling
