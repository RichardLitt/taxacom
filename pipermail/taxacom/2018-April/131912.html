My Taxonomist Appreciation Day / ORCID proof-of-concept caught the
attention of the ORCID Organization and I was asked to write about it
for their blog,
https://orcid.org/blog/2018/04/06/compendium-taxonomists-orcid. The
list of taxonomists & systematists continues to grow in dribs and
drabs. Because 2020 will be a big year (or ought to be), perhaps we
can find a more professional home for this w/ a widespread campaign.

Cheers,

David P. Shorthouse
