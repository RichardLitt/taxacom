2018 
On May 2, 2018 at 2:00 PM Eastern, the Biodiversity Collections Network will host a webinar on Collections Communications.  The program will discuss the findings from a BCoN workshop on the topic, as well as some of the opportunities for training and outreach that have arisen since the workshop.  

The program is free, but registration is required.  Please register for this and future webinars at https://bcon.aibs.org/events/ <https://bcon.aibs.org/events/>

The schedule for future programs is also available in the Events section of the BCoN website at https://bcon.aibs.org.

We look forward to seeing you at a future program.
