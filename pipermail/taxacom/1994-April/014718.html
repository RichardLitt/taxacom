1994 
The University of Hawaii office of Technology Transfer and Development is
seeking pictures/images for a CD-ROM project on the toxic and poisonous
plants of North America.  They are willing to settle on use fees with
photographers.

They plan to use some of the images from the Image Archive on the
Biodiversity Gopher and will be contacting the photographers directly to
make the arrangements.

If you have any slides/images of toxic North American plants available,
please contact:

   Jonathan Roberts   roberts_j at uhunix.uhcc.hawaii.edu

   or Ron Bagalban at (808) 539-3821 (Hawaii)
