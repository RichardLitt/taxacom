Hi All,
       I see that among excluded names are names proposed after 1930 based on the "work of extant animals".  I assume such "work" might include foot prints, hair, scat, or other traces left by the animal.  Would that include body parts such as isolated teeth?
      Anyway, I would be interested to know of some examples of excluded names proposed before 1930 that are based on such "work" of animals.
