> (a) In the algae: -phyceae (class) and -phycidae (subclass);"
>
> These are feminine plurals, from Greek phykos, seaweed, plus the
> suffixes -eus, -ea, -eum (specifically, the plural form of -ea) or
> -ides.

Indeed the ICBN does not cover gender of anything but genera, since this is the only case where gender affects part of a name: specific and
infraspecific epithets, when adjectives, take their endings from the gender of the genus.

The ICBN (2000) does state that 'phykos' in botanical usage is treated as  masculine, Art 62.2 (c)
