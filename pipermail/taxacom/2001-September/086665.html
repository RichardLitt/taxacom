Hello,

An article that you and others might find quite interesting is by C. G. G.
J. van Steenis: Malayan Bignoniaceae, their taxonomy, origin, and
Geographical Distribution, Receuil des Travaux botaniques neerlandais, 24:
787-1049.  1927.    Perhaps especially pages 1015-1043 and the
maps.  Although I don't recall him proposing plate tectonics, he was ready
to believe in continental movements at that early date.  There are also
citations to earlier works, for example by Wegener, Burkill, and
Willis.  He discusses generalities that go beyond just the Bignoniaceae.

Steve Manning
