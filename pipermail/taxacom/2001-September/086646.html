Position:       Insect Morphological Systematist (postdoctoral) at
the level of Assistant Research Scientist III
                        Illinois Natural History Survey (full-time,
grant-supported position).

Location:       Illinois Natural History Survey, Champaign, Illinois
under the direction of Dr. Kevin P. Johnson (217) 244-9267.  For
further details about the position, contact Kevin Johnson by phone or
e-mail: kjohnson at inhs.uiuc.edu

Description:    Successful candidate will conduct a systematic study
of lice in the avian Ischnocera using morphological characters.
Postdoc will borrow slide mounted specimens from major collections,
score nymphal and adult morphological characters, conduct
phylogenetic analyses based on morphological characters, and prepare
results for publication.  Position includes the potential for
training in molecular systematics.  Morphological phylogeny will be
integrated with molecular data to study the evolution of microhabitat
specialization in avian lice and to develop methods for comparing
morphological and molecular data sets.  Funding is available for two
years with the possibility of extension to a third year.

Qualifications:         Ph.D. (postdoctoral) in organismal biology,
systematics, entomology, or related field. Experience with
entomology, morphological taxonomy and systematics, and slide mounted
specimens is preferred.  Demonstrated ability to work independently,
present results of research, and publish in peer-reviewed journals is
required.

Salary:                 $28,000

Benefits:       Generous Vacation, sick and personal leave.  State
Universities Retirement System and State health, dental and life
insurance.

Starting Date:       October 1, 2001 or thereafter.

Application:    To ensure full consideration, applications should be
received by September 26, 2001 or until filled  To apply send CV,
three letters of reference, statement of research experience and
interests (max. 2 pages), and a cover letter to:
