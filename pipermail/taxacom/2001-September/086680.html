Dear Taxacomers,
I am working on Pediobius species associated with cereal crop stem-
borers in West Africa. I intend to produce a phylogeography of all
Pediobius species worldwide. In respect of this, I am searching for
specimens anywhere. I am therefore asking taxacomers to help even
with very few specimens, fresh, preserved in alcohol or dried are
welcome.
Looking forward to hearing from you.
