Dear all,
      I am pleased to announce that the Tables of Contents of all issues of
Entomologica Fennica are now online at
http://sekj.pc.helsinki.fi/EF/entfen.htm . The web pages are still at the
bare-bones stage, no frills (but extremely fast download times!). Abstracts
are available for this year's volume, and the abstracts for previous years
will be forthcoming. Entomologica Fennica is a journal publishing original
research reports and reviews focusing on the taxonomy, zoogeography and
faunistics as well as on conservation biology, and on related subjects of
insects and other arthropods in the Holarctic region. Our journal is
indexed in: Biological Abstracts, Current Contents A, B & ES, Entomology
Abstracts, BIOSIS, BIOSIS U.K., CABS, Cambridge Scientific Abstracts, Inst.
for Scientific Info Publication Processing.
     As the new Editor-in-Chief of Entomologica Fennica I would like to
issue a call for papers. Please see our web pages for instructions to authors.

Thanks for your attention!
