An exotic-looking Polygonum was found in Iowa City and it's such a
distinctive plant that I thought someone might be able to tell me which
species it is just from a verbal description (I don't have a photograph).

It's herbaceous, but ca. 5 ft. tall with wide-spreading branches making it
ca. 5 feet in diametre, and pendulous racemes of bright magenta
flowers.  The leaves are ovate with a narrowly acute or acuminate apex and
the petioles are winged.
