2001 
The Institute of Systematic Botany of the University of Zurich.

We would like to invite applications for the position of Herbarium Curator 
at the Institute of Systematic Botany of the University of Zurich.  The 
curation of this herbarium of ca. 3 million specimens is shared between the 
Eidgenossische Technische Hochschule and the University of Zurich.  The 
successful applicant would be expected to manage and curate the Angiosperm 
collections and develop an own research programme, in addition, he / she 
may also supervise graduate students.  The Institute, which is housed in 
the Botanic Gardens, is well equipped for morphological, taxonomic and 
systematic molecular research, and includes an excellent library, thus 
providing a good research environment.

Interested persons should include their curriculum vitae, publication list, 
as well as three potential referees.  Applications close on 20 October, 
2001.  Please send to:
Institute of Systematic Botany, University of Zurich, Zollikerstrasse 107, 
CH-8008 Zurich, Switzerland.  Ph: 0041 1 634 84 11, Fax: 0041 1 634 84 03, 
email: plinder at systbot.unizh.ch
