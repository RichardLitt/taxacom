For any given taxon, it's important to test whether the characters used
seem to actually delineate plausible groups or just vary within
populations.  That is true of molecular and morphological characters.
Whether these particular characters are good, I do not know, but I would
want to see intermediate populations sampled to look for a break between
them, rather than just "way over here had lots of differences from way over
there".  As another example, I know of a genus name where the cited
diagnostic character was the length of a variable region in the DNA.  Not a
good choice - given that the region is quite variable in this particular
family of snails, different taxa converge on the same length.  The actual
sequences would have been a much better marker.
