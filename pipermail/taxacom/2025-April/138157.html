Dear Taxacom community,

I'm pleased to announce the new wiki page *Storytelling with IndExs* 
under https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fistc.cetaf.org%2FStorytelling_with_IndExsin&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C318846dd744243ff8dad08dd840cdd76%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638811913019723197%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=jNYkRtoyVewiGwsg%2BN%2FmooGpEZNlVev59heXFl2tI24%3D&reserved=0 the CETAF ISTC Wiki.

More information on the IndExs directory as service which offers 
bibliographic information on the more than 2,400 botanical and 
mycological specimen series called exsiccatae. The online service exists 
since 24 years and is primarily relevant for collection management and 
collection management systems in herbaria. Examples from more than 110 
herbaria are included, partly with relation to GBIF occurrence data records.

see also

content to make IndExs more complete.
