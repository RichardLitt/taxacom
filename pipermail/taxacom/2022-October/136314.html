> As an outsider to all of this kerfuffle in the reptile world, what I would
> find interesting in any Taxacome discussion is the nature of non-code
> compliant decisions being made. The code is just an evolving agreement
> among some, and usually followed by most. But if one does not accept the
> code in some respect, then one might expect an explanation so anyone may
> decide for themselves whether such a decision makes sense - whether or not
> they agree with it. I am guilty, along with various lepidopterists, in not
> following the code with respect to gender agreement modifications of
> existing names. But I otherwise try to follow the code as best I can or as
> best as I understand. And I can accept being criticised for the exception.
> I don't care about whether anyone may be considered a taxonomic 'vandal' or
> not, just the nature of their decision, with which I may or may not agree.
