Dear all,*
The International Zoological Congress of Grigore Antipa Museum (CZGA)* 
is a zoological meeting held in Bucharest, Romania, and organized by the 
"Grigore Antipa" National Museum of Natural History.

The congress includes invited lectures and oral/poster presentations by 
participants.

The 11th edition (CZGA 2022) will be organized in an online-only format 
between *December 5th-7th, 2022.*

The general topic of the congress is */Leveraging zoological collections./*

We want to discuss how zoological collections are useful to our society, 
other than for biological taxonomy studies. While we will accept 
contributions on this classical topic, we encourage the submission of 
contributions showing other ways of using zoological collections (i.e. 
tracking population changes, monitoring environmental stressors 
occurrence, tracking pathogen introduction, tracking invasive species 
introduction etc.). The examples given are by no mean exhaustive and you 
can contribute any work that supports the intended topic of the congress.
