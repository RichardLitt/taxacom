> The NTNU University Museum is seeking a highly qualified and motivated
> candidate for an associate professor position in integrative taxonomy and
> phylogenetics. The position is part of NTNUs ambitious Onsager program and
> includes substantial research funding with the goal of qualifying for full
> professor and receive tenure within 6 years.
>
> The position is central to improve the knowledge of species and their
> evolutionary relationships in taxon rich organism groups and important for
> strengthening collection-based research at the Department of Natural
> History. More specifically, the successful candidate will carry out
> cutting-edge research within the field of taxonomy and phylogenetics using
> a combination of tools and data (morphological, molecular, ecological,
> symbiome, etc.) to analyse biodiversity at the species-level.
