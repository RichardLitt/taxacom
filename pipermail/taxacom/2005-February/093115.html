Does anybody know what happened to "FishGopher" (e.g.,
http://www.as.ua.edu/biology/uaic/fishgopher.html). This service
searched multiple databases for information on fish specimens, but
seems to have died. Some of the data providers serve up data through
the GBIF portal, but not all (e.g., Academy of Natural Sciences of
Philadelphia, Cornell University, University of Alabama, and University
of Michigan).
