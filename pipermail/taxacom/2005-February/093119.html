Dear collageus,
 
Motivated by the emergent situation after the windstorm disaster in the High
Tatra Mts (the Carpathian Mountains) and other regions of Europe, we invite
you to take part at conference "Calamities in mountain areas and their
impact against the nature".
 
The conference will be held on 18. March 2005 at the Comenius University,
Bratislava, Slovakia.
 
Detailed information and the registration form are available at
http://www.fns.uniba.sk/~kzo/en_novikov_2005.htm
 
With my best wishes,
Ed Stloukal
