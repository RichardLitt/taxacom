5TH INTERNATIONAL CONVENTION ON ENVIRONMENT AND DEVELOPMENT

www.medioambiente.cu



Additional information at: convencion at ama.cu



MAIN EVENTS AND ACTIVITIES



* 4th Congress on Protected Areas

* 5th Congress on Environmental Education for Sustainable Development

* 1st Congress on Entrepreneurial Environmental Management

* 1st Congress on Tourism and Environment

* 1st Congress on Health, Quality Life and Environment

* 2nd Cuba-USA Meeting on Environment

* 1st International Symposium on Hydrographical Watersheds Management

* 1st International Symposium on Hydric Resources Management

* 2nd International Symposium on Coastal Ecosystems Management



Each event will have the presentation of master lectures, papers, oral
presentations, posters and the holding of 5 Round Tables on the following
topics:



* Synergy between Environmental Conventions

* Peace, Culture and Environment

* Globalization, Economy and Environment

* Disasters and their link to the protection of Environment

* José Martí and Environment
