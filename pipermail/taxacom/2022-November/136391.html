But 'invasive' species are 'introduced', and in this sense are just as
displaced. What happens after is relative (how does one know when the
threshold of 'outcompeting' and 'displacing' natives?). The term 'invasive'
is also problematic in that the taxa in question never 'invaded' in the
active sense, but were originally displaced by human activity. Even
'native' taxa can become 'invasive' under new environmental conditions
within the native habitat or region.
