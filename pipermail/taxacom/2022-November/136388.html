Dear all,

The Systematics Association President?s Lecture this year will feature the second edition of ?The Joy of Discovery?, an event that provides a forum to share the excitement of discovering a new taxon or discovering the identity of an unknown specimen. We will have five short talks from five taxonomists, all from different continents, representative of our international membership from across the globe.

When: Thursday 24th November, 6 pm GMT.
Where: This is an online event and attendance is free.
