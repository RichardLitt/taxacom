Maybe not a bad idea to dump journal hardcopies that are freely available in places such as the Biodiversity Heritage Library (the Canadian Journal of Zoology is not!). If not, donate the journal so some library ? maybe they have space.

However, you may want to check the status of your journal in BHL: some of their scans are rather low quality and many of them have rather bad OCR ? so likely many of them have to be re-scanned or re-processed.

If you have spare money, donate some money to BHL or include them in your beneficiary list of  your retirement account or will (which is what I have done).
