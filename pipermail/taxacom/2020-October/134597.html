In order to complete its team, the Conservatoire et Jardin botaniques of 
the City of Geneva is looking for a SCIENTIFIC ASSISTANT at 80%.

Your mission and your responsibilities

You are conducting research in the taxonomy of phanerogams. As the 
co-editor of publication, you manage and supervise the scientific and 
editorial publications of the Institute, in particular the journals 
Candollea and Boissiera. You also contribute to the management and 
curation of the collections of the general herbarium.

https://www.geneve.ch/fr/administration-municipale/offres-emploi/adjoint-adjointe-scientifique
