The International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN) is seeking candidates to fill the position of Executive Secretary. 
The ICZN Secretariat is located in the Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum, Singapore.

NOTE: 
Closing date for applications: 30 October 2020
This is a very short application deadline, but with a Secretariat of only one person, we have to push this forward to ascertain the best possible transfer of relevant information to the incoming Secretary.

More information: https://nuscareers.taleo.net/careersection/nusep/jobdetail.ftl?job=00DKN
