Dear All,
Nominations are open for the TDWG Executive Committee (2021-2022).

All the details can be found here
https://www.tdwg.org/news/2020/call-for-nominations/

Positions are open for the Deputy Chair, Chair of the Functional
Subcommittee for Communications and Outreach, Chair of the Functional
Subcommittee for Funding and Partnerships, Africa Representative, Asian
Representative, North American Representative and the European
Representative.

If you are even curious about the posts please feel free to
contact anyone on the current Executive Committee who will be only too
willing to discuss the positions and possible roles you could play (
https://www.tdwg.org/about/executive/).

The deadline to receive nominations is the 16 November 2020.

Regards
Quentin Groom
