Rod to skip to (I don't need to comment on the other points):
"Yes, so the question is:

a) can we build services that make retrieving basic information on  
names trivial

b) would such services be sufficiently attractive that users who  
wanted to do this in bulk would pay for that service?"

I think the answer would be if you are primarily dealing with only  
names - then no. However, if names and their associated taxonomies are  
being used to find and assist in interpreting data - then the service  
is needed, but the question becomes who will pay?

Paying is linked to the quality of the service and if you can't judge  
whether what you are getting is quality or not means that one will  
often go to the "free service" not knowing where the problems are and  
not realising that what you are relying is not accurate. The whole  
issue, like many aspects is sociological, affecting the way science  
operates.
