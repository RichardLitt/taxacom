Dear Taxacomers,

We are glad to let you know the recent publication by Vences,
Guayasamin, Miralles and De la Riva on supraspecific
classification, reviewing criteria for the selection of clades for naming as
Linnaean taxa.

The paper is in open access this adress:

http://www.mapress.com/zootaxa/2013/f/zt03636p244.pdf

We hope that you will find our Taxon Naming Criteria useful
in future discussions, even if certainly some of you will
disagree with our specific arguments.

A. Miralles.
