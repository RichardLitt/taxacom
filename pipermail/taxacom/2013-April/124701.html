I don't recall that one from Darwin.  But this is my favorite Darwin quote
along these lines:

"Hence, in determining whether a form should be ranked as a species or a
variety, the opinion of naturalists having sound judgment and wide
experience seems the only guide to follow."
(Origin of Species)

Translation: "A species is what taxonomists say it is."
