Dear All,
as part of the pro-iBiosphere <http://www.pro-ibiosphere.eu/> project we 
are investigating the current use of digital tools for taxonomy. To help 
us understand the domain we have a questionnaire on the topic 
(https://www.surveymonkey.com/s/eTools).
The questionnaire is aimed mainly at the creators of taxonomic 
knowledge, primarily taxonomists, but also the other people that get 
involved in the process including editors and database managers.
I imagine that many of you have already seen and completed it. However, 
if I have missed any of you, please take a few moments to complete it.
Many thanks for your help.
