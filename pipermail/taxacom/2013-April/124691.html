Doug's post. In my little world of Frasera (Kingdom Plantae),

to the aggregators years ago, this has not been corrected.  In fact, as 
I just checked, my comments have disappeared from the great data bases 
in the sky.  I could choose to re-submit the comments, check that they 
appear online, followup, pursue, irritate, heckle, etc., a process 
requiring multiple hours.  Instead I'll just cut it off at this point (5 
minutes) and go back to the work I want to do.
