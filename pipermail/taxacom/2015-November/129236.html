For those who are interested in the plant genus Carex L. (Cyperaceae): in
December 2011 Carex Europaea, volume 1, was published by the German
publisher Margraf Publishers. Unfortunately this book, in English,  was soon
out of stock. However, I am glad to announce that from early December 2015 a
completely updated version will be available, but now as e-book. For all
information, see this link: http://shop2014.2margraf.de/index.php?id=186
