Hello Taxacom colleagues

The decline of small museums around the world continues. Non-scientist 
directors are told by conservative governments to cut costs: because the 
executives don't understand the value of scientific research, the 
science staff and facilities go first. Read about the further decline of 
the Australian Museum in this Sydney Morning Herald article by 
journalist Kate Aubusson.

http://www.smh.com.au/nsw/australian-museum-guts-staff-putting-priceless-artefacts-at-risk-union-20151112-gkxac0.html
or http://tinyurl.com/obv3mbj

Writing letters to the current director Kim McKay might help although I 
think that key scientific staff,such as the Imaging Laboratory Manager 
Sue Lindsay, have already been made redundant.

Disclaimer: I was made redundant at this organization in 2013 under the 
previous non-scientist director,  breaking the long string of 
distinguished scientists who directed the Australian Museum.
