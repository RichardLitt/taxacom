     19 June 1996

     Dear Colleagues-

     The deadline for applications for a curator position at the N.C. State
     Museum of Natural Sciences has been extended.  Applications will now be
     accepted until close of business on 27 June 1996.

     Details of the job are posted below.  Application should consist of a
     VITA and a letter of interest in the position.

     Please forward this to other appropriate listservers or to any colleague
     who might be interested. Thanks!

     P.S. Please do not respond to me - I do not work at the museum and I'm
     only passing this along as a favor.

     Bill Adams

     *********************************************************************
     JOB TITLE: NATURAL SCIENCE RESEARCH CURATOR II
     VACANCY#: 28177
     DIVISION: N.C. MUSEUM OF NATURAL SCIENCES
     SALARY GRD/RANGE: 72 $29,748 - $39,558    APPT TYPE: PERM FULL-TIME
     CLOSING DATE: 06/18/96                    LOCATION: RALEIGH, NC
     DESCRIPTION OF WORK:
        THIS POSITION SERVES AS PRINCIPLE CURATOR OF THE AQUATIC
     MACROINVERTEBRATE COLLECTION, PROVIDES EXPERTISE TO OTHER
     SECTIONS,GENERAL PUBLIC AND OTHER AGENCIES. ALSO CONDUCTS ORIGINAL
     RESEARCH ON AQUATIC MACROINVERTEBRATES.
     TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE:
        POSSESSION OF A MASTER'S DEGREE IN BIOLOGY, ZOOLOGY, OR A SCIENCE
     CURRICULUM RELATED TO AREA OF EXPERTISE AND THREE YEARS OF EXPERIENCE
     IN THE SPECIALTY AREA; OR AN EQUIVALENT COMBINATION OF EDUCATION AND
     EXPERIENCE.
        PREFERRED EXPERIENCE: PH.D IN ZOOLOGY OR RELATED FIELD.
     CONSIDERABLE MILEAGE ON ZOOLOGY IN GENERAL AND SPECIFICALLY FRESHWATER
     CRUSTACEANS AND MOLLUSKS GROUPS.  CONSIDERABLE KNOWLEDGE OF CONDUCTING
     SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH AND PRODUCING PUBLICATIONS. KNOWLEDGE OF TAXONOMIC
     PRINCIPLES IS ESSENTIAL.  GOOD PUBLIC RELATION SKILLS ARE A MUST.
     KNOWLEDGE OF BUILDING, PROPER MAINTENANCE, AND USE OF RESEARCH
     COLLECTIONS IS ESSENTIAL.
