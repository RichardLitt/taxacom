If you are "going away" for the summer field season, or whatever, you
can SET TAXACOM NOMAIL rather than unsubscribing (in order to keep your
emailbox from filling up over the period). To do this, send email to
LISTSERV at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU  (NOT to TAXACOM) with a one-line message:

SET TAXACOM NOMAIL

and when you return, send email to the same place saying:

SET TAXACOM MAIL

(or SET TAXACOM DIGEST, or SET TAXACOM INDEX, as you prefer).

Students: If you might be returning to a different email userid when
you return, it would be better to SIGNOFF TAXACOM, because you won't
be able to reSET yourself from your new userid. You'll have to
subscribe anew from there.
