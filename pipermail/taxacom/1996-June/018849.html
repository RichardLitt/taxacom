The graduate students in the Botany Department at the University of
Washington (your hosts to AIBS meeting this year) has put together a local
AIBS guide webpage to help all those planning on atttending the
conference.  The URL of this page is

        http://weber.u.washington.edu/~phylo/aibs/aibs.html

In this guide you'll find lots of information on Seattle, including maps
showing the relative locations of the hotel, the campus, the airport, etc.
It also contains restaurant suggestions, guides to pubs, coffee, weather,
and more.

Please forward this message widely to all those planning on
attending this conference.  Thank you.
