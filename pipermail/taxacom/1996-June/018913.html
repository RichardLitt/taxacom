Hi. There was a fairly large response to the message I posted about the
Maquipucuna field station, so put all of the information I have about the
three field stations I visited on the website:

http://www.lam.mus.ca.us/lacmnh/departments/research/entomology/phorids/proj

        You will find the names of 3 field stations- Maquipucuna, Bilsa and
Yasuni- near the end of the text on that page. Just click on the one in
which you are interested, and the info will pop up. Each has the name of a
contact in Ecuador to get in touch with, but I'm glad to try and answer
questions, too.
