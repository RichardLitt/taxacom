Dear TDWG Supporters

Apologies to those who may have already received this post. 

The TDWG Infrastructure Project (http://www.tdwg.org/activities/tip/) has
developed a Biodiversity Data Standards Technical Survey to try and address
two issues-

1. To improve communication within TDWG. Communication have been criticized
for being too technical or not technical enough. We are seeking a better
understanding of our audience.

2. To identify our skills and resources so to better manage our standards
development.

The survey can be found at

http://www.tdwg.org/about-tdwg/surveys/

The survey is predominantly in the form of tick boxes. It will take from 5
to 15 minutes. 

Your assistance is sought to ensure that we have an accurate picture of the
community that we try to serve. If you have colleagues who might make use of
TDWG products in the future, please also pass this request to them.

Many Thanks

Roger Hyam
Standards Architect, TDWG Infrastructure Project
