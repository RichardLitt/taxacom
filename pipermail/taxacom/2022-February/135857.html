More generally, homonymy (including misspellings) is quite often bad news
for online databases.  At a minimum, author and date need to be included.
Linking names extracted from miscellaneous sources to indications of the
higher taxon would often help as well, e.g., decreasing confusion between
the lichen and the gastropod Graphis.
