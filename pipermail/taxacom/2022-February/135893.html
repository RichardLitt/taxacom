The disruption of revising all past names for such mindless reasoning is
just not worth it. Personally I care little for the etymology of a name
apart from its use to determine spelling. The journal Copeia recently
renamed itself because apparently Edward Cope was not considered worthy.
Personally I thought that quite mindless, but fine change the name. But
nomenclature should be left be, this discontinuity in the literature is too
harmful and outweighs the importance of caring who its named after.

Cheers Scott
