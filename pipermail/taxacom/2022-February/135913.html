Hi Dirk,
        This has apparently been going on for quite some time.  Wikipedia has an article on "Taxonomy" in general, and that article redirects one to a "Taxonomy (biology)" for biological taxonomy in particular.  I guess the widening use of the term taxonomy (in economics and other fields) could cause some problems if one is doing a google search for taxonomy and a geographic term.
        But one could get around that by searching for "biological taxonomy" and the geographic term.   Try a search for: "biological taxonomy" in Europe.  The results are much better if interested in biological taxonomy.
