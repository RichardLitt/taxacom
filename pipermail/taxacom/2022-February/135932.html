The name has been ignored. This probably helps its unacceptance. I found it
in papers of a conference, in Russian language (not an abstract, but a full
paper with descriptions and figures). In fact, maybe more correct word is
not "probably", but "perhaps" or "possibly". The Russian word "vozmozhno"
is translated either way.
The whole phrase is translated as, "Perhaps, the specimens of B.inermis
from the Peru-Chile Trench form a special subspecies B.i.peruana n.ssp."

Thanks to all!
