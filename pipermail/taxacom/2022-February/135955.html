Hi All,
      This weekend I discovered Cavalier-Smith's very recent paper (December 2021), published 9 months after he died.  It is a wonderful article and a synthesis of many decades of his research on protists.  And I was so glad to see that he gave a formal name ( Opizoa ) to the basal Opisthokonta (which I refer to as archaeopisthokonts).  He also rerooted eukaryotes (on Phylum Malawimonada) and more firmly placed other "orphan" taxa as well.  Chromista is still there, but without Picozoa.
      Therefore, below I am updating my Kingdom Protista classification one more time (now with 23 Phyla, plus the 3 exgroup Kingdoms).
