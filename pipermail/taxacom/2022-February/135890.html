> In my opinion, Mosyakin makes important and valid arguments against 
> employing "...politically motivated censorship and totalitarian 
> cleansing of scientific history, and of the biological nomenclature

A small correction; what you and Mosyakin are referring to is a 
manifestation of "cancel culture", while "woke" is a separate cultural 
phenomenon. Though the two may intersect, they are not directly linked, 
and a person can easily be part of one cultural phenomenon and not part 
of the other.
