Hi,
Unlike China and other countries, in Finland researchers receive no money
for their publications. The universities do receive money for publications.
There is a national system for assessing publications, called Publication
Forum (EN), Julkaisufoorumi (FI). The JuFo maintains and develops the
classification of scientific publication channels. The base of the JuFo
system is a qualitative classification of scientific publication channels
in all scientific fields. The evaluation of publication channels takes
place in 23 field specific expert panels. The levels are: 1 = basic, 2 leading, 3 = top. There is also a level 0, for channels that do not meet
the criteria.
The system is transparent and is well documented. It also uses as a source
of information the Norwegian and Danish equivalents. Detailed information
can be found at https://www.julkaisufoorumi.fi/en
If in something Finland is privileged, that would be the high level of
democracy and low level of corruption.
