Dear colleagues,

I would like to share with you an article we just got accepted for
publication.

The paper evaluates advantages and issues of compiling morphological
datasets from images of museum specimens. We have focused on plant
specimens, but we believe our findings to hold true for 2D-images of other
organisms as well.

https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/2041-210X.13450?campaign=wolacceptedarticle

I hope you like it and I would be pleased if you could share it with your
colleagues too.

All the best,

Leo Borges

properly diagramated preprint from here:
