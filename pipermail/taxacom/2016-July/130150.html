2016 
I am pleased to announce the publication of Phycologia Europaea Rhodophyta
which updates the Scandinavian taxonomic literature published in 1996. The
new edition is significantly enlarged including taxa from the NE Atlantic,
the Mediterranean, the Black and Caspian Seas, as also Scandinavia and the
Arctic Ocean.



The text is analytic compiling protologues, illustrations, and other
information from essential publications since Linné.

It is synthetic grouping taxa based on the same type.

It is synoptic summarizing for the first time the European literature on
red algae.

Chapters on early algal classifications and the roots of systematics are
included.



The work has been published in two volumes with a total of 1554 pages.

It is similarly priced as the 1st edition (900 SEK) and has a Swedish ISBN
978-91-637-8523.



Copies are available from the author.
