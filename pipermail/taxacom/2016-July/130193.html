Collections Manager, National Herbarium of New South Wales, Botanic Gardens & Centennial Parklands , Sydney, Australia

We are looking for someone with experience and passion for managing scientific collections and the data associated with them. The National Herbarium of New South Wales which is the major centre for taxonomic and systematic botanical research in New South Wales and the collection of approximately 1.2 million plant specimens is one of the largest and most important in Australia. The Manager, Collections manages the physical and electronic curation of the National Herbarium of New South Wales, the Daniel Solander Library and the art collections of the Botanic Gardens & Centennial Parklands.

If you are interested in this role please visit http://iworkfor.nsw.gov.au/job/manager-collections-37805

For further information please contact Brett Summerell, Director Science & Conservation: brett.summerell at environment.nsw.gov.au<https://mail.environment.nsw.gov.au/owa/UrlBlockedError.aspx>, +61 2 9231 8113
