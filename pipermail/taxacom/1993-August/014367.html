As if his ego wasn't big enough already, Jim Croft's ANBG gopher gets a
mention in this week's Network World (one of the endless weekly magazines
on computing).  In an article on Gopher, the ANBG gopher was cited as a
link to the Australian Postal Codes (big deal, eh?).  Another item for his
WWW vitae!

At any rate, the article, by Ed Krol, gives a brief description of Gopher
and praises it pretty highly.
