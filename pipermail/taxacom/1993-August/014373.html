1993 
BOTANICAL INFORMATION MANAGER.  The Nature Conservancy seeks a masters
level candidate for the position of Botanical Information Manager at the
Conservancy's Headquarters Office in Arlington, VA (near Washington, DC.)
Major responsibilities include maintenance of the Conservancy's central
botanical databases, reconciliation of alternative taxonomic
classifications, and review of species status information.  Candidates
should have an advanced degree in systematic botany, or equivalent
experience, extensive familiarity with North American floristic
literature, and at least a year's experience with large multifile database
systems.  Must also have demonstrated ability to work independently and on
team projects, give attention to details, and follow established
procedures.  Familiarity with work of Heritage Programs desirable.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION:

                         Chief Botanist
