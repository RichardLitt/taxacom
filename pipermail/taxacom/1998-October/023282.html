Subscribers to Taxacom may be interested in visiting the new WWW
pages that we have just posted on the Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh
site.

These pages outline the on-going work of RBGE and its collaborators on
the islands of the Soqotra (Socotra) Archipelago. They included the
text from several published and unpublished articles along with many
images of the plants of the islands.

In the near future checklists of the plants and birds of the islands
will be added, along with additional articles.

Please visit the site at:

http://www.rbge.org.uk/Arabia/Soqotra/
