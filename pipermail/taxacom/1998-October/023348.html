        Last september, I collected in Canada during the NEMF98 (NorthEastern

microscopical control was made) :

Gomphidius glutinosus
Paxillus atrotomentosus
Paxillus involutus

        That is my personnal experience of these taxa outside Europe. Sorry for
such a little contribution to your question.
