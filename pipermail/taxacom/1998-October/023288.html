1998 
This is a call for papers for the Records of the Hawaii Biological Survey
for 1998. HBS Records have been published yearly since 1995 and reflect
the latest information available on the fauna and flora of the Hawaiian
Islands.  Primarily short notes (1-4 pages) are encouraged that pertain to
new species, new island or state records, range extensions, or checklists
(new or revised) of complete groups of organisms in Hawaii. For larger
articles, authors are asked to contact the editor first before submitting
mss.

A pdf file of guidelines for authors can be found on the web at:
http://www.bishop.hawaii.org/bishop/HBS/guidelines.pdf

.. or prospective authors can follow the format in previous issues of the
Records, published in the Bishop Museum Occasional Papers series.

Deadline for submission of mss for this HBS Records is 15 December 1998.
We expect publication to be in April 1999. Please feel free to email me
with with any questions you may have.

Neal Evenhuis
Editor, Records of the Hawaii Biological Survey
http://www.bishop.hawaii.org/bishop/HBS
