1998 
Several have asked for a summary of the responses to my
question about using herbarium specimens for DNA type
research.  So here is a compilation of the references I
received...
flowering plants--
Ballard, H.E.Jr., M.D. Devine, & K.J. Sytsma. 1997. Centrifugal
  allopatric speciation in the Viola ...  American Institute of
  Biological Sciences. Montreal.
Ballard, H.E.Jr, M.D. Devine, & K.J. Sytsma. 1998. Insights into
  geographic speciation in the Viola ...  Society for the Study
  of Evolution. Vancouver, B.C.
Ballard, H.E.Jr, K.J. Sytsma, & S. Carlquist. 1996. ITS sequence
  data illuminate the biogeographic origin ... American Institute
  of Biological Sciences. Seattle WA.
Ballard, H.E.Jr, K.J. Sytsma, & S. Carlquist. 1997. Further
  revelations on adaptive radiation in Hawaiian Viola...
  American Institute of Biological Sciences. Montreal.
Ballard, H.E.Jr, K.J. Sytsma, & R.R. Kowal. 1998. Shrinking
  the violets: phylogenetic relationships... Systematic
  Botany.
Ballard, H.E.Jr. & K.J. Sytsma. (ms subm) Internal transcribed
  spacer DNA... Evolution
Cosner, M.E., R.K. Jansen & T.G. Lammers. 1994. Phylogenetic
  relationships in the Campanulales... Plant Systematics &
  Evolution 190: 79-95.
Hodges, S.A., Ballard, H.E.Jr, M.L. Arnold, & M.W. Chase. 1995.
  Generic relationships in the Violaceae... American Journal
  of Botany.
Nadot, S., H.E. Ballard Jr., J.B. Creach, & I. Dajoz. (ms subm)
  The evolution of pollen heteromorphism in Viola...
  Evolution.
Prather, L.A. & R.K. Jansen. 1998. Phylogeny of Cobaea...
  Systematic Botany 23: 57-72.
???, Uta-Regina, H.H. Hilger & W. Martin. 1996. Island
  colonization and evolution... Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 93:
  11740-11745.
lichens--
Goffinet, B. & T. Goward. 1998. Is Nephroma silvae-veteris...
  in Lichenographia Thomsoniana: North American lichenology
  in honor of John W. Thomson. NY
other suggestions given (no references just the author names)--
  Scott Rogers & A. Bendich (fungi and plants)
  Catherine Lafarge-England (mosses)
  Bernard Goffinet (additional lichen pubs and also mosses)
  Lockerman & Jansen in "Sampling the Green World" by Stuessy
     and Sohmer
  Eva Wallander (Oleaceae)
  etc.

Cheers & thanks again for the examples.
Anita
