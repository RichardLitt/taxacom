                          Museum Technician

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture announces a PENDING SEARCH for one Museum Technician, GS5 (9),
at its location at the National Museum of Natural History, Washington, DC.
The position will support a broad range of activities in research on
Diptera.  The vacancy is a permanent, full-time position. The official
opening date for applications has not been set but is expected shortly.

For additional information, interested applicants should contact:
