Vacancy announcement

A position of 'assistent' (junior scientific officer)is vacant at the
herbarium (department vascular plants) of the National Botanic Garden of
Belgium (BR). The job will consist of taxonomic research on tropical
(especially African) angiosperms.
The position is open to botanists or agronomists (EU citizens).
Applications should be sent before 26 November 1998.

Further information and/or a fax copy of the announcement in the Official
Journal of Belgium can be asked to Prof. E. Robbrecht.
