Thanks to all who provided examples of research that
has utilized herbarium specimens for DNA/RNA etc extracts.

researchers have borrowed our specimens for such use and
thus I was unsure just how often this has been done (I'm
certainly glad it is).  The information will come in
handy for upcoming presentations regarding herbarium usage.
Thanks again.
