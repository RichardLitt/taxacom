1997 
Postdoctoral position


Molecular systematics


Department of Plant Sciences, University of Oxford (U.K.)

and

Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute, Panama



A postdoctoral research position funded by the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation is available immediately for
two years to work on the molecular systematics of tropical epiphytes.  Candidates should have experience in
the use of molecular techniques and their application to phylogenetic and taxonomic questions.  The person
appointed will be required to spend time at both the University of Oxford (U.K.) and STRI, Panama.

Applications, including a c.v. and the names of three referees, should be sent as soon as possible to Professor
J.A.C. Smith, Department of Plant Sciences, University of Oxford, South Parks Road, Oxford, OX1 3RB,
U.K., from whom further details can be obtained.  Informal enquiries are also welcome (tel. 01865 275009;
fax 01865 275074; e-mail andrew.smith at plants.ox.ac.uk).
