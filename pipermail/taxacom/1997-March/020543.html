Tengo el gusto de anunciar, que desde el 20 de avril 1997 yo
soy el Director del Herbario de CEAMISH en Cuernavaca, Mexico
(acronimo de Index Herbariorum: HUMO). Unos de mis metos como
director sera' incrementar y diversificar la coleccion del
herbario. Asimismo, deseo establecer programas de intercambio
con otros herbarios, en otras partes de America del Norte
igualmente con en otras partes del mundo. La region cerca de
Cuernavaca es principalmente de selva tropical decidua, pero
ademas existe vegetacion de pino, encino, y oyamel, cerca de
la Universidad de Morelos. Si Ud. quiere establecer un
programa de intercambio con nosotros, favor de escribirme.
Podre ser localizado en mi direccion en Tucson hasta el 15 de
avril.

It is with great pleasure that I announce my appointment as
Herbarium Director, effective 20 April 1997, of CEAMISH in
Cuernavaca, Mexico (Index Herbariorum acronym: HUMO). One of
my chief goals as director will be increasing the size and
diversity of the herbarium collection. We will therefore seek
to establish exchange programs with other herbaria not only
in other parts of North America but in other parts of the
world as well. The region around Cuernavaca is primarily
deciduous tropical forest, but several mountain ranges
covered with pine-oak forest are located within a short
distance of the University of Morelos. If you would like to
establish an exchange program, please do not hesitate to
contact me. Alternatively, if you are seeking an institution
for deposition of vouchers or duplicates of work done in Mexico
or elsehwere, I can assure you that any specimens sent to
HUMO will be well-cared for and put to productive use.
   I can be reached at my Tucson address until about
15 April.
