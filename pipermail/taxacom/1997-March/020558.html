The Canadian Museum of Nature and Queen's University are running a Summer
course on biodiversity and systematics (July 1997).  The course consists of
two modules (a short general overview) and an optional followup module
offering more in-depth study and hands on experience.

For more info:

Web site is at http://biology.queensu.ca/~biodiv

or e-mail a request for info to biodiv at biology.queensu.ca
