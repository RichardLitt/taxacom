I thank Tom Lammers for his eloquent locution on the subject
of electronic publication. We are entering a new age of
information transfer; let us make sure we get the rules right.

biologists will chuckle over the discussions we are having
today. Let us hope so. Let us hope they do not curse us
for making a royal mess of things.
