> As much as I hat to ask the question, is there a mailing list like this
> for DNA sequencing/ PCR type issues?  I did a quick google search that

Have a look at the BIOSCI bionet Newsgroup Archives. There are several
molecular-oriented newsgroups there, and it is easy to dip into a group
and see how active it is. If nothing else it will give some leads.

http://www.bio.net/archives.html

BIOSCI bionet was an internet pioneer in biology newsgroups. It used to be
possible to subscribe to a group also by e-mail as well as with Usenet
clients and via WWW, but I am not sure about the situation now for all
groups. However, the list I moderate there (ANNELIDA) still runs by
e-mail.
