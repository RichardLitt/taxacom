The uBio project at the MBL/WHOI Library has a full-time position for a
taxonomist with some computer skills to assist in the development of
biodiversity informatics services.  The position is labeled
"Bioinformatics taxonomist" but is really a biodiversity/biological
informatics position.

The full posting is listed on the MBL Human Resources website at:

http://www.mbl.edu/inside/what/human_resources/job_search.php?
func=detail&par=job_id=373

Inquiries can be directed to DJ Patterson (dpatterson at mbl.edu).
