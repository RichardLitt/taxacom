For those who may be interested, I have just published a guest editorial
entitled "The information age and agricultural entomology" in Bulletin of
Entomological Research 83: 471-474, 1993. It deals with various aspects of
the impact of the information age (esp. Internet) on museum collections,
services to agriculture and other users, the ways information is
distributed, etc.  I'll be happy to send copies by snail mail or email.
