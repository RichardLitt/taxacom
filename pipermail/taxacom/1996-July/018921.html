1996 
The American Society of Plant Taxonomists web site has moved to:

http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/aspt/aspthome.htm

The new site, under development by the ASPT's Internet Communications
Committee, provides access to member-generated materials from servers
at California State University (Stanislaus), the Smithsonian
Institution (Botany), the University of Michigan (MICH), and Texas
A&M University.  These include searchable listings of the ASPT
program for this year's meeting and the membership directory.  Future
development of the site will be driven by membership input mediated
by email links to committee members from the site.
