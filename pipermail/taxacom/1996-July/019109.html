You can search the American Society of Plant Taxonomists membership
directory at
        http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/aspt/aspthome.htm

to find individuals who work on Asteraceae.  You can get their addresses
and, if they have provided it, their e-mail addresses and telephone
numbers.  Their e-mail addresses are hot-linked so you can click on their
address to send them an e-mail.  The search will only find individuals
who have provided information to identify their research interests.  It's
not a discussion list, but it may be a good starting point.
