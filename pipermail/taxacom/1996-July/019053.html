                        Systematic Entomologist

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture announces a pending search for a research position on tortricoid
moths (Lepidoptera) at its location at the National Museum of Natural
History, Washington, D.C.  The position will be for a full-time, permanent
Research Entomologist, GS11/12/13.  The official opening date for
applications has not been set but is expected shortly.

Interested applicants should contact Dr. Manya B. Stoetzel, Research Leader,
Systematic Entomology Laboratory, Bldg. 046, BARC-West, Beltsville, MD
20705-2350 (301-504-5183, FAX 301-504-6482) or Dr. Ronald W. Hodges
(202-382-1778; FAX 202-786-9422) for additional information.

                          Museum Technician

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture announces a pending search for two Museum Technicians, GS5 (7),
at its location at the National Museum of Natural History, Washington, D.C.
The positions will support a broad range of activities in research on
insects.  Both vacancies are for two-year term appointments with the
possibility of renewal for an additional two years.  The official opening
date for applications has not been set but is expected shortly.

Interested applicants should contact Dr. Manya B. Stoetzel, Research Leader,
Systematic Entomology Laboratory, Bldg. 046, BARC-West, Beltsville, MD
20705-2350 (301-504-5183, FAX 301-504-6482) or Dr. Thomas J. Henry
(202-382-1780; FAX 202-786-9422) for additional information.
