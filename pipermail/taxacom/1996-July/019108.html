ANNOUNCEMENT - Call for Papers

Third International Triticeae Symposium

May 4-8, 1997
Aleppo, SYRIA
 -------------------------------------------

At the Second International Triticeae Symposium, Logan, Utah, USA, June
20-24, 1994, the participants unanimously accepted a proposal for the
Third
International Triticeae Symposium to be held in Aleppo, Syria. The
International Plant Genetic Resources Institute, West Asia and North
Africa
Regional Office (IPGRI-WANA) and the International Center for
Agricultural
Research in the Dry Areas (ICARDA) will serve as hosts.

The purposes of the symposium are: (1) to exchange the latest scientific
information and advancements related to annual and perennial Triticeae
species; and (2) to promote the exchange of ideas for developing
coordinated
or collaborative research.

Program

Plenary lectures will be delivered by invited speakers. Scientific papers

may be presented by registered participants orally or as posters.
Volunteered papers can be submitted in one of the following topics:

1.   Evolutionary Genomic Relationships in the Triticeae
2.   Biodiversity and Biogeography
3.   Genetic Resources and Core Collections in Breeding and Research
4.   Evaluation and Pre-breeding of Cereals and Forages
5.   Quality and Utilization


Facilities

The Symposium will be held on the campus of ICARDA, 35 km south of
Aleppo,
Syria. Blocks of rooms will be reserved for  Symposium participants in
hotels in Aleppo at a special rate. Complimentary daily bus transport to
ICARDA will be provided by the sponsors. A special form for
"Accommodation"
will be mailed to those who return the "First Call for Papers".


Local Climate

Aleppo, situated in northern Syria, has a comfortable Mediterranean
climate.
In May, the mid-days can be quite warm, but the evenings and nights are
usually pleasant.


Transportation

Participants can fly directly to Aleppo from Europe or they can fly to
Damascus and drive to Aleppo. For those participants arriving in
Damascus,
ICARDA will provide ground transportation to Aleppo, as needed.


Registration

A registration fee of US$ 250  entitles a participant to: attendance at
all
scientific programs, a copy of the abstracts and programs, a copy of the
Symposium Proceedings (to be published early 1998), refreshments during
breaks, and a banquet.
