1996 
> Does any taxacomer know of a simple way to look for
>           available generic names?

Zoological Record is working on this and hopes to have a name index
on the www shortly - this will search all names used in Zoological
Record volumes 115- 132, plus names for other non-animal groups
provided by collaborating organisations.  Further phases of this project
include the addition of other name data such as Neave. A notice will
 be sent to Taxacom when the name index  is publicly available.
