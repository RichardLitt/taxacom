A new discussion list, BIOCODE, has been set up for the Draft
BioCode, prospective International Rules for the Scientific Names of
Organisms.


For More Information:

Send email to LISTSERV at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU with this one-line message--

INFO BIOCODE


To Subscribe:

Send email to LISTSERV at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU with this one-line message--

SUBSCRIBE BIOCODE Yourfirstname Yourlastname
