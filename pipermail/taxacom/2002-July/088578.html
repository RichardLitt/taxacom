On behalf of the Nationaal Herbarium Nederland (NHN), IAPT and The Linnean
Society of London, we would like to invite you to attend the symposium
Plant species-level systematics: patterns, processes and new applications.
This symposium will be held in Leiden, The Netherlands from 13 till 15
November 2002. Registration is still possible as we have not reached the
maximum capacity of participants yet.

Topics will include Plant Species Radiations, Molecular Evolution in Time
and Space, Multiple Genomes and Identification and Diagnostics and brings
together some of the world's leading scientists.

Sessions will be devoted to phyletic radiations and associated key
innovations, phylogeography, biogeography, molecular dating, plant
hybridisation and polyploidisation and new genomic tools for identification
of plants.

Please visit our website (http://www.nationaalherbarium.nl/symposium2002/)
for more information and recent updates in the programme. If you have
questions or comments, please email us at symposium2002 at nhn.leidenuniv.nl.

sincerely,
Barbara Gravendeel & Pieter Pelser
members of the symposium organising committee
