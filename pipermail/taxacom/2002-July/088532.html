Personally, I believe that insistence on precise definitions is often
unhelpful and at base illusory; but a common set of agreed terms (or an

starting a thread on the term "character", I recommend a glance at my own
discussion of the matter - Sys. Zool. 34:229-233, 1985 - that is, if your
library goes back to the Dark Ages!
