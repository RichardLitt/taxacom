Searching for "staining well" is likely to go nowhere these days.  ("my timber is staining well")

Another name which might be more successful in English would be "solid watch glass", although it may now have dropped out of use.
Once upon a time biologists used dishes resembling antique pocket-watch covers to hold their specimens.
Hence "solid watch glass" for a more stable dish in the form of the dished glass block that is sought.
