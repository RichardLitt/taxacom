On 11/5/25 7:50 PM, John Grehan via Taxacom wrote:
> Does anyone know of a standard terminology for this kind of blade shape?

The standard form I've seen (as in "seen *repeatedly*") is securiform.

https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wiktionary.org%2Fwiki%2Fsecuriform&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Ce9e13740868b49393de908de1d4f20dd%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638980422843176294%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C80000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=0crdkZlbtkW66WPH0cWvYI33Ut3THB0WvcP3v%2FD6RL4%3D&reserved=0

Most often I've seen this used to describe the terminal segment of 
antennae or palpi. Any coleopterist should be familiar with it.

The only other term I know, which I've only seen used in species 
epithets, is "dolabratus", which would Anglicize as "dolabrate".
