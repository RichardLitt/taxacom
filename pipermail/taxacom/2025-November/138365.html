Mainly a curiosity question. Describing a valva on moth genitalia that has
a narrow, concave shaped stem then expands distally to form a broad blade
with convex distal edge and sharp corners each end - sort of like half of a
double headed battle axe as illustrated here il_fullxfull.2909846231_nrxg.jpg

Does anyone know of a standard terminology for this kind of blade shape?
Even if there is, it might not be practical if it's not familiar or
descriptive enough, or is too idiosyncratic. I looked over a lot of leaf

axes, but not for the shape of axe blades separate from the axe as a whole..
