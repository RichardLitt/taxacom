Greetings,

perhaps someone familiar with latin may be able to review a Google
translation (with my interpretation) I did for a brief Latin description of
a moth to see if I may have missed any precision. Below the original and my
translation. In particular, word 'maris' is problematic as I get that to
mean 'sea' but it refers to the filiform antennae (which is OK - the
antenna is thin, threadlike). The original description did not illustrate
the moth, but I now have that if anyone needs to see it to help assess my
translation.

Many thanks,
John Grehan

antennis flavescentibus; alias anticis supra fuscis fascia transversa
testacea, albo-marginata, oblqua, postice interrupta et erga basin directa
ibique nigrounipuntata; posticis fuso-brunneis. Long. al., exp. 30. mill.
Hab, in Caffraria orientali. Mus. Holm. Antennae maris filiformis.

Antennae yellow; otherwise anteriorly above the dark transverse shell-like
fascia testacea, white-margined, oblique, interrupted posteriorly and
directed towards the base [of the wing] there also punctured by a black
spot; posteriorly dark-brown. Wing expanse 30 mm. Location, in Caffraria
orientali [Eastern Cape, South Africa]. Mus. Holm. Antennae *maris*
filiform.
