Just a small correction. The translations of the article of Knapp, McNeill &
Turland have been published not only in PhytoKeys, but also in the inaugural
issue of MycoKeys today: http://www.pensoft.net/journals/mycokeys/issue/1/

Besides, MycoKeys publlished also the article of David Hawkesworth: A new
dawn for the naming of fungi: impacts of decisions made in Melbourne in July
2011 on the future publication and regulation of fungal
names<http://www.pensoft.net/journals/mycokeys/article/2062/a-new-dawn-for-the-naming-of-fungi-impacts-of-decisions-made-in-melbourne-in-july-2011-on-the-future-publication-and-reg>which
analyses the changes from the viewpoint of their importance for
mycology.

More in the assotiated press release: Revolutionary changes to the Botanical
Code published in 16 journals and 5
languages<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2011-09/pp-rct091411.php>
