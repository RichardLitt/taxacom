Chris Thomson wrote :

"We will miss Peter ..."


Yes, we will.  Peter had a keen mind and quick wit, combined with 
willingness to respond thoughtfully to his critics.  When I first met 
him (I was a new Ph.D. with little experience) he made me feel most 
comfortable and we were immediately able to strike up conversations on a 
variety of subjects, not just our interest in numerical taxonomy  (sensu 
lato).  His talks and addresses at the NT meetings were delightful and 
filled with insight.

I am deeply saddened to learn of his passing.

Sincerely,

Dick Jensen
