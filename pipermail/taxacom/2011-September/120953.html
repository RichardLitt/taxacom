Dear colleagues,

We are glad to inform you that the Festschrift

Advances in the Systematics of Fossil and Modern Insects: Honouring
Alexandr Rasnitsyn. ZooKeys 130

http://www.pensoft.net/journals/zookeys/ 

has been published today, September 24, on Alexandr’s 75th birthday.

The 542-page volume is devoted to studies of very diverse groups of
insects, from Carboniferous palaeopterans to amber and modern wasps, the
scope as broad as the research interests of the hero of the day.

We wish him good health, happiness, and new discoveries.

Editors, contributors, colleagues, and friends
