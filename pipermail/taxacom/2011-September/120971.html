2011 
TO BE DISTRIBUTED 
TO ANY POTENTIALLY INTERESTED AND QUALIFIED GRADUATE STUDENTS AND RECENT POST 
DOCS
Please note that 
Science Branch of the Canadian Department of Fisheries and Oceans based in St 
John's Newfoundland is still searching for two Post Docs to commence work in 
early 2012.
Research will be on 
the development of management strategy evaluations for important shrimp and 
groundfish stocks in the Northwest Atlantic.
The positions are for 
one year extendable to three years at standard Canadian post-doc salary levels.  
Travel costs at beginning and end of appointment will be 
covered.
Successful candidates 
will gain considerable experience working as a fisheries scientist in a federal 
government laboratory as well as participation in the work of an 
international RFMO (NAFO). 
These positions are 
open to Canadians and non-Canadians with a suitable background in quantitative 
fisheries analysis and statistics.
For further 
information please contact Peter.Shelton at dfo-mpo.gc.ca
