Just another anthropological aside: The recent allusion to the suffering of
the pangolin is poignant, in that pangolins are considered holy by certain
African secret societies, because of their passivity under attack. Their
response to danger makes them appear to be delivering themselves up
willingly to death. (I am not making this up).
