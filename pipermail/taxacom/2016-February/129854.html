There's not much in it. But it's known 'holotype' is definitely wrong as a
label , and the revisor should have a free choice of which is the best
syntype specimen for a lectotype. Whatever happens, the history should be
reported that specimen x was previously museum-labelled (background to
this unknown) as the holotype although a holotype was not designated in
the publication.
