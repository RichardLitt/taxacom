Let's keep this simple, Geoff! The "by whom" is a side issue. Even if a holotype is designated in an original description, one might still not know who added the holotype label to a specimen, but one assumes it was the original author. The main point here is that a lectotype should be designated in accordance with an unpublished holotype *all other things being equal*, regardless of who added the holotype label.

Stephen
