There's probably a book in that subject (if not already published) on the
untimely ends or timely escapes of naturalists. This story reminded me of
J.D. Hooker's fortunate temporary incarceration, and the less fortunate end
to Nickolai Romanoff who was murdered by the Bolsheviks (which came to my
attention with the moth Triodia mlokossevitschi (Romanoff, 1884). No doubt
there are many other such stories.
