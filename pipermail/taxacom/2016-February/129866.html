When I said: They are syntypes, but the one labelled as holotype should be chosen as lectotype (if a lectotype is to be designated).

I didn't say anything that was wrong or misleading! I didn't say that it was a rule (or recommendation) in the Code. I just offered my opinion that one should, all other things being equal, designate the unpublished holotype as lectotype (if a lectotype is needed). If you read more into it than was there, that's your bad. Taxacom is, after all, a forum for opinions.

Stephen
