Karl does make the good point that there may be no need to designate a lectotype in some (many?) cases. Yet, I still see supposedly bona fide taxonomists publishing papers that consist of little more than exhaustive lectotype designations in advance of taxonomic revision of the group, as if it is an end in itself!
