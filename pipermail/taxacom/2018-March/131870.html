Dear colleagues

The ICES Annual Science Conference will be held this year in Hamburg, 
Germany, from 24-27 September .

The submission deadline is March 19.

As convener of the session A: *Mesopelagic ecosystems: fish and 
invertebrate population biomass and biodiversity, and role in carbon 
flux,* I would be very happy if you could consider submitting a summary 
for this session.

For more information on this session: 
http://www.ices.dk/news-and-events/asc/asc2018/Pages/Theme-session-A.aspx

Subjects that can be presented here:

Taxonomy of the mesopelagic community;

Trophic ecology;

Abundance and biomass of mesopelagic;

Reproductive biology of taxa pretensive to the mesopelagic community; Etc.

Consider submitting some work and pass this ad on to any colleague you 
think might be interested in.

Greetings,

Antonina.
