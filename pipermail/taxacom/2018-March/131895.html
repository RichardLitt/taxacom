2018 
In case of interest to any listers who are not aware of it, the Journal of
Threatened Taxa provides a potentially nice publication venue as it will
publish taxonomic articles, has no page charges, and is open access at
http://threatenedtaxa.org/index.php/JoTT

While most articles are from the Indian region, the potential scope is
global I think. My own experience of the journal was fairly good, some
delay with the review process, but otherwise worked out well. Below is a
list of articles from the latest issue:

*The status of Nepal’s mammals*
*-- *Rajan Amin, Hem Sagar Baral, Babu Ram Lamichhane, Laxman Prasad
Poudyal, Samantha Lee, Shant Raj Jnawali, Krishna Prasad Acharya, Gopal
Prasad Upadhyaya, Megh Bahadur Pandey, Rinjan Shrestha, Dipesh Joshi,
Janine Griffiths, Ambika Prasad Khatiwada & Naresh Subedi, Pp. 11361–11378
*DOI**:* http://doi.org/10.11609/jott.3712.10.3.11361-11378

*The bat fauna (Mammalia: Chiroptera) of the United Arab Emirates: a review
of published records and museum specimens with conservation notes*
-- J. Judas, Gabor Csorba & Petr Benda, Pp. 11379–11390
*DOI**:* http://doi.org/10.11609/jott.3096.10.3.11379-11390
