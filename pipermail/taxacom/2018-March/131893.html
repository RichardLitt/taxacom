The deadline for abstract submissions for the upcoming joint meeting of SPNHC (Society for the Preservation of Natural History Collections) and Biodiversity Information Standards (TDWG) is Monday 26 March at 23:59 UTC. The call for abstracts and more about the meeting may be found https://tdwg.github.io/conferences/2018/call-for-abstracts
