Dear friends,
I am happy to say that our journal Modern Phytomorphology
<http://ojs.phytomorphology.org/index.php/MP/about/submissions#authorGuidelines>
starting from this year was included in Clarivate Analytics (former Thomson
Reuters department) ESCI database and is also available in WoS.
Hence, I would like to thank all who contributed and helped with journal
development and also I invite botanists to publish your scientific outcomes
in our journal. Just want to remind that all publications are totally free
of charges and are open access on line.

Best regards, Andriy.
