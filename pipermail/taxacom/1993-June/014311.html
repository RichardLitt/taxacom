  29: Undetermined Rainforest Plants (herbs)
  By: M BALA SUBRAMANIAM  [481]
Date: 17:21  6/ 4/93

To All

I am a phytochemist for a japanese multinational drug co. involve in new
drug design based on natural models (secondary metabolites).
As a personal collection I do have vast numbers of still undetermined
plant spp. obtained on my regular forest expeditions in south east asian
rainforest, many are still not determined yet or unable to obtain even
provisional determination/indentification from the botanical authorities
in south east asia.
As a personal hobby i have created callus from these spp.

I would like to share these specimens with those who keen or interested
in these spp. for their projects, etc.
Those interested, i can help in any ways, as an individual to collect
specific plant spp. etc, too.
Those keen leave msg. for 481, i will log on usually two-three months
regularly. Excuse me for mistakes, as i am new to bbs communication
regards
