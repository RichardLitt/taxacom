The Draft Report of the U.S. Systematics Agenda 2000 Steering Committee
entitled "Charting the Biosphere" is available on the Taxacom FTP Server
(huh.harvard.edu) as files:

/pub/standards/asc/systematics_2000/sa2k.wp51 (WordPerfect 5.1)
/pub/standards/asc/systematics_2000/sa2k.asc  (ASCII text)
/pub/standards/asc/systematics_2000/sa2k.ps   (PostScript)


The document is also available in the Biodiversity and Biological
Collections Gopher (huh.harvard.edu) under:

  Standards Organizations and Reports/
    Association of Systematics Collections/
      Systematics Agenda 2000/


The authors of the report invite community review and comment.
