1994 
Celebrate Spring at the Central Oregon Plant Show



*       Displays of live native wildflowers

*       Plant identification seminar

*       Slide shows at 3:30 and 6:30

*       Ethical plant collecting

*       A wealth of information about our local flora

*       Experts available for identifying your unknown
        plants

Guests include:

*       David Wagner, Northwest Botanical Institute

*       Maret Pujet, Sisters District Ecologist, Deschutes National Forest

*       Cindi O'Neil, Forest Ecologist, Deschutes National Forest

*       Dr. Stu Garrett, Native Plant Society of Oregon

*       Howie Brounstein, Botanist/Herbalist, C & W Herbs, Inc.

Tuesday, May 24            3:00-9:00 PM

at the Central Oregon Environmental Center
16 NW Kansas Ave., Bend, Oregon, USA

Free Admission


Sponsored by the Deschutes National Forest Wildflower Week
and the High Desert Chapter of the Native Plant Society.

For more information contact Howie Brounstein at C&W Herbs,(503)-389-6348.
