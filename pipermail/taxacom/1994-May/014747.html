In reply to Stephen Gaimari's assertion that the zoological collections
use the codens provided by Arnett & al. (1993), this situation could
perhaps be true in North America, but it is not in Europe (at least
in the entomology area I know). I would bet on a World mandatory list
similar to that of herbaria. Madrid Museum coden is MNMS in Arnett,
and I am afraid nobody in Europe will use it, being the commonest
abbreviations MNCN or MCNM. On the other hand, public collections
listed in this work for Europe are very incomplete.
