> Can one of our Swedish friends (or anyone how knows) tell me what
> the current institution code is for the:
> 'Royal Natural History Museum, Stockholm'
> This was its English name in 1915. Please inform me if the name
> has changed as well as the correct Swedish name.

The current English name for "Naturhistoriska riksmuseet" is the "Swedish
Museum of Natural History". The acronyms for both these forms are used as
codes:  NRM or SMNH.
