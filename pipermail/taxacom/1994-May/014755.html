Regarding Meredith's request to locate a previously posted address:

One way to find the address of the "RangeMapper" contact is to
send email to listserv at harvarda.bitnet (or listserv at harvarda.harvard.edu)
[not to taxacom!} with the following message:

//     JOB  Echo=No
Database Search DD=MIP
//MIP  DD   *
search (rangemapper)                in TAXACOM  since 93/11/01
print all
/*
//theend EOJ

You could substitute any boolean term(s) in lieu of "rangemapper" to find
other stuff.
Peter
