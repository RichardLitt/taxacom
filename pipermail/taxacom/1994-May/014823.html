The Berkeley Museum of Paleontology type species on-line catalogs lists
localities to county or similar reference only, because of the market in
fossils.  A further request will get locality data, but each request must
be confirmed as a legitimate, usually scientific, query.  If we do not know
the requestor, we ask that they supply a reference and statement of their
problem.  We treat locality requests in much the same way as specimen
loans.

We have had few requests, probably because the information is available in
publications anyway.
