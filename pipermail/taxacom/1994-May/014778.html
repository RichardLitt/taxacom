**********Position Announcement**********

           Executive Director
Santa Barbara Museum of Natural History
       Santa Barbara, California

Renowned natural science museum seeks dynamic individual to provide
leadership and vision to a 46 member staff, board of trustees, and
volunteers. Responsibilities include fund raising, developing long range
plans, implementing institutional goals, establishing and maintaining
contacts and visibility in the community, and supervising day to day
operations. Must have demonstrated fund raising abilities, administrative
experience, and budgeting and financial management expertise. With annual
budget of $2.7 million, the museum presents extensive education and outreach
 programs to a
diverse community, conducts research, and maintains significant
collections in anthropology, vertebrate and invertebrate zoology, botany,
and geology. Facility includes the 11-acre main campus in historic
Mission Canyon and a Sea Center on Santa Barbara's Stearns Wharf. Salary
commensurate with experience. Send letter, resume, and at least three
references (board, staff, donor) by May 23 to:

Museum Management Consultants
559 Pacific Ave.
Suite 8
San Francisco, CA 94133
