On 14/05/2013 21:32, Stephen Thorpe wrote:
> Of course we must not confuse taxonomy with nomenclature here! 
> Anyone's taxonomy can be ignored if it is insufficiently backed up 
> with scientific evidence. Nobody's nomenclature can be ignored if it 
> is validly published. A publication is "scientic literature", or not, 
> depending on its content. If it tackles taxonomy, then it is by 
> definition INSIDE the scientific literature.

Those are indeed the stipulations of the Code. However, as you will see 
from the last paragraphs of the paper, there is an increasing body of 
opinion in herpetological systematics that is no longer willing to see 
the entire future of herpetological nomenclature handed over to a very 
small group of people who create names on an industrial scale in the 
hope that a small percentage will then be validated by real evidence and 
have to be used.
