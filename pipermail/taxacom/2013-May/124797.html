This is not good form (see Rec. 50D) but the intent is clear enough:
    C. Papyrus Auct. non L. ; Clarke, l. c., p. 374 (1901) p. p. n. t.
    qu. Mozambique Dist. (Whyte).
reads as something like
    C. papyrus as used by Clarke, for a plant as found in the
    Mozambique District

There is no such thing as a "non-type", the intent is to refer to a
part of the taxon (in this case a species) that does not include the
type of the name of the taxon.

I hope this helps?
Paul
