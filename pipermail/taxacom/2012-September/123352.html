2012 
59th Annual Systematics Symposium


Missouri Botanical Garden


12-13 October 2012


(With support from the National Science Foundation)

 

China

To celebrate the Flora of China project

Organizing committee: P. Mick Richardson and Nicholas Turland

 

Friday 7:30 - 9:30 p.m. Informal mixer in Ridgway Center.

 

Saturday 8:30 a.m. - 8:30 p.m. Talks in Ridgway Center.

 

John MacKinnon (EU-China Biodiversity Program) China's floral richness:
Is it safe?

Jianguo "Jack" Liu (Michigan State University) Giant pandas and
conservation in China

Jun Wen (Smithsonian Institution) Evolution of plant diversity in China

David Boufford (Harvard University) & Hang Sun (Kunming Institute of
Botany) Hengduan Mountains: not all biodiversity is tropical

Sushma Reddy (Loyola University Chicago) Phylogeny and biogeography of
birds of China

Pu Huang (Washington University in St. Louis) The domestication of rice

Jan Salick (MBG) Evening speaker: Global warming and mountain plants

 

Registration must be accompanied by a $50.00 registration fee, which
also covers the cost of refreshments at the Friday mixer and lunch (but
not dinner) on Saturday. The cost of the dinner on Saturday is an
additional $75.00. Information on local hotels and motels will be
available to registrants. No refunds will be granted after 24 September.
There is no guarantee of food being available if you register after 24
September. For electronic registration and payment go to
http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/research/symposium/welcome.shtml.

 

P. Mick Richardson (mick.richardson at mobot.org, Tel: 314 577 5176; Fax:
314 577 0820).
