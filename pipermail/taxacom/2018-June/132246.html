Exactly. Why is 'identity'/'diversity' always assessed by gender and race?
It seems a very undiverse way of measuring diversity. In my experience,
culture and age are usually better indicators of how someone will
behave. E.g. the African students here in New Zealand who were born here
are just like typical NZ young people and very different from students in
Africa.
