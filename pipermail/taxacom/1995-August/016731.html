1995 
                          Netscape Training Manual

  I have developed a succinct training manual that quickly teaches the
basic components of Netscape.  Written for both students and teachers to
use, The Netscape Road Map manual is designed to simply lead a new user
through all the major components of the Netscape 1.1 browser.  Also
available with the manual is a disk with 30 URL's favorite education
sites on the internet and a series of 30 overheads to be used while
teaching from the manual.

  Also available,

                             The GINA ROAD MAP

  I have also developed a manual that quickly teaches the basic
components of GINA.  Again, this manual is written for both students and
teachers to use.  The GINA manual covers the major features of GINA 2.0
and includes forms that can be used as overheads for direct instruction
of the software.

     If you are interested in either, please send me a message
indicating which one or both to:
