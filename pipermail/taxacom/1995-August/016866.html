On Fri, 25 Aug 1995 13:42:25 -0500, T.K. Wilson wrote:

>Alan Harvey wrote:
>> ... could someone explain the difference between
>> "fixation" and "preservation"?
>In museum
>collections "preservation" is only required.  However, if microscope
>slides are to be made of the tissue, then "fixation" is required
>first as the fixing/preservation fluids interact with subsequent
>staining.

I and many others will disagree.  For curation of vertebrates there is a
two part process, fixation and storage in a preservative.  Depending on the
use of the material the storage material may or may not be the same.  See
papers on the BBC gopher under ASIH Curation muse.bio.cornell.edu
