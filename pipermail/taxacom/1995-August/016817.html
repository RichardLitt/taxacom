The botanists of Texas A&M invite you to experience an educational
and enjoyable (virtual) field trip through a unique bog system in
East Texas.  Don your rubber boots and join us at:

http://csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/flynnbog/FB1.HTML

This tour works best with Netscape 1.1 or better.  You may see a few
"unknown image" markers; these are placeholders for images we are
waiting for.  Please let us know if you experience any other
problems.
