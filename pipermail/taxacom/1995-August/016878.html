1995 
                     Vertebrate Population Biologist
                   University of California at Berkeley

Applications are invited for a tenure-track position in animal population
biology at the Assistant Professor - Assistant Curator level in the
Department of Integrative Biology and the Museum of Vertebrate Zoology.
Preference will be given for a person with interests in mammals and/or
birds, with a focus on genetical and evolutionary approaches to
populations, life history evolution, behavior, or physiological ecology.
The appointee will be expected to develop a strong research program, to
teach in appropriate course, and to undertake curatorial duties.  This
position involves 50% service in the Museum and offers an 11 month
appointment.  In addition to its important collections the Museum operates
a field station and shared laboratory facilities.  Curatorial duties will
be commensurate with the experience and interests of the candidate and
relate to the collection or other museum activities.

Applications must be posted by Nov. 1, 1995, and the appointment will
begin July 1, 1996.  Submit a curriculum vitae, three letters of
recommendation, and a statement of research and teaching goals and
curatorial experience to:

Chair, Search Committee
Department of Integrative Biology
3060 Valley Life Sciences Building
University of California
Berkeley, CA 94720-3140.

The University of California is an affirmative action/equal opportunity
employer.
