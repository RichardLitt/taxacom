This picture is not good enough to identify the fungus. However, for
identification, hymenial (lower) and upper surfaces must be seen; are
there pores, folds, ridges, spines?? Anyway, it is neither Pycnoporus nor
Pycnaporellus. There is some similarity with Phlebia s.l. (of corticioid
fungi).
