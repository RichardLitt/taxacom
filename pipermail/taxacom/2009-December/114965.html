Public Call for Evidence
Council of Canadian Academies? Expert Panel on the State and Trends of Biodiversity Science in Canada
 
The Council of Canadian Academies (<http://www.scienceadvice.ca>www.scienceadvice.ca) has convened an Expert Panel to conduct an assessment of: 
?The state and trends of biodiversity science in Canada: Are we equipped to understand the challenges of our biodiversity resources?? 
This assessment has been requested by the Minister of Canadian Heritage on behalf of the Canadian Museum of Nature, and is supported by other members of the Federal Biodiversity Information Partnership.  The charge to the Panel focuses specifically on the state of taxonomic and biosystematics research in Canada ? research that discovers, distinguishes, identifies and classifies species of organisms. These areas of research support effective approaches to biodiversity conservation, the maintenance of ecosystem services and our ability to mitigate and adapt to climate change. 
The expert panel welcomes input from interested organizations and individuals, and has launched a Public Call for Evidence to facilitate this.  For details on how to provide evidence to the panel, please visit <http://www.scienceadvice.ca/documents/%282009-12-04%29%20Biodiversity%20-%20Public%20Call%20for%20Evidence.pdf>http://www.scienceadvice.ca/documents/%282009-12-04%29%20Biodiversity%20-%20Public%20Call%20for%20Evidence.pdf
The panel is also conducting a web-based survey of taxonomic expertise in Canada, and a survey of collections, more information is available at <http://www.scienceadvice.ca/biodiversity>www.scienceadvice.ca/biodiversity
