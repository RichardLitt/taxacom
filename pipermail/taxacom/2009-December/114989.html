Dear All,

Lucid,?arguably the best interactive, matrix key software available, is celebrating the 10th anniversary of its initial release of the "Lucid Professional" identification tool by providing FREE copies of "Lucid v3.3".? Initially released in 2006, Lucid v3.3 operates on Windows98, ME, NT, 2000, XP, 2003, Vista; Mac-OSX; Linux; and Solaris - and is capable of producing keys for deployment on CD or the Internet. To take advantage of this free offer, please go to the www.lucidcentral.org?and click on "Lucid v3.3 free offer".

This offer is likely to be of particular interest to lecturers and trainers who can now provide their students with free Lucid key builders for project and field work, although anyone is eligible to download this free product.? Lucid v3.3 is forward compatible with the latest Lucid v3.5 builder, so that any keys developed using Lucid v3.3 can be imported to Lucid v3.5.
