Dear colleagues,
We are happy to invite you to publish your research articles in our
journal Modern
Phytomorphology <https://phytomorphology.org/journal/> focused on
structural botany, but also accepting papers from other fields of plant
biology.

This is a small journal which still has no impact factor, but the
publication is absolutely free and all articles are freely available
on-line. It was established by enthusiasts and for enthusiasts, for
developing of open access in plant biology, and has no any commercial
background. We are really making much to develop the journal in frames of
our limited funds. For example, in this year we rebuild our website,
started archiving of all articles in Zenodo (special thanks for this to
Donat Agosti!) and obtained DOIs. We also working on integration in OJS 3
platform and hope to finish this in next year, which will allow better
indexation and navigation. Developing of our journal was also highly
evaluated by Fulbright Foundation, which provided me with a grant to study
OA publishing in USA.

Also I will be very thankful for any proposals and comments on developing
of the journal, for your ideas and feelings!

Sincerely, Andrew.
