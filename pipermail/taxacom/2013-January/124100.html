2013 
Registration for the Seventh International Triticeae Symposium is now open. The program covers a wide range of topics, the common theme being the focus on this economically and ecologically important group of plants (think Triticum/wheat, Hordeum/barley, Elymus, and Leymus). Each of the topics will be introduced by leading researchers in the area. The meeting will take place in Chengdu, China, June 9-13, 2013.  Early registration ends on March 31. All those interested in attending are invited to register and submit an abstract at http://xms7its.xg2.ywwm.org/A/?C-1-11.Html  (English), http://xms7its.xg2.ywwm.org/ (Chinese).
