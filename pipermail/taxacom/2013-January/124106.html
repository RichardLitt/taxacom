Go to - http://east.tnc.org/reports/all_assessments.  You will find many such assessments that have shaped conservation policies and strategies across major portions of the globe.  Reports range from "Alaska - Coastal Forests and Mountains Ecoregion Assessment and Resource Synthesis" to "Yunnan Great Rivers Project Conservation Assessment" and many between!

Note that only North American assessments dwell on species occurrence data, and the majority of that data are derived from NatureServe's database.  Species-level occurrences only work well for planning if you have fairly consistent data across a geographic unit drawn from representative taxa.  That is what NatureServe specializes in relative to "imperiled" species.  Note that if you go to the NatureServe web site, most of the data you access is a distillation of the data available to subscribers and member organizations.
