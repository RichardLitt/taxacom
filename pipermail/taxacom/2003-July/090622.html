2003 
Taxonomic Databases Working Group (TDWG)
Annual Meeting, 21-26 October 2003
Instituto Gulbenkian de Ciência, Oeiras, Portugal

The provisional schedule of events for the 2003 meeting is given
below.  Please note that this schedule is subject to change - further
details will appear on the TDWG website (http://www.tdwg.org) as
they become available. Events listed on the same day will run in
parallel, except events described as plenary, which will run
sequentially.

Oct 21, Tue:
  Structure of Descriptive Data (subgroup working meeting)

Oct 22, Wed:
  Structure of Descriptive Data (subgroup working meeting)
  ABCD  Protocol section (subgroup working meeting)

Oct 23, Thu:
  Taxonomic Names (subgroup working meeting)

Oct 24, Fri:
  ABCD  Schema section (subgroup working meeting)
  DiGIR Training (space limited; pre-registration with GBIF required)
  Evening Reception

Oct 25, Sat:
  Spatial Data Workshop (by invitation)
  Structure of Descriptive Data (subgroup working meeting)
  Contributed Papers (plenary)
  TDWG Banquet

Oct 26, Sun:
  Contributed Papers (plenary)
  Review and forecast (plenary)
  Business meeting (plenary)
