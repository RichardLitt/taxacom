POSTDOCTORAL FELLOWSHIP IN TROPICAL ASIAN BOTANY
Arnold Arboretum of Harvard University

A postdoctoral fellow is sought for a National Science Foundation funded
three-year research project of the Arnold Arboretum and the Forest
Herbarium Bangkok.  The project is to document the botanical diversity of
Peninsular Thailand.  The Harvard-based position will include plant
collecting and identification, database and interactive key development for
the web, and biogeographic analysis. Strong background in systematic
botany, floristics and computing preferred. Field experience in tropical
Asia an advantage. Send CV and names of three referees to: David Middleton,
Harvard University Herbaria, 22 Divinity Avenue, Cambridge, MA 02138-2020,
USA. dmiddlet at oeb.harvard.edu.  The Arnold Arboretum of Harvard University
is an equal opportunity/affirmative action employer.
