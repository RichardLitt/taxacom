The Encyclopedia of Life (EOL) seeks applications from individuals with
experience in implementing large-scale software development projects to
lead the informatics group of the Encyclopedia of Life project. The
Director, Biodiversity Informatics Group, is a senior position with
responsibility for managing a team of developers and IT support staff
located at the Marine Biological Laboratory (MBL) in Woods Hole,
Massachusetts, USA. The successful candidate will be employed by the MBL
and will be expected to take up residence nearby. The Director of the
Biodiversity Informatics Group will work with other EOL leaders to
establish team goals, deadlines, processes, and expectations, and will
then implement and oversee the development process. In addition, s/he
will collaborate in preparing grant proposals.

 

Further information about the position and instructions for applying can
be found at the MBL web site (https://mbl.simplehire.com/).
