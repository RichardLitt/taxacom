Thanks to all who responded. I also checked in IPNI (should have 
thought of that before) which confirms that the synonymy is from the 
Japanese literature

Melilotus officinalis (L.) Lam. subsp. albus (Medik.) H.Ohashi & 
Tateishi Sci. Rep. Tohoku Univ., Ser. 4, Biol. 38(4): 319. 1984

But the consensus still seems to be that these are good species, not 
just colour forms - I've never seen any reports of intermediates or 
of interbreeding.
