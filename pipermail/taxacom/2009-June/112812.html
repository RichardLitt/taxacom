2009 
We would like to announce the first issue of the Journal of the New York
Entomological Society under its new name, Entomologica Americana. 

 

Entomologica Americana publishes original research on the taxonomy,
classification, phylogeny, biogeography, behavior and natural history of
insects and other arthropod taxa primarily from the Americas. Manuscripts
are published as research articles, shorter scientific notes or book
reviews.

 

Articles are available on-line to BioOne subscribers at
http://www.bioone.org/loi/nynt.1.

 

We accept manuscript submissions electronically through Allen Track. 

 

To submit a manuscript, click http://nyes.allentrack2.net/cgi-bin/main.plex.
