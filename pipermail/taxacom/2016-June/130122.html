Announcement

 
BASICS of TAXONOMY  5th edition

o 10- 21 October 2016 o Sven Lovén Centre for Marine Sciences, Kristineberg

Course credits: 5 ECTS

 
Course topics covered: DELTA, digital drawing, scientific illustration, scientific writing and communication, Scratchpads.

 
More info: http://taxonomytraining.eu/content/basics-taxonomy-describing-illustrating-and-communicating-biodiversity-0 <http://taxonomytraining.eu/content/basics-taxonomy-describing-illustrating-and-communicating-biodiversity-0> 

 
 
PHILOSOPHY OF BIOLOGICAL SYSTEMATICS  3rd edition

o 10-14 October 2016  Biological Station Drøbak, Norway

Course credits: 2 ECTS

 
This is a great opportunity to critically examine what is required for systematics to function as a part of scientific inquiry, and discover many of the problems with current methods.

 
More info: http://taxonomytraining.eu/content/philosophy-biological-systematics-1 <http://taxonomytraining.eu/content/philosophy-biological-systematics-1> 

 
The deadline for registering is 1 July 2016.

 
Please share this with colleagues, students, and friends you think might be interested. 

Thank you for spreading the word!
