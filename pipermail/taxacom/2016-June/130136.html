Rick and others

Another route to this info is filtering in GBIF.org for specimens with
images if they are shared from the institutions - e.g.
<http://www.gbif.org/occurrence/search?TAXON_KEY=5539859&MEDIA_TYPE=StillIm
age> for Cicuta mexicana. A new faceted search on the right hand side also
allows you to check if any type specimen images are available. Not saying
you will find more this way but it would be a quick means of getting to
the info if herbaria use the multimedia extension when publishing their
datasets to GBIF.
