Hi Jason,

You misunderstand my postings. They are intended to be an exchange of
viewpoints, not to convince anyone of anything. Each of us convince
ourselves according to the merits ascribed by each of us as individuals.
And what may convince one person may not convince another. Just look at all
those dying of COVID who still do not believe the disease exists.

As for 'simpler' models, that is not an objective criterion. What is
simpler for one is not necessarily for another. Some might, for example,
consider rafting of  iguana lizards across the Pacific from South America
to be 'simpler' than their having been part of an ancestral Pacific
metapopulation that occupied Creatcous islands within the Pacific basin
that were tectonically dislocated. Whatever 'simpler' might mean, it all
depends on context.

Cheers, John
