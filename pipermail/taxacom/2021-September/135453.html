Dear Taxacommers,


This is a gentle reminder that the Red List of Taxonomists portal
<https://red-list-taxonomists.eu/>, where taxonomy experts in the field of
entomology can register to help map and assess expertise across Europe will
remain open until 30th September 2021. The final outcome of the initiative
is to provide action points necessary to overcome the risks, preserve and
support this important scientific community.

More than 1,000 insect taxonomists - both professional and citizen
scientists - from across the European region have already signed up!

Please help us with distributing the link of the Red List of Taxonomists
<https://red-list-taxonomists.eu/> to through your taxon-based societies,
institutions and email groups.

The Red List of Taxonomists is a recently launched
<https://etendering.ted.europa.eu/cft/cft-display.html?cftId=6669> European
Commission-funded initiative by the Consortium of European Taxonomic
Facilities <https://cetaf.org/> (CETAF), the International Union for
Conservation of Nature <https://www.iucn.org/> (IUCN) and the scholarly
publisher best-known for its biodiversity-themed journals Pensoft
<https://pensoft.net/>.

Best regards,
