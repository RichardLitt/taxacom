Where I recently finished my undergrad degree, evolution is a manditory
course and is designed to be taken in the final year of the program as its
intent is to tie everything together.

While the course does focus to a degree on natural selection (Darwin,
Malthus, etc.), things such as speciation and species concepts (including
the phylogenetic species concept), mass extinctions, inter- and
intraspecific interactions and co-evolution are also discussed.  The
instructor also invites experts from other departments in to explain thigs
such as human evolution and the evolution of the universe (we can't be an
expert in everything).

Do you think that this is a better way to handle the topic of evolution?
It is not discussed as fact, theory or fiction and we are not asked to
believe it if we don't want to, but it allows for some light to be shed on
the subject.
