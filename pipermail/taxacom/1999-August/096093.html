> From: Thomas Schlemmermeyer <termites at USP.BR>
> To: Taxacom

> How are integer number data (e.g., number of articles, number of
> segments) dealt with [when translating from DELTA to Nexus format]?

They are converted to multistate characters by means of a KEY STATES
directive. There is a complete example of conversion to Nexus format in
Section 3.6.7 of the DELTA User's Guide.

Questions about details of using the DELTA programs should be sent to
DELTA-L (see http://listserv.surfnet.nl/archives/delta-l.html) or to DELTA
User Support (delta at ento.csiro.au), rather than to Taxacom.
