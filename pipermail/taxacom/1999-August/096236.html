"The Africanized Honey Bee (AHB) is a result of mating between
African bees and European honey bees of North and South America.
In 1956, a geneticist brought African queens to Brazil with the idea of
developing a superior honey bee, one more suited to tropical conditions.
Unfortunately, bees from 26 experimental colonies headed by African
queens swarmed near Sao Paulo, Brazil. The bees interbred in the wild
with the European honey bees, resulting in "Africanized" offspring. These
bees are moving northward about 100 to 300 miles per year. They have
spread throughout most of South America, Mexico, southern parts of Texas,
New Mexico, Arizona, and California. "

The source is:
http://www.ag.ohio-state.edu/~ohioline/hyg-fact/2000/2124.html
