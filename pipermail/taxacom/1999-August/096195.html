Actually, the two target states of the creationists are Texas and California 
but at a different level and for a different reason.  Public school texts for the 
two states are selected at statewide level by a single commission in each 
state.  Obviously, the publishers are very responsive to whatever 'common' 
demand the two states might have as their combined orders overwhelm any 
other orders in the U.S.  And, that, can well determine the 'bent' if not the full 
content of the texts written and published.

Thus, the creationists have been trying to get control of the boards selecting 
the texts in the two states.  It is my understanding that recently the tilt in Texas 
went (or nearly went) in that direction and that both states' textbook commissions 
are still a focus of efforts by the creationists.

The cost considerations (and, specifically, economies of scale) of the publishers 
may will be the critical factor that determines how many children in the U.S.
are ultimately to get which content.                                     Jim
