First of all, Jim, it's not clear if you are disturbed that "4.00" is not the
correctly rounded number or  that they rounded the number.   
(I'm curious...where do you round pi?)  Ah, precision
vs. results.

Now about the Nebraska legislature:  Jim, I checked P. Beckman's STORY OF PI
and on pages 174-179 he relates there are probably apocryphal accounts of
state legislatures trying to impose the Biblical Pi=3 [I Kings vii.23 and 2
Chronicles vi.2----Note, we're still on the same thread as we started!].
Beckman cannot support these stories but  relates in great detail [all 5 pages]
 how one state legislature tried in 1897 to  introduce a bill [House Bill 246, 
January 18, 1897] which would accurately define Pi and that the state had sole
 rights to its use which implies others would have to pay to use this exact definition.  
The state, by the way, was Indiana and a copy of the bill is in its archives.
