Dear Taxacomers,

I have been asked to pass on the following message.  It concerns changes to the organisational structure of the plant biosystematics and herbarium (CHR) groups at Landcare Research, New Zealand:

Over the past few years both Landcare Research and the Foundation for Research, Science and Technology (the latter being the major science funder) have reviewed the position and direction of the programme ?Nationally Significant Biological Collections? in relation to other science programmes in biosystematics.  Following these reviews, we have decided to break up the ?Collections? programme into component parts that better align to the users of the Collections.  Work that supported plant biosystematics is now associated with the programme ?Biosystematics of New Zealand Plants (from 1 July 1999).  This is aimed to ensure that the operations of the Herbarium and databases are driven by research needs.  By focussing more clearly on user needs we can establish priorities for curation and database management and better utilise the currently limited resources we have for general Herbarium management.

This process has required us to realign responsibilities and staffing in line with funding and research needs.  The following is an update on staff of the programme Biosystematics of New Zealand Plants.

We expect these changes to lead towards plant biosystematics research that increases its responsiveness to user needs and maintains high quality research.  We will also aim to expand our services to Government agencies, involvement with Botanical Societies and provision of loans to overseas Herbaria.
