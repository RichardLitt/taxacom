The term was introduced by Ax (1987) [The Phylogenetic System: The
Systematization of Living Organisms on the Basis of Their Phylogenesis.]
and has been used by many carabid beetles worker (e.g. Shpeley & Ball,
1993; and many of Ball's paper, me too for that matter).

S&B (1993:18)  State that the term is a "substitute for 'sister taxon'
or 'sister group'. The advantage of using adelphotaxon is that, being of
classical origin, it will be the same in all European languages."
