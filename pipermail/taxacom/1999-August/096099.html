As I already outlined in a private mail to John Grehan, the debate about
creationism seems rather bizarre for an outsider and requires, in my opinion,
first a deep-rooted historical and sociological understanding.

We have to face the following facts: In the USA, the separation between public
and churches is far more rigid than in some other societies (in Germany, for
example, churches and state cooperate in many areas).
Interestingly, many Americans are very christian and puritan.
Hence, the steady tentatives of some clerical circles to interfer with public
education systems.

For constitutional reasons, these disturbances cannot be done with religious
ambitions, therefore scientific motives are alleged. And, "scientific
creationism" is born.

I think evolution cannot be falsified. This should not bother us. Evolution is
about Natural History. Explaining history requires other criteria of
falsification than strict empirical laws, like those of physics.
