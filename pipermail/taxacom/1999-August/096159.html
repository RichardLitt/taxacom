The assumption of selection theory, as I understand it, is that selection
works on random variation - i.e. no selection no evolution - that mammalian
ears for example, would not evolved without differential reproduction
through selection. Orthogenesis is a model where new structures, taxa,
etc., can evolve without requiring differential survivial. The only
constraint is if such a feature is detrimental in some way with respect to
other variants, in which selection would indeed play an active role in the
outcome.

Darwin's orthogenesis was not natural selection. It was laws of growth.
