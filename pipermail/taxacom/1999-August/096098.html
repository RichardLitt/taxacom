A couple messages have mentioned random patterns. My understanding is that
random number tables are massaged so that there are no obvious patterns even
if - just through randomness - a pattern like "22222222222222222222222222"
might turn up when generating a table.

I generated five random data sets mimicking 800-bp molecular data sets with
5 taxa each. All produced single, fully resolved trees. In most, there was
at least one branch with Bremer support of 4, and in one, there was one
branch with Bremer support of 10.
The CI of all trees was around .4, similar to that of real data sets.
Clearly, real molecular data in published trees usually do support
morphological data sufficiently to have drowned out a "random pattern" that
is quite distinct, but tests are needed to evaluate molecular data that do
not.

I have a MSDOS utility that will generate (psuedo-)random data sets. Anyone
who wants a copy, let me know.
