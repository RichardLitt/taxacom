I only wanted to make comments about the nature of phylogenetic predictions.
If one agrees with Mike Dallwitz and the late Ernst Mayr and the majority of
the scientific community that predictions may be made about the past as well,
one should still consider that phylogenetic predictions are comparatively weak
predictions as the underlying laws (natural selection and common descent) are
by its nature laws which are not very predictive and which do allow a lot of
chance and unpredictability.

I will now outline why creationist predictions, if there would be any, were
complete nonsense. As God forgot to create Bacteria, we would have an
ever-increasing amount of organic waste on earth, which is not the case.
