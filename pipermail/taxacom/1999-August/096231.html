Dear All:

Martin Tribe wrote:
> As far as I can see Warbling Vireo has been split into (Eastern) Warbling
> Vireo Vireo gilvus and Western Warbling Vireo Vireo swainsoni. What I want
> to know is:
> 1. Is this an accepted split?

Now I ask:

When should a non-specialist taxonomist (e.g. a non-Vireo chap) *not*
accept a taxonomic decision made by a specialist (i.e. a Vireo girl)?
If ever? Or never? How much leeway should a taxonomist have in
"interpreting" a taxonomic action taken by someone in a taxon other
than one's own speciality?

As an example, as budding curc / beetle person I have some doubts
about the proliferation of names (?taxa) in the South African
butterfly fauna. But who am I to [gently] challenge my Lep
colleagues? And as another example, a new family of beetles was
recently "split off" from an old,  larger family: should one
willy-nilly accept the latest published opinion on such a matter?


RIAAN STALS
