I never said it was sufficient. I do maintain that it is necessary.

Lateral transfer can be used to explain any distribution of traits--even
traits that follow a pattern that could be explained by descent. Thus it is
a "non-explanation". That doesn't mean that it can't happen, and says
nothing about its importance; rather, it cannot be a key piece of any
"methodology" (to use your word, perhaps correctly :-).
