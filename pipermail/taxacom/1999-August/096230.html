        Thanks to the several of you who have responded with data, in answering
my query on the weight of a full herbarium case. Here's what the figures
seem to be:

        empty case, ca. 84" tall....245-275 lbs.
        "full" case.................415-500 lbs.

        "Full" means containing a reasonable amount of specimens, i.e, not so
tightly packed in that they are hard to get out.
