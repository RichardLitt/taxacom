So are ALL names published in e-pub journals without Zoobank registration nomina nuda before the printed copy appears?
Are journals aware of it?

This creates a date problem at least for names published in December 🙂

Let's say, e-pub by Smith appears online on December 28, 2022
Printed copy appears on January 15, 2023
So Abus Smith, 2022, gen.n is a nomen nudum and the available name is Abus Smith, 2023 ?
