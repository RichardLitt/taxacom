Dear all,

The Plant Pest Diagnostics Branch of the California Department of Food & Agriculture is seeking applicants for upcoming job openings for one or more systematic/taxonomic entomologists. Submitting your application by the due date will determine your eligibility to be considered for these upcoming positions, and any that may come up during the life of the eligible list being established. This is the only opportunity to get on this eligibility list.

This is a three part process:
1) THIS ANNOUNCEMENT is for the application to determine those meeting Minimum Qualifications
2) A Supplemental Examination will be given to those applicants who meet the Minimum Qualifications (in November or December 2022) to determine their place on the eligibility list
3) Job applications when vacancies are announced, available ONLY to those on the eligibility list

The Open Submission Period for the civil service examination application started a few months ago and is open until the final filing date.
FINAL FILING DATE: October 31, 2022.
The official Examination Bulletin is at https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calcareers.ca.gov%2FJOBSGEN%2F2FA15.PDF&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C2ed9c0f8f3f642213eb708da98136464%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C637989506440151601%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=3VI5A36Y230G9YkxcVNI2VG9rkwAwyvODClA8azOuqg%3D&reserved=0

Class/Position: Associate Insect Biosystematist.

Current Salary Range: $6,224 to $7,734 per month
[Note, besides incremental salary increases, the next promotional level is Senior Insect Biosystematist, salary range: $6,992 to $8,692 per month]

Plant Pest Diagnostics Center

We are seeking applications from highly-qualified, morphology-leaning systematic/taxonomic entomologists for full time employment as scientists in the Entomology Lab at the Plant Pest Diagnostics Center (CDFA) in Sacramento, California. Please visit https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cdfa.ca.gov%2Fplant%2Fppd%2Fentomology.html&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C2ed9c0f8f3f642213eb708da98136464%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C637989506440151601%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ASGL3vKl3lXQKBnzyE3K83Udqz0AUj0NVH5Di8nHNkc%3D&reserved=0 for an introduction to our lab and the California State Collection of Arthropods, which currently has a full-time scientific staff of 8 Senior Insect Biosystematists, 1 Senior Environmental Scientist Supervisor, 1 Environmental Program Manager I and 4 Environmental Scientists, as well as technicians, students and postdocs, representing a wide variety of taxonomic expertise across insect, other arthropod and mollusk taxa.

This process results in an eligible list (from which we must hire), which lasts for at least one year (and up to two years), so we encourage systematic and taxonomic entomologists across all taxa to apply. We are not specifically soliciting for any particular taxonomic group, so consider this open to taxonomic expertise within insect or other arthropod groups.

The primary duties for an Associate Insect Biosystematist include the following:

1) Perform timely and accurate identifications and diagnostics of the taxonomic group(s) assigned - broad familiarity and knowledge of your group is necessary for this function. Provide expert consultation to internal and external client groups.

2) Conduct research in systematics and taxonomy of insects or other arthropods (note, this does not have to be the same group as diagnostic responsibility), resulting in scientific publications.

3) Contribute to the growth and development of the California State Collection of Arthropods, our primary reference collection, currently home to nearly 3 million curated specimens, and growing rapidly.

4) Participate in the general functions and activities of the Entomology Lab and the greater Plant Pest Diagnostics Center team.
