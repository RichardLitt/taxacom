RESEARCH POSITION IN SYSTEMATIC ENTOMOLOGY

Swedish Museum of Natural History, Stockholm, Sweden
(Naturhistoriska riksmuseet)

Entomological research at the Swedish Museum of Natural History aims at =
significant contributions to the scientific knowledge and understanding =
of the diversity of terrestrial arthropods (arachnids, myriapods, =
hexapods) on Earth. Research is mainly collection based, yet allows for =
more theoretical studies.
=20
The vacant position is primarily a research position, but curation will =
be part of the responsibilities. It is expected that the successful =
applicant, as an integral part of the entomological team, will share our =
high ambitions for the next millenium.

PhD and demonstrated excellence in systematic entomology required. =
Skills in collaboration, administration, and capacity building are =
further assetts.

The application - including curriculum vitae, list of publications, =
statement of research interests, and the five most important papers - =
should be sent to Naturhistoriska riksmuseet, Box 50007, SE - 10405 =
Stockholm, SWEDEN. Closing date for the application is 16 August 1999.

Please visit the museum homepages at http://www.nrm.se/welcome.html.en. =
Further inquiries may be addressed to Dr Thomas Pape, head of entomology =
- thomas.pape at nrm.se
