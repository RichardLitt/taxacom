Dear TAXACOM Subscriber,

You can now browse the Taxacom archives via the www.

Check out http://usobi.org/archives/taxacom.html

You can also "search" the archives from there, "change" your
subscription's SETtings (e.g., mail to nomail), and
"subscribe/unsubscribe". Instructions on each command are also available
there.

(Frankly, I prefer the e-mail interface for its succinctness, but one
must be more familiar with the various commands to make efficient use
of the e-mail command interface. I also prefer to send/receive messages
via email.  Your mileage may vary; check it out.)
