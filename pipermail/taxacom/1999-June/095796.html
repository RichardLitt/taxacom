My announcement last month that PANDORA, Richard Pankhurst's taxonomic
database program, is available free of charge via the Internet from
http://metalab.unc.edu/pub/academic/biology/ecology+evolution/software/pandora
has produced an enormous response.  Dr. Pankhurst invites the many new
PANDORA users to send him their e-mail addresses so that he can provide
notices of new releases etc.
