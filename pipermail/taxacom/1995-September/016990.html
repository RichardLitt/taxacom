1995 
3-Year Postdoctoral Fellowship
Diatom Collection
California Academy of Sciences


A NSF-funded, full-time post-doctoral fellowship is immediately
available in the Diatom Collection of the California Academy of
Sciences.  This research fellow will coordinate efforts of the inter-
institutional project, "Computerized Database of Verified Diatom
Names".  Research background in botanical taxonomy and systematics
is essential; experience in nomenclature and/or diatom research
preferred.  Computer experience essential.  Salary negotiable; excellent
benefits.  Applications, including a cover letter, curriculum vita and the
names, addresses and phone numbers of three references should be
submitted to:

California Academy of Sciences
Human Resources Office
Golden Gate Park
San Francisco, CA  94118

Applications will be accepted until the position is filled.  Questions may
be directed to Dr. J.P. Kociolek, phone: 415-750-7277, email:
pkociolek at cas.calacademy.org


The California Academy of Sciences is an Equal Opportunity Employer
committed to diversity.
