1995 
 The Universidad del Valle (Cali, Colombia) announces availability of
 its Boletin del Museo de Entomologia (Bulletin of the Entomology Museum).

 Published twice a year, with emphasis on basic entomological research,
 taxonomy, systematics, ecology and natural history, articles can be submitted
 and published in English, Portuguese or Spanish. The Museum has published
 five issues (three volumes) thus far. Articles in the Boletin are referreed

 The Boletin is available for both institutional and personal exchange
 or subscription.

 If you are interested in exchange or subscription, please notify us at
 the address below, providing your address, and we will send you a sample copy.
