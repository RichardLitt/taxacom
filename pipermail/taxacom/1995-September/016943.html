Dear Taxacomers,

Returning to a now-ancient thread, that of suppliers of herbarium
drying cabinets, I can now comment on the product and service of one
supplier.

I just received two herbarium drying cabinets which Steel Fixture
Manufacturing Company of Topeka, Kansas made according to my
specifications and drawings.  The cabinets are exactly as they should
be, and the employees of the company were courteous and professional,
calling to let me know they were on the way and providing detailed
unpacking-and-checking-for-damage instructions.  All that remains is
for us to supply the heat source.

If anyone needs any sort of steel storage cabinet (whether for
plants, mammals, insects, or what-have-you), I can recommend this
company and their work--and their prices are very reasonable as well.

It's nice to find something that works the way it's supposed to,
isn't it?
