1995 
The staff of the Essig Museum of Entomology at the University of
California, Berkeley is proud to announce the availability of our WWW
site.  We invite you to check it out at:

        http://www.mip.berkeley.edu/essig/

It is still under development but is none-the-less chock full of
information on our collections, information on the rich
history of entomology in western North America, information on some of
our type holdings (more of that to come soon).

We welcome any comments and suggestions.  For those of you who will want
more bug pictures, they are a top priority.  Thanks.
