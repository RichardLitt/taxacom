2001 
Funding is available for a Ph.D. student to undertake a molecular phylogeny
of the genus Setaria, and a taxonomic revision of the Old World species.
Start date is mid-August, 2002.  A strong background in botany is required,
and interest in combining field and lab work is important.  Previous
research experience and graduate-level course work are preferred.  The
student will join an active and international lab investigating molecular
systematics, developmental morphology, and evolution of development (see
the lab website at
http://www.umsl.edu/divisions/artscience/biology/Kellogg/Kellogg/home.html).  Pl
ease apply for admission to the Ph.D. program in the Department of Biology
at University of Missouri-St. Louis.  Application materials can be obtained
from Maryann Hempen, mhempen at jinx.umsl.edu, or via the UMSL web site,
http://www.umsl.edu/.  The application deadline is Dec. 15.  One copy of
the application should be sent to the Graduate School at UM-St. Louis, and
a second directly to Dr. Elizabeth A. Kellogg, Department of Biology,
UM-St. Louis, 8001 Natural Bridge Rd., St. Louis, MO 63121, U.S.A.  For
more information about the position or the lab, please contact
tkellogg at umsl.edu.
