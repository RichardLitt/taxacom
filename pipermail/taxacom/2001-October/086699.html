Postdoctoral Position

The "Real Jard?n Bot?nico (RJB), CSIC (Madrid, Spain)" offers a
full-time contract. This 2-year position has been made available by the
European project Euro+Med. Applicants interested in the Spanish branch
of this European taxonomy project should  have the following
requirements:

- PhD
- Fluent in English and Spanish.
- Skills in team collaboration.
- Experience in databases and herbarium managing
- Trained in Plant Taxonomy, including nomenclature, botanical
terminology, biogeography of Europe and Mediterranean plants, etc.
- User of internet and Microsoft applications (Word, Access and
Excel programmes).


 The successful candidate will joint the Spanish team as soon as a
candidate has been selected.

Duties and responsibilities
- Active participation in correspondence with European groups.
- File management on electronic formats.
- Selecting, sorting, and correcting databases of botanical names.
- Processing botanical data produced by the research fellows.
- Other floristics tasks are needed.

Applicants should submit a letter of interest, contact address,
curriculum vitae, and three letters of reference (optional) to:
