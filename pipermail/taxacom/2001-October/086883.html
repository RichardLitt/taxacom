 A new guide to Economic Botany Links is available at:

 http://www.rbgkew.org.uk/scihort/eblinks/

 It is designed to answer some of the queries received at RBG Kew
 about useful & poisonous plants, and to act as a resource for the
 wider ethnobotanical community, particularly students.

 Please try it - and let me know (direct, not to the list) if you have
any suggestions for improvements.
