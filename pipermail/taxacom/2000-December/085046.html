Thomas,

A relatively up-to-date list of Recent mollusks from Antarctica (including
deep-sea stuff) is R. K. Dell's "Antarctic Mollusca", Bulletin of the Royal
Society of New Zealand 27, 1990, 311 pp. Specialists in fossil Antarctic
mollusks include Anton Oleinik (Florida Atlantic University, Boca Raton,
Florida) and William J. Zinsmeister (Purdue University, West Lafayette,
Indiana).
