Taxacomers may be interested in the following announcement that was sent to
the DELTA list.

>From the viewpoint of  compiling databases, it is of some interest to note
the large number of taxa included in this interactive key.

And, from a herbarium data perspective,  plant collectors in Australia can
now name their own soils!!

Regards,   Lyn Craven
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
>
>I wish to announce the release of The Australian Soil Classification - An
>Interactive Key which has been produced by CSIRO Land and Water and CSIRO
>Entomology. The key uses the Intkey interface, consists of 11848 taxa
>defined by 115 characters and contains more than 200 illustrations.
>
>The key is available from CSIRO Publishing
>(http://www.publish.csiro.au/books/bookpage.cfm?PID=2653).
>
>A demonstration version allowing identification to the Soil Order (ie. 14
>taxa) is available on the Australian Collaborative Land Evaluation  Web Site
>(http://www.cbr.clw.csiro.au/aclep/interactivekey.htm).
