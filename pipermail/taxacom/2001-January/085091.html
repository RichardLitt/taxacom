A Rochester NY botanist, Bonnie Glickman passed away on January 1st.  She
was a member and past president of the Botany/Entomology Section,
http://www.ggw.org/ras/botany/ of the Rochester Academy of Sciences,
http://www.ggw.org/ras/ and the Bergen Swamp Preservation Society,
http://www.home.eznet.net/~drakeenv/bergen/
She taught biology at Monroe Community College in Rochester.
