This is an announcement of an opening of a position for a librarian at the
Botanical Research Institute of Texas.  Questions should be directed to me
and not the list: ssohmer at brit.org



Position Opening

LIBRARIAN: Botanical Research Institute of Texas

GENERAL SUMMARY:
To organize, maintain, and provide access to library materials and
information for a growing botanical research institution.

DESCRIPTION:
The Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT) seeks a librarian to
organize and manage a collection of approximately 73,000 volumes, with a
growth rate of several thousand volumes per year. In addition, the
librarian will recommend and oversee the installation of an online library
system; introduce other online services; handle reference and interlibrary
loan needs of BRIT staff; and provide administrative and long range
planning and development.

EDUCATION REQUIREMENTS:
Master's degree in library or information science from an ALA accredited
program.

DESIRABLE KNOWLEDGE, ABILITIES AND SKILLS:
Comprehension of descriptive cataloging, classification, AACR2, and OCLC;
knowledge of library management techniques, resource sharing, networking,
and automated library systems; knowledge of general and specialized
reference tools including on-line information resources; ability to work
with research and other staff, the Board of Trustees, and community at
large and to supervise volunteers; knowledge of conservation and
preservation principles and methods; ability to adapt readily to changes in
conditions or assignments; ability to work independently; understands and
accepts BRIT's mission and goals (To conserve our natural heritage by
deepening our knowledge of the plant and achieving public understanding of
the value plants bring to life).

BRIT offers an excellent benefits package; retirement program; sick and
vacation leave.

Applicants should send a letter of application, resume, and salary
requirements to:
Botanical Research Institute of Texas
Human Resources
Attn: Marilyn McCullough
509 Pecan Street
Fort Worth, TEXAS 76102-4060

Position open until filled.

BRIT is an Equal Opportunity Employer
