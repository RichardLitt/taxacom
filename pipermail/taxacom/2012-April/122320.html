Dear colleagues,

I regret to inform you that Erast Parmasto passed away yesterday afternoon.
Erast was leading expert in fungal taxonomy. During his long and distinguished career he published 170 scientific papers and described more than 300 new taxa or new combinations.

There is a memorial web page available at  http://erast.ut.ee/ErastParmasto/
