2012 
The 
Candidate Part of List of Available Names (LAN, ICZN Article 79) for
species-group taxa in the phylum Rotifera
is now open for public comment for one year:

http://iczn.org/lan/rotifer

We have provided links to the list as it was submitted in final copy by the
rotifer LAN committee, a link to the article in ZooTaxa describing the list,
a mechanism for you to add name-by-name comments to both the A and B lists
and a forum for general commentary. Finally, you may email us if you would
like to provide comments that remain confidential.

This is the first candidate for the LAN that has made it to this stage of
public input, thus we are particularly interested in your feedback on the
mechanism as well as the specifics of this list.
