I just went through the molecular data for 44 taxa and 1134 sequences
which generated a parsimony analysis supporting a human-chimpanzee clade
(Prasad et al 2008 Mol Biol Evol 25: 1795-1808) for sequences
orthologous to a 1.0 Mb region of human chromosome 7.  In the coding
sequences found no apomorphies at all for the large bodied hominoids so
the result is cladistically impossible. I have written to the lead
author for an explanation. They also analyzed a larger non coding
sequence partition, but that did not include anything for the orangutan
and so is uninfirmative.
