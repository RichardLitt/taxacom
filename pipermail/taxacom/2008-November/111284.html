Additional nformation about the Sixth International Triticeae Symposium,
which will be held in Kyoto, Japan in the summer of 2009, has been
posted to http://www.shigen.nig.ac.jp/6ITS/

 

The Symposium is designed to bring together a diversity of scientists,
all of whom work on the Triticeae, the tribe of grasses that includes
wheat, barley and rye as well as numerous species used for forage and
soil stabilization.
