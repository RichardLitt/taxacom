You may want to have a look at these two papers for more information
about this method.

Gotelli, N. J., and R. K. Colwell. 2001. Quantifying biodiversity:
procedures and pitfalls in the measurement and comparison of species
richness. Ecology Letters 4:379-391. doi:
10.1046/j.1461-0248.2001.00230.x.

Palmer, M., D. McGlinn, and J. Fridley. 2008. Artifacts and
Artifictions in Biodiversity Research. Folia Geobotanica 43:245-257.
doi: 10.1007/s12224-008-9012-y.

Hope this helps,
