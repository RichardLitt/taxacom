_Latest News on the
Adelaide Conference_
  * Preparations for the conference have entered a new phase, breaking
    records for expressions of interest from potential presenters. By the
    time submissions closed, _450 abstracts_ had been received from _57
    countries_, far surpassing levels of interest leading up to previous
    barcode conferences.
