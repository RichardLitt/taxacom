Recently BHL scanned more volumes from the Bulletin of Zoological Nomenclature, and I've extracted articles from these scans and added them to BioStor. You can get a complete list of articles identified so far at http://biostor.org/issn/0007-5167

There are still gaps, and the article metadata (titles, authorship, etc.) is still incomplete, but if you are looking for ICZN cases and opinions, you'll find many if not most of them in this collection.
