That case was in regards to your case against DSE over venomoid snakes for
the most part. The ICZN is not mentioned in the ruling. It is also by the
State Victoian Court and they do not have jurisdiction over the rest of
Australia let alone international issues.

Ray the ICZN comes under the umbrella of the IUBS making it an
International Agreement and its a voluntary one at that. The GSLWG global
species list working group is also under the umbrella of the IUBS hence is
also an international agreement. Local courts cannot and do not because
they know better, dictate international organizations.

You won that case against DSE and from my understanding of it you should
have, but the courts did not demand all Australian Scietists have to follow
the code, they cannot do that.

Cheers Scott
