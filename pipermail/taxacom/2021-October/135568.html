>From what I saw some years back, hominid fossils can be a bit of a mess
when it comes to taxonomy - especially in the name game. The names are
really less important as entities than labels for particular fossils, and
really the type fossil only in my view. Otherwise there can be all sorts of
obfuscations and imagination. There are, for example,
isolated 'Australopithecus' fossil teeth that have a surface dentition
conforming to orangutans. There is one type fossil (Sahelanthropus) that
consists of a lower and upper jaw with dental morphology that does not
match! Probably just the tip of the iceberg.
