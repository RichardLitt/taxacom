> >> So now his latest plot is to set himself up as head honcho to make

> The ICZN does in fact actively encourage taxonomic specialist
> associations to create official lists that they can then submit to the
> Commission for formal endorsement, under the provisions of Article 79,
> known as the LAN (Lists of Available Names). We would be very happy to
> have the herpetological community submit such a proposal to us, and even
> if they choose not to engage with the Commission, *we support the
> principle* that the community should be able to determine, *by
> consensus*, how they wish to handle matters within their disciplines.
>
> The existence of the Commission is, itself, due to a consensus agreement
> across the entire zoological community, so it would be absurdly
> hypocritical to say that consensus decisions by segments of that
> community are "in contempt of the rules" - consensus is EXACTLY how
> rules are made in the first place. Obviously Mr. Hoser personally
> objects to being on the receiving end of a community boycott, but
> instead of changing his behavior to adhere to the ethical and scientific
> standards of the community, he screams "foul" and just makes things
> worse. This is beyond tiresome.
>
