        If bodoensis is accepted as a new name, I would regard it as a subspecies within species Homo sapiens (sensu lato).
Below is how I classified the genus Homo a couple years ago, with a paraphyletic H. s. heidelbergensis giving rise to H. s. neanderthalensis, and H. s. rhodesiensis splitting off next:

>                1   H. h. rudolfensis
>
>               2A   H. h. habilis

>               2B   H. h. floresiensis ("hobbit")
>
>                3   {{H. erectus + H. sapiens}} (exgroup marker)
>
>
>
>   _a_ Homo erectus%
>
