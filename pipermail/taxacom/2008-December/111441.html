Dear Colleagues,
 
My museum is putting together an exhibition on the Arctic and we're
looking for a high-quality (i.e. high-resolution), color, digital image
of a (living) musk ox (Ovibos moschatus), preferably from Greenland or
Canada. We have a musk ox skull and we'd like to supplement it with a
photograph of a whole animal. (Photographs of preserved specimens
wouldn't really work).
 
I know this request is a bit off-topic, but I'm thinking that some of
you may have done field work in the Arctic, and may have some
photographs to share. Alternatively, if you have a colleague who may be
doing work in the Arctic, please forward this request to them.
 
Thanks very much in advance, and Happy Holidays,
