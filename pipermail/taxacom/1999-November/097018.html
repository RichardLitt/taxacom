    Two barbarian doofera were overheard to say (while stirring their pot of
chopped-up enemy stew):
    "My most gooder datums and spectrums revelates dat one specie of
pollytician iz truely supeerieur too much uther genuses, probly cawsed buy
disorientating langwig witch onely lawyours's brainz can registrate."
     To which his dinner guest thoughtfuly replied:
It depends on what the meaning of "iz" iz.
