Dear Dr Rasmussen

Out of interest I did a search on "Utricularia" in our library's
online catalogue , which includes (supposedly) everything published
since 1991 that we've received, including papers in journals. It
only came up with twelve entries, none of them either phylogenetic
or molecular. Thus, from this search at least, the phylogeny of
Utricularia would appear to be a wide open field waiting for someone
to be entrapped by the bladders ....
