1999 
Ever feel like your field is finally getting away from you?  If, like me,
you're over 50, read this:

Pagel, Mark.  1999.  Inferring the historical patterns of biological
evolution. Nature 401:877-884 (Oct 28 1999).

Very interesting comments on the Cambrian explosion, thermophile origin of
life (please, Ken, no more rants about Woese), punctuated equilibrium and
more, but all from a statistical base much beyond my understanding.

The article is of interest to taxonomists because it shows how these
statistical techniques can wring surprising information from phylogenetic
trees.
