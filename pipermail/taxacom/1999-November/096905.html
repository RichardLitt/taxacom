Taxacom Subscribers:

Just a reminder -- Taxacom is now being archived on the web at a new
address (below).  The web archive is useful for browsing chronologically,
or by subject; or for doing full text (term) searches on the collection of
messages since November 1992.  It also can be used as an alternative to
receiving Taxacom mail--for following the discussion threads at your
convenience.

The web interface to Taxacom also allows you to easily adjust your
subscription and delivery options or to remove yourself from the list.  If
you haven't seen it, it would be worth a look.

The URL is:  http://www.usobi.org/archives

The old web archive at:  www.biodiversity.uno.edu is no longer active and
will be removed soon.

Jim Beach
