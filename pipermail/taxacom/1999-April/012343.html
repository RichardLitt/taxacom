Dear all,

We are preparing a CD-ROM in the Flora Malesiana series for the
Leguminosae. Part I, the Mimosoideae will be published this summer, and
those who visit the IBC in St Louis will see it demonstrated.

However, we are still looking for pictures for a number of species. We
hope there are people who will be so kind to look into their slide
collection. If we include slides in the CD-ROM they are only in 72 dpi,
and we always mention the author; in the introduction we give also the
address of the author. For more information please contact us.

Below (and as a separate attachment in WORD6) we give a list of species
of which we still need a picture. We hope you can help us!

Thank you in advance for your help!

Ingrid de Kort & Jeannette Ridder-Numan, The National Herbarium of the
Netherlands.
