  The following position is now open for applications.  The ad is also in
this week's issue of SCIENCE.  Please give to any colleagues or students
that may be interested.

SYSTEMATIST-PHYLOGENETICIST

Position: Assistant Professor (Tenure Track)

The Department of Biology at the University of New Mexico is seeking
applicants for a full-time, tenure-track position at the Assistant
Professor level in systematics with expertise in phylogenetic theory and
analysis, to begin August 21, 2000, pending budgetary capability.

Minimum requirements:   Ph.D. in some sub-discipline of biology by the
start date. Preferred characteristics: postdoctoral experience; familiarity
with statistical approaches to phylogenetic inference and a thorough
understanding of the theory of phylogenetic analysis and algorithm
construction; expectation of developing an independent, vigorous research
program; commitment to excellence in teaching at the undergraduate through
graduate level; and enthusiasm for working with colleagues whose interests
include a broad array of disciplines and organisms.  Teaching
responsibilities are likely to include a course in Phylogenetic Theory and
Analysis at the upper division/graduate level and participation in
Introductory Biology and the Systematics curriculum.
Competitive salary and start-up funds provided.

Application:
To apply for the Systematist-Phylogeneticist position, provide the following:
* A curriculum vitae
* A bibliography with 2-3 reprints of significant publications
* A brief description of research interests
* A brief statement of teaching interests and philosophy
* Arrange to have three letters of recommendation sent to the
 Systematist-Phylogeneticist Search Committee within one week of the closing
 date.

E-mail applications will be accepted provided the original is mailed within
24 hours. E-mail address for applications or inquiries: vkent at unm.edu

The applicant is responsible for having three letters of recommendation to
be sent to:
Systematist/Phylogeneticist Search Committee
Department of Biology, University of New Mexico,
Albuquerque, NM 87131-1091, Tel: (505) 277-3411;  Fax: (505) 277-0304.
Completed applications must be received at the above address by 25 June 1999.
