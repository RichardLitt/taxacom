On Tue, 20 Apr 1999, Thomas Schlemmermeyer wrote:
>  Are there any recent papers (more recent than 1992) that deal with
>  biogeographic history of groups before triassic times?
>     Yours,   Thomas
Thomas,
        "before Triassic times" is a very long time indeed.  And,
"groups" is rather vague, too.  Here are two books to get you started.
        John

Gierlowski-Kordesch, E., and Kelts, K.  1994.  Global Geological Record of
Lake Basins Volume 1.  Cambridge University Press.  Cambridge, England, U.K.
427 pp.

Kelin, George D.  1994.  Pangea: Paleoclimate, Tectonics, and Sedimentation
during Accretion, Zenith, and Breakup of a Supercontinent.  Geological
Scoeity of America.  Boulder, Colorado.  Special Paper 288:1-295.
