The Young Systematists Special Issue of ODE: promoting early Career Scientists in Taxonomy and Systematics

Dear Taxacom-Members,

we are proud to announce that a special issue of the GfBS-journal Organisms Diversity & Evolution (ODE) has recently been published. It is online available at:  http://www.springerlink.com/content/1439-6092/12/4/
This special issue of ODE both highlights and promotes the high quality, diversity and impact of the research being performed by early career taxonomists and systematists. The Young Systematists are the students group of the Society for Biological Systematics (http://www.jusys.gfbs-home.de/).

Attached you may find the Editorial of this special issue of early career taxonomists and systematists. The remaining papers are freely available at the OLD Springer-Homepage as mentioned above. If you should be redirected to the NEW SpringerLink page, please follow this path. Click on the "Current Issue" button and choose "Access old SpringerLink". There you can choose the new issue 12(4) with all contributions.


Sincere regards,


Andre Koch

On behalf of the guest-editors André Koch, Thomas Hülsken, and Jana Hoffmann.
