Dear Taxacom-Listers:

With the valuable assistance of many kind colleagues and friends, I have
created a new scientific journal entitled, *Life: The Excitement of Biology*.
*LEB* is a peer-reviewed venue whose mission is to provide the global
scientific community with diverse, high-quality, readable, and interesting
articles on a wide variety of biological and related scientific topics.

http://blaypublishers.com/archives/

and apologies for any duplicate emails you may receive due to cross-posting.
