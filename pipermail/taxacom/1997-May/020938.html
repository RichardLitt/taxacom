  Mycologia Memoirs 8: 1-148, "The genus Collybia (agaricales) in the
  northeastern United States and adjacent Canada" was published as hardcopy in
  1983.  The book was printed by J. Cramer in Germany, coprighted by The New
  York Botanical Garden, and published in collaboration with the Mycological
  Society of America.  Hard copy went out-of-print several years ago.

  An electronic, revised version has been prepared.  It can be accessed at:
  http://www.nybg.org/bsci/res/col/colintro.html

  An epithet locator will be added to the pages in the next few days.
