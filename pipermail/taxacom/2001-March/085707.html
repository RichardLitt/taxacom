>Does anyone know of a paper describing the various bark types found in the
>eucalypts, eg string, gum, bloodwood, ironbark, etc.  I have been told of
>one possibly published in the 1950s.

You could have a look in the little more recent interactive key to the
Eucalypts of SE Australia, Euclid.  There is a good general discussion on
morphology of the group, including bark types.  Also there are some good
photographs on the CDROM.  Using the key you will be able to see which
species have which bark types...

http://www.anbg.gov.au/cpbr/euclid/euclid.html
