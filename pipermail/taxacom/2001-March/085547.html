2001 
The 13th Entomological Congress organised by the Entomological Society of Southern Africa will be held on 2 - 5 July 2001 at the University of Natal, Pietermaritzburg, South Africa.

The following Symposia and Workshops will be presented, in addition to general sessions:
Biodiversity and Insect Conservation
Forensic Entomology
Fruit Flies
Hymenoptera
Insect Pathology
Insect Rearing
IUCN Southern African Invertebrates Specialist Group
Southern African Stem Borer Management Project (final meeting)
Taxonomists as Team Players
Permits and Legislation for Collection of Invertebrates
Post-Congress morning workshop on Spatial Data and the African Entomologist

Further information is available from brothers at nu.ac.za .
Registrations and abstracts should be received before 18 March 2001.
