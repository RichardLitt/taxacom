Meeting Announcement and Call for Papers
Computer-based interactive keys systems and internet applications
4th July 2001, London.
There will be a meeting of the Royal Entomological Society's Technology
Special Interest Group in London on 4th July 2001. The meeting will be on
the latest developments in computer applications for key-based species
identification and internet applications related to entomology.
If you are interested in attending the meetng or wish to submit a paper,
please contact the convenor directly.
