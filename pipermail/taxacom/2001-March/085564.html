Wendy is now the new curator of the University of Georgia Herbarium in
Athens, Georgia. She began in the position last August. The good news for
her is that UGA changed the position from "staff" to "faculty" and she will
be teaching classes. Her first class next fall will be "Botanical
Illustration." Her appointment is joint with the Georgia Museum of Natural
History.

The herbarium website is:
