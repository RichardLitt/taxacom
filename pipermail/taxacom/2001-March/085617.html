2001 
ROYAL BOTANIC GARDEN EDINBURGH & University of Edinburgh

Post Graduate Studentships

Applications are invited for 5 NERC and 1 internally funded
studentships to study for the 12 month MSc/Diploma course in "The
Biodiversity and Taxonomy of Plants".

The course covers traditional and modern approaches to plant taxonomy
and biodiversity, and is suitable for post graduate students in plant
systematics, biogeography, conservation, ecology and related fields.

For further information please contact: Dr Stephan Helfer, Royal
