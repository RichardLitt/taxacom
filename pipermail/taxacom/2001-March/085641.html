I would suggest you the "R" software (http://r-project.org) which is free
and has most of things one needs in phenetic analyses (PCA, factor analysis,
multidimensional scaling, many clustering methods etc. etc.). You can
download R itself as well as some additional "contributed packages" (e.g.
"cluster", "maptree", "multiv"...), depending on your particular needs.
