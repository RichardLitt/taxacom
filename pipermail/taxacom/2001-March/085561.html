Consult Englera no 7. subtitled "Botany in Berlin" (ed. Hildemar Scholz)
which was published on the occasion of the XIV Int. Botanical Congress
(1987). Pages 219-252 by Paul Hiepko contain the info you want. It is a
fascinating account of the Botanical Museum Berlin-Dahlem and its
history. Sleumer also wrote an account of the surviving families in Kew
Bull. 1949: 172-175. (1949).
