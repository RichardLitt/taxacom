For those on this list interested in host plant records in the Lepidoptera,
the following was recently published:

Patrick, B.H. 2021 Indigenous hostplants of New Zealand’s endemic
Lepidoptera. Canterbury Botanical Society 52:4-36

It lists Lep families that have caterpillars that specialize on one genus
or species of vascular plant. Anyone interested in a copy can contact the
author at Brian.Patrick at wildlands.co.nz
