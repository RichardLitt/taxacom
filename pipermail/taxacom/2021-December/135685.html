Dear colleagues,
Let me introduce the call for the second annual prize for making the data
on Ukrainian natural history collections open to the world.
Any contribution (i.e., publication of datasets, publication of the
collections' elaboration in OA journals, organization of open topic-related
evens) that promotes the open access and availability of the Ukrainian
natural history collections is welcomed.
For more details, please see
https://sites.google.com/view/andriynovikovprize/about-the-prize

I will be very thankful for sharing this information about potential
applicants.
I will also be thankful for any suggestions and critics on this my
initiative - perhaps you can recommend some meaningful updates.

Best regards, Andriy.
