 Hi Les,
In my current predicament and frame of mind, it would be very easy for me to agree with your poor estimation of iNaturalist. However, it isn't entirely accurate. This particular case is complicated. 
The case started out, within N.Z. here, with Danilo Hegg's approach to the identification of a cockroach, but Danilo is no longer really the problem. We could have sorted the issue out, here in N.Z., without too much drama. Danilo did try to publicly undermine my reputation as an identifier of insects, by claiming that I had made a mistake and that my identification was a lazy one, dubious, and lacking any evidence. Of course, unknown to Danilo, it wasn't actually my identification! I was following our official government biosecurity authority (MPI). I did subsequently provide Danilo with evidence, in the form of the original description (and only recent taxonomic treatment) for the cockroach (Balta bicolor), a description which does fit the N.Z. species about as well as one can reasonably expect for a written description from 1943. Strangely, Danilo claimed that the description doesn't fit the N.Z. species, and he misquoted the description as saying that the tegmina were conspicuously bicoloured, when in fact it said that the limbs (legs) are conspicuously bicoloured and the tegmina are usually bicoloured. Danilo had already rolled back my IDs of Balta bicolor on about 50 observations, effectively ruining what I estimate to have been about 1 week's worth of unpaid work by me, spread over several years. There is no easy way to rectify what Danilo has done, not without his cooperation, and he made it pretty clear to me that he had made his mind up and wasn't about to backtrack. So that was the initial problem. At the time, I had no reason to think that Danilo's approach to identifications was going to stop there, so I made the decision to suspend him, as a purely temporary measure, so as to stop him in his tracks until I could get some sort of discussion going about the issue. It was that decision by me which led to the mess I'm in now, losing my curator status and facing a possible 1 year suspension altogether from the site, if certain people overseas have their way. The ring leader overseas appears to be someone called Charlie Hohn. He is the person who accused me of a "heinous abuse of power"! I'm not Putin invading the Ukraine, for God's sake! There were a few minor mistakes and infringements of community guidelines on my part, trying to deal with the rapidly escalating situation which started with Danilo. Hohn and his associates are milking them for all that they are worth. I am reliably informed (from a 2 hour zoom meeting last night) that [quote]head office in California is under huge political pressure to be seen to be doing something about my infringements[unquote]. One can only speculate what exactly is Hohn's problem and why he seems to have such a big influence on head office? Is he a donor? I really don't know. For whatever reason, Hohn and his associates have jumped on my case, and have blown what should have been a resolvable in-house squabble within N.Z. into something of an "international incident".
Cases like this probably don't happen very often on iNaturalist, and "an impartial governing board willing to adjudicate cases like this" possibly wouldn't be the answer to this one, given how "political" it seems to have become. The unfortunate thing for me is that Danilo's actions apparently didn't infringe any community guidelines, whereas my reactions apparently did, which hands Charlie Hohn a loaded gun, so to speak!
Cheers, Stephen
