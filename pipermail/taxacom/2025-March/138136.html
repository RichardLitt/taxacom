Not surprising to see the center of origin-chance dispersal theory being
concordant with Creation theory about chance dispersal.

"Dipsosaurus catalinensis. So the genus has some aquatic connection"

This island record might provide a neat example of metapopulation survival
of a widespread ancestor in the region. I'm not immediately familiar with
the tectonic and geological history of this region, but possible that
multiple islands involved, and likely quite a complex situation given the
accretion of huge (e.g. nearly half of Mexico, and that is after
compression) terrestrial arcs formerly occupying the Pacific west of North
America.
