Thanks John. However, that's what the paper is about. Really, of course you
are free to criticise, but I don't think these authors deserve your scorn.
They present their data, they discuss it thoroughly, and they include as
possible explanations similar scenarios to those you believe in even if
they don't use the specialist wording you would use. It's a molecular
paper. Let's assess it on that basis. Future papers with further molecular
data from a wider array of species might confirm their conclusions or might
have a different interpretation. This is the usual progression, as we know.
We shall see.
