If protoiguanid-agamids, and then pre-  and proto-iguanids were already in
the west Pacific, there is no need for fully-fledged iguanids, let alone
Brachylophus, to migrate anywhere. The allopatry within Iguania
(pleurodonts - Fiji and east, vs acrodonts - Solomons and west) is the key
to the family distribution boundaries there (i.e.
phylogenetic-biogeographic breaks), but the authors don't mention it.
