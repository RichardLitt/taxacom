The List of presumed extinct taxa on the Jepson Interchage is not
current.  Here is a list of the 'rediscoveries' I am aware of from by
contact or literature.  The Jepson Manual status list WAS current as of
the effective date when the treatment passed through final edits, in
some cases over a decade now...All told, there have been ca. 80 taxa on
the 'presumed extinct' list for California, but most have been relocated
eventually; the overwhelming problem is the evidence of absence/absence
of evidence dilemma

Astragalus pycnostachyus var. lanosissimus - rediscovered 1997

Astragalus tener var. ferrisiae - collected in 1989, determined as a
rediscovery in ca. 1992

Atriplex parishii - rediscovered in 1992

Chorizanthe orcuttiana - known from 3 occurrences, date of relocation??

Clarkia mosquinii - rediscovered 1991, as to the two chromosome races
named as subspecies, both have apparently been found (Gottleib and
Janeway in prog.?)

Helianthus nuttallii subsp. parishii - relocated 2001, on a site about
to become a huge mega home development

Orthocarpus pachystachyus - rediscovered in 1996

Plagiobothrys glaber - rediscovered in 2002

Sidalcea keckii - relocated some years ago, ca. 1995? Two known sites
are extant

Trifolium amoenum - somewhat famous since it is in cultivation at Bodega
Lab, relocated ca 1996
