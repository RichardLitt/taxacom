Abdul:

Patricia Eckel's Latin diagnosis is just fine and in providing it she has corrected one of the errors in the original English text -- it is a new variety that is the subject of the diagnosis, not a specimen (even if the proposed taxon is only known from the one specimen).  However, there is another error.

Indigofera rubromarginata Thulin var. longipedunculata cannot "differ" from Indigofera rubromarginata Thulin, because it is being named as an integral part of that species.  The Latin diagnosis should start either as:
"Varietas nova Indigoferae rubromarginatae Thulin var. rubromarginata proxima, ..." or 
"Varietas nova Indigoferae rubromarginatae Thulin typicae proxima, ..."

[-- though in writing this, I begin to wonder .. surely "proxima" would require the dative not the genitive case ("Indigofera rubromarginata ... typica proxima") -- I don't have my Stearn handy, but Patricia can, I am sure, tell me why I am wrong.]
