I am team-teaching an advanced systematics course (graduate level) starting
next week.  The course will focus on basic principles and procedures in
taxonomy, nomenclature, and classification, and we will also cover
phylogenetic systematics.  One topic that we plan to discuss is specimen
collection/biotic inventory and related to that, the current issues
surrounding permits and regulations.  We would also like to discuss the
ethics of specimen collection.  While there are a number of references that
deal with the mechanics of specimen collection and preservation, I have not
been able to find anything dealing with current issues pertaining to
permits and regulations, nor have I found anything on the ethics of
specimen collection.  Is anyone aware of any articles dealing with these
subjects?
