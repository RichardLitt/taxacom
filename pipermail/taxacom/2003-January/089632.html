John and others,
     I'm not sure I would call it a "standard" Indian Ocean biogeographic
pattern.  I see no reason to expect that Dietes ever occurred much further
north than its present relict distribution.  I would guess it once did very
well in Antarctica (possibly where the genus originated).
     The big question is where the Lord Howe Island species came
from----presumably New Zealand, Australia, or perhaps even New Caledonia.
Whether relict populations of Dietes might still occur "naturally" in any of
those places is hard to say (but it wouldn't surprise me if they did).
