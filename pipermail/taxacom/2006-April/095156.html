Dear taxacomers,


The Botany and Plant Pathology Department at Oregon State University, in
Corvallis, Oregon has recently approved the hiring of an Instructor/Insect
Identification Specialist.  The job entails working with the Plant Pathology
Clinic at OSU to help identify insects of agricultural interest within Oregon,
as well as conducting research and teaching.  The official announcement did not
come out in time to meet the Entomology Society of Americas deadline for
printing in American Entomologist  and thus we are sending it to relevant
newsgoups/listserves.  To view the full job announcement:
http://oregonstate.edu/admin/hr/jobs/academic/001-2239.html.

Please pass this information and link on to any interested parties you may know.
