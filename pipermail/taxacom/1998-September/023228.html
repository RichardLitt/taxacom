Mail forwarded from Wim Vader:

        Troms=F8 Museum is preparing an exhibition, showing a large Leathery Turtle
Dermochelys coriacea that washed ashore here in northern Norway last year,
as well as one of the accompanying Pilot Fishes Naucrates ductor. Both the
fishes and the turtle itself were the northermost representatatives of
their species ever.
        One piece of data that we have been unable to collect from the literature
at our disposal is what colour eyes the Pilot fish has. We should like to
have also that detail correct in our exhibition, hence this request (in
somewhat inappropriate lists). If anybody is able to help us either
directly or by forwarding this request to a fishlist, we shall be very
grateful.
