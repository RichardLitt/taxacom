1998 
Postdoctoral Research Fellowships in Biological Informatics
National Science Foundation
Deadline: December 11, 1998

With the termination this year of the Postdoctoral Research
Fellowships in the Biosciences Related to the Environment, through
the NSF, a new program has just been announced, noted above, with
a deadline this year of December 11, 1998. "Biological Informatics"
is broadly defined, as noted in the document, and includes
applications in the fields of Biogeography, Phylogenetics, and
Comparative Biology. The new announcement is available on the NSF
website homepage (www.nsf.gov); go to the bottom of the page, click
on Documents, and then request document NSF 98-162.
NSF Postdoctoral Fellowships are restricted by law to citizens,
nationals, or lawfully admitted permanent resident aliens of the
United States.
Please let your colleagues and students know of this, if
interested.
