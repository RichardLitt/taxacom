2007 
 
Position Opening 
Web Programmer
Biodiversity Institute
University of Kansas 
 
The University of Kansas Biodiversity Institute seeks a full-time, web
programmer to develop and oversee all aspects of the Institute's
research and public web services and products. We require a Bachelor's
degree, two years professional experience in software development with
object-oriented languages, experience in software development using PHP
or Python, web application servers, JavaScript and XML, CSS and web
server/SQL database server configuration. For a complete position
description and to apply, visit https://jobs.ku.edu (select "Search
Postings" and then search on position number 00206451). 

Application review begins 26 October 2007.  EO/AA.  We celebrate
diversity in all life forms.

The workplace is located in Dyche Hall on the University campus within
an open-office Informatics Department which includes a high-energy,
friendly, smart, good-looking, and experienced group of 10 software
engineers and associated technical staff. The BI is a healthy, dynamic,
nourishing intellectual environment which values creativity,
professional growth and collaborative interaction. The University of
Kansas offers a fine benefits package for this permanent full-time
position. 

Lawrence Kansas is a progressive university town with a full complement
of cultural, intellectual, athletic and spiritual options. Far from the
traffic jams and maddening crowds of the U.S. east or west coast,
average commute times are measured in seconds.  Lawrence has an award
winning micro-brewery, and amazingly good Japanese, Indian, Italian and
Thai restaurants, as well as other satisfying regional and international
dining options. 

If you want to locate us, launch Google Earth and let it zoom into the
default location. That's Lawrence, Kansas.
