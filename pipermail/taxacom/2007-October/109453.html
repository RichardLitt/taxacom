Pro-barcoders (and ante-barcoders) may be interested to see the latest 
newsletter http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/files/newsl/edit_newsletter5.pdf of The 
European Distributed Institute of Taxonomy (E.D.I.T.).

E.D.I.T. is a European Union "Network of Excellence" in « Taxonomy for 
Biodiversity and Ecosystem Research ».( http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/ )

E.D.I.T. started on the 1st of March 2006. It will last 5 years

It receives a contribution (I think of 11.9 million euros) from the European 
Commission's Sixth Framework Programme ( 
http://www.e-taxonomy.eu/files/CDC.pdf )
