Dear taxacom'ers

Apologies for cross postings.

The 2007 issue of The Proceedings of TDWG has just been published from the
Bratislava conference.  This conference draws together work of the TDWG
Infrastructure Project (http://www.tdwg.org/activities/tip/).  

The Table of Contents and individual abstracts are accessible from

http://www.tdwg.org/proceedings/issue/view/ISBN%20978-1-930723-71-9,

the PDF version of the complete journal can be found at 

http://www.tdwg.org/fileadmin/2007meeting/documents/Proceedings_of_TDWG_2007
.pdf.

and the conference presentations can be found at

http://www.tdwg.org/conference2007/programme/how-to-upload-slides/

Anna Weitzman and Lee Belbin acknowledge with thanks the Session Chairs,
authors and the peer reviewers in helping with scheduling and the processing
of abstracts. And - a special thanks to Eduard Stloukal and his team for an
excellent meeting.
