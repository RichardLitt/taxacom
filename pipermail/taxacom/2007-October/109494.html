Hi all,

http://www.eigenfactor.org

Was pointed to it today. Interesting site if you haven't seen it 
already, & worth a play to see the connections between research fields 
in the dynamic maps (try the slider tool). Molecular & Cell Biology is 
BIG in (year 2005 data) science, with 512 journals & 20% of the traffic.

They don't have Zoology or Biology as categories in the mapping 
displays, but they're there as choices in the advanced search. Botany is 
presumably as Plant sciences.

Overall Systematic Biology comes in at 96, but is right up there at 
third in ecology & evolution journals. TREE is first. Zootaxa isn't 
featuring yet.

Etc. Cool!
