2007 
As many of you know, Verne Grant died in May of this year.  There was 
a memorial service here in Austin, and at it, his wife and colleague 
Karen Grant displayed some pictures of Verne from various times of 
his life.  We thought that those in the botanical and general 
scientific community who knew and respected him would like to see 
those pictures, too, so we've posted them at the Plant Resources 
Center web site, along with the obituary by Karen Grant and Billie 
Turner that was originally published in the ASPT Newsletter.  Our web 
site is:
http://www.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/
and there is an obvious link from there.
