The Field Museum will host a one-day symposium on Darwin and his 
Theory of Evolution. The symposium celebrates the temporary exhibit 
on Darwin, his life and his science, currently on display at the 
Museum (through Jan. 1, 2008). World-class experts from the United 
States and Great Britain will explore Darwin's place in the history 
of biology, the philosophical dimensions of Darwin's theory of 
evolution, evolutionary innovation in the plant and animal kingdoms, 
microevolutionary processes in lizards and birds (Darwin's finches), 
problems of co-evolution, the evolution of the HIV virus, and the 
origin of man.

Saturday, November 3, 9:00 AM through 5:00 PM.
James Simpson Theatre, West Entrance, The Field Museum, 1400 S. Lake 
Shore Drive, Chicago, IL

ADMISSION IS FREE - THE SYMPOSIUM IS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC.
