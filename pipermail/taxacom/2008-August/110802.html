Late registration for TDWG2008 in Fremantle will apply in one month (2400
UTC London September 6). Those coming to Fremantle are therefore urged to
register as soon as possible at
http://www.tdwg.org/conference2008/registration/.  

 

The deadline for submission of an abstract is 2400 UTC London September 1.
This applies for oral presentations, posters or computer demonstrations.
Instructions for authors can be found at
http://www.tdwg.org/conference2008/abstracts/.  

 

The conference program can be found at
http://www.tdwg.org/conference2008/program/.
