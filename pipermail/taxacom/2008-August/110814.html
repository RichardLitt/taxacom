Classical examples are vicariants Platanus orientalis of Mediterranean 
Region and P. occidentalis of America; Catalpa ovata  of China and Japan and 
C. bignonioides of America. Several other examples include Pinus jeffreyi 
(Am) and Pinus wallichiana (Himal), Juglans regia and J. californica, 
Sambucus mexicana and Sambucus wightiana and so on.
