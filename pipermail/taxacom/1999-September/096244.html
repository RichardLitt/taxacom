1999 
the publishers of Zoologischer Anzeiger, Urban & Fischer (Jena) offer a new
service called Table-of-Content-Alert (ToC-Alert) for free and irrespective
of journal subscription: you can subscribe to ToC-Alert by visiting
http://www.urbanfischer.de/journals, where you click on BIOLOGY,
ZOOLOGISCHER ANZEIGER, and then TOC ALERT.

As soon as your subscription is completed, you will regularly receive the
Table of Contents of the most recent issue of Zool.Anz. as soon as it is
released from the press, and also from time to time a list of forthcoming
papers.

I crosspost this also to ENTOMO-L and biolsyst.
