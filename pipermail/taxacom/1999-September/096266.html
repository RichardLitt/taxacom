The Flowering Plant Gateway at

http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/newgate/cronang.htm

now includes the Angiosperm Phylogeny Group classification system as
a comparative element (relative to systems of Thorne, Cronquist, and
Takhtajan).  This site, designed for student use, provides overviews
of the various systems, context views of varying family placements,
and query access to a database of URLs that carry content relevant to
a given family.
