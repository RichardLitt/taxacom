The Institute of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology  - National Taiwan University

Position Announcement

Professor, Associate, or Assistant Professor in Plant Ecology

The Institute of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at National Taiwan University invites applications for a faculty position in terrestrial plant ecology at professor, associate professor or assistant professor level with an anticipated start date of August 1, 2010.  All areas of plant ecology will be considered, however, experience or interests in community and population ecology, theoretical and modeling approaches, conservation issues, vegetation dynamics, and/or plant systematics are preferred. The successful applicant should have a Ph.D. in a related field and preferably post-doctoral experience.  Please send curriculum vitae, proof and transcripts of Ph.D. degree statement of interest and plans in both research and teaching, four copies of publications and three reference letters before January 10, 2010 to: Professor Pei-Fen Lee, Institute of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, National Taiwan University, Taipei 106, Taiwan. Fax: +886-2-23686750. Email: leepf at ntu.edu.tw
