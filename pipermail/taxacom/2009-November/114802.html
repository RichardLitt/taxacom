See message from Dmitry Geltman below.

Armen Takhtajan was born on June 10, 1910 in Shusha, Nagorno-Karabakh, a disputed, largely Armenian, enclave within Azerbaijan on the southern Caucasus. Most of his professional career was spent at the Komarov Botanical Institute in St Petersburg (formerly Leningrad). He is best known for his system of classification of flowering-plants and in this respect collaborated with, and was a good friend of, the late Art Cronquist (1919-1992).
