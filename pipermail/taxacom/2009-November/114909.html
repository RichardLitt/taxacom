Unfortunately the collection on display at the AMNH probably never
belonged to Alfred Russel Wallace - see my comments about it on my
Wallace blog http://wallacefund.info/en/news_and_views 

A few days ago I found a letter Wallace wrote to Henry Walter Bates in
1846 which further reinforces my conclusion and I will write a post
about this for my blog soon.

Wallace's private collection of insects as described in his
autobiography My Life, is in fact housed at the Natural History Museum,
London - see
http://www.nhm.ac.uk/nature-online/collections-at-the-museum/wallace-col
lection/introduction3.jsp I initiated the project to buy it and an large
collection of Wallace's manuscripts from Wallace's grandson in 2002.
