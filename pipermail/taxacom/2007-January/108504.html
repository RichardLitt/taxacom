The publishers have just notified me of a couple of printer's errors that slipped through the system in the Species Numbers publication.

An erratum will shortly be added to published copies. 

On Page 34: Under
In the Loricifera Column under World Accepted, it reads 2298 and should be 22 with 98 as the footnote (should be superscripted & smaller font).

Row = Tardigrada, Column = World Accepted, it reads 980100 and should be 980 with 100 as the footnote (should be superscripted & smaller font).

I apologise for any inconvenience
