Dear all,

This is the relaunch / revival of a request that we posted on TAXACOM 
in December 2006.

questionnaire on taxonomic training. So please take some time to 
answer our request. Thank you so much !

I take the opportunity to thank those who already answered the 
questionnaire and helped spread the information further.
