Dear all,

The EDIT Distributed European School of Taxonomy announces, within the 
framework of the Modern Taxonomy course programme, a DNA-Barcoding course:
"Integrative taxonomy and taxonomic expertise in the framework of the 
DNA-barcoding initiative", 7-11 February 2011, Muséum national 
d'Histoire naturelle, Paris.
Training coordinator: Sarah Samadi.

The course is preferentially for PhD students. Registration is free.
Deadline for application is approaching: before 10 December 2010.

Info at:
http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/modern-taxonomy-course-programme-2010-2011

With best regards,
