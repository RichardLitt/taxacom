The bird genus homonym is way senior to the beetle genus and on the beetle
genus page there is a disambiguation warning thus, with a direct link to
the bird genus.

The name Mimus is ambiguous and also refers to:

    * Kingdom: Animalia
    * Phylum: Chordata
    * Class: Aves
    * Order: Passeriformes
    * Family: Mimidae
    * Genus: Mimus
