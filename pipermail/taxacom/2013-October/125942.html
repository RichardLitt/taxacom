Basic Wikipedia policy is that it does not particularly matter if 
something is true or not. An article is supposed to give a good 
representation of what is in the literature, presenting the various 
points of view in proportion to how much support they enjoy. 
This is called Neutral Point of View (NPOV).

[Of course there are many articles on Wikipedia that violate
this fundamental policy, and that do claim to present The Truth,
especially articles on taxa, where The Truth is commonplace]
