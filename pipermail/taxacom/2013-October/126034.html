As Rafael emphasized, the fact that localities of naturalised or even cultivated plants are not distinguished from natural ones is a serious problem. Also, with many birds, breeding records need to be distinguished, otherwise the maps will be much inferior to those in standard treatments such as Handbook of the birds of the world.

I don't understand why the Canada/US border is still showing up so clearly in many groups (have a look at Papilionidae for example). Why are most US entomological collections still not incorporated?

Mike: GBIF may not be wasting our time directly - you don't have to look at it -but presumably it is all paid for by us as taxpayers.
