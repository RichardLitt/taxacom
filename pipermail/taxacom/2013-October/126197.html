2013 
 
International community of mycologists (unite.ut.ee) published in
Molecular Ecology paper on DNA sequence based species hypotheses
(http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mec.12481/abstract [1] ). 
There is also short introduction to the Species Hypotheses (SH) in our
homepage unite.ut.ee 

We are looking forward to use DwC vocabulary for the SHs and therefore
are most thankful if any DwC expert could explore the UNITE SH approach
and give us feedback if there is DwC vocabulary which can be utilised
for the SHs. 

I and our developer Kessy are in TDWG conference in Florence and ready
to discuss this topic if any DwC expert will show up. During coffe
breaks I will stand close to our poster "Towards a unified paradigm for
sequence based species of Fungi"
