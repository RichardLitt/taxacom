Ray,

as a WP editor I looked at the page about you. Unless you were referring to
some other page, that page is first up not protected, and second has not
had any significant content editing since February 2012, and very
sporadically before that. There have in the past been some discussions
about several issues that have seemed contentious, but seemed to have come
to a consensus view in the end. As has been mentioned you can as the person
the page is about visit https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AUTOPROB to
have your issues reviewed. No editor can just delete a page without reason,
nor can we edit it without any information. I would recommend you do not
edit it yourself as that would cause issues with NPoV policies on WP and
would therefore be revoked in all likelihood. If you don't like it I guess
you will have to go through AUTOPROB and follow up with it there.

Cheers, Scott
