Small correction, Donat: the Biodiversity Data Journal is free for the
authors.

I would like to point your attention on the term Advanced Open
Access<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2013-10/pp-aoa102313.php>.
Open access is not just payments or waivers and being open is not
sufficient enough! The quality of services to improve the discoverability
and especially re-usability of knowledge matters!

Cheers,
Lyubomir
