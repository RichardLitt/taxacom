Being judged solely by impact factor is not good. However, having 
recently researched the topic for a report, I am now thinking it is the 
scientists themselves who are driving the demand for high impact 
factors, more than their funders.
After all, what scientist wouldn't want their research published in 
Nature or Science.
Quentin
