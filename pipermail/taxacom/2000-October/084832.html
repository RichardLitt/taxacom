As part of the Manual of Grasses for North America project, we developed a
document listing all the counties (and some metropolitan areas) in the
U.S.A. to aid in gathering county-level data for grass distributions in
North America.  It occurred to me that there might be others interested in
such a listing. I have posted it, as a word document, to the Web - it can
be accessed from http://herbarium.usu.edu/ - click on "county list,
U.S.A." in left hand column.  I shall make a pdf version available as soon
as I have the latest version of Acrobat installed; mine is half a version
too old.  I could also send it as an email attachment but, being lazy,
would prefer not to.
