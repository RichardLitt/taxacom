2000 
The Entomological Collections Network will meet at McGill University
in downtown Montreal on Saturday December 2, (and Sunday December 3
if necessary) with a reception on Saturday evening. The campus is
within walking distance from several downtown hotels, including those
being used for the ESC/ESA joint annual meeting. We have titles from
a number of speakers, but there is still room for additional
presentations on collection issues, informatics, inventories, and
surveys etc. Further information on hotels, registration, and
transportations will follow in the week of October 23-27.

Contact Terry Wheeler (Ste Anne de Bellevue) for information about
local arrangements at wheeler at nrs.mcgill.ca and Jeff Cumming (Ottawa)
at cummingjm at em.agr.ca for details on the program.
