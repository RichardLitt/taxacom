Cladist and non cladist proponents hold to their views just as strongly as
each other, so its like casting stones within a glass house. It was Popper
or someone else who argued that scientists should be bold or assertive
about the views they hold (not being lukewarm about it). Holding strongly
to one's views is how science works and as far as I can see is a good thing
- at least it makes for interesting argument/controversy (if one is not
against that aspect of science). Science would otherwise be quite dull.

Sidenote - the beaten opponent also tries to find someone to blame. Losing
in politics is the same as 'losing' in science. No one's to blame any more
than anyone else.

John Grehan
