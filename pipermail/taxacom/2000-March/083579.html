2000 
This is to announce the second conference of the Southern African
Society for Systematic Biologists, to be held from 10-15 July 2000 at
Mtunzini, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.
A varied programme is planned in this subtropical area with easy access
to game reserves and other areas of biological interest.
For further information please visit our website:
http://contra.biology.und.ac.za/sassb
or e-mail contrafa at gwise.sci.und.ac.za or brothers at zoology.unp.ac.za
We look forward to welcoming you.
