Database Programmer Position
Specify Software Project
Natural History Museum and Biodiversity Research Center
University of Kansas


Part-time (75%) or full time position beginning as soon as possible.  For
the highly motivated individual, we have excellent benefits, a casual
University environment and the opportunity to work with scientific database
project.

Required qualifications: A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and 1 to 3
years professional programming experience OR 5 years professional
programming experience; Demonstrated ability to program or run applications
in Windows 95+/NT environment and design and/or maintain relational
databases and formulate SQL statements; Minimum 1 year professional
experience programming in an object-oriented programming language; Good
written and oral communications skills. Review of applications will begin
on March 17 and will continue until the position is filled. Application
materials include a cover letter addressing all qualifications, resume and
three references.

For a complete position announcement contact: Kathy Coggins, Specify
Project Manager,
Biodiversity Research Center, Dyche Hall, University of Kansas, Lawrence,
KS 66045.
(785) 864-4621, kcoggins at falcon.cc.ukans.edu or http://usobi.org/Specify/Jobs.
EO/AA Employer.
