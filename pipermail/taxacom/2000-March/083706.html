TO ALL AUCHENORRHYNCHA WORKERS.

(if you don't know what Auchenorrhyncha means
then you don't need to read on)

Following a decision taken at the 10th International Auchenorrhyncha
Congress in Cardiff in October 1999 a website has been established for
Auchenorrhyncha workers.

Tymbal - the Auchenorrhyncha Website was launched earlier this year at
http://www.agric.nsw.gov.au/Hort/ascu/tymbal/tymbal.htm

The aim of the Tymbal website is to provide a means of contact and
communication between workers on leafhoppers, planthoppers, cicadas and
their relatives. I would like to receive reports of current research,
research requests, notices of relevant meetings, reports and photographs
from past meetings and any other matters that you feel might be of
interest to fellow workers.

As editor of the site, I reserve the right to have any contribution peer
reviewed in order to maintain the quality and relevance of the
information presented.

Please enjoy

Murray
