I recently developed maps in ArcView showing the location of herbaria in the
48 contiguous states of the US and southern Canada.  The purpose of the maps
was simply to help us decide which herbaria to ask for help in filling in
the gaps in some of the distribution we have gathered for the Manual of
Grasses for North America, but it occurred to me that they might be useful
to others. I have, therefore, posted them to the Web:
http://biology.usu.edu/herbarium/herbne.htm
