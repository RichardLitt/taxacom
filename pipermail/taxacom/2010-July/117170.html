2010 
An additional Ph.D. position is open at the Florida Museum of Natural  
History and in the Department of Biology, University of Florida,  
starting on Fall 2011, under the supervision of Dr. Nico Cellinese.  
The successful applicant will work on the systematics, evolution, and  
biogeography of Mediterranean Campanulaceae with special focus on the  
origin and evolution of island endemics of the Aegean archipelago and  
surrounding, using chloroplast and nuclear DNA sequences.  Active  
interaction with international collaborators and extensive fieldwork  
in the Aegean Archipelago and surrounding areas are expected.  
Demonstrated experience in molecular, phylogenetic methods and/or  
ecological niche modeling is highly desirable. Guidelines to apply can  
be found at http://www.biology.ufl.edu/Graduate/Application.aspx  
(applications are due by the 15th December, 2010).

For any information, please contact Nico Cellinese  
ncellinese(at)flmnh.ufl.edu
