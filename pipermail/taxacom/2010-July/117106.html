It is not about formats: it is about reading content online rather than
hard cover.

If it where about format, then I would make the argument and stress, that,
just as you point out with your discussion below, should not have it own
format but be rather generic and with the content with some basic semantic
mark-up. The other point is, that it has to be open access and archived
