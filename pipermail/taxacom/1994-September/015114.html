Please post this ANNOUNCEMENT on the bulletin board:

PLANT SYSTEMATIST
Duke University

The Department of Botany invites applications and nominations for a
tenure-track position at the assistant professor level to begin September
1995.  Appointment at the associate professor level may be possible.
Candidates should possess taxonomic expertise with a particular plant
group, together with experience in modern approaches to plant systematics.
Send  vitae, a statement of research and teaching interests, and names of
three references to:

 Professor Janis Antonovics, Chair of the Search Committee, Department of
Botany, Duke University, Box  90338, Durham NC 27708-0338.

Applications received by November 15 will be guaranteed consideration.

Duke University is an Equal Opportunity/Affirmative Action Employer.
