1994 
Come to Edmonton, Alberta June 15 to 19, 1995 for the annual meetings of
the American Society of Ichthyologists and Herpetologists (ASIH),
American Elasmobranch Society (AES) and Herpetologists League (HL) on the
campus of the University of Alberta (with its museums of zoology and
paleontology).  This will be the 75th annual meeting for the ASIH!

For further information, please contact:

We look forward to seeing you in Edmonton in June!
