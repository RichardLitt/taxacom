Please post this ANNOUNCEMENT on the bulletin board:

GRADUATE FELLOWSHIPS
IN PLANT SYSTEMATICS

THE DEPARTMENT OF BOTANY
DUKE UNIVERSITY

        THE DEPARTMENT OF BOTANY OF DUKE UNIVERSITY IS PLEASED TO ANNOUNCE
THE AVAILABILITY OF GRADUATE FELLOWSHIPS IN PLANT SYSTEMATICS FOR 1995.
THESE FELLOWSHIPS HAVE BEEN MADE POSSIBLE BY A GRANT FROM THE A.W. MELLON
FOUNDATION.  APPLICANTS MAY PURSUE Ph.D. STUDIES ON ANY PLANT GROUP (OR
ACROSS PLANT GROUPS) INCLUDING ALGAE, FUNGI, LICHENS, BRYOPHYTES, AND
VASCULAR PLANTS.  THESE AWARDS INCLUDE TUITION, STIPEND AND MEDICAL
BENEFITS.  SEPARATE FUNDS ARE AVAILABLE (ON A COMPETITIVE BASIS TO PROGRAM
STUDENTS) FOR RESEARCH TRAVEL AND LABORATORY SUPPLIES.

        ALL APPLICANTS FOR THE PH.D. DEGREE IN BOTANY AT DUKE UNIVERSITY
WILL BE CONSIDERED AUTOMATICALLY FOR THESE FELLOWSHIPS IF THEIR
APPLICATIONS INDICATE PLANS TO STUDY SYSTEMATICS.  APPLICATIONS MAY BE
OBTAINED BY WRITING TO :

The Director Of Graduate Studies, Department Of Botany, Duke University,
Durham, NC 27708-0338
or to
The Graduate School, Duke University, Box 90070, Durham, NC  27708-0070.

DEADLINE FOR APPLICATIONS IS DECEMBER 31, 1994.

INTERESTED STUDENTS ARE ENCOURAGED TO CONTACT THE MELLON PROGRAM FACULTY
DIRECTLY FOR MORE INFORMATION
