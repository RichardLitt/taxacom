This touches on some reasons why the Nature decision will fail, because in
fact there is no parallel in it to GenBank. Not only are GenBank sequences
publicly available, the whole operation is reasonably well-financed. If
some "deep pocket" wanted to start a "TaxaBank" with funding and procedures
similar to GenBank, in short order the Codes would come around.
