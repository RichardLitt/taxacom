1995 
Forwarded message from Rijksherbarium/Hortus Botanicus, Leiden, the Netherlands

RIJKSHERBARIUM will move to a new building, the modern Van Steenis Gebouw
near the BioScience Park in Leiden, as announced by the Director Pieter
Baas. Due to the move, rehousing and rearrangement, the Rijksherbarium
collections (L) will be closed for visitors and no loans will be processed
between August 1 and December 1, 1995.
