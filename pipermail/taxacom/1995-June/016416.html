Re: National Biological Service/congressional addresses

A little further digging has yielded some email addresses for the
some members of the US Senate and House of Representatives.  About
60 in each legislative body have email addresses: you can find these
by gophering to: gopher at house.gov or
                 gopher://ftp.senate.gov/00h/member_e-mail_addresses
 or you may find the house addresses on WWW at www.house.gov.

Quoting part of the House email address list:

>        The Document Room of the U.S. House of Representatives
> has also been assigned an electronic public mailbox.//snip//
> The House Document room distributes House Bills, Resolutions,
> Reports, Documents, and Public Laws for the 104th Congress.
> House Reports, Documents, and Public Laws for the 103rd Congress
> are also maintained.  The public can obtain 2 copies of up to 12
> different House documents per day.  Customers should include
> their telephone number with their mailing address.  All document
> orders will be filled and mailed the same day they are received.
> For more detailed information or questions, call the Document
> Room at (202) 225-3456, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.
>
>     House Document Room
>     Rm. B18 Ford House Office Building
>     Washington, D.C.  20515
>     HDOCS at HR.HOUSE.GOV

I did not find any similar service for the Senate.
