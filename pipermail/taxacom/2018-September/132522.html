Dear forum,

I am searching for copies the following papers. I have tried all the usual
sources, without success. Do you have a copy you are prepared to share?
Thanks in advance for your constructive help.

1- *Delucchi, V.; Renfer, A.; Aeschlimann, J.P. 1974*, Contribution à la
connaissance des lépidoptères associés au mélèze en haute altitude et de
leurs parasitoids. *Recherches Agronomiques Suisse * *13(1/2)*:435-451

2- *Čapeck, M. 1972:* Verzeichnis der aus Schädlingsinsekten erzogenen
Parasiten. Teil V. - Die Brackwespen (Braconidae: Hymenoptera). –
Entomologické Problemy Bratislava *10*: 125-140.

3- *Roman A., 1925* - Schwedische Schlupfwespen, alte und neue. Arkiv for
Zoologi. 1*7A(4)(1924):*1-34.

4- *Thompson W.R. **A **1953.* Catalogue of the Parasites and Predators of
Insect Pests. Section 2, Host Parasite Catalogue. Part 2, Hosts of
Hymenoptera (Agaonidae to Braconidae) Commonwealth Institute of Biological
Control; Ottawa, ON, Canada.

5- *Capek M, Lukas J. 1989.* Apocrita Parasitica, Ichneumoidea, Braconidae.
Acta Faunistica Entomologica Musei Nationalis Pragae. *19:* 27-44

6- *Papp J., 1959:* The Microgaster Latr., Microplitis Foerst., and
Hygroplitis Thoms. Species of the Carpathian Basin (Hymenoptera,
Braconidae). Annales Historico-Naturales Musei Nationalis Hungarici.* 51*:
397-413.

7- *O’Connor, J.P., Nash,R. and Achterberg, C. van, **1999**. A catalogue
of the Irish Braconidae (Hymenoptera: Ichneumonoidea)* Occasional
Publication of  the Irish Biogeographical Society No.4  123 p. 7 figs., 4
plates Complete synonymic catalogue. Lists type material.

8- *Lacatusu, M. and Filipescu, C. 1989*. [Insecta. Hymenoptera: Braconidae
- general section. Subfamilies Cardiochilinae, Microgasterinae, Acaeliinae
and Miracinae.] Fauna Republicii Socialiste Romania. *9(11): * 1-318.

All the best,
