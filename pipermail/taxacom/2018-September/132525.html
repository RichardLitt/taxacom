Unfortunately, it is more complicated than that.The original work was published e-only and without evidence in the work itself of registration in ZooBank.The journal PLOS BIOLOGY does not provide an intended archive under its registration in ZooBank.Therefore, this work cannot be regarded as published for the purposes of zoological nomenclature in its electronic form.Thus, unless there is a printed version of the work, the correction does not point to a published work.Even the correction itself does not comply with the ICZN in its electronic form.The placozoan names therefore remain unavailable.The Commission is aware of the problems associated with such published but non-compliant works.Until a solution has been implemented, it is strongly recommended to consult with the Commission before attempting to 'resurrect' names and nomenclatural acts.
