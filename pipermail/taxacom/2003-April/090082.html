Rick and All:

We will present testimony in support of the University of Nebraska State
Museum to the Academic Planning Committee at UNL, tomorrow, 9 April, 2003.
Wish us luck.  Terry Yates will appear before the committee, representing
the systematics community in the U.S.A.

We plan to make a really good presentation to the committee tomorrow.

At the same time, we hope that we can get back to work on taxonomy and
systematics!

Thank you ALL for your letters of support.  They have been printed and
presented to the APC for our meeting tomorrow!

Scott
