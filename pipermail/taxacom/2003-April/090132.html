BIOSIS is the pleased to announce that the article  proposing Zoological
Record as a register for new animal names, and published in Bulletin of
Zoological Nomenclature 60(1), is available at two locations on the web:

http://www.biosis.org/register.html

http://www.iczn.org/register.htm

A full version of the article is available for download as an Adobe Acrobat
PDF file at both locations.

We would continue to encourage authors to notify us of the publication of
new names by submitting reprints of articles to:
