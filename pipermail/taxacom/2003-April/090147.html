Request for Cultivated Information of Metasequoia glyptostroboides
(Taxodiaceae ~ Cupressaceae) in your local area, either a country, state or
garden / arboretum sightings. Records can pertain to either a single tree
or numerous individuals on a plantation. Please email me at
jinshuangma at bbg.org with it's size, height, Diameter at Breast Height, date
of cultivation, as well as detailed locality data - Information will be
combined into final report of a worldwide survey currently being conducted,
and will be recorded on the natural history of Metasequoia
glyptostroboides - Thank you very much for your help!

- For detail information about this tree, please see www.metasequoia.org
