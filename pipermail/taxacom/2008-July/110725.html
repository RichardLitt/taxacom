The University of Gothenburg invites interested students to apply for  
the advanded masters level course in Systematic Biology (November- 
January). It is an advanced course in taxonomic theory and practice  
aiming to deepen the knowledge of philosophical, historical, and  
theoretical issues in contemporary taxonomy. The course also includes  
practical sessions in phylogenetic analysis and nucleotide  
sequencing. It prepares for a degree project in botanical and/or  
zoological systematics. Send an email to  
studievagledare at biology.gu.se, telling that you are applying to  
particate in the course Sysematic Biology (BIO520). They will ask you  
for any necessary information.
