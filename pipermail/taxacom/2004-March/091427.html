THE NATURAL HISTORY MUSEUM, LONDON
DEPARTMENT OF BOTANY

SYSTEMS AND DATABASE MANAGER

Starting Salary ?26,490-?32,775 (Depending on experience)

The Natural History Museum, London invites applications from experienced 
professionals  to manage database and other systems in the Department of 
Botany.  Duties will include providing advice and technical expertise for 
development of databases; developing  web interfaces for databases and 
advising on compliance to standards. The post holder has responsibility for 
the  departmental IT budget and will also be expected to support 
development of GIS resources.

Candidates will be expected to have a degree or post graduate qualification 
in a biological science related subject, information technology and 
preferably both.  Experience in database and web development are essential.

For full details of requirements, a full job description and equal 
opportunities form please Email: SYSNHM at hs-scientific.co.uk, or write to: 
The Natural History Museum, c/o Hudson Shribman Scientific Recruitment, 
Vernon House, Sicilian Ave, London, WC1A 2QS, UK. Application will be by 
CV, together with a covering letter outlining how you match the 
requirements for this post, and the names and addresses of three referees; 
two of which must be work referees, including your current/ most recent 
employer.  Please indicate if you would prefer referees not to be contacted 
prior to interview

Closing date: 12th March 2004.
