The American Museum of Natural History is seeking a Scientific Assistant in
Ichthyology.

Responsibilities involve providing assistance with the curation of a large,
worldwide collection of fishes, including preparation of research
materials, specimen identification and processing, bibliographic and
clerical work, data processing and analysis, and other duties as assigned.
Must be willing/able to handle specimens and preservatives.

BA/BS in biological sciences; background in ichthyology or fisheries
science and/or training in vertebrate zoology and basic computer skills
desired. Full time, $27,433/year + excellent benefits. Please email, fax or
mail resume to Dr. Scott Schaefer, Division of Vertebrate Zoology, American
Museum of Natural History, Central Park West @ 79th St, NYC, 10024, fax
212-769-5642, schaefer at amnh.org. EOE.
