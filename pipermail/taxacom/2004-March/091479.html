Hello all,

I've put the entire Latin text of Linnaeus's Philosophia Botanica
online at http://botanicallatin.org/philbot/philbot.html, lovingly
hand-typed from my own copy of the book.  (To skip my introduction, go
to http://botanicallatin.org/philbot/pbcont.html.) It includes the
full text except for the indexes, and all the illustrations. Each part
or aphorism is a separate html file.

Please let me know if you have any corrections or suggestions for
improvement.
