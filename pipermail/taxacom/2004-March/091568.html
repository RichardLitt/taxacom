A plant systematist position continues to be open at the USDA NRCS
National Plant Data Center, Baton Rouge, LA.  Play a key role on the
expanded development of the PLANTS database and Web site <plants.usda.gov>
and associated applications; developing polyclave identification keys;
maintaining and developing PLANTS data; and working with the botanical
community.  PLANTS currently receives over 12 million hits a month and
serves the federal, state, and local governments; organizations; plus the
public.
