2004 
Flora of China Volume 5 (Ulmaceae through Basellaceae) is now browseable
and searchable via the web interface to the on-line database:
http://flora.huh.harvard.edu:8080/flora/flora_page.jsp?flora_id=2
http://flora.huh.harvard.edu:8080/flora/search.jsp?flora_id=2

In addition, web-based interactive identification keys (ActKey)
http://flora.huh.harvard.edu:8080/actkey/index.jsp
are available for large genera including Elatostema, Ficus, Pilea, and
Polygonum in this volume.

Comments are always welcome.  Thank you.

Anthony R. Brach
Editorial Assistant
