I've modified something on Wikipedia, after signing up as a contributor, and my modification has not been changed for a couple years. Apparently, if you are signed up as an editor, you are notified if "your" entry has been changed by someone else.
 
Some entries in Wikipedia are naturally targets of kooks, kids and antinomians but I think most entries are stable and secure. Wikipedia has great value as a place to start study, and entries commonly list major hardcopy sources of information.
