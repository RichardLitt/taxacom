At 12:37 PM 2/26/2009, Janet Sullivan wrote:
>OK, after a bit of time in the dictionary and on the internet I give up. 
>Why is New Jersey called "Nova Caesarea" and New York "Nova Eboracum"?

New Jersey is named for the Isle of Jersey in the English Channel; the 
Roman name of the island was Caesarea.  Similarly, Eboracum was the Roman 
name for the English city of York, from which New York takes its name.
