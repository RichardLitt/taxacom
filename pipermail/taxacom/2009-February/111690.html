Hi all,

One take-home message from this thread so far might be a recognition that a single point of collation of all this information is definitely beneficial - and then a commitment to use it and keep the relevant information up-to-date. My vote would be to use the TDWG database cited below, since this has a reasonable critical mass already and also to my mind is a reasonable if not exact fit with the TDWG mandate - also you do not have to be a member of TDWG to contribute to it.

(A quick check and yes, I did remember to register the three main projects I am involved with there, after some prodding - thanks to Lee Belbin!)
