Subject: Small grants for plant taxonomic research by young African
scientists

 

Dear colleagues,

 

this is to inform you about the Moabi Foundation of Wageningen, the
Netherlands. We provide small grants for plant taxonomic research by
young African scientists to assist them with the start-up of their
career. Grants are for visits to the Biosystematics Group and herbarium
in Wageningen, support for field work, visits to congresses and for
finishing publications.

Please support us, so if you sympathise with this goal go to:
www.moabi.nl <http://www.moabi.nl/>
