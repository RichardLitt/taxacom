A fairly useful source for geo-referencing Indian collection data is

Lozupone, Patsy, Bruce M. Beehler, and S. Dillon Ripley. 2004.
Ornithological Gazetteer of the Indian Subcontinent. Center for
Applied Biodiversity Science, Conservation International,
Washington, DC, USA. 

(Many of the older bird and insect workers in the region were all-round collector-naturalists.) It used to be available online but no more. Let me know if you want the PDF. However on p. 111 it has the entry for "Mangpu" as

"Not found. A locality at 1100 m in Sikkim.
Fieldworker/publication: Handbook 8: 205 (1636)."

http://geonames.nga.mil/ggmagaz/geonames4.asp
gives Mangphu as Decimal Lat:26.966667 Long: 88.366667 

hope that helps,
