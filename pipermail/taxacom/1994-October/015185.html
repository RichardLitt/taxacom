1994 
An annotated bibliography of entomological literature related to the
California Islands is now available on the the biodiversity and biological
collections gopher (muse.bio.cornell.edu 70), under "Biodiversity
Resources/Faunas." Further information on the bibliography, which includes
over 1000 citations, can be found in the associated "readme" file on same
gopher menu.  The bibliography will be mirrored on the Bishop Museum
gopher (bishop.bishop.hawaii.org 70) in the near future.  Questions,
comments and additions can be directed to Scott Miller, Bishop Museum, Box
19000A, Honolulu, Hawaii 96817, USA.
