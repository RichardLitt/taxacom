TENTH CONGRESS OF THE ENTOMOLOGICAL SOCIETY
OF SOUTHERN AFRICA 1995


Rhodes University campus
Monday 3rd June to Friday 7th July, 1995


                     FIRST CIRCULAR: SEPTEMBER 1994

       The Tenth Congress of the Entomological Society of Southern
       Africa will be hosted jointly by Rhodes University and the
           University of Fort Hare, and will be held at Rhodes
        University. As usual, registration will take place on the
      Monday; congress sessions will start on Tuesday and end with
      workshop sessions on Friday. The proposed programme includes
          paper and poster sessions, workshops, a photographic
      exhibition, informal social functions and a formal Conference
                                 dinner.
       Don't forget that the Standard Bank National Arts Festival
      starts in Grahamstown on the Thursday of the conference week!
   If you mean to stay on for the festival, you must book accomodation
            immediately. The hotels are already booked out!


Accommodation:
              Bed-and-breakfast accommodation will be available in the
Rhodes University residences which are close to the Congress
venue, at about R65 per night. We encourage you to stay there and
generate the Conference Spirit which is so easily lost when
delegates disperse. There is no official "lock-out" time; tea and
coffee will be available in the common rooms continuously; and
towels and duvets are provided!
              Inexpensive lunches will be provided if you indicate your
need on the enclosed form; otherwise they can be bought in the
campus cafeterias or in the town (but remember that the festival
days will be crowded). A series of social events are planned for
the evenings (see below).
              Hotels that are within walking distance are indicated in the
table below. Please indicate your preference for residence or
hotel accommodation on the enclosed form to assist with early
planning. We will deal only with residence bookings; please make
your own hotel bookings.

Visitors from other countries
              We encourage as many people as possible from African and
other countries to attend the Conference. If you feel that there
is anyone that we should make a point of seding the second
circular to, please let us know. We are investigating
possiblities of raising funds to pay for one or two special
guests. If you are expecting any visitors who may be interested
in taking part in our Congress please encourage them to do so.

Entomological Exhibition
              We are arranging a photography exhibition of the "Big
Twelve" insects. Photographs of  any insect taxa will be welcome,
so use this summer to fill your portfolio. If you (will) have
exhibits that you would like to submit, please tell us.

              The following events are being planned. The costs are not
yet final.
Welcoming Social Free

Sightseeing, local tours and Arts Festival
              For accompanying persons during Congress sessions, there are
museums, cultural centres, historical sites and shops to visit.
Guided tours can be arranged if enough support is indicated on
the enclosed form. The National Arts Festival starts on the
Thursday of the conference, and continues for a week afterwards.
If you intend staying on for the festival, you must book
accomodation immediately. The hotels are already fully booked!
Detailed information about other accomodation and event booking
kits are available from the Grahamstown Publicity Association,
ph. 0461 23241.

              Please return the enclosed questionaire by 5th December 1994
at the latest. Please note that only those people who respond to
the first circular will automatically receive the second circular
in February.
              We hope that you will be able to attend the Conference and
we look forward to your reply!
