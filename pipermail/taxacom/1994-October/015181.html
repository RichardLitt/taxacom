1994 
                    Eric Holttum Memorial
                  PTERIDOPHYTE SYMPOSIUM '95
                   Pteridology in Perspective

       The Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, U.K., 17-21 July, 1995
          Post Symposium Field Excursion 23-30 July, 1995

This international symposium commemorates Professor R.E. Holttum, who was
pre-eminent among the pteridologists of the 20th Century.

The Symposium will be structured largely from contributed papers with two
1/4 day poster sessions. All aspects of pteridology may be covered in
relation to both extant and fossil pteridophytes world-wide.

The second and final circular, with registration and accommodation booking
forms, is available from:
Pteridophyte Symposium '95, Miss J.M. Ide, c/o R.J. Johns, The Herbarium,
The Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, Richmond, Surrey TW9 3AE, UK.
email p.edwards at rbgkew.org.uk
fax +44 81 332 5197

The closing date for offering presentations has passed, and the programme
of oral presentations has been structured. It may be possible for late
offers to be accommodated as poster presentations; contact the co-
ordinator of the scientific programme, Josephine M. Camus, with a short
outline by email to  j.camus at nhm.ac.uk
