1994 
                   XIV Brazilian Congress of Paleontology

                      Uberaba - Minas Gerais - Brasil

                           July 23-29 1995

   Next Brazilian Congress of Paleontology will be held at Uberaba,
   Minas Gerais. The first circular was distributed recentely and main
   topics are:

   . Vertebrate Paleontology
   . Invertebrate Paleontology
   . Paleobotanics & Palinology
   . Micropaleontology
   . Paleoecology & Paleogeography

   Papers should be submited until January 20, 1995.

   Further details call:
