Ken - dispersal and vicariance are equally important in panbiogeography
- dispersal for range expansion and maintenance, vicariance for
differentiation.

Your beliefs about the upper age of taxa are noted. They are your beliefs
(and perhaps shared by others as you note) and that is OK. But not
scientifically contestable since they are just your beliefs.
