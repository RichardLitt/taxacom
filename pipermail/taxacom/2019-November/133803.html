Ken, you totally misundertand the panbiogeograhic objection. It is NOT
about calibration errors, it is about transmogrifying minimum dates from
fossil calibrations into actual or maximal dates. This is just
scientifically not possible. Therefore we do indeed dismiss
misrepresentation of minimums as maximums. Quite rightly so. That is
science.

There is nothing 'tired' about correctly pointing out that making maxiums
ages out of minimums is neither scientific or valid.



   Go beyond the Meliaceae-Simaroubaceae-Rutaceae clade, and you are likely
to see how evidence from outgroup relatives fits better with dispersal than
with vicariance.

If this is fossil based calibration then these dates are minumums only.

This gets to Ken's personal belief about how much older a taxon can be. But
this is about his beliefs and he is welcome to them, but they cannot be
scientifically contested unless there is some scientific basis specified
