1997 
A complete catalog of scientific publications of the University of Kansas
Natural History Museum and Biodiversity Research Center that deal with
living and fossil vertebrates is now available on the museum's web site at

www.nhm.ukans.edu/pubs.html.

You can browse current publications in the museum's Scientific Papers and
Special Publications series as well as other work from the museum's 50-year
publishing history.

Scientific publications that deal with other than vertebrates will be added
to the catalog in the near future.

You can place orders by e-mail from the web site, by mail or by fax.
Details are available at the web site.
