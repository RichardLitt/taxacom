Hello all:
The Pennsylvania Flora Project has placed a searchable database on line at
http://www.upenn.edu/paflora.  The database includes a checklist of all
plant taxa collected in Pennsylvania based on information from herbarium
specimens.  Searchable fields include taxon, growth habit, origin, federal
wetland status, federal and state classification of rare, threatened and
endangered plants and listings of plants covered by federal and/or state
noxious weed legislation.

Please let us know if you find our site useful and if you have suggestions
for improvements.
