I have not seen the information offered by Dr Landry, but note that in
their "complete" treatment of Lepidoptera, van Nieukerken et al. (in
Zootaxa, Zhang et al., 2011) give the authority for Adelidae as Bruand,
1850 (may be correct or incorrect, I have no other information).
