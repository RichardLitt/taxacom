Dear colleagues,

On behalf of the Organizing Committee, I wish to inform you that the 
10th International Symposium on the Biology of Terrestrial Isopods 
will be held in Budapest, Hungary, from 27 to 30 August, 2017.

The symposium will be followed by a course on the taxonomy of 
Oniscidea for students, postdocs and young researchers.

For more information, please check the symposium homepage 
http://bio.univet.hu/istib2017/main.html

Best wishes,

Stefano Taiti
