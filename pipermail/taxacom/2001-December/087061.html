>Museum Informatics/Systems Manager Position
>
>The University of Colorado Museum is accepting applications for a new
>technology position focused on the management and dissemination of natural
>history collections data.  We are seeking a detail-oriented individual
>with an interest in museums and museum collections.  Excellent time
>management skills, the ability to work with a diverse group of people in a
>changing environment, and effective verbal communication are a
>must.  Applicants must have experience in bio-informatics, library or
>archive science, and understand data flow associations with these
>systems.  Expertise with database networks and data sharing issues,
>including remote access via ODBC using SQL, serving database information
>via HTML/XML, synchronizing data, and troubleshooting basic network
>problems is essential.  Candidates will have experience with Access,
>FileMaker Pro, Paradox, and experience with server-based databases like
>Microsoft SQL server.  The desired candidate has demonstrated ability to
>work effectively on multiple platforms and operating systems, to develop,
>build and manage web sites, to use web-based wyswyg layout programs and
>data back-up systems, and to use scanning and image archiving
>applications.  A Bachelor's or technical degree in a related field and a
>minimum of one-year professional IT experience is required.  This position
>is salaried at $52,000, plus benefits, and is funded initially for a
>one-year appointment.  Please send cover letter, resume, and 3 references
>to University of Colorado Museum, Assistant Director, Henderson Building,
>218 UCB, Boulder, CO. 80309 or to cumuseum at colorado.edu  Subject:  Museum
>Informatics.  Applications are due by 5pm on January 18, 2002.  The
>University of Colorado at Boulder is committed to diversity and equality
>in education and employment.
