Entomological Collections Network, 2001 Meetings

December 8-9, 2001

Town & Country Hotel, San Diego, CA
Garden Salon 1, Conference Center, East side (Level 2)

FINAL PROGRAM AT  http://entmuseum9.ucr.edu/entmus/ECN2001.pdf

Schedule runs from 8:30 AM - 5:30 PM on Saturday, 8:30 AM - Noon on
Sunday. There will be coffee breaks at the meeting room, and a
Saturday night dinner reception (6:30 - 8:30 PM) for registered

name badges, programs, and receipts at the door.

Hope to see you there!
