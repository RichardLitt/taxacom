Dear All

I would like to draw your attention to the plant systematic Postgraduate
Scholarship that was offered as part of a series of Postgraduate
Scholarships, by the Cooperative Research Centre for Australian Weed
management (Weeds CRC), in an advertisement in The Weekend Australian 1-2
December 2001, pp 14.

The plant systematic scholarship offered is for working on the evolutionary
relationships in the Verbenaceae with particular emphasis on invasive taxa
within the genus Lantana. The successful applicant will be based at CSIRO
Plant Industries, Canberra.

For further information about this particular project access:
http://www.anbg.gov.au/cpbr/positions/lantana-phd.html

The scholarship is offered by the Cooperative Research Centre for Australian
Weed management (Weeds CRC). The scholarship is for a three-year period,
subject to satisfactory progress and approved continuation of funding by the
Weeds CRC. Applicants shout have a good Honours degree or equivalent, and
have excellent quantitative skills.

Application in TRIPLICATE including a full CV, a copy of undergraduate
transcript, a statement addressing the selection criteria, and the names and
addresses of three referees, should be forwarded to Dr. Chris Preston,
University of Adelaide, PMB 1, Glen Osmond, SA 5064. Phone (08)-8303-7237

Closing date for applications: 5.00 pm, Wednesday 9 January, 2002

Reference number: CRAWM PGO1

For further Information about the institutions involved in this particular
CRC Australian Weeds Management Postgraduate scholarship access the
following links

The Weeds CRC: http://www.waite.adelaide.edu.au/CRCWMS
The CRC program: http://www.isr.gov.au/crc/index.html
CRIRO Plant Industries: http://www.pi.csiro.au
