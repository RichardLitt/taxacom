    I made an error in my earlier post.  Order Rodentiformes is my
emendation for Rodentia.  Since most chordate orders already used the
-iformes suffix, I chose to use that ending for Chordata, and the -ida
suffix for the invertebrate phyla (following the Treatise on Invertebrate
Paleontology).
                        -----Ken Kinman
