The herbarium at California State University, Chico, USA (CHSC) has more
journals available to trade for herbarium specimens.  Most of this is new
material since the last offering.  If you are interested, make an offer
(number of specimens and general description) for any part of this
offering.  Particular interests here are Cyperaceae, Ericaceae, and
Lamiaceae, though such material is not required for a trade.  Offers of any
herbarium specimen material from any part of the world will be considered. 
We prefer to trade with another herbarium or similar institution.
