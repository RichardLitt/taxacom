The Flora of North America Association is pleased to announce publication
of FNA Volume 12:  Magnoliophyta: Vitaceae to Garryaceae by Oxford
University Press.  This is the 20th published volume in the planned
30-volume series. The link for ordering copies is

https://global.oup.com/academic/product/fna-volume-12-magnoliophyta-vitaceae-to-garryaceae-9780190643720?q=9780190643720&lang=en&cc=us.

A list of families included in the volume can be found by clicking the
"Table of Contents" tab.  Many thanks to the 53 authors who contributed
treatments to the volume.
