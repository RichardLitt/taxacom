Check the article in Systematic Entomology:

SMITH, A. D. (2013). Phylogenetic revision of the North American Asidini
(Coleoptera: Tenebrionidae). Systematic Entomology, Volume 38, Issue 3,
pages 585-614, July 2013.

A.D. Smith himself considers it as the place where he is describing all the
new taxa.
See his Abstract:
"...Seven current genera: Craniotus LeConte, Heterasida Casey, Litasida
Casey, Microschatia Solier, Pelecyphorus Solier, Philolithus Lacordaire,
Stenomorpha Solier, are redescribed, and Ardamimicus Smith gen.n.,
Ferveoventer Smith gen.n. and Micrasida Smith gen.n. are described,
including Ardamimicus cognatoi Smith sp.n., Ferveoventer browni Smith sp.n.
and Micrasida obrienorum Smith sp.n. ..."

Adolf Ceska, Victoria, British Columbia, Canada
