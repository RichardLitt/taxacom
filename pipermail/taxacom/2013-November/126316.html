Donating only one copy would not make much sense. Besides of the problem
with his or her fire insurance this student would also have the minor
problem that his or her Ph. D. degree would not be recognised (of course
the library reports back to the university authorities that the 50 copies
were donated).
I've never heard of such a story, but maybe only because students in
German usually don't have their own garage. And those who have maybe they
don't like to see it burnt down, so they prefer donating all the books at
once.

Francisco
