We observe individual organisms in nature.  We note that multiple individual organism share certain characteristics. We notice patterns of shared characteristics, and draw lines around clusters of organisms. Those clusters exist at many different taxonomic levels.  We do not "observe" taxa (species, or higher ranks) in nature.  We only "observe" individual organisms, and our pattern-matching brains like to see patterns among sets of those organisms.
