Dear Colleagues,

I am pleased to inform you that the digital version of the herbarium of 
P?l Kitaibel (1757-1817) is available online: 
http://muzeum.arcanum.hu/kitaibel/opt/a130921.htm?v=pdf&a=start
P?l Kitaibel was the most significant early scientist, first of all 
botanist, of the Carpathian Basin and the author of a number of vascular 
plant taxa, mostly together with Adam Waldstein (learn more here: 
http://www.nhmus.hu/modules/Tar-Noveny/studia/barina2007a.pdf).
The home page is in Hungarian, but it is possible to search for words 
(=szavak), specimen ID (=azonos?t?), original name (=eredeti n?v), text 
of label (c?dulasz?veg), revising person (=revide?l?) and for the taxon 
name after revision(s) (=rev?zi? ut?ni n?v). Use wildcards as *: any 
text; !: one character; ?: in place of 0 or 1
