Dear list members,

I would like to draw your attention to the next International Congress of Dipterology (http://www.icd8.org) and the symposium on molecular identification of Diptera that we are organizing. The symposium will cover all aspects related to molecular identification, life stage association and species delimitation. At this point, we are interested in hearing from potential participants at this symposium and would be grateful if you could send me or any other person from the organizing committee a hint if you plan to attend (see webpage for addresses).

The congress is held in Potsdam, Germany 10-15 August 2014. Early bird registration ends February 1.
