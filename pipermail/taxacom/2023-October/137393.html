A Taxacom subscribers found him/herself in an e-mail routing problem due to
an unrelated e-mail account problem.  That caused Taxacom to start
unsubscribing people in an attempt to correct the issue.  Our university
e-mail administrator traced and isolated the problem a few minutes ago and
resubbed everyone who was erroneously unsubbed.

If you sent an email to the Taxacom list in the last 24 hours or so, you
might resend it if you are concerned that the entire list might not have
received it.
