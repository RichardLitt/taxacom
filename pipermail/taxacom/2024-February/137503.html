Dear Colleagues,

On behalf of Dr. Peter Raven, we are on the hunt for missing issues of the journal Onira: Botanical Leaflets, published in Chile by Pierfelice Ravenna. We have not so far been able to locate these in online catalogs, so if you have any of the series and would be kind enough to check your institutions' holdings for the missing ones, that would be most sincerely appreciated. The following issues are missing from major herbaria with strong botanical holdings:

Vol. 2, numbers 1-4
Vol. 3, number 3
Vol. 8, number 15
Vol. 9, numbers 1 and 5
Vol. 10, number 8

Many thanks!
