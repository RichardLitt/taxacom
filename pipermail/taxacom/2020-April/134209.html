In consideration of a comment sent to me separately, I would note that
Norman and I did agree on vicariance as the driver of evolution and that
centers of origin for the evolution of allopatry was a fantasy. And even
though he disagreed with aspects (at least) of panbiogeographic methodology
he did agree that it generated the same conclusions about these matters and
from recollection he did not oppose the tectonic predictions made from
panbiogeographic methodology (in other words the 'wrong' method still  gave
the 'right' answers). These issues certainly generated some interesting
debate.

John Grehan
