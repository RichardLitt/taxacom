2019 
We have just released a new, improved version, of the FLORAMO app!
Flóramo is a field guide to identify over 250 species of wild plants found in the páramo, a unique vegetation type at the top of the Andes Mountains of tropical South America. It contains field images, species descriptions, and an identification key using life forms, flower colors or scientific names. Additional features include searches into the Missouri Botanical Garden Tropicos® database. This free app is now available for iPhone (NEW) and Android systems, in English and español.
