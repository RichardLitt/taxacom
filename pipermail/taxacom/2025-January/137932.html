Ethanol is not illegal where I live, but taxed the same as alcoholic 
beverages, hence practically unaffordable. I have used isopropanol 
(isopropyl alcohol) for years. It is cheaper, readily available, and 
does the job. I did not keep the reference, but saw a study comparing 
ant specimens preserved in different liquids which suggested that 
preservation in isopropanol is not an impediment to DNA extraction, 
although I admittedly have no experience of this. Colour loss is not 
worse than colour loss in ethanol.

Someone mentioned propylene glycol. Easy to find in many parts of Africa 
but only if you go to the right place: it is the liquid used in solar 
heaters, and suppliers of those also sell glycol. Nobody else knows what 
it is.
