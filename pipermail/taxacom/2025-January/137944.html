Dear Colleagues and Friends,

The two open-access journals “Diversity” and “Taxonomy”, published by MDPI, Basel, Switzerland, are inviting you to contribute to the Special Issue “Diversity and Taxonomy of Scarabaeoidea” (https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mdpi.com%2Fjournal%2Fdiversity%2Fspecial_issues%2F5993P88LY3&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cdce173ca21cc4379d9a308dd3e7c6594%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638735426188940888%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Tvgw6tfk6aQ0qWiT9bKcr8%2FCTJAARU9d2kPzDpeUXlI%3D&reserved=0, https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mdpi.com%2Fjournal%2Ftaxonomy%2Fspecial_issues%2FIC75WN3I5C&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cdce173ca21cc4379d9a308dd3e7c6594%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638735426188956407%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=VEsDaUmUZD2nbxJWlXAGS0EaffjZ4W4SSkFGxqXNILA%3D&reserved=0). I agreed to serve as Editor-in-Chief for this Special Issue after I had excellent experience with the journal Diversity in 2023, in terms of quality of peer review, editorial handling, and speed of publication.
As these journals are open-access, they require an Article Processing Charge, which is higher for the established journal Diversity (https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mdpi.com%2Fjournal%2Fdiversity%2Fapc&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cdce173ca21cc4379d9a308dd3e7c6594%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638735426188964654%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=a7XQRWtWpc7LV2KgOySYDmVEID2BHswwFU8YoqlzOpQ%3D&reserved=0) than for the relatively new journal Taxonomy (https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mdpi.com%2Fjournal%2Ftaxonomy%2Fapc&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cdce173ca21cc4379d9a308dd3e7c6594%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638735426188972927%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=3JM7hO481qfbDIF1e1eU0kZyG%2FcZX8mo%2FOXQIy9n2Bg%3D&reserved=0). If you do not have funding for such fees, reductions are possible in cases. Let me know what you would like to submit, and I find out what we can do to accommodate your budget situation.
Manuscripts of purely taxonomic content would be assigned to Taxonomy, manuscripts considering broader questions of diversity would be assigned to Diversity.
The language of publication is English. For authors who are not fluent in English, the publisher offers a English Language Editing Service, which is not included in the Article Processing Charge, but seems to be reasonable: https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mdpi.com%2Fauthors%2Fenglish&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Cdce173ca21cc4379d9a308dd3e7c6594%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638735426188980613%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=huPlIcdGkYZgqqH83j%2B4zEkxT8J6OUp6L1nzQGoGp%2FE%3D&reserved=0
If you are not afraid of having your manuscript published fast (and well-reviewed), please consider this opportunity. The current submission deadline is 31 October 2025.

Thanks, and all the best

Frank
