"For free"! Congratulations, John, this means that some people out there think your papers would enhance their journal. Free means you don't have to pay the APC funds. I'm always happy to get a waiver of open access payment because open access is the only real way to get people to actually read one's stuff, cherish it, print it out and scribble notes in the margins.

I've seen good books published by good authors with good publishers on topics of immense interest, and they cost US$250 to 300, nowadays. Who reads these? You either can't afford them or mark your thoughts in them if they are library books and who goes to a library except maybe once a month to browse the journals?

We authors are in a squeeze, needing open access publication but unless your academic outfit is rich, you gotta pay the APC yourself or sell your copyright to the Devil. "For free!" is a marvel, given that the new publication model is still too new.


Although MDPI seems like a franchising house with some of its myriad journals being amateurish and somewhat venal, the ones that I have gone to are fully professional and the reviewers are genuine. (I just got one of my papers raked over the coals by a disgustingly intelligent and knowledgeable reviewer for the journal Sustainability. If I fix it up, apparently, I will get it published although I will need a second mortgage for the APC. :))

There are poison journal publishers, though. Crimson Publications is one, and others are whistle-blown on a Web site. For these, yes, use your email filter, otherwise feel complimented.
