You may be interested in my perspective piece that just came out in
Systematics & Biodiversity entitled 'Combining new technology with classic
taxonomy to overcome hurdles to discovering dark taxa'.

https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoi.org%2F10.1080%2F14772000.2025.2454014&data=05%7C02%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7C3f3ec3e8946b42174e0e08dd4217ffe2%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638739393050179799%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=eLc8O6hQIEqouk2tiaeMuBkbMaoM0jGTylQr%2Bbs%2F%2BgI%3D&reserved=0

My intention is to remind people of important caveats in using automation
to discover species, and to reaffirm the place of traditional taxonomy. You
may not agree.
