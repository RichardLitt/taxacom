1996 
The Flora of China Project, a collaborative project to publish the first
modern English-language account of the approximately 30,000 species of
vascular plants of China, announces the availability of our new WWW site.

The URL is http://foc.harvard.edu:2048/

Please browse the web pages containing introductory information,
manuscripts, images, links to searchable data, and information about
editorial centers.

We welcome your comments and questions.

Anthony R. Brach
