1996 
The University of Arkansas Arthropod Museum is happy to announce the
availability of our new site on the World Wide Web.  The site contains
photos and information on our collection, especially, but also on current
projects associated with the museum, publications, and our staff and
associated students.  We intend to update the information regularly and to
eventually make available more detailed information on our holdings than is
currently listed.  The site contains electronic mail access to the Curator
and
Collection Manager, for those interested in data or loan requests.  The
site also contains a link to the Department of Entomology at the University
of Arkansas; currently that site is under construction and should be up by
later this spring.

To access the University of Arkansas website:

http://comp.uark.edu/~gwallis/museum.html

The site is also accessible via Yahoo by searching EDUCATION > UNIVERSITIES >
UNITED STATES > UNIVERSITY OF ARKANSAS > LIBRARIES AND MUSEUMS > ARTHROPOD
MUSEUM.
