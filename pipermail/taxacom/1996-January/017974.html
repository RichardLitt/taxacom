1996 
I am in the process of preparing a database wherein each Article and Note in
the International Code of Botanical Nomenclature is reviewed and explained.
The Code is NOT reproduced. To understand the discussion one must HAVE A
COPY OF THE CODE. The purpose of the guide will be to provide information,
explanations, examples (if appropriate), and definitions of terms relative to
each article. The database will be fully searchable.

With luck this will be online before I need it for my advanced systematics
class, or by March.
