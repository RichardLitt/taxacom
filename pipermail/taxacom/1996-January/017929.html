Some time ago there was a query about this locality. I have now erased the
name of the sender and apologise for posting this to the complete list.
The query has now been seen by a colleague, Henk Beentje. He has asked me
to say that Bogos is a Hildebrandt locality. H. visited 'Bogos, prope Keren'
 between July and October 1872. Bogos has not been located but Keren is in
Eritrea at 1547 N, 3828 E.
