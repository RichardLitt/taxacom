JOURNAL OF  THE UKRAINIAN ENTOMOLOGICAL SOCIETY  is  a quarterly
journal published in  Ukrainian, Russian,  English,  German  and
French; articles  in  Ukrainian  and Russian  are  supplied with
abstracts in one of European languages and  vice  versa. JUES is
pulished at European standard polygraphic level,  incl. numerous
colour illustrations.   The  Editor ivites to  contribute: there
are no restrictions as to the subject, geographic area  etc. All
disciplines  of theoretical  and  applied  entomology   are fall
within  the  scope  of  JUES  on  the  World  basis: fauna   and
systematics,  descriptive and  comparative  morphology, ecology,
behaviour,  physiology,    entomological  aspects    of   nature
protection,   biodiversity,  applied  (agricultural,  technical,
medical  etc.) entomology,   history of  entomology, personalia,
reviews.  JUES is   intended  to  be manbaged   author friendly,
offering free reprints and no page or  illustration  charge from
subscribers.
   For editorial policy and instructions to contributors contact
Yuri P. Nekrutenko on <ypnekrut at mbat.freenet.kiev.ua> or  Valery
A.Korneyev on   <korval at entom.freenet.kiev.ua>    or both  under
headquarters address above. For  membership/subscription outside
Ukraine contact  Willy   De   Prins   on  <wdprins at innet.be>  or
Diksmuidelaan 176, B-2600 Antwerpen, Belgium.  - Join us!
