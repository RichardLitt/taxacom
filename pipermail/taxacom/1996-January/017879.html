     Paul Lago's recent success in getting help with several obscure
localities via a request on TAXACOM prompts my posting of this more
ambitious list.  These are the residue of over 15,000 records of staphylinid
beetles from over 100 insect collections (abbreviations at right, mostly
from Arnett et al.'s "Insect and Spider Collections of the World").  I have
already checked the usual sources (USGS GNIS CD-ROM, "The American Atlas",
"Street Atlas USA", Delorme state atlases and various road maps and older
atlases, etc.) but have either not found these sites or they are ambiguous
(e.g., 2 or more towns of the same name in one state).  Some are no doubt
misspellings or excessive abbreviations found on specimen labels, or
mis-transcribed from the labels, and others may be obsolete names.
     The desired end product is coordinates (lat./long.) to the nearest
minute, which will be used to plot distribution maps for publication.
However, any information anyone can provide that would let me pinpoint the
localities would be greatly appreciated, including county for those that
lack it.  Many of the collectors are still active and may be able to help if
they happen to see this list.  (I am posting this to ENT-LIST as well as
TAXACOM - apologies to those who subscribe to both!)
