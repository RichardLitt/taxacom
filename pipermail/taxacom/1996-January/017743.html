1996 
The University of Michigan Herbarium (MICH) is pleased to announce its
new web site.  The new site includes the usual information on staff and
collections, plus catalogs of ASPT Systematic Botany Monographs and Herbarium
publications.  The Fungal Collection portion includes the ability to
search our fungal type specimen database of 6,482 collections, loan
policies, data model, etc.

 The address is: http://www.herb.lsa.umich.edu/umherb.htm
