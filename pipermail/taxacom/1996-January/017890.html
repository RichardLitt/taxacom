     Since posting the long list of US localities last Friday, I discovered
that my old "Century Atlas of the World" (1911 edition) plots Township and
Range numbers for most states that use them.  Thus I have been able to
pinpoint all but one (in MI) of those cryptic T/R/S numbers, and anyone who
has been trying to figure them out can skip them now!
     And, thanks to the many colleagues who have already sent useful data,
or are still working on the list!
