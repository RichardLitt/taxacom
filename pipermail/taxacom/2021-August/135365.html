The Bishop Museum in Honolulu, Hawaii is now taking applications for the position of Entomology Collection Manager. The collection is the largest in Hawai’i and the Pacific with ca. 15 million specimens and particularly large and significant holdings from the Hawaiian Islands, New Guinea, the Philippines, Ryukyu Islands, and South East Asia.
