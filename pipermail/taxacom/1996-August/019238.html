             DIRECTOR, ROYAL BOTANIC GARDENS SYDNEY


     A new director is being sought for the Royal Botanic Gardens in
     Sydney, Australia. This is Australia's oldest scientific institution.
     It incorporates 3 gardens sites (Sydney, Mt Annan and Mt Tomah) and
     the scientific arm, the National Herbarium of NSW.

     Applications close 20 September 1996. Information is available from
     Anne Hardman (phone 61-2-9228 3529; fax 61-2-9228 3102), or email
     Barbara Briggs (bgb at rbgsyd.gov.au) for a copy of the advertisement.
