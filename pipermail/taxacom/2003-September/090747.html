TAXACOM Subscribers:

The email and web addresses for this list have changed, as of this
message.  To submit messages to the TAXACOM list for others to read, send
them to TAXACOM at LISTSERV.NHM.KU.EDU.

The new web address for viewing archives is:

<http://listserv.nhm.ku.edu/archives/taxacom.html>

To send commands to the LISTSERV (unsubscribe, etc) send them to
LISTSERV at LISTSERV.NHM.KU.EDU

No action needs to be taken by you except to send all future submissions to
the new email address TAXACOM at LISTSERV.NHM.KU.EDU

If you need further clarification, please reply to me directly.
