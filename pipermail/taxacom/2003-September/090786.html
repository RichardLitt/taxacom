Dear scarab enthusiasts,

Version 3 of the "Checklist of the Scarabaeoidea of the Nearctic
Realm" has just been released.  Copies of the list can be downloaded
in PDF or MS Word format from the following website:
http://www-museum.unl.edu/research/entomology/nearctic.htm

This version contains many corrections and updates from the version
released last year. The checklist is continuously being updated so I
would still like to hear from anyone who has found errors or
omissions.

Thanks again to everyone who sent in corrections and comments on the
earlier versions!!

Sincerely,
Andrew Smith
