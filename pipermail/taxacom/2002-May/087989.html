  The Torrey Botanical Society has some extra copies of numbers of the the
Journal of the Torrey Botanical Society. If you wish to replenish your
missing Torrey Journals, here is an opportunity to do so. The extra copies
are available free of charge, first come, first serve. The volumes (all
numbers) that are available are 114 (1987) through 123 (1996).
  Please direct requests to Linda D. Marschner, Recording Secretary, TBS,
Brooklyn Botanic Garden, 1000 Washington Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11225-1099;
<lindamarschner at bbg.org>.
