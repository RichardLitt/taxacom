collector in China, New Guinea, S America, Syria, and possibly other places
as well. I do not know much more than is stated in the Flora Malesiana and
by Desmond. He also seems to have been awarded a medal by the Royal
Geographical Society. Can anybody refer to a source of biographical data on
him? Biography, necrology, etc.
