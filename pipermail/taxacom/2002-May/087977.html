Dear friends,

We are very pleased to present you our web page on insects of Nicaragua. It's completely in spanish language but we feel it could be usefull for people who like neotropical insects. It's inside the Insectarium Virtuel web site from Spain. 

The adress is :
www.insectariumvirtual.com/termitero/nicaragua/MEL HOME PAGE.htm

If it doesn't work directly, you can enter the insectarium virtual web site at : 
www.insectariumvirtual.com
than click on the main image to get the main menu, click newly on the termite nest (termitero) than on Nicaragua. You get a small map of Nicaragua which is the welcome image, click on and get the main menu of our web page.

This page is equivalent to 1,500 printed pages.

"Fauna de Nicaragua" section includes a full checkilist of the 10,000 reported insect and terrestrial arthropod species from Nicaragua. 

"Documentos de interes" section presents many small documents, usefull for education. Also a study on butterflies of Nicaragua and the complete text of "Diversidad Zoologica de Nicaragua".

Your criticals are welcome, they will help us to make the web site better.

P.S. Si esta direccion es de Nicaragua, por favor avisarme, de esta manera la proxima información la recibirá en español.

Sincerely,

Jean-Michel.
