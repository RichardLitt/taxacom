Dear Taxacomers:


I am pleased to announce that the Tables of Contents of all
issues of POLIBOTÁNICA are now online at

http://www.encb.ipn.mx


POLIBOTÁNICA is a journal that publishes the Department of
Botany of the National School of Biological Sciences of the
National Polytechnic Institute from Mexico has as purpose to
give to know to the national and international scientific
community the results of botanical investigations for to
conserve and to take advantage of the flora of our country
and of other places of the world. The journal is a forum
where the investigators of diverse countries can present
their results on relevant aspects of the botany; only
considers for publication articles that represent original
botanical research, is distributed internationally and has a
International Editorial Commitee. Subscription are avaible
on an annual or biannual basis. Our journal is indexed in:
CLASE.

As Editor-in-Chief of POLIBOTÁNICA I would like to issue a
call for papers. Please see our web pages for instructions
to authors.

Thanks for your attention!
