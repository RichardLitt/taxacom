Hi all,

We are recruiting for the “Pauline Ladiges Plant Systematics Research Fellowship”.

This is a joint research position between The Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria and the School of BioSciences, The University of Melbourne, supported by an endowment fund through both institutions. The Fellow will conduct research in the field plant systematics with a focus on the terrestrial flora of Australasia. They will collaborate on existing or developing projects between RBGV and UoM, and also to develop their own research interests, applying for further grant funding, as appropriate.

The position is initially for 3 years, with the possibility of extension to five years subject to performance.

Details of the position are available here: https://jobs.careers.vic.gov.au/jobtools/jncustomsearch.viewFullSingle?in_organid=17835&in_jnCounter=223654889
