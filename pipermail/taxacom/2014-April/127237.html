It is my understanding that isopropyl alcohol trashes DNA or otherwise makes it very difficult to extract.  Think on that for pickling museum specimens.  I know of no circumstance where you can get (or ship) isopropyl but not get (or ship) 80-95% ethanol.

Just a thought.   Dan Janzen
