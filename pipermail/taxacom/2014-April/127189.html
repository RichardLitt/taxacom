Assume in this case that the photo was not taken at the location or time of
collection, but I can see that if someone takes a field image then that
information may well be embedded.

I can well understand the immense amount of secrecy that often surrounds
the discovery of ?iconic? groups such as hominids, although in this case
the secrecy even continues after publication in order to prevent others
from making direct examination and coming to different conclusions (such as
contesting hominid status for example).

John Grehan
