Dear Taxacomers,
Let me introduce two new volumes of "Modern Phytomorphology" which were
recently published both on-line, and as hard copies.
All articles are freely available.

You can browse separated articles
<http://phytomorphology.org/journal/archive/volume-5/>or download aggregated
version
<http://phytomorphology.org/wordpress/go.php?http://phytomorphology.org/PDF/MP5/Vol5full.pdf>of
volume 5. In the same way, you can find separated articles
<http://phytomorphology.org/journal/archive/volume-6/>or download full
version
<http://phytomorphology.org/wordpress/go.php?http://phytomorphology.org/PDF/MP6/Vol6full.pdf>of
volume 6.
Also you are welcome to browse previous volumes in our
archive<http://phytomorphology.org/journal/archive/>
.

The publishing of these two volumes is of a special significance because of
occupation of Ukraine by Russian soldiers. Most likely, the Crimea and
eastern regions are referred there as parts of Ukraine for the last time.

Sincerely, A. Novikoff
