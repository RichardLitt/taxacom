Note that it helps to be careful with terminology, as in 
"valid species":

* under the ICNafp, all species names are valid, by definition
   (if it has not been validly published, it is not a name).
* under the ICZN, a species name is valid if it is the correct 
   name to use, for a species with that circumscription and 
   position.
* whether or not something is a "valid species", is a matter 
   of taxonomy, not of nomenclature.
