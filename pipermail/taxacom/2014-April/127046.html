Antionio,

I fully agree, geology and evolution are not in-congruent. What are
sometimes in-congruent are particular hypotheses of geological history and
particular biogeographic patterns, but this in-congruence does not indicate
an absence of relationship between the two. Also, particular biogeographic
approaches may be problematic, but others perhaps less so. It all depends
on what you mean by contradictions between other groups and geological
evidence.

John Grehan
