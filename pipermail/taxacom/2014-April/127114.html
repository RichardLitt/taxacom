Antionio,

One could indeed start of with a particular geological reconstruction and
see how phylogeny fits with that or not. You would need to be specific as
to what you see as problematic about Rosen for me to comment on that. When
it comes to asserting geological connections between the Antilles and
continental ?mainland? this does not necessarily indicate anything in
particular about the origin of Antillean biota. One might also take into
consideration geological predictions for the origin of some Greater
Antillean formations out in the Pacific.



One could also examine the spatial relationships between taxa and
geomorphology and see if there are any correlations. Correlations, though
not proof of historical relationship, may led to a predication of such an
association.  Take for example the occurrence of Bontia in relation to the
Myoporeae, the Troidini, Bataceae, Crossosomatales for a Pacific context,
and in particular the nice tectonic boundary of Masteria spiders sandwiched
between the Motagua fault and the Romeral fault zone (see Heads? book for
all these examples).



John Grehan
