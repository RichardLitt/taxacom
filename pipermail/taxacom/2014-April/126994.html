See article 9 on typification in the ICBN.  In this case I believe that a
lectotype should be designated from one of the isotypes. As far as I
understand, the name should still be validly published. This would not be
different from a situation where the holotype was lost or destroyed.

http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art9#9.14

   - *9.2.* A lectotype is a specimen or illustration designated from the
   original material as the nomenclatural type, in conformity with
Art. 9.11<http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art9#9.11>
    and 9.12 <http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art9#9.12>, if
   no holotype was indicated at the time of publication, or if the holotype is
   missing, or if a type is found to belong to more than one taxon (see also
   Art. 9.14 <http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art9#9.14>).
   For sanctioned names, a lectotype may be selected from among elements
   associated with either or both the protologue and the sanctioning treatment
   (Art. 9.10 <http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art9#9.10>).
