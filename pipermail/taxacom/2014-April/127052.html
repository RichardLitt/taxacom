 principle of co-ordination applies,

 are deemed to have
 been automatically introduced at the same time should they
 be used at a
 different rank (such as a species).? So, should I
 elevate? XX yy n subsp zz
 Blank 2014 to species, the correct name is? XX yy Blank

 hasn't already been
 > used in genus XX.
