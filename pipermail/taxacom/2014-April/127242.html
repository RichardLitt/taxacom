I think that the actual culprit for trashing DNA is water.  The problem
with isopropanol is that it is not as effective for replacing the water as
ethanol is, allowing the DNA to degrade.  Similarly a low concentration of
ethanol won't preserve the DNA.  Depending on the size and composition of
the taxon, the water from the tissues may significantly dilute the
preservative.
