Hi Juerg,
       Well, certainly not a nude rock.  However, as I explained in an earlier posting, the end-Cretaceous extinction wiped out a lot of taxa (most vertebrates and probably all mammals).  This left it with a lot of empty niches which new mammals and birds, etc. would fill during the early-to-mid Tertiary.  
        But I suppose John would deny that the end-Cretaceous extinction was that bad in Madagascar.  I am giving up arguing with him about Madagascar, because he just repeats the same old fallback positions, and now claiming that there is no "analytical evidence" for dispersal to Madagascar.  It might not be strictly analytical evidence according his definition of analytical, but there is plenty of evidence.  He simply refuses to accept any of it.   I should have given up trying to reason with him a long time ago.
