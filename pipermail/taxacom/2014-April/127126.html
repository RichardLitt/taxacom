Dr. Wagner,

Please read 16.4.2 again. It is not about extant species, it is about extant 
SPECIMENS, ie ones preserved in a collection somewhere, not live ones 
running, flying or swimming around.

There is nothing in the ICZN Code that states that the holotype must be a 
dead specimen.

Of course it is preferable that a dead specimen or a sample of its tissue 
should be designated as holotype, but failure to provide a specimen or 
sample per se does not invalidate the name.
