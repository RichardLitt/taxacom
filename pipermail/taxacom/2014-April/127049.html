Dear Javier,

My question would be: Are talking about plants or animals?

In ICNafp:

"11.2. A name has no priority outside the rank in which it is 
published." (see http://www.iapt-taxon.org/nomen/main.php?page=art11)

I know that it sounds unfair, however, you can give a brand-new name to 
subspecies, if you raising it to species level; and you will get the 
authorship too, e.g. *Xx zz Javier*.

It is up to you (or to the author): She/he can propose a new combination 
at species level (this would be wise in my opinion), or give a new name 
with new authorship (this I called tricky way).

On the other hand, I don't know the situation in Zoological Code. 
Therefore I was asking you whether you talking about plants or animals.

Best wishes,

Ayhan
