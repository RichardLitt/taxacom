Hi Ken,

All recent work supports the idea that Leiopelma and Ascaphus form a clade
and that this is the sister of all other extant frogs (e.g. Pyron & Wiens,
Mol Phylogen Evol 61: 543. 2011; Zhang et al., Mol. Biol. Evol. 30: 1899.
2013). This idea is not out of date!

Michael
