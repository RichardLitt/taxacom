Evolution of live beings is most definitely NOT only dichotomous!  What
process could possibly create that pattern other than lots of extinction?
At the earliest stages it is definitely reticulate, then may appear
dichotomous if intermating completely ends, and even then only after a
long period of time ensues and then other gene exchange mechanisms apply
at some level.
