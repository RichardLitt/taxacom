Hi all,
      I've never stored insects in alcohol, but I've read that both ethanol and isopropanol can make them brittle (some say ethanol is worse, others say isopropanol is worse).  But there does seem to be agreement that concentrations above 70% of either of them can definitely make them brittle.  Anyway, I am wondering if the addition of glycerol helps mitigate that problem.  A few of my spider specimens were saved from dehydration when the alcohol evaporated over the years, but the glycerol kept them moist.                             -----------------Ken                   P.S.  I couldn't find anything about DNA being trashed by isopropanol.  It is often used to extract DNA, so does it only trash the DNA over a long time period?  Most of my spiders were collected in the 1970s.
