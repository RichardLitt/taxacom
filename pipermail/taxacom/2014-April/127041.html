Ken,


A majority opinion (growing consensus) is not a predictor that a scientific
position is necessarily correct.


There is indeed currently a lack of evidence that trans-Atlantic
relationships among differentiated taxa arose from overwater dispersal.


The ratio of dispersal to vicariance has never been shown to be high. It is
just invoked.


How would one 'explore' the conjecture that aplocheiloid fish rafted from
Africa to South America?


What 'growing pattern of occasional raftings across an early (narrower)
Atlantic ocean'?


I am not aware of "growing amounts of evidence that caviomorph rodents and
New World monkeys resulted from such raftings." All the literature I have
seen to date just says so out of a belief or based on fictional divergence
dates. I will look that the link and comment later.


Ken - you are not, and have never been, under any obligation to convince
anyone of anything. And you are also not under any obligation to contribute
to any discussion. If it interests you to do so then fine, if not then
don't. Better than complaining about the pain.


John Grehan
