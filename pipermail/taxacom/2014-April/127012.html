On 4/5/14 10:32 PM, Alain Dubois wrote:
> Dear Colleagues,
>
> On 9-10 July 2014, a workshop devoted to "Burning questions and problems of
> zoological nomenclature" will be held in Linz (Austria).
>
> The objectives of this meeting are threefold:
>
> (1) to allow the encounter of zoologists interested in questions of
> nomenclature, questioning the pertinence of some recent evolutions of the
> *Code*, and worrying about other possible evolutions;
>
> (2) to discuss freely and openly some of the burning questions and problems
> of zoological nomenclature;
>
> (3) to prepare a special issue of the journal *Bionomina* (Magnolia Press)
> reporting these discussions and proposing some solutions to some of the
> problems identified.
>
> Scientific Committee: Erna Aescht, Linz (Secretary); Aaron Bauer,
> Villanova; Edward C. Dickinson, Eastbourne; Pierre-Andr? Crochet,
> Montpellier; Alain Dubois, Paris (Chairman); Ivan L?bl, Gen?ve; Antoine
> Louchart, Lyon; Andr? Nem?sio, Uberl?ndia; Annemarie Ohler, Paris.
>
> This meeting is open to all interested zootaxonomists willing to contribute
> to this constructive discussion.
>
> If you would like to receive information about the meeting and its detailed
> program, just contact Erna Aescht <e.aescht at landesmuseum.at> or myself <
> adbionomina at gmail.com>.
>
>
