Making the statement into ...

Under the ICZN, the  principle of co-ordination applies,  and new names at
certain levels (such as subspecies)  are deemed to have been automatically
introduced at the same time should they  be used at a  different rank (such
as a species).  So, should I  elevate  XX yy n subsp zz  Blank 2014 to
species, the correct name is  XX zz Blank  2014.
