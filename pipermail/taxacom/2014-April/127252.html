Taxacomers,

I don't suppose there's anything any of us can do about this, but I 
wonder if others have thoughts about the tendency, at least in Canada, 
to list species in the most irrational possible order, given the way 
adjectives precede the the noun in English. I've seen situations like 
having the Adolphan Chorus Frog and Zealandan Chorus Frog at opposite 
ends of a list, despite their being only doubtfully distinct species.

fred schueler.
