Hi Everyone,

You're invited! to the GEOLocate Collaborative Georeferencing Demo 
Webinar 
<https://www.idigbio.org/content/collaborative-georeferencing-demo-webinar>

*Who:* Nelson E. Rios, Manager of Collections and Bioinformatics, 
GEOLocate Software Developer, Tulane University Biodiversity Research 
Institute <http://www.tubri.org/>
*Where:* http://idigbio.adobeconnect.com/geotrain (headphones recommended)
*When: *Thursday, May 1st, 2014 from 1 - 2 PM EDT.
*What to bring:* Your questions, your insights
*Keywords:* GEOLocate, georeferencing, collaboration, data integration
*On Twitter:* @iDigBio #geolocate

For Georeferencing large amounts of locality data, use the power of 
collaboration. Come see and hear Nelson Rios, from GEOLocate and the 
Tulane University Biodiversity Research Institute, walk us through a 
collaborative process. The GEOLocate Collaborative Georeferencing 
<http://www.museum.tulane.edu/geolocate/community/default.html> suite of 
tools provides an elegant way to manage groups of people georeferencing 
your project's locality data.

Please save the date and invite your colleagues and friends! We eagerly 
look forward to your questions and community input.

Best,
Deb, on behalf of Nelson Rios, and the entire Georeferencing Working 
Group at iDigBio
