> I did not see any evidence from Ken refuting a leiopelmatid clade

> the biogeography of this group in relation to all other frogs of some

* I haven't chased this further, but... "While some taxonomists have 
suggested combining the North American frogs of the genus Ascaphus in 
the Ascaphidae family with the New Zealand frogs of the genus Leiopelma 
in the Leiopelmatidae family, the current consensus is that these two 
groups constitute two separate families.[4][5][6] ...The family 
Ascaphidae, of the same suborder, shares these primitive 
characteristics, and hence the two have often been described as related, 
or even part of the same family." 
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiopelmatidae
