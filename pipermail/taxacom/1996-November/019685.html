                        Quick Reference Card

               Use the following addresses & commands:

TAXACOM at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU  [to send a message to the entire list]

LISTSERV at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU [to change your subscription status]
  to subscribe send this message:    subscribe taxacom your name
  to unsubscribe send this message:  unsubscribe taxacom

TAXACOM-REQUEST at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU [to contact the management or to
  solve any problem requiring human intervention.]

SEND THESE COMMANDS IN A MESSAGE TO: LISTSERV at CMSA.BERKELEY.EDU

  TO GET TAXACOM LISTSERV INFORMATION:

    HELP                    [to receive a list of LISTSERV commands]
    INFO REFCARD            [to receive a LISTSERV user manual]


  TO START OR STOP A TAXACOM SUBSCRIPTION:

    UNSUBSCRIBE TAXACOM            [to terminate your own subscription]
    SUBSCRIBE TAXACOM Your Name    [to start a subscription]

  TO CHANGE TAXACOM MAIL OPTIONS:

    SET TAXACOM NOMAIL      [to receive no mail but still be counted
                               as a Taxacom subscriber for posting
                               privileges (see below)]
    SET TAXACOM MAIL        [to receive mail, *DEFAULT SETTING* for
                               new subscribers]
    SET TAXACOM DIGEST      [to receive all messages each day within
                               a single mailing]
    SET TAXACOM INDEX       [to receive a short index each day of that
                             day's mailings (author, subject, date, size)
                             within a single message, individual
                             messages can then be requested via e-mail]

    QUERY TAXACOM           [to check the state of your subscription]
    INDEX TAXACOM           [to obtain a list of monthly archive files]

    REVIEW TAXACOM BY COUNTRY   [to list all Taxacom members by country]
    REVIEW TAXACOM BY NAME      [to list all Taxacom members by name]


  TO REVIEW TAXACOM'S PROBLEM SUBSCRIPTION DELETION POLICY:

    GET DELETION POLICY TAXACOM



TAXACOM POSTING POLICY

In order to minimize the nuisance of junk mail to the list, posting to
Taxacom is restricted to subscribers only.

This helps to reduce spamming but adds a little overhead to managing the
list.  It is not uncommon for existing subscribers to find that a posting
to the Taxacom has been rejected by the listserver as being from a
non-subscriber.  This situation usually occurs for one of two reasons: 1)
your institution has changed your actual e-mail address, usually by a
small change to the domain name, or 2) a person subscribed under another
account and is receiving forwarded mail to a new address - one not on the
Taxacom list.

In the latter case, the solution is to unsubscribe yourself from the
original account and resubscribe from your new account.  If your domain
name has changed without your action, send an e-mail to:

        taxacom-request at cmsa.berkeley.edu

and we will delete you from the list at your old address.  Once you
resubscribe, you will be able to post messages again.

A related situation may arise, when a person reads Taxacom on the WWW
interface, and does not wish to subscribe but wants to post messages.  In
these cases we recommend that you subscribe to the list with the 'SET
NOMAIL' option in your subscription e-mail to the list.  Then you will be
recognized as a subscriber for posting purposes but receive no mail.


TOO MUCH MAIL?  OTHER WAYS TO ENJOY TAXACOM:

Postings to Taxacom are archived and can be browsed through Gopher at
the Biodiversity and Biological Collections Gopher (muse.bio.cornell.edu).

The TAXACOM List Archive is also indexed by date, author and thread, and
is conveniently browsable as a World Wide Web Hypermail archive.  If you
would prefer to read TAXACOM at your leisure without receiving mailings,
the WWW archive is highly recommended.

   The URL is:  http://kaw.keil.ukans.edu/archive/taxacom.html

If you need help, please, do not hesitate to contact the management
at: Taxacom-request at cmsa.berkeley.edu.  We will be glad to help you.
