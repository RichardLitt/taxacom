Research Entomologist Position - Leafhoppers/Auchenorrhyncha

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, USDA is pleased to announce the
upcoming recruitment of a full-time permanent Research Entomologist position
on the systematics of Leafhoppers and associated Auchenorrhynchus Homoptera.
The incumbent will be stationed at the National Museum of Natural History,
Smithsonian Institution, Washington, D.C.
