1996 
The Coleopterists Society is pleased to announce its WWW pages, reachable at:
   http://www.auburn.edu/beetles/

The site includes information about the society and its various programs,
some beetle resources (more to be added), and links to numerous
beetle-related or other sites of likely interest.  We will continue adding
features to the pages.

Many thanks to member-webmaster George Folkerts for putting it all together!
