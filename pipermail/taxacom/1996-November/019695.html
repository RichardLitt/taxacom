1996 
The Crater lake Natural History Association provides grants (up to
$3000) to aid scholarly studies at Crater Lake National Park and Oregon
Caves National Monument in SW Oregon, USA. Special consideration will
be given to those projects that enhance understanding or aid
interpretation. Projects may be generated by the National Park Service or
by others. For applications and particulars contact: Research Committee,
Crater Lake Natural History Association, Box 157, Crater Lake, OR 97604
