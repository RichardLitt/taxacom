Many thanks to all who commented on the first draft of the key to the tree
genera of Borneo at "django.harvard.edu/users/jjarvie/Borneo.htm".  It has
been substantially revised and now contains 513 genera and 135 characters.
This draft is available in both Indonesian and English as are DELTA
generated HTML descriptions in both languages.  As always, any feedback is
appreciated.
