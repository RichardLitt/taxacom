The Transvaal Museum, Pretoria, South Africa and the Department
of Zoology and Entomology, University of Pretoria have begun an
intiative to account for the faunal biodiversity of southern Africa.
We need to have our first list of taxa reviewed by as many interested
animal systematists with expertise in southern African taxa as
possible.

Please visit our list at the following URL:
