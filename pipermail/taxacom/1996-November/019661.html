Robert Poole is right. For example, the American Society of Plant
Taxonomists,
publishers of SYSTEMATIC BOTANY and SYSTEMATIC BOTANY MONOGRAPHS, has a
simple approach to requests for reproduction of copyrighted material.  If
the request is for including the material in another publication (e.g., a
book or another scientific paper), the policy is to grant permission with
the stipulation that the ASPT be properly credited.  If the request is to
reproduce material for a course packet, e.g., providing copies to all
students in a class, then the request is processed by the Copyright
Clearance Center, which collects a minimal fee for each page copied.

I suspect that other professional societies have similar policies.
