Please note that posts copied or sent to the ICZN List from addresses not registered as members of the list are automatically discarded by the list server software. Some recent messages have suffered that fate so I suggest that posters should check that their addresses are registered if they want their messages to reach the list. Sometimes the software will also discard messages sent to multiple recipients.
