Lycus melanurus Schönherr, 1817. The original description is this one:

https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/133008#page/84/mode/1up
Type locality Sierra Leone.

Referred to description here:
https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/216050#page/554/mode/1up

Description was attributed to Dalman, name was not attributed to Dalman, so under Art. 50.1.1. the authorship for the name must be attributed to Schönherr alone.
ö is correct, oe is for those who cannot create the ö.

Figures were published here (Fig. 7):
https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/216050#page/793/mode/1up

Seems to suggest to me that in the case of the Pretoria beetle we are not dealing with melanurus, but with a different species. The beetle from Sierra Leone seems more slender. The structure at the proximal section of the elytra seems different. Just some thoughts, I am no Coleoptera expert.

Other specimens from Mozambique look like the Pretoria specimen:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Lycus_melanurus


If this helps.
Cheers
Francisco

I am wanting to add the author of the name to the taxonomic backbone of SCAN. A google search sees it listed as Hook-winged Net-winged Beetle<https://www.inaturalist.org/taxa/477080-Lycus-melanurus> but the only references to it take me back to the iNaturalist record. Please, if someone out there knows these creatures, would you tell me whether a) Dalman, 1817 is the author and b) whether that is the currently accepted name or is it (and possibly others) now under some other genus?
