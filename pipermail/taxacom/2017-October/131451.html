Hycleus oculatus (Thunberg 1791) was formerly placed in Mylabris - the appearance from photos is very Mylabris like but I assume Hycleus is distinct from Mylabris. There are 17 observations of the species on iNaturalist, mostly from southern Africa - Namibia (5), Botswana (3), South Africa (8) - plus 1 from Kenya.
See also:
http://www.biodiversity.org.na/taxondisplay.php?nr=10798
