2007 
EDIT workpackage 6 will be holding a workshop on the data structure  
necessary for a data warehouse to support revisionary taxonomy.

The meeting will be in London of 26th & 27th April 2007.

This workshop has also been described as "commonalities" in earlier  
EDIT documents and was originally a one day workshop at the NHML and  
a separate Botanical one day workshop at Kew.  Funding to attend the  
meeting has been distributed to EDIT partner organisations, so please  
check with your local EDIT coordinator.

For the programme and details on how to register, please see:

http://www.editwebrevisions.info/
