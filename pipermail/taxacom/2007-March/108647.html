BEETLE MORPHOLOGY AND CYBERTAXONOMY
A postdoctoral fellowship is available immediately, starting date
negotiable, to participate in studies of the morphological evolution and
systematic relationships of Coleoptera (including Tenebrionidae) and to
assist Quentin D. Wheeler, director of the International Institute of
Species Exploration.  The successful candidate will collaborate with and
support the personal research of Dr. Wheeler as well as assist in
activities of the IISE and contribute to the curation of the Hasbrouk
insect collection.  Work will include dissection and comparative
morphology, digital imaging, phylogenetic analyses, and descriptive
taxonomy.  Qualifications include an earned doctorate in insect taxonomy
and knowledge of beetle morphology; experience in information science or
Website design desirable. Salary commensurate with experience, minimum
$36,996.  Send curriculum vitae, brief statement of taxonomic background
and interests, and the names and e-mail addresses of two references to
Ms. Shannon Keen, Assistant to the Vice President and Dean, College of
Liberal Arts and Sciences, P.O. Box 876505, Arizona State University,
Tempe, AZ 85287-6505.  Electronic applications welcome to:
shannon.keen at asu.edu.  Review of applications will begin February 15,
2007, and will continue until position is filled.
