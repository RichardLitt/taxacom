Oh, well.  That's what you get when an aquatic entomologist looks at 
terrestrial green things.  Paul Davidson is correct, it is a liverwort, 
not a moss.  And, we have seen what we interpret to be fungal hyphae 
sprouting from the "stems" of the liverwort.  So, the situation is all the 
more intriguing -- not only are we out of the Arctic, we are on a 
different group of plants.  We'll try to get some photos and post them.
