with respect to the comment in particular that "Wouldn't a paper (if credit
for recognition of this particular situation, and/or one more publication,
are the objective) describing the case, WITHOUT actually naming either
specimen as a new species (arguably), be sufficient ?"

I would agree that is a potential alternative. As noted in my other email
comment, I am making a subjective judgement call. If the specimens were
from an ecologically and geographically contiguous area I would take a
different approach and did this for a specimen from India where I and a
colleague published a descriptive paper because we could not definitively
conclude whether we had a new species or not (largely because type
specimens were not available to us).

John Grehan
