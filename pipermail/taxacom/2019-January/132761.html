Not quite sure what is being referred to as 'flawed and unscientific' but
when it comes to credit it is really not in the name of the species, but
the author names that go with the species name. So I might, for example,
name a species Pfitzneriella antonkozlovi after the collector, but it is
Pfitzeriella antonkozlovi Grehan & Mielke, 2018. It is the author name/s
that claims credit for naming the species.

John Grehan
