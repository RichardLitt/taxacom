Dear Taxacom list,

We invite you to review a compiled list of user stories on the future use of
DiSSCo <https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/PB2MY9G>, the Distributed System of
Scientific Collections <http://dissco.eu>, that has recently been accepted
on the ESFRI (European Strategy Forum on Research Infrastructures) roadmap.

DiSSCo is a new pan-European Research Infrastructure initiative of 21
European countries that positions European natural science collections at
the centre of data-driven scientific excellence and innovation. DiSSCo will
mobilise, unify and deliver bio- and geo-diversity information at the
scale, form and precision required by scientific communities; transforming
a currently fragmented landscape into a coherent and responsive Research
Infrastructure.

Your input is very important to the data infrastructural design of DiSSCo
so that DiSSCo can accommodate all envisioned future uses. The survey will
only take 10 minutes of your time https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/PB2MY9G.
