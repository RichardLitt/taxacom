Wouldn't a paper (if credit for recognition of this particular situation,
and/or one more publication, are the objective) describing the case,
WITHOUT actually naming either specimen as a new species (arguably), be
sufficient ?

In that approach, there's no road clutter lying about on the taxonomic /
nomenclatural highway, while the discussion about moth labial palp
contributions to moth classification (species recognition) can be presented
formally and discussed more fully. Meanwhile, perhaps another specimen,
with genitalia (and palps), will materialize.....

Peter
