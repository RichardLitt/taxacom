The International Lepidoptera Survey (TILS) has just finished published
Volume Three of
The Taxonomic Report (TTR) in CD

The CDs may be ordered on line at
http://tils-ttr.org/subscribe.html
The cost is $25 post paid Air to anywhere.

The first page of all issues can be previewed at
http://tils-ttr.org/report03.html

We also operate a Leps list serve on Yahoo at
http://groups.yahoo.com/group/TILS-leps-talk/
One can subscribe to this group at this URL.  It is a fairly active group
averaging 10 - 20 emails a day.  One can also customize their subscription
to just one daily digest email per 24 hr. period.
