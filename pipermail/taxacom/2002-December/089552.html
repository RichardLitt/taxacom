Elizabeth E. Bascom Fellowships in Botany for Latin American Women

The Missouri Botanical Garden announces the fifth annual competition for the
Elizabeth E. Bascom Fellowships in botany for Latin American women. The
competition is open to young Latin American women who work in the field of
botany or are botanists at universities or other governmental or
non-governmental scientific institutions in Mexico, Central America, and
South America. Applicants must have an undergraduate degree from a
university. The fields of investigation are limited to systematic botany,
ecology or conservation. During their stay at the Garden, the fellowship
recipients will have access to the herbarium, library, and botanical
database of the Missouri Botanical Garden. Further information in English is
available at < http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/Research/bascomengl.shtml
<http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/Research/bascomengl.shtml> > or in Spanish at
<http://www.mobot.org/MOBOT/Research/bascom.shtml>.
