1994 
I'm in Knoxville TN, at the joint meetings hosted by AIBS.

The plenary speaker, Dr. Thomas Lovejoy, exhorted biologists
to take action *now* in the interest of public service, to
help solve the problems facing our biosphere.  As a superb
example of what he had in mind, he offered the CSIRO computer
identification key.  That is, Mike Dallwitz' INTKEY and DELTA
packages, and the various databases encoded in DELTA format
for use with INTKEY.

Just thought TAXACOM readers would like to know.
