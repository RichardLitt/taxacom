1994 
REQUEST FOR INFORMATION ON NATIONAL RED DATA BOOKS / RED LISTS

WCMC, the World Conservation Monitoring Centre, is currently
compiling a table of national "Red Data Books" and "Red Lists"
of animal and plant species threatened at the country level. In
order to ensure that no such documents are over-looked, WCMC is
therefore seeking details of ANY lists or books of nationally
threatened species compiled in the past few years or still in
preparation. Please e-mail chris.magin at wcmc.org.uk if you are
involved in the production of such books / lists or
know of any that have been recently published. Thank you in
advance for your cooperation.
