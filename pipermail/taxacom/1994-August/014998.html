1994 
From:  The family of Professor Donald E. Johnston
To:  Friends and Colleagues

Don Johnston, Professor of Entomology and Director of the Acarology
Laboratory at Ohio State University, died unexpectedly, but peacefully, at
home on Tuesday, August 9, 1994.  We are holding a Memorial Service for him
on Friday, August 12, at 7 PM at the Weir-Arend Funeral Home, 4221 N. High
Street, Columbus, Ohio.  Family will receive friends following the service.
In lieu of flowers, contributions may be sent to the Department of
Entomology, The Ohio State University, 1735 Neil Ave., Columbus, Ohio,
43210, to establish a scholarship fund for support of students in the Summer
Acarology Program.
