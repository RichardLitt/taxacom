              STRATIGRAPHIC RANGE OF CRETACEOUS MEGA

                  AND MICROFOSSILS OF BRAZIL

                    Edited by Gerhard Beurlen,
                   Diogenes de Almeida Campos &
                      Marta Claudia Viviers

   Rio de Janeiro, Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro, Centro de Ciencias
       Matematicas e da Natureza, Instituto de Geociencias,
   407pp. ISBN 85-7108-109-3.

   The stratigraphic distribution of Cretaceous Brazilian fossils
   are presented, including details concerning their basins. The book
   includes four chapters with a summary for all micro and megafossils.
