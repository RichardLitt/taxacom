I am Anton Savchenko, systematic mycologist at the University of Tartu

note-taking software: https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fsav-che%2FTL-2&data=05%7C01%7Ctaxacom%40lists.ku.edu%7Ce1b3028f170a4add4fe908dbf655a8e0%7C3c176536afe643f5b96636feabbe3c1a%7C0%7C0%7C638374620002634322%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Miftg1Kx2d6qS3d0gR3VcCKEDWC7jTimgPO3PahY3Tk%3D&reserved=0.
