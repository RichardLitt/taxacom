> In my efforts to document the publication of new genus names for the period
> 1753-present, I have come upon the following 2 as indexed by ION/Zoological
> Record for which both the original publication (title, abstract, full text)
> and taxonomic placement of the 2 names concerned are proving elusive.
>
> The paper is given in ION as:
> [The Ojlemyrflint (Ordovician) as a microfossil site.] Geschiebekunde
> Aktuell, 29(4), Nov 2013: 106-135 (authorship probably by Schallreuter, R.;
> Hinz-Schallreuter, I)
>
> (title is translated from the original by them)
>
> and the new genus names established therein are given as Borina
> Schallreuter
> & Hinz-Schallreuter, 2013 ("Ostracoda") and Sphaeropetasus Schallreuter &
> Hinz-Schallreuter, 2013 ("Animalia"), neither name then yielding any hits
> in either Google or Google Scholar for me; similarly the original
> publication is also elusive via my usual desktop search routes.
>
> Any additional information on either these 2 new genera, or the
> title/abstract/text of the original work in which they appear, would be
> much appreciated!
