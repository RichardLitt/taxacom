2004 
        On May 5 of this year Dr. Juergen Schmidl of Bioform assured me by
e-mail that the following books would be published this summer. We have a
very small book budget at my Museum, and I set aside nearly all of my share
to purchase these important books. Now I am afraid that I may lose the money
because I must purchase and receive books before the end of our fiscal year
which is the close of next month. Dr. Schmidl has not responded to a recent
e-mail, and his web site does not list the books any longer. Does anyone
know what is going on and whether these important books will be published
this summer? Many thanks, Gary.
