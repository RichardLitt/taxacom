The "Denkschriften der Österreichischen Akademie der Wissenschaften" are 
digitized and available at
< http://www.landesmuseum.at/datenbanken/digilit/ > a wonderful database 
for old and new literature on Austrian (historical sense) natural 
history.

Unfortunately the articles of Vol 71(2) are not included at < 
http://www.landesmuseum.at/datenbanken/digilit/?litnr=30505 >. There is 
only a link to Hans Rebel. Maybe you contact the reponsible scientists 
at the museum in Linz to get a copy.


Best wishes
Gerhard Haszprunar
