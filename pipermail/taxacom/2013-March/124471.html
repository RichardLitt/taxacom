Dear Mary,

Actually BHL has quite a bit on Stipa arida, including one of your papers http://bit.ly/11aweI9

The search results for Stipa arida are here: http://biodiversitylibrary.org/name/Stipa_arida

I suspect the problem is that the new interface makes it hard to see the search results. If you paste Stipa arida into the search box you get a page that rather prominently says "No Books/Journals found".  However , if you scan along you'll see it actually has a hit in "Scientific names". The old interface made the results in each category easier to see.

Regards

Rod

So, nice idea - keep working on it.
