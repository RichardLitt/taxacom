2013 
Are you interested in the intersection of biology, software, and mathematics? Come to Snowbird, Utah for the iEvoBio Meeting, June 25-26 - in conjunction with Evolution 2013<http://www.evolutionmeeting.org/>!

The iEvoBio Meeting<http://ievobio.org> brings together biologists working in evolution, systematics and biodiversity, with software developers, and mathematicians.

We are now accepting submissions for participation in the conference. There are three ways to participate:


  *
Lightning Talk: present for 5 min on a method, idea, or software product about bioinformatics.
  *
Software Bazaar: Demo your open-source software product.
  *
Birds of a Feather: Suggest or participate in an informal group of folks with a common interest. Suggestions will be considered both before and during the meeting.

We have two fabulous keynote speakers<http://ievobio.org/2013/02/27/keynote-speakers-announced/> lined up:


  *
Holly Bik (@Dr_Bik<https://twitter.com/Dr_Bik>; http://www.hollybik.com/)
  *
Heather Piwowar (@researchremix<https://twitter.com/researchremix>; http://researchremix.org/)

Awards for early career participants:


  *
Travel award: http://ievobio.org/travel-awards/
  *
Outstanding lightning talk award
