Dear Colleagues,

A new project is announced at the web-site of Zoological Institute 
(Saint-Petersburg, Russia)
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/index.htm

- Catalogue of Alticini genera of the World (Coleoptera: Chrysomelidae: 
Galerucinae)
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/alticini.htm

A total of all 534 genera are presented by a separate page and provided 
with key information:
- Genus name
- Type species
- Designation
- Species number
- Distribution
- Host plants
- Reference
- Image of representative (images of  40 genera are currently available 
and continually increasing).

There is also a compilation of all the genera in a single table
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/alticinw.htm

The author of the project - Konstantin Nadein (Kiev, Ukraine)
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/nadein.htm

You are welcome to visit our web-site and share this information with 
your colleagues.

With best regards,
Andrei Lobanov
web-editor
