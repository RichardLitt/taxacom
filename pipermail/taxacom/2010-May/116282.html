A position for a thesis M.S. student in plant systematics (with the option
for continuing to the PhD)  has recently opened in the Department of Biology
at Wake Forest University. The student will learn molecular systematic
techniques and software and be trained in basic herbarium curation. The
student will be supported via a TA in one of the Core Courses in the
Department. The student will start in August 2010. Please address questions
to Kathy Kron.
