(Apologies for redundant postings)

The 15th International Congress of Myriapodology, 18-22 July 2011, Brisbane, Australia.

Website: http://www.15icm.org

This will be the first time a myriapodology congress has been held in Australia, and only the second time in the Southern Hemisphere. The conference emphasis will be on the myriapod fauna of Asia and the Pacific Basin. A flyer in PDF format is available for email forwarding and for printing out and posting; please contact me directly for a copy.
