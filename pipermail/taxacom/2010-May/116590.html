VACANCY ANNOUNCEMENT



Post-doctoral position description

E. Desmond Lee Laboratory of Molecular Systematics

University of Missouri-St. Louis


One or two post-doctoral positions are available in the Department of  
Biology, University of Missouri St. Louis, with appointments for one  
year with the possibility of renewal.  A Ph.D. in some aspect of  
plant biology is required.  We are particularly interested in  
applicants with experience in plant evolutionary biology, molecular  
systematics, comparative development or comparative genomics.  
Applications should include a brief cover letter outlining  
qualifications and interests, curriculum vitae, and names, phone  
numbers, and email addresses of three references.  Send applications  
via email to Dr. Elizabeth A. Kellogg, tkellogg at umsl.edu.    
Applications must be received by June 14, 2010.

The University of Missouri-St. Louis is an affirmative action, equal  
opportunity employer, committed to excellence through diversity.
