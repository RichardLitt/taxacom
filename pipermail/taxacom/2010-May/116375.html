And two more online resources about how to collect plant specimens: 

on our website: http://www.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/plant_info/identifying_plants/processing_plant_specimens/Preserving_plant_specimens

and on the BRI website: http://www.derm.qld.gov.au/wildlife-ecosystems/plants/queensland_herbarium/botanical_information.html#_How_to_collect_specimens

The latter has copious photographs, whereas ours relies on line drawings.
