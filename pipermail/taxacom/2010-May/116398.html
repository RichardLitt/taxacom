Eduardo,

I am the director of SilverBiology and the creator of the web portal 
SilverCollection.  We are a small team of biodiveristy informatics 
programmers that focus on providing quality web applications for 
herbaria and other biological collections.  We are based out of the 
United States and are active members of TDWG, SPNHC and regional United 
States consortia.  We are currently working with herbaria in conjunction 
with National Science Foundation grants.  We also do consulting with 
GBIF on taxonomic projects.  Our focus with SilverCollection is to make 
this a global product that works with most collection management 
software as well as our other related herbaria software we produce.

If there is any other questions please feel free to contact me directly.
