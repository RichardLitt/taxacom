2009 
The deadline for abstract submission for the scientific conference 'Plant conservation for the next decade: a celebration of Kew's 250th anniversary' has been extended to April 30th. Confirmed keynote speakers now include Dr Peter Raven, Dr Judy West, Dr Joseph Cobbinah, Dr Saw Leng Guan, Professor Richard Hobbs, Professor Hongwen Huang, Professor Michael E Kane, Ms Sara Oldfield, and Kew's current and previous Directors Professor Stephen Hopper, Professor Sir Ghillean Prance and Professor Sir Peter Crane.
Abstracts can be submitted online through the conference website:
