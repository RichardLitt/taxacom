Dear everyone, 

Following the publication of the proposed amendment to the Code to allow electronic publication of nomenclatural acts, a selection of comments made so far has been assembled and published in the March 2009 issue of the Bulletin of Zoological Nomenclature. This document is available online (http://www.iczn.org/BZN%2066(1)%20Contributions%20to%20the%20discussion%20on%20electronic%20publication.pdf), and you are encouraged to circulate this to stimulate the discussion further and ensure that input can be received from as many interested parties as possible.

Comments can be sent directly to Dr Ellinor Michel, Executive Secretary of the ICZN (iczn at nhm.ac.uk ) or to Dr Gary Rosenberg, chair of the ICZN Editorial Committee (rosenberg at ansp.org).

Best wishes, and have a fantastic weekend !

Natalie
