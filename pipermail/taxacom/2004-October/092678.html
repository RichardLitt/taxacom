2004 
We (of the University of Tennessee Botany department) are sorry to announce
that Dr. Patricia Walne passed away Thursday, October 21st at her home in
Bloomington, Indiana.  She was a member of the Botany Department for more
than 30 years.  She received numerous awards during her career including the
Benwood Distinguished Professorship and Macebearer. She was a Fulbright
Research Scholar in Denmark and a recipient of the prestigious Darbaker
Prize for meritorious research from the Botanical Society of America.   She
will be missed by her colleagues in the University community and in Botany.
