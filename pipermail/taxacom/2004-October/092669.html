The Taxonomic Search Engine I recently developed
(http://darwin.zoology.gla.ac.uk/~rpage/portal/ ) now queries the
GenBank taxonomy, in addition to ITIS, Index Fungorum, uBio, and
IPNI. Although GenBank is not an authoritative source of taxonomic
names, they do have many names not in these other databases.
