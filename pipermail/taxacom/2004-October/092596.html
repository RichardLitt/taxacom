2004 
CALL FOR ABSTRACTS
The sixth annual Young Systematists' Forum will be held at The
Natural History Museum, London on Thursday 9th December 2004.
This meeting provides an exciting setting for postgraduate students and
young postdoctoral researchers to present their data, often for the
first time, to a scientific audience interested in taxonomy, systematics
and phylogenetic inference.
There is space for up to 15 speakers at this year's YSF with additional
places available for those wishing to present a poster. A prize will be
awarded for the most promising oral and poster presentations.
Registration is FREE. For further details, please see the Systematics
Association website (www.systass.org) or contact Mark Carine
(m.carine at nhm.ac.uk).
