2004 
The Institute of Natural Resource Analysis and Management (INRAM) is pleased to announce the release of our new Gateway to New Mexico Biodiversity.  This web site and searchable database gives access to over 280,000 specimen records from NM.  These records come from 21 collections at Eastern New Mexico University, New Mexico State University, University of New Mexico, and Western New Mexico University.  The taxonomic coverage includes plants, invertebrates, and vertebrates.  More data and more capabilities will be added in the coming months.

Please visit us at http://biodiversity.inram.org/
