1994 
The purpose of this mail is to inform you that an "INBio WWW Server" is now
available for all those willing to obtain information on Costa Rican
biological diversity.

On this first implementaion phase, the server contains general information
about INBio and a publication on Lamelicornios, including an on-line
identification key. Currently, other INBio publications are being processed
for retrieval in the coming months.

The server's Internet address is: "www.inbio.ac.cr" (163.178.202.2)
