Reminder -



Systematics - the science that underpins biology

26-28 August 2015 - University of Oxford, UK.



Symposium 1 (26 August) The value of long term monitoring plots for plant systematics and ecology in the tropics

Symposium 2 (28 August) Comparative approaches to the origin of biodiversity

Symposium 3 (28 August) Accelerating the pace of taxonomy

Symposium 4 (27 August) Rooted in deep time: Palaeontological contributions to systematics



Contributed papers deadline: midnight 1 July 2015



Accommodation: at Christ Church College must be booked by 3 July 2015



Conference banquet: takes place in The Great Hall (replicated as Hogwart's Hall in the Harry Potter movies)



After dinner speaker: Entomologist and regular BBC broadcaster George McGavin.



Hashtag: #SABiennial



Details:  http://systass.org/biennial2015/
