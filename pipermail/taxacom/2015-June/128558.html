Dear colleagues,

Please join us for the 2016 GGBN International Conference on Biodiversity Biobanking, to be held at the andel's Hotel from 21-24 June in Berlin, Germany.  The Global Genome Biodiversity Network (GGBN) is a global network of well-managed collections of genomic tissue samples from across the Tree of Life, benefiting society through biodiversity research, development and conservation. The theme for this program will be Meeting the Challenge: How to Preserve a Cross-Section of the Tree of Life.

For more information, please visit our new meeting webpage at: http://meetings.ggbn.org/site/ggbn2016
Conference flyer: http://meetings.ggbn.org/documents/2016ConferenceFlyer.pdf

The Conference Committee welcomes your ideas for sessions and speakers. Suggestions for sessions and speakers can be submitted to ggbn at si.edu<mailto:ggbn at si.edu> through July 14th 2015.

We look forward to seeing you next year in Berlin!

With Kind Regards,
