Dear friends

Just over a year ago, the providers and users of biosystematic
information in Namibia got together to determine their needs for the
future. The results have been out since April, but have not been
available online before.

For those who may be interested, have a look at the pages starting with:
http://www.biodiversity.org.na/documents/EUWSProceedings/0-euwshome.htm

People with fast connections may want to download the full document in
pdf format (at a rather hefty 6 Mb) from:
http://www.biodiversity.org.na/documents/EUWSProceedings/Nam%20EUWS%20Proceedings.pdf

Regards
