>Back issues or sets of Quaestiones Entomologicae are available free of 
>charge or for cost of shipping!
>
>See http://www.biology.ualberta.ca/old_site/uasm//QEcontents.htm
>
>Although Quaestiones Entomologicae, fondly known as Quaest Ent, ceased 
>publication in 1990,  the journal has had a remarkably durable impact, 
>particularly in insect systematics. We continue to receive regular 
>requests for back issues, but we now need to reduce the size of our 
>inventory. Upon request, we will distributed single issues free of 
>charge, or sets (except for two issues that we are out of: 7[1] and 
>11[3]) for the cost of shipping (approximately: $40.00 within Canada; 
>$60.00 Cdn to the United States; and $170.00 Cdn for overseas). The 
>full table of contents is listed in the website above, with issues 
>indicated for each volume.
>
>Issues or sets may be obtained by contacting Danny Shpeley at: 
>dshpeley at gpu.srv.ualberta.ca Please give your surface mail address and 
>indicate what issue you would like.
>
>Publication of Quaestiones Entomologicae was started in 1965 as part of

>a memorial project for Professor E.H. Strickland, the founder of the 
>Department of Entomology at the University of Alberta in 1922. It was 
>intended to provide prompt relatively low-cost publication for 
>comprehensive accounts of entomological research of greater than 
>average length.
>
>Feel free to distribute this notice on other listserves.
