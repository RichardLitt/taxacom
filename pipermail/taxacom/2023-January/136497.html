Dear all,

Thank you John for the request.

As Adam said, the name would not be available from either source because 
it was consistently treated as an abberation, thus deemed to be 
infrasubspecific (Art. 45.6.2) and excluded from the provisions of the 
Code (Art. 1.3.4).

P. 272 in Krulikowsky's article in Deutsche Entomologische Zeitschrift, 
Iris 21 (4) was published 14 April 1909, the article in Societas 
Entomologica 23 on 01 April 1908. So even if the taxon had been treated 
as a subspecific name, the usage in 1909 would just have been a 
subsequent usage of the name that would have been introduced in 1908 
(and there in 1908 it would have been made available, as Stephen 
confirmed), not a nomen nudum.

Best wishes
Francisco
