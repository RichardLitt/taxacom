In the interest of harmony between the various Codes,
it seems much preferable to maintain "rank" in its
present (universally understood) meaning.

Whether or not the terms "family-group", "genus-group",
and "species-group" are a good idea is another matter, but
any change will cause confusion. New terms had better
be a big improvement to justify a change.

Paul
