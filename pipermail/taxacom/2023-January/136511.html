Hi Doug,
Actually your immediate approach would sound more logical, and I've 
never understood why they selected these terms "rank" and "group" for 
these meanings. It would make much more sense to talk of three ranks 
family, genus and species, and then subdivide in subranks or so. The 
term "group" has so many different meanings in taxonomy that it does not 
seem to fit really well for this purpose it has in the Code.

The Linz Zoocode Committee uses the term "series" instead of "group", so 
also they are not happy with the ICZN terminology in this point.

Best wishes
Francisco
