On Thu, Jan 26, 2023 at 11:09 PM Stephen Thorpe <stephen_thorpe at yahoo.co.nz> wrote:

 It depends on the details of the specific case. Often the term "nomen nudum" is used to mean an unavailable name for any reason, so the species may still be recognisable. Also, if the specimens forming the basis of a nomen nudum can be traced and examined, then the species may also be recognisable.Cheers, Stephen
