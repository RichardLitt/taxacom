As of this Wednesday, more than 13,000 botanical types are online
accessible at www.zuerich-herbarien.unizh.ch
The types can also be accessed through the GBIF portal.

Focus points of this collection is New Caledonia and South Africa, and
probably many types may be found which went lost during the World War
destructions in Berlin.
