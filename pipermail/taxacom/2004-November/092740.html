Seven rare books from the Missouri Botanical Garden Library have been
digitized and are now available online at
http://ridgwaydb.mobot.org/mobot/rarebooks/.  This popular site now presents
77 volumes by 27 authors and includes more than 3,300 beautiful
illustrations.  Of particular note is Peter Sch?ffer's "Herbarius," an
interesting work on medicinal plants that was published circa 1480.  The
recent additions include:
