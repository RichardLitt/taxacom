Dear Taxacomers,

I have a draft version of an interactive key to the tree and shrub genera of
Borneo available at "django.harvard.edu/users/jjarvie/borneo.htm".  This has
424 genera and uses 119 characters.  Also there are descriptions generated
by the DELTA tonatha directive which makes htm pages for all taxa, and a lot
of illustrations.  If any are interested, I would greatly appreciate
feedback on the content and the presentation before this becomes the first
edition!

Many thanks,
