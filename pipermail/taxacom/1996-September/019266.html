Are there any short courses in plant taxonomy that would be suitable as
training for people in ancillary disciplines, such as ethnobotany. Several
ethnobotanists have asked me about this - they want something more intensive
than sitting in on an undergraduate introductory course (in any case, not so
easy to find these days). I can think of several good 1-year Master's
degrees, e.g. at Reading and Edinburgh in the UK, and there are plenty of
1-week courses for the general public at field study centres. Is there
anything in-between?

Details of suitable courses would be very welcome - whether in North America
or the UK.
