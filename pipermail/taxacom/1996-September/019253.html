To anyone reading this who curates a collection of bird specimens:

I have a student studying the structure of cormorant wings and flight
feathers, and I'd like to borrow outspread wing specimens of any cormorant
other than Double-crested (Phalacrocorax auritus), Neotropic (P.
brasilianus), Brandt's (P. penicillatus), Pelagic (P. pelagicus), and
Red-faced (P. urile).  Please post me directly if you have such specimens.
Thanks.
