1996 
The Directory of UK Systematic Biology Expertise is now available on-line
from the UK Systematics Forum's Home Page at:

http://www.nhm.ac.uk/uksf/

This directory contains contact details for UK experts, information on their
taxonomic, geographic and stratigraphic areas of expertise and their current
research interests. An on-line questionnaire is available for other UK
systematists wishing to register.
