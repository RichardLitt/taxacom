Dear Colleagues,

Please note that there is a lecturer's post available at the School of
Biological Sciences, the National University of Singapore.  This is a
three-year contract with renewal subject to job performance.  It is
preferable for candidates to have the following:
1.      Recent PhD
2.      1-2 years postdoctoral experience
3.      Specialized in Malesian cryptogamic botany, cryptogamic botany of
other regions or, higher plants but able to teach cryptogamic botany.
4.      Experience in field botany.

The appointment involves teaching including cryptogamic botany at the
undergraduate and Honours levels as well as conducting a one-week  field
course in Peninsular Malaysia.   Lecturers are also expected to perform
administrative duties and carry out research.  Research facilities and
salary scales are competitive with most of the best universities.  Salaries
are for 12 months of the year with at least a 1 month bonus plus a variable
component depending on the performance of the Singapore economy.  For the
last three years, this component  has been equivalent to about 2 months'
salary.  Rental housing is availble at about 10% of the monthly salary.
Salary will be computed based on the credentials and experience of the
candidate.

Interested candidates can send their CV to:
