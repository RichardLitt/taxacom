Is there anyone out there who has good pictures (standard 2x2
transparencies) of Trichostema or Caryopteris (Labiatae)?  I am
particularly interested in close-up shots of the flowers.

assortment of angiosperms in flower, including many Labiatae) or reimburse
you for duplicating and mailing your slides to me.

but I would be very interested in pictures of any other species of
Caryopteris and any species of Trichostema.
