Besides the systematic view, one might want to know whether a given species
or clade occurs in a given area. Simply listing species is not a trivial
task, hence a list of species implies more than a sequence of
taxonomically/alphabetically listed taxa. To know that a species or clade is
present or absent (if sampling has been good enough) in a given area implies
a number of explanatory hypothesis to understand why the species occur (or
not) in that area.
