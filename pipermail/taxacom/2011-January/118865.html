Thorne and Reveal (Botanical Review 73: 67-182. 2007) suggested 253,300 species in 13,372 genera, or 18.9 species per genus, of extant flowering plants. A forthcoming paper on extant gymnosperms (Christenhusz et al., Phytotaxa) will account for some 84 genera in about 1025-1060 species or 12.2 to 12.6 species per genus.
