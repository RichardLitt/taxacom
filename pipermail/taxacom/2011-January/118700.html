John,

The problem is well known, but quality publishing needs to be paid somehow,
either through library subscriptions (again inaccessible to the developing
world) or through open access.

It remains only to be seen, how long subscription-based journals shall be
able to publish without author-side (authors' institutions in most cases)
charges.

Otherwise, I only commented the price level.

Lyubomir
