ENTOMOLOGY COLLECTIONS MANAGER

The University of Nebraska State Museum seeks a permanent, full-time
collections manager for the Division of Entomology.  Duties include
managing a major research collection, preparation of specimens, processing
loans, electronic databasing, insect identification, organization of
collections, maintenance of lab facilities & equipment, public outreach,
some field work, some research in area of specialty.  The Division's
collections (ca. 2 million specimens) rank among the 20 largest collections
in the United States.  Minimum qualifications are M.S. in entomology with
systematics experience (or equivalent) & experience with collections
management.  Salary $26,480.  Excellent benefits.  To apply send letter of
interest, C.V., & names, addresses, & phone numbers of 3 references by 1
November 1997 to Dr. Brett C. Ratcliffe, Curator, W436 Nebraska Hall,
University of Nebraska, Lincoln, NE, 68588-0514 (phone 402-472-2614; FAX
402-472-8949; internet bcr at unlinfo.unl.edu).  The University of
Nebraska-Lincoln does not discriminate in its academic, admissions, or
employment programs & abides by all federal regulations pertaining to same.
