1997 
                        Systematic Entomologist

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture is sending out a notice (not a formal announcement) of our
intent to advertise a research position for an insect systematist with a
strong background in molecular techniques.  The position will be located at
Beltsville, MD.  The position will be for a full-time, permanent Research
Entomologist, GS11/12.  The official opening date for applications has not
been set but is expected shortly.


                        Systematic Entomologist

The Systematic Entomology Laboratory, United States Department of
Agriculture is sending out a notice (not a formal announcement) of our
intent to advertise a research position in weevils (Coleoptera) at our
location at the National Museum of Natural History, Washington, DC.  The
position will be for a full-time, permanent Research Entomologist, GS11/12.
The official opening date for applications has not been set but is expected
shortly.

Interested applicants for either of these positions should contact Dr. Manya
B. Stoetzel, Research Leader, Systematic Entomology Laboratory, Bldg. 046,
BARC-West, Beltsville, MD 20705-2350 (301-504-5183, FAX 301-504-6482,
mstoetze at sel.barc.usda.gov).
