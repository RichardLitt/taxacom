     In cleaning up my office prior to a move, I've come across the
     following volume. I have no need of it; therefore, its free to the
     first person that e-mails me a mailing address.

     Morphology and Systematic of Scale Insects - No. 12

     It includes includes two works-

     Revision of the Family Kermesidae (Homoptera) in the Nearctic Region
     Based on Adult and Third Instar Females by Bullington and Kosztarab

     and,

     A Morphological and Systematic Study of the First and Second Instars
     of the Family Kermesidae in the Nearctic Region (Homoptera:Coccoidea)
     by Baer and Kosztarab.

     Its Tech. Bulletin 85-11 of the Va. Ag. Exper. Station.

     Thanks, Bill Adams
