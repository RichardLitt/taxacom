Dear friends

This is just to inform you that the discussion group on the
problems of the genus Hieracium as suggested at the Krkonose
Hieracium Workshop was already established. You can
subscribe simply by sending the following message:

To: listserver at savba.sk
Subject:

subscribe hieracium First name_Family name

The discussion group will not be moderated (at least for the
initial stage). There is also a suggestion to keep it in English
in order to enable to participate as many "hieraciologists"
as possible.

With the best wishes

Karol
