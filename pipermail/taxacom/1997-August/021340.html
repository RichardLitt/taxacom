1997 
DIRECTORIES OF PACIFIC ENTOMOLOGISTS

Two different directories of entomologists related to the Pacific region
have recently become available:

DIRECTORY OF ENTOMOLOGISTS WITH PACIFIC INTERESTS ON WWW

This directory is now available on Internet for browsing or downloading.
It has been compiled by the Entomology Subcommittee of the Pacific
Science Association in cooperation with the Bishop Museum, and includes
names, addresses, and interests for all entomologists with Pacific
interests who have responded to various questionaires over the years.
The present list derives from that published by E.H. Bryan in 1966, was
updated by Geoff Scudder in the 1980s, and has been further updated by
Gordon Nishida, Neal Evenhuis and Scott Miller for the 1990s.  We know it
is incomplete and welcome additions and corrections.  The directory will
be continually updated as information is gathered.

     http://www.bishop.hawaii.org/bishop/psa/pacentodir.html

DIRECTORY OF ENTOMOLOGISTS LIVING IN THE SOUTHWEST PACIFIC

The latest directory published by the Australian Entomological Society
has been expanded to include over 900 entomologists living in Australia,
New Zealand, and the islands of the Southwest Pacific.  It is available
for Aus $ 10.00 from Australian Entomological Society, c/o CSIRO Division
of Entomology, Box 1700, Canberra ACT 2601, Australia.

     Jennings, J.T. (ed.). 1995. Directory of South-west Pacific
     entomologists. Australian Entomological Society, Canberra. 91 pp.
     ISBN 064623899X.
