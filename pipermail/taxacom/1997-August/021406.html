1997 
Michael J. Wynne, Curator of Algae, would be happy to hear from persons
interested in exchanging duplicates of pressed and labeled marine algae.
He has accumulated a significant number of duplicate specimens of red,
brown and green algae from his own collections, made around the world.
These collections are from the following locations:

        Australia                       Indian Ocean
        British Columbia, Canada        New Zealand
        Caribbean                       South Africa
        France                          Washington [State], USA

If you are interested in carrying out such exchanges of
marine algal specimens, please contact M. Wynne either at his e-mail
address:

        mwynne at umich.edu

or at:

    Department of Biology
    The University of Michigan
    Ann Arbor, MI 48109-1048
    USA

Thanks,

Robert Fogel
Curator of Fungi
Professor of Biology
