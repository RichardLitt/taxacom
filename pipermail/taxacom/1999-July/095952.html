1999 
1.  Specialist In Lower Plants

National Museums & Galleries Of Wales, Department Of Biodiversity &
Systematic Biology (Biosyb)

(FIVE YEAR CONTRACT)
Starting Salary of: c£18,500, superannuated REF: Bio/LP

This is an opportunity for an enthusiastic, systematic botanist to be
responsible for a significant national collection and develop an area
of excellence in a field within lower plants - Bryology, Mycology,
Algology. Ideally, you will have a higher degree and should be able to
contribute to biodiversity studies both in Wales and internationally.


For further details and an application form, please telephone our
Jobline answerphone service on 01222 573348 quoting the relevant
reference. Closing date for applications: 6 August 1999.

The NMGW is an equal opportunities employer.
Applications are welcome from all sections of the community.

2.  Administrator


National Museums & Galleries Of Wales, Department Of Biodiversity &
Systematic Biology (Biosyb)

ADMINISTRATOR
Starting Salary of: c£18,500, superannuated REF: Bio/AD

This is an opportunity for an experienced administrator to join the
management team of the department where you will co-ordinate internal
systems and support and monitor project activity. Duties will
includemanagement of budgets and external funding bids, report writing
and project management. You should possess a Business Management
qualification, be an experienced project manager and preferably have
knowledge of the biological sciences.

For further details and an application form, please telephone our
Jobline answerphone service on 01222 573348 quoting the relevant
reference. Closing date for applications: 6 August 1999.
