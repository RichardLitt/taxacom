Chers tous,

between December 1, 1999 and July 31, 2000 the Mycological Herbarium of the
National Museum of Natural History, Paris (PC), will be closed to the public
due to renovation works at the building. No loans will be handled during
this period.

A partir du 1 decembre 1999 jusqu'au 31 juillet 2000, l'herbier mycologique
du Museum National d'Histoire Naturelle, Paris (PC), sera ferme au public a
cause de travaux de renovation. Aucune demande en pret sera consideree
pendant cette periode.
