2011 
The data entry project at herbarium MU is progressing apace.  Data entry for Lycopodiaceae, Selaginellaceae, and Isoetaceae is now complete.  Entry for all Gymnosperms is done, and monocot families through Poaceae are finished.  For access to the data, please visit our web site at http://wan1.mbi.muohio.edu/herbariummu/.

Images of vascular plant type specimens will be added soon.

For further information or to request loans, please contact the curator, Michael Vincent, at the address below.
