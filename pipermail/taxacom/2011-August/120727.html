2011 
The latest volume of our open access publication, the Bulletin of the
Buffalo Society of Natural Sciences, includes three articles that may be
of taxonomic or biogeographic interest to some on this list. They are:

 

Beatriz Ortiz-Santana and Ernst E. Both.  A preliminary survey of the
genus Buchwaldoboletus

 

Frank M. Climo and Karen Mahlfeld. Kokopapa, a new genus of New Zealand
land snails (Pulmonata: Punctidae), with description of six new species
and discussion of South Island, New Zealand biogeography

 

John Grehan. Notes on the biogeography and phylogeny of eastern Asian
ghost moths (Lepidoptera: Hepialidae)           

Pdf copies can be downloaded from
http://www.sciencebuff.org/research/publications/bulletin/
