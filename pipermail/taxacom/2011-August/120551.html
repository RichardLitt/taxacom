2011 
The 2011 Biodiversity Information Standards (TDWG) Conference will be held
in New Orleans, October 17-21 (http://www.tdwg.org/conference2011/).  The
theme of this year's conference is  "Digitization Methods, Technologies, and
Standards for Biodiversity Collections".   Register now through August 15th
to take advantage of reduced early registration rates.  Regular registration
will be open August 16th through September 26th.    

 

The meeting will be held at the Astor Crowne Plaza Hotel in Downtown New
Orleans (http://www.astorneworleans.com/).  Reserve you accommodations here:
