Many thanks to Lisa and Gerwin for these leads - Wandtafeln is clearly the magic word that finds these wallcharts. It's also led me to a web page at Harvard:

http://www.huh.harvard.edu/libraries/Botanische_Wandtafeln.htm

which gives the full references for a couple of papers:
Bucchi, Massimiano. (1998) Images of Science in the Classroom: Wallcharts and Science Education 1850-1920. The British Journal for the History of Science, Vol. 31, No. 2, pp. 161-184.

Schmid, Rudolph. (1990) Wall Charts (Wandtafeln) - Remembrance of Things Past. Taxon, Vol. 39, pp. 471-472.

Mark
