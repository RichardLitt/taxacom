If any of you have dealings with a printer, virtually every printshop
will have a Panose color book. They might have an old one they'd give
away. New ones cost about $75 from printers ink suppliers.

Advantage:  if you have anything printed in spot color, there's the
color with the formula to make it; if you use a computer illustrator
program (Corel Draw, Adobe Illustrator, Macromedia Freehand, etc.), they
all come with standard Pantone colors as part of their pallette so all
you need to do on the computer is calibrate your monitor; colors are
standard in the printing business worldwide; pantone has a huge number
of colors.
