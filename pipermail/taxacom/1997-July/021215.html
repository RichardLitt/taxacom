At 11:36 AM 07-08-97 -0400, Susan Farmer wrote:

>My understanding is that it becomes Planta rubra var. hypothetica

Correct.  See Art. 11.6.

>My understnading that when an autonym is created in this fashion, that
>autonymic names of all intermediate ranks are also created.

No.  Art. 22.3 and 26.3 explicitly state that only the *corresponding*
autonym is created.  Publishing Planta  hypothetica var. alba only creates
var. hypothetica.  It does not create subsp. hypothetica, subvar.
hypothetica,  or forma hypothetica.
