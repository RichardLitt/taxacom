>Does anyone know how old the oldest existing herbarium specimen is?  And,
>of course, what additional information is there about it (e.g., where, of
>what, by whom).

Luca Ghini (1490? - 1556) professor of botany at University of Bologna is
identified as the originator of the practice in "The Herbarium Handbook"
ed. by L. Forman & D. Bridson (RBG Kew, 1989).
