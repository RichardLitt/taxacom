        For those fortunate enough to be able to find a copy, there is
a comparatively inexpensive color atlas: the Reinhold Color Atlas, published
in 1962. This book has 1266 color swatches, identified by number and by
name (although many names cover a range of swatches).
        The colors are not biased towards any specific use, but cover the
entire range of colors equally.
