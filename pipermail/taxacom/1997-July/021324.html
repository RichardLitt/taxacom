LucID Player 1.0 Released
**************************

LucID is a software based interactive identification system for using and
building identification keys.

LucID has been developed by the Cooperative Research Centre for Tropical
Pest Management (CTPM) and the Department of Entomology at the University
of Queensland.

We believe that LucID represents a major innovation in the delivery of
taxonomic information.

The LucID Player that has just been released on CD. It includes a
demonstration version of the LucID Builder (soon to be released), a
Delta-LucID convertor, a key to the orders of Insects and samples of a
number of other keys currently under construction.

Further information and a downloadable demo are available from our website at:

http://www.ctpm.uq.edu.au/Software/Lucid.html
