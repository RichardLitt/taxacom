Some preliminary proposals for changes to the DELTA format are available in
files newdelta.txt (plain text) or newdelta.ps (PostScript) on the following
Internet hosts.
    huh.harvard.edu       (directory: /pub/software/delta/standard)
    life.anu.edu.au       (directory: /pub/biodiversity/delta/standard)
    spider.ento.csiro.au  (directory: /delta/standard)
Access is by anonymous ftp from any of these hosts, or by Gopher from the first
two.

The proposals will be discussed at a meeting to be held in Perth, Western
Australia, on 3-5 October 1993.
