Rob



Australian Indigenous names often reflect different life stages of the same species or group of species, other times even the same species in different seasons. I am very much in favour of representing Indigenous names where possible, but alongside the scientific names. Those names help maintain indigenous languages as well as provide access to indigenous ecological or other knowledge. My exhibition work is aligned along exactly that, and involves consultation with indigenous researchers and community groups.



Tony
