Fellow Taxacomers:

The New York Botanical Garden has begun construction of its new Plant
Studies Center. This 70,000 square-foot building will house the
library and herbarium. Construction will be completed in the year
2000.

In the meantime, you can monitor the progress of the construction of
the Plant Studies Center at:
http://www.nybg.org/bsci/const/construc.html where images of the
construction will be updated approximately weekly.
