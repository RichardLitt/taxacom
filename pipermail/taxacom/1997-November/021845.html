The Flowering Plant Gateway  site referenced by Jim:

http://www.isc.tamu.edu/FLORA/cronang.htm

uses 'classification system' data tables to machine-generate suites
of 'browse' pages for the Thorne, Cronquist, and Takhtahan
classification systems with some comparative inter-linking and, on
selection of a family name, reference to an indexed file that carries
links to web-based info from various sources.  The Thorne system, as
presented by the gateway, is current as of 1992.  The update
mentioned by Jim will be incorporated into the gateway system ASAP.
