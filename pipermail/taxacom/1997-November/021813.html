1997 
MEETING ANNOUNCEMENT, CALL FOR REGISTRATION AND ABSTRACTS

for the 1998 WORLD CONGRESS OF MALACOLOGY

13th International Congress of Unitas Malacologica &
64th Annual Meeting of The American Malacological Union
together with other participating malacological organizations

at the Smithsonian Institution, Washington, DC, 25-30 July 1998

Deadlines:
Registration -- 1 March 1998
Abstracts -- 1 April 1998

Scientific sessions include three symposia ("Refining Molluscan Characters,"
"Interactions Between Mollusks and Humans," "Bridging Temporal Scales in
Malacology: Uniting the Living and Dead") as well as contributed papers and
posters.

Congress information and copies of the registration and abstract forms can
be found at the UNITAS MALACOLOGICA congress web site on Field Museum's server

  <http://www.fmnh.org/wcm>

These documents also will be posted (by snail mail) to the memberships of
the co-organizing societies.
