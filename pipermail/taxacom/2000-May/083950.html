Hi all,
        I've been looking also at Toby Schuh's textbook, and can echo
Chris's endorsement for morphological character analysis.  If you are
interested in analysis of molecular data, however, definitely consult
other sources.  The book contains a number of major misunderstandings
about molecular data interpretation, and is thus not suitable for
students as an overall systematics text.
