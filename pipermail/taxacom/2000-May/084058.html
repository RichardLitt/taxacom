More taxonomy related are the "habitus" and "uscita" bloopers:

"Habitus" : During an identification course for invertebrates, the students are
confronted with a specimen of Crustaceae. One student starts to scream: "I know
it! I know it! This must be habitus!"

"Uscita" : A couple of tourists is driving down south on an Italian highway.
Here and then there are signs on the highway, indicating "uscita". One of the
tourists starts to ponder: "Uscita really must be a very large town, there are
more signs than to Milano".
