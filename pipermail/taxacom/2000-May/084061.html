Tribolium Desv, Opusc. Sci. Phys. Nat. 64 (1831).

Synonyms are Lasiochloa Kunth 1832, Urochlaena Nees (1841), Brizopyrum Stapf
(1898) and Plagiochloa Adamson & Sprague (1941)

The most recent revision of the genus is by H.P. Linder and G. Davidse in
Bot Jahrb. Syst. 119:445-507 (1997).
