SOON TO BE BACK IN PRINT AND AVAILABLE AGAIN

The Families and Genera of Bats

by Gerrit S. Miller Jr.

First published in 1907 by the Smithsonian this classic has long been
out-of-print and very difficult to find. It is a classification of the
families and genera of bats, based primarily on skeletal and dental
identifiers and was based on Miller's extensive travels and study of the
Chiroptera.

ISBN 1-930665-02-4
