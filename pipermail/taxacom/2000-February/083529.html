- CALL FOR PAPERS -
SESSION 20: Special environment entomology
SYMPOSIUM: Cave-dwelling insects as model systems for evolutionary and
conservation biology (convenors: Hannelore Hoch, Berlin, and Frank G.
Howarth, Honolulu)

Dear colleagues,
Deadlines for submission of abstracts for the XXI International
Congress of Entomology are fast approaching. Contributions are still
accepted for the above symposium on cave-dwelling insects. We invite
you to present your work pertinent to the subject in our symposium,
and we look forward to meeting you in Brazil in August.

Please send a copy of your abstract to each of the conveners. We shall
inform you about the final program in due course.
