Subject: Positions Available:  Amer. Museum of Natural History

For the AMNH Digital Library Project:

Senior Programmer

Interesting new digital library project needs a programmer with
extensive experience in relational database design, SQL, Oracle
developer/Designer/Forms and expertise in web integration with
emphasis on experimental, multi-media interfaces.  Experience
with ARC/INFO (or other GIS work) a plus. Must have BA degree or
higher in computer science with specialization in databases or
equivalent experience.  Excellent supervisory/interpersonal/
communication skills as well as interest in natural history
important.  Positions offered based upon level of experience.
Term until 2005.  Please send resume with salary requirement to:
Asst. Director, Library Services, AMNH, CPW @ 79th St., NYC 10024
or email to techlib at amnh.org No phone calls please.  EOE.

Junior Programmer

Exciting opportunity for an Oracle programmer with some
experience in web integration with multi-media, digital library
application.  Must have a BA degree or higher with a
specialization in relational databases.  Some knowledge of Oracle
Developer/Designer/Forms; excellent interpersonal/communication
skills.  GIS experience (ARC/INFO or ARCVIEW) would be useful.
Positions offered based upon level of experience.  Term until
2005.  Please send resume with salary requirement to: Asst.
Director, Library Services, AMNH, CPW @ 79th St., NYC 10024 or
email to techlib at amnh.org.  No phone calls please.  EOE.
