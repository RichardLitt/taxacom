>Resolution is not really publication quality, but nothing
>is in digital photography short of the $26,000.00 models

Most publications print at 300dpi- so as long as the image is not enlarged,
a camera with a resolution of 1200 X 1500 pixels will do a 4x5" picture
that, as far as resolution goes, should be publication quality.

People interested in digital imaging with microscopes should see
"Economical Computer Microscopy for Junior High and High School Use" by
Matt W.MacGown
http://www.agctr.lsu.edu/wwwac/dept/entomology/mattspage.htm
