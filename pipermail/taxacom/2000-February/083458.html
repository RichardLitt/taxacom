There appears to be some confusion about who is the current Director of
the University of Michigan Herbarium based on several inquiries I have
received recently.

Prof. Gerald R. Smith has been the Herbarium Director since 1 July 1999,
He is also the Director, Museum of Zoology; Curator of Fishes, Museum of
Zoology; Curator of Lower Vertebrates, Museum of Paleontology; Professor
of Biology; and Professor of Geological Sciences.
