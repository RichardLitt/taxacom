Dear Taxacom members,

The International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN) is seeking nominations for five new Commissioners this year. More details are available here: https://www.biotaxa.org/bzn/article/download/50641/43994

The duties of a Commissioner include:

  1.  Voting on applications to the Commission (or Cases)
  2.  reviewing Rulings, Official Corrections and other articles issued by the Commission
  3.  contributing to nomenclatural discussions on mailing lists
  4.  attendance at General and Special Sessions of the ICZN
  5.  further duties to be specified if participating in committees, e.g. ZooBank, Code, Finance, Editorial

Interested parties should fill out the following form<https://www.iczn.org/assets/190dcaa88b/ICZN_Commissioner_Nomination.docx>, either including it as an attachment to the ICZN Secretariat<mailto:iczn at nus.edu.sg> or mailing it to the following address:
