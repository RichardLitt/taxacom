2020 
Naturalis Biodiversity Center (Leiden, The Netherlands) is looking for a
Project Officer who will take ownership and lead on the work of two
DiSSCo-linked projects (SYNTHESYS+ and DiSSCo Prepare). More information:
https://www.dissco.eu/hiring-project-officer/

DiSSCo is a consortium of 21 European countries and 120 natural science
collections institutions that strive to set up a pan-European research
infrastructure for science collections, both biological and geological, as
driver for science and society. The new infrastructure has been accepted on
the European Strategy Forum for Research Infrastructures (ESFRI) Roadmap in
2018 and the consortium will start working on a project for implementation
of the preparatory phase in March 2020. For the success of the DISSCo ESFRI
preparatory phase, the consortium needs to further organize itself to
enable valuable contributions to the European effort. The Preparatory Phase
of DiSSCo is currently implemented through a series of EU-funded projects
that together contribute to the implementation of the DiSSCo multi-annual
action plan.
