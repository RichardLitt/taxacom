The Natural History Museum of Denmark is an institute of its own under the Faculty of Sciences, University of Copenhagen. The museum employs the vast majority of the university's taxonomists s.l. (i.e., including systematists). The University has two further units employing taxonomists s.l.: The Department of Biology and The Faculty of Life Sciences. The latter two have, unlike the museum, lost a number of taxonomists s.l. during recent rounds of sacking, meaning that the University's taxonomic expertise is now even more concentrated in the museum.

on behalf of the Natural History Museum of Denmark
