1999 
A revised list of suprageneric names for the vascular plants is now
available at http://www.inform.umd.edu/PBIO/fam/inspvindex.html (arranged by
genera) and at http://www.inform.umd.edu/PBIO/fam/sgindex.html (arranged by
families). These listings take into account the realization that a set of
Berchtold and Presl names I reported at the rank of tribe are not validly
published as the authors used the rank of family below that of natural order
(=family) - see http://www.inform.umd.edu/PBIO/fam/Berch&Presl.html as well as
other recent finds at http://www.inform.umd.edu/PBIO/fam/NEWS.html -
A more up-to-date set of names is always available at
http://matrix.nal.usda.gov:8080/star/supragenericname.html ---
