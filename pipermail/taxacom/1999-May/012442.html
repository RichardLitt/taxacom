Vascular plant names listings can be downloaded from the USDA plants
site at:

http://plants.usda.gov/plantproj/plants/plntmenu.html
(see 'download plants')

and the US National Plant Germplasm System site at:

http://www.ars-grin.gov/npgs/
(see: http://www.ars-grin.gov/npgs/tax/index.html)

Herbarium specimen data downloads are available from the Northern
Prairie Herbarium at:

http://www.npwrc.usgs.gov/resource/othrdata/herbar/herbar.htm

and

http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/output/outputa.html

which includes data from herbaria involved with the herbarium
specimen browser at:

http://www.csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/ftc/ftphsb.htm
