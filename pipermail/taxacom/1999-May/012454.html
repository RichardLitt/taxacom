Hi All,

Museum Victoria's web query access into our Victorian Butterfly, Frog and
Snake collection management databases is now available.  While individual
specimen record data is available, the site has been designed to encourage
public (ie. non-scientific) use of this data.  In particular, it addresses
some of the educational requirements of our Victorian Secondary School
curriculum in the areas of biodiversity and geography.

The URL is:

http://www.mov.vic.gov.au/bioinformatics/

Feedback on this site would be most welcome.

Yours,

Ken Walker
