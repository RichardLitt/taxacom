If anyone on the list happens to be familiar with the Rhytididae (mollusca)
I would be very interested to know if there have been any distributional
and phylogenetic updates on the group since A. C. Van Bruggen's (1980) paper on
the Gondwanaland connections.

The group as circumscribed by Van Bruggen has an Indian Ocean (Gondwanic)
baseline, and classic range confoming to what might be expected with a
Gondwanic origin.
