Four *Assistant Curator* positions have just been posted on the Field
Museum website (https://www.fieldmuseum.org/about/careers). [At the moment,
they're on page 3 of the listing, presumably as the newest entries.]

They are in Neotropical Plants, African Anthropology and Archeology,
Herpetology, and Fossil Reptiles.
The museum will post the positions in *Nature* and *Science *as well.

Please use the contact instructions in the postings for any questions - I
have no further information on the positions or the search.
