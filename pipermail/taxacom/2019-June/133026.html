Dear Colleagues of the Taxacom community,



Please find attached a job advertisement for a postdoc position to build a
robust molecular phylogeny of Brassicaceae and to explore evolutionary
transitions towards woodiness. This is a collaborative effort between the
group of Klaus Mummenhoff (Osnabrück Univ, Germany) and Frederic Lens
(Naturalis Biodiversity Center, The Netherlands).

The postdoc will work in the DFG-funded research project: “Reaching for the
sky: exploring massive convergent evolution towards woodiness in
Brassicaceae” and involves:

·      Building molecular phylogenies based on hybridization sequencing

·      Trait-based diversification analyses





I highly appreciate if you can distribute this job posting within your
institute/among peers.





Thank you very much and best wishes,

Klaus Mummenhoff and Frederic Lens
