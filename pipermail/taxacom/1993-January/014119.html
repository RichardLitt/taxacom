--------------
English manual
--------------

The first part of the English manual is now available on the
SysTax FTP-server as man-e-wp.exe and man-e-ps.exe (self-extrac-
ting archive files for DOS-Pc) or as man-e-ps (postscript file).

It comprises a description of the basic functions of the SysTax
system. This will make it possible for all users to test and to
use the Taxonomy- and the Synonymy-screens of the program.

The screen texts and the error messages are now completely
translated to English.
