Dispersal is actually very important in biogeography. What is misunderstood here is that dispersal ability and dispersal are two different things. Biogeographic (spatial analysis) indicates hat means of dispersal are more about means of survival and that process is operative in one way (as mobilism) in the generation of an ancestral range, and in another way (as immobilism) in descendant differentiation.
