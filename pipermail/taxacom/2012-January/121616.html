Dear all, 


The Distributed European School of Taxonomy is offering a training course on "Basics of Taxonomy: describing, illustrating and writing biodiversity". The course is ideal for early career and future taxonomists who want to learn more about DELTA, digital drawing, and scientific illustration, writing and communication. 


Deadline for registration is approaching: 15 January 2012 

Course dates: 16-27 April 2012 

Location: Sven Lovén Centre for Marine Sciences,
Kristineberg, Sweden 

Target audience: MSc students, PhD students, early career researchers; from Europe as well as outside of Europe 

Detailed information and registration at: 
http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/basics-taxonomy-describing-illustrating-and-writing-biodiversity-1

PhD students from the Swedish Taxonomy Initiative's Research School might receive support from the STI to attend this course. 
For details about the procedure, these students should contact STI. 

Thank you for spreading the word,
