Hi Beth,

Well, I can give one name, Proconsul, Hopwood, 1933.  There is some pretty
earthy reasoning for naming after a person whom 
Hopwood did not like. It is named after a District Officer in East Africa, I
believe.  I gave a quick check, but could not find the earthy stuff.

In the entomology world, there are many entomologists who named species
after their girlfriends, mistresses and such, usually as anagrams.

To be accepted for publication, I'm thinking they would have to be a bit
more subtle than your last sentence suggests.
