2012 
The abstract submission deadline for the ATBC Asia-Pacific meeting in Xishuangbanna, China 24-27 March 2012 is extended to 31 January 2012. Check the website for more information  http://www.tropicalbio.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=195&Itemid=95 

There are symposia on:

    Forest dynamics - Shin-ichiro Aiba (aiba at sci.kagoshima-u.ac.jp)
    Plant-animal conservation - Stephen Brown (stephen.browne at fauna-flora.org)
    Functional traits - Cao Kunfang (caokf at xtbg.ac.cn) & Kaoru Kitajima (kitajima at botany.ufl.edu)
    Peat swamps - Mary-Rose Posa (dbspmrc at nus.edu.sg) and Grace Blackham
    Soil dynamics and carbon - Douglas Schaefer (xiedaoan at xtbg.ac.cn)
    Insect-plant evolutionary ecology - Peng Yang Qiong (pengyq at xtbg.ac.cn)
    Tropical forest restoration - Steve Elliot (Stephen_elliott1 at yahoo.com)
    The future of taxonomy - John Kress (kressj at si.edu)
    Monitoring insect populations - David Roubik (roubikd at si.edu)
    Biogeography - Chuck Cannon (chuck.cannon at gmail.com)
    Species distribution modelling - Niels Raes (raes at nhn.leidenuniv.nl)
    Consequences of hunting for biodiversity - Rhett D Harrison (rharrison at xtbg.org.cn)
    Conservation issues in SE Asia - Bosco Chan (boscokf at kfbg.org)
    Bamboo ecology and conservation - Yu-Long Ding (yldong at vip.163.com)
    Phylogenetic techniques, providing new answers to old questions - Alice C. Hughes (ah3881 at hotmail.co.uk)
