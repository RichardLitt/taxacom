Curator position in zoology (Helsinki)

The Zoological Unit of the Finnish Museum of Natural History,  
University of Helsinki, Finland, invites applications for a position  
of a curator with specialization either on vertebrates or  
invertebrates, exclusive of insects. This is a permanent appointment,  
starting in August 2012.

Please find the full announcement and instructions at
http://www.luomus.fi/english/zoology/job.htm .

The closing date is 6 February 2012.
