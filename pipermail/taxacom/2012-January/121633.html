The late Stanislaw Bleszynski, a crambid specialist from Poland, named a
genus.  He called it "La".
In the genus La, he described:
La cucaracha 
La golandrina
and other species names which I cannot recall. Some in Ottawa did not
approved of this sort of playing.
I think it was in the late 60s.  He was killed in a car accident somewhere
about 1970, maybe later. 
I met him when he was a post-doctorate fellow in Ottawa.

Robin
