There was no dispute between Lamarck and Deshayes. Lamarck was idolized by 
Deshayes. In his second edition of Lamarck's "Histoire naturelle des animaux 
sans vertèbres", Deshayes went to great lengths to conserve names introduced 
by Lamarck that should have been placed in synonymy.
