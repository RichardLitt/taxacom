2012 
Starting on 1st of January, PhytoKeys published several plant taxonomy
papers electronically, one per day. Today the issue was completed and is
available at: http://www.pensoft.net/journals/phytokeys/issue/8/. The
articles will be archived in PubMedCentral.
<http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/journals/1603/>

In future, PhytoKeys will publish taxonomic papers on the day they are
approved by the editors. PhytoKeys will continue printing a full-color
printed version of the journal and sending  complimentary copies to leading
botanical libraries to ensure 100 % secure archiving.

Four press releases are associated to the present issue:

Brave new world: Pioneering electronic publication of new plant
species<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2012-01/pp-jbb123011.php>

Early land plants: Early adopters - The first electronically described
liverwort species comes from New
Zealand<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2012-01/pp-elp010412.php>

Jeanne Baret, botanist and first female circumnavigator, finally
commemorated in name of new
species<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2012-01/pp-jbb123011.php>
A new wild ginger discovered from the evergreen forest of Western Ghats of
South India<http://www.eurekalert.org/pub_releases/2012-01/pp-anw010512.php>
