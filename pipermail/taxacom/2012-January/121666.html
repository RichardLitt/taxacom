Dear Michael etc.,

Here are the most common species names of bees, based on the ITIS publication of 19508 species names (http://www.itis.gov/beechecklist.html). As you can see some of the most common names are actually in honor of great bee taxonomists (and their wives, Wilmatt was TDA Cockerell's wife). The list here is only for names used more than 15 times, I can send the complete list if needed...:

micheneri         38
rufipes               31
cockerelli          26
friesei                22
similis                21
bicolor               21
rozeni                20
mourei              20
timberlakei       19
elegans             19
rufiventris         18
nigripes             17
tricolor              16
wilmattae         16
minor                 16
schwarzi            15
rufescens          15
montana           15
occidentalis      15
minima              15
hurdi                  15
australis            15
clypeata            15
