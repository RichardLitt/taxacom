Hi Ken, 
Trans-tropical Pacific groups in New Guinea and tropical America (matching Bataceae) are quite common. Examples include the plant groups Polygalaceae tribe Moutabeeae and a clade in Monimaceae (both from molecular studies), and the spider Masteria (from Platnick's website). The disjunction may involve central Pacific terranes (some including wood fossils) that accreted westward to New Guinea etc., eastward to the Caribbean, Colombia etc.
