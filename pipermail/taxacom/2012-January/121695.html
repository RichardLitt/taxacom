Colleagues:  Posted on behalf of SPNHC 2012 by Rich Rabeler.



It is with great pleasure that the Local Organizing Committee of SPNHC 2012 announces that registration is NOW OPEN!





Please join us at the Peabody Museum of Natural History at Yale University in New Haven, Connecticut for the SPNHC 2012 Annual Meeting (June 11-16) for Emerging Technology and Innovation in Natural History Collections Management.

http://peabody.yale.edu/collections/spnhc2012/home

Early bird registration rates through April 6th.



*Abstracts*

Abstract submission is open through April 13th for poster and oral sessions.



*Travel grants available*

Graduate students and emerging professionals can apply for a Fitzgerald Travel Grant designed to assist members with the cost of attending the Society’s annual meeting. A total of $3,000 is available and individual awards will be for a minimum of $750 USD each.



Questions? Email us at spnhc2012 at yale.edu<mailto:spnhc2012 at yale.edu>.



See you in New Haven!

-SPNHC 2012 Local Committee
