All identification keys, published in ZooKeys (since issue 50), PhytoKeys,
and MycoKeys are automatically indexed in
KeyCentral<http://keycentral.identifylife.org/>,
a platform of IdentifyLife <http://www.identifylife.org> - a collaborative
partnership of the Atlas of Living Austlalia, Encyclopedia of Life and
Moore Foundation.

KeyCentral <http://keycentral.identifylife.org/> collects information on
various kinds of keys and allows users to search through taxa, regions,
keywords, etc. Each key description links back to the original source, no
matter whether it is an online key or a journal article.

The technology used by Pensoft's journals, based on
TaxPub<http://sourceforge.net/projects/taxpub/>,
allows several keys published in one and the same article to be indexed as
separate entities, increasing the probability for discovery, visibility and
citation for the authors.
