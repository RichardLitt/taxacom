Heads has already pointed out that all organisms have a means of
dispersal. To 'explain' distributions by a presumed role for that
dispersal ability is to explain nothing if one then ignores the
geographic character of any one organism with respect to any other. The
tectonic correlations for the biogeography of taxa suggests that the
means of dispersal operated with respect to the geological (and
associated environmental conditions) history of those tectonics.

John Grehan
