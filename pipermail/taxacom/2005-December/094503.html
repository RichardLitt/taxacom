Well, it's a great comic and the thrust is a good political comment, but
before we get complacent, remember that the opposition accepts
microevolution (viruses evolving into new types of essentially the same
species) but not macroevolution (speciation where you get a different
species). Doonesbury's strip doesn't quite hit the mark, and one must rely
on other arguments (fossils, demonstration of recent internal barriers to
crossing plus (one hopes) morphological differences, and the like).
Creationists and intelligent designers are not impressed with this comic.
