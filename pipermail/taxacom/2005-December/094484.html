VegBank.org has a pretty nice map of the states and provinces:
http://vegbank.org/vegbank/forms/plot-map-northamerica.jsp?mapSize=largest

As long as your needs aren't commercial, you could snag that one.  Since
you probably don't want the states and provinces colored according to how
many plots VegBank has, the blank original map is here:

http://aldo.vegbank.org/~lee/misc/vegbank_blank_northam_map.png

I generally size it down after shading it in and soften the lines a bit,
if you have a program that does that sort of thing.
