Glabella: feminine diminutive of hairless, bald, smooth: glaber (m.:
glabellus).  Not sure about aemula, but could be from emulatus, rivaling
(emulating), hence aemulor, to rival or envy, thence a feminine.  Not
very likely to have been names!
