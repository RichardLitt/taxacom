On 1/21/16 11:13 AM, Hans Henderickx wrote:
> However, according the ICZN about online publishing the names in an online publication are only 'legally' registered after registration in ZooBank.
> See http://iczn.org/node/40562 . Until than the publication should be considered as 'non valid' and the names as 'unavailable'
> see:
> http://iczn.org/content/electronic-publication-made-available-amendment-code
Not sure if that's the best way to phrase it. A name that exists ONLY in 
digital form requires a ZooBank registration, *given in the publication 
itself*, in order to be available. If the work itself does not mention 
the registration, and it is actually still registered, that isn't enough 
to make it available - it is not the registration itself that makes the 
name available, but the explicit indication in the work. If the same 
work is later printed, and its earlier digital version was unavailable, 
then the name becomes available from the printed version's date.
