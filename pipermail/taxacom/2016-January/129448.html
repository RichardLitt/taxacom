Cut the ad hominem rhetoric Frank. I don't see evil everywhere. But I can do the maths and see that $20/page multiplied by many thousands/year of taxonomic papers of limited interest amounts to a significant amount of public money spent on making stuff available to read when hardly anyone wants to read it. It is also an incentive for some authors to pad out their papers to be as long winded as possible.
