Following the principle that the work becomes available when all requirements are fulfilled (see my previous email reply to Laurent on this list), and assuming all other requirements for publication are met, my interpretation would be that the date of publication for purposes of priority should be 23 January 2016. If numerous copies of the paper edition were simultaneously obtainable prior to this date, and if the paper edition is in compliance with the Code for published works printed on paper, then the date of publication for purposes of priority should be interpreted as the date on which numerous copies of the printed edition were simultaneously obtainable (see Art. 21.9).What is, or is not visible through the ZooBank website is irrelevant. The Code makes reference to content in the Official Register of Zoological Nomenclature, only a subset of which is visible on the website itself.
