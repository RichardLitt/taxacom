2016 
Dear Friends and Colleagues,

As part of the World Congress of Malacology, to be held in Malaysia in July 2016, we will hold a symposium on the use of mollusks in climate change research. Selected papers from the symposium will be published in the Journal of Molluscan Research.

This is a nice opportunity to enjoy a wonderful place while doing excellent science. Please visit the site of the congress for more information
http://wcm2016.usm.my/

Hope to see many of you there. Have a nice 2016.
