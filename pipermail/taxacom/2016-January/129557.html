It's just been pointed out to me that this list contains four criteria, 
not three.
> " The requirements for electronic publications are that the work be 
> registered in ZooBank before it is published, that the work itself 
> state the date of publication and contain evidence that registration 
> has occurred, and that the ZooBank registration state both the name of 
> an electronic archive intended to preserve the work and the ISSN or 
> ISBN associated with the work."
>
(1) work registered in ZooBank before it is published
(2) work itself states the date of publication
(3) work contains evidence that registration has occurred
(4) the ZooBank registration states both the name of an electronic 
archive intended to preserve the work and the ISSN or ISBN associated 
with the work

 From what I can see, two of these are fulfilled (1 and 2) but not the 
latter two.
