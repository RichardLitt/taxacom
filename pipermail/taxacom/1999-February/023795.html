> traditional sources such as Zoological Record or the ITIS

For Tomolonus, you might want to check our Checklist of the Collembola
of the World at
http://www.geocities.com/~fransjanssens/

Tomolonus is a Tomoceridae genus. In the Tomoceridae checklist,
clicking on the keyword 'genus' will pop up the global
distribution map for that Genus. Also, check the Tomoceridae key and its
rationale for a bit more detail on morphology and taxonomy.
