The Clayton Herbarium database and specimen images are now available
on-line to the public and botanists via the World Wide Web. Clayton plant
collections date from the 1730s and were collected in Virginia, many are
Linnaean types.

Clayton home page:
http://www.nhm.ac.uk/botany/clayton/index.html

Clayton search page
http://wallace.nhm.ac.uk:8030/Cfdocs/clayton/
