This, and the comments on pronunciation by non-Anglophones reminded me of
the wonderful generic names proposed for uropodine mites by Werner
Hirschmann in 1979.  Germans apparently don't have a phobia against long
words.  Let the following roll off your tongues!  (Note that he also
conspicuously honored his Hungarian colleagues, J. Balogh & Z. Kaszab, who
supervised the collecting of much of the material).
