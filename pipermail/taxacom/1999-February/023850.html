1999 
The second Systematics Association Biennial Conference is being held at the
University of Glasgow 23-27 Aug 1999.

The Biennial Conferences of the Systematics Association are intended to
provide a
forum for all systematists to describe and discuss their research. This
Second Biennial Conference in the University of Glasgow will continue with
the very successful mixture of open sessions and focussed thematic
sessions. However the organizers are keen to stress that this conference is
open to everyone, and would encourage all systematists to participate,
whatever your background, research topic, or experience.

Thematic sessions currently under preparation include Co-Evolution,
Biometrics and Character Coding, and Cladogenesis.

The deadline for submitting abstracts (by email) is 1 May 1999.

Details on registration, submitting talk and poster titles, and
accomodation can be found on the meeting Web site:

http://www.geology.gla.ac.uk/palaeo/syst99/
