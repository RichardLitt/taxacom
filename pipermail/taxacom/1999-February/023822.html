1999 
FIRST ANNOUNCEMENT:

29-31 October 1999

Harvard University Herbaria
22 Divinity Avenue
Cambridge, Massachusetts 02138
USA.

The explosion in the creation and utilization of biodiversity data
systems has brought to the fore the need for standards, models
and interoperability.  This workshop is organized by the
International Taxonomic Databases Working Group, TDWG, a
specialist group of IUBS, to further the creation and use of
standards in the biodiversity data systems community.

                                    ***************************
