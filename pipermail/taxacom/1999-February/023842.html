        CAUTION on the last sentence:  Triton includes only names that have
appeared in Zoological Record during the past 20 years.  This is very
useful, and perhaps virtually complete for new names proposed during this
period, but only the more commonly used older generic (and specific) names
are there.  Thus, it is far from adequate as a way to locate ANY possible
earlier use of a proposed new generic name!
