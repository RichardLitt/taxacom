Friends:

    If I wasn't so inept with computers, I would have probably made
it easier to view the pictures of my unknown lily. Thanks to those of
you who have offered ideas as to its identity; I believe it probably
is a species of Bulbine. Thanks also to Monique Reed (TAES) for
suggesting that I make the illustrations available electronically, and
to my colleague, Tim Mousseau, here at USC who has provided a nice
little URL:

        http://cricket.biol.sc.edu/lily.html

 which contains 3 pictures of the plant. The description is there,
too.

    Later John Nelson
