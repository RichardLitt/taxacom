Dear Taxacomers - a question to the cladists among us.

What do you think the best reweighting strategy for parsimony analysis is
when you have multiple islands of equally parsimonious trees? If you
include all the equally parsimonious trees when calculating the mean value
of whatever weighting function you are going to use, then the final weight
is likely to be influenced by the size of the islands. With maximum values
the problem may not be so bad, but nevertheless, very large islands are
more likely to include extremes.

All views welcomed,
