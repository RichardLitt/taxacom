Dear all,

I have been asked to notify TAXACOM about the following molecular
systematist position. Please note further details are available not
from me but from Stephen.Harris at Plant-sciences.ox.ac.uk.

University of Oxford
Department of Plant Sciences
Oxford Forestry Institute
Postdoctoral Position for Molecular Systematist

A postdoctoral position is available in the Molecular Systematics
Laboratory to work on the molecular systematics of neotropical pines.
Applicants should have a PhD in Plant Sciences or related subject.
Experience in the application of molecular methodologies to
systematic problems is required and a knowledge of pines would be an
advantage.

The position is funded through the Biotechnology Programme of DGXII
and is part of a European Union Forestry collaboration. The post is for a
fixed term of 2 years and is available immediately. Salary in the
range Sterling 14,317 - 17,466 on the RS1A scale. For more details
contact Dr. S.A. Harris Tel: 01865-275112 email: Stephen.Harris@
Plant-sciences.ox.ac.uk

Applications supported by a curriculum vitae and the names of two
referees should be addressed to The Administrator, Department of
Plant Sciences, South Parks Road, Oxford, OX1 3RB, UK, Fax: (44)-
(0)1865-275074, quoting reference FRA//2. Closing date is 31.1.97.

Colin Hughes
