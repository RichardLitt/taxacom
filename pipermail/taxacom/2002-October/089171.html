If you want to get a new approach for the identification of woody plants with a web interface: try ARTEMIS

ARTEMIS is an alternative to the classical dichotomous identification keys, relying to photographic illustrations for the explanation of concepts and taxonomic characters

ARTEMIS focuses to families and groups of related genera of woody plants (trees, shrubs, and lianas); tropical forests are of main concern, but trees of temperate forests are also identifiable 
  
ARTEMIS makes use of non reproductive characters, these being choosed upon classical vegetative traits and also architectural traits of the plant body

ARTEMIS is continuously updated

ARTEMIS was concepted by Roland Keller (botanist) and Beno?t Carpentier (programmer) at Lausanne University (Switzerland)
