Taxacom-ers,

Regarding the ancient art of georeferencing, I'd like to
recommend a couple MaNIS documents for your reference.

Go to the MaNIS documentation site at:
http://elib.cs.berkeley.edu/manis/Documents.html
and from there, select:

Georeferencing Guidelines
http://elib.cs.berkeley.edu/manis/GeorefGuide.html

Also from there, select the "Collaborative Georeferencing"
PowerPoint presentation just posted:

http://elib.cs.berkeley.edu/manis/GeorefCollaboration021021.ppt

The latter is a cursory introduction to the problems involved in
retrospective georeferencing.
