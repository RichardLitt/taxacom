The Centre for Plant Diversity and Systematics, University of Reading.
18th March - 28th March 2003

The course, suitable for experienced post-graduates or post-docs,
offers:
-in-depth coverage of techniques in molecular systematics and
comparative sequence analysis
-the treatment of theoretical issues in formal lectures coupled with
seminars and practical workshops

More details from

http://www.plantsci.rdg.ac.uk/molecularcourse.htm

from participants in previous years: "Brilliant!", "A very good
course",  "Thank you! for all your efforts and the great atmosphere on
the course. I really have learned a lot".
