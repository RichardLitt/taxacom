2002 
The National Museum of Natural History of the Smithsonian Institution is
searching for an excellent research scientist, who will also provide
intellectual, marketing and resource enhancement leadership for
invertebrate zoology and marine biology activities at the museum.  The ad
appeared in the 4 October issue of Science and can be found online at
www.sciencemag.org.  The official Smithsonian vacancy announcement and
detailed conditions are online at www.sihr.si.edu.  Applications are being
accepted until 8 November 2002.  Apologies to the international audience of
Taxacom, but this position is only open to U.S. citizens.
