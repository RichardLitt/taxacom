2020 
Hi All,
        Not much in the news about the current plague of desert locusts (the worst in 25 years) in east Africa, as well as causing problems as far east as Pakistan.   Starvation causes the deaths of up to 9,000,000 people each year, but how many of those might be due to desert locusts?  I haven't found any estimates yet, but it could perhaps cause deaths in the hundreds of thousands during 2020.
       Although desert locusts are edible, I doubt that they would provide much food in the long run (most of them don't stick around long after devastating an area).  Therefore, I might have to add genus Schistocerca to my list of deadliest genera which could result in over 50,000 deaths per year.   NOTE:  I probably should add genus Aedes to the list, since it carries several viruses which cause a total of over 50,000 deaths each year.
