Remember this (below) exchange? There was some doubt that the Wuhan coronavirus is a major problem.

I saw, however, a calculation on the Web that goes something like this:

There are 7.53  billion people on Earth, 7,530,000,000.
Say, 20% get the coronavirus.
That’s 1.506 billion or 1,506,000,000 people.
The death rate is presently almost exactly 0.02.
That's 30,012,000 deaths worldwide.

There are 329 million people in the USA, 329,000,000.
Again, say 20% get the coronavirus.
That is 65,800,000 people.
The death rate may be 0.02.
That's 1.316 million deaths, 1,316,000.

The death rate from the flu in the USA was 34,000 in 2019.
If the coronavirus is not contained (as was the case SARS and MERS), or a vaccine rapidly devised and distributed, then:
It would take 38 years for the deaths from flu to reach the projected deaths from coronavirus.
If nothing is done.

The coronavirus is definitely a far worse problem than the flu.

Dear All,

      The news media seems to be overly hyping the threat of the new coronovirus.  However, the threat might not be any more than the related SARS virus (which killed about 800 people worldwide).  Furthermore, the threat of the flu is far greater (well over 8,000 deaths in the U.S. so far this season and probably hundreds of thousands of deaths worldwide).  Hysteria like this could needlessly divert resources from detecting something even more deadly (which could cause a real pandemic).
