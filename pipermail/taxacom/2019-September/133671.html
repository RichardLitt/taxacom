> H.-W. B ROLE MANN
> HAPLOGONOPHORA nom. nov.
> (Melagonozonia, pro p., Brolemann, 1916.)
> Depuis notre publication de 1916 (Bull. Soc. Entom., France, année 1916,
> n° 1, p. 53), nous sommes arrivé à la conviction qu’il est impossible de
> maintenir côte à côte, dans le même Ordre, les Spiroboloïdes et les
> luloïdes. Nous instituons donc pour les premiers une coupe nouvelle,
> l’ordre des Haplogonophora, qui, jusqu’ici, ne contient que le sous-ordre
> des Spiroboloïdea.
> Pour unifier notre nomenclature, nous modifions les noms des
> Meta-gonozonia et des Progonozonia, créés en 1916, et les remplaçons par
> oeuxde :
> Metagonophora, qui ne comprend plus que les formes du type de nos luloïdes
> européens et peut-être les Cambalidae, et
> Progonophora, englobant les Spirostreptoidea et sous-ordres voisins, ainsi
> que les Polydesmoïdes.
> Nous aurons l’occasion de revenir ultérieurement sur ces grandes divisions
> des Diplopodes.

> In English (Google Translate)
>
> 278
> H.-W. B ROLE MANN
> HAPLOGONOPHORA name. Nov.
> (Melagonozonia, pro p., Brolemann, 1916.)
> Since our publication in 1916 (Bull Soc Entom, France, year 1916, No. 1,
> p.53), we have come to the conviction that it is impossible to maintain,
> side by side, in the same Order, the Spiroboloid and luloid. We thus
> institute for the first a new section, the order of the Haplogonophora,
> which until now contains only the suborder Spiroboloidea.
> To unify our nomenclature, we modify the names of Meta-gonozonia and
> Progonozonia, created in 1916, and replace them with:
> Metagonophora, which only includes forms of the type of our European
> luloids and perhaps Cambalidae, and
> Progonophora, encompassing Spirostreptoidea and neighboring suborders, as
> well as Polydesmoids.
> We will have the opportunity to return later to these great divisions of
> the Diplopoda.
>
> So looks like you are correct, this is not a genus name.

> Dear Taxacomers,
>
> I am looking at some presently unresolved names I have listed in Myriapoda,
> originating from Nomenclator Zoologicus (listed therefore as genera or
> subgenera), and have got to "Haplogonophora Broelemann 1932" which is
> listed with the publication details "Arch. Zool. exp. gén. Paris, 72,
> 278.". The full details of the original work appear to be:
>
> Brölemann, H. W. (1931). Myriapodes recueillis en Nouvelle -Calédonie et
> aux Loyality. Archives de Zoologie expérimentale et générale, 72(2):
> 275-316. Paris (
> http://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=sourcedetails&id$4465), however
> I have not seen this and it does not appear to be in BHL for copyright
> reasons.
>
> Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirobolida) has "Haplogonophora
> Brolemann, 1931" listed as a synonym of the order Spirobolida Bollman, 1893
> in Diplopoda, but without additional information RE sources. So I am
> wondering if Nomen. Zool. is in error and this name is in fact a higher
> taxon name and not a genus.
>
> MilliBase (http://www.millibase.org/) does not appear to contain this name
> at present, either as a genus or a higher taxon name, valid name or
> synonym, so any advice from readers of Taxacom would be welcome (for
> example if someone has access to page 278 of the work cited above).
