Hi John,

I am no expert and cannot answer your question exactly, but the following
recent sources may be relevant:

- Santibáñez-López et al., 2019: Phylogenomics facilitates stable scorpion
systematics: Reassessing the relationships of Vaejovidae and a new
higher-level classification of Scorpiones (Arachnida).
http://www.sharmalabuw.org/uploads/1/3/6/1/13619635/santib%C3%A1%C3%B1ez_l%C3%B3pez_et_al._2019.pdf

- Sharma et al., 2018: A revised dated phylogeny of scorpions: Phylogenomic
support for ancient divergence of the temperate Gondwanan family
Bothriuridae.
https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Prashant_Sharma18/publication/322722851_A_revised_dated_phylogeny_of_scorpions_Phylogenomic_support_for_ancient_divergence_of_the_temperate_Gondwanan_family_Bothriuridae/links/5a74b43ba6fdcc10269599ba/A-revised-dated-phylogeny-of-scorpions-Phylogenomic-support-for-ancient-divergence-of-the-temperate-Gondwanan-family-Bothriuridae.pdf

- Rein, 2019: The Scorpion Files: Iuridae.
https://www.ntnu.no/ub/scorpion-files/iuridae.php

Otherwise, I would just check the genera of interest in Google Scholar with
a date filter, for example,
https://scholar.google.com/scholar?as_ylo 15&q=Iurus , and see what shows
up...

In case they are useful, and you have not already seen them.

Regards - Tony
