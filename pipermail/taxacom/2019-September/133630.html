Thank you for making this available on the TAXACOM listserv!


Submissions are invited to the 10th International Symposium on the
Chrysomelidae (s.l.) which will be held on Friday, 24 July, 2020, during
the International Congress of Entomology (ICE 2020), July 19th - 24th, 2020
in Helsinki, Finland. Oral presentations and posters on all aspects of the
biology of leaf and seed beetles are welcome. The symposium will cover
phylogeny, ecology, taxonomy, or morphology and other equally interesting
topics. Please contact Caroline S. Chaboo (insectrescons at gmail.com
<sttdj01 at gmail.com>) and Theo Michael Schmitt (
michael.schmitt at uni-greifswald.de) with questions. Submissions to the
symposium are done through this link:
https://submit.peerageofscience.org/conference/ICE_2020/110107 or
https://submit.peerageofscience.org/conference/ICE_2020/110152 Conference
information can be found here: https://ice2020helsinki.fi/
