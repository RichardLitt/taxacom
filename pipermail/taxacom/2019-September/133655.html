Dear Taxacom members, 

After the three-year-long pause, the Natural
History Museum of Montenegro, is resuming regular publication of journal
for science and popular science „NATURA MONTENEGRINA“.  

The
Journal became an important source of scientific information, primarily
about the research and protection of the biodiversity of the BALKAN
region (Southern Europe). Although the journal was focused on the Balkan
Peninsula, it published the results of the research from other regions
of Europe and Asia. 

We invite you to contribute to the next volumes of
the journal with your scientific or popular-science contributions and
surveys. 

You can find more details about the aims and scopes of the
journal, the instructions for authors, the members of our editorial
board, and other relevant information at the web address:


http://pmcg.co.me/natura-montenegrina/ 

At this web page, you can
also download, free of charge, previously published papers. 

I look
forward to further collaboration. Best regards, 

Dr Marko G. Karaman
