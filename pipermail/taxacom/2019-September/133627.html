I guess that there was a time when only the people who did the work and wrote the paper were listed as authors. These days it seems that anyone remotely involved, such as the night watch person, dishwasher, janitor, post clerk, cleaners and any stray person walking past who made a comment could be included as authors. 
Just joking but it wasn't too long ago that many of these "authors" would have simply been thanked in the acknowledgements.
