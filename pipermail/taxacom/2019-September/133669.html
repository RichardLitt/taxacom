Dear Taxacomers,

I am looking at some presently unresolved names I have listed in Myriapoda,
originating from Nomenclator Zoologicus (listed therefore as genera or
subgenera), and have got to "Haplogonophora Broelemann 1932" which is
listed with the publication details "Arch. Zool. exp. gén. Paris, 72,
278.". The full details of the original work appear to be:

Brölemann, H. W. (1931). Myriapodes recueillis en Nouvelle -Calédonie et
aux Loyality. Archives de Zoologie expérimentale et générale, 72(2):
275-316. Paris (
http://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=sourcedetails&id$4465), however
I have not seen this and it does not appear to be in BHL for copyright
reasons.

Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirobolida) has "Haplogonophora
Brolemann, 1931" listed as a synonym of the order Spirobolida Bollman, 1893
in Diplopoda, but without additional information RE sources. So I am
wondering if Nomen. Zool. is in error and this name is in fact a higher
taxon name and not a genus.

MilliBase (http://www.millibase.org/) does not appear to contain this name
at present, either as a genus or a higher taxon name, valid name or
synonym, so any advice from readers of Taxacom would be welcome (for
example if someone has access to page 278 of the work cited above).

Thanks in advance - Tony
