The underwing pattern and basal depth of the wings seems a much better fit for African Hawk Eagle. I think the "crown" is a product of the way that the bird's head is being held. From what has been shared, there is no suggestion that THIS bird harmed a child, just that locals think it's the same as birds that they believe to have harmed/killed children. This may or may not be correct in both aspects.

This rather looks like a young African Hawk Eagle, Aquila spilogaster,
to me.
(The pics are not really ideal (too close) to appreciate the general
structure of the bird. In any case, the pattern of the flight feathers
(tight, thin, weakly contrasting barring) does not match African Crowned
Eagle.)
