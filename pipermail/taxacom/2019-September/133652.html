This rather looks like a young African Hawk Eagle, Aquila spilogaster,
to me.
(The pics are not really ideal (too close) to appreciate the general
structure of the bird. In any case, the pattern of the flight feathers
(tight, thin, weakly contrasting barring) does not match African Crowned
Eagle.)
