Another source is my own compilation, IRMNG, which has content compiled
from the literature over a number of years but is not
necessarily up-to-date or completely internally consistent (or all names
allocated to families as yet) at this time (i.e., may throw up some names
of interest for further checking, but is not authoritative). You can search
for the name of a higher taxon (i.e., genus or above) as per a URL of this
type:

http://www.irmng.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&searchpar=0&tComp=is&tName=Iuridae

In case this helps.
