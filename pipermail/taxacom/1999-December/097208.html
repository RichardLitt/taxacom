1999 
Next year two subsequent symposia will be organised in Leiden, the
Netherlands:

"Hennig XIX", 29 May - 2 June 2000, organised by the National Herbarium of
the Netherlands - Universiteit Leiden branch, Naturalis - Nationaal
Natuurhistorisch Museum, Research school Biodiversity and the Hennig Society.

"Biogeography of Southeast Asia 2000 - organisms and orogenesis", 4 - 9
June 2000, organised by the Naturalis - Nationaal Natuurhistorisch Museum,
National Herbarium of the Netherlands - Universiteit Leiden branch, and the
Research school Sedimentary Geology.

Please visit the website:

                http://nhncml.leidenuniv.nl/symposia/

for further information on the contents and the registration, and for the
call for papers and abstracts.
