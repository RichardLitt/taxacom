2008 
The International Triticeae Consortium is pleased to announce that the
next International Triticeae Symposium will take place at Kyoto
University on June 1-5, 2009.  The first announcement is located at
http://herbarium.usu.edu/triticeae/symposium6.htm. The meeting will
feature prsentations on the interfaces between systematics, phylogeny,
evolution, domestication, genetic resources, and genomics. Additional
information will be made available at this site as it becomes available.
Meanwhile, if you are interested in presenting a paper at the meeting,
please send an email to the secretary of the local organizing committee,
Dr. Kazuhiro Sato (kazsato [symbol for at] ib.okayama-u.ac.jp
<mailto:kazsato at rib.okayama-u.ac.jp> ) or the chair of the sponsoring
organization, Dr. Roland von Bothmer Roland.von.Bothmer [symbol for at]
ltj.slu.se <mailto:Roland.von.Bothmer at ltj.slu.se>  (or me).
 
Mary
