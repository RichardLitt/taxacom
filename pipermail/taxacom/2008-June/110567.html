On 18 Jun 2008, at 17:01, Ken-ichi Ueda wrote:

> Wow, that's awesome Rod, probably exactly what I want.  Does the
> <ubio:hasSYNConcept> entity always reference an accepted name, or can
> it reference any synonymous concept, accepted or otherwise?  I'm
> having trouble finding the docs for the ubio and gla predicates used
> in those RDFs.

The gla and ubio predicates date from early efforts to assign LSIDs to  
taxonomic names. My now defunct Taxonomic Search Engine (see http://dx.doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-6-48 
  ) assigned LSIDs to names served by several name servers. I order to  
do this I developed a simple vocabulary ("gla"). You can get some  
basic information about each gla predicate by resolving the  
corresponding LSID. For example, gla:rank is urn:lsid:lsid.zoology.gla.ac.uk:predicates:rank 
. Put this into your resolver of choice and you will get a RDF  
document describing the term. There's also some background in my 2006  
paper http://jbi.nhm.ku.edu/index.php/jbi/article/viewArticle/25 .

When uBio started serving LSIDs they used my vocabulary for some  
terms, and added their own. Simililary, you can get RDF for any uBio  
predicate by resolving the corresponding LSID "urn:lsid:ubio.org:predicates 
:" + the predicate, e.g. urn:lsid:ubio.org:predicates:hasSYNConcept .

My vocabulary, and that of uBio were develpped before TDWG developed  
their own (http://wiki.tdwg.org/twiki/bin/view/TAG/LsidVocs ), which  
IMHO are rather more verbose than they need to be.
