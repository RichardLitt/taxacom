Dear colleagues!

Within the limits of constantly replenished atlas of beetles
of Russia and Adjacent Lands,
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/atlas.htm
(the number of specific pages already exceeds 3500)
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/atlascar.htm
recently based on efforts
of well known expert on the genus Carabus Dmitry Obydov
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/eng/obydov.htm
unique sections on several subgenera of the genus Carabus
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/carab.htm
are made.
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/carab06.htm
These sections include the original illustrated indexes
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/carab04.htm
of species and subspecies, very convenient for fast viewing and
comparison of taxa and specimens, and also large-scale photos of
the beetles, among which many type specimens.

I invite to visit these pages (and at the same time the large
illustrated indexes of genera and subgenera of Cerambycidae
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/atlcegen.htm
and Tenebrionidae
http://www.zin.ru/Animalia/Coleoptera/rus/atl_te.htm
also).

Andrei Lobanov
web-editor & bild-editor
