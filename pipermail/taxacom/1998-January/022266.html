I too am not convinced about the accuracy of GPS. Our group managed to
borrow one when mapping a forest reserve border in N. Tanzania. Even with
full satellite reception co-ordinates were usually way out, especially
the altitude (once putting us at an altitude of minus 45 metres when
standing on a hill top!). We resorted back to our compass and rope and
clinometre but the worrying thing is the group we borrowed it from were
taking the co-ordinates from the gps to map where certain plant species
exist in the forests for future reference, i for sure would not like to be
the person in the future wondering around the forest in a few years time
looking for plants at imaginary co-ordinates.

Matt Davey
