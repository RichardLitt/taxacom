I have used two editions of the Trimble Scoutmaster (see:
http://www.trimble.com/index.htm) and have found them excellent.  Small
but durable; inexpensive but not cheap; far more features than I need.
The model purchased about 1 year ago has a port for external antenna
and a port for connection to a computer.  When Trimble started selling
direct from the factory the price dropped about 50%.  The model I
purchased, Scoutmaster GPS Flash, was about US$1000.  They had
cheaper models too.
