        The Macintosh species distribution mapping program 'RangeMapper'
can handle county dot maps (for the 48 contiguous U.S. states, not for
Canada) as well as direct lat/long plotting anywhere. The program is
simple (in the sense of 'easy to use') but not free. Contact Tundra
Vole Software for info on RangeMapper 2.3, at: <tvole at polarnet.com>.
