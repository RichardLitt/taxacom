At about US$15 I recommend Versamap- you may purchase detailed maps from
the author (I think they are about $5 per disk, for regions of the US). It
runs under DOS and is very simple to use.  It can use lat-longitudes from
databases to produce distribution maps.

VersaMap v1.50 (300K+) draws outline maps on 14 map
       projections. VERSAMAP is supplied with a digital world map
       from the VERSAMAP Micro World Database (an enhanced
       version of Micro World Data Bank II) which includes boundaries
       for the new countries formed from the Soviet Union and
       Yugoslavia, and for Eritrea and Slovakia. More detailed maps,
       from the VERSAMAP Micro World Database, from the CIA's
       World Data Bank II, and from the USGS 1:2,000,000 scale Digital
       Line Graphs, may be purchased from the author. Individual
       geographic features, such as coastlines, rivers, islands, and
       political boundaries can be plotted from these databases. The
       program can plot data (points or lines) supplied by the user. Text
       may be added to maps with Courier or Helvetica fonts. Maps can
       be output to dot matrix or laser printers; or as PCX, CGM, DXF,
       PIC, or ASCII files.

Here is a repository of GIS shareware for both Macs and Windows:
