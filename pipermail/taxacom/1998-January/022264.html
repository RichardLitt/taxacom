For Christmas 1996, my sone bough a trailblazer GPS unit for me for
$200!  It was a display model with instrcutions, case, box, etc.  The
unit works just fine, and Trailblazer sold me a set of instructions for
$15.  The was saving of about $300.  Watch for sales at your local
sporting goods which sell GPS units.
