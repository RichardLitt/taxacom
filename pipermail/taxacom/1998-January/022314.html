1998 
Missouri Botanical Garden seeks Senior Herbarium Assistant to join the
Research Division.

The selected individual will expedite and facilitate identification,
labeling and storage of plant material entering the herbarium; assist
curators with daily management of research projects; provide editorial
assistance in writing of scientific reports and proposals; and perform
various research activities according to the nature of the project.
Position may include field work and assist researchers in gathering
bibliographic and electronic data.

Qualifications include a Bachelor's degree in botany or related field;
Master's degree preferred.  Previous herbarium and field experience also
preferred.  Knowledge of computer database management and familiarity with
botanical literature required.  Reading knowledge of foreign language
helpful.

Missouri Botanical Garden offers outstanding benefits, including health,
dental, vision, and life insurance, and retirement and tax deferred
annuity programs.  Position is open immediately.  Review of applications
begin immediately and will be accepted until position is filled.  To
apply, please submit resume, including names and phone numbers of three
references to Missouri Botanical Garden, Human Resource Management, P.O.
Box 299, St. Louis, Missouri  63110-0299.  E-mail: rland at admin.mobot.org.
Web site: www.mobot.org.  Equal Opportunity Employer
