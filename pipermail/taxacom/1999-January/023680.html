1999 
Algologia's summer courses take place the first week of July and September
at Sarti in the North Aegean. You are welcome to attend the lectures on the
taxonomy, systematics and biogeography of Mediterranean seaweeds, the
preparation of herbarium specimens, the species identification, and the
excursions to several biotopes.

For further information please contact Algologia or visit the web site
