I am in (frantic) search of a missing package, and I send this
message in hopes that if it arrives by some chance at your
institution you might be kind enough to direct it to me.

bearing the TAMU--College Station stamp and 18 exchange specimens
(some or all unmounted) collected by Mark Mayfield and Billie Turner
