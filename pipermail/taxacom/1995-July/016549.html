NEWS FLASH: AMERICAN ENTOMOLOGICAL INSTITUTE PUBLICATIONS

The American Entomological Institute is now directly publishing
its journals "Memoirs of the American Entomological Institute"
and "Contributions of the American Entomological Institute", and
is actively seeking manuscripts for its publications.  The focus
of the Institute's journals is arthropod systematics.  The
"Memoirs of the American Entomological Institute" (55 volumes to
date, beginning in 1961) deals with monographic studies or
catalogs of 300 printed pages or more; "Contributions of the
American Entomological Institute" (27 volumes to date) handles
smaller papers (40-250 pages).  These publications appear at
irregular intervals as manuscripts are received, averaging 2-3
"Memoirs" and one volume of "Contributions" per year.
Publication time for these refereed publications is rapid and
page charges are either minimal ("Contributions") or lacking
("Memoirs").

Examples of "Memoirs" include:
Vol. 34. O'Brien, C.W. & G.J. Wibmer. 1982. Annotated checklist
  of the weevils (Curculionidae s.l.) of North America, Central
  America,and the West Indies (Coleoptera: Curculionoidea).
Vol. 44. Wirth, W.W. & A.A. Hubert. 1989. The _Culicoides_ of
  Southeast Asia (Diptera: Ceratopogonidae)
Vol. 46. Gordh, G. 1990. A catalog of the world Bethylidae
  (Hymenoptera: Aculeata)
Vol. 47. Gauld, I. 1991. The Ichneumonidae of Costa Rica, 1.
Vol. 51. Johnson, N.F. 1992. Catalog of World species of
  Proctotrupoidea, exclusive of Platygastridae (Hymenoptera).

Some forthcoming "Memoirs" titles:
Gauld, I.  The Ichneumonidae (Hymenoptera) of Costa Rica, 2.
Yang, L., & J.C. Morse.  The Leptoceridae (Trichoptera) of China.

The Institute journals are continuing series and have not been
replaced by the new titles "Memoirs of Entomology, International"
and "Contributions of Entomology, International".
