One of the better manuals for herbarium management and practices was
prepared at the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.  It is used in their training
course for herbarium personal:
  Forman, L., and D. Bridson.  1989.  The Herbarium Handbook.  iv, 214
    pages, 52 figures.  Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew.
