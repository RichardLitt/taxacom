1995 
The USDA APHIS proposal for new proceedures for permits for
importing organisms into the US or between states (mostly as
relates to biological control) has been formally withdrawn
(Federal Register 60(116): 31647, 1995).  Basically the many
comments on the proposed rule caused USDA to feel a massive
re-write of the proceedures is necessary, so they will start
over.
