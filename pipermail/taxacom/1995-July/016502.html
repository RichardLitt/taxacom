1995 
We started tinkering with plant images captured from camcorder tapes
early this year and have progressed, at roughly weekly intervals, to
experiment with lighting, magnification, etc.  The pages at:

http://www.isc.tamu.edu/FLORA/tfpflo1.html

carry examples of images taken from hi-8 film using a low-end video
capture board.  Direct imaging (no film) would probably be a bit
sharper as would a dedicated camera (as opposed to a camcorder)
connected to a scope with good lighting control.
