On Wed, 19 Jul 1995, Stephen Halford wrote:

> There is probably some additional positive fallout from such an idea,
> showing up governments with excessively restrictive (*or liberal!*)
> permit requirements, and shaming 'em into more "conventional" attitudes.

On the several occassions in which I have been involved when governments
compared permit requirements, the most stringent requirements were
adopted by all.  Except in one case in which even more restrictive
requirements were adopted to "prove" that the particular country could
protect its national patrimony better.  Each time it turned into macho
one-up-manship contest.
