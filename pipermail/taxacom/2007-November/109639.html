Dear All,

I am please to inform you that the

African Insect Taxonomy Toolkit - Find everything on African Insects you
want to know

http://taxonomy.icipe.org/index.html

is now online. I have tried to collect as much information on African
Insects, usually from a taxonomic perspective, but also included pests
and pest related organisations. This includes citations and internet
links. The toolkit is not a final product, and will be continiously
extended and modified. So please do tell me if you have more useful
links and papers to be included in the toolkit. Every bit of information
is welcomed!

At this occasion I would also like to thank the IT Department, Glenn and
Ernest, for their technical help!

Best
Fabian Haas
