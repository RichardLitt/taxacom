Calling all taxonomists! 2023 sees the 200th anniversary of the birth of
the prolific natural history collector and co-discoverer of natural
selection, Alfred Russel Wallace (1823-1913). He collected around 126,000
specimens of birds, beetles, butterflies etc in S.E. Asia (the 'Malay
Archipelago'), including probably more than 5,000 new species. Many of the
birds were shot by his paid assistants, but most of the 110,000 insect
specimens were personally collected by him. Given his vast contribution to
our understanding of the natural history and biogeography of this region,
and of biology in general, it would be a fitting tribute if species/genera
could be named after him and published during (or just after) the
anniversary year. So far about 280 taxa have been named after him, most of
which are listed here:
