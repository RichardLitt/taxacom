On 12/1/22 1:39 AM, Laurent Raty via Taxacom wrote:
> Mathews' opinions on some of Meuschen's names were since (partly) 
> followed. One of Meuschen's species names, Rallus cinereus Meuschen 
> 1787 (p. 40), was used to displace Gracula carunculata Gmelin 1788, 
> and is now Creatophora cinerea (Meuschen 1787).

This would appear to be a case of an unavailable name presently in use, 
and therefore a significant problem in need of resolution.

In entomology, we have similar problems with names authored by Voet, all 
unavailable under Art. 11 but a few of which are still in use. It has 
been difficult, at times, to convince people to stop using these names.
