Similar phenomena are common in other situations, such as a monograph on
the taxa from a particular locality that has multiple species receiving the
same locality- or stratigraphy-based epithet.  MacNeil & Dockery (1984)
used the specific epithet menthafontinalis for multiple species from the
Mint Springs Formation.  Common Latin adjectives are used as specific
epithets multiple times in many early publications (vulgaris Linnaeus 1758
is not very helpful, for example.)

An even more unhelpful proposal  to promote flexibility in taxonomic rank
usage was to give names as
Whateverhighertaxonomiccategoryisinviewatthemoment specificepithet.  E.g.,
Homo sapiens, Hominidae sapiens, Primates sapiens, Mammalia sapiens...  The
number of homonyms that would create is appalling.
