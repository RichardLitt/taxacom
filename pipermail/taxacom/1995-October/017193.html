As an older person, I truely hope that the world will NOT go to the
publishing of descriptions of new taxa etc via the eletronic media. I for
one have many many visual problems with this form of communications. It
is much much easier for me to be able to cruse the journals, in my hot
little hand, and prop the needed ones up on the counter and use them than
it is to sit around with an extra 8 cubic feet of eletronic equipment
next to my scope to work. it is even more of a pain to have to hunt down
a working printer, etcd to run off a hard copy. PLEASE stay with "old
fashioned" hard copy in the journals. New - is not always better!!

CHEERS!!! rof
