1995 
The Bishop Museum is pleased to announce that our bird, mammal, and
herpetology type specimen database is now accessible through our WWW and
gopher servers at:

Web:
gopher://bernice.bishop.hawaii.org:70/11/BMData
                                     ^^
                                     = the number "eleven"

Gopher:
gopher.bishop.hawaii.org

The database contains selected information on the 17 bird, 11 mammal, and
6 herpetology type specimens in the Vertebrate Zoology collection at
Bishop Museum. Only primary types are included at this time. Specimens are
mainly from Hawaii and New Guinea.

Please refer comments and queries to:

Carla Kishinami, Collection Manager
Vertebrate Zoology
Bishop Museum
carlak at bishop.bishop.hawaii.org

Sincerely,

Neal L. Evenhuis
