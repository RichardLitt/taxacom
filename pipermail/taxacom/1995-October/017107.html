The botanists at Texas A&M are pleased to invite you to join us on a
(virtual) field trip to explore the unique flora of a sandstone
outcrop in Grimes Co., Texas.  Many interesting plants grow here,
including _Agalinis navasotensis_, which grows nowhere else.  The site
includes text and LOTS of nice images.  The URL is:
http://csdl.tamu.edu/FLORA/NAV-OUTCROP/OUTCROPINTRO.HTML

If you liked our bog trip, you'll love this one...

Bugs and problems with the page can be reported to
monique at bio.tamu.edu or manhart at bio.tamu.edu

Enjoy!
