COMPUTING JOB AVAILABLE AT CHARLES STURT UNIVERSITY
===================================================

Charles Sturt University

Charles Sturt University is one of Australia's leading regional
universities and has a reputation for providing quality vocational
education.  The university's main campuses are at Albury-Wodonga,
Bathurst, Wagga Wagga, and the Australian Graduate School of Police
Management in Sydney and Goulburn. The university seeks to employ
academic staff who will contribute to its growth and development.
--------------------------------------------------
Faculty of Science and Agriculture
School of Environmental and Information Science

Location      Albury-Wodonga

Position:     Associate Lecturer/Lecturer

The successful applicant will undertake and to assist research to
assist with teaching and implementation of the new course in Applied
Computing in the School of Environmental and Information Sciences
at the Albury-Wodonga Campus. Preference will be given to candidates
with knowledge of and interest in one of the three main computing
directions within the School: environmental informatics, complex
systems, and network publishing.  An ability to contribute to
research programmes of the Johnstone Research Centre for Parks,
Recreation and Heritage would be an advantage.  A PhD (or near
completion) is required.

For further information about the position and the School contact:
     Professor David Green
     Telephone              +61 60 41 8984
     Email                  dgreen at life.csu.edu.au

SALARY DETAILS
     Lecturer               $42,198 - $51,111
     Associate Lecturer     $29,539 - $40,087

NB: Appointment level for the Associate Lecturer/Lecturer positions
will depend on qualifications and experience with commencement
salary being within the range.  All positions may be offered on
either a fixed term or a tenured basis unless otherwise specified.

APPLICATION DETAILS
