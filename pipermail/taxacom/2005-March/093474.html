PhD, Post-doc positions in Systematic Botany at the University of Zurich

I would like to invite applications for two positions (one at a PhD
level, one at postdoctoral level) to work on the phylogeny,
systematics, and biogeography of the austral grass subfamily
Danthonioideae. I am particularly interested in applicants with
knowledge of, or an interest in, grass systematics, molecular
phylogenetics, and / or statistics. The positions are available from
1 June 2005, and the funding is for three years. For more details
about the project, please contact Peter Linder, at the email address
below.

Applicants should send an up-to-date CV, as well as the names, email
addresses, and telephone numbers of two referees, to Prof. Peter
Linder, at email address: plinder at systbot.unizh.ch, before 1 May 2005.
