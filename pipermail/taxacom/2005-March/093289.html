A TAXACOM subscriber recently wrote that he had stumbled on this
bio-bibliography by chance and that he found it useful as many people are
included who did important work in other fields.  The following is the
announcement of the just-posted 2nd edition.

> We are pleased to announce the posting, on the website of the American
> Malacological Society, of the Second Edition of the following publication:
>
> Eugene V. Coan, Alan R. Kabat & Richard E. Petit (2005), "2,400 Years of
> Malacology," online at:
> http://erato.acnatsci.org/ams/publications/epubs.html
>
http://erato.acnatsci.org/ams/publications/2400_malacology.html
>
> This publication is a comprehensive catalog of biographical and
> bibliographical papers on malacologists, conchologists, paleontologists,
and others with an
> interest in mollusks.
>
> Since the first edition was posted, in June 2004, we have received
comments
> and additions from a number of colleagues -- for which we are most
grateful --
> and we have continued our own searching through the extensive literature.
>
> This catalog is a work in progress, and we will be posting updated
versions
> periodically.  We encourage readers to explore and use this catalog, and
we
> look forward to receiving your comments.
>
> Gene Coan
> Alan Kabat
> Richard Petit - r.e.petit at att.net
