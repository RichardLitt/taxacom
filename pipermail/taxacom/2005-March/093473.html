Job Description
Description:  As seen in the 18 February issue of Science:



RESEARCH ASSISTANT
Department of Entomology
Insect Taxonomy
?25,000 p.a. dependent on experience London

Highly committed, independent, and able to work in a team, you will collaborate 
with the Keeper in his research program including studies in comparative 
morphology, descriptive taxonomy, species inventories, data-basing, digital 
imaging, and related research. You should hold a Ph.D. or equivalent in insect 
taxonomy or related field and have an interest in collections-based research.

For further technical information, please contact: Dr. Quentin D. Wheeler, 
Keeper and Head of Entomology, e-mail: q.wheeler at nhm.ac.uk. For further 
information and details of how to apply, please e-mail: nhm at tribalresourcing.com.

Please quote reference: Natural History Museum RA closing date: 25 March 2005 or 
until suitable candidate is identified. To confirm receipt of your application 
call the Museum response line at telephone: 0870-241-9031 between 11:00 a.m. and 
2:00 p.m., Monday to Friday only. No telephone requests for details will be 
accepted.

At the Natural History Museum, we value the diversity of our employees and the 
unique perspectives they contribute.
