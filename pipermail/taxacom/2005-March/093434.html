> My point in my post was that, reliability measures (bootstrap proportions,
> posterior probabilities) should be corrected for external assumptions. After
> correction, I suspect, molecularly based cladograms will have almost as poor

My experience (both from reading the literature and personal) has been
that morphology usually produces trees that are at least as resolved and
*much* better supported than molecular trees.  It's truly a pity that
morphological studies get such short shrift in funding these days.
