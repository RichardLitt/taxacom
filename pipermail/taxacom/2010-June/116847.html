Dear Sajid,

You do not say what resources you have tried so far. Commonly Google, Google Images, and Wikipedia are good internet starting points, presuming that you have access to these (I know that in some countries Google and / or Wikipedia access may be restricted). These will often bring up either images and descriptive information directly (Google images, Wikipedia) or links to online sources with additional information (Google, Wikipedia).

You can do the relevant searches at (examples for Hibiscus tiliaceus):

Google:
http://www.google.com.au/search?q=Hibiscus+tiliaceus

Google images (for photographs on the web):
http://www.google.com.au/images?q=Hibiscus+tiliaceus

Wikipedia:
http://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special%3ASearch&search=Hibiscus+tiliaceus

If you substitute "Avicennia+alba" for "Hibiscus+tiliaceus" you can obtain information on that species.

If you have already tried these sources and not found what you want, you will probably need to access more specialised systems or library resources but the Google/Wikipedia results should point to at least some of these.

Hoping the above is perhaps helpful,
