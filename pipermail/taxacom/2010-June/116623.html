The Botanische Staatssammlung M?nchen (www.botanischestaatssammlung.de 
<http://www.botanischestaatssammlung.de/>) houses important fungal 
collections with around 350,000 specimens. Recently, we got a precious 
collection of 1000 specimens of mushrooms and other macrofungi from 
Papua New Guinea determined only at genus level. i. e.:

Agaricus, Agrocybe, Albatrellus, Amanita, Armillaria, Aseroe, 
Auricularia, Auriscalpium, Austroboletus, Boletellus, Boletus, 
Bondarzewia, Calocera, Calvatia, Camarophyllus, Campanella, 
Cantharellus, Clavaria, Clavariadelphus, Claviceps, Clavulina, 
Clitocybe, Clitopilus, Collybia, Coltricia, Conocybe, Coprinus, 
Cordyceps, Corticium, Cortinarius, Crepidotus, Cyptotrama, Cystoderma, 
Dermocybe, Dictyophora, Elaphomyces, Entoloma, Filoboletus, Fistulina, 
Galerina, Ganoderma, Geastrum, Geoglossum, Grifola, Gymnopilus, 
Gyroporus, Hebeloma, Helotium, Helvella, Hemimycena, Hohenbuehelia, 
Hydnellum, Hydnum, Hydropus, Hygrocybe, Hygrophorus, Hypholoma, 
Hypomyces, Hysterangium, Inocybe, Isaria, Laccaria, Lactarius, 
Laetarius, Leccinum, Lentinula, Lentinus, Lenzites, Leotia, Lepiota 
Lepista, Lycogala, Lycoperdon, Lyophyllum, Macrolepiota, Marasmiellus, 
Marasmius, Melanotus, Merulius, Morchella, Mutinus, Mycena, Mycenella, 
Omphalia, Omphalina, Oudemansiella, Panellus, Panus, Peziza, Phallus, 
Phellinus, Pholiota, Phylloporus, Phyllotopsis, Pleurotus, Pluteus, 
Polyporus, Poromycena, Psathyrella, Pseudohyrella, Pseudomphalina, 
Psilocybe, Pterula, Pulveroboletus, Pycnoporus, Ramaria, Resupinatus, 
Rhizopogon, Russula, Sarcodon, Schizophyllum, Scleroderma, Spathularia, 
Stereum, Strobilomyces, Suillus, Thelephora, Trametes, Tremella, 
Tricholoma, Tylopilus, Urnula, Vascellum, Volvariella, Xerocomus, 
Xeromphalina, Xerula, as well as some other unidentified 
Aphyllophorales, Ascomycetes Tricholomataceae, Uredinales.

The material was collected between 2001 and 2007 by the German 
mycologist Dr. Roland Treu. It was legally exported from Papua New 
Guinea and formally accessed at the Botanische Staatssammlung M?nchen. 
We herewith offer to send the material for loan to recognized botanical 
and mycological institutions for non-commercial research projects, e.g. 
floristic and monographic studies or similar scientific purposes. The 
loan request has to follow the regulations under 
http://www.botanischestaatssammlung.de/herbarium/loans.html.
