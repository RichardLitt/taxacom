Taxacom - I'm forwarding a message from Biodiversity Heritage Library's
Collections Coordinator, Bianca Lipscomb, about a new feature we just
released that allows users to submit scanning requests.  We prioritized
this development based on results of our recent user survey and other
strong user feedback.  We'll be picking off more of these "quick & easy"
requests over the next couple of months and informing users as they are
completed.
