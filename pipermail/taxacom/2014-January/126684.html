Hello all,

The Organization for Tropical Studies ( <http://www.ots.ac.cr>
www.ots.ac.cr) is offering a course in ?Tropical Plant Systematics?
http://bit.ly/11WBLjk 

This course emphasizes a strong conceptual foundation in phylogenetic
systematics in an intensive field setting. The course travels to diverse
habitats around Costa Rica, from cloud forest and paramo to tropical dry
forest and Atlantic lowland rain forest.

The course will be taught by Dr. Brad Boyle from June 11 to July 14, 2014. 

 

Credits: 6 semester credits awarded by the University of Costa Rica

 

Application Deadline: February 10, 2014 for priority consideration, followed
by rolling admission until fully enrolled.

 

For detailed information please visit http://bit.ly/11WBLjk 

 

I greatly appreciate the distribution of this information to students that
might be interested.
