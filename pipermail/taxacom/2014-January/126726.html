Dear all,


The DEST Expert-in-training programme offers a variety of unique training-on-the-job topics going from rotifers and midges up to bats and field research. The trainee will join a project team and be coached by an highly skilled and enthusiastic expert(s). Courses are open to trainees from Europe and outside of Europe.


Registration deadline for some of the courses is approaching: 31 January !

Info at:

http://www.taxonomytraining.eu/content/expert-training-programme-2013-2014
