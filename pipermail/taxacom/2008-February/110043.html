2008 
NORTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY LIBRARIES

The North Carolina State University Libraries, recognized as the first 
recipient of the Association of College
and Research Libraries’ Excellence in Academic Libraries Award, offers a 
working environment of innovation,
teamwork, and continuous interaction with students and faculty to 
further the educational mission of NC State University.
NCSU Libraries invites applications and nominations for the following 
position:


ASSOCIATE HEAD AND CURATOR, SPECIAL COLLECTIONS RESEARCH CENTER

Shares management responsibility for a department of 7 librarians, 3 
staff, and 5 graduate assistants, and represents the department in the 
absence of the department head. In the role of Curator, works closely 
with faculty, researchers, administrators, and collection managers to 
build primary source, rare book, and born-digital collections; builds 
distinguished collections in focus areas, such as the history of 
computing and simulation, architecture, plant and forestry genetics and 
genomics, and veterinary medicine, that complement the general 
collection in areas of strength and emphasis for the university. 
Qualifications include: ALA-accredited MLS or equivalent advanced degree 
in a related field. Minimum of three years’ relevant professional 
experience in collecting and managing special collections. Minimum 
salary $55,000 with required qualifications or $60,000 with preferred 
qualifications. Search by position number: C-60-0807.


Position will remain open until a suitable candidate is found.
See full vacancy announcement and further information at 
www.lib.ncsu.edu/jobs/epa.html

Apply online at https://jobs.ncsu.edu/

Affirmative Action/Equal Opportunity Employer
NC State welcomes all persons without regard to sexual orientation
Persons with disabilities requiring accommodations in the application 
and interview process please call (919) 515-3148.
