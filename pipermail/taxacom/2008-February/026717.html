2008 
UNIVERSIDAD DE LOS ANDES

The Department of Biological Sciences at the Universidad de los Andes  
(Bogotá, Colombia) seeks to fill a position for a full time assistant  
or associate professor with formal training and research experience in  
Population Genetics and Biostatistics. Applicants must have a Ph.D.  
degree, preferably with postdoctoral research and teaching experience.  
Researchers with experience in the quantitative analysis of molecular  
data in the context of evolutionary studies are especially welcome.

The successful candidate is expected to teach introductory and  
advanced courses in biostatistics, to supervise undergraduate and  
graduate students, and to promote and conduct research projects in the  
proposed field.

Send curriculum vitae, copies of recent publications, a research  
program, and two letters of recommendation by May 2, 2008 to:

Faculty Search Committee
Departamento de Ciencias Biológicas
Universidad de Los Andes
Carrera 1 No. 18A-10
P.O. Box 4976
Bogotá, Colombia
ccontbio at uniandes.edu.co
