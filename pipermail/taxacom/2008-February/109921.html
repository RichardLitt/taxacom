The 24th European Congress of Arachnology takes place from  25 - 29 August
2008 in Bern, Switzerland. It covers all arachnid orders except Acari with
the Carl Clerck symposium on systematics of spiders as a major starting
point. 
If you have interested colleagues, please forward this mail. 
All relevant information can be found here http://www.esa2008.unibe.ch
