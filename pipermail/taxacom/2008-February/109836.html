Dear Taxacomers,

The deadline for an Early registration for the International Organisation of
Plant Biosystematists meeting devoted to the Evolution of Plants in
Mountainous and Alpine Habitats that will be held in the Vysoké Tatry Mts.
in Slovakia from 2 to 4 July 2008 was extended until 31st March 2008.

Please note, however, that if you like to present an oral presentation you
should contact us as soon as possible.  

Thanks to the generous support of the Systematics Association we can cover
the fee for one senior scientist and five PhD students from the developing
countries and countries in economical transition (for details and deadline
for application see the web page of the symposium www.guarant.cz/iopb2008/).


For Registration form and information about registration please click here:

Information: http://www.guarant.cz/iopb2008/Text/registration?MenuItemId=3 

Form: https://secure.guarant.cz/iopb2008/Userpages/Interactiveappforms.aspx 

If you have any questions or any difficulties with the registration, please
contact our office at iopb2008 at guarant.cz

We are looking forward to see you in the Vysoke Tatry Mountains in 2008!

 
Karol Marhold & IOPB Team
