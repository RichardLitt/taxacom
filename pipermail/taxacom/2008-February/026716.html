2008 
UNIVERSIDAD DE LOS ANDES

The Department of Biological Sciences at the Universidad de los Andes  
(Bogotá, Colombia) seeks to fill a position for a full time assistant  
or associate professor with formal training and research experience in  
Parasitology. Applicants must have a Ph.D. degree, preferably with  
postdoctoral research and teaching experience. Researchers with  
experience in Molecular Biology and Tropical Parasitology are  
especially encouraged to apply.

The successful candidate is expected to teach and supervise  
undergraduate and graduate students, and to promote and conduct  
research projects in the proposed field.

Send curriculum vitae, copies of recent publications, a research  
program, and two letters of recommendation by December 15, 2008 to:


Faculty Search Committee
Departamento de Ciencias Biológicas
Universidad de Los Andes
Carrera 1 No. 18A-10
P.O. Box 4976
Bogotá, Colombia
