Henrik,

For the LepTree project (which is more phylogeny than taxonomy) I have 
begun tracking a number of measures of collaboration and data sharing 
among lepidopterists. As you mentioned, I started with an analysis of 
co-authorship patterns using our bibliographic database. I am also 
tracking registrations and activity on our project website: 
http://www.leptree.net (number of discussions, comments, pages posted, 
etc). I will be summarizing this for a manuscript soon, but feel free to 
email me offlist for preliminary results.
