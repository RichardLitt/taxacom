Protistology-UK Spring Meeting 6-8 April 2016, Bournemouth University

Protistology-UK (formerly the British Society for Protist Biology) is pleased to announce the launch of its new website (www.protistology.org.uk) and its upcoming Spring Meeting in Bournemouth (6-8 April 2016).

The Meeting will include sessions on the eukaryotic genome, protist ecology, eukaryote taxonomy and diversity studies in the high throughput sequencing era, and a half-day applied phycology session sponsored by Phyconet (www.phyconet.org.uk).

Early Bird registration ends on 20 March. Abstract deadline 25 March. Please go to our website to submit abstracts for posters and talks, and to apply for student bursaries.
