Intensive course in molecular systematics

The Centre for Plant Diversity and Systematics,  University of Reading.
8th April - 16th April 2002.

The course offers:
· in-depth coverage of techniques in molecular systematics and
comparative sequence analysis
· the treatment of theoretical issues in formal lectures coupled with
seminars and practical workshops
