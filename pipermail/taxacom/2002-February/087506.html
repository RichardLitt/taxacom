Dear Taxacom Subscribers,

Is there a desire among subscribers to increase the daily limit
of postings to Taxacom from 25 to some other number? To decrease
it?

PLEASE REPLY DIRECTLY TO ME AND ***NOT*** TO THE TAXACOM LIST.
Please just vote "increase by 10" or "decrease by 10", without
any comment. I don't have to be convinced; I just need a sense
of Yes or No.

I will not change the limit either way unless I receive at least
40 "votes"  asking for a change in the same direction.

On various recent days/weeks, there have been more than 25
Taxacom postings per day sent to the list.

Taxacom is configured to allow up to 25 postings/day, and then
to "hold" any excess for that day until the list administrator
"frees" the excess at which time those are also distributed (up
to the next 25).

I tend to wait until the next day to free those excess postings.
So, for instance, today at 11:15am (my time) I freed the list,
which was held since 2pm yesterday (thus accumulating not only
more postings from yesterday afternoon and evening, but also for
this morning). Fifteen new postings were thus freed this morning
(towards today's quota of 25 postings).
