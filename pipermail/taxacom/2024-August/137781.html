This is horrible news!Stephen Thorpe often held very odd opinions and 
defended these at length. All too often I did not agree with these and, 
given what happened at Wikispecies, I was not alone in this respect, but 
his dedication to his core competency was never in doubt. This is an 
atrocious way to die, and certainly a substantial loss. Paul
