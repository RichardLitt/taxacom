> Dear Rubiaceae specialist or Gentianales researcher,
>
> The deadline for early registration (reduced registration rate) and 
> abstract submission for the Third International Rubiaceae Conference 
> is approaching rapidly: 31 May 2006.
>
> On behalf of the organizing committee we want to repeat our invitation 
> for participation in this conference (University of Leuven, Belgium, 
> 18-21 September 2006).
>
> The Third Rubiaceae Conference offers a forum to all Rubiaceae 
> scientists to present the results they achieved since the last 
> Rubiaceae conference more than a decade ago, but is also open to 
> scientists working on other families of the order Gentianales.
>
> The proceedings will be published as a special volume of the Annals of 
> the Missouri Botanical Garden.
>
> Further information is available at the conference website: 
> http://www.kuleuven.ac.be/bio/sys/rubiaceae_conference
>
>
> We hope that you will be able to attend the conference.
