If I read the question correctly (software), it looks like you need
hardware - a photo/slide scanner. There was recently a number of posts on
this same topic on the Leps-l bulletin board (you could check their
archives on the web). There are some really good ones but cost about $500
to $1000 for really good ones. The best thing to do is visit the various
stores that sell these and check out the models and features. I'm sure
various other TAXACOM subscribers will post some specifics here as to what
they use and recommend. I still have not gotten one - which I will
eventually need to do.
