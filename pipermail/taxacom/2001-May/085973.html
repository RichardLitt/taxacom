Biodiversity Director
Pennsylvania Department of Conservation and Natural Resources (DCNR)
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, Harrisburg

This Executive Policy Specialist position coordinates DCNR's biodiversity
efforts including analyzing policies, regulations, practices, and
information management and exchange activities; developing goals and
objectives for the conservation and proliferation of Pennsylvania's native
biological diversity; facilitating the development of a strategic plan;
tracking DCNR's progress towards biodiversity goals; and coordinating
biodiversity education and outreach efforts.

Requires four years of environmental policy analysis and planning
experience which includes, or is supplemented by, one year in the
evaluation, development, or implementation of biodiversity policies or
programs and a bachelor's degree in an environmental or natural science, or
a closely related field; or equivalent experience/training is required.

Salary Range: $45,418 to $69,027, with attractive benefits package.  Equal
Opportunity Employer.

Send resumes and a letter summarizing qualifications to Department of
Conservation and Natural Resources; Bureau of Personnel; P.O. Box 8768;
Harrisburg, PA  17105-8768 by May 18, 2001.  For additional information
contact Sherry Cebular at 717-783-5788 or AT&T Relay Service at
1-800-654-5984 (TTY#).
