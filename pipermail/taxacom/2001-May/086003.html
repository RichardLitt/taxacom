2001 
Professor William T. Stearn, author of Botanical Latin and Dictionary of
Plant Names for Gardeners, and the author of some 470 publications, died on
9 May 2001 at the age of 90.

To any who knew William, he was a legend. Robust and hearty of spirit,
charming by nature and with an encyclopaedic mind, he was a true classical
botanist - knowledgable of plants, their nomenclature and their history.

His obituary appears in The Times of London and may be seen at:

http://www.thetimes.co.uk/article/0,,60-200191,00.html

He will be missed.

Jim Reveal
