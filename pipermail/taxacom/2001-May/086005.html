2001 
The Australian National Insect Collection and CSIRO Entomology are happy to
announce that Australian Ants Online is now available at www.ozants.com and
www.ento.csiro.au/science/ants/.  The site, prepared by Steve Shattuck and
Natalie Barnett, provides information on the biology and identification of
the entire Australian ant fauna to genus level with catalogues and
distribution information to species level.

Be sure to visit the site regularly as we work towards a complete guide to
the Australian ant fauna at the species level.
