Taxacom Community:

     A web-site has been created in association with the new publication:
Webster, G. L., and R. M. Rhode. 2001. Plant Diversity of an Andean Cloud
Forest: Inventory of the Vascular Plants of Maquipucuna, Ecuador. University
of California Publications in Botany, 82. Berkeley, CA. [ISBN 0-520-09830-7]
     The web-site is at <http://herbarium.ucdavis.edu/maqui/> and is
entitled Inventory of the Vascular Plants of Maquipucuna, Ecuador. It
contains an overview of the floristic project and florula, associated links,
sample images, and updated and corrected checklists. For more information,
contact <herbarium at ucdavis.edu>.

Thanks!
