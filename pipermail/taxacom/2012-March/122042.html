Attention tropical botanists:

check the late Leonardo Co's Digital Flora Website (www.philippineplants.org) that was launched to commemorate his first death anniversary in November 2011. The species listed in this website are linked to photographs at www.phytoimages.siu.edu. If you search Philippines in the country search field, you will notice that we already have 16,666 plant photos uploaded there, 10,795 of which were Leonard's.
