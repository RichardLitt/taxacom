The Department of Biological Sciences, National Sun Yat-Sen University, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, invites applications for a professorship of ecology, evolution or biodiversity
 
Job Title: Assistant/Associate Professor
Employer: National Sun Yat-Sen University
http://www.nsysu.edu.tw/bin/home.php?Lang=en
http://biology.nsysu.edu.tw/bin/home.php?Lang=en Location: Kaohsiung, Taiwan

Job Description
The Department of Biological Sciences of National Sun Yat-Sen University is seeking for candidates for a tenure-track faculty position in ecology, evolution, or biodiversity. The successful candidate is expected to interact with scientifically diverse faculty across campus and develop a well-recognized research program. 
Qualifications & Experience
 
Essential: A Ph.D. degree in a biological or related subjects and postdoctoral research experience
Desirable: Capable to communicate and teach in Mandarin and English.
 
Application
Applicants should submit a statement of career goals, a resume, the graduate transcript, publication reprints, and three letters of recommendation by June 30, 2012 to:
 
Prof. CHENG Jiin-Tsuey
Chairman
Department of Biological Sciences
National Sun Yat-Sen University
70, Lien Hai Rd., Kaohsiung, TAIWAN, R.O.C. 80424
 
For any other questions about the position please contact Dr. YEN Shen-Horn at +886 7 5252000 ext 3612 or shenhornyen at mail.nsysu.edu.tw
