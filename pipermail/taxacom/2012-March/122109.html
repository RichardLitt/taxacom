2012 
Dear friends and colleagues,

one and a half months are left for the low early registration rates for the the international lepidopterists' meeting in Denver in July!

The combined 2012 Annual Meeting of the Lepidopterists' Society and the Societas Europaea Lepidopterologica will be hosted by the Denver Museum of Nature & Science from 23 to 29 July 2012. We offer an exciting setting at the foot of the Rocky Mountains in a museum with great exhibits, access to the research collection, 21 field trips (19 of them free of charge), moderately priced accomodation, great food and interesting events. No membership necessary.

For registration and additional information (updated yesterday) visit http://www.dmns.org/krell-lab

Please send your suggested talk and poster titles (and abstracts when available) to the program chair, Todd Gilligan, at tgilliga at gmail.com AND copy to lepidopterist at dmns.org

Currently the following symposia are planned  (besides student and contributes papers):
Digital imaging
Trans-Beringian connections in Noctuidea
Lepidoptera - plant interactions
New developments in zoological nomenclature


For an overview of the Museums's research collections, visit http://www.dmns.org/science/collections/dmns-zoology-collections

See you in Denver in July!
