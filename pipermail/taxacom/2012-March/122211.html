Phylum Annelida

A monograph on the Polychaeta of southern Africa. J. H. Day,
1967 British Museum. Freely available under creative commons license on
archive.org (& BHL).

Offered Nabu for $27.93 & 29.45 for the 2 vols. This is a copyright breach
(no commercial use). Claimed to be pre 1923, which it is not.

Hmm, I wonder how much 900 pages cost to print & handle?  Not a lot of
profit in it.

This was the only non public domain Polychaeta topic Nabu was offering.

I'll log in to Amazon later and warn people this is freely available
digitised. It is a very good monograph for beginners and still popular.
