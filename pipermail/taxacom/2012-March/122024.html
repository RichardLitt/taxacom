I would disagree. Yes, there is a lot of garbage out there, but this one seems to be neither automatically compiled nor ill-intended. I am not sure what it intention is, but I would at least inform the authors that almost every single name they provide is wrong or has a spelling error, and that some more research on their side is necessary. They might not even know how bad it is. If they don't care, so be it, but it costs not too much to at least drop them a message.
I am not sure that the ICZN serves the public better by ignoring such web 'publications'. It certainly would not help the ICZN's perceived relevance in the public eye.
