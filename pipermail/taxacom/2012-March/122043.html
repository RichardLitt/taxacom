Dear Friends,

We have updated the web pages for the 12th International Symposium on Flatworm Biology, ISFB 2012,
at  http://bit.ly/ISFB2012

There is now a list of confirmed invited speakers, including Andreas Hejnol, Peter Reddien, Karen Smeets,
Simon Creer, Christopher Laumer.

We have added a couple of pre-conference activities. In particular, we would like to draw your attention to the workshop that will be given by Fredrik Ronquist 11 June, where he will introduce new software for phylogenetic research. Fredrik will also give a lecture on the state of the art of Bayesian phylogenetics. Not to be missed if you are interested in evolutionary biology!

We are looking forward to seeing you in Stockholm in June!

>From the local organizing committee,

Ulf Jondelius and Karin Nilsson
